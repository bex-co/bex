## Objective

Check that the dashboard, GraphQL, MCP, REST and Blueprint agree on maxmemory-change semantics for Off and persisted stores, and compare them with render.com.

## Context

- Render documents that a persistence-mode change restarts the store, and that changes to or from Off lose data (render.com/docs/key-value). It says nothing about maxmemory.

## Steps

1. Look for Render's documented or observable behavior on a maxmemory change.
2. Record any divergence in ADR018 and ADR021.
3. File the runtime `CONFIG SET` follow-up if it is still open.
