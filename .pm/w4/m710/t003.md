## Objective

Add Render's status line and address check to the shared panel, so that a reader sees how many ranges get through every level together, and can type an address to learn whether it passes and, if not, which level refuses it.

## Context

- Render's bundle: find it by the strings "Validate IP address" and "allowed from".
  - The status line is a count badge followed by "IP range is allowed from outside of your private network." or, for more than one, "IP ranges are allowed from …". The count is the intersection of every level's CIDRs: for each pair of ranges, the narrower one survives if one contains the other.
  - The field's label is "Validate IP address" and its placeholder is `0.0.0.0`. Its icon tooltip has four states: "Enter an IP address", "This IP address is not valid", "Access is allowed for this IP address" and "Access is not allowed for this IP address". The tooltip is also announced in an `aria-live` alert.
  - When an address is refused, the message names the first refusing level in this order: "Access is not allowed for this IP address based on your Workspace Inbound IP Rules.", then "… Environment Inbound IP Rules.", then "… based on your `<resource type>` Inbound IP Rules.".
  - The resource's own level uses the editor's unsaved draft when there is one.
- The CIDR helpers are in `dashboard/src/common/lib/ip-allow-list.ts`.

## Steps

1. Write the intersection and the containment check as pure functions in `common/lib/ip-allow-list.ts`, for IPv4 and IPv6.
2. Render the status line and the field in the shared panel, fed by every level the panel shows and by the editor's draft.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/common/lib/ip-allow-list.ts`
- The t001 component and its callers

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With the workspace at `10.0.0.0/8`, the environment at `10.1.0.0/16` and the service at `10.1.2.0/24` and `192.168.0.0/16`, the line reads 1 range. `10.1.2.3` is allowed. `10.2.0.1` is refused by the environment. `192.168.1.1` is refused by the workspace.
- [ ] Editing the resource's own rules without saving changes the check's answer at once.

## Out of scope

- Fetching the viewer's own address from a third party.
