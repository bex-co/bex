## Objective

A publicly routable web service has its host Ingress, pointed at the activator alias, before its first build finishes. The activator answers t001's first-deploy response for an App with no `status.activeRevision` that is still building, deploying, or waiting on its first pod. The host-cache miss on handover can no longer leak the bare `service unavailable`.

## Context

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go:1171-1173`: the first build halts the reconcile before `reconcileKubernetes` creates the Service and Ingress (`:2553-2580`).
- `ingressBackend` (`:2893-2939`) sends a non-serving sleep-eligible App to the activator. A paid App goes to its own endpoint-less Service (presumably Traefik's `503 no available server`; unverified).
- The activator branches on App state (`cmd/activator/main.go:134-185`). The unrouted branch at `:142-146` runs on a cache miss.
- Keep w1/m179/w1/132: a failed or canceled first release stays at zero with its own 503.

## Steps

1. Before halting on the first build, ensure the activator alias and the host Ingress exist for a publicly routable App with no active revision, for every plan.
2. Add the activator branch, ordered after maintenance and suspended and before wake: never-served and not failed or canceled gets the first-deploy response, with no wake patch.
3. Make the handover safe. Either the operator primes or invalidates the activator's host cache when it creates the alias, or a cache miss for a host the operator routes answers the first-deploy response rather than the bare unrouted one. Choose whichever keeps truly unknown hosts on today's response.
