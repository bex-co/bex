# ADR004 says tenant pods keep "the two Kubernetes variables"; production shows the kubelet injects eight

Why: ADR004 is the contract for what a tenant process sees. `w4/m123` fixed the 174-variable leak, but its written contract now undercounts what remains, so the next reader (or QA pass) will "find" six unexplained `KUBERNETES_*` variables.

Found in the `w4/m123` live re-probe, `/qa-find-bugs` pass 193, 2026-09-26: a Free web service running `mendhak/http-https-echo:35` with `ECHO_INCLUDE_ENV_VARS=1` reported `KUBERNETES_SERVICE_HOST`, `KUBERNETES_SERVICE_PORT`, `KUBERNETES_SERVICE_PORT_HTTPS`, `KUBERNETES_PORT`, `KUBERNETES_PORT_443_TCP`, `KUBERNETES_PORT_443_TCP_ADDR`, `KUBERNETES_PORT_443_TCP_PORT`, and `KUBERNETES_PORT_443_TCP_PROTO`. That is the master Service's full Docker-link set, not a pair. No sibling or `CM_ACME_*` variables appeared (m123 holds).

- **Fix:** in `docs/ADR004-app-deployment.md:158` (and the surrounding envVars contract), replace "the two Kubernetes variables" with the eight names above. Keep the existing explanation (kubelet `getServiceEnvVarMap` treats the `default/kubernetes` Service as unconditional). Also say that image-defined variables (`PATH`, `HOME`, runtime versions) pass through unchanged. If ADR018's env row repeats "two", correct it too.

Estimate: ~10m.
