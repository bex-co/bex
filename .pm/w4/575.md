# 575 — A disk deleted from a live service leaves its disk coverage stream open, so the month reads `disk` degraded

Why: since w4/496 (2026-11-11) an hour older than the 48 h catch-up window with no metered row counts as skipped, so a stream that simply stopped producing rows is reported `degraded` instead of pinning `through`. A disk deleted while its service lives is exactly that: its `disk_gb_seconds` stream is never closed, so from 48 h after the deletion the workspace's Billing caveat names `disk` as a degraded source for the rest of the month, though nothing is undercounted.

- **Where:** `internal/apps/disks.go` `DeleteDisk` does not close the `(service, disk_gb_seconds, disk)` stream; `CloseUsageSourceStreams` runs only for deleted resources (`usage.meterRetiredThrough`), and `ReconcileUsageSourceStreams` closes by resource, not by meter kind, so a live service keeps every stream open. The disk meter writes no row for zero GB-seconds (`usage.catchUpDisksThrough`), so nothing ever evidences the hours after the deletion.
- **Fix:** close the disk stream at the deletion hour + 1 when a disk is deleted (or have the disk meter close streams for services with no disk at the window), with a test that the next month's coverage and this month's after the deletion read healthy.
- **Source / severity / estimate:** w6 session's w4/496 follow-up review, 2026-11-11. **Minor** (a false `degraded` caveat; billing unaffected). ~45m.
