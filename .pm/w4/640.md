# 640 — On a first visit with no workspace cookie, opening a service from another workspace shows that workspace but saves the first one: the provider's fallback overwrites the adopted selection, and deleting the service then lands on the wrong workspace's Overview

Why: w6/091 made a service page adopt its own workspace, so its actions are judged in the right place. A user who arrives without a `bex.selectedWorkspaceId` cookie gets that workspace on screen but not in the cookie. Examples: a new browser, a private window, an email or Slack link, a cleared cookie. On a later full load or route invalidation they are in their first workspace again. Deleting the service sends them to `/`, which then shows another workspace's projects and services under the "Deleted …" toast.

- **Source / severity / estimate:** live `/qa-find-bugs w4` loop, round 322, 2026-11-13 20:21–20:38 UTC (`muse.env`; pin `1c4d721bc8dc`). This was the first round driven through the dashboard, from an isolated headless Chromium (`playwright-core` from the npx cache, because the shared MCP browser was held by another session). **Minor** (a wrong-workspace landing; nothing is changed in the other workspace). ~45m.
- **Account:** the QA user belongs to `bex` (`tea-d98210cbbpdc73dcrkvg`, listed first), `tian-personal` and `bex-canary` (`tea-daif693dqjvc73e7as3g`). The fixtures were in `bex-canary`.

- **Root cause (`dashboard/src/features/workspaces/`):**
  - `useAdoptResourceWorkspace` (`hooks/use-adopt-resource-workspace.ts:16-31`, called from `services/components/service-detail-layout.tsx:58`) calls `setCurrentWorkspaceId(ownerId)`. That sets `preferredId` and writes the cookie (`context/index.tsx:92-100`).
  - `WorkspaceProvider`'s membership effect (`context/index.tsx:57-90`) persists `workspaces[0]` as a fallback when the preferred id does not exist (`:83`). On a first visit `preferredId` is `null`.
  - React runs a commit's effects child-first, so in the commit where the workspace list and the service both resolve:
    - the layout's adoption effect writes bex-canary;
    - then the provider's effect, still seeing `preferredId === null` from that render, overwrites the cookie with bex.
  - The next render's `preferredId` is bex-canary, so the provider's effect takes its no-op branch. The cookie stays wrong while the screen is right.
  - **Not traced:** why `navigate({ to: "/" })` after the delete (`services/components/delete-service-card.tsx:63`) shows the cookie's workspace, when the breadcrumb navigation keeps the in-memory one. The root route reads `workspaceId` from its context (`common/root-route/root-component.tsx:15`, `:50`), and `onWorkspaceChange` is `router.invalidate()` (`:34-51`). Find which one re-reads the cookie on this path.
- **Fix (target behavior):** the persisted cookie always equals the effective selection (`selectedId`).
  - Make one writer, for example an effect in `WorkspaceProvider` that persists `selectedId` whenever it changes.
  - Or have the fallback branch skip the write when a preference was set in the same commit.
  - Either way, after a first-visit deep link the cookie reads the adopted workspace, and every navigation, reload or post-delete landing stays there.
- **Blast radius:**
  - `setCurrentWorkspaceId` has 8 callers: `workspace-switcher.tsx:76`, `use-adopt-resource-workspace.ts:28`, `routes/w.$.tsx:68`, `env-group-actions.tsx:149`, `delete-workspace-card.tsx:58`, `leave-workspace-card.tsx:70`, `use-invite-redemption.ts:68`, `new.workspace.tsx:125`.
  - `useAdoptResourceWorkspace` is also used by `agent-automation` detail and form pages.
  - All of them are subject to the same overwrite on a first visit. Regression tests:

    - an existing cookie for another workspace: adoption still moves it, as w6/091 verified;
    - the last-workspace removal path (`clearPersistedWorkspaceId`, w6/m144) is unchanged.
