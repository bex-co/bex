## Objective

Changing the stored pod template moves the Deployment's pod-template hash, so every tenant workload restarts once when this ships. Make that a decision with a plan, not a surprise at 3am.

## Context

- `applyPodSpecServerDefaults`' doc comment is explicit that server-default mirroring cannot roll a pod *because* the stored object is byte-identical. This change is the opposite case, which is exactly why it lives in the projection.
- The roll is a normal rolling update per Deployment, not a platform-wide outage — but it is every tenant service at once as the operator sweeps, which is a different load profile from ordinary deploys.
- Free-tier services that are hibernated have no pods to roll; they pick the new template up on their next wake. Confirm that and say so.

## Steps

1. Measure the blast: how many App Deployments exist in production, and what the operator's resync does to them when the projection changes.
2. Decide the rollout shape — let the normal reconcile sweep do it, or stage it — and record the choice with its reason.
3. Decide what users are told and where (a changelog entry, a status note, or nothing if a single rolling restart is below the bar — but decide it, do not default to silence).
4. Verify a hibernated free service adopts the new template on wake without an extra roll.
