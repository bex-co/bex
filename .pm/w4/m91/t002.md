## Objective

The Disk panel's "Capacity" label and the chart reference line must show the datastore's logical/billed disk size, so used/capacity fullness matches the autoscaling section and Details "Storage" on the same page. Once t001 makes the backend return the logical value this is largely automatic, but the locale annotation is now wrong and must be corrected, and the page must be verified end to end.

## Steps

1. With t001 shipped, confirm the panel already renders the logical capacity (it reads whatever the backend series carries). No value-computation change should be needed in the panel itself.

3. Verify on both a 1 GB and a 5 GB Postgres detail page that "Capacity" matches Details "Storage" and the autoscaling "N GB current", and that the used/capacity ratio matches the autoscale "% full" basis at desktop and narrow-mobile widths.
