## Objective

Turn round 180's single production sample (a 6 m 30 s wait) into a measured profile, and decide what the replacement in t002 must guarantee.

## Steps

1. On the mock cluster (`scripts/mock-cluster.sh`, pre-approved), create N = 1, 3 and 6 repo-backed Free services within 30 s. For each, record:
   - its credential write;
   - each `EnsureActive` probe result;
   - each zot bounce (`bounced zot to activate stale per-app registry credential`);
   - the build Job's creation.
2. Start a large build's push, and separately a cold pull of a registry-hosted image, then trigger a bounce. Record whether each fails or retries, and how long the registry is unreachable.
3. Explain the round 180 overrun, a wait longer than one grace plus one cooldown, from the measurements.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A table of wait times by N, and of bounce effects on an in-flight push and pull, recorded in the README.
