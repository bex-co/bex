## Objective

Turn round 180's single production sample (a 6 m 30 s wait) into a measured profile, and decide what the replacement in t002 must guarantee.

## Steps

1. On the mock cluster (`scripts/mock-cluster.sh`, pre-approved), create N = 1, 3 and 6 repo-backed Free services within 30 s. For each, record:
   - its credential write;
   - each `EnsureActive` probe result;
   - each zot bounce (`bounced zot to activate stale per-app registry credential`);
   - the build Job's creation.
2. Start a large build's push, and separately a cold pull of a registry-hosted image, then trigger a bounce. Record whether each fails or retries, and how long the registry is unreachable.
3. Explain the round 180 overrun, a wait longer than one grace plus one cooldown, from the measurements.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A table of wait times by N, and of bounce effects on an in-flight push and pull, recorded in the README.

## Outcome (2026-11-14)

Done on the local CAPD mock cluster, against zot `v2.1.22` at the production digest. The method and the table are in the milestone README's "t001: activation measured on the mock cluster".

- **Without a nudge:** a raw Secret write activated in 16.6 s (the kubelet's periodic sync).
- **With the annotation nudge:** 1.2 s.
- **Six users written back to back:** all active within 4.9 s.
- **Isolation:** `403`.
- **Revocation:** refused in 0.8–1.1 s.
- **Restarts:** none.

The live-App variant (N repo-backed Free services through the operator) was not run, because the mock cluster runs an operator image from before this change, so the Secret writes were driven directly. A restart's effect on an in-flight push or pull was not re-measured, because it would have meant restarting the shared mock registry. Round 190's production evidence stands for it.
