## Objective

Retain the already loaded, still-in-window log rows, continuation and reader position across automatic relative-range ticks, including the pending response interval.

## Context

The reproduced 140→100 drop follows `LogViewer → useLiveRange → useLogHistory → useOlderLogPages`. The clock changes transport variables, the pager clears its older rows, Apollo exposes an empty pending result for the new window, and the list remounts pinned to the bottom. See [finding.md](finding.md) for both reproductions and actual pinned-library paths.

## Steps

1. Distinguish the reader identity (resource, actual filters, explicit preset/custom selection) from effective start/end bounds advanced by that selection's clock.
2. Keep the previous usable head and older pages for that same reader during refresh. Merge new head results with stable keys and preserve backward continuation; reconcile the moving lower bound so expired rows do not accumulate forever.
3. Keep the populated LogLineList mounted and its unpinned anchor stable during automatic refresh. First-ever loading may use the existing structural skeleton.
4. Deliberate resource/filter/range changes reset immediately and discard old in-flight responses; never show one resource's previousData under another id or permission state. Same-reader transport failure keeps useful content with honest failure handling.
5. Preserve current SSE subscription identity, lower-bound/resume behavior, custom fixed bounds and explicit range/filter URL state. Do not disable the shared metrics clock or raise the backend page cap.
