## Objective

Guard bex's prices with Render's, as the plan-table test guards the plan comparison rows.

## Context

- [ADR030](../../../docs/ADR030-pricing.md) §2: compute, Postgres, Key Value, build minutes and cron are 30% off Render (× 0.70); bandwidth is 90% off.
- `pricing.yaml` has 7 compute tiers, 6 Postgres tiers and 9 Key Value tiers. Render plans bex cannot run yet belong to w6/blocked/m332 and w6/blocked/114 (node capacity).
- Render's own pages disagree on Key Value connections: the pricing page lists `256mb` 200 and `1g` 250, [compute-plans](https://render.com/docs/compute-plans) lists 250 and 1,000; bex enforces 250 and 1,000 (w6/m456). ADR030 §9 already records two such disagreements (the Workspaces and Enforce 2FA rows) with the source bex follows.

## Steps

1. Map every Render plan row to one disposition: a bex tier (its `usdPerSecond` × seconds per month equals Render's price × 0.70 within one cent), a gap with its owner (w6/m332, w6/114), or n/a with the reason (Workflows, billed elsewhere; Sandboxes, ADR104).
2. Check the cron minimum and the per-minute cron rates against Render's.
3. Record the Key Value connection disagreement in the fixture's disposition and in ADR030 §9, with compute-plans as the source bex follows and why.
4. Fail on a fixture row with no disposition and on a disposition whose row is gone.
