## Objective

Put the four price tables and the FAQ on render.com/pricing into fixtures beside the plan-table fixtures, so a test can compare bex's prices with Render's.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/pricing/testdata/` has `render-plan-table.tsv` (92 rows of the plan comparison table, read 2026-11-16) and `render-features-by-plan.tsv`; `TestEveryRenderPlanRowHasADisposition` (`plan_table_test.go:584`) guards them. On 2026-11-27 every row still matched the live page except for parentheses in the markdown form.
- The price tables are not captured anywhere: `pricing.yaml` carries them only as comments ("Render $7.00 × 0.70"), and tests such as `TestWorkspacePlanFeesAreThirtyPercentOffRender` (`pricing_test.go:49`) hard-code the expected values.
- render.com/pricing.md (read 2026-11-27) lists 15 compute plans (`free` … `12c-96g`), 24 Postgres plans (`free` … `128c-1024g`, with connection limits), 9 Key Value plans (with connection limits) and 11 cron plans (per minute), plus Workflows and Sandboxes rates.

## Steps

1. Capture each table as a TSV with its read date and source URL in a header comment, every column as displayed.
2. Capture the FAQ's questions and answers that state a billing rule (the plan-fee waiver, proration, the $1 card check) in a small fixture.
