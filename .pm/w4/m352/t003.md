## Objective

REST `POST /v1/services/{id}/shell-ticket`, GraphQL `createShellSession`, native SSH (raw `ssh` and `render ssh`) and the Web Shell agree: a service with a live deploy and a deploy in progress opens a session on its serving instance, and keeps it open until that instance stops.

## Steps

1. Exercise all four surfaces during a `pre_deploy_in_progress` deploy (raw `ssh` and `render ssh` need a disposable `qa-` SSH key, deleted afterwards).
2. Compare against Render: its CLI "verifies a live deploy" (ADR035 § Context). Capture what Render does to an open session during a deploy, if a Render account is available, and record it in ADR035.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The four surfaces agree with the target behavior. Any drift is filed as a follow-up.
