## Objective

Add tests that assert this milestone's behavior and its failure modes. No trivial or snapshot-everything tests.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The gate: every App phase × {never served, serving} → open or refuse, with the refusal's message.
- [ ] Both transports: a session over a serving instance survives a deploy's ticks and ends when its pod is deleted. Its stream is never moved to another replica (ADR035 § Fail-closed behavior).
- [ ] Regressions that must still refuse or end:
  - suspended;
  - Free;
  - a protected environment for a non-Admin (w8/193);
  - a revoked member, ended at the next tick (codex round-9 #6);
  - a first deploy not yet live.

## Outcome (2026-11-14)

- Gate matrix: `TestSSHSessionReadyIgnoresThePhase` covers every `AppPhase` (plus empty) × {serving, no revision, no image} with the exact refusal message; `TestSSHSessionReadyRefusalReasons` pins each reason code.
- Both transports over the production resolver: a session survives ~8 ticks in each of Building, Deploying and Running and ends at the tick after its pod is deleted; the exec target stays the original pod (never moved).
- Regressions: suspended and Free (`TestWebSocketSuspensionEndsTheShellNamed`, `TestWebSocketFreePlanEndsTheShellNamed`, `TestSSHEligibilityFailsClosed`, `TestCreateShellSessionSuspendedIsConflict`); protected environment for a non-Admin (`TestProtectedMemberShellNeedsAdmin`, and a protected-environment `FORBIDDEN` end reads only `revoked`); revoked member at the next tick (`TestWebSocketMidStreamRevocationEndsShell`, `TestWebSocketRevocationEndsTheShellWithoutDetail`, native `TestMidStreamRevocationEnds*`); first deploy not live (`TestCreateShellSessionDuringDeploy`, gate matrix).
