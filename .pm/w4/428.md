# 428 — A port-changing rollout of an image-port service still leaves its Services without a ready endpoint between the old pod's drain and promotion

Why: w4/m243 t012 removed the revision pin for same-port rollouts (every deploy, env save and restart), but a rollout that changes the image network's ports still pins the stable Services to the serving revision until `promoteImageNetwork` runs after `deploymentRolloutReady`. By then the Deployment has scaled the old ReplicaSet away and its pod is draining (not ready), so the Services have no ready endpoint until the next reconcile moves the selector: Traefik's raw `503 no available server` for about 1–5 s on a `PORT` change.

**Severity:** minor (only port-changing deploys; same-port deploys are fixed). **Estimate:** ~1h. **Source:** split from w4/m243 t012 in the loopx w4 drain, 2026-10-28.

## Fix (target behavior)

- Promote the candidate network as soon as a candidate pod is Ready and before the old ReplicaSet scales down, or hold the old ReplicaSet until the selector has moved. No request may reach a pod without the new listener (t007).
- The failed-rollout restore (`app_controller.go` "served release has no matching image network metadata") requires `ServingNetwork.Revision == ActiveRevision`; an early promotion must keep that path working (a failed port change still restores the serving network, a rollback goes `live`).

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/image_network.go`, `app_controller.go` (`reportKubernetesRunning`, the restore path)
