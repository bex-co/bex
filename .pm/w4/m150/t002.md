## Objective

Constrain the fix to header selection and preserve every already-working caller identified in the finding.

## Context

There are three production `matchPattern` calls: one route and two header consumers; two normal-header call sites and two error-header call sites. The finding enumerates three SetHeaders adapter callers, two creation/setter conversions, Blueprint projection, two UI URL families and all seven resource types.

## Steps

1. Re-run those exact caller searches on implementation HEAD and document any changes. Apply header matching globally across static sites, but keep the route caller compatible.
2. Audit GET/HEAD success, explicit rewrite, implicit fallback, redirect and resolved-site 400/404/413/502/503. Preserve existing-object precedence, request-path selection, content/MIME/cache behavior, body metadata exclusions and platform Retry-After.
3. Keep unknown-host 404 and method 405 headerless. Keep authentication, authorization, deleting-resource absence and non-static gates unchanged; verify web/private/worker/cron plus the separate Postgres/Key Value families with local tests.
4. Check header persistence through the two writers and Blueprint projection. Create-time headers, REST/MCP writes, named/legacy read aliases and custom-domain host aliases were not live-probed by this hunt; assign and execute safe verification rather than assuming coverage.
5. Record choices for malformed patterns, escaped/literal metacharacters and encoded/Unicode paths. Do not silently broaden selectors beyond the documented grammar or change resource error taxonomy.
