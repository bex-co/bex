# 650 — Every new service still orders its own Let's Encrypt certificate for `<slug>.onbex.co`, though `*.onbex.co` covers it: the 50-per-week `onbex.co` budget is exhausted, and real customers' new services sit on errored Certificates

Why: Let's Encrypt allows 50 new certificates per registered domain per week. Each App's serving Ingress carries the cert-manager HTTP-01 issuer and a per-host TLS secret, so every service created — QA churn included — orders a certificate for its platform host. The platform already serves a `*.onbex.co` wildcard as Traefik's default certificate (ADR093), so these per-host certificates add nothing for platform hosts, and once the budget is gone the orders fail for days.

- **Source / severity / estimate:** found by `/loopx w4` while diagnosing w4/m357, 2026-11-14 03:15 UTC, read-only on the production cluster. **Minor today** (TLS still works: the wildcard is the fallback), but it starves every other `onbex.co` issuance and leaves failing Certificates and alerts behind. ~1h.
- **Evidence (production, 2026-11-14):**
  - `kubectl get orders -A`: `tea-dbq2tg234ugc73cu1bjg-hgzyapi-tls-1-3481472454` and `…-quchartapi-tls-1-3296502864` are `errored`: `429 urn:ietf:params:acme:error:rateLimited: too many certificates (50) already issued for "onbex.co" in the last 168h0m0s, retry after 2026-11-14 03:15:36 UTC`.
  - Their Certificates (`READY False`) name a single platform host each (`hgzyapi.onbex.co`), in a customer workspace, not a QA one.
  - `openssl s_client -servername hgzyapi.onbex.co` serves `CN=*.onbex.co` (Let's Encrypt YR1, until 2027-01-29), and `curl https://hgzyapi.onbex.co/` answers 200: the fallback hides the failure from visitors.
  - A healthy service's Certificate (`tea-daif693dqjvc73e7as3g-hello-go-tls`) also lists only `hello-go.onbex.co`.
- **Root cause:** `applyIngressRouting` (`lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go:543-560`) stamps `cert-manager.io/cluster-issuer` on every App Ingress, and `appendIngressHostRoute` (`:567-569`) adds a TLS entry with a per-host Secret for every host, platform hosts included. ADR093 kept "per-host certificates (HTTP-01, unchanged here)" for all hosts.
- **Fix (target behavior):** a host the platform wildcard covers (a single label under `BEX_BASE_DOMAIN`) gets no per-host certificate: no TLS Secret entry for it (Traefik's default TLSStore serves the wildcard), and an Ingress with only such hosts carries no cluster-issuer annotation. Custom domains keep their per-host HTTP-01 certificates (ADR005), and so do deeper names the wildcard does not cover. Existing per-host Certificates and Secrets for platform hosts are left to expire or are removed once (decide; removing frees nothing in the budget but clears `CertificateNotReady` noise).
  - Amend ADR093 (and ADR005's per-host line) to say platform hosts are served by the wildcard alone, and why: the shared weekly budget.
  - Check HTTP→HTTPS and HSTS behavior is unchanged with the TLS entry gone (Traefik's websecure entrypoint still terminates with the default certificate).
- **Tests:** an App with only its platform host gets an Ingress with no TLS Secret entry and no issuer annotation; an App with a custom domain keeps both for that host; an App with both gets a TLS entry for the custom domain only.
- **Relation:** w4/m357 (a new public Postgres keeps its private-CA certificate) suspects the same budget for `bex.co`; datastore hosts are under `bex.co`, a different registered domain, so this fix does not touch that budget.
- **DoD (live):** create a fresh `qa-` web service; no Certificate or Order appears for its `<slug>.onbex.co`, and its URL serves `*.onbex.co`. A custom domain added to it still gets its own certificate. Delete the fixture.
