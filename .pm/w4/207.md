# A ~50 s outage right after a service's first deploy produced no `server_failed`/`server_available` at all

Why: on production after w5/m113, two of three identical health-check outages on one service were stamped exactly, but the first (≈78 s after its first deploy ended) left **no** availability events. Users saw 503s for 51 s, and webhooks, push and the Events tab said nothing.

- **Severity:** major (an outage goes unreported), but intermittent (1 of 3).
- **Repro (loop61, 2026-10-06 UTC, pin `0903a31b6` ⊇ `01f24d0a3`, `bex-canary`):** owned Free `qa-20261006-l61-hc` (`srv-db24dqf4lo4c73eda6a0`, `traefik/whoami`, `WHOAMI_PORT_NUMBER=8080`, port 8080, `healthCheckPath: /health`; deleted afterwards). `deploy_ended` succeeded at 00:55:18Z. Each run: `curl` `/` every ~2 s from outside, then `POST /health` body `500`; the liveness restart recovers it.

  | run | flip | first 503 | first 200 again | `server_failed` | `server_available` |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 1 | 00:56:11 | **00:56:36** | 00:57:27 (51 s) | **none** | **none** |
  | 2 | 01:00:55 | 01:01:17 | 01:02:06 (49 s) | 01:01:16Z | 01:02:06Z |
  | 3 | 01:05:44 | 01:06:06 | 01:06:57 (51 s) | 01:06:06Z | 01:06:56Z |

  `GET /v1/services/<id>/events?limit=15` was re-read at 01:00:30 (3 min after run 1 recovered): only `deploy_started` and `deploy_ended`; GraphQL `phase: Running`.
- **Cause: unverified.** Two candidates, both in `lego/backend/internal/store/`:
  1. **Debounce window.** `debounceUnhealthy` (`reconciler.go:290-308`, w3/m78) suppresses the first unhealthy pass, and the resync is 30 s. If the operator's Ready=False lands just after a pass, only one pass may fall inside a ~50 s outage. It is suppressed, the next pass is healthy again, and no edge ever records. m78 documents the debounce as "merely delayed one tick" for real outages and never decides outages shorter than ~two passes.
  2. **No healthy baseline yet.** The checkpoint's first healthy conclusion after the initial rollout may not have been recorded by 00:56:33. Without a healthy previous availability, `observedStateFacts` has no healthy→unhealthy edge to emit. Runs 2 and 3, minutes later, had one.
- **Next step (diagnosis first):** on dev-N, replay the same flip at +60 s, +90 s and +240 s after a first deploy, logging each reconciler pass's `ObservedServiceState` (availability, suppressed, ReadyTransitionAt) and the checkpoint row. Then decide between "emit a backdated `server_failed` when a suppressed unhealthy pass is followed by recovery inside the window" (target: every user-visible outage ≥ one resync leaves both edges) and "establish the healthy baseline on deploy success".
- **Blast radius:** the service and datastore observation guards (`guardServiceObservation`, `guardDatastoreObservation`) share the debounce, so the datastore twin needs the same probe.
- **Related:** w4/200 and w5/m113 (the lateness fix, verified exact here in runs 2 and 3); w3/m78 (the debounce's origin).
- **Render:** Render reports instance failures as events regardless of duration.
- **Estimate:** diagnosis ~45m; the fix depends on which cause it is.
