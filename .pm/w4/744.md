# Bandwidth served to a DDoS attack is not billed, as on Render

Why: Render parity. Render's FAQ promises "As part of Render's DDoS protection, Render does not bill for bandwidth usage incurred from a DDoS attack". bex bills every response byte, so a flood grows a customer's bandwidth bill where Render's would not, and neither ADR018's DDoS row nor `w21/126` mentions billing.

- **Render (read 2026-11-27):** [faq](https://render.com/docs/faq), "What does Render bill for?" › Outbound bandwidth: inbound bandwidth is free, and bandwidth from a DDoS attack is not billed.
- **bex:** `egress_bytes` counts every response byte at the Traefik router (`traefik_router_responses_bytes_total`, `lego/backend/internal/egressquery/query.go`), with no exclusion. ADR018 § Custom domains: Automatic DDoS protection for every tenant host is ◐ and parked on `w21/126` (the user's sign-off or the Cloudflare decision).
- **Do:** decide how bex recognises attack traffic (for example, traffic its edge rate limits or drops, or a window an operator marks as an attack) and exclude it from `egress_bytes`, or credit it. Record the rule in [ADR023](../../docs/ADR023-usage-metering.md) and the DDoS row. If it waits for `w21/126`'s decision, park it on that decision.
- **End state:** bandwidth served during a recognised attack does not appear on the customer's invoice, and ADR023 states the rule.
- **Size:** the decision and the ledger about 30m; the exclusion may need a promotion.
- **Source:** w4/m688 t002, a full read of Render's FAQ, 2026-11-27.
