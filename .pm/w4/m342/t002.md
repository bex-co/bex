## Objective

Select every isolation boundary by the stable `environment-id` (t001), and decide "isolated" from the environment's state in the policies themselves. A toggle then takes effect on the pods already running, in one reconcile, in both directions. Pods are never replaced, and same-environment traffic never drops.

## Context

- **The blanket allow:** `sameNamespaceNetworkPolicy` (`lego/backend/internal/store/namespaces.go:728-745`) selects pods where `network-isolation` `DoesNotExist`. It is the reason an old, unlabelled pod of a newly isolated service stays reachable from outside, and a newly unlabelled pod cannot reach a still-labelled one.
- **The per-App policy:** `reconcileProtectedAppNetworkPolicy` (`lego/operator/internal/controller/environment_network_policy.go:83-123`) selects `app=<name>`, with peers `network-isolation=<env>` or `environment-id=<env>`.
- **The datastore peer policy:** `reconcileEnvironmentPeerPolicy` (`:41-80`).
- **Key Value's client selector:** `keyvalue_internal_network.go:53-59`.

## Steps

1. Choose one shape and record it in ADR043. Suggested:
   - The blanket `allow-same-namespace` excludes pods whose `environment-id` is **In** the workspace's isolated environments: a `matchExpressions` `NotIn` list, maintained wherever an environment's isolation is written.
   - One policy per isolated environment selects `environment-id=<env>`, with ingress and egress to `environment-id=<env>` peers. That covers Apps, datastores and cron pods alike.
   - A toggle rewrites those two objects only.
   - Pods with no environment are unaffected.
2. Retire `syncPolicyLabels`' isolation half and the label-driven per-App policy once nothing selects by `network-isolation`.
3. Datastores move to the same per-environment policy in t003.
4. Record why the rollout-based design was dropped: the restarts, the 20–44 s enforcement and the same-environment gap from round 177.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] In envtest, toggling isolation on an environment with two Running Apps creates no new ReplicaSet. The policies converge in one reconcile.
- [ ] An outside pod is refused, and an inside pod admitted, by the policies alone, with no pod replaced.
