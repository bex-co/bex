## Objective

A client can tell, before the click, that a Developer in a protected environment may not reveal a datastore's connection info, change its IP rules, or touch an env group's values. Decided by the verbs' own rule (`core.RestrictedDecision`, w4/m578), never a second copy of it.

## Context

- `postgres.ActionCapabilities` (`postgres/actioncaps.go:36`) and `keyvalue.ActionCapabilities` (`keyvalue/actioncaps.go:34`) back GraphQL `databaseActions` / `keyValueActions`. w4/m578 added suspend and resume through `core.RestrictedDecision`.
- Add action ids beside `core.ActionSuspend` (`core/decision.go`), for example `view_connection_info` and `update_ip_allow_list`, each decided by `RestrictedDecision` over the permission the verb opens with (`can_view_sensitive`, `can_operate` or whatever the patch checks) and the verb from t001/t002.
- Env groups have no projection. Either add one (`envGroupActions`, GraphQL) or let the dashboard decide from the group's environment `protectedStatus` and the workspace `canManage`, as `useProtectedPlacement` does for Move. Pick the one that cannot drift from t003's gate and record why.
- The mobile app reads the same projections and hides a denied action (ADR087); an unknown action id reads as absent there.

## Steps

1. Add the action ids and their decisions; update the golden schema if the projection's GraphQL shape changes (`UPDATE_SCHEMA_GOLDEN=1 go test ./internal/api -run TestGraphQLSchemaMatchesTheGolden`).
2. Tests: projection and verb agree for an Admin, a Developer and a Viewer, protected and not (the m578 tables in each package's actioncaps tests).
