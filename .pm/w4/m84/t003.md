## Objective

Turn `VerifyDomain` into a fresh DNS ownership check that promotes exactly the claim whose challenge was observed, then converges the App projection to serve it.

## Context

Main's `DomainOwnershipVerifier` already gives tests a deterministic resolver seam, but `VerifyDomain` currently rereads certificate/TLS status and the add path uses a deterministic pre-proof. A pending claim needs its persisted random value checked at `_bex-challenge.<registrable-domain>` and an atomic compare-and-promote so delete/recreate or challenge rotation cannot validate the wrong row.

## Steps

1. Read the authorized pending claim and derive the exact TXT record name while taking the expected value only from its durable row.
2. Treat resolver timeout/error, absent value, and mismatch as a named pending/conflict result with no state change.
3. Promote by claim id + expected challenge/state in one conditional Store write; a stale verifier result must lose harmlessly.
4. Trigger/reuse normal desired-state projection after promotion and return pending certificate/server status honestly until the operator/cert-manager reports active.
5. Define idempotent verification for an already-verified owning claim and fresh authorization on every attempt.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Wrong/missing TXT never changes the claim or App serving hosts.
- [ ] Correct TXT promotes once and the verified hostname reaches normal projection.
- [ ] Delete/recreate and concurrent verify races cannot promote a stale challenge.
- [ ] Resolver failure fails closed and remains retryable.
- [ ] Already-verified verify is idempotent and still authorization-gated.

## Out of scope

- Continuous background DNS polling, domain expiry policy, nameserver delegation, or wildcard certificates beyond the existing supported hostname rules.
