## Objective

Compare the completed claim/verify workflow with Render's current custom-domain contract and keep REST, GraphQL, MCP, dashboard, docs, and CLI-observable behavior intentionally aligned.

## Context

Render requires domain verification and exposes DNS instructions/status, while bex's stronger random TXT claim may be a deliberate extension. The official Render CLI and pinned artifacts are the evidence sources; inference from dashboard labels alone is not enough. Do not weaken the non-serving pending invariant to imitate a less explicit surface.

## Steps

1. Re-capture or inspect Render add/list/get/verify behavior, response fields, conflict/error shape, and dashboard state language.
2. Exercise the same lifecycle through bex REST, GraphQL, MCP, dashboard, and official CLI where supported.
3. Update ADR005/ADR018 and pinned artifacts with exact matches and deliberate security extensions; file real drift separately.
