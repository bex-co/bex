## Objective

(`lego/backend/internal/metrics/source.go:413`) — the same Prometheus
`increase()` primitive t001 removes from `http_requests` for extrapolating
bursty traffic. Verify live whether bursty egress misreports the same way, and
either apply the same exact-count treatment or record a written, evidenced
decision that the chart is deliberately a rate (labelled as one).
