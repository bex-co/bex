# w4/260 — Environment Export closes during background refresh

Why: users lose an open export menu while reading its options, even though the page, resource and eventual availability are unchanged.

**Severity:** minor. **Estimate:** 55m. Functional live QA a209, 2026-10-11 UTC, production dashboard, research HEAD `155fb3cb5`. Credentials remained inside the login helper using `muse.env`. Filing only.

## Reproduction and target behavior

On the owned Free prebuilt web service `qa-20261011-a209-rollback` (`srv-db5sgu49vsac73cmev20`), open Environment → Export, then leave the page untouched. At 390px, the menu was present from 17:50:04.568 through 17:50:34.618 and absent at 17:50:39.629, remaining closed through 17:50:59.667. A fresh desktop reload reproduced the closure: the original Export button disappeared at 17:54:31.267, a disabled replacement briefly appeared inside a tooltip span, and an enabled replacement appeared 63 ms later. The menu remained closed through 17:55:00.711, at the same URL. Only DOM observation was added; requests and responses were not altered.

Expected: an already-open menu stays mounted through a same-context background refresh. While availability is checking, its export items are disabled; when the normal successful refresh completes, the same menu becomes usable again without replaying a selection. Escape, outside click and an explicit completed selection still close it. This does not change existing availability checks or the exported values.

Control: choosing Copy env vars immediately succeeded at 17:48:14.823 with `QA_A209_MARKER="qa-a209-v2"\n` and the Environment copied toast. Saved V2 and running V1 were intentionally different. The failure is lost menu state, not wrong export contents. Download completion was not exercised in this pass.

## Researched cause and bounded work

`dashboard/src/features/services/components/service-environment-editor.tsx:729–764` puts the **whole DropdownMenu root** inside PermissionTooltip. `dashboard/src/features/capabilities/components/permission-tooltip.tsx:30–43` returns children directly without a reason, but places them under Tooltip/TooltipTrigger/span when a reason exists. The normal freshness transition changes that position and remounts the root. The provider's receipt-expiry timer and checking state are at `capabilities-provider.tsx:284–306`; its poll is at 259–262. The capture proves the transient wrapper/replacement and closure, not a guaranteed expiry on every poll.

Actual installed React is 19.2.3, Radix dropdown-menu 2.1.16 and tooltip 1.2.8. Opened pinned dropdown implementation `node_modules/@radix-ui/react-dropdown-menu/dist/index.js:90–117`: the root owns uncontrolled open state with `defaultOpen ?? false`. Trigger handlers/ref composition at 123–155 toggle that root; they do not preserve state after its removal. React documents that removing a component resets state at its tree position ([state preservation](https://react.dev/learn/preserving-and-resetting-state), consulted 2026-10-11). This explains the capture without attributing it to an outside click or a whole-page loading skeleton.

**Work units, total 55m:**

1. **Consumer census / compatibility, 10m:** preserve the two production EnvironmentEditor consumers: ServiceEnvironmentEditor (`service-environment-editor.tsx:124`) and EnvGroupEditors (`features/env-groups/components/env-group-editors.tsx:30`). ServiceEnvPage mounts the service wrapper once (`routes/services.$serviceId.env.tsx:58`) and is reused by `routes/static.$serviceId.env.tsx:13`. Web/static/cron/worker/private share the service environment surface; Postgres/Key Value do not use this editor. Group export and other service families were not reproduced live. Audit their real component callers rather than globally changing PermissionTooltip.
2. **Local menu lifetime fix, 15m:** keep DropdownMenu's root and content at a stable position outside the changing tooltip wrapper; wrap only the trigger or use an equivalently stable local tree. Preserve the disabled-button explanation and anchor/ref behavior. Apply existing loading/error/exporting/sensitive availability conditions to items while open, retaining `exportEnvironment`'s guard at 555–573. No provider or global tooltip rewrite.
3. **Behavioral verification and replay, 30m:** use real mounted Radix primitives, change the reason from absent → checking → absent, and assert the same open menu survives, cannot export while checking, recovers without automatic export, preserves focus and anchor bounds, then closes normally by keyboard/selection. Cover both consumers, Copy/Download success and existing error behavior, followed by fresh desktop and 390px live replay across the captured expiry ordering. Run relevant dashboard checks and simplify the local diff.
