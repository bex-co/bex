## Objective

`service_woken` carries the moment the woken instance became ready, not the pass that noticed it. The Activity feed then orders a cycle causally even when an availability edge is emitted in the same pass.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/store/event_facts.go:505-512`: `makeFact` stamps phase facts with `obs.At`.
- `:496-501` `availabilityEdgeAt` (`w4/196`) back-dates only the availability edges to `obs.ReadyTransitionAt`. Within its bounds: after the checkpoint's last change, not after the observation.
- Live: wake 1 had its Ready transition and first 200 at 00:01:25, but `service_woken` at 00:01:40. Wake 3: 200 at 00:15:22, woken at 00:15:25. `w4/m147`: 200 at 07:03:15, woken at 07:03:27.
- `ObservedServiceState.ReadyTransitionAt` is set for a healthy Running conclusion (`reconciler.go:1840-1845`).

## Steps

1. Stamp the `woken` fact (`event_facts.go:534`) with the Ready=True transition, using the same bound as `availabilityEdgeAt`. Fall back to `obs.At` when that time is missing or out of bounds.
2. Keep the source key stable. It embeds `obs.At`, and replay idempotency depends on it; change only `At`.
3. Add a test: Hibernated (held) → Running observed 15 s after its Ready transition. `service_woken.At` must equal the transition, and the fact must sort at or after `service_hibernated`.
