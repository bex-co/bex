# 399 — Pausing the service log tail doesn't freeze the view: the history window keeps sliding every 30 s, so new lines keep appearing under "Live tail paused"

Why: a user pauses Logs to read a burst. The point of pausing is that the list stops moving, but new matching lines keep arriving every 30 s, and in a relative range the oldest rows expire from under the reader, while the footer says "Live tail paused".

**Severity:** minor (the paused view drifts; nothing is lost and Live resumes correctly). **Estimate:** ~45m. **Source:** live `/qa-find-bugs w4` loop run l2t6, 2026-10-23/24 UTC (`muse.env`, `bex-canary`, pin `29d63e1f7bea`). Fixture: Free BusyBox web service `qa-20261023-l2t6-log` (`srv-dbdvc3pf0c2s738fsgd0`), printing `l2t6 tick N` every 2 s and `l2t6 marker-five N` every 10 s. It was deleted (`GET` → `404`); session revoked.

## Probe

1. `/services/srv-dbdvc3pf0c2s738fsgd0/logs?text=marker-five` (Last hour, Live on). Live works: the newest marker advances. Search narrows to markers only, URL-backed.
2. Turn **Live** off: the switch reads `aria-checked="false"`, the footer says "Live tail paused", and the URL gains `&live=0`.
3. Over the next 65 s, without touching the page, the newest marker shown went **150 → 160 → 175** (sampled at +5 s, +35 s, +65 s).
4. Cause, observed directly:
   - Wrapping `window.EventSource` shows the `/v1/logs/subscribe?…` stream **is** closed on pause (its message count stops).
   - A GraphQL capture shows the page re-running `Logs` every 30 s with a sliding window while paused. At `00:11:24Z` it sent `startTime 23:11:24.810Z → endTime 00:11:24.810Z`, and at `00:11:54Z` it sent `23:11:54.809Z → 00:11:54.809Z` (`limit: 100`). The same batch re-sent `LogLabelValues` ×4, `Server`, `Deploys` and `DeployActions`.

## Root cause

`dashboard/src/features/logs/components/log-viewer.tsx:176-179` derives the history window from `useLiveRange(range, …)`. That hook (`dashboard/src/features/metrics/hooks/use-live-range.ts:29-63`, shared with Metrics) re-anchors `now` on a `setInterval` of `range.resolutionSeconds` (30 s for "Last hour") for any relative range, and knows nothing about Live. `useLiveLogs({ enabled: live && liveSupported })` closes the stream, but the history query keeps sliding. w4/m192 fixed paused rows **disappearing**; it didn't make the paused view stop advancing.

## Fix (target behavior)

- While Live is off, the service log view is a snapshot. The relative window freezes at the moment of pause: no history refetch, no new rows, no expiry. Turning Live back on re-anchors to now and resumes the stream. Older-page loading ("Load older") still works while paused.
- Gate the clock: e.g. `useLiveRange(range, key, { paused: !live })`, or freeze the window in `LogViewer` when `live` is false. Leave Metrics' own refresh unchanged (`services.$serviceId.metrics.tsx:82`, `scaling-recent-metrics.tsx:59` are the other callers).
- Control, from the code: the datastore log viewer (`datastore-log-viewer.tsx:195-212`) samples its window once per reader (`rangeWindow(range)` on reader change) and never slides, so its view already stays put. That is the behavior the service viewer should match while paused.
- Test: with fake timers, pause and advance 90 s: no `Logs` refetch and identical rows. Resume: one refetch with a fresh `endTime`.

**Unverified:** the datastore viewer live (read from code only); deploy-log panel (it has no Live switch).
