## Objective

Check that every surface reporting bandwidth agrees with the bill, and compare with Render.

## Steps

1. For a workspace with a window, compare REST `GET /v1/usage`, GraphQL `usage`, MCP `get_usage`, the Billing page and the Outbound Bandwidth footer. Each shows the same billed bandwidth, with the window's hours left out.
2. Render: no surface exposes an attack window or an attack line, and its bill simply carries none of the attack's bandwidth. Confirm bex adds no customer-facing line or field that Render lacks, or record any it adds in the ADR018 row.
3. File each difference as a note.
