## Objective

A deploy whose image metadata read was answered with 429 until the rollout budget ran out fails under a rate-limited reason that carries Render's remedies (wait for the limit to reset, use a credential for an account with a higher limit, or mirror the image), not under `image_registry_unreachable` ("did not answer … for {minutes} minutes").

## Context

- The wait already tells 429 apart: `reasonImageRegistryRateLimited` and `imageRegistryRateLimitedMessage` (`lego/operator/internal/controller/image_network.go:693-694`, `:772-773`).
- The final failure is `registryUnreachableError` → `DeployReasonImageRegistryUnreachable` (`lego/types/v1alpha1/deploy_reasons.go:51`, `:226`; w4/692), and `imageMetadataFailureReason` (`image_network.go:780-788`) maps it to `ReasonImagePullFailed`, so bex-api emits `image_pull_failed`.
- Render: "Rate limited (`429` error) … The registry rejected the image pull because it exceeded the rate limit."

## Steps

1. Record whether the last failed read was a 429 on the registry wait, and fail with a new `image_registry_rate_limited` reason when it was.
2. Word it in English in `deploy_reasons.go` and in the 7 other languages in `dashboard/src/features/deploys/locales/`.
3. Keep `ReasonImagePullFailed`, so the deploy still records `image_pull_failed`.
