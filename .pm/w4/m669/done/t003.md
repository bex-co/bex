## Objective

Keep the Ready condition's cause on the `image_pull_failed` fact, as `artifact_fetch_failed` already keeps its message, and answer it as `details.message` on REST, GraphQL, MCP and the webhook payload. Show it in the Image Pull Failed email and the dashboard Events feed.

## Context

- Render's schema requires `message` and `imageURL`; the reason is a deploy-reason template, so the dashboard words it in its own language.

## Steps

1. Store the condition's worded reason (and its reason code) on the fact at both sites.
2. Answer it as `details.message`, falling back to today's constant only for a fact with no stored cause (older rows).
3. Put the cause and remedy under the email's lead, and show it on the dashboard's `image_pull_failed` row.
