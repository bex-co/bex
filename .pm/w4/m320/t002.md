## Objective

An image change on a service with a serving release rolls one ReplicaSet. If discovery must change the pod template, the serving release keeps its pods and its traffic until promotion. It is never drained by a template change.

## Context

- After detection, `selectWebImageHTTP` returns "reproject probes and routing on the chosen port first" (`web_image_http.go:164`).
- The provisional HTTP probe (`deployment_projection.go:351-358`) then gives way to the final probe, and `MinReadySeconds` falls from 150 to 0 (`:627-628`). The new ReplicaSet becomes available at once, and the Deployment drains the serving release.
- Live, Run A: a second 1.26.3 instance started at 18:25:30, and the serving 1.27.5 stopped answering at 18:25:36, 37 s before the deploy was live. The deploy took 108 s, against 46 s for a same-image redeploy.
- w2/078 avoided the second roll only for a first deploy (comment at `deployment_projection.go:353-357`).

## Steps

1. Choose one of these:
   - (a) keep the candidate's template unchanged after detection: the provisional probe stays, or the final probe is already in place;
   - (b) keep the hold and the t001 pin through the re-roll until promotion.
2. Record the choice and the reason in the code comment beside w2/078's.
3. Envtest: one image change produces one new ReplicaSet (a), or the serving pods stay until promotion (b).
