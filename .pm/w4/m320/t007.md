## Objective

Check that the change is consistent on every surface it exposes, and against render.com.

## Context

- Render's sequence (render.com/docs/deploys, zero-downtime deploys): the original instance receives all traffic while the new one starts. Once the new one is healthy, all traffic moves to it, and the original is sent SIGTERM after 60 s.
- Surfaces:
  - REST `GET /v1/services/{id}/deploys` and `/deploys/{id}`, and `POST …/cancel`;
  - GraphQL deploys;
  - MCP `list_deploys`/`get_deploy`/`cancel_deploy`;
  - the dashboard deploy list, header, detail page and cancel dialog (`services.eventsCancelConfirmBody`);
  - Events `deploy_started`/`deploy_ended`.

## Steps

1. Confirm each surface reports `live` and `canceled` at the new moments, with the same fields and error shapes.
2. Update ADR004 and the ADR018 deploy rows to say traffic moves at promotion. Add a w4/405 note that the accepted gap is closed.
3. File any drift as follow-up work.

- `docs/ADR004-app-deployment.md`
- `docs/ADR018-render-parity.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every surface agrees. The ADRs state that traffic moves at promotion, and that a cancel before promotion means the candidate never answered.
