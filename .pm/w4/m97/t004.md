## Objective

Finish routing the content-save and cleanup paths through the corrected metadata contract so they cannot restore stale snapshots.

## Context

- `patchEnvironmentAuthorized` captures metadata before claiming a content revision and calls `touch` after writes.
- `abortGroupPatch` restores `oldMeta`; `pruneStaleLinks` mutates the pre-rollout link set after releasing the content claim.

## Steps

1. Change content timestamp updates to preserve current names, scope, and links instead of re-persisting the fetched metadata map.
2. Limit compensation to fields/state owned by the failed operation and enforce conditional restoration.
3. Prune only the ids actually discovered stale from the latest link set, keeping current metadata and newly added links.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/envgroups/patch.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/envgroups/service.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A rename or link committed during a paused content save remains after successful save and after compensation.
- [ ] Stale-link cleanup preserves a concurrently added valid link, current name, and current Environment.
- [ ] A real rollout failure remains visible and retryable, while since-deleted services retain w6/m108's tolerant behavior.

## Out of scope

- Changing per-service rollout retry policy.
- Recovering a process that terminates while a content operation is busy (m98).
