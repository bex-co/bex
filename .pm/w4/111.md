## `w4/m118` — cron facts (pass 114)

m118 (`7bbe64bf5`, 2026-09-21 00:07) **removed** three audit-verb mappings and added the two fact types to `allFactTypes`:

```
-	"apps.TriggerCronRun":       TypeCronJobRunStarted,
-	"apps.CancelCronRun":        TypeCronJobRunEnded,
-	"apps.CancelCurrentCronRun": TypeCronJobRunEnded,
```

Live on `qa-20260921-cron` (a `*/10 * * * *` busybox cron, since deleted), three runs all `successful`:

| run             | started  | finished | logged                          | event fact |
| --------------- | -------- | -------- | ------------------------------- | ---------- |
| manual trigger  | 09:16:06 | 09:16:12 | `qa-cron-ok` + date at 09:16:09 | `cron_job_run_started` @ **09:16:06** |
| **scheduled**   | 09:20:00 | 09:20:04 | `qa-cron-ok` + date at 09:20:01 | **none**   |
| manual trigger  | 09:22:11 | 09:22:16 | probe output at 09:22:13        | none       |

`serviceEvents(type:"cron_job_run_ended")` → `[]` at 09:21:20, five minutes after two runs had finished.

**The timestamp is what settles it.** The single `cron_job_run_started` fires at 09:16:06 — the exact instant of the `runCronJob` call, not of the run's execution — and the scheduled run that demonstrably executed (it logged at 09:20:01) produced nothing. That is the pre-m118 `apps.TriggerCronRun → cron_job_run_started` mapping still in force, and it is precisely the defect m118 describes: _"the feed showed a manual trigger and nothing else: a schedule that started and failed on its own was invisible in Activity."_

The trap worth recording: the type **name** `cron_job_run_started` is identical before and after m118, so seeing one in the feed does **not** prove the fix is live. Only the timestamp (trigger-time vs run-time) and the scheduled run's absence distinguish them.
