# 617 — The Chinese dashboard names the Pre-Deploy Command two ways: "预部署命令" on the Settings row, and "部署前命令" in its own paid-plan notice and the five build-minutes messages

Why: a Chinese reader sees the field labelled "预部署命令", and directly under it "付费套餐才可使用部署前命令。". The Usage page's billing copy uses the second term too. Two names for one feature read as two features, and the notice no longer visibly refers to the field above it.

- **Source / severity / estimate:** live `/qa-find-bugs w4` loop, round 231, 2026-11-12 21:06 UTC (`muse.env`, workspace `bex-canary`; pin `33e1b1e6117c`). **Minor** (i18n wording). ~15m.
- **Probe:** `https://dashboard.bex.co/services/srv-dbr2f8q585js73afgk00/settings?lang=zh` (a Free web service; deleted afterwards). The Deploy card reads, in order: "预部署命令 / 可选 / 在新镜像开始接收流量前运行一次（例如数据库迁移）。… / 付费套餐才可使用部署前命令。".
- **Root cause:** the zh catalogs translate "pre-deploy command" two ways.
  - `grep -c "预部署" dashboard/src/features/*/locales/zh.ts` counts 25 uses: deploys 10, metrics 2, services 11, webhooks 2. Among them are the row label (`services/locales/zh.ts:2759`), its edit button (`:2777`) and its toast (`:2782`).
  - `grep -rn "部署前" dashboard/src --include=zh.ts` gives 7 lines. Six of them render the English "pre-deploy command(s)":
    - `services/locales/zh.ts:2768` (`services.preDeployPaidOnly`, en "Pre-deploy commands are available on paid plans.");
    - `usage/locales/zh.ts:66`, `:76`, `:82`, `:123`, `:152` (en `usage/locales/en.ts:67`, `:77`, `:83`, `:124`, `:153`, "… builds and pre-deploy commands …").
  - The seventh, `blueprints/locales/zh.ts:117` ("可在部署前预览蓝图文件", "before deploying"), means something else and stays.
- **Fix (target behavior):** "预部署命令" everywhere the English says "pre-deploy command". That is the majority term and the field's own label.
  - `services.preDeployPaidOnly` becomes "付费套餐才可使用预部署命令。".
  - The five usage strings read "构建和预部署命令…".
  - No en change.
- **Tests:** if the zh catalog test has a term check (as w4/573 added for 您/你), add "部署前命令" as a forbidden form. Otherwise, add an assertion in `services` and `usage` locale tests that no zh message contains "部署前命令".
