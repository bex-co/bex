## Objective

Make the dashboard handle the new refusals the way it already handles delete and suspend: parse the named protected-environment error, show the server-issued phrase, and retry with it.

## Context

- The pattern exists and is documented in ADR032's "Dashboard operations catch-up (w5/m31)": _"Delete, suspend, and the reachable direct-deploy override path (Blueprint Sync) parse only the backend's named protected-environment error, display its exact `apps.ProtectedConfirmation` phrase, and retry with `confirm`. The dashboard does not precompute the phrase."_
- That last clause is the constraint: the phrase comes from the server's error, never rebuilt client-side, so a verb-word change in t001 or t002 cannot desynchronize the UI.
- The affected controls are on the service settings page — the inline editors for source/image, environment variables, and whichever settings t001 included.

## Steps

1. Route the newly guarded mutations through the existing protected-error parse-and-retry path.
2. Confirm the dialog text uses the phrase from the error verbatim.
3. Check the unprotected case still saves in one step with no dialog.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/features/services/`
- `dashboard/src/features/env-groups/` (if env vars are in t001's set)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Editing the image of a protected service shows the confirmation dialog with the server's phrase and succeeds on confirm.
- [ ] The phrase is never constructed client-side.
- [ ] An unprotected service saves without a dialog.

## Out of scope

- Any new confirmation component — reuse the existing one.
