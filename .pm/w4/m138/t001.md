## Objective

A sync started from the dashboard appears in Sync History as soon as it is recorded, and runs started elsewhere (auto-sync on push, CLI, MCP) appear within one poll interval while the page is open.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/blueprints/hooks/use-blueprint-syncs.ts:20-24`: no `pollInterval`.
- `use-sync-blueprint.ts:43`: mutation with no refetch or cache update. Route `blueprints.$blueprintId.tsx:198` drops `refetch`.
- Siblings poll with `RESOURCE_POLL_INTERVAL_MS` (`use-blueprint.ts:25`). Use the same visible-tab cadence, and keep `w1/m153`'s rule that background polls must not close dialogs or drop typing.
- Keep `w6/043`'s loading-state gating intact.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After a successful dashboard sync, the new run is the top Sync History row without a reload.
- [ ] While the page is open, a run recorded by another client appears within one poll interval.
- [ ] An open Sync dialog is not closed or reset by the poll.
