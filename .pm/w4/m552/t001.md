## Objective

Settle what Render's log line detail view is before building it.

## Context

- Render changelog 2025-12-17, "Improved logs UX in the Render dashboard and CLI": "Click individual log lines to open a detail view."
- bex's log explorers share one line list since w3/m509; w3/m528 read Render's `Logs-*.js` chunk for its buttons and display options, and neither records a detail view. The capture lives in [project-observability.md](../../../docs/render-artifacts/project-observability.md).

## Steps

1. Find the detail view in Render's `Logs-*.js` (and the chunks it imports).
2. Record: what opens it (click; Enter on the keyboard row cursor) and how a click that ends a text selection is told apart; its layout (side panel, sheet, inline) at desktop and narrow widths; every field it shows (message, each timestamp form, instance, level, type, and the request fields: method, status, host, path, response time, bytes, client IP, user agent, request id), and their labels and order; its actions (copy, view in context, filter by a label, SSH); how it closes (Esc, ×, clicking another line); whether it updates while Live tail runs.
3. Add a "Line detail view" section to `docs/render-artifacts/project-observability.md`.
