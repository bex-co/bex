## Objective

With IXON set, VSTOP (Ctrl-S) stops output from the replica to the master, and VSTART (Ctrl-Q) resumes it, as Linux's `stop_tty()` and `start_tty()` do. Both characters are consumed. Today they reach the reader, and since w4/m545 they are at least not echoed.

## Context

- Linux `drivers/tty/n_tty.c`:
  - `n_tty_receive_char_flow_ctrl()` consumes START and STOP under IXON, before the ISIG checks, and calls `start_tty()` / `stop_tty()`.
  - `n_tty_receive_signal_char()` calls `start_tty()` under IXON.
  - With IXON and IXANY, any input character restarts output (`n_tty_receive_char_special()`), unless output was stopped by `tcflow(TCOOFF)`.
- Linux `drivers/tty/pty.c`:
  - `pty_write()` writes nothing while `tty->flow.stopped`, so a blocking `write()` on the replica waits, and the master reads nothing new.
  - `pty_stop()` and `pty_start()` report `TIOCPKT_STOP` and `TIOCPKT_START` to a packet-mode master.
- Check whether gVisor implements `TCXONC` (`tcflow`) in `pkg/sentry/fsimpl/devpts/` and `replica.go`. If it does not, leave it as a known difference rather than widening this task.
- gVisor (fork `bex/echo-signal-chars`): `outputQueueWrite` (~:415) and `outputQueueTransformer` (~:476) are where a stopped flag holds output. Readiness: `replicaReadiness` (~:305) must not report writable while output is stopped.

## Steps

1. Add the stopped state, the START and STOP handling, the signal-character restart and IXANY, all guarded by t001's disabled-character helper.
2. Add syscall tests in `test/syscalls/linux/pty.cc`:
   - After `\x13`, a replica write is held: the master reads nothing within a timeout.
   - After `\x11`, the master reads it.
   - Neither character reaches the replica's reader.
   - `-ixon` passes both through.
   - IXANY restarts on any character.
   - Packet mode reports `TIOCPKT_STOP` and `TIOCPKT_START`.
3. Run them natively, on runsc, and on runsc with the fix reverted.
