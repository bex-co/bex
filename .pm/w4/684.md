# 684 — Workspace Settings' Audit Log section grows ~1190 px when its first page loads (~1320 px at 390): the loading state draws two bars and hides the export controls, so Danger Zone jumps ~1300 px

Why: the dashboard rule ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), "Dashboard preloading skeletons must match their post-loading contents") asks a pending state to keep the ready panel's regions at stable heights. The export controls are the same before and after the events arrive. In any active admin workspace the ready table is the full 20-event first page: `bex-canary` wrote 1000 audit rows in the 29 h before this probe.

- **Source / severity / estimate:** live `/qa-find-bugs w4` round 492, 2026-11-16 07:00–07:06 UTC, pin `3c6654df60a2`, workspace `bex-canary` (Scale, admin). **Minor:** a layout shift, and nothing is lost. But a "Danger Zone" rail click made during the load lands on the audit table instead. Same class as [w4/680](done/680.md). ~45m.
- **Probe:** start from `/`, hold every `/graphql` request 4 s with a Playwright route, then click Settings in the sidebar. Read each `#<section>` box at 1.8 s, and again 6 s after the hold is released.
  - The workspace context is already loaded on this path, so the page renders its sections and each card draws its own loading state. The route's `WorkspaceSettingsPageSkeleton` and the page's inline `!currentWorkspace && loading` skeleton do not mount.
  - At 1440:
    - `#audit-logs` is 154 → 1344 px. Its header is 40 px. The 56 px content (two bars) becomes a 78 px export row (Start date, End date, Export as CSV, the 90-day hint) plus 1144 px of table (header, 20 rows, Load more).
    - `#danger-zone` moves from top 5423 to 6724.
  - At 390:
    - `#audit-logs` is 194 → 1514 px (the export row wraps to 208 px).
    - `#danger-zone` moves from top 7156 to 8591.
  - Without the hold, the 154 px card appears 0.55–0.95 s after the click and jumps to 1344 px at 0.97–1.37 s (two runs).
  - Clicking the rail's "Danger Zone" during the hold scrolls there (`scrollTop` 4981). Once the log loads, Danger Zone's top is at 1743 px in a 900 px viewport, and the view shows Registry Credentials and the Audit Log table.
- **Cause:**
  - `AuditLogPanel` hides `AuditLogExport` while `loading` (`dashboard/src/features/audit/components/audit-log-panel.tsx:72-73`).
  - In its place it draws `PanelTableSkeleton` (`:97-98`): two `h-6` bars with no header row (`common/components/panel-states.tsx:34-45`).
  - The first page is `PAGE_SIZE = 20` (`features/audit/hooks/use-audit-log.ts:12`; `loading` at `:147`).
  - `AuditLogPanelSkeleton` (`features/audit/components/audit-log-panel-skeleton.tsx`, the route and inline skeleton) does draw the export row, but over the same two bars.
- **Fix:**
  - While the first page loads, draw the frame of the state an admin on the audit-log plan (Pro, w8/m138) lands in:
    - the export row at its ready geometry;
    - the five column headers;
    - `PAGE_SIZE` row placeholders at `AuditEventRow`'s height;
    - the Load more button's slot.
  - When the workspace is known to be below that plan, draw the plan notice's frame instead. The plan comes from the workspace read, as [w4/432](done/432.md) did for Webhooks. Today `planRequired` is known only after the audit read fails (`use-audit-log.ts:140`).
  - Share the frame with `AuditLogPanelSkeleton` so the route and inline skeletons match too.
  - Add a test like w4/680's `notifications-page-skeleton.test.tsx`.
- **Blast radius:**
  - `PanelTableSkeleton` has 9 non-test users: API keys, the audit panel and its skeleton, connected agents, the registry credentials panel and its skeleton, active sessions, SSH keys, webhook deliveries and the webhooks panel.
  - This note changes only the audit pair, and the shared helper stays as it is.
