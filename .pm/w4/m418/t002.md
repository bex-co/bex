## Objective

Make `PatchEnvironment` (`saveMode: deploy`) on an image-backed service rerun the image it is running. REST, GraphQL and MCP share this path.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/secrets/batch.go`: `servedArtifact` (`:195`), the precheck (`:141`), `selectServedBuild` (`:463`), `deferServedBuildDeploy` (`:506`), `DeployServedBuild`.
- Decision (Render's Wait, w4/667; Override, w4/653): an image service with a live release defers behind a deploy in flight under Wait, and the selection is made when the waiting deploy runs. Under Override it deploys at once.
- With no live release, an image service keeps deploying `Spec.Image`. It must never get the 409 `ENVIRONMENT_DEPLOY_NEEDS_LIVE_RELEASE`.

## Steps

1. Split "has a live release" (precheck, deferral) from "which image to select" (t001's helper) for image services.
2. Update `servedArtifact`'s comment: "Save, rebuild, and deploy" on an image service still pulls the saved image.
3. Add tests: an override is served and the save reruns its digest; a saved tag is served and nothing is selected; no history deploys the saved image with no 409; Wait defers and Override deploys at once, each with an image service.
