## Objective

From one UTC instant (the cutover), the capacity kind is what a paid Postgres pays for its storage. `storage_gb_seconds` becomes a reading shown at $0 for both datastore kinds and is never sent to Stripe. Before the cutover nothing changes: those hours were exported on the used-bytes basis and are not re-priced.

## Context

- **Rates:** `Sheet.rateFor` (`lego/backend/internal/pricing/pricing.go:829`), `unitFor` (`:750`, GB-mo), the meter-name list (`:340`), the `storage` comment (`:79`) and `pricing.yaml:150`. The rate is unchanged at $0.21/GB-month, and Free stays $0.
- **Stripe:** `stripeMeterEvent` (`lego/backend/internal/billing/stripe.go:574`). Send the capacity kind on the existing `storage_gb_hours` meter, re-based to GB-hours. That needs no new Price or Subscription item, and a Subscription is capped at 20 items. From the cutover, skip `storage_gb_seconds`. Update the comment in `scripts/stripe-billing-setup.py:24` to match; it needs no new meter.
- **Cutover precedent:** `sandboxUsage.billedFrom` in `pricing.yaml:231`, a UTC hour boundary. `UsageMonthToDate` and `CompactUsage` (`store/usage.go:756`, `:823`) classify rows of `sandboxUsageKinds` by `window_start` against it. Put the cutover in `pricing.yaml` as a UTC instant after the deploy that carries it.
  - A UTC month start keeps every month on one basis, which makes compaction simple.
  - Before it, capacity rows are shown at $0. From it, used rows are.
- **Compaction must keep both readings.** `CompactUsage` drops `unbilled` rows today, so the Billing page's month picker (six months, three kept hot) would lose the used reading of older months. Fold the shown storage rows into `usage_monthly`, and classify them again on the monthly read (by month against the cutover). Keep them out of every billed total.
- **Key Value:**
  - `MonthlyEstimate` adds a storage line to a Key Value (`pricing/monthly.go:221`). Drop it.
  - `StorageMonthlyUSDPerGB(tier, key_value)` answers 0.00.
  - The `MonthlyResource.StorageGB` comment (`:136`) changes.
- **Postgres estimate:** its storage line was already priced on provisioned GB. Now it equals the invoice, which ADR030 must say (t004).

## Steps

1. Add the cutover to the pricing sheet and to the PGStore classification. Store reads that price, export, settle or compact must agree.
2. Rate the capacity kind for paid Postgres and 0 for Free. From the cutover, rate `storage_gb_seconds` 0 as a shown line for both kinds.
3. Map Stripe export: capacity goes to `storage_gb_hours`, and used rows from the cutover are skipped. Drop the Key Value storage line from the Blueprint estimate.
4. Tests:
   - an hour on each side of the cutover prices and exports on its own basis, with no hour billed on both kinds;
   - a Free Postgres sends nothing;
   - a Key Value sends nothing from the cutover;
   - compaction keeps both readings and the billed total.
