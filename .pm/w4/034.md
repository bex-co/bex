# 034 — Fresh authorization for established live-log tails

Re-owned from `.pm/w1/046.md` F18 on 2026-08-17 after `/pm-brainstorm for w4`. Keep this as a sub-hour inbox task, not a milestone.

Current main already has the other half of the long-lived-log hardening: `logs.Service.acquireSubscription` enforces global, per-subject, and per-workspace caps for the shared SSE/WebSocket/NDJSON route (`BEX_MAX_SSE_CONNS*`). Do not rebuild those caps.

The remaining gap is authorization lifetime. `FollowLogs` calls `AuthorizeApp(can_view_logs)` once, then a successful SSE/WebSocket/NDJSON tail can outlive membership or key revocation indefinitely. Add a one-minute-default watchdog (new documented log-stream revalidation knob; negative may preserve the old admission-only mode) that calls `AuthorizeAppFresh(core.RelCanViewLogs, app)` so OpenFGA's positive cache cannot mask revocation. Reuse a package-neutral watchdog pattern or add the small loop locally — `logs` must not import `internal/sshgateway` merely for its helper. Any deny, checker failure, resource disappearance, or caller cancellation terminates the stream and releases its cap slots; an allowed check must not interrupt the tail.

Touch `lego/backend/internal/logs/{service.go,rest.go,logs_test.go}`, `lego/backend/cmd/api/main.go`, `.env.example`, and `CLAUDE.md`. Pin both SSE and WebSocket behavior (NDJSON shares the SSE path): a stale cached allow plus fresh deny ends within the configured interval, checker failure fails closed, healthy rechecks preserve delivery, and reconnect still performs normal admission authorization.
