## Objective

Services without a Health Check Path are TCP-checked (startup, readiness and liveness on the port) and still carry the plain `sleep` preStop. Give them the same "drain only while serving" behavior, or record why not, and bring ADR004 in line.

## Steps

1. Choose one and record it in ADR004 with the trade-off:
   - **(a)** Mount the probe binary for every drained pod, adding the init container and volume. Every web and private service's template changes on its next roll (lazy adoption), and `health-probe drain --tcp` checks the port.
   - **(b)** Keep `sleep` for TCP-checked pods, and record the 2-minute restart as a divergence in ADR018 with the user's sign-off. Under the parity rule, (b) needs that sign-off; default to (a).
2. Amend ADR004:
   - line 96: "self-heals in about a minute" holds again after this milestone;
   - fix its stale "15s … where bex takes 30s" divergence, since w8/189 made it 15 s;
   - § Rollout headroom: the drain applies to an instance that is still serving.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A TCP-checked web service whose listener stops is restarted within the liveness window plus 5 s (envtest, or the kind cluster).
- [ ] ADR004's health-check and drain paragraphs describe the shipped behavior.
