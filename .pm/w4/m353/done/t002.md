## Objective

Services without a Health Check Path are TCP-checked (startup, readiness and liveness on the port) and still carry the plain `sleep` preStop. Give them the same "drain only while serving" behavior, or record why not, and bring ADR004 in line.

## Steps

1. Choose one and record it in ADR004 with the trade-off:
   - **(a)** Mount the probe binary for every drained pod, adding the init container and volume. Every web and private service's template changes on its next roll (lazy adoption), and `health-probe drain --tcp` checks the port.
   - **(b)** Keep `sleep` for TCP-checked pods, and record the 2-minute restart as a divergence in ADR018 with the user's sign-off. Under the parity rule, (b) needs that sign-off; default to (a).
2. Amend ADR004:
   - line 96: "self-heals in about a minute" holds again after this milestone;
   - fix its stale "15s … where bex takes 30s" divergence, since w8/189 made it 15 s;
   - § Rollout headroom: the drain applies to an instance that is still serving.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A TCP-checked web service whose listener stops is restarted within the liveness window plus 5 s (envtest, or the kind cluster).
- [ ] ADR004's health-check and drain paragraphs describe the shipped behavior.

## Decision and outcome (2026-11-14)

**Decision: (a).** Every drained pod (web and private services, without a disk) carries the helper. TCP-checked services gain the `bex-health-probe-install` init container and the `/bex-health` volume only for their drain. Their probes stay the kubelet's TCP check, and the drain runs `health-probe drain --seconds 60 tcp --port N`. Option (b) would have kept a two-minute restart without the user's sign-off, which the parity rule forbids. The template change rides each service's next roll (lazy adoption), never the operator upgrade.

ADR004 is amended in three places:

- the health-check paragraph: "self-heals in about a minute" holds again, with the round 229 explanation;
- the stale "15s … where bex takes 30s" divergence is rewritten to the shipped 15 s (w8/189);
- § Rollout headroom: the drain applies only to an instance still serving, with the mechanism, option (a), the rejected probe-level grace period, the cost and the kind measurements.

ADR018's health-check row gains a "Restart timing (w4/m353)" note.

Kind check: a busybox `httpd` killed while the shell kept PID 1 alive, under a TCP liveness probe and the checked drain, was restarted 61 s after the kill (kill decision and container exit both at 12:36:53).
