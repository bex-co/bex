## Objective

Make `GET`, `PATCH` and `DELETE /v1/webhooks/{id}`, `GET /v1/webhooks/{id}/events` and the resend route (plus their GraphQL/MCP twins) load the endpoint by id and authorize on its own workspace when `ownerId` is absent.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/webhooks/rest.go:200-254`; the service verbs in `internal/webhooks/service.go`.
- Pattern: `internal/envgroups/service.go:1667-1679` (`fetchGroup`).
- Live: `whk-db1h3s68inbs73f0v75g`. Without the owner, GET and DELETE returned 404; with it, 200 and 204.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every webhook by-id verb works without `ownerId` for an endpoint in a non-default workspace the caller belongs to. A non-member id returns 404.

## Outcome (2026-10-04)

Done. `webhooks.Service.scopeEndpoint` routes `Get`, `Update`, `SetEnabled`, `Delete`, `ListDeliveriesFiltered` and `Resend` through the shared `core.Base.ScopeByID` (new, `internal/core/byid.go`). It uses `store.WebhookEndpointWorkspace` (an unscoped `tenant_id` routing read). REST, GraphQL and MCP all call these verbs. Tests:
- `TestByIDVerbsResolveTheEndpointsOwnWorkspace`: no-owner get/update/enable/deliveries/delete in tea-b; non-member, missing and mismatched-owner calls get the same 404.
- `TestWebhookEndpointWorkspaceRoutesByID` (real PG 17).
- `TestScopeByID` (core contract, including fail-closed on a membership outage).

The old cross-workspace test's fake resolver claimed membership in every workspace. It now answers honestly.

**Correction (2026-10-05, w4/199):** the non-member rule above was wrong.
