## Objective

Every persistent Key Value instance that existed before t003 ends up on an encrypted volume with every key it held.

## Context

- The StatefulSet's claim template is immutable, so the move recreates the StatefulSet with a new claim and restores the data onto it.
- The controller already hands persistence over between pods (`persistenceToken`, `persistenceLegacyPod`, `keyvalue_controller.go:755`) and a backup CronJob exists ([ADR021](../../../docs/ADR021-keyvalue-management.md), [ADR050](../../../docs/ADR050-encrypted-platform-backups.md) Tier A). Reuse one of them rather than adding a third copy path.

## Steps

1. Implement the move: snapshot the data, recreate the StatefulSet on the encrypted claim, restore, and serve again under the same Service, credentials and connection strings.
2. Start it per instance, write the runbook, and move bex's own fixture instances first.
3. Measure and record the downtime of one move.

## Files

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/keyvalue_controller.go` (+ tests), `docs/runbooks/<new>.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] envtest: the move ends on the encrypted class, keeps the Service and credentials, and is safe to resume after an interruption.
- [ ] On dev-4 an instance with keys moves and still returns them.
- [ ] On production no KeyValue PVC remains on `hcloud-volumes`.

## Out of scope
