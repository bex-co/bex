## Objective

The App's Ingress serves `*.<domain>`, which Kubernetes Ingress supports natively. Its certificate is issued by DNS-01: cert-manager follows the tenant's `_acme-challenge` CNAME into a bex-controlled zone, the same pattern ADR093 uses for `*.onbex.co`. HTTP-01 stays the issuer for concrete hosts.

## Context

- Precedent: the ADR093 cert-manager DNS-01 issuer at `deploy/gitops/charts/onbex-default-tls/`.
- Certificates load per Secret (w8/m118): a pending wildcard Secret must not block other hosts' certificates.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An envtest or fixture shows the wildcard Ingress host and the DNS-01 Certificate.
- [ ] A concrete host under the wildcard keeps its own rule and certificate.
