# Direct egress health is gated fleet-wide: one missed egress-meter scrape on any node skips every app's direct egress for the hour

Why: `egressquery.Health` multiplies `min(min_over_time(up{job="bex-egress-meter"}[1h]))` and `min(count_over_time(…)) >= 0.8×expected` across **every** egress-meter target. One missed scrape on any node, or a node that joins or leaves mid-hour (autoscaling), fails the product, so `queryEgressSourcesEvidence` skips the `direct` source for every app in the cluster that hour. That hour's direct egress is then billed as zero (an undercount) and recorded `degraded`, which pins `usage.coverage.through` to the month start for the rest of the month.

- **Source / severity / estimate:** found by w4/m139 t002's production read, loopx w4, 2026-10-31 (read-only `bex-db` replica + `prometheus-server`); **major** (real unbilled egress plus a permanent "Partial data" badge), ~2h. ADR023 § Observability reads vs billing reads, ADR040.
- **Evidence (2026-10-31):**
  - `bex-canary` (`tea-daif693dqjvc73e7as3g`): every `egress_bytes/direct` stream is `degraded` in 40–55% of October's hours. For example, `srv-daif6dsmg29s73d1umvg` has 394 degraded and 346 healthy hours, with the first degraded hour at 2026-10-01T00Z (`scripts/usage-coverage-diagnose.sql`, plus a per-stream state count over `usage_source_health`).
  - Prometheus, 7 days to 2026-10-31T20Z: 21 of 168 hours have `min(min_over_time(up{job="bex-egress-meter"}[1h])) < 1`. Down scrapes per instance were 7–25; three nodes (`10.10.0.8`, `.11`, `.18`) hit the 10 s scrape timeout. `bex_egress_meter_healthy == 0` samples were 179 on `10.10.0.4` and ≤23 elsewhere. `bex_egress_meter_last_counter_loss_time_seconds` stayed 0 everywhere, so no counter was lost.
  - Example window 2026-10-31 18:00–19:00Z: `min(up)` = 0 from one missed scrape, while `bex_egress_meter_healthy` was 1 and `resets` was 0. The app's own counter was sound, but its hour was still skipped.
- **Expected:** a source is judged on the evidence its own series depend on. A missed scrape or meter restart on a node that never ran the app's pods during the window does not skip that app's hour. A node joining or leaving inside the window counts only for the part of the window it existed.
- **Fix direction (decide in the fix; keep "undercount, never invent"):**
  1. Scope the `up` / `bex_egress_meter_healthy` / `count_over_time` terms to the instances that exported the app's own `bex_app_direct_egress_bytes_total` series during the window (for example, join on `instance` with `group_left`). Require full scrape coverage only from those instances.
  2. Judge `count_over_time` against each target's own lifetime in the window, not the full hour, so autoscaler churn stops failing the hour.
  3. Separately, look at the 10 s scrape timeouts on three nodes and the 179 unhealthy samples on `10.10.0.4`.
- **Tests:** `egressquery` query-shape tests plus a fake-Prometheus case: one node misses a scrape while the app's series lives on another node, and the app's hour stays healthy and billed.
- **Not covered here:** the other m139 t002 cause (hibernated Free services' `instance` streams marked degraded) was fixed in the same loopx run. Already-recorded degraded hours are not re-metered; October's coverage stays partial.
- **Dedupe:** w1/m51 introduced the per-source gating, and w1/034 the skip instead of defer. No open item covers fleet-wide gating. No DO_NOT_DO item applies.
