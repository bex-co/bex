## Objective

`core.Base.AuthorizeApp`'s cross-tenant name-collision fallback loop can, since w4/m20/t001 widened denied-read recording, serialize one synchronous `audit_events` write (bounded by `auditRecordTimeout`, 2s) per rejected candidate before a GET 403s. Pick one of the two shapes filed in the note — (1) detach the fallback loop's audit writes from the response path (bounded background goroutine, the `TouchAPIKey` fire-and-forget shape; loosens the "recorded before the caller sees the outcome" guarantee for this loop only) or (2) cap per-request individually-audited candidates and record one aggregate denial beyond the cap — implement it, and document the chosen semantics.
