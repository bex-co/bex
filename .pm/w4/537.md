# 537 — The service Environment tab names every group row's button just "Link" or "Unlink"

Why: a screen-reader or voice-control user hears "Link, button" once per available group, with no group named, so linking the wrong group is one misstep away. The group page's own Unlink already names its target.

- **Source / severity / estimate:** live `/qa-find-bugs w4` loop, round 3, 2026-11-10 08:11 UTC (`muse.env`, workspace `bex-canary`, pin `4630a48a765e`). Fixture: the service `qa-20261110-env` from [w4/536](536.md) (deleted). **Minor** (accessibility). ~20m.
- **Probe:** on `/services/srv-dbpd242j0hgs73eavmsg/env`, `page.locator('main').ariaSnapshot()` listed `link "qa-20261025-b5p1-grp"`, `button "Link"`, `link "qa-20261110-grp"`, `button "Link"`. After linking, the linked row's control read `button "Unlink"`.
- **Root cause:**
  - `dashboard/src/features/services/components/env-groups-panel.tsx:594-612` renders one Button per group row. Its only name is `t("services.envGroupUnlink")` or `t("services.envGroupLink")` (`features/services/locales/en.ts:1103-1110`: "Link", "Unlink"), and it has no `aria-label`.
  - The group page's `features/env-groups/components/linked-services-card.tsx:159-165` already uses `aria-label={t("envGroups.unlinkServiceAria", {…})}` (w4/150, `177a3b0d4`).
- **Fix:**
  - Add `aria-label` "Link {group}" / "Unlink {group}", leaving the visible text unchanged.
  - Add en and zh keys, following the `envGroups.unlinkServiceAria` pattern.
  - The busy spinner state keeps the name.
- **Blast radius:** this button is the panel's only per-row control. The other Buttons (`:277`, `:319`, `:382`) are Create group and two Try again. One component, one call site per row.
- **Dedupe:**
  - w4/150 (done) covered the group page and the Team table, not `env-groups-panel.tsx`.
  - w4/510 and w4/511 are other pages.
  - No open item covers this panel.
- **DoD** (live): on a service Environment tab with two available groups, `ariaSnapshot` lists `button "Link qa-…-a"` and `button "Link qa-…-b"`. After linking one, it lists `button "Unlink qa-…-a"`. The zh locale names them too.
