## Steps

1. Read t001's urgency table through the existing settings query. Do not hard-code a client copy.
2. Render each event's urgency (localized Routine / Important / Critical) beside its label.
3. For each selected event ranked below the effective minimum (the global minimum, or the override's own if set), show an inline notice at that event, plus a summary near Save. Example: "Deploy succeeded is Routine and won't be pushed while Minimum urgency is Important."
