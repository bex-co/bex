## Objective

Make the shared-hook policy explicit and preserve the callers whose fixed-window pagination already works.

## Context

The exhaustive inventory in finding.md counts three useLiveRange call sites; two useLogHistory call sites; two useOlderLogPages call sites; one LogViewer mount; two datastore mounts; and three typed deploy query legs through one helper. A service-relative-clock policy must not silently change all of these.

## Steps

1. Verify the full inventory and seven resource-family dispositions. Exercise web plus cron/worker/private aliases as applicable; static has no runtime Logs but shares deploy history. Postgres and Key Value use the datastore viewer.
2. Keep same-window deploy polls from clearing older build/predeploy/app pages. A new resource/deploy/window must clear them. Preserve the passing long-build-first-line control.
3. Verify both datastore mounts keep whole-window paging, honest capped/complete notices and their supported filters. Their memoized resolved range does not use the service's automatic clock; do not add unrelated freshness behavior.
4. Preserve Metrics and Scaling relative clocks and absolute custom windows. Keep URL type/range aliases, input alias application, and canonical service/datastore/deploy route behavior.
5. Trace pending, complete-empty, partial/time-budgeted, no-store, forbidden, not-found, session-expired, query-timeout and transport-error states. Preserve authorization and stale-response isolation rather than turning errors into empty successful lists.
6. Verify same-reader rollover and simultaneous SSE arrival/paging, including a slow older-page result crossing a tick. Retain useful rows and a valid cursor without duplicates or indefinite out-of-window retention.
