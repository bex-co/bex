## Objective

Prove that interrupted operations recover safely, stale writers cannot commit, and healthy groups remain available.

## Context

- Crash recovery must be tested at persisted operation boundaries and across multiple Service instances.
- A green unit suite alone does not demonstrate that real OpenBao and Kubernetes reject stale writes.

## Steps

1. Inject termination/failure after claim and each source/projection/phase boundary; recover from another instance and assert the exact coherent result.
2. Pause an old writer across reclamation and prove its late writes fail; cover active-owner protection, acknowledged saves, generated values, clone source protection, and list isolation.
3. Run backend race/suite and required checks plus affected dashboard gates; execute the real dev-4 restart drill and record redacted evidence and cleanup.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A second instance recovers interrupted work within the documented bound and produces a coherent source/projection/revision state.
- [ ] Stale-owner, active-operation, clone, list-isolation, authorization, and no-secret-leak cases are covered.
- [ ] Required checks and the scoped live restart drill pass; limitations and cleanup are recorded.

## Out of scope

- Retries, sleeps, skipped assertions, or snapshot-only tests that conceal failures.
- Production fault injection without a separately authorized operational window.
