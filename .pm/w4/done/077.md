## Metrics: swept across five axes, all correct

Read-only on `beancount-forum` (`srv-d9nqg9dcavls73fp8m2g`) and `beancount-cms-v2` (`srv-d9bj8s3eg85c7390eb9g`).

- **Ranges genuinely shift.** 1h/60s → 61 points spanning exactly 1.0h; 6h/300s → 73 points spanning 6.0h; 24h/900s → 97 points spanning 24.0h. Each honours both the window and the requested resolution.
- **REST and GraphQL agree to the float.** Same service, same 1h window, same 60s resolution: both report first point `2026-09-14T05:36:37Z` value `0.035862181983371585`. REST spells the field `timestamp` and GraphQL `time` — the same per-surface shape difference the role enums and `cidrs`/`cidrBlock` show, not drift.
- **Empty states are honest.** A window before the service existed (2026-01-01→02) and one entirely in the future (2027-01-01→02) both return **zero series and zero points** — not a row of fabricated zeros.
- **Aggregation does what it says.** Without it, 7 per-instance series; `aggregateAllMethod:"AVG"` collapses to **1** series whose label field changes from `instance` to `aggregate`; `"SUM"` is refused with `bad request: aggregateAllMethod must be MIN, MAX, or AVG`.
- **`percentage:true` is gated, not mislabelled.** On `MEMORY` the unit becomes `percentage`; on `HTTP_REQUESTS`/`BANDWIDTH`/`HTTP_LATENCY` the flag is ignored and the units stay `count`/`bytes`/`seconds`. A silently-ignored parameter is a mild contract wart, but nothing is reported as a percentage that isn't one.

An incidental corroboration of `w4/m100`: one `MEMORY` query on a standard-plan service returns **16 per-instance series** in a single answer, and individual instance series carry far fewer points than the aggregate (20 vs 73 over the same 6h window) because pods come and go — so an unaggregated chart draws several fragmentary lines by construction.
