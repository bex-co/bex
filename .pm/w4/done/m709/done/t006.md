## Objective

Check that every surface reporting bandwidth agrees with the bill, and compare with Render.

## Steps

1. For a workspace with a window, compare REST `GET /v1/usage`, GraphQL `usage`, MCP `get_usage`, the Billing page and the Outbound Bandwidth footer. Each shows the same billed bandwidth, with the window's hours left out.
2. Render: no surface exposes an attack window or an attack line, and its bill simply carries none of the attack's bandwidth. Confirm bex adds no customer-facing line or field that Render lacks, or record any it adds in the ADR018 row.
3. File each difference as a note.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every surface agrees, and any difference from Render is filed or recorded.

## Record (2026-11-27)

- **Surfaces agree.** REST `GET /v1/usage`, GraphQL `usage` (which the Billing page reads, `dashboard/src/features/usage/api/usage.graphql`) and MCP `get_usage` all come from `usage.Service.monthToDateAt` over `store.UsageMonthToDate`, which leaves a live window's egress hours out. On dev-4 all three read 46 GiB without a window, 6 GiB with one and 46 GiB once it was withdrawn (t005). The Outbound Bandwidth footer takes the same hours off through `EgressAttackWindowsOverlapping` and the footer's own source (`TestMonthToDateBandwidthLeavesAttackHoursOut`). The service's bandwidth chart is unchanged.
- **Render.** No Render surface shows an attack window or an attack line ([faq](https://render.com/docs/faq), [ddos-protection](https://render.com/docs/ddos-protection), [outbound-bandwidth](https://render.com/docs/outbound-bandwidth), read 2026-11-27). bex adds no field or line to REST, GraphQL, MCP or the dashboard: the window's hours are left out, not shown as an `unbilled` line, and the verb is on the control-plane API only. The one trace a customer can meet is the credit's invoice line for hours Stripe had already rated, "Outbound bandwidth from a DDoS attack, not billed (_Month YYYY_)", which the ADR018 Custom domains DDoS row records. No difference to file.
