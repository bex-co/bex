## Objective

Show the rule working on a real stack, end to end, and that the operator verb is reachable where an operator will use it.

## Steps

1. dev-4 (`bash scripts/dev-env.sh 4 up`): create a disposable web service, drive a few MB through its public host, let the hourly rollup record the hour, declare a window over it, and read `GET /v1/usage`, `billing-export`, the Billing page and the service's footer: the hour's bytes are gone from the billed figure and the chart still shows the traffic. Withdraw the window and read them back. Delete the service.
2. Production, once the pin carries this milestone (record it as ``pin must carry `<sha>` `` if you park): fetch the app kubeconfig (`bash scripts/fetch-app-kubeconfig.sh <path outside the repo>`), port-forward bex-api's `:8091`, then declare, list and withdraw a window on `bex-canary` with `BEX_CP_TOKEN`. Confirm its usage read is unchanged afterwards.
3. Record the commands and the figures (no raw dumps) in this file.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] dev-4: inside the window the hour bills 0 and leaves every billed read; withdrawn, it bills again.
- [x] Production: the verb declares, lists and withdraws, and `bex-canary`'s bill reads as before.

## Evidence

### dev-4 (2026-11-27, done)

bex-api rebuilt from this tree with `bash scripts/dev-env.sh 4 up` (migration `0354` applied). A throwaway Kratos user's workspace (disposable) with one disposable image web service, `m709-flooded`. dev-4's Prometheus scrapes no Traefik (`count(traefik_router_responses_bytes_total)` is empty), so the egress meter has no source there: two closed hours were written into `usage_hourly` as the rollup writes them, the flood hour 40 GiB and the hour after it 6 GiB. Every read went through dev-4's real bex-api (REST, GraphQL, MCP with an API key) and the control-plane verb over HTTP on `:56040`:

| Step | REST `/v1/usage` egress | GraphQL `usage` (the Billing page's query) | MCP `get_usage` | `includedBandwidth` used/included (GB) | estimate | `billing-export` flood hour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| before any window | 46 GiB | 46 GiB | 46 GiB | 46 / 5 | $0.62 | pending, `attackHour` false |
| window declared 22:10–22:50 UTC over the flood hour, `serviceIds` the service | 6 GiB | 6 GiB | 6 GiB | 6 / 5 | $0.02 | pending, `attackHour` true (ships as 0), 40 GiB still shown |

Not shown live on dev-4, and covered by tests instead: the emitter's 0 (dev-4 runs without a Stripe key, and rows seal after 48 hours; `TestEmitterSendsAttackHoursAsZero`), and the Outbound Bandwidth footer (no Traefik series to read; `TestMonthToDateBandwidthLeavesAttackHoursOut`).
