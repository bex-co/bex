# At 390px an environment card's header crushes the environment's name, and its skeleton's header and filters do not match the ready card

Why: the project page's card jumps 16px at 1440 and 64px at 390 when it loads, and at 390 a protected environment's name reads one letter per line. Both break the dashboard's skeleton and layout rules (`dashboard/AGENTS.md`). Found by w4/m550 t005 on 2026-11-22, on dev-4 and on production pin `19fdb46f1758` alike.

- **Ready card at 390:** "staging" wraps to "stagi / ng" and its "3 resources" count breaks mid-word. "production" stacks one letter per line beside the Protected badge, Manage resources and the kebab (`dashboard/src/features/environments/components/environment-card.tsx`, the card header).
- **Skeleton:** `ProjectEnvironmentCardSkeleton` (`dashboard/src/common/components/route-skeletons.tsx`) draws a title row with no Manage resources button or kebab. Where the ready card has five filter pills (two rows at 390), it draws two. It also draws a `sm:w-36` box beside search that the ready card does not have.
- **Measured** (staging with three services, from the page top): the selection toolbar starts at y 381 pending and 397 ready at 1440, and 425 and 489 at 390. w4/m550 fixed only the toolbar row, which now wraps as the toolbar does (76px at 390 in both states).
- **End state:** at 390 the name and count keep whole words (truncated, or wrapped under the actions). The skeleton's header, filter rows and search row match the ready card at 1440 and 390, so the toolbar's y is the same pending and ready at both widths.

## Done (2026-11-23)

- **Header:** `EnvironmentCardHeader` (`dashboard/src/features/environments/components/environment-card-header.tsx`) is shared by the ready card and its skeleton. The title line is one 22px row: the name truncates (its full text in a tooltip), and the Protected badge and the count never shrink or wrap. Where the header is narrower than 28rem (390 and 360px phones, a 768px tablet beside the sidebar), Manage resources and the kebab drop to their own row under it. Wider, they sit at the right, centred on the title line.
- **Skeletons:** the card's skeleton draws that header, with Manage resources at its ready size and the kebab. `ProjectResourceFiltersSkeleton` draws the five type pills at their ready sizes, so they wrap onto the same rows, over one full-width search box.
