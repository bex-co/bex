# A disk can be sized up to 10 000 GB on bex, where Render's dashboard stops at 1000 GB and sends larger sizes to support

Why: Render parity — w4/m652's parity check (t005, 2026-11-26) read Render's size control and found a cap bex does not have, with no ADR018 sign-off for the difference.

- **Render:** both of its dashboard disk forms (the create form's Advanced card and the Disk page) use a size stepper from 1 to 1000 GB, and above that the field says "For sizes larger than 1 TB, please email support@render.com" (public bundle `index-D5V4ZgbE.js`, read 2026-11-26; [render-artifacts/new-service-wizard.md](../../../docs/render-artifacts/new-service-wizard.md#the-advanced-section-read-2026-11-25-w4m651-t001)). Render's docs state no maximum ([ADR082](../../../docs/ADR082-persistent-disks.md) § Size), and what its API answers for `sizeGB` above 1000 was not checked: that is a write on the reference account.
- **bex:** every surface accepts 1–10 000 GB, Hetzner's volume cap (`diskMaxSizeGB`, `lego/backend/internal/apps/disks.go`; `DISK_MAX_SIZE_GB`, `dashboard/src/features/services/lib/disk.ts`). ADR082 states the 10 000 GB cap; ADR018's Persistent disks row does not list it as a divergence.
- **End state:** either the dashboard's size fields stop at 1000 GB with Render's support message (and REST/GraphQL/MCP/Blueprint follow whatever Render's API answers above 1000 GB), or ADR018's Persistent disks row records the larger cap as a divergence with the user's sign-off.

**Done 2026-11-26 (`/loopx w4`).** Render's answer above 1000 GB was readable without a write: the Disk page's add form maps the server error `/disk size cannot exceed 1TB/i` to "Disk size cannot exceed 1TB (1000GB).", the size schema is `.max(1e3, …)` and renders "For sizes larger than 1 TB, please contact support" (a link to Render's support), and Render's Terraform provider checks `size_gb` with `int64validator.Between(1, 1000)` (`render-oss/terraform-provider-render` `internal/provider/types/resource/services.go:368` at `06af456d9bdb`). So every bex surface now stops at 1000 GB:

- **Dashboard:** the create page's Advanced card and the Disk tab's add and grow fields take at most 1000 GB, and above it say "For sizes larger than 1 TB, please contact support." (Render's words, plain text: bex has no support address to link). The create page holds Deploy, the add form refuses to send, and the grow button is disabled. A disk already above 1 TB cannot be grown from the page, as on Render.
- **API:** REST `POST`/`PATCH /v1/disks` and service create, GraphQL `addDisk`/`updateDisk`/`createService(disk:)`, MCP `add_disk`/`update_disk` and Blueprint validate/apply answer `400 disk size cannot exceed 1TB (1000GB)`, and nothing is written. A disk support raised past 1 TB keeps its size when a request or a Blueprint sync repeats it; only a further grow is refused. The CRD still admits Hetzner's 10 000 GB.
- **Tests:** `lego/backend/internal/apps/disk_size_cap_test.go` (every surface, the kept size, Blueprint), `disk-section.test.tsx`, `create-service-input.test.ts` and `services.new.test.tsx`; each fails against the old 10 000 GB cap. ADR082 § Size, ADR018's Persistent disks row and `render-artifacts/disks.md` § Size limit record the cap and its evidence.
