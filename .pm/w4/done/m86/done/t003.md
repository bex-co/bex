## Objective

Expose whether the live kpack `ClusterBuilder` is Ready and how old its committed builder resolution is through the existing operator build-metrics boundary.

## Context

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/build_metrics.go` already owns build-plane Prometheus signals.
- The platform `ClusterBuilder` lives in the kpack build substrate and carries Ready conditions the operator can observe without introducing a business-logic dependency.
- Image age must come from committed resolution metadata established by t001, not an unreliable mutable tag or scrape-time guess.

## Steps

1. Wire a bounded observation of the configured `ClusterBuilder` condition and resolved-at metadata into the build metrics collector.
2. Export fixed-name, low-cardinality readiness and age metrics with explicit absent/unknown behavior.
3. Keep collection best-effort for reconciliation while making malformed or missing freshness metadata diagnosable.

## Files

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/build_metrics.go`
- `lego/operator/internal/controller/build_metrics_test.go`
- `lego/operator/cmd/manager/`
- `lego/operator/config/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Ready true/false/unknown and absent-builder cases produce documented metric values without unbounded labels.
- [x] Image age is derived from committed resolution metadata and advances monotonically with time until the reviewed metadata changes.
- [x] Metric collection cannot block or fail App reconciliation.
- [x] Unit tests cover Ready, unready, missing, and malformed-metadata cases.

## Out of scope

- Replacing kpack, mutating the `ClusterBuilder`, or exposing tenant/image identifiers as metric labels.
