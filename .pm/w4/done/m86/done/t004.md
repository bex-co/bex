## Objective

Turn the scheduled drift and live builder metrics into a documented operational freshness contract with tested alerts.

## Context

- `deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml` and `deploy/gitops/base/rules/alerts_test.yml` carry the production rule and promtool-test precedent.
- Readiness and freshness are distinct: a Ready builder may be stale, and a recently resolved builder may be unready.
- The SLO must state operator response and reviewed-update cadence without promising silent automatic upgrades.

## Steps

1. Choose and document a configurable/default maximum builder-resolution age grounded in ADR060's toolchain-risk rationale.
2. Add separate alerts for sustained non-Ready state and over-age resolution, with stable annotations pointing to the resolver workflow/runbook.
3. Add fire and clear promtool cases for each rule and document the operator update procedure.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Sustained unready and over-age fixtures fire distinct alerts, and healthy/fresh fixtures remain quiet.
- [x] Alert labels are low cardinality and annotations identify the exact remediation workflow.
- [x] The documented procedure requires reviewing the new digest before updating the inventory.
- [x] Prometheus rule validation and tests pass.

## Out of scope

- Paging on every upstream digest change, automatic image rollout, or changing build-capacity alerts owned by `w7/m83`.
