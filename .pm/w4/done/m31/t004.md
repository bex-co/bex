## Resolution (2026-07-16)

Ran 4 parallel review agents: reuse, a dedicated correctness-adjacent concurrency check (given the security-critical hot path, this replaced a generic "efficiency" pass), simplification, and altitude. The concurrency check found a real, confirmed race in the first-cut epoch design (see t001's resolution — a stale positive result could still land after a concurrent `invalidate` completed, since the epoch check and `cache.Put` weren't atomic with `invalidate`'s bump); fixed with a narrow `epochMu` before proceeding to any other finding, per this task's own "prefer declined-with-reason over cleverness, but fix real bugs" priority. Applied: (1) the `epochMu` correctness fix; (2) replaced the fragile `Server.CLIAuth` field (populated only after `Handler()` returns, breaking every other cap's "configure before `Handler()`" convention and risking a nil-pointer if the ordering were ever gotten wrong) with `Server.DeviceRateLimiter`, set before `Handler()` like `RateLimiter` itself, consumed inside `Handler()` where `cliauth.Service` is actually constructed; (3) small test-boilerplate dedup in both `auth_test.go` (`blockingHydra` helper) and `cliauth/service_test.go` (`newDeviceLimitedMux` helper) — each pattern had just been copy-pasted a second time within this same diff, hitting the codebase's own "graduate on the 2nd copy" threshold. Declined: extracting a shared `internal/core` keyed-rate-limiter generic (three now-near-identical hand-copies: `cliauth.DeviceRateLimiter`, `api.RateLimiter`, and the pre-existing `deploys.DeployHookRateLimiter`) — real, but touches `deploys/`, outside this milestone's scope; filed as `w4/028.md`.
