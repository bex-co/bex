# A prebuilt image over 10 GB compressed is refused before any node pulls it, with a reason that says so

Why: Render parity. Render's docs say "The Docker image's compressed size cannot exceed 10 GB". bex pulls an image of any size onto shared nodes, where one oversized image can fill a node's disk and take its neighbours down with it.

- **Render:** [render.com/docs/deploying-an-image](https://render.com/docs/deploying-an-image) § Image size: "The Docker image's compressed size cannot exceed 10 GB." Its create form's `isDeployableImage` check also has an image-too-large outcome (w1/153, which covers the check before create).
- **bex:** the image metadata read already fetches the platform manifest's layers with their compressed `size` (`layerDescriptor`, `lego/operator/internal/registry/image_config.go:236-240`, filled at `:509`), but never sums them. The only 10 GB cap is sandbox image imports (`defaultMaxImageBytes = 10 * store.BytesPerDecimalGB`, `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/images.go:102`).
- **Do:** sum the layer sizes in the metadata read. Above 10 × 10⁹ bytes, fail the deploy before any pull with a new deploy reason (`lego/types/v1alpha1/deploy_reasons.go`, all 8 dashboard languages) that names the image, its compressed size and the 10 GB limit. Fail it under `ReasonImagePullFailed`, so it records `image_pull_failed` as bex's other pre-pull refusals do (w2/072). Add an envtest with a fake registry at just under and just over the limit, and record the limit in ADR018's Create service row (`docs/ADR018/services-lifecycle.md`).
- **End state:** an image whose compressed layers total more than 10 GB fails its deploy in seconds with that reason on REST, GraphQL, MCP and the dashboard, and the previous release keeps serving. An image at 9.9 GB deploys as before.
- **Size:** about 1h, too small for a milestone.

**Done 2026-11-27.** The image metadata read now sums the hosting platform manifest's compressed layer sizes (`ImageConfig.CompressedBytes`, `compressedBytes` in `lego/operator/internal/registry/image_config.go`, which refuses negative or overflowing sizes). `resolveImageNetwork` (`lego/operator/internal/controller/image_network.go`) refuses a prebuilt image over `appv1alpha1.MaxImageCompressedBytes` (10 × 10⁹ bytes, `lego/types/v1alpha1/image_network.go`, now also bex-api's pre-create limit in `registryread`) with `imageTooLargeError`. `imageMetadataFailureReason` fails it under `ImagePullFailed`, so bex-api records `image_pull_failed`, and the new reason `image_too_large` ("the image {image} is {size} GB compressed, over the 10 GB limit on an image's compressed size …", size rounded up to the hundredth) is in all 8 dashboard languages. A bex build's own image is not refused. Envtest "A prebuilt image over 10 GB compressed (w4/734)" (`image_size_envtest_test.go`): a web service's deploy at 10,000,000,001 bytes fails with the reason while rev-1 keeps its pod template, and later passes read no manifest; an image of exactly 10 GB goes live; a new private service at 12.35 GB fails with no Deployment. The two refusing specs fail with the check disabled. `TestReadImageConfigSumsTheCompressedLayerSizes` (an index uses the amd64 manifest's sizes), `TestReadImageConfigRefusesOutOfRangeLayerSizes`, `TestImageTooLargeNamesItsSizeRoundedUp`, and the backend's `TestDeployReasonsRoundTrip` and `TestDashboardWordsEveryDeployReason` cover the rest. Recorded in ADR018's Create service row, ADR004 and `service-events.md`. Background workers, cron jobs and configured-port web services read no metadata before the pull: [w4/754](../754.md).
