# 637 — `bex-canary`'s usage reads "Metered through 2026-11-11 16:00" two days later: `through` trails the last closed hour by 47 h, the catch-up horizon, while today's resources are metered. The likely cause is coverage waiting 48 h on holes the m334 gauge already knows no meter will fill

Why: Billing and `GET /v1/usage` tell the owner that usage is complete only through a point two days back, with every source listed as degraded. That reads as two days of missing metering, but today's resources are metered to the hour. w4/496 set the target "`through` within about an hour of now" for a healthy month.

- **Reading the number:**
  - `through` is the minimum, over active streams, of each stream's contiguous watermark (`aggregateUsageCoverage`, `lego/backend/internal/store/usage.go:566-614`).
  - A stream's watermark stops at the first window with no metered row that is not yet "settled", meaning older than `latestClosed − UsageCatchupWindow − 1h` (`evaluateStreamCoverage`, `:535-564`; `usageSettledBefore`, `:516-518`).
  - A watermark exactly 47 h behind is therefore one stream holding a hole right at the settle edge. Either the hole is new every hour, or the stream is stuck.
- **Likely cause (not verified: it needs the store, which a QA session cannot read):** coverage waits on holes the m334 gauge already treats as unfillable.
  - `oldestFillableHoles` (`lego/backend/internal/usage/metrics.go:84-114`) skips a deleted App's hole when it is in a kind the App is not owed: "Egress without a recorded identity is never metered after the deletion, so such a hole is an undercount that coverage reports, not one an operator can fill in time" (`retiredAppKinds`, `service.go:972-986`).
  - `evaluateStreamCoverage` has no such predicate. It holds the watermark on that same hole until it settles, 48 h later.
  - `bex-canary` deletes QA services every hour. Some never served, so they recorded no egress identity, and each such deletion can pin `through` for the next 48 h. That explains a lag that sits at the horizon.
  - **The other candidate** is a live meter stuck on an unavailable source. `catchUpAppMeter` (`service.go:1121-1164`) stops at that window and retries it every pass, and once its cursor falls behind the floor it keeps retrying the floor window, so `through` would also sit at now − 47 h. In that case m334's `UsageHourNearlyUnmeterable` should be firing.
- **Fix (target behavior):**
  - Coverage's watermark passes at once a hole no meter will fill, using the same predicate as `oldestFillableHoles`, which should become one shared function. It still marks the stream degraded ("undercount, never invent", ADR023).
  - It keeps stopping at a fillable hole inside the catch-up window, as w4/496 decided.
  - In a workspace whose live meters are current, `through` is then within about an hour of the last closed hour.
- **First step for the worker:** run `UnmeteredUsageHoles` (`store/usage.go:483`) for `tea-daif693dqjvc73e7as3g` and name the hole at 2026-11-11 16:00.

  - If it is a live meter, find why its source has been unavailable for 47 h. That is an outage, and the m334 alert's silence would be a second bug.
