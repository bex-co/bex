# Environment inbound-IP layer: cross-feature lifecycle edges left open by m28

← filed by `w4/m28`'s consistency review 2026-07-15. m28 covered every environments-owned path (ACL writes, membership verbs, delete, creates, blueprint groupings/regrouping, the projector's fallback-create). Three edges cross feature boundaries and were deliberately not fixed inline:

1. **`projects.SetServices` stale `environment_id` now enforcement-bearing.** Pulling a service out of its project (`projects/service.go` → `store/projects.go`) leaves `apps.environment_id` pointing at an environment of a project the service no longer belongs to; `ListEnvironmentServices` filters on both columns, so the app vanishes from every future environment fan-out while its stamped `spec.environmentIPAllowList` stays frozen — stale rules (or a frozen deny-all) with no clearing path. Pre-m28 this ADR032-documented staleness was label-only. Fix sketch: `SetProjectServices` clears `environment_id` for departing rows (a store-level cascade, keeping projects ignorant of environments), plus a one-shot sweep for existing drifted rows; the projector's LEFT JOIN then clears the layer on recreation, and the environments fan-out on the next mutation.

2. **`projects.Delete` cascades environments away without the layer fan-out.** `DELETE FROM projects` cascades child environments; member App/DB/KV CRs keep `spec.environmentIPAllowList` forever (a deny-all environment ⇒ members blocked until manually cleared). Fix sketch: projects.Delete enumerates child environments and delegates to the environments Delete path (composition-root wiring), or a store-returned list of cascaded env ids drives the same member-clear fan-out.

3. **No backfill for pre-m28 members.** Environments that already had non-empty rules: member Apps carry no layer until the environment's next write touches them (env rules silently unenforced on apps until then), and datastores whose own `spec.ipAllowList` was overwritten by the pre-m28 clobber keep those env rules as their own list (harmless double-enforcement once the layer lands — identical ∩ identical — but confusing to read). Fix sketch: an idempotent one-shot sweep (the `ipallowlist-normalize.sh` shape) that re-runs the fan-out for every environment; optionally drops a datastore's own list when it is byte-identical to its environment's (provably the clobber's residue).
