## Objective

Re-read the live pricing page every week and fail when any captured row (the plan comparison table, the features-by-plan page, the four price tables, the FAQ) differs from its fixture.

## Context

- Today the plan-table fixtures are re-captured by hand ("To take in a change to Render's tables, re-capture the fixture", ADR030 §9), so a Render change waits for someone to notice.
- Render's dashboard bundle runs a Starter price experiment (`enable_starter_price`, [dashboard-operations.md](../../../../../docs/render-artifacts/dashboard-operations.md)); when it goes GA the price table changes.
- `.github/workflows/render-schema-drift.yml` runs the weekly Render drift jobs; a workflow's `paths` must list every file outside its tree its tests read (`scripts/ci_test_inputs.py`).

## Steps

1. Write `scripts/render-pricing-drift.sh` (and its `.test.sh`): fetch `https://render.com/pricing.md` (and `platform-features-by-plan.md`), normalize the markdown (parentheses, ticks) to the fixtures' form, and fail on any changed, added or removed row, naming the fixture row and the bex constant its disposition points at.
2. Add a weekly `render-pricing` job to `render-schema-drift.yml` and dispatch it once on `main`.

## Files

- `scripts/render-pricing-drift.sh` (new)
- `scripts/render-pricing-drift.test.sh` (new)
- `.github/workflows/render-schema-drift.yml`
