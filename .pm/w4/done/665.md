# 665 — Since pin `c99e7815ebec`, a new image web service takes 2–5 minutes to go live (was about 75 s): its deploy waits 23–104 s before it starts, and its route takes about a minute to follow a detected port

Why: Render starts a prebuilt image's deploy at once and routes as soon as its port scan finds the listener. On bex, a Free `traefik/whoami` service, which listens on its declared port 80 within a second of starting, now sits on "This service's first deploy is in progress" (`503`) for two to five minutes. Round 209 measured 75 s from create to `live` for the same image, and that was before w4/m350 removed the second roll. Every new image service and every image deploy waits, and CI jobs polling a create wait with them.

- **Source / severity / estimate:** live `/qa-find-bugs w4` loop, rounds 361–362, 2026-11-14 13:11–14:06 UTC (`muse.env`, workspace `bex-canary`; pin `c99e7815ebec`, synced 13:09 UTC). **Major** (latency on every image deploy; nothing fails). ~1h30m once the operator's reconcile timeline is readable; cause unverified.
- **Fixtures:** Free web services from `POST /v1/services` with `docker.io/traefik/whoami:v1.10`, no env and no port, unless noted. All were deleted afterwards (`DELETE` 204, then `GET` 404).

    Port discovery itself is not slow: it chose port 80 26 s after the process started, the 20 s `portDetectGrace` plus one probe (`lego/operator/internal/controller/port_detect.go:57`, `web_image_http.go:129-137`).   - **Not the registry credential:** a live poll of the build narration while `qa-20261114-r362-p80q` was queued showed only `==> Deploy queued`. No `==> Preparing registry credentials` (w4/m347's `ReasonRegistryCredsPending`, `progress.go:177-180`) appeared, and its queue was 23 s.
- **Root cause: unverified.** The operator's reconcile timeline is not visible from the QA account. The pattern (dispatch varying from 15 s to 104 s, and a minute between the detection pass and the routing pass, though each such pass asks for `RequeueAfter: 5 * time.Second`, `web_image_http.go:266`) fits a congested App controller work queue, not one slow step. This pin added or changed several things that reconcile more often or do more per pass:
  - w4/m347: a pending credential re-probes every 3 s (was 10 s);
  - w4/m342 with w4/648: isolation policy convergence;
  - w4/m350: the first release's open-port probe;
  - w4/m364: the operator reading pod metrics for autoscaled Apps.
  - The 13:11 samples, minutes after the operator rolled, still queued only 15–18 s. The queue grew from 13:25 on, which also fits a backlog that built up.
- **Fix (target behavior):** a new image service's deploy starts within 15 s of its create, as on round 209. It goes `live` within about 30 s of its process listening, on a port it declares or one discovery finds. Each discovery step is one 5 s requeue apart, not a queue wait.

## Persistence check — QA loop round 364 (2026-11-14 14:22–14:27 UTC, same pin)

Two more Free `traefik/whoami:v1.10` creates, run one after the other, about 75 minutes after the rollout. Both were deleted afterwards (`GET` 404).

| created (UTC) | service | queue (create → start) | process up → `live` | create → `live` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 14:22:03 | `qa-20261114-r364-p80` (`srv-dbs6t2vla3oc73cu7osg`) | 22 s | 63 s (first `200` at 14:23:27, 46 s) | 100 s |
| 14:23:56 | `qa-20261114-r364-p80b` (`srv-dbs6tv7la3oc73cu7p6g`) | 99 s | 57 s | 164 s |

- After the process starts, both runs were close to round 209 (46–63 s). The first run's URL served as soon as its `stallReason` read `Detected HTTP on port 80`, with no minute-long gap this time.
- The queue before dispatch still swings from 22 s to 99 s between back-to-back creates. Dispatch latency is the part that persists. Weight task 1's reading toward create → dispatch.
- The second run's poll loop missed the `Detected…` `stallReason` window, which lasted at least 16 s in every other run. A poll request probably hung, so that run's URL timing is not used.

- **Closed 2026-11-14 (/loopx w4):** not the pin, and not one of its operator changes: the App controller had been saturated by its own private-network listener sweep since at least 2026-11-13 10:00 UTC (the start of Prometheus retention). `scanPrivatePorts` swept every Running web and private service's pod (about 20 of them) every 2 min inside the reconcile pass, and since tenant pods drop probes to closed ports, every sweep ran its full 15 s `portDetectTimeout`. Production readings (operator `:8080` metrics and Prometheus, read-only): `controller_runtime_reconcile_time_seconds` rate 1.85–1.93 s/s against `max_concurrent_reconciles{controller="app"} 2` in every 6 h window since then; 2,300–2,350 reconciles of 10–20 s per 6 h (about 385 an hour, 30–47% of all passes) and only about 5% between 2.5 s and 15 s; mean `workqueue_queue_duration_seconds` 17–25 s at baseline; `BexOperatorReconcileQueueSlow` firing without a break. From 11:10 to 13:10 UTC it collapsed: once the queue wait outgrew the 2 min interval, every pass was a sweep, throughput fell to a flat 7.1–7.3 reconciles a minute (2 workers ÷ about 16.7 s) and the mean queue wait held at 85–92 s. The 13:09 pin rollout restarted the operator and reset the sweep timers, which ended the collapse (30–40 s waits after it, depth 12–25): the regression lined up with the pin, but the pin did not cause it. Fix: the sweep runs in the background (`privatePortSweeps`; `private_ports.go`). The pass that finds one due starts it and returns at once, and a pass during it starts no second one. When it ends it wakes its App through a channel source, and the next pass publishes the record unless the App's revision or UID moved on. `MaxConcurrentReconciles` stays 2, because without the sweeps the measured load is about 0.3 of a worker. New alert `BexOperatorAppReconcileSlowPasses` (over 10% of App passes longer than 10 s for 15 min; production read 30–47%), with promtool tests. Tests: `TestScanPrivatePortsNeverBlocksThePass` (a sweep that never ends does not hold the pass, starts once, wakes its App, and the woken pass takes its record) and `TestScanPrivatePortsDropsAnOutrunSweep`; the existing private-port tests run across the passes a sweep spans; `make test`; `scripts/gitops-validate.sh`. Not changed: the unready-pod detection scans (`detectBoundPort`, `detectPrivatePrimary`, web image HTTP discovery) still run inside the pass, up to 15 s each, but only while a rollout is unready. Live DoD replays after the next production deploy.
