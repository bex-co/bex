# 671 — bex-api answered 502, then 503 to every request for about 20 s, right after one tenant sent a burst of ~1 MiB secret-file writes

Why: if a single authenticated tenant can make every bex-api pod unavailable with a few large secret-file writes, every workspace's dashboard, REST, GraphQL and MCP go down with it. The timing below points that way, but the cause was not seen.

- **Source / severity / estimate:** live `/qa-find-bugs w4` loop, round 393, 2026-11-15 05:20–05:25 UTC (`muse.env`, workspace `bex-canary`; pin `2d9bbae6bb8d`). **Major if confirmed** (platform-wide availability); **cause unverified**. ~1h to reproduce and pin down on the mock cluster, then the fix.
- **Do not reproduce on production.** Use the mock cluster or a `dev-N` environment (pre-approved, AGENTS.md).
- **What was sent:** while replaying w4/m363's DoD 2 ([w4/m363 t009](../blocked/m363/done/t009.md)), one QA session sent about 25 `PUT /v1/services/srv-dbsk0rn35ies73a66120/secret-files/qa-fill.txt` requests between 05:20:50 and 05:24:30 UTC. Each body carried 385,600–434,177 bytes of content beside a stored 614,400-byte file, keeping the service's files map at about 1 MiB. They came serially, 2–17 s apart.
- **What came back:**

  | time (UTC) | request | answer |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | 05:20:5x–05:22:13 | totals at exactly 1,048,576 | `500 internal error` every time (t009) |
  | 05:21:21 | total 1,048,000 (accepted before and after) | **502** |
  | 05:23:0x–05:23:4x | totals 1,048,322–1,048,575, two tries each | 500s, with **502** on the second try at 1,048,322 and at 1,048,575 |
  | 05:23:5x | total 1,048,576, twice | **503**, **503** |
  | 05:24:44–05:25:01 | `GET /v1/owners?limit=1` and `GET /v1/services/{id}` every 5 s | **503** on every request |
  | 05:25:06 on | the same GETs | 200 |

  `GET https://api.bex.co/healthz` and `/readyz` answered `ok` afterwards. Neither exposes uptime or restarts. No deploy pin moved in that window (the pin stayed `2d9bbae6bb8d`, 2026-11-14 22:44 UTC).
- **Reading (unverified):**
  - A 502 is the edge losing an upstream connection mid-request; a 503 for every request means no ready bex-api pod.
  - Scattered 502s turning into a full 503 fits pods dying under the large writes (OOM, or a crash on the store-error path behind t009's 500) and restarting together.
  - Other causes fit too. OpenBao raft struggling with ~1 MiB entries could stall request handlers until the probes failed. A coincident restart for an unrelated reason would also fit.
  - bex-api's readiness is a start/stop flag (`lego/backend/cmd/api/main.go:275`, `:676`), not an OpenBao check. So a 503 storm means the pods themselves were not ready, not that a dependency check failed.
- **To do:**
  1. Read production's bex-api pod restarts and `OOMKilled` reasons, and OpenBao's raft apply latency and errors, for 05:20–05:26 UTC on 2026-11-15 (ops access, as w21/064 and w4/665 used).
  2. On the mock cluster, send the same sequence (a ~600 KiB file, then ~25 writes keeping the map near 1 MiB, some over the raft cap). Watch bex-api memory, restarts and the edge's answers.
  3. If a tenant can crash or starve bex-api this way, bound it.
     - Per-request memory: decode, marshal and project once, streaming where possible.
     - Concurrency on large environment writes: a per-workspace semaphore or a lower body cap per request.
     - The store-error path: fail the one request with a 4xx/5xx, never the process.
- **Unverified:** everything above "what came back". Only the edge's answers and their timing were seen.
- **Render:** n/a (availability).
- **Dedupe:** `grep -rlE "503|OOMKilled|out of memory" .pm/w4/*.md .pm/w21` has no bex-api-wide outage item. w4/657 (done) raised the environment body cap to 4 MiB (`MaxEnvironmentBodyBytes`, `lego/backend/internal/secrets/service.go:994`), which is what lets ~1 MiB writes through.

- **Done 2026-11-15 (/loopx w4): confirmed, and the secret-file burst was not the only trigger.** Production readings (Prometheus and Loki, read-only):
  - bex-api ran with a **128Mi** memory limit and no `GOMEMLIMIT`, idling at 65–95 MiB working set.
  - Both replicas (`bex-api-76bd9d9697-{rjhqw,vrdfp}`) sat at 127–128 MiB from 05:14:31 UTC on and restarted about 10 times between 05:15 and 05:29 ("listening on :8090" at 05:15:47, 05:18:18, 05:19:53, 05:21:06, 05:21:41, 05:24:05, 05:25:01, 05:25:36, 05:27:44, 05:28:27). Last-terminated reasons were `OOMKilled`, `Error` and `Completed`. Readiness flapped on both, and `up{job="bex-api"}` dropped at 05:19:42, 05:24:42 and 05:26:42. That is the 502s and the 503 window.
  - The first restart (05:15:26) came **before** the QA writes, whose first `openbao versioned secret write failed` is at 05:20:16. It lined up with the agent-session completer harvesting `ags-dbsjomf35ies73daah60`, a log of 64k–88k chunks, on both pods about once a minute.
  - A local measurement: parse + coalesce of a 16 MiB driver log (`maxTranscriptLogBytes`) allocates about 68 MiB.
  - The ~1 MiB environment writes, with bodies up to 4 MiB (`MaxEnvironmentBodyBytes`), add their own copies.
  - The same OOM kills also hit bex-api on 2026-11-14 at 09:00 and 11:00 UTC and on 2026-11-15 at 10:00 UTC.
  - OpenBao stayed up. Its 500s are t009's raft entry cap, not this outage.
  - Mock-cluster reproduction (to-do 2) was skipped: the production readings settle the cause.
- **Fix:** `lego/operator/config/api/deployment.yaml` sets requests 128Mi, limit 512Mi and `GOMEMLIMIT=400MiB`. New alert `BexApiRestarting` fires on 2+ api-container restarts in 15m across replicas, which `BexApiDown` (5m at zero) and `PlatformPodCrashLooping` (10m back-off) both missed. It has a promtool test in `alerts_test.yml` and a row in the ADR010 catalog.
- **Follow-up:** [w4/677](677.md) bounds the transcript harvest's own memory.
- **Live check:** after the next production deploy, `kubectl -n bex-system get deploy bex-api` should show the 512Mi limit, and `kube_pod_container_status_restarts_total{container="api"}` should stay flat through a large agent-session harvest.
