## Objective

Keep the group's membership and linked App Secret references consistent when link, unlink, and delete requests overlap or a write fails.

## Context

- `linkFetched` and `UnlinkService` patch the App before replacing the group's link set.
- `DeleteEnvGroup` enumerates a fetched link set before removing projection Secrets and store paths.

## Steps

1. Use current conditional group state for link, unlink, and delete admission, keeping fresh sensitive and Environment-scope authorization at the existing boundary.
2. Preserve App optimistic concurrency while changing group Secret references; a metadata conflict or late failure must compensate or expose a recoverable partial outcome without undoing newer App changes.
3. Make repeated operations converge, and fence delayed links/content writers from recreating or referencing a deleted group.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Two concurrent links to different services preserve both successful links and both App reference sets.
- [ ] Link/unlink/delete races leave no successful operation with contradictory group membership and App references.
- [ ] Failure and retry do not clobber unrelated App edits or silently delete a Secret still referenced by a linked App.

## Out of scope

- Changing service rollout policy or native build-cache behavior.
- A new cross-feature global transaction framework.
