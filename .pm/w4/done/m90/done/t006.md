## Objective

Verify the shipped flow against Render and across every bex surface it affects, documenting intentional differences instead of silently drifting.

## Context

- Live Render evidence from 2026-08-31 is summarized in the milestone and should replace the stale caveat in `docs/render-artifacts/new-workspace.md` that no authenticated capture existed.
- Intentional differences: bex prices remain 30% lower; Enterprise remains self-selectable until separately decided; `BEX_REQUIRE_PAYMENT_METHOD=all` requires a Hobby card although Render Hobby leaves it optional.
- Deliberate parity-by-absence: workspace mutation remains dashboard GraphQL-only; Render's REST owners API is read-only and it exposes no MCP create tool.

## Steps

1. Walk Render and bex side by side at desktop and 390px mobile: billing-email prefill/editability, plan transitions, Payment Method state, validation, cancel, and successful create.
2. Confirm the created bex workspace—not the old one—owns the Customer email, payment default, contract, local mapping, and marker.
3. Check GraphQL error semantics and any reused billing readiness/state against REST/MCP presenters; preserve the intentional lack of workspace mutations there.
4. Update the Render artifact and ADR018 row with fresh evidence, intentional drift, and concrete follow-ups only where real gaps remain.
