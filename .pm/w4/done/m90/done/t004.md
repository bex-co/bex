## Objective

Make `/new/workspace` visibly collect the new workspace's billing email and payment method with Render's form hierarchy and server-driven requirements.

## Context

- Root route context already carries the Kratos Session; `session.identity.traits.email` is used by `UserNav` and can prefill without a new “current user” API.
- The route currently renders `CreateWorkspacePaymentPanel` only for paid plans and passes `useBillingOnboarding`, which resolves the selected current workspace.
- Render shows Billing Email under Workspace Details on every plan, disables/resets it to account email on Hobby, and presents Payment Method as a workspace-specific section. Its paid plans require a card; bex `all` mode intentionally also requires Hobby.
- Dashboard has no Stripe.js dependency today. Use `@stripe/react-stripe-js` + `@stripe/stripe-js` and Payment Element, loaded from Stripe; never a hand-built card field or legacy Card Element.

## Steps

1. Add the required, accessible Billing Email field, prefilled from Kratos; implement Hobby reset/read-only behavior and shared client/server-valid email semantics.
2. Replace `CreateWorkspacePaymentPanel`'s current-workspace billing hook with a dedicated create-attempt hook and Payment Element/Add payment method UI for the reserved workspace.
3. Drive required/optional/disabled behavior from the backend policy. Disable final Create while required email/payment setup is incomplete and surface field/provider errors in place.
4. Submit via `elements.submit()` + ConfirmationToken, call prepare, confirm the SetupIntent with `redirect: "if_required"` where supported, and finalize only after server verification.
5. Add the minimum Stripe CSP origins and publishable-key/bootstrap plumbing without weakening the rest of the dashboard policy; add en/zh copy and accessible loading/focus/error states.
