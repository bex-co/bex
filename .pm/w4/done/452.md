# Two egress meters time out their scrapes, and two others keep reporting themselves unhealthy

Why: w4/442 made the direct-egress gate read only the meters holding an app's counter. A meter that reports `bex_egress_meter_healthy == 0` still, correctly, skips the direct egress of every app it holds for that hour. That is billed as zero and recorded as degraded. Scrape timeouts lose no bytes now, but they hide the meter's health samples.

- **Source / severity / estimate:** split out of w4/442's third fix point, loopx w4, 2026-11-01 (read-only `prometheus-server`); **minor**, ~1h investigation.
- **Evidence (24 h to 2026-11-01T05Z):**
  - `bex_egress_meter_healthy == 0` samples: 69 on `10.10.0.4:9091` and 27 on `10.10.0.6:9091`. Over 7 days to 10-31: 179 on `.4`, ≤23 elsewhere.
  - In w4/442's replay, these were the four hours on 2026-10-31 that still skipped `srv-daif6dsmg29s73d1umvg`.
  - Failed scrapes: 14 on `10.10.0.8` and 13 on `10.10.0.11`, whose `scrape_duration_seconds` reached the 10.0 s timeout. Other meters answer in under 0.13 s.
- **Look at:** which component of the health predicate fails on `.4` and `.6`: hooks, Pod-IP attribution (`bex_egress_meter_expected_pods` vs `bex_egress_meter_attributed_pod_ips`), exclusions or checkpoint errors (`bex_egress_meter_reconcile_errors_total`). Also why `/metrics` on `.8` and `.11` sometimes hangs for 10 s (map size, node pressure, lock contention in `counterRows`).
- **Dedupe:** w4/442 is done (gate scoping). No other open item covers meter health. No DO_NOT_DO item applies.

**Done (loopx w4, 2026-11-01).**

- **Unhealthy meters, root cause:** `bex_egress_meter_reconcile_errors_total` rose 204 times on `.4` and 86 on `.6` in 24 h (0 elsewhere), each logging `reconcile Pod attribution: pod <pub-…|purge-…|dskbak-…|predeploy-…>: namespace and app id are required`. The meter lists node pods by `app.bex.co/app`, but the platform's Job pods carry that label without `bex.co/app-id`, deliberately, so no Service selects them (w4/m181). One such pod failed the whole node's attribution, so the meter read unhealthy, and since w4/442 every app it holds was skipped, for as long as the Job ran. `desiredPodResources` now skips pods without an app id. Test: `TestDesiredPodResourcesSkipsJobPodsWithoutAnAppID`.
- **Scrape timeouts:** `10.10.0.8` and `10.10.0.11` are no longer nodes. Their last samples were 2026-10-31, and the autoscaler removed them. The 10 s timeouts were final scrapes against nodes going away, not a meter defect, and after w4/442 `up` no longer gates direct egress.
- **Follow-up:** w4/453, pre-deploy Job egress (the user's own command) is still unattributed.
