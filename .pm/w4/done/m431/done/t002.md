## Objective

`GET`/`POST /v1/deploy-hooks?key=…&ref=<sha>` deploys that commit and turns the service's auto-deploy off, as Render's deploy hook does. A hook call without `ref` (including `imgURL` only) leaves auto-deploy alone.

## Context

- `deploys/deployhook.go:378-381` builds `TriggerParams{CommitID: ref, ImageURL: imgURL}` and calls `triggerFetched`.
- Under the Wait overlap policy a hook call can land queued (202, `deployhook.go:385-392`); the auto-deploy change must apply when the queued release opens, or at acceptance, consistently with how a queued rollback applies its own flag (`deploys/restart_wait.go:215`).

## Steps

1. Pass `DisableAutoDeploy: ref != ""` from the hook.
2. Carry the flag through the queued (Wait) path so a 202 call still turns auto-deploy off when its deploy opens.
3. Tests: hook with `ref` → 200, auto-deploy off, `manual_deploy` event; hook with `ref` while a deploy runs → 202, and auto-deploy off once it opens; hook without `ref` → unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A hook call with `ref` turns auto-deploy off on both the 200 and the 202 path.
- [x] A hook call without `ref` does not.

## Outcome

Done 2026-11-16: the hook passes `DisableAutoDeploy: ref != ""`; a queued (written-through) 202 turns it off at acceptance, and a deferred (Wait, behind a selected release) 202 carries the flag in `store.DeferredRequest.DisableAutoDeploy` and turns it off when `runDeferredDeploy` opens it. Mutation-checked: reverting either change fails `TestDeployHookRef*` / `TestDeferredDeployHookRefTurnsAutoDeployOffWhenItOpens`.
