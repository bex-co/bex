# Service payloads don't expose the `slug`/`subdomain` field

Found while doing m19's t008 (Render parity check), out of m19's diff so filed rather than fixed.

Render's own service object carries a `slug` field (the globally-unique public-host segment, distinct from `name`) — bex now has the equivalent concept (`App.spec.subdomain`, `apps.slug`, minted w4/m19) but does not expose it on the REST/GraphQL/MCP service object. It is only observable indirectly, baked into the served URL (`serviceDetails.url`/`dashboardUrl`) — a client cannot read the bare slug back, e.g. to build its own links or detect that a random suffix was minted.

Not urgent: nothing in the current dashboard or MCP flows needs the bare slug (the URL is enough to reach the service), and adding it is a small, additive, non-breaking change (a new `slug`/`subdomain` field on `AppView` + the three render fragments) whenever a consumer needs it.

Source: `w4/m19` t008, 2026-07-14.
