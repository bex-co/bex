# Autoscaling ranges match Render's: `minInstances` at least 1, and targets at most 90%

Why: bex accepts `minInstances: 0`, stores it, and reads it back, while the operator always runs at least one instance, so the setting a user reads is not the one that runs; Render's own schema and dashboard bound the range at 1 instance and 90%.

- **Render:** its pinned Blueprint schema (`lego/backend/internal/apps/schema/render.yaml.json`, `serverService.scaling`) has `minInstances` `"minimum": 1` and `targetCPUPercent`/`targetMemoryPercent` `"maximum": 90`. Its Scaling page (`docs/render-artifacts/manual-scaling.md:34`) has a 1–100 instance slider and 1–90% targets. Render's REST bounds are not published.
- **bex:** `lego/backend/internal/apps/service.go:7188-7213` allows `minInstances` ≥ 0 and targets 1–100 on REST, GraphQL and MCP; the operator clamps to at least 1 (`lego/operator/internal/controller/autoscale.go:292-297`).
- **Do (recommendation, Render's REST bounds unverified):** refuse `minInstances` below 1 and targets above 90 on every surface with a named 400, as the Blueprint schema already does; check that no stored autoscaling config holds 0 or more than 90 before shipping, and record the bounds in ADR018.
- **End state:** an autoscaling write with `minInstances: 0` or a target of 95 is refused with a message naming Render's range; reads always match what runs.
- **Size:** about 45m.
