# 672 — Intermittent: a Free image web service answered Traefik's `no available server` (503) for 63 s during a rollback that overlapped another deploy; zero-downtime did not hold, and on-demand reruns did not reproduce it

Why: Render's deploys, rollbacks included, keep the serving instance in rotation until the new one is healthy. Twice in two QA rounds, a Free image service's URL had no backend at all mid-rollout. In one run that lasted a full minute. The same flows rerun with tighter polling stayed at 200, so the trigger is not pinned. It is filed as an intermittent outage with its full record, so the next reader can reproduce it with operator access.

- **Source / severity / estimate:** live `/qa-find-bugs w4` loop, rounds 396–397, 2026-11-15 06:12–06:53 UTC (`muse.env`, workspace `bex-canary`; pin `89ac87445bec`). **Major when it happens** (the service is down for up to a minute); **intermittent, cause unverified**. ~1h to reproduce with operator-side capture, then the fix.
- **Fixtures:** Free busybox web services whose page serves `busybox | head -1`: `sh -c 'mkdir -p /w; busybox | head -1 > /w/index.html; exec httpd -f -p 10000 -h /w'`, first `busybox:1.36`, then `imageUrl` deploys of `busybox:1.37`. All were deleted afterwards (`GET` 404). The services' event histories went with them.
- **Seen (URL polled with `curl -m 3`):**
  1. **Round 396 run 1** (`qa-20261115-r396-rb`, `srv-dbskqpeoou4c73fdrf3g`, 4 s poll):
     - A (`POST …/deploys {}`, back to the saved `1.36` from a live `imageUrl` `1.37`) read `update_in_progress` at 06:16:07, and a rollback to the first deploy was requested at 06:16:08 (`queued`).
     - The poll read `503 no available server` at 06:16:10 (one sample) and again at 06:17:10 (one sample), after A went `live` (06:17:02) and as the rollback rolled out (`live` 06:17:35).
  2. **Round 397 run 1** (`qa-20261115-r397-cx`, `srv-dbsl58tdergc73ccght0`, 0.5 s poll):
     - D0 `1.36`, then D1 `imageUrl` `1.37` (`live` 06:37:21).
     - Rollback to D0 at 06:37:24 (`dep-dbsl695dergc73ccghv0`). It read `update_in_progress` at 06:37:42.
     - A manual deploy (`dep-dbsl6duoou4c73fdrfrg`) was queued behind it at 06:37:43 and canceled at 06:37:46 (`200`; w4/666 holds).
     - **The URL answered `503 no available server` on every poll from 06:37:40 to 06:38:43 (63 s)**, starting before the queued deploy existed. It answered `200 1.36` from 06:38:43. The rollback read `live` at 06:38:46.
- **Not seen, same shapes, 0.5 s poll:**
  - round 396 run 2: A in progress, rollback queued behind it, A `live`, rollback `live`. 200 throughout.
  - round 396: an `imageUrl` `1.37` deploy over a live `1.36`. 200 throughout.
  - round 397: `imageUrl` `1.37`, then `deploys {}` back to the saved `1.36`. 200 throughout, apart from one `000` (3 s timeout) at 06:46:26, 90 s after `live`.
  - round 397 run 3 (`qa-20261115-r397-rb2`, fresh service): D0 `1.36`, D1 `imageUrl` `1.37`, then a rollback to D0 alone. 200 throughout, switching at 06:52:17.
- **Reading (unverified):**
  - `no available server` is Traefik with no ready endpoint for the service's route. Both the serving pod and the candidate were out of the endpoints at once.
  - Both outages came in runs where two deploy operations overlapped: a rollback requested or running while another deploy was in progress or queued and canceled. The 63 s window began as the rollback started rolling, which points at the candidate/serving swap. Candidates: the w4/m320 serving-release hold (`webImageReleaseHeld`), the w4/652/661 deferred selection, or w4/666's survivor naming when a queued deploy is canceled. None was checked.
  - The window in run 2 matches a Free pod's start plus port discovery (about 60 s). That fits the Service selector moving to the candidate's revision before the candidate is Ready, with the old pod already gone.
