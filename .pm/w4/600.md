# 600 — The zh dashboard shows every absolute timestamp in English ("November 11, 2026 at 11:06 PM"), while relative ages beside them are Chinese ("6分钟")

Why: in the zh dashboard, the deploy header, status timeline, Billing, audit rows, webhooks, env groups, disks and Recovery mix Chinese labels with English month names and AM/PM. The rest of the zh work (w4/573, w4/587, w4/m336) translates the copy, but no task covers the dates.

- **Source / severity / estimate:** live `/qa-find-bugs w4` loop, round 175, 2026-11-12 07:06–07:12 UTC (`muse.env`, workspace `bex-canary`; pin `10acf5314735`). **Minor** (i18n gap). ~45m.
- **Probe:** `https://dashboard.bex.co/services/srv-dbqm93s5ph4s73f9gpbg/deploys/dep-dbqmas45ph4s73f9gph0?lang=zh`, from the owned fixture `qa-20261112-r175-web` (deleted afterwards). The browser zone was `America/Los_Angeles` and its locale was en-US. The accessibility snapshot reads:
  - `term: "创建于:"` / `definition: November 11, 2026 at 11:06 PM`, and the same under `更新时间:` and `开始于:`;
  - `heading "状态时间线"`, then `paragraph: 已创建部署` / `time: November 11, 2026 at 11:06 PM`, and the same for `部署已开始` and `进行中`;
  - the log pane's line clock `11:07:28 PM`;
  - the header's relative age `time: 6分钟`, which is Chinese.
  - The same page under the deploy B fixture (`qa-20261112-r175-sup`) reads `创建于: November 11, 2026 at 11:10 PM`.
- **Root cause:** each formatter fixes its own locale, and none of them reads the dashboard language.
  - `formatDateTime` and `formatDateLong` (`dashboard/src/common/lib/format/format-date-time.ts:18-25`, `:34-41`) use the date-fns pattern `"MMMM d, yyyy 'at' h:mm a"` with no locale. Its comment says this is deliberate: "a fixed en-US-style pattern (not the viewer's locale) so the calendar text … is identical everywhere".
  - `formatInstantDetails`, which feeds the hover tooltip (`common/components/instant-tooltip.tsx:62`), builds both `LOCAL_FORMAT` and `UTC_FORMAT` as `Intl.DateTimeFormat("en-US", …)` (`format-instant.ts:51-62`). The comment there says the same.
  - `LocalDateTime` and `LocalDate` (`common/components/local-time.tsx:78`, `:91`) and `useLocalDateTime` and `useLocalDate` (`common/hooks/use-local-date.ts`) call the two formatters with no language.
  - The log clock `formatLogTimestamp` (`features/logs/lib/format.ts:4-13`) passes `undefined` to `toLocaleTimeString`. It follows the **browser's** locale, not the dashboard's: a zh-language dashboard in an en-US browser shows `11:07:28 PM`, and an en dashboard in a zh browser shows a Chinese clock.
  - **The precedent that works:** `formatTimeAgo` (`format-instant.ts:29-39`) takes `language` and passes date-fns `zhCN` when it starts with `zh`. That is why the relative ages are Chinese.
- **Fix (target behavior):**
  - Absolute dates and times follow the **dashboard language**.
  - zh reads `2026年11月11日 下午11:06`, the date-fns `zhCN` long date with a short time. Date-only fields read `2026年11月11日`. The tooltip reads `2026年11月11日 下午11:06:22 PST`.
  - en is byte-for-byte unchanged: `November 11, 2026 at 11:06 PM`.
  - The log line clock follows the dashboard language too (en `11:07:28 PM`, unchanged), not the browser's.
  - Thread the language the way `RelativeTime` does, from `useTranslations`. The hydration guard stays as it is: `LocalTime` already renders after hydration, and the language is known on both sides.
  - Update the two "deliberately en-US" comments.
