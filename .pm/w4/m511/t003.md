## Objective

Each time a replica becomes stale, one `postgres_read_replica_stale` event is recorded for its database, readable on REST, GraphQL and MCP with Render's empty details, and deliverable to a webhook.

## Context

- `datastore_event_facts` and its writer: `lego/backend/internal/store/datastore_event_facts.go` (the `postgres_cluster_leader_changed` pattern at `:57`, `:91`, `:195`); vocabulary `lego/backend/internal/eventvocab/datastore.go`.
- Render's member for Postgres Read Replica Stale has no fields; Render names no recovery event.
- Webhooks vocabulary `lego/backend/internal/webhooks/service.go`; fixture `docs/render-artifacts/fixtures/render-webhook-vocabulary-2026-11-02.json`; member test `lego/backend/internal/events/render_member_test.go`.

## Steps

1. Add the type; record one fact per episode, keyed on the replica and the episode's start.
2. Serve it on the events surfaces with `details: {}`; add it to the webhook vocabulary and `bexSupported`.
3. Tests: one fact per episode, none on recovery, a second episode a second fact, member check.
