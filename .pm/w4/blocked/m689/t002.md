## Objective

Apply t001's rule when a month closes: a qualifying workspace's (or organization's) invoice carries one line that offsets that month's plan fee.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/billing/cron_minimum.go` adds a pending invoice item to the live Subscription for each cron minimum, idempotent by a deterministic key; the waiver follows the same mechanism with a negative amount.
- DO_NOT_DO forbids ADR071 tenant billing credits (`bco-` operations, the credit executor, `BEX_STRIPE_CREDIT_KEY`): the waiver is an automatic invoice line from the billing worker's own key, never a credit grant.
- The audit log and the store already record service lifetimes and member actions; read them rather than adding a new tracker if they are enough for t001's rule.

## Steps

1. At month close, find workspaces and organizations that qualify under t001.
2. Add one negative pending invoice item, "<Plan> plan fee waived for <Month YYYY>: no services or activity", equal to the fee billed for that month, idempotent per workspace (or organization) and month.
3. Skip a workspace whose fee was comped or not billed.

- [ ] Unit tests: an empty Pro workspace gets exactly one waiver line; one that held a suspended service for an hour gets none; one with a member's deploy gets none; an organization with one non-qualifying workspace gets none; a retry creates nothing more.
