## Objective

Turn Render's one sentence into a rule bex can apply and test, and record it as a new ADR030 section.

## Context

- render.com/pricing FAQ, read 2026-11-27: "If a workspace has no services (live or suspended) and no activity during a month, the workspace subscription fee for that month will be waived." No other Render page states the rule (`render.com/docs/llms.txt` searched).
- Plan fees are licensed monthly Stripe SKUs, not meters ([ADR030](../../../../../docs/ADR030-pricing.md) §2); Scale and Enterprise are charged once per organization (ADR030 §2, ADR115 D2). Hobby has no fee.
- Render publishes no definition of a service or of activity, so bex's definitions are bex's choices where Render publishes nothing, not divergences (as `docs/ADR018/billing-invoicing.md` records its other unsigned choices).

## Steps

1. Recommend and record: a service is any web service, private service, background worker, cron job, static site, Postgres or Key Value, in any state, at any moment of the month; activity is any workspace audit-log event written by a member or an API key other than sign-ins, sign-outs and billing-page reads.
2. For an organization: its plan fee is waived only when every workspace in it qualifies.
3. Say which month's fee a waiver offsets, given when Stripe bills the licensed item (in advance or in arrears), and write it as ADR030 §19.

## Record (2026-11-26)

[ADR030](../../../../../docs/ADR030-pricing.md) §19 quotes Render's FAQ (re-read 2026-11-26), defines a service and activity, waives an organization's fee only when every workspace qualifies, and puts the waiver on the invoice after the calendar month, since Stripe bills a licensed Price in advance. It also records that bex charges no plan fee today, so the waiver has nothing to offset until the fee is charged.
