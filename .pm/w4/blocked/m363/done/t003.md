## Objective

The Environment editor's secret-file total check uses the server's files bound and counts contents only. The copy states it. A single 600 KiB file is no longer marked invalid.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/services/lib/environment-draft.ts`:
  - `:14` `MAX_SECRET_FILE_BYTES = 1024 * 1024` is the per-file upload ceiling.
  - `:24` `MAX_ENVIRONMENT_MAP_BYTES = 512 * 1024` is the total for both maps.
  - `:254-282` `flagOverLimit`; `:272` `knownBytes > MAX_ENVIRONMENT_MAP_BYTES`.
- Copy: `dashboard/src/features/services/locales/en.ts:5669` "Secret files are limited to 500 and 512 KiB in total per service or group." and `zh.ts:5535`.
- The same editor serves the service Environment tab (`dashboard/src/routes/services.$serviceId.env.tsx`) and the environment group page (`dashboard/src/features/env-groups/components/env-group-editors.tsx:3`, `EnvironmentEditor`).
- The create form's secret files (`dashboard/src/features/services/lib/create-service-input.ts:261`) validate names only. Check whether it needs the total too.

## Steps

1. Add a files total constant equal to the server's, and count contents only for files. Env vars keep 512 KiB counted as today.
2. Update the en and zh strings to the new number.
3. Update `environment-draft.test.ts` (`:341-356` pins 512 KiB of names plus contents for files).
