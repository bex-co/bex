## Objective

Safely create a journal from the current Snapshot-only dataset before applying the AOF-enabled startup template.

## Context

Valkey 8.1.9 loads its old AOF when started with appendonly yes. scripts/restore-keyvalue.sh already enables AOF on a live RDB-loaded dataset, rewrites, restarts and verifies; managed settings skip that handoff.

## Steps

1. Implement the operator-owned handoff using the existing restore/upstream sequence or an equally safe staged conversion, keeping the current dataset authoritative until the new journal is complete.
2. Authenticate from the exact owned credential Secret without logging/exposing passwords. Bound connection/rewrite waits and validate enabled state, scheduled/in-progress work and successful completion before rollout.
3. Make retries idempotent and handle interruption, failed/full-disk rewrite, active writes, suspended resources and a superseding desired generation according to t001. Retain recoverable data on failure.
4. Roll only after the handoff, preserve PVC/resource/connection identities and coordinate completion with the existing readiness path, including w1/m166's improvements on main.

## Files

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/keyvalue_controller.go`
- `A focused operator persistence helper if justified by the transition`
- `scripts/restore-keyvalue.sh (read as precedent; do not invoke its throwaway restore on the live resource)`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The same acknowledged snapshot keyspace and expiration deadlines survive enabling journaling even when stale AOF files already exist.
- [ ] A completed mode change survives a later ordinary restart and does not depend on a successful PING as a data-integrity check.
- [ ] Conversion errors/retries never publish the old journal as the new authoritative dataset.

## Out of scope

- Restoring or editing another tenant's data.
- Blind reuse of a not-running rewrite check without completion/error validation.
