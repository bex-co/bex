## Objective

Project the targets on every surface.

## Result

- **2026-11-11 (loopx w4):** done. REST and MCP (both `toRenderEvent`) send each side as Render's autoscaling config, `{enabled, min, max, criteria: {cpu: {enabled, percentage}, memory: {enabled, percentage}}}`. A disable's `toConfig` is its `fromConfig` with `enabled: false`, as Render's disable keeps the config. A side with no config to show (an enable's `fromConfig`) reads as `GET …/autoscaling` does with autoscaling off: zero bounds, no criterion enabled. An enabled side recorded before the targets were carries no `criteria`. GraphQL `Details` gains `autoscalingCPUFrom`/`To` and `autoscalingMemoryFrom`/`To`.
