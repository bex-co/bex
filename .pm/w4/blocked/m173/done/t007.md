## Objective

A resource that ran in an hour must get that hour metered even if it was deleted before the rollup. Today the rollup enumerates only live rows, so short-lived resources are never billed, and every deleted resource's final partial hour is lost. This is the DoD's "No silent zeros" bullet, which the deployed t001 fix does not address.

## Context

- **Live replay 2026-10-05 (loop36/37, pin `f837cec46294`, which includes t001's `e2510371c`).**
  - Fixtures, both in the 07:00–08:00 UTC hour and deleted to 404:
    - `qa-20261005-l36-m173` (`srv-db1kvtmtdtps73dl0btg`, whoami): live 07:21:41, deleted 07:27:11 (~330 s).
    - `qa-20261005-l36-kv` (`red-db1kvtsq11qc73f6oepg`, Free Key Value): created 07:21:27, deleted 07:29:54 (~490 s).
  - At 08:11 and again at 08:20 UTC, `GET /v1/usage?ownerId=tea-daif693dqjvc73e7as3g` had **no entry** for either. The same holds for every loop 13–35 fixture that lived and died inside one hour.
  - Controls: resources alive across an hour boundary (e.g. `qa-20261005-m137-pg`) do get rows.
- **Cause:**
  - `lego/backend/internal/usage/service.go:752-797`: `rollup` and `catchUp` iterate `s.Store.ListApps(ctx)`, which is `SELECT … FROM apps` with no deleted rows (`internal/store/store.go:1070-1072`).
  - Datastores iterate `s.listDatastores(ctx)` (live CRs).
  - `reconcileSourceInventory` then only sees what was listed. A deleted resource has no row to `catchUpAppThrough`.
- The `deleted: true` entries in `/v1/usage` exist only for hours that were rolled up while the resource was alive.

## Steps

1. Give the rollup a source for resources deleted since the last closed window: a tombstone or `deleted_at` row kept until the hour after deletion is metered, or an event-fact lookup. Meter them through their deletion hour.
2. Same for datastores (CRs vanish on delete; keep their identity and namespace until the final hour is metered).
3. Make sure the Prometheus series for terminated pods is still within its retention at rollup time (it is, for one-hour-old data).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Store-backed test: an App created and deleted inside one window gets a positive `instance_seconds` row for that window. A datastore likewise.
- [ ] Live: re-run the replay above; both fixtures get rows of ≈330 and ≈490 `instance_seconds`. → carried by t006's post-deploy DoD replay ("No silent zeros").

## Out of scope

- Re-billing past hours (t002's decision covers historical repair).

## Second replay and timing cause (2026-10-05, loop38)

- `qa-20261005-l38-bnd` (`srv-db1m9ketdtps73dl0c70`, whoami): live **08:50:41**, kept awake with a ping every ~110 s, deleted **09:06:32**, so it was alive across the 09:00 boundary.
- At 09:07 UTC `/v1/usage` still had **no entry** for it.
- **Timing cause:** `RunWithIntervals` starts `time.NewTicker(Interval)` with `Interval = time.Hour` (`lego/backend/internal/usage/service.go:490,522-542`). The rollup of hour H therefore fires at bex-api's start minute plus N hours, not just after H ends. After the 06:55Z deploy restart that is about :55, so hour 07 rolled at ~08:55 and hour 08 rolls at ~09:55.
- Any resource deleted between its hour's end and that tick (up to ~59 min later) loses the hour entirely, on top of the "created and deleted inside one hour" case above. With several bex-api replicas the tick offset differs per replica.
- **Fix addition:** align the rollup to the hour boundary (first tick at the next `:00` plus a small Prometheus settle delay, then hourly). This alone does not fix the deleted-resource enumeration: keep the deleted-resource tombstone from step 1.

Done 2026-10-18 (loopx w4):
- Migration 0151 adds `usage_retired_resources`. An `AFTER DELETE` trigger on `apps` covers every App deletion path, including the workspace cascade. `RollbackAppCreation` discards the record.
- `DeletePostgres`/`DeleteKeyValue` record the CR (identity, namespace, display name, public flag, plan, creation time) through `store.RetiredDatastoreFor` before deleting it, and fail as unavailable if they cannot.
- `usage.meterRetiredThrough` runs in every rollup and catch-up. It meters each retired resource through its deletion hour with the existing per-meter cursors, using the 48h floor from the current window. Apps get `instance_seconds` and `build_seconds` from the derived pod identity; their egress is not metered, because router names need the live CR. Datastores go through the live datastore pass, so names are retained and all three meters run.
- An App whose name was reused by an App created by its deletion hour is skipped, because that App already bills the same pods.
- Coverage streams close at the deletion hour. Compaction prunes rows past the catch-up window or for deleted workspaces.
- Datastore catch-up gains the App rule's creation-hour floor.
- Tests: `usage/retired_test.go` (330/495 instance-seconds, public egress, retained name, stream close, same-name skip, pruning), `store/usage_retired_pg_test.go` (trigger, rollback, re-record, close, prune, tenant cascade on real Postgres), and `postgres`/`keyvalue` `retire_usage_test.go` (record first, fail closed). Mutation-checked: removing the retired pass, the public flag or the same-name skip fails them. ADR023 documents the contract.
