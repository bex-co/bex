## Objective

Decide (and record in ADR023/ADR040) whether to recompute affected `usage_hourly` rows for paid tiers: Stripe meter events already sent under ADR040's identifier rules may need repair via its ambiguity/repair path. At minimum, document the cut-over and quantify paid exposure with a read-only query.

## Progress (2026-10-04)

The documentation half is done:
- ADR023 has a new "Instance running time" section with the old/new shapes and the cut-over.
- ADR040 notes that any repair goes through the audited `mark_repaired` path.

**Open, gated:**
- (1) A read-only production query quantifying paid-tier exposure: the sum of over-counted paid `instance_seconds` hours and the meter events already exported for them. This needs production DB access.
- (2) The user's decision whether to recompute those `usage_hourly` rows and repair the exported Stripe events.
