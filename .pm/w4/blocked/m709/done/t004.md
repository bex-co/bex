## Objective

When a window covers hours whose meter events Stripe already has (declared more than 48 hours after the attack), credit what those hours billed above the allowance, once per window.

## Context

- Stripe can cancel a meter event only within 24 hours of receiving it. bex's emitter sends a row 48 hours after its hour, so an already-sent row needs a credit, not a cancellation.
- bex already reads Stripe billing-credit grants for the Billing page (`lego/backend/internal/billing/read.go`, `Credits`, ADR071) and sends usage through `billing/emitter.go` (`toEvent`, `transactionID`).
- An invoice may still be open (the current period) or finalized (an earlier month).

## Steps

1. Compute the amount: the bytes the window's sent hours billed above the allowance (their recorded `BilledQuantity`), priced at the workspace's bandwidth rate for that period.
2. Choose the Stripe instrument for an open period (for example a credit grant or a negative invoice item on the upcoming invoice) and for a finalized invoice (a credit note). Key each by the window id so a retry or a second replica never credits twice. Record the choice in ADR023's section.
3. Extending a window credits only the newly covered hours. Withdrawing one after its credit went out reverses nothing automatically, and the runbook says so.
4. Test with the Stripe test double the billing tests already use.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/billing/` (a new file for the credit, wired where the emitter runs), tests beside it

- [x] A window over sent hours produces exactly one credit of the right amount on the right invoice, however often the job runs.
- [x] A window over unsent hours produces no credit (t003 already sent 0).

## Out of scope
