## Objective

A shell or SSH session can open, and stays open across revalidation, while the service has a Ready instance of its serving release, whatever its latest deploy is doing. Drop the `v.Phase != Running` clause from `sshSessionReady`. Keep the never-served refusal (`Revision == ""` or `Image == ""`, the first deploy) and let `readySSHInstances` decide the rest. Amend ADR035 to match.

## Context

- Mechanism and probes: [README § Finding](README.md#finding-round-226).
- The gate: `sshSessionReady` (`lego/backend/internal/apps/service.go:1332-1344`), clause `:1340-1341`.
- Its 2 callers: `ResolveSSHSession` (`:1729`) and `CreateShellSession` (`:1803`).
- The target filter that already encodes "a running instance of the serving release": `readySSHInstances` (`:1844-1884`) and `readyCurrentAppPod` (`:1886`). With no target, `ResolveSSHSession` answers `409 service has no Ready instance`.
- `ResolveSSHSession` has 6 call sites, at connect and in the one-minute revalidation, for both the web shell and native SSH (README § Mechanism). They all move with the gate.
- ADR035 line 57 ("requires Running phase, an active revision, and a Ready current-image pod") and § Browser Web Shell → Connection path, step 1 ("Running with a live revision").

## Steps

1. In `sshSessionReady`, replace the phase clause with `v.Revision == "" || v.Image == ""`. Keep the message "the service has no running instance yet; wait for the current deploy to go live" for that case.
2. Keep the order and the `409` class of the other refusals (suspended, type, plan; w4/506).
3. `CreateShellSession` does not list pods today. Decide whether it should also refuse when no Ready target exists, or leave that to the gateway's connect-time `ResolveSSHSession`. Today's `readySSHInstances` 409 at connect is the fallback either way. Record the choice in the doc comment.
4. Amend ADR035 (line 57 and § Browser Web Shell): the gate is an active revision plus a Ready current-image pod, so a deploy's build, pre-deploy and rollout leave the serving instance reachable. A session ends when its pod goes away, which, with zero-downtime deploys, is after the new release is live.
5. Check the Hibernated phase: Free services are already refused by plan, so no new path opens.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Table test over `sshSessionReady`: an `AppView` with `Phase` `Building` or `Deploying` and a non-empty `Revision` and `Image` passes. An empty `Revision` or `Image` keeps the "no running instance yet" refusal. Suspended, Free and the static and cron types keep their messages.
- [ ] `ResolveSSHSession` test: an App in `Deploying` with one Ready pod of `ActiveRevision` resolves to it. A pre-deploy or build pod, or a pod of the new revision, is never a target.
- [ ] Revalidation test (web shell and native SSH): a stream whose App moves `Running` → `Deploying` → `Running` survives the ticks while its pod is Ready, and ends when the pod is deleted.

## Outcome (2026-11-14)

- Premise confirmed at `6d2b608d2`: `sshSessionReady` refused `v.Phase != Running` (`lego/backend/internal/apps/service.go:1345`), the only clause that turns false over a running instance.
- The gate is now `v.Revision == "" || v.Image == ""` (the first-deploy sentence unchanged); suspended/type/plan refusals keep their order and `409`. Each refusal now wraps in `apps.ShellRefusal{Reason}` (`suspended`, `ineligible`, `instance_gone`) without changing its status or message; `ResolveSSHSession`'s "no Ready instance" 409 and its pinned-replica not-found carry `instance_gone` too.
- Step 3 choice: `CreateShellSession` does not list pods; the gateway's connect-time `ResolveSSHSession` is the one authoritative Ready-pod check (recorded in its doc comment). Hibernated: Free is refused by plan first, so no new path opens.
- ADR035 amended: line 57, § Browser Web Shell step 1, and a new § Sessions across a deploy.
- Tests: `TestSSHSessionReadyIgnoresThePhase` (every phase × serving/never-served), `TestResolveSSHSessionDuringDeployTargetsTheServingRelease` (new-revision and pre-deploy pods never targets), `TestCreateShellSessionDuringDeploy`, and the transport revalidation tests over the real resolver: `TestWebSocketShellSurvivesADeployAndEndsWithItsInstance`, `TestSingleChannelExecSurvivesADeployAndEndsWithItsInstance` (fixture `gatewaytest.NewServingApp`).
