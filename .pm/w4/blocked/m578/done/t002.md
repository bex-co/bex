## Objective

When `serverActions` denies `suspend` or `resume` with t001's protected-environment reason, the service row menu, the header's Resume and the Settings card show the action disabled with Render's _This action is limited to admins._ A role denial keeps "Your role can’t perform this action. Ask a workspace admin." In both cases the click does nothing.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/capabilities/lib/resource-actions.ts`: `KNOWN_REASONS` folds an unknown reason into `authz_unavailable`, and `gateAction` maps every `denied` to `capabilities.actionDenied`, whatever the reason.
- The consumers already gate on `gateAction`/`gateReason`: `features/services/components/service-row-actions.tsx` (`reasonFor`), `suspend-service-card.tsx` and `resume-service-button.tsx`. With t001 alone they already disable both actions for a Developer, but with the generic role copy.
- Render's copy is already in the dashboard as `projects.bulkAdminOnly` (`features/projects/locales/*.ts`, w4/m550). Move it to a key both surfaces share (the `capabilities.*` namespace) rather than adding a second copy. Every locale file has it: en, zh, ja, ko, de, es, pt, ru.
- Tests: `features/capabilities/lib/__tests__/` (the normalizer and `gateAction`) and `features/services/components/__tests__/service-row-actions.test.tsx` (it mocks `useServerActions`).

## Steps

1. Add the reason to `KNOWN_REASONS`, and let `gateAction` pick the admin-only key for a `denied` decision with that reason. Keep the type narrow: unknown reasons still read as generic.
2. Share the copy key with the bulk toolbar (`bulkLifecycleReason`).
3. Tests: the normalizer keeps the reason. `gateAction` maps it to the admin-only key and `insufficient_permission` to the role key. The row menu shows the admin-only reason on Suspend for a protected denial and sends nothing on select.

## Record (2026-11-22)

- `KNOWN_REASONS` keeps `protected_environment_admin_required`, and `gateAction` maps a denial with it to `capabilities.actionAdminOnly`, Render's _This action is limited to admins._ The key moved from `projects.bulkAdminOnly` into the capabilities locales (all eight), and `bulkLifecycleReason` uses it.
- `projectionReason` (`features/capabilities/lib/resource-actions.ts`) is the one way a control reads its gate from a projection hook's state; the row menu, the Settings card and the header's Resume use it.
- The service controls pass `service.suspended` to `useServerActions`, which re-asks the projection when the state flips, so after a Suspend the Resume that replaces it is not explained by the old state's `not_suspended` until the next poll. The live check found the row menu's still-open Suspend dialog then reading the new `suspended` answer for 2–3 s; a dispatched action's dialog now reads no gate reason while it runs (regression test in `service-row-actions.test.tsx`, which fails without the fix).
- Tests: the normalizer keeps the reason and folds an unknown one; `gateAction` maps it and each other denial; the row menu and the Settings card show the admin-only reason and send nothing. They fail with the reason dropped from `KNOWN_REASONS` (checked).
