## Objective

A verb that changes nothing records on denial only, whichever relation gates it. A mutation keeps recording success and denial. Today `writeRelations` (`lego/backend/internal/core/audit.go:1063-1072`) makes every check of `can_manage`, `can_manage_billing` and the other write relations record its success. That turns `billing.Status`, `audit.List` and `members.ListInvites` into 392 of 861 rows in 17 hours of `bex-canary`.

## Context

- **The recording path.** `authorizeAndAudit` and `emit` (`audit.go:1199-1211`) record the outcome. `auditsDenial` (`:1096-1098`) decides denials. `callerVerb` names the verb (`"<pkg>.<Verb>"`).
- **Known reads that record success:**
  - `billing.(*Service).Status` (`billing/service.go:132`, `can_manage_billing` at `:262`);
  - `audit.(*Service).List` (`audit/service.go:232`, `can_manage`);
  - `members.(*Service).ListInvites` (`members/service.go:617`, `can_manage`);
  - `apps.(*Service).PlanBlueprint` (`apps/blueprint_onboarding.go:268`, `can_create`; confirm it writes nothing);
  - `workspaces.PipelineSettings` (`can_manage_billing`; check whether the recorded call is the read or the save).
- **The read events Render does record** (`ViewEnvVarValuesEvent`, `ViewConnectionInfoEvent`) are typed events added in t002/t003. They are not authorization rows, so this change does not suppress them.

## Steps

1. Enumerate every verb that authorizes a `writeRelations` member: `grep -rn "RelCanManage\b\|RelCanManageBilling\|RelCanCreate\|RelCanOperate\|RelCanManageKeys\|RelCanManageSSHKeys" lego/backend/internal --include='*.go'`, excluding tests. Mark each as mutation or read, and paste the count and the read list into this task.
2. Pick one mechanism and apply it to the whole read list:
   - a read-only marker on the authorize call, for example `AuthorizeRead(ctx, rel)`; or
   - a verb allowlist next to `writeRelations`.

   Successful reads stop recording. Denials keep recording, exactly as `readRelations` denials do today.
3. Keep `members.List`, `apps.List` and the other `can_view` reads as they are (unrecorded on success).

## Result (2026-11-11)

- **Enumeration.** 342 non-test call sites in 74 files mention a write relation. Of the verbs they resolve to (`callerVerb` names), 27 reach a recording entry point (`Authorize`, `AuthorizeTarget`, `AuthorizeOn*`, `AuthorizeWorkspace*`, `AuthorizeApp`, `AuthorizeDatabase`, `AuthorizeKeyValue`; the `*Fresh` variants, `GetApp`, `AuthorizeLabeled` and `Can*` don't record) and change nothing:
  - billing, audit, members and keys: `billing.Status`, `audit.List`, `members.ListInvites`, `apikeys.ListAPIKeys`, `sshkeys.List`;
  - workspace and repository reads: `workspaces.PipelineSettings`, `workspaces.WorkspaceCreationPolicy`, `github.GetClaimSelection`, `apps.NameAvailable`;
  - agent sessions: `agentsessions.Capabilities`, `List`, `Get`, `Wait`, `Transcript`, `ToolOutput`, `ModelCost`, `Tree`, `ListAttachments`, `GetAttachment`, `AttachmentURL`, `AttachmentInline`, `AgentSkills`, `AgentSkill`, `AgentSkillVersions`, `AgentSkillVersion`, `AgentSecrets`, `AgentAutomations`, `AgentAutomation`, `AgentAutomationRuns`. `Wait` alone could write about 260 rows per call.
- **Not reads, so they keep recording:**
  - `apps.PlanBlueprint` persists its plan;
  - `agentsessions.AttachTicket` mints a live-turn credential;
  - `workspaces.ResumeWorkspaceCreation` can create a Stripe customer;
  - `sandbox.ConnectFile` wakes a sandbox.
- **Mechanism.** A verb allowlist next to `writeRelations` (`core.readVerbs`, checked in `authorizeAndRecord`). Successful calls stop recording and denials record as before. `TestReadVerbsNameServiceMethods` keeps every name a real `*Service` method.
- **Tests:** `TestReadVerbsGatedByAWriteRelationRecordDenialsOnly` (allowed → nothing, denied → one denied row, mutations through the same relations → their allowed row).
- **Out of scope, noted:** four datastore `Clear*ID` write paths record under a closure name (`postgres.ClearProjectID.func1` and so on), and `AuthorizeLiveApp` names its denials `core.AuthorizeLiveApp`.
