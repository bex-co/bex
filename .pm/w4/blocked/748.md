# bex publishes a shared responsibility model and a penetration testing policy, as Render does, and the repository has a SECURITY.md

Why: Render parity. Render's security and HIPAA docs rest on a shared responsibility page, and its penetration testing page lets customers test their own services without asking while forbidding tests of the platform, other tenants and denial of service. A bex customer cannot tell whether testing their own service is allowed, what bex secures and what they must, or where the repository takes a vulnerability report.

- **Render (read 2026-11-27):** [shared-responsibility-model](https://render.com/docs/shared-responsibility-model) splits Render's duties (infrastructure, patching, network, availability, breach transparency) from the customer's (their code, access, data); [penetration-testing](https://render.com/docs/penetration-testing): "customers are welcome to carry out security assessments or penetration tests of their own Render-hosted services without prior approval", with the forbidden targets and a vulnerability disclosure program.
- **bex:** `bex.co/docs/shared-responsibility-model` and `bex.co/docs/penetration-testing` answer 404, and bex.co's terms of service neither allow testing your own services nor name denial-of-service tests. `bex.co/.well-known/security.txt` names a contact, and GitHub private vulnerability reporting is on, but the repository has no `SECURITY.md`. bex already does most of the platform side Render lists (kernel and runtime patching, `platform-rebuild.yml`, the private network, status.bex.co).
- **Do:** add `SECURITY.md` in this repository naming the report channels. Draft both pages from what bex does today, with the policy wording for the user to approve, and publish them on bex.co's docs, which live in the site repository (pushing there needs the user's permission, as `w4/730` does).
- **End state:** `SECURITY.md` is on `main`, and `bex.co/docs/shared-responsibility-model` and `bex.co/docs/penetration-testing` answer 200 with the user-approved wording.
- **Size:** about 1h, the site push gated on the user.
- **Source:** w4/m688 t002, full reads of Render's shared-responsibility-model and penetration-testing pages, 2026-11-27.

### Draft: `docs/shared-responsibility-model.md`

````markdown
---
id: shared-responsibility-model
title: Shared responsibility model
description: Understand how bex and its customers work together to keep applications secure, and which security duties belong to each.
keywords: [bex, security, shared responsibility, compliance, patching]
---

bex's security controls assume that customers keep sound internal controls of
their own. Using bex safely depends on customers actively managing the
responsibilities assigned to them: safeguarding their users' data, controlling
access, and upholding their own security practices.

This page sets out bex's responsibilities, the customer's, and the areas the two
share. The lists are a foundation, not an exhaustive set of controls: add the
controls your own security needs and compliance requirements call for.

## Customer responsibilities

The customer is responsible for:

- Protecting every secret in their workspace, from API keys to environment
  variables and secret files ([Secrets](./platform/secrets.md),
  [Credential rotation](./platform/credential-rotation.md))
- Managing and reviewing who can reach their workspaces, and with which role
  ([Members](./platform/members.md), [Audit logs](./platform/audit-logs.md))
- Deciding which data they put on bex, and making sure it is transmitted
  securely and encrypted where it needs to be
- Keeping informed about bex's communications that affect security, their
  obligations or service availability
- Securing what their own services output, such as reports, exports and logs
- Complying with the laws and regulations that apply to them
- The security of their application's own configuration: its code,
  dependencies, Dockerfiles, images and settings
- Regular security assessments of their applications
  ([Penetration testing](./penetration-testing.md))
- Protecting the workstations used to reach bex
- Their own business continuity and disaster recovery plans
- Deleting their data from bex when they stop using it
  ([Delete your account](./platform/account-deletion.md))

## bex responsibilities

bex is responsible for:

- Keeping the operating systems of the servers that run services patched and up
  to date
- All internal networking between services and datastores, including the
  isolation between workspaces' private networks
  ([Private network](./platform/private-network.md))
- Securing the infrastructure that hosts the platform's hardware, software,
  networking and facilities
- Managing the underlying servers, networks and storage
- Keeping the runtimes of the languages and frameworks bex builds secure and up
  to date
- Transparency about its operational status
  ([status.bex.co](https://status.bex.co)) and about any security breach
- Securing the platform, including the middleware and integrated services it
  provides, such as the edge, TLS certificates ([TLS](./platform/tls.md)),
  managed Postgres and Key Value, builds and the API
- Documenting the platform's security features

## Shared responsibilities

bex and the customer share responsibility for:

- Monitoring and responding to incidents, depending on the nature of the
  incident and where in the stack it happens
- Security training
- Data privacy: both cooperate so that data privacy follows best practices and
  the compliance requirements that apply
- Regular reviews of their security practices
````

### Draft: `docs/penetration-testing.md`

````markdown
---
id: penetration-testing
title: Penetration testing policy
description: Understand which security assessments and penetration tests bex customers may run, which are prohibited, and how to report a security issue.
keywords: [bex, security, penetration testing, pentest, vulnerability disclosure]
---

bex customers are welcome to carry out security assessments or penetration
tests of their own bex-hosted services without prior approval from bex. This
helps customers find and fix vulnerabilities in their own application
environments.

## Prohibited testing

Direct testing of bex's core infrastructure, its APIs, its dashboard or any other
service not provisioned for your own use is forbidden without explicit consent
from bex.

Testing of another bex user's services or infrastructure is not permitted
without their explicit consent.

Any form of denial-of-service (DoS) testing against bex infrastructure, customer
environments included, is expressly prohibited. It puts the availability of
every service on the platform at risk.

## Communicating with bex

If you discover a security issue in the bex product, report it privately and
immediately, through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/bex-co/bex/security/advisories/new)
or to the contact in [security.txt](https://bex.co/.well-known/security.txt).
[SECURITY.md](https://github.com/bex-co/bex/blob/main/SECURITY.md) says what a
report should include.

If bex detects abusive activity related to your security testing, we will
contact you to stop it.
````
