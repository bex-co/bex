## Objective

Refuse reserved PostgreSQL identifiers with a coded 400 before any write: database names `postgres`, `template0` and `template1`; roles `postgres`, `streaming_replica` and anything starting `pg_` (PostgreSQL reserves that prefix). Cover create (REST/GraphQL/MCP), add-user and Blueprint `databases[]`, and mirror the rule in the dashboard.

## Context

- `lego/types/v1alpha1/database_types.go:39` `ValidPostgresIdentifier` (syntax only).
- Callers: `lego/backend/internal/postgres/service.go:300` (`validatePhysicalIdentifier`, create), `internal/postgres/access.go:138` (add-user), `internal/apps/deploy.go:2431,2434` (Blueprint).
- Dashboard: `dashboard/src/features/databases/lib/identifiers.ts` (`isValidPostgresIdentifier`, used by `components/create-database-dialog.tsx:105-107`) and the add-user form behind `hooks/use-access-control.ts`.

## Steps

1. Add a reserved-identifier check with separate database-name and role rules, plus a `POSTGRES_IDENTIFIER_RESERVED` error that names the field.
2. Call it from all three backend paths and the dashboard validators (en/zh copy).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each reserved value returns 400 on REST/GraphQL/MCP create, add-user and Blueprint validate. The controls `qa_owner` and `orders_data` still pass.

## Out of scope

- Repairing already-wedged databases (t002).
