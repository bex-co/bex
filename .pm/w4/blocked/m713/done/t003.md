## Objective

On the Billing page's charge tree, a Postgres reads "Storage" for its billed capacity and "Storage used" at $0 for its used bytes. A Key Value shows only "Storage used" at $0. In zh and every other shipped locale, the text is translated, not raw meter names.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/usage/lib/charge-labels.ts`: `CHARGE_LABEL_KEYS`, plus `UNBILLED_CHARGE_LABEL_KEYS` for shown lines. `charge-labels.test.ts` reads the Go kind constants, so the new kind fails the suite until it has a label.
- Locales: `dashboard/src/features/usage/locales/{en,zh,de,ja,ru,ko,es,pt}.ts` (`usage.chargeStorage` at `en.ts:816`).
- `dashboard/src/features/usage/api/usage.graphql` header comment: the kinds list and "Postgres storage".
- `charges-card.tsx` and its tests.
- The plan review for a Postgres (`storageMonthlyUsdPerGB`, `lego/backend/internal/postgres/tiers.go:85`) already multiplies capacity by instances. Check that its copy does not say "used".

## Steps

1. Add the label keys and the translations.
2. Update the charge tree so the shown storage line reads as a reading, not a second charge. This is the way unbilled sandbox lines read.
3. Run `yarn lint` and `yarn test` in `dashboard/`.
