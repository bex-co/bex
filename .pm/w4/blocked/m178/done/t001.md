## Objective

After a successful cross-project move, complete a fresh source Project cache read before publishing that route's next loader snapshot. Keep the one target membership mutation and preserve successful-move semantics if its later refresh fails.

## Context

- [Live finding, trace and complete probe](finding.md)
- Scope is the source Project snapshot after a confirmed move; product fixes were not implemented by the hunt.

## Steps

1. Trace the finding's target-only mutation response, asynchronous Projects refresh and retained cache-first source loader.
2. Use a lifecycle-independent explicit Project refresh by source ID, or another proven source-cache reconciliation, before Router invalidation; do not depend on dropdown watchers remaining mounted.
3. Keep ordinary title-loader policy, backend membership, named refusals and the single-write move intact.
