## Objective

A deploy canceled before promotion ends with the same serving pods it started with, and with no candidate pod left in any endpoint. The dashboard's promise, "If an earlier deploy succeeded, it stays live", becomes literally true.

## Context

- `settleCanceledRelease` (`lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go:789`) restores the prior release's snapshot (w1/m152).
- Live, Run B: the API returned `canceled` at 18:27:24. The canceled 1.27.5 still answered until 18:27:38.9.
- Live, Run 0: after a dashboard Cancel, the canceled :34 answered at 18:15:46 and 18:15:50. The prior release came back on a fresh pod, because the second roll had drained its live pod.
- The dialog copy is `services.eventsCancelConfirmBody` (w4/334).

## Steps

1. With t001 and t002 in place, confirm that cancel during discovery leaves the selector on the serving revision and scales the candidate away.
2. Envtest: cancel during discovery. The serving pods' UIDs are unchanged, and no candidate pod is ever an endpoint.

## Result (2026-11-11)

- Before promotion the candidate is pinned out of every endpoint and the serving pods are held, so a cancel has nothing to undo: `settleCanceledRelease` restores the served template (w1/m152), and the Deployment scales the candidate away while the serving ReplicaSet keeps its pods (same UIDs).
- `pinServedThroughCancel` (`image_network.go`) keeps the Services pinned to the served revision through that rollback when the served release's recorded template carries that revision (the pin clears when the restored rollout completes), so no candidate pod joins an endpoint between the selector write and the pod's removal. Without the record (a projected rollback rolls new pods) nothing is pinned.
- Cancel of a first deploy is unchanged (not held: no serving network).
