## Objective

The App's Ingress serves `*.<domain>`, which Kubernetes Ingress supports natively. Its certificate is issued by DNS-01: cert-manager follows the tenant's `_acme-challenge` CNAME into a bex-controlled zone, the same pattern ADR093 uses for `*.onbex.co`. HTTP-01 stays the issuer for concrete hosts.

## Context

- Precedent: the ADR093 cert-manager DNS-01 issuer at `deploy/gitops/charts/onbex-default-tls/`.
- Certificates load per Secret (w8/m118): a pending wildcard Secret must not block other hosts' certificates.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] An envtest or fixture shows the wildcard Ingress host and the DNS-01 Certificate.
- [x] A concrete host under the wildcard keeps its own rule and certificate.

## Result (2026-11-11)

- **Routing:**
  - An App's wildcards route on their own `<app>.wildcard` Ingress, at Traefik `router.priority: 1`. A concrete host under a wildcard keeps its own longer-ruled Host route.
  - The Ingress carries the same middlewares as the serving one.
  - It is deleted when the App has no wildcard, on a worker cleanup, and in `quiesceTLSProducers`.
  - `ingressRoutes` checks both Ingresses.
- **Lookups:** the activator and the static server look a host up by its own name, then by `*.<parent>`, so a sleeping Free service or a static site wakes or serves on any name under its wildcard.
- **Certificates:**
  - The wildcard Ingress names `BEX_WILDCARD_CLUSTER_ISSUER` for ingress-shim. Its Secret is per host (`<app>-tls-wildcard.<base>`), so a pending wildcard never blocks another host's certificate (w8/m118).
  - With no issuer, the wildcard routes with no certificate request, since HTTP-01 can't issue one.
  - The DNS-01 issuer and its delegation zone are t011 (gated). `onbex.co` can't be the delegation zone: see ADR005 § Wildcard domains.
- **Tests:** `wildcard_ingress_test.go` and the activator and static-server fallbacks. The operator suite (envtest) passes.
