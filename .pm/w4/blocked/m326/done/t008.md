## Objective

Run `/simplify` over the code this milestone changed (reuse, simplification, efficiency), preserving behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `/simplify` has run, and its fixes or skips are noted here.

## Result (2026-11-11)

`/simplify` (reuse, quality, efficiency):

- **Fixed (high):** a name at any depth under someone's wildcard record verified for anyone through the shared edge addresses. `PointsAtService` now refuses a CNAME chain ending at another service's platform host, and overlap is checked at every depth.
- **Fixed (high):** delegating DNS-01 into `onbex.co` let a tenant point its `_acme-challenge` at another service's platform host. cert-manager would then write a TXT record that stops the `*.onbex.co` wildcard answering for that name. The delegation zone is now its own setting, with its own token and nothing else in it, and provisioning it is gated (t011). The onbex token and ADR093 are unchanged.
- **Fixed:**
  - Workspace identity is the tenant id.
  - The Blueprint preview has a namespace.
  - The wildcard Ingress is `<app>.wildcard`; the dot avoids colliding with another App's Ingress.
  - Probe Hosts and URLs skip wildcards.
  - `ingressRoutes` checks both Ingresses.
  - The worker cleanup deletes the wildcard Ingress.
  - Wildcard Challenge diagnostics are fixed.
  - Wildcard entries are refused in `BEX_RESERVED_DOMAINS`.
  - The sweep tick shares one claim index.
  - The backend uses the types helpers.
  - The REST mapping is a type conversion.
- **Skipped:**
  - A shared two-step host lookup helper (two small call sites).
  - Making `DelegatesChallenge` part of `DomainTrafficChecker` (every test fake would need it).
  - The maintenance-URI guard for a host under the App's own wildcard (the activator refuses it at runtime).
  - Two replicas promoting an overlapping pair in the same instant (a residual, recorded in ADR005).
- **Efficiency:** the wildcard overlap scan costs about 1–3 ms at 50k hosts, and only for wildcards. The Ingress deletes are cache-backed.
