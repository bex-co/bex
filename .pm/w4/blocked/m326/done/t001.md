## Objective

Record Render's documented wildcard custom-domain behavior from render.com/docs/custom-domains, then amend ADR005's named divergence (`docs/ADR005-custom-domain.md:95`) and ADR018's custom-domains row (`docs/ADR018-render-parity.md:169`) to the target. Cover:

- the DNS records the tenant publishes (the `*` CNAME and the `_acme-challenge` delegation);
- how Render verifies the domain;
- plan availability;
- how a concrete host under the wildcard is treated;
- what Render refuses.

## Context

- The code comment at `lego/backend/internal/apps/domains.go:290-297` claims "Render offers no tenant wildcard custom domain". ADR005:95 says Render supports them. Correct the comment along with the ADRs.

## Result (2026-11-11)

- **Captured** from Render's custom-domain guide in [`docs/render-artifacts/wildcard-custom-domains.md`](../../../../../docs/render-artifacts/wildcard-custom-domains.md). Render needs three CNAMEs: `*` to the service's `onrender.com` host, `_acme-challenge` to `<slug>.verify.renderdns.com`, and `_cf-custom-hostname` to `<slug>.hostname.renderdns.com`. It also requires that the root domain point at Render, and that CAA have `issuewild` entries.
- **Divergences:**
  - bex needs two records: the `_cf-custom-hostname` record serves Render's Cloudflare edge.
  - bex has no root-domain rule, which comes from the same edge.
- **Docs:** ADR005 § Wildcard domains replaces the old refusal and its "Revisit only as its own decision" divergence. ADR018's Custom domains row now carries wildcards instead of "wildcard rejection … security superset".
