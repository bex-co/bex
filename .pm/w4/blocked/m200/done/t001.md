## Objective

Deliver a producer remedy that sends complete logical stdout/stderr messages before combining the pod-log response, preserving each first-chunk timestamp and all payload bytes.

## Context

See finding.md. The executed CRI reader replay reproduces the join and proves that merely selecting two streams leaves embedded/missing timestamps. Declared node pin is Kubernetes 1.34.9; the live node version remains unverified.

## Steps

1. Confirm the applicable node/runtime source/artifact identities and re-run the included unmodified ReadLogs probe at that version.
2. Check supported upstream artifacts against the exact mixed P/F replay. Prefer one that actually passes; do not assume a version bump or split-stream option corrects it.
3. If needed, prepare a narrow reproducible patch at the producer's CRI reassembly boundary: maintain pending payload/first timestamp by stream, emit each completed record atomically, and preserve independent stdout/stderr records. Specify bounded pending state, finite EOF, live cancellation, incomplete-record and over-budget behavior before implementation.
4. Prove the two repeated 48 KB cases and the 24 KB/short controls. Preserve arbitrary literal timestamp-looking text; do not strip candidate prefixes from combined tenant bytes.
5. Keep the default pod-backed Live source and its independence from Loki. Record the actual supported artifact or patch/build inputs for t002.
