## Objective

Determine and verify the full consumer impact of a node log-producer correction, including already-correct finite reads and ordinary pod-log clients.

## Steps

1. Audit both production pod-log factories (one composition call each), the three direct finite-reader callers (datastore, App wrapper, pre-deploy), and the two shared follower callers (App and build). Include the Postgres/Key Value fallback shims.
2. Cover web/private/worker/cron runtime, static build without app runtime, build/pre-deploy Jobs, and managed Postgres/Key Value finite reads. Keep production web evidence distinct from local/source coverage.
3. Check ordinary kubectl logs with and without timestamps, short/blank/CRLF/unterminated lines, simultaneous partials on both streams, identical repeated payloads, literal timestamp-like content and valid large UTF-8 records.
4. Pin temporal behavior for SinceTime's inclusive boundary and reconnect, TailLines starting amid a partial record, LimitBytes, finite EOF, follow cancellation, restart and removed-container diagnostics. The Stream type's TailLines restriction and shared newline-state defect prohibit a blind split-stream fallback.
5. Keep authorization, namespace/instance selection, subscription quotas, and currently refused live source/filter classes unchanged. Validate zero-traffic/empty reads honestly.
