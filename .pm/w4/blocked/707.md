# 707 — The Billing page's pending frame is about 800 px too short at 1440 (about 1400 px at 390): it reserves three included-usage cards where the page shows five, and its generic card placeholders do not reflow on a phone

Why: the root `AGENTS.md` rule says a pending skeleton previews the exact ready state at the same breakpoint, with stable heights for the major regions. `/billing`'s skeleton is a stack of generic `CardSkeleton`s. w4/680 rebuilt the Notifications page's skeleton the same way, but left `/billing` in its Unverified list because its ready sections were not compared. They are compared here. Everything below the Plan card moves when the data lands. A visitor who clicks `Charges` or `Invoice history` in the section rail while the page loads lands far above that section.

- **Source / severity / estimate:** live `/qa-find-bugs w4` round 1122, 2026-11-18 02:15 UTC, pin `14eaa71b98e5`, workspace `bex-canary` (Scale, current month). Read-only. **Minor:** layout shift on load; nothing is lost. ~45m.

- **Cause:**
  - `dashboard/src/common/components/route-skeletons.tsx:612-651`, `BillingPageSkeleton`, draws every region with the generic `CardSkeleton` from `common/components/detail-skeletons.tsx:169`: a title bar and N equal lines. The calls are `:627` Plan, `:630` Payment method, `:633`/`:635`/`:637` included usage, `:640` Charges and `:646` Credit balance. None mirrors its card's own layout, so none reflows the way the ready card does.
  - The `included-usage` region reserves three cards. `features/usage/components/usage-page.tsx:142-150` renders five:
    - `WorkspaceResourceCaps` (`:143`), which shows the plan's Services / Postgres / Key Value caps as three tiles;
    - `FreeHoursCard`;
    - `BuildMinutesCard`;
    - `IncludedBandwidthCard`;
    - `CustomDomainsCard` (`:149`, added by `075eb5f6a` on 2026-11-16 without a skeleton frame).
  - Both extra cards are plan-shaped, not data-shaped. `resource-caps.tsx:132,154` renders when any cap is above 0. `custom-domains-card.tsx:32` renders unless the plan is Enterprise (unlimited) or the month is past. So on the default view, the current month on a non-Enterprise plan, the page always shows them.
- **Fix:**
  - Target behavior: at 1440 and at 390, each Billing region's pending frame has the ready region's structure and height for the default view, in w4/680's shape:
    - Plan: title, then the plan box with its name, description and `Change plan` button.
    - Payment method: the card line, both buttons and the Stripe note.
    - Included usage: the caps card with its three tiles in the ready grid (stacking at 390), then one frame per included-usage card.
    - Credit balance: its rows.
  - Draw the caps and custom-domains frames under the same predicates the ready cards use. Read them from the workspace plan the client already holds (the `Workspaces` query, which every page load sends at both widths), and omit them on Enterprise.
  - Reserve titles and descriptions with the invisible-copy helper, as w4/680 did, so they wrap like the ready copy in every language.
  - Leave the data-shaped parts out: the operator-exclusion banner, the Charges tree's category and line count, and the invoice rows. Charges keeps a fixed frame, and its height is accepted as data-dependent.
  - Tests: extend the slow-navigation sweep that w4/680 extended for `/notifications`. On `/billing` it should compare each region's height with its ready section, within a few pixels, at 1440 and 390, for a non-Enterprise workspace with no operator banner. The sweep should fail on today's skeleton.

## Fix (2026-11-18, `/loopx w4` worker): shipped, live check waits on the deploy

- `dashboard/src/features/usage/components/billing-cards-skeleton.tsx` (skeleton-only module) holds one pending card per ready card, each with its own translated copy reserved by `ReservedText`:
  - `PlanCardSkeleton`: the plan box with the workspace's plan name and description beside `Change plan`, or the org-plan note on a secondary workspace. It reads the plan from `useWorkspace()` and falls back to Hobby until the workspace list arrives.
  - `PaymentMethodCardSkeleton`: the card line, `Update payment method` and `Open billing portal` (each `ReservedButton` has the ready box), and the Stripe note.
  - `IncludedUsageSkeleton`: the caps card with its three tiles (Postgres and Key Value carry the `Free: 0 of 1` line bex-api reports on every plan), then Free instance hours, pipeline minutes, bandwidth and custom domains. Custom domains is dropped on Enterprise.
  - `CreditBalanceCardSkeleton`: the label, the amount, the applied line and the card-still-required note.
- `BillingPageSkeleton` (`route-skeletons.tsx`) draws these. Charges, Agent model and Invoice history keep their frames.
- Each allowance's used line is reserved with an active workspace's figures (`10.0 of 750`, `100 of 1000`, `1.00 of 25`, `1 of 15`). At 390 those lines sit at their wrap point (316 px of column), so a workspace with much shorter or longer figures can wrap one line differently.
