# Blueprint claim kinds are bare strings at about twenty call sites

Why: the claim kinds `service`, `database` and `key_value` are typed out at each call site, and only migration 0112's `CHECK` guards them. A typo passed to `GetBlueprintResourceOwner` reads as "unclaimed" rather than failing.

- **Where:** `lego/backend/internal/apps/blueprint_ownership.go`, `apps/blueprint.go` (`resourceOwnedByBlueprint`'s callers) and `apps/deploy.go`. `store.BlueprintResourceClaim.Kind` is a plain `string`.
- **Fix:** declare the claim kinds as constants beside `store.BlueprintResourceClaim`, use them at every site and in `blueprintClaimResourceKind`, and add a test that keeps the constants equal to the migration's `CHECK` set.
- **Also:** the detach path drops a resource's kind into `BlueprintResource.Type`, and `blueprint_state_plan.go` rebuilds it with an if-chain. Return the kind with each detached resource instead.
- Found by w5/098's reuse and quality reviews (2026-10-07).
