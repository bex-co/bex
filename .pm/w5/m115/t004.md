## Objective

The two verbs the w4/m172 sweep missed (by-parent-id create and ownerless unregister) act in the right workspace.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/environments/service.go:530` `Create` and `:548` `CreateWithACL` authorize `RelCanCreate` on the caller's default workspace before `requireProject` checks the project's own. REST `POST /v1/environments` and GraphQL `createEnvironment` can't carry ownerId. So a viewer in workspace A who is an admin in B gets 403 creating an environment in a B project, audited in A.
- `notifications/subscriptions.go:138` `UnregisterDeviceSubscription` without ownerId acts only in the default workspace and answers `{"revoked":false}`. w4/m172 fixed this shape for mark-read.

## Steps

1. Environment create authorizes in the project's workspace, with a single gate like `projects.authorizedProject`.
2. Device unregister resolves the subscription's own workspace.
