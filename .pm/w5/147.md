# A deleted namesake's in-flight secrets write can take over a create's prepared Secret

Why: a create's prepared projection Secret is ownerless until the new App adopts it, and `projectSource` treats an ownerless Secret as no other App's. A secrets write still in flight for the deleted namesake, landing in that window, makes the dead App the Secret's controller and replaces its data. The new App then adopts a Secret projecting another service's values, or garbage collection deletes it under the new service.

- **Where:** `lego/backend/internal/secrets/projection.go` around :153. An ownerless prepared Secret does not count as `controlledByAnother`, so `confirmLiveApp` is skipped. Since w5/134 the window spans the claim, the store write and the revision stamp (`files.go` `prepareProjection`). The stamp's optimistic lock now makes the create fail rather than adopt a changed Secret, but the dead App's write still lands.
- **Fix:** run `confirmLiveApp` before a write takes over an ownerless Secret, too: an App that no longer exists must not claim any Secret. Alternatively, mark prepared Secrets so that only their own create's commit or abort may touch them.
- **Test:** a deleted App's env write, landing between a namesake create's claim and its stamp, leaves the prepared Secret ownerless with the create's data.
- Found by w5/134's quality review (2026-10-07). It predates w5/134: at HEAD the stamped v1 already lost to any stale revision above 1.
