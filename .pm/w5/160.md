# A Hobby workspace's billing email is not checked while the identity provider is down

Why: the Hobby rule "billing email must match the account email" is skipped whenever the account email can't be read, so during a Kratos outage any billing email is accepted. Since w5/m136, `LookupEmails` reports that failure, so the check can refuse instead.

- **Where:** `lego/backend/internal/workspaces/service.go` (the Hobby branch of workspace creation, ~:366). It ignores `LookupEmails`' error, and an unread address skips the match exactly as an unknown one does.
- **Fix:** when the lookup errors and the subject's address did not resolve, refuse with `core.ErrIdentityLookupFailed` (503) rather than accept the submitted email. A subject Kratos answers for without an email keeps today's behavior.
- **Test:** a Hobby create with a failing identity lookup answers 503 and creates nothing. A successful lookup still enforces the match.
- Found by w5/m136's quality review (2026-10-08).
