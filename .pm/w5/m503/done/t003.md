## Objective

Make the route's latency measurable and watched.

## Context

- `bex_api_http_request_duration_seconds` buckets jump from 10 to 30 s.
- Every alert needs a panel ([ADR088](../../../../docs/ADR088-platform-observability-ui.md) §6, `scripts/obs-coverage-check.sh`).

## Steps

1. Add 15, 20 and 25 s buckets (to this histogram or a logs-specific one) and labels for the query shape (window size class, type, filtered or not, direction).
2. Add a latency alert for `GET /v1/logs` with a panel on the platform dashboard, and a promtool unit test.
3. Run `bash scripts/gitops-validate.sh`.

## Files

- lego/backend/internal/logs/ (metrics)
- deploy/gitops/base/rules/alerting_rules.yml
- deploy/gitops/base/rules/*_test.yml
- deploy/gitops/base/values/grafana.values.yaml

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The alert fires in its unit test at the threshold, has a panel, and the gitops validation passes.

## Out of scope

- Alerts for other routes.
