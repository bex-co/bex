## Objective

A remove, a role change below admin, or a leave of the workspace's creator clears `tenants.owner_identity_id` in the same transaction as the membership change, under the membership advisory lock, so nothing can observe a removed creator still bound as owner.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/store/accounts.go:355-370` (`RemoveAccountMember`: another human Admin required, delete membership, clear `owner_identity_id`).
- `lego/backend/internal/store/members.go:36-46`, `:242`, `:280` (`ErrOwnerMember`/`ErrOwnerRole` backstops), the last-admin backstop beside them.

## Steps

1. Share the detach with `RemoveAccountMember` rather than copying it.
2. Remove: delete the membership and, if the subject is the bound owner, clear the binding.
3. Role change below admin: change the role and clear the binding.
4. Leave: the same as remove, with the last-human-Admin check.
5. Retire `ErrOwnerMember`/`ErrOwnerRole`; keep the last-admin backstop.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/store/members.go`, `lego/backend/internal/store/accounts.go`
