# A billing notice re-mails every owner each time one owner can't be reached

Why: a billing notice that fails for one owner, because that owner has no resolvable email or one send failed, is rescheduled whole. Each retry mails every owner again, so the owners who were reached keep receiving the same notice for as long as the failing one fails.

- **Where:** `lego/backend/internal/billing/worker.go` `drainNotifications` reschedules on any `NotifyBilling` error (`FailBillingNotification` with backoff). `lego/backend/internal/notifications/service.go` `notifyBilling` sends to every owner each attempt and joins the per-owner errors. Check whether `ClaimBillingNotifications` caps attempts. If it doesn't, an owner who can never be resolved, such as one who deleted their Kratos identity, repeats the notice indefinitely.
- **Fix:** record the owners each notice reached and mail only the rest on a retry, or treat an owner with no address as permanently skipped (logged) rather than retryable, keeping retries for transient send failures.
- **Test:** a notice whose second owner has no address mails the first owner once across two worker ticks; a transient send failure is retried for its owner only.
