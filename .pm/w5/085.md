# A canceled release's in-flight pre-deploy finishes while its row reads canceled

Why: canceling a release whose pre-deploy Job is running leaves that Job to finish. That's deliberate: a half-run migration is worse than a finished one, and the step runs with `BackoffLimit 0` for that reason. But `TransitionDeploy` settles the row's `pre_deploy_status` to `canceled` at close, and the reconciler projects pre-deploy outcomes only onto open rows. So the deploy page says "Pre-deploy command canceled" for a migration that ran to completion. This holds whether the release ended by user Cancel or by suspend (w5/m114).

Seen on the w5/m114 dev-5 replay, 2026-10-05: Job `predeploy-…-m114-suspend-gen-3` showed `succeeded=1` after its deploy closed canceled.

## Fix (~45m)

1. After a close, project the operator's recorded verdict for the row's own generation (`status.preDeploy.generation == row.generation`, succeeded or failed) onto the closed row. Keep `canceled` only for a step that was actually stopped.
2. Or decide that canceling stops the Job, and record that trade-off in ADR004.
3. Test: cancel during a running pre-deploy, let the Job succeed, and expect the closed row to read `succeeded`.
