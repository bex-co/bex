## Objective

After the creator is removed, demoted or leaves, their next session neither regains admin on the old workspace nor ends up with no workspace.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/api/tenancy.go:141-183` (`EnsureTenant`, `TenantForOwner`, the admin re-grant), ADR024 `:130-135`.
- `core.Base`'s default-workspace shortcut that skips the membership round-trip (`lego/backend/internal/core/base.go`, `resolveWorkspaceUncached`), and the resolver caches m102 invalidates on leave.
- The OpenFGA admin tuple for the old workspace.

## Steps

1. With the binding cleared, `TenantForOwner` misses and onboarding creates a new personal workspace (per t001); verify the old workspace is never re-granted.
2. Remove the old workspace's FGA tuples for the subject in the same flow as a normal member removal; invalidate the resolver caches.
3. A demoted creator keeps the new role and no admin tuple.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/api/tenancy.go`, `lego/backend/internal/core/base.go` (if the shortcut needs it), the members FGA writes

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Integration test: after removal, three session requests in a row keep the subject out of the old workspace and inside a new one; after demotion, the role stays.

## Outcome (2026-11-13)

With the binding cleared, `TenantForOwner` misses and onboarding mints a new personal workspace; t008 added the race guard (onboarding re-reads the binding after a grant it wrote, `tenantService.personalTenant`). `TestRemovingTheCreatorNeitherRestoresAdminNorStrandsTheAccount` runs three session requests through fresh tenant services: each lands in the same new workspace, the old workspace's row stays gone and no admin is re-granted there. `TestDemotingTheCreatorInTheStoreEndsTheBinding` covers the demoted creator.
