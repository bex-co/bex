## Objective

Make every writer produce exactly the templated text, and keep a stall's hint from appearing and disappearing between reconcile passes.

## Context

- `detectBoundPort` and `detectPrivatePrimary` return text only on passes where `dueUnreadyScan` (once per 15 s) lets them scan. The in-flight rollout requeues every 5 s (`app_controller.go` near `:3784-3797`). So the stall text loses its hint on most passes.
- `r.portScanned` and `r.portCandidate` already keep per-App scan state in memory.

## Steps

1. Format each hint and cause through `FormatDeployReason` at its writer, keeping the English byte-identical.
2. Keep the last scan's hint per App and rollout, replaying it on passes that do not scan. Clear it when the rollout's pod changes or the scan finds the port answering.
3. Tests: each writer's text parses to its code; three consecutive passes without a scan keep the same stall text.
