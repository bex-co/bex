## Objective

Give every bex-authored sentence that rides a stall's `{hint}` tail or a `build_never_started` `{cause}` its own template, and parse such a tail into its sentences.

## Context

- `lego/types/v1alpha1/deploy_reasons.go`: m329's mechanism (templates, `FormatDeployReason`, `ParseDeployReason`) and m336's `{hint}` tail on the probe-stall templates, with single-token parameters.
- The hint sentences: `imagePortStallHint` (`app_controller.go:6124-6133`), the fallback-HTTP hint in `rolloutStallDiagnosis` (`app_controller.go` near `:6048`), `detectBoundPort` and `noOpenPortMessage` (`port_detect.go:138-210`), `detectPrivatePrimary` (`private_ports.go:233`, `:250`).
- The queued causes: `admitBuild` (`app_controller.go:1563`), the slot-race message (`:1253`), the capacity wait (`:1308`, whose Kubernetes tail stays English) and the datastore waits from `prepareSourceBuildEnv`.
- The gRPC stall, `probeStallText`'s "the configured health check" target (`app_controller.go` near `:6441`).

## Steps

1. Add a template per hint and cause sentence, each starting with its leading space where it is appended, with single-token parameters.
2. Add `ParseDeployReasonParts(tail)`, which consumes the tail as a sequence of templated sentences and returns the parts plus any unparsed remainder.
3. Add the gRPC stall's waiting and failed templates.
4. Extend `TestDeployReasonsRoundTrip` and the most-specific-first test to the new templates.

## Files

- `lego/types/v1alpha1/deploy_reasons.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/gqlutil/deploy_reason_test.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every new template formats and parses back to its code and parameters.
- [ ] A tail of two hint sentences parses into two parts in order; an unknown trailing sentence is returned as the remainder, unchanged.
- [ ] No existing sentence parses to a different code than before.

## Out of scope

- Kubelet and Kubernetes text inside a cause (it is not bex's)
- Rewording any English sentence

## Result

- **2026-11-12 (loopx w5):** done. 4 `probe_other_*` stall codes (a check that is neither a GET nor a TCP connect), 15 port-hint codes (`hint_image_default_port` … `hint_no_open_service_port`, each starting with its space) and 11 queued-cause codes (`queued_build_limit` {noun}/{active}/{limit}, `queued_cluster_full`, `queued_slot_taken`, `queued_database`, `queued_datastore`, `queued_build_credential`, `queued_pull_credential`, `queued_capacity` {detail} and three build-package variants). `ParseDeployReasonParts` reads a tail sentence by sentence and returns the remainder. Hint and cause templates are never tried at the top level, so every stored sentence parses as before: the review checked 913 sample sentences through the old and new parsers, and only the gRPC stall changed, from no code to `probe_other_*`.
