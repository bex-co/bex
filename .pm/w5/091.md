# Every env and secret-file projection carries its source revision

Why: only the revision-aware env patch owns its Secret by source revision. Every other writer projects with a plain upsert, so a stale projection can land over a newer one, and a restore's Secret write can overwrite a projection that landed after its CAS.

- **Today** (`lego/backend/internal/secrets`): `projectCASEnv` annotates `<service>-env` with `app.bex.co/env-source-revision`, and its rollback acts only while that revision owns the object. `projectEnv`, `projectFiles` and `compensateEnvironment` call `upsertSecret`, which neither checks nor updates the annotation.
- **Gaps:**
  - writer A commits v5, writer B commits v6 and projects it, then A projects its v5 map over B's; the store lists B's value until the next write;
  - the window w5/m119 left: a restore's Secret write can overwrite a projection a newer writer landed between the restore's CAS and that write (ADR013).
- **Fix:** project every env and files Secret through one revision-owned primitive (`projectCASEnv` generalized by kind: path, Secret name, annotation, App reference). It refuses an older revision and updates under resourceVersion preconditions. `compensateEnvironment` then rolls back only a Secret its write still owns.
- **Test:** the A/B stale-projection order, and a restore racing a newer writer's projection.
- **Size:** likely more than an hour (env, files, create-time seeding, deletes); promote when taken.

Source: w5/m119 (2026-10-05).
