## Objective

An App spec change waits seconds, not a minute, for its pass at production-like load.

## Context

- t002's § Findings.
- Candidate fixes (choose by the evidence, not all): more App workers (`lego/operator/config/manager/manager.yaml:181`; controller-runtime never runs one App twice at once), fewer API reads per pass, spaced or jittered idle requeues, or handling a spec change (such as an Ingress repoint) ahead of observation-only work.

## Steps

1. Implement the fix t002's evidence supports, with the reason recorded in the manifest or code comment beside it.
   - **Done 2026-11-19.** The stopping manager hands its lease back (`withLeaderElection` in `cmd/manager/main.go`, `LeaderElectionReleaseOnCancel`), so the next one leads within the election's 2 s retry period instead of after the 15 s lease. An App mid-change when a manager starts (deleting, an unobserved generation, a build or rollout in flight) is queued at the default priority ahead of the startup sweep's `LowPriority` replay (`inFlightFirstAtStart`, `internal/controller/startup_priority.go`, wired beside `For` in `SetupWithManager`). `BEX_APP_RECONCILE_WORKERS` stays 2: the sweep no longer holds a change, and its settled Apps are not waiting on anything a tenant sees.
   - Not changed here: the second operator restart per deploy (`deploy.yml`'s `rollout restart`), filed as its own item because removing it needs a check that no deploy step relies on it to reload a Secret the operator reads at start.
2. Record what the after numbers must show on production (t007 reads them).
