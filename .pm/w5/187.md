# 187 — HMAC ticket flavors that share a secret are not domain-separated

Why: a ticket of one flavor would verify as another that shares its secret if it ever carried the right claims; today only disjoint required claims keep them apart, so a future claim change could make them interchangeable.

- **Source:** security review round 24 (w5/m371, ADR107), agent-plane hardening note H-1.
- **Evidence:** `hmacticket.mac` signs only the claims body (`lego/backend/internal/hmacticket/hmacticket.go`); shellticket and agentsessionticket share `BEX_SHELL_TICKET_SECRET`, sandboxexec and sandboxfiles share `BEX_SANDBOX_EXEC_SECRET`. The sandbox connect tokens already derive per-flavor keys (`sandbox/connect.go`).
- **Fix:** MAC over `flavor + "\n" + body` (or derive a per-flavor key as connect.go does). Tickets live seconds to minutes, so a deploy that accepts both forms for one ticket lifetime is enough; bex-api and the ssh-gateway roll together.
- **Size:** ~1h with tests (a ticket minted for one flavor is refused by the other).
