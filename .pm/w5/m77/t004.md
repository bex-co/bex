## Objective

Remove repeated filesystem setup and JSON/SSE encoding from the in-pod driver hot path. Use one bounded append sink per turn and one encoded frame per published part while preserving redaction, replay, limits, and completion harvest format.

## Context

- `session.ts` currently awaits `appendFile` for each mapped UI-message chunk.
- `stream-hub.ts` repeatedly calls `JSON.stringify`/`frame` for publication, byte accounting, history eviction, replay, and each connected client.
- `/var/log/bex-agent/session.jsonl` remains the ADR051 recovery artifact and must stay readable before status transitions to succeeded.
- The hub's 4 MiB/4096-part history and 1 MiB part limit are security/resource bounds, not tuning suggestions.

## Steps

1. Create one mode-0600 append handle or bounded write stream for each turn and close/flush it before terminal status is persisted.
2. Preserve the 16 MiB session-log cap, record shape, redaction pass, truncation evidence, and cleanup behavior.
3. Represent hub history as the sanitized part plus its single encoded SSE frame and byte length.
4. Reuse the encoded frame for accounting, replay, eviction, and fan-out without changing public bytes.
5. Cover write/close failures so the turn converges to an honest failed/incomplete state rather than hanging.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] One turn opens one transcript append sink and closes it before successful status publication.
- [ ] Each published part is JSON/SSE encoded once and reused across hub operations and clients.
- [ ] Existing byte/part limits, redaction, timestamps, JSONL harvest shape, and `[DONE]` behavior are unchanged.
- [ ] Disk failure, client backpressure, oversized part, history eviction, and terminal flush tests converge without leaks or hangs.
- [ ] Baseline fixture shows fewer file-open and encoding operations with byte-identical replay.

## Out of scope

- Changing transcript retention or quotas.
- Persisting raw ACP instead of UI-message parts.
