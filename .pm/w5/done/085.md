# A canceled release's in-flight pre-deploy finishes while its row reads canceled

Why: canceling a release whose pre-deploy Job is running leaves that Job to finish. That's deliberate: a half-run migration is worse than a finished one, and the step runs with `BackoffLimit 0` for that reason. But `TransitionDeploy` settles the row's `pre_deploy_status` to `canceled` at close, and the reconciler projects pre-deploy outcomes only onto open rows. So the deploy page says "Pre-deploy command canceled" for a migration that ran to completion. This holds whether the release ended by user Cancel or by suspend (w5/m114).

Seen on the w5/m114 dev-5 replay, 2026-10-05: Job `predeploy-…-m114-suspend-gen-3` showed `succeeded=1` after its deploy closed canceled.

## Fix (~45m)

1. After a close, project the operator's recorded verdict for the row's own generation (`status.preDeploy.generation == row.generation`, succeeded or failed) onto the closed row. Keep `canceled` only for a step that was actually stopped.
2. Or decide that canceling stops the Job, and record that trade-off in ADR004.
3. Test: cancel during a running pre-deploy, let the Job succeed, and expect the closed row to read `succeeded`.

## Outcome (2026-10-06)

Done, with fix 1. Canceling still lets the step finish, and the deploy now records how the step ended.

1. **Operator.** Once a release is canceled, `reconcilePreDeploy` no longer reads its step's Job. This covers a user Cancel and a suspend that ended the rollout, since both stamp the release canceled. The step therefore stayed `Running` on `status.preDeploy` forever. `settleCanceledRelease` now reads the Job until it ends and records `Succeeded` or `Failed` under the canceled release's generation, dated by the Job's own finish. It neither fails the service nor rolls the release. While the Job runs it re-checks on the 30s child-health cadence; the step's pod ending already triggers a pass. The gate and this observer share one helper that turns a finished Job into a verdict, so the gate's verdicts are now also dated by the Job rather than by the pass that read it.
2. **bex-api.** For the release the cancel stamp names, once its recorded step has ended, the reconciler replaces the `canceled` that the close settled onto the still-running step with that verdict. The same statement records the step's `pre_deploy_ended` event, which the close never could (`Store.SettleCanceledPreDeploy`, one CTE). Only a canceled row whose step the close settled qualifies. A repeat changes nothing. A step stays `canceled` when a newer release's step stopped it, or when its Job was gone before anyone read it.
3. **Tests.**
   - Envtests: a step that succeeds after a cancel, one that fails, one ended by a suspend (a parked pass requeues nothing else, so the observer's re-check is what brings the release back), and a canceled first release.
   - The note's own test (cancel during a running step, let the Job succeed, the closed row reads `succeeded`) runs end to end in `TestCancelDuringARunningPreDeployRecordsHowTheStepEnded`, alongside a scope table.
   - Real Postgres: `TestPGCanceledReleaseStepTakesItsVerdict`.
   - Mutants are caught: observer off, read-time dating, either requeue dropped, verdict not set, stamp ignored, the status filter dropped, and the event insert dropped.
4. Contract doc on `status.preDeploy` (regenerated CRD) and ADR004 § Pre-deploy command updated.
