## Objective

A failing test showing a group treating a new same-name service as linked, and restarting it on unlink.

## Context

- Links live in group metadata in the KV store (`lego/backend/internal/envgroups/service.go:225`, `links []string`), stored as whatever identifier the caller used. Blueprint `envVarGroups`/`fromGroup` links by name.
- On unlink, `rollLinked` (`envgroups/service.go:1182`) restarts linked services with `Rollout.Patch(TriggerConfigChange)`.
- w4/183's `unlinkDeletedService` (`:1247`) only handles "service not found".

## Steps

1. Scenario: a Blueprint links group G to "web"; delete "web"; create an unrelated "web". Assert G lists no linked service and that unlinking doesn't restart the new one.
2. Confirm red at HEAD.
