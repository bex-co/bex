# Env-group writes treat a replaced linked service as a failure instead of a stale link

Why: since w5/157 a locked App patch refuses with `core.ErrServiceReplaced` when the service it read was deleted and recreated under its name. Env-group fan-outs handle `ErrNotFound` for a deleted linked service, but not this answer. A group save counts the replaced service as a failed rollout instead of pruning its link, and a group delete stops partway.

- **Where:**
  - `lego/backend/internal/envgroups/patch.go` :327 counts the error as a failed rollout rather than a stale link to prune.
  - `envgroups/service.go` `DeleteEnvGroup`'s `detach` (:1317) aborts after `clearMetaCAS`.
  - A plain delete in the same window fails these paths the same way, per w5/157's quality review.
- **Fix:** treat `core.ErrServiceReplaced` like `core.ErrNotFound` wherever a linked service's patch is judged: prune the stale link, and let the delete continue past it.
- **Test:**
  - a group save whose linked service is deleted and recreated between the save's read and its patch reports no failed rollout and drops the link;
  - a group delete in the same window completes.
- Found by w5/157's quality review (2026-10-07).

## Result (2026-10-07)

- A group save's fan-out (`rollLink`, extracted from the loop) and a group delete's `detach` judge a link once more when its service was deleted, or deleted and recreated, between their read and their patch. That covers `core.ErrServiceReplaced` from w5/157 and Kubernetes' own 404. The App now at that name is rolled or detached only if it mounts the group, and the link is otherwise stale: pruned by a save, ignored by a delete.
- Before, a save counted the service as a failed rollout, and a delete stopped after clearing the group's metadata, leaking its Secrets and store content without a tombstone.
- Tests in `TestAGroupWriteJudgesARecreatedLinkedServiceAgain`:
  - a delete detaches a recreated namesake that mounts the group;
  - a save rolls such a namesake;
  - a save prunes the link when the namesake does not mount the group.
- Mutants: removing either retry fails its test. The plain-deletion half of the retry has no committed test; the review's throwaway test exercised it.
- Left as they are: the single-service verbs (`linkFetched`, `UnlinkService`, `persistCreate`) answer 409 for a replaced service before any group state is saved, which is w5/157's intended behavior.
