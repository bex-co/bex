## Objective

A Kratos batch that fails is reported, not silently dropped. The billing notifier retries owners it could not resolve and skips only owners confirmed to have no address. With Kratos unwired, the billing notifier takes its "identity lookup unavailable" path.

## Context

- `workspaces/kratos.go` `lookupMany` drops failed batches. `LookupEmails` is consumed by workspaces, members, webhooks and notifications.
- `api/server.go` `identityEmailLookup{d.Identities}` is non-nil even when `d.Identities` is nil (`cmd/api/main.go` sets it only with `BEX_KRATOS_ADMIN_URL`).

## Steps

1. Give the lookup an error signal in a shape all four callers can share. The others keep omitting unresolved subjects.
2. `notifyBilling` treats lookup failure as transient for the affected owners.
3. Wire the notifier's `Identities` as nil when Kratos is unwired.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/workspaces/kratos.go`, `internal/api/server.go`, `internal/notifications/service.go`, the members/webhooks/workspaces call sites, tests.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A failed batch retries its owners without re-mailing resolved ones. A confirmed no-address owner is skipped. With Kratos unset, the notice is logged and completed.

## Out of scope

- Changing members/webhooks behavior beyond the signature.
