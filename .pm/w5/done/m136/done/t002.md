## Objective

`notifyBilling` skips subjects in `n.MailedTo` and records each successful send as it happens, so a failure or crash partway does not re-mail anyone already reached.

## Context

- `notifications/service.go` `notifyBilling`, its `billingOwnerStore` capability; `billing/worker.go` `drainNotifications`.

## Steps

1. Extend the notifier's store capability with the record call.
2. Skip already-mailed owners. Record after each successful send. Return an error only when an unreached owner still failed.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/notifications/service.go`, its tests.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Two ticks with one transient failure: the healthy owner is mailed once, and the failing owner is retried alone.

## Out of scope

- Other notification kinds.
