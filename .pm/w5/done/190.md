# 190 — Hosting and obs hardening from round 24: approve binds its plan, Grafana sessions end on removal, retired Postgres logins end their sessions

Why: defense-in-depth gaps the round-24 hosting hunt found that are not exploitable across a role or tenant today.

- **Source:** security review round 24 (w5/m371, ADR107), hosting hardening notes.
- **Blueprint approve:** `ApproveBlueprint` approves the newest planned sync with no `syncId` binding, so a concurrent re-plan by another developer replaces what is approved (same role, not an escalation). Bind the approval to the plan the caller saw.
- **obs.bex.co:** a Grafana session survives removal from the ops workspace for up to 24 h; shorten the session or re-check membership. Confirm the break-glass local login and basic auth are not publicly reachable (deployment test), and set `oauth_allow_insecure_email_lookup=false` explicitly.
- **Postgres:** retiring a login does not terminate its open sessions; a point-in-time restore brings back logins retired after a leak (`postgres/recovery.go`). Terminate on retire, and re-retire after a restore.
- **Size:** each part sub-hour; work them separately.
- **Promoted 2026-11-13** to [w5/m384](../blocked/m384/README.md) by `/loopx w5` (three parts together over an hour; the Postgres part needs a privileged SQL path).
