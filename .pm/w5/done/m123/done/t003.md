## Objective

A served release whose ReplicaSet can't create pods stops holding the newer release, with a diagnosis, and a young unready pod doesn't delay a paid hotfix past a documented bound.

## Context

- `servedWakeBudget` (`app_controller.go:5723-5726`, 5 minutes) is measured by pod age. When the ReplicaSet can't create pods (quota, the image-signing webhook after a key rotation, an admission rule the restored template no longer passes), there is no pod to age.
- Today the newer release, possibly the fix, is held forever. Ready shows "RolloutProgressing" with no diagnosis, and the backend closes the row at 18 minutes with a generic line.
- The check isn't limited to wakes: a paid service whose current pod is young and unready delays a hotfix by up to 5 minutes.

## Steps

1. Measure the wait from the hold's start (or ReplicaSet creation failures), not from pod age.
2. Scope it to wakes, or document the paid-service bound.
3. Surface a diagnosis when pods can't be created.
