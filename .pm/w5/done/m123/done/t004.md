## Objective

The wake, park and hold tests share one harness that can model a cache lagging behind writes.

## Context

- Helpers are spread across `auto_hibernate_wake`, `wake_over_failed_*` and `wake_for_release` tests, with four `mark*` helpers.
- `TestParkingPassNeverWritesDeploying` checks intercepted writes instead of the stored phase. `TestIdleMidRolloutDeferralIsBounded` calls the predicate directly.

## Steps

1. Merge the helpers into one harness with fixture options (interceptors, plus a lagging-cache client).
2. Replace the four `mark*` helpers with one `setDeploymentStatus`.
3. Assert the stored phase in the parking test, and check deferral through a reconcile.
