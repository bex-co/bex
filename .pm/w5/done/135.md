# An ownerless Secret a crashed service create left behind blocks that name for good

Why: create-time preparation writes a service's projection Secret ownerless and adopts it once the App exists. A bex-api crash or restart between the two leaves the Secret ownerless forever: no owner reference means garbage collection never removes it, and since w5/128 every later create of that service name with env vars or files is refused with "still being created or deleted; retry shortly", which no retry fixes.

- **Where:** `lego/backend/internal/secrets/files.go` `createPreparedSecret` treats any ownerless Secret as another create's in-flight preparation (`errSecretNameHeld`).
- **Fix:** take over an ownerless prepared Secret older than the prepare window (its `creationTimestamp` well past the create's timeout), or sweep such Secrets; leave a recent one to the create that wrote it.
- **Test:** an ownerless prepared Secret older than the window is taken over by a new create; a fresh one still refuses it.
- Found by w5/128's quality review (2026-10-07).

**Result (2026-10-07):**

- **An abandoned claim can be taken over.** `vacated`, the generalized `ownedByDeletedNamesake`, lets a create take over an ownerless projection Secret that is:
  - claimed longer ago than any create runs (`preparationWindow`, 6 × `core.CreateSecretsTimeout`), and
  - at a name no App holds.

  Before this, a bex-api crash between prepare and commit left the name refused for good. The takeover replaces the crashed create's map.
- **Dating the claim.**
  - `prepareProjection` stamps `app.bex.co/prepared-at` on its claim, a takeover included, because a taken-over Secret keeps its first `creationTimestamp`. Adoption removes it.
  - Secrets from before claims were dated are dated by their creation, but only if they carry the leg's projection revision. Env-group Secrets, ownerless by design, are never a create's.
  - A Secret of unknown age, one with any other owner reference, or one at a live App's name is never taken over.
- **The window is enforced.** `writeNewApp` now runs prepare to commit under `core.CreateSecretsTimeout` (2m). Before, REST and MCP-stdio handlers had no deadline, so the window rested on an assumption.
- **Simplify.**
  - Quality found the unenforced window and the `creationTimestamp` fallback accepting env-group Secrets, and filled two test gaps.
  - Reuse fixed the takeover comment this change made stale, paired `setPreparedAt` with its reader, and moved the deleting-Secret refusal ahead of the App lookup.
  - Efficiency confirmed no added calls.
- **Tests.**
  - `TestACreateTakesOverAnAbandonedPreparation` has seven cases: abandoned (with the crashed map replaced and the claim re-dated), abandoned before claims were dated, still preparing, claimed recently but created long ago, a live App's name, no create's Secret, and another owner.
  - The takeover and adoption tests check the claim date.
  - `TestACreatesPreparedSecretsRunWithinTheirBound` checks the create timeout.
  - Nine mutants each fail a test. The secrets, apps, api and core suites and `make lint` pass.
- **Follow-up.** w5/148: a crashed create's other projection leg outlives the service that takes its name.
