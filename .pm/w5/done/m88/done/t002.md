## Objective

Route each existing terminal path through the same timing/outcome ownership contract, including failures outside the Completer.

## Context

- agentsession.ModelAuthFailer calls FinalizeAgentSession outside the Completer's observeTurn sites.
- SetAgentSessionFailure, cancellation, and AbandonAgentDispatch settle sessions through other paths.
- m85's exact-turn/generation guards must protect the timing facts as well as lifecycle state.

## Steps

1. Wire completion, driver failure, model-auth rejection, cancellation, dispatch failure/recovery, and lost-sandbox recovery to the shared terminal fact.
2. Return whether the guarded terminal transition actually occurred, along with its immutable turn timing/outcome, to the observation path.
3. Treat no-op retries, stale generations, and prompt-less lifecycle transitions explicitly.

- `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/completion.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every named terminal path supplies the correct turn identity, outcome, and available timestamps.
- [x] A losing CAS or repeated callback produces no second observation.
- [x] Stale sandbox reports cannot alter or time a newer turn.

## Out of scope

- Changing tenant-visible phase/error semantics or the model-auth fast-fail policy.
- Replacing the durable dispatch mechanism shipped by m85.
