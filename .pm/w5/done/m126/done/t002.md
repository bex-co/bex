## Objective

For each service the Blueprint would create, preview runs the create plan its apply runs (`planNewApp`) and reports its refusal at the service's path.

## Context

- `apps/service.go` `planNewApp` checks, in order: store name (display names included), the custom-domain gate, claimed hosts, the registry credential, create-time secrets, and a server-side dry-run of the exact App (CRD rules, count cap).
- The stack create builds its request through `specFromCreate` and `createFromStack`.

## Steps

1. Build each new service's App as `createFromStack` would, without writing.
2. Run `planNewApp` under the preview's context; map its refusal to a located validation error.
3. Leave existing services to t004's change and protection checks.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/apps/blueprint.go`, `deploy.go`, `service.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A Blueprint with a taken service name, a claimed host, an unknown registry credential or a CRD-invalid field previews with that service's refusal and the apply's code.
- [x] The preview writes nothing and records no allowed audit row.

## Out of scope

- Datastores (t003); stack-wide gates (t004).
