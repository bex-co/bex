# 195 — An App outside every workspace (`default/hello-static`) still serves on the platform domain

Why: ADR043 says workspace = namespace and the fleet migrated on 2026-07-28, but production's `default` namespace still holds a static site that no API, dashboard, billing or quota sees, and nothing reports such an App.

- **Evidence (production, read-only, 2026-11-19):** `kubectl get apps.app.bex.co -A` lists 27 Apps; 26 are in workspace namespaces and one, `default/hello-static` (`type: static_site`, `spec.image: hello-static:demo1`, created 2026-07-12 by `kubectl apply`, no workspace labels), is `Running` and serves `https://hello-static.onbex.co` (200) from the object-store origin, with an Ingress and an ExternalName Service in `default`. bex-api prunes orphaned App CRs only in workspace namespaces (`lego/backend/internal/workspaces/service.go`). Tests use its names only as fixture strings (`lego/backend/internal/logs/shipper_attribution_test.go`).
- **Recommendation (the proposal's, not a user decision):** delete it. It is a demo, and deleting it removes only its public URL. First check that nothing outside the repository links to `hello-static.onbex.co` (search the docs site and public pages), then `kubectl -n default delete app hello-static` and confirm the operator's finalizer removes its Ingress and Service.
- **Guard:** add a check that reports any App CR outside a workspace namespace (an alert on the operator's App metrics, or a `gitops-validate`-style production check), so a hand-applied App cannot hide again.
- **Done when:** production has no App outside a workspace namespace, and the check reports one in a test. ~45m.

## Outcome — DONE 2026-11-19

- **Where it came from:** hand-applied with `kubectl apply` at 2026-07-12T23:18Z (a `last-applied-configuration` annotation, no workspace label), 16 minutes after `3277fa527` wired the static-site store into the operator Deployment. It was w1/m21's production smoke test: its page says "Render-parity `static_site` · w1/m21", and its spec carries the smoke test's `/old` redirect and `X-Frame-Options` header. w1/m57, w4/m113 and w8/m123 later read it as a live fixture; all three are done, the shipper test uses its names only as strings, and `registry-migration-readiness.sh` excludes unlabeled Apps.
- **Nothing depended on it:** its only host was `hello-static.onbex.co` (one Ingress; no other Ingress, ConfigMap or App named it, and no App shared its name or `hello-static/` object-store prefix). Loki held 10,187 requests over the 30 days before deletion: none carried a `Referer`, and 83% answered 404/405/503 to scanners probing `/.env`, `/.git/config`, `/login` and `/actuator/*`. The rest were one-off page fetches by crawlers (Censys, Netcraft, Palo Alto) and a few browsers; no client fetched it on a schedule. A web search found no page linking it.
- **Deleted 2026-11-19T18:52Z:** `kubectl -n default delete app hello-static`. The finalizer finished in about 10 s: the Ingress, Middleware, ExternalName Service and TLS Secret are gone, a purge Job deleted the four `hello-static/rev-1/` objects, and `https://hello-static.onbex.co/` answers 404. Production now has 33 Apps, every one in its workspace's namespace. The manifests and the four published files were saved outside the repository first. The saved App cannot be re-applied as is: the current CRD refuses a `static_site` with `spec.image` (the finalizer's writes logged that refusal and retried through it).
- **Guard:** the operator exports `bex_app_placement_outside_workspace{namespace,name,workspace}` for every App whose namespace is not its `app.bex.co/workspace` label (`lego/operator/internal/controller/app_placement_metrics.go`, metadata-only list at scrape time) and `bex_app_placement_observe_errors_total`. `AppOutsideWorkspace` (warning, 30m) names such an App and `AppPlacementObservationFailing` says when the listing fails (`deploy/gitops/base/rules/alerting_rules.yml`); the bex-operator scrape keeps `bex_app_placement_*`, and Platform availability graphs both. `TestAppPlacementMetrics*` and `rules/app_placement_alerts_test.yml` report a hand-applied App in `default`, a mislabeled one and a failed listing.
