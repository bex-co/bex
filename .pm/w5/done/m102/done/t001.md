## Objective

Settle the Leave contract before writing it: the exact refusal set, the authorization it needs (it is not an admin action), what happens to the caller's resolved workspace afterwards, and what Render's own Leave semantics are as far as the pinned artifacts show.

## Context

- `w5/m101` refuses self-removal through `members.Remove`; this verb is its replacement path.
- Authorization shape: unlike every other member verb, Leave is not `can_manage` — the caller acts on their own membership. The nearest precedent is `AcceptInvite` (`lego/backend/internal/members/service.go:729`), gated as "an authenticated, onboarded caller".
- The owner rule from m101 applies here too: an owner cannot leave, because `tenants.owner_identity_id` would strand them (ownership transfer is deferred, `.pm/FUTURE-MAYBE.md`).
- Post-leave resolution: `tenantService.Tenant` / `InvalidateTenant` (`lego/backend/internal/api/tenancy.go:357`, `:413`) — the w5/m13 live finding (blank workspace switcher for 30 s after a self-delete) is the exact failure to avoid.
- Render evidence: `docs/render-artifacts/team-members.graphql`, `docs/render-artifacts/workspace-family-walk.md`.

## Steps

1. Write the decision into the milestone README (or a short `docs/render-artifacts/` note): refusal set, authorization gate, audit verb name, cache invalidation, landing behavior.
2. Check the pinned Render artifacts for Leave and record what is and is not capturable from bex's tier.
3. Decide the wire shape: a dedicated verb (`leaveWorkspace`) rather than an overloaded `removeWorkspaceMember(subject: me)` — and record why.
4. Confirm the seat-usage formula and any billing coupling react correctly to a leave (a freed seat).
