# Agent-session dispatch is not crash-safe across a bex-api restart

Why: a session created while bex-api rolls can strand — the OpenSandbox sandbox gets created but the replica dies before `RecordAgentSessionDispatch` writes `sandbox_id`, so the driver's every credential mint is denied (`currentSandboxCaller` needs `podName == sandbox_id+"-0"`) and the session burns its 30 m turn timeout with no failure reason and no recovery.

Observed live 2026-08-30 during the w5/m83 E2E: `ags-da9p720k98cs738k20c0` (repo `bex-co/web-beancount`) — three bex-api ReplicaSets churned within ~10 min of an Argo sync; the session's row kept `sandbox_id=''` while pod `271ec9ce-…-0` ran and minted `denied` every ~3 s. Canceled manually.

Sketch of a durable fix (milestone-sized, don't do inline): make dispatch recoverable — either write a pre-dispatch intent row (sandbox name is deterministic enough to adopt on restart) and have a `core.Poll` worker reconcile `creating` sessions with no `sandbox_id` after a grace (adopt the sandbox by session-label lookup, or terminalize with an actionable `failure_reason` per w5/m80's standard), or make create→dispatch idempotent and re-runnable. Also worth a metric for `creating`-without-sandbox age.

## Disposition — 2026-09-06

Promoted to **w5/m85** after current-code triage confirmed the gap. Recovery requires durable dispatch intent, generation/state guards, polling and orphan cleanup; a timeout-only patch is insufficient.
