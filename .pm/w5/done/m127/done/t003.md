## Objective

Every env and secret-file projection goes through the primitive, stamped with the revision its write committed.

## Steps

1. Route the batch patch's env and files legs, the single-map setters (through `updateMapCAS`'s `mapWrite`), and `materializeFiles`.
2. Have create-time `prepareProjection` record its revision.
3. Delete `projectEnv` / `upsertSecret` once nothing calls them.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A writer that projects an older revision after a newer one leaves the newer projection.
