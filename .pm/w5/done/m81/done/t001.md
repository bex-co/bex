## Objective

Add Prometheus histograms for how long an agent-session turn runs and how long a session takes to reach a terminal phase, extending the two existing counters. These are the signals that would have made the ~192s bad-key turn visible without a pod-level investigation.

## Context

- Existing metrics: `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/completion_metrics.go` has only `bex_agent_session_status_reads_total{outcome}` and `bex_agent_session_terminal_convergences_total{reason}` — no duration histograms.
- Turn timing exists in the store: `agent_session_turns` has `created_at` + `completed_at` (`store/agentsessions.go`), surfaced as `TranscriptTurn.CreatedAt/CompletedAt`. Terminal timing derives from session-row `created_at`/`updated_at`.
- The Completer (`completion.go` `Reconcile`/`finalize`) is where a turn/session becomes terminal — the natural emit point.
- Steer and rehydrate create additional turns; the histogram must attribute per-turn duration without double-counting the session-level convergence.

## Steps

1. Define `bex_agent_session_turn_duration_seconds` (per-turn `completed_at - created_at`) and `bex_agent_session_terminal_convergence_seconds` (create→terminal), with buckets spanning sub-second to minutes.
2. Emit them from the Completer at turn/session terminalization, labelled by terminal outcome (completed/failed/canceled) — reuse the existing `terminalConvergenceReason` labelling where it fits.
3. Ensure steer/rehydrate turns each record their own turn duration once, and the session-convergence histogram records once per session.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/completion_metrics.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/completion.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Turn-duration and terminal-convergence histograms are defined with sensible buckets.
- [ ] They emit at the Completer's terminalization point, labelled by outcome.
- [ ] Steer/rehydrate turns are counted once each; session convergence once per session (no double-count).
