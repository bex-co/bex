## Objective

Add a `Tracker` method that patches the App with `client.MergeFromWithOptimisticLock`. On a conflict it re-reads the App and runs the whole sequence again: snapshot, mutate, stamp, patch. It opens the deploy row once, after the patch that lands.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/rollout/rollout.go` `Tracker.Patch`: `MergeFrom`, no lock, shared by every App-spec writer.

## Steps

1. Add the locked variant beside `Patch`, with a bounded number of retries.
2. Keep `Patch` unchanged for its other callers.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/rollout/rollout.go` and its tests

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A conflict re-reads the App and re-runs the mutate. The landing patch carries the mutate's result applied to the fresh App.
- [ ] One deploy row per successful call.

## Out of scope

- Moving other App-spec writers onto the lock.
