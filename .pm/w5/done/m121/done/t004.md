## Objective

Self-hosters can attach domains under their own parent domain without weakening protection of platform hosts.

## Context

- `platformHostReserved` reserves the dashboard's whole parent domain. A self-hoster with `BEX_DASHBOARD_URL=https://bex.acme.com` can't attach `www.acme.com` or point a maintenance URI at it.

## Steps

1. Document the current behavior and the options, e.g. reserving the exact platform hosts plus explicitly configured zones.
2. If the change affects production behavior, get the user's decision, then implement the chosen option.
