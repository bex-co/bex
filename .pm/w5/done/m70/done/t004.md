## Objective

Surface the t003 `credits` block on the Billing section, learning from Render's billing page: a "Credits remaining $X" stat card (hidden entirely at zero/absent), a "Credits applied −$X → amount due $Y" line in the invoice-preview card, and an earliest-expiry note ("$20 of it expires 2026-11-15") when any grant has one.

## Context

- GraphQL types: regenerate via the offline codegen path (`dashboard/CLAUDE.md` § Offline codegen) and splice per-feature.
- Components: `dashboard/src/features/usage/components/usage-page.tsx` (stat/preview cards), `billing-onboarding.tsx` (gate copy).
- **Gate copy caveat (recorded 2026-08-17):** credit does NOT bypass the ADR046 payment gate — a credit-holding cardless workspace is still 402-gated. The onboarding/payment-required copy must say a card is still required even with credit, or "why must I add a card when I have $50 credit" becomes the first support ticket. Credit-first semantics belong to ADR071 and are out of scope.
- All strings through `useTranslations()` with `usage.*` keys, en + zh.

## Steps

1. Splice the `credits` field into the billing GraphQL query + generated types (offline codegen reconcile).
2. Stat card in the Billing section: render only when `credits.available > 0`; format from `currency`.
3. Invoice-preview card: applied-credit + amount-due line (display-side arithmetic from the credits block, per the t003 caveat); earliest-expiry note from `grants[].expiresAt`.
4. Extend the onboarding/payment-required copy with the card-still-required-with-credit sentence (en/zh).
5. Component tests: card hidden at zero/absent, shown with balance, expiry note, copy present.
