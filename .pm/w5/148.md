# A crashed create's other projection leg outlives the service that takes its name

Why: since w5/135 a create takes over the abandoned prepared Secret of the leg it seeds (env or files). A crashed create may have prepared both legs, and the leg the new create does not seed stays behind:
- its store map persists (store-off), so the new service inherits the crashed create's keys on its first write;
- or, once the maps are purged, its ownerless Secret keeps the crashed data at a higher revision, and `projectSource` treats an ownerless Secret as the App's own. A later `SetEnvVar` answers OK while the projection stays the stale one.

A create that seeds nothing proceeds past both legs.

- **Where:** `lego/backend/internal/secrets/projection.go` around :154–168 (`projectSource` takes an ownerless Secret as the App's own) and `files.go` `PrepareCreateSecrets` (only seeded legs are touched).
- **Fix:**
  - In `projectSource`, treat an ownerless Secret that `abandonedPreparation` flags as a predecessor and replace it, as w5/118 does for a deleted App's.
  - In `PrepareCreateSecrets`, `abortProjection` an unseeded leg whose Secret is vacated, so the new service starts clean.
- **Test:** a crashed create with both legs, followed by a create seeding only files. The env leg's stale Secret and map are gone, and a later env write projects.
- Found by w5/135's quality review (2026-10-07). Store-on deployments are covered by the apps row's UNIQUE (tenant, name), which holds the name while the create's row exists.
