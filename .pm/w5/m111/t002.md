## Objective

Count a pod at a step only if it reported recently, not because the 5-minute lookback carried its last sample forward.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/usage/service.go:1132` `instanceSecondsQuery`: `sum(count_over_time((max by (pod) (container_memory_working_set_bytes{M}))[3599s:15s]))`.
- The Metrics page solved the same problem with `instanceLivenessWindow` (`metrics/source.go:257-262`, 90s); range selectors have no lookback.
- Reviewer candidate `sum(count_over_time((max by (pod) (count_over_time(container_memory_working_set_bytes{M}[30s])))[3599s:15s]))` read 315 and 3750 on the fixtures, with or without stale markers.
- Alternative: `track_timestamps_staleness: true` on the cadvisor job. That also fixes the Metrics page, but it is a platform GitOps change and needs Prometheus ≥ 2.48 plus a check of cAdvisor's timestamp behavior.

## Steps

1. Implement the liveness-gated query. Prefer it over the scrape-config change, because billing shouldn't depend on scrape config. Choose the inner window so one missed 15 s scrape doesn't drop a step, and justify it in the comment.
2. Optionally file the scrape-config change as a separate w5 note.
3. Keep w4/m173 t001's guarantees: per-pod `max by (pod)`, Pending pods not billed, and the `[3599s:15s]` window boundary.
