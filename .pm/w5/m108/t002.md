## Objective

Make the handled manual intent durable without relying on display-history entries or the latest cancellation slot.

## Context

- The selected design must survive Job deletion and history eviction with bounded state.
- Status/spec writes can race with a new trigger or controller crash; creation and cancellation remain idempotent.

## Steps

1. Choose a minimal durable acknowledgement of the handled intent and define the exact persistence ordering around creation, terminal observation and cancellation.
2. Protect a fresh RunAt from an older reconcile acknowledgement and avoid recording settlement before execution is safely accounted for.
3. Update types, deepcopy and CRD generation as required; preserve existing preemption and cancellation intent behavior. Keep backend ownership of `spec.runAt`; do not erase a trigger to acknowledge it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The failing eviction/cancellation-slot reproduction passes with no Job recreation.
- [ ] State remains bounded and a fresh manual trigger is not accidentally suppressed.
- [ ] Crash windows and status conflicts cannot discard an unhandled valid intent.

## Out of scope

- Unbounded tombstones; API history-retention changes; new scheduling policy.
