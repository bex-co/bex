## Objective

Verify durable settlement across upgrades and lifecycle transitions using current and legacy Apps.

## Context

- Existing Apps lack any newly added settlement marker; many retain an old RunAt.
- Completed/failed manual runs should not become replayable when their backing Jobs disappear either.

## Steps

1. Define a conservative compatibility disposition for existing Apps with terminal history, active Jobs, missing Jobs and already-evicted history.
2. Exercise controller restart, status conflict, suspend/resume and a newer trigger racing with an older cancellation.
3. Use a disposable dev-5 service to observe cancellation, more than ten later runs/history eviction, later cancellation and a fresh successful trigger; clean up.
