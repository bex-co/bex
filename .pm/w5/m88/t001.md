## Objective

Make timing depend on the accepted turn and its actual running/terminal transitions rather than unrelated session updates.

## Context

- AgentSessionTurn already stores CreatedAt and CompletedAt; AgentSession.UpdatedAt also changes for pin, archive, and lifecycle writes.
- Completer duration observations currently subtract record.UpdatedAt.
- Dispatch acceptance and recovery already serialize on the accepted turn.

## Steps

1. Define the precise acceptance, running, provisioning, and terminal timestamp boundaries.
2. Reuse the existing durable turn row and add narrowly scoped fields/migration only where necessary.
3. Write timing facts atomically with the relevant guarded transitions and specify legacy-row and rolling-upgrade behavior.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/store/agentsessions.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/store/agentdispatch.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/store/migrations/`
- `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/service.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Pin/unpin and archive metadata writes cannot alter a turn's timing anchor.
- [ ] New turns have the timing needed for their actual lifecycle; old rows are handled explicitly without guessing precision.
- [ ] A prompt-less resume does not mint a new turn or reset a completed turn's clock.

## Out of scope

- Broad session-state-machine rewrites.
- Native first-token correlation or model-token metering.
