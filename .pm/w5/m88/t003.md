## Objective

Expose correct turn/outcome metrics through the existing Prometheus registry with clear timing boundaries and bounded labels.

## Context

- CompletionMetrics defines running-duration and provisioning histograms plus bounded terminal-convergence counters.
- Internal callback paths and composition may need one shared observer, but normal metric reset/scrape behavior remains Prometheus semantics.
- A turn rejected before running must not be recorded as a zero-duration successful provisioning or running turn.

## Steps

1. Wire the successful transition result to the shared metrics observer and registry.
2. Emit measured running and acceptance/provisioning durations only for the applicable paths, plus bounded outcomes.
3. Keep absent/legacy timing explicit; document metric-name compatibility and reset/scrape limitations where semantics change.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Concurrent terminalizers yield one outcome observation in the controlled concurrency proof.
- [ ] Never-running failures do not fabricate a running-duration sample.
- [ ] Collectors use only a closed set of platform labels and register once per process.

## Out of scope

- Per-session or per-workspace metric labels.
- A lossless telemetry ledger, billing-grade event export, or another model proxy.
