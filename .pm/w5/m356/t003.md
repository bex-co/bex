## Objective

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/gqlutil/deploy_reason.go` (`DeployReasonType`, `DeployReasonField`), used by `deploys/graphql.go` and `events/graphql.go` for the failure, stall and cancel templates.

## Steps

1. Add `parts: [DeployReason!]!` to `DeployReason`: the parsed sentences of a `hint` or `cause` parameter, empty when there are none. Add `remainder: String!` for any tail text no template words.
2. Regenerate the dashboard's GraphQL types.
3. Tests: a stall with two hints returns two parts; a cause with an unknown Kubernetes tail returns its remainder.
