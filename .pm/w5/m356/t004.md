## Objective

Word every part in the dashboard's language, after the main sentence.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/deploys/lib/deploy-reason.ts` (`deployReasonText`, m336's `deploys.reasonParam.<name>.<value>` hook) and its callers: the deploy header, the Deploys list and Events.
- `dashboard/src/features/deploys/locales/en.ts` and `zh.ts`. `TestDashboardWordsEveryDeployReason` requires each en message to equal its template.

## Steps

1. Query `parts` and `remainder` wherever the templates are queried.
2. `deployReasonText` renders the main sentence without its hint or cause, then each part worded, then the remainder in English.
3. Add en and zh keys for every new code.
4. Word `restart_wait_timed_out`'s `{wait}` as a localized duration (e.g. 3 小时).
5. Tests: en reads exactly as the API's sentence; zh reads the parts in Chinese; an unknown remainder stays English.
