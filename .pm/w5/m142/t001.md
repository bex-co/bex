## Objective

When an App's instance disappears or goes unready because its node went NotReady/was removed, or its pod carries a `DisruptionTarget` condition, record that cause on the App status (a reason the store can read), distinct from container failures.

## Context

- The operator projects instance failures into App status in `lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go`; container reasons (CrashLoopBackOff, OOMKilled, exit codes) already flow there.
- Pods evicted by node failure or drain get `status.conditions[type=DisruptionTarget]` (reason e.g. `DeletionByTaintManager`, `TerminationByKubelet`); a NotReady node's pods go unready with no container failure.
- Reading node conditions needs a new RBAC marker (`nodes: get;list;watch`) and `make manifests`.

## Steps

1. Detect node-caused loss: pod `DisruptionTarget`, or pod on a node whose Ready condition is False/Unknown or that no longer exists.
2. Record a distinct reason (e.g. `NodeLost`) on the App's availability status; container failures keep their reasons.
3. Add RBAC and regenerate.

## Files

- lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go
- lego/operator/config/rbac/role.yaml

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Envtest/unit: a pod with `DisruptionTarget` yields `NodeLost`; an OOMKilled container on a Ready node does not.
- [ ] Reading nodes fails safe: without the permission, behavior is today's.

## Out of scope

- Platform alerting on node loss (already ops-side).
