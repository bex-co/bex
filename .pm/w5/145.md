# Email-only single identity reads load credentials, and notifications look each recipient up in turn

Why: w5/133 moved the email-only batch reads to `LookupEmails`, which asks Kratos for no credentials. The single reads still go through `GET /admin/identities/{id}`, which always lists credentials. A deploy notification's fan-out makes one such request per recipient, where one batched email read would do.

- **Where:** single readers of email, name or verification only:
  - `lego/backend/internal/notifications/service.go`: the deploy fan-out around :829 and the billing-owner loop around :185, both through `api/server.go` `LookupEmail`;
  - `webhooks/worker.go` around :760;
  - `members/service.go` InviterEmail around :813, the members seam's only `LookupIdentity` caller;
  - `workspaces/service.go`: the Hobby billing email around :366, and CurrentUser through KeyOwner around :685;
  - `cmd/api/main.go`: the opsrole and identityclaims readers around :1036 and :1054.

  No single reader reads `MFAEnabled`.
- **Fix:**
  - Have `identityEmailLookup.LookupEmail` answer from `LookupEmails(ctx, []string{subject})`. A non-UUID subject, a 404 and a failure still come back as a miss.
  - Resolve a notification's recipients with one `LookupEmails` before the fan-out.
  - Point InviterEmail at `LookupEmails`, then drop `LookupIdentity` from `members.IdentityLookup` and its adapter and fakes.
  - Leave the email-verification readers (the auth gate, identityclaims) on the single read until a probe confirms that a list read without an include still returns `verifiable_addresses`.
- **Test:** a deploy notification to three recipients makes one Kratos request, and it carries no `include_credential`.
- Found by w5/133's reviews (2026-10-07).
