## Objective

Make the merged rail behave correctly in the two states the old `<aside>` never had to handle: the ⌘B collapsed icon rail, and small screens.

## Context

- The old rail opted out of both: `className="… hidden w-60 shrink-0 flex-col border-r lg:flex"` — hidden below `lg`, and never part of the `Sidebar` collapse system at all. Its docstring records the small-screen contract: _"Hidden below `lg`, where the header's back link and `/agents` remain the navigation."_ Now that the list lives in `<Sidebar collapsible="icon">`, it inherits that component's collapse and mobile behavior instead.
- Devin's collapsed state: a 52px icon rail keeping nav icons, with the session list **dropped entirely** — sessions have no meaningful icon representation. Evidence `.playwright-mcp/devin-collapsed.png`. Devin also moves the expand trigger into the main content header when collapsed.
- Our `ui/sidebar.tsx` collapses via `group-data-[collapsible=icon]:hidden` (see `service-sidebar.tsx:51` for the established pattern of hiding a contextual block in icon mode).

## Steps

1. Hide the whole `Recent` group in icon mode with the existing `group-data-[collapsible=icon]:hidden` pattern — do not attempt per-session icons or avatars.
2. Confirm the nav groups still render as icons when collapsed, unchanged from every other page.
3. Verify the mobile/`Sheet` sidebar path in `ui/sidebar.tsx`: decide whether the sessions group belongs in the mobile drawer (it reasonably does, since the drawer is the small-screen navigation) and make that call explicitly rather than by default. Record the choice in the component docstring.
4. Ensure `/agents` on a narrow viewport still offers a route to the session list — either the mobile drawer group or the `?view=list` page — so the old rail's "hidden below lg" behavior does not become a dead end now that the in-page rail is gone.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/common/components/dashboard-layout/agent-sessions-nav-section.tsx`
- `dashboard/src/common/components/ui/sidebar.tsx` (read-only unless the mobile path needs a hook)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Collapsed (⌘B / icon mode): nav icons render, the `Recent` group is absent, and no clipped/overflowing session text remains.
- [ ] Expanding restores the sessions group with its list intact.
- [ ] Below `lg`: sessions are reachable — via the mobile drawer or `?view=list` — and the choice is documented in the component.
- [ ] No horizontal overflow or layout shift on `/agents` at 360px, 768px, 1280px.

## Out of scope

- Redesigning the mobile drawer itself.
- Matching Devin's exact 52px rail width — bex's `Sidebar` sets its own.
