# The autoscaler's scale-down window has three more gaps

Why: w5/109 voids a pending scale-down when the signal recovers, but the stabilization stamp still records only when a dip began. It also survives readings it never sees, and an unreadable stamp freezes it, so a scale-down can still commit on one low reading, or never commit.

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/autoscale.go` `applyAutoscaling`:
  - **An unreadable stamp holds forever.** A stamp that fails to parse holds every dip and is never re-stamped, so a sustained dip never scales down. w5/109 at least clears it on the next decision at or above the current count. Fix: treat a parse error as no stamp, then re-stamp and hold.
  - **Depth is not tracked.** A shallow dip (want 4 of 5) stamps the window, and one deep reading after it (want 1) commits the deep count at once. HPA instead commits the highest recommendation seen within the window. Fix: record the highest desired count while the window runs, alongside the stamp, and commit that.
  - **The stamp outlives passes that skip autoscaling.** `desiredReplicas` (`app_controller.go`) calls `applyAutoscaling` only while autoscaling is on and the App is not suspended. A stamp from before a suspend, or before autoscaling was turned off, therefore survives. Once the App resumes after the window, one low reading commits. Fix: clear the stamp whenever autoscaling does not run for the App, or record when it last ran.
- Test: each case holds a dip for a full window from its own start.
- Found by w5/109's quality review (2026-10-07).
