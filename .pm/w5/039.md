# Pre-existing: paid KeyValue backup fails at the snapshot step (Valkey connectivity)

Surfaced 2026-08-04 while enabling ADR050 Tier A encryption in prod. **Not an ADR050 regression** — the encrypt step is downstream of the failure and never runs.

Symptom: a manual `kvbak-red-d9p49kdrtmes73c34ovg` Job (and the never-yet-run scheduled one) fails in the **`snapshot`** initContainer with:

```
Could not connect to Valkey at red-d9p49kdrtmes73c34ovg:6379: Try again
```

Evidence it predates ADR050:

- the KeyValue instance is `Ready` (pod `red-…-0` 2/2 Running), yet
- the scheduled `kvbak` CronJob has no `lastSuccessfulTime`/`lastScheduleTime`, and
- `s3://bex-tfstate/keyvalue/red-d9p49kdrtmes73c34ovg/` has **0 objects** — no KV backup has ever succeeded for this instance.

`Try again` is a DNS temporary-resolution failure, so the likely cause is the backup Job pod not resolving/reaching the Valkey Service — a NetworkPolicy (ADR043 tenant isolation) or Service-name/DNS issue, owned by ADR021 KeyValue backups, not ADR050.

Next steps:

- Check whether the backup Job's pod is permitted to reach the tenant Valkey Service by NetworkPolicy, and that the Service name the snapshot step dials (`<kv-name>:6379`) resolves from the Job's namespace.
- Verify against the next scheduled run (03:20–03:39 UTC slot); if it also fails, fix the connectivity in the operator's kvbak Job wiring (`lego/operator/internal/controller/keyvalue_backup.go` snapshot step) or the tenant NetworkPolicy.
- Once green, confirm the object lands as `*.rdb.gz.age` (ADR050 Tier A is already correctly wired).
