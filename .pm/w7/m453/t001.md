## Objective

Give bex-api one source for how far back each workspace plan may restore a Postgres: 3 days on Hobby, 7 on Pro, Scale and Enterprise. Add the floor a plan change keeps, so an upgrade from Hobby does not backfill the window. Render: "Upgrading from Hobby does not retroactively "backfill" your recovery window. Instead, your existing 3-day window extends to 7 days going forward." bex keeps 30 days of backups in storage, so without a kept floor an upgrade would suddenly offer 7 days of points Render would never have kept.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/core/plan_gate.go` already has the shape twice. `planRetention` is a map with `window`, `shortest` and `upgrades`. `logRetention` and `metricsRetention` are its two instances. `LogFloor` and `LogsVisibleFromAfterPlanChange` are the floor and the no-backfill rule (w9/m389 t004).
- `lego/backend/internal/store/workspaces.go:237` `UpdateTenantPlan` is every plan change's one write. It locks the row and stores `logs_visible_from` (migration `0262_workspace_logs_visible_from`). `WorkspaceLogPlan` (line 271) reads the plan and that floor in one query, and `core.Base.WorkspaceLogPlanOf` (`plan_gate.go:329`) is the reader seam.
- The latest migration on main when this was filed is `0282_sandbox_snapshots`. Take the next free number from `origin/main` when you write it.
- A workspace where no plan applies (local dev, no store, `core.DefaultTenant`) gets no floor, as for logs.

## Steps

1. Add `pitrRetention = planRetention{hobby: 3d, pro: 7d, scale: 7d, enterprise: 7d}`, citing render.com/docs/postgresql-backups and the pricing row (read 2026-11-16). Add exported `PITRRetention(plan)`, `LongestPITRRetention()` (t003's guard needs it) and `PITRRetentionUpgrade(plan)`.
2. Add `PITRFloor(plan, visibleFrom, now)` and `PITRVisibleFromAfterPlanChange(oldPlan, newPlan, visibleFrom, at)`. Write them as generic `planRetention` methods that the log functions also call, rather than copying `LogFloor`.
3. Add migration `<next>_workspace_pitr_visible_from` (nullable `timestamptz` on `tenants`, with a down migration). `UpdateTenantPlan` writes it in the same locked transaction as `logs_visible_from`.
4. Add a reader that returns the plan and the PITR floor in one query, the way `WorkspaceLogPlan` does: a `core.WorkspacePITRPlanReader` and `Base.WorkspacePITRPlanOf`, or one wider reader that returns both floors. Pick whichever keeps the seam smallest. A plain `WorkspacePlanReader` keeps no floor.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Table tests: `PITRRetention` is 3d/7d/7d/7d, and an unknown plan gets the shortest window. `PITRFloor` is `now - window`, or `visibleFrom` while that is later.
- [ ] A Hobby→Pro change at `T` keeps a floor of `T-3d`. That floor lapses once `now-7d` passes it, which happens 4 days after the upgrade. A Pro→Hobby change keeps nothing. Each case is a test.
- [ ] Real-DB store test: `UpdateTenantPlan` stores and clears `pitr_visible_from` on the same transitions, and the new reader returns it.
