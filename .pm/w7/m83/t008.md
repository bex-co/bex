## Objective

Pin the milestone's behavior with tests and guard self-tests that fail against the pre-milestone tree.

## Context

- Two of the six deliverables **are** guards; their value is entirely in the self-test proving they turn red. A guard without a failing case is decoration.
- The metrics need behavioral assertions (a queued build advances `bex_builds_queued`; scheduling it moves the observation to `queue_seconds`), not existence checks.
- `make test` from `lego/operator/` is the CI-enforced suite; the guard self-tests run in their own CI path.

## Steps

1. Test `bex_build_queue_seconds` records the admission→scheduled interval, and the gauges rise and return to zero.
2. Prove both guard self-tests (t003 prewarm toleration, t004 arithmetic floor) turn CI red on a real regression and green on the fixed tree.
3. Test the `BEX_MAX_ACTIVE_BUILDS` ceiling: enforced when set, byte-identical when unset; overshoot deletes the newest.
4. Confirm the alert rules load (t002).

## Files

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/` tests
- the guard self-tests in `scripts/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each DoD clause has a test or self-test that fails against the pre-milestone tree.
- [ ] Both guards are proven red-and-green, not just present.
- [ ] Unset `BEX_MAX_ACTIVE_BUILDS` is asserted byte-identical.
- [ ] No trivial or tautological tests.

## Out of scope

- Live production verification — the DoD is deliberately repo-verifiable (see the milestone README).
