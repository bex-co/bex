## Objective

Finish the lifecycle integration so retry, cancellation, and older in-flight cache writers cannot apply a reset to the wrong release or undo it.

## Context

- The operator has release-generation, build-supersede, cancellation, and retry behavior that outlives a single request or reconcile.
- Cache save is a separate phase and can race with a newer request. The guarantee must be checked against actual ordering, not assumed from request order.

## Steps

1. Drive clear-request retry, operator restart, cancel-before-start, cancel-during-build, and superseding-normal/clear sequences through the release machinery.
2. Fence or order older cache saves according to t001 so they cannot restore the obsolete cache after a successful reset.
3. Ensure repeat reconciliation does not repeatedly clear a cache and later normal deploys do not inherit stale reset intent.
4. Preserve source-build identity, exact-image rollback, and m87's environment revision behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each retry remains associated with its intended reset/release; restart loses no pending intent.
- [ ] A canceled or superseded request cannot clear an unrelated newer build's cache.
- [ ] An older cache writer cannot undo an effective reset.
- [ ] Repeated reconcile and later normal deploys do not replay a completed reset.

## Out of scope

- A general distributed build scheduler or fairness redesign.
- Changing cancellation, rollback, or newest-wins policy beyond this integration.
