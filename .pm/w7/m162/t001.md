## Objective

Define expected archiver coverage for the platform database inventory without deriving expectations only from live targets.

## Context

- [Milestone scope, source evidence and definition of done](README.md).
- PlatformDatabaseBackupStale in deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml compares time() against cnpg_pg_stat_archiver_last_archived_time; an absent series produces no alert. The cnpg-platform-db scrape selects only current primaries for bex-db, kratos-db, hydra-db and openfga-db. w7/m156 proves a separate pg_stat_wal collector failure occurs, but does not prove archiver telemetry is currently absent.
- Complements shipped backup/restore and staleness work. Does not redo restore drills or depend on m156's CNPG upgrade; pg_stat_wal and pg_stat_archiver are explicitly separate signals.

## Steps

1. Inspect the four platform Cluster declarations and current cnpg-platform-db scrape relabeling.
2. Choose a manifest-backed or otherwise independent expected namespace/cluster inventory that survives target loss.
3. Specify coverage semantics for healthy primary replacement, exporter-up/metric-missing, and total discovery loss.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Expected coverage includes bex-db, kratos-db, hydra-db and openfga-db with correct namespaces.
- [ ] The expectation persists when any or every scrape target vanishes.
- [ ] Failover grace and metric-versus-target absence are documented.

## Out of scope

- CNPG upgrades, WAL statistic resets, or changes to backup retention and storage.
- Changing tenant datastore metrics or tenant-facing recovery contracts.
- Treating monitoring absence as proof that stored backups are corrupt or missing.
