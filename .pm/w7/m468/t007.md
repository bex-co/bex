## Objective

Show the two providers work against the real GitLab and Bitbucket on production.

## Context

- Needs, from the user: a GitLab application (gitlab.com → Preferences → Applications, scopes `read_user openid email`, redirect URI `https://auth.bex.co/self-service/methods/oidc/callback/gitlab`) and a Bitbucket OAuth consumer (Bitbucket workspace settings → OAuth consumers, callback URL Dex's callback from t002, permissions Account Email and Read). Their pairs go in `.env` as `BEX_GITLAB_OIDC_*` and `BEX_BITBUCKET_OIDC_*`, then `scripts/gh-secrets.sh`.
- Also needs a GitLab account and a Bitbucket account to sign in with.
- A production pin must carry the milestone: `git merge-base --is-ancestor <the m468 commit> <the sha in the newest "pin platform images to" commit on main>`.
- `scripts/auth-oidc-verify.sh` reports the deployment side.

## Steps

1. Run `scripts/auth-oidc-verify.sh` against production and confirm both providers are enabled and Dex is healthy.
2. On `https://dashboard.bex.co`, check the login, sign-up and account settings pages offer GitLab and Bitbucket with their logos.
3. Sign up with GitLab, sign out and sign back in; do the same with Bitbucket. Check each identity's email and verified state.
4. Delete the test identities, or record why they stay.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both providers sign a person up and back in on production.
