## Objective

Wire the separate static-server and publish/purge identities through GitOps and the operator, roll them into production, verify the full static-site lifecycle, and revoke the old shared credential.

## Context

- The static-server currently reads `static-s3` in `bex-system`; the operator passes `BEX_STATIC_S3_SECRET` to publish/purge Jobs in `BEX_BUILD_NAMESPACE`.
- The production incident showed that a publish Secret missing from `bex-build` fails only when the Job starts.
- Rotation must preserve an already-published site's availability and avoid exposing credentials in logs or git.

## Steps

1. Give static-server and publish/purge independently named Secret contracts and update manifests, configuration, documentation, and validation.
2. Install the out-of-band Secrets in their exact namespaces and roll the platform through the normal signed deployment workflow.
3. Prove existing content remains readable, a fresh revision publishes, and App deletion purges only its static prefix.
4. Re-run the positive/negative bucket-access matrix from the live identities, then revoke the former shared key.
5. Capture non-secret production evidence and rollback results.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Static serving and publishing use different Secret names and different provider identities.
- [ ] Missing either namespace-local Secret fails clearly before or at reconciliation with an actionable condition.
- [ ] A production create/deploy/read/redeploy/delete lifecycle succeeds using only the new credentials.
- [ ] Negative probes prove neither identity can read tfstate or backup buckets, and the server identity cannot write static content.
- [ ] The old shared key is revoked after rollback-safe verification; no secret material appears in git, CI, logs, or evidence.

## Out of scope

- Per-App object-store credentials unless the bucket-scoped two-role model fails the documented threat model.
