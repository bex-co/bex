## Objective

Audit the static-site serving/publish plane as hardened by `w7/m54` plus the manual 2026-07-28 production repair, for privilege-separation and authority defects beyond what m54 already owns. Focus on the publisher/reader S3 identity split, the publish Job's credential reach across namespaces, and residual alias/host-authority questions the PSL waiver did not settle.

## Context

- Two distinct S3 identities by design: the read-only `bex-static-read-s3` (`bex-static` identity, static-server origin) vs. the publisher (`BEX_STATIC_PUBLISH_S3_SECRET` / `bex-static-publish-s3`). Verify they are genuinely separated in prod — the static-server cannot write/purge, the publisher is bucket-scoped to the dedicated static bucket (never `bex-tfstate`), and neither is over-granted.
- The publish Job runs in `BEX_BUILD_NAMESPACE` and pulls the just-built tenant image via `BEX_REGISTRY_BUILD_PULL_SECRET` (the apps-ns `reg-pull-<name>` is unreachable there). Check the build-ns credential's read scope and that a tenant can't coerce the publish Job into pulling/publishing another tenant's content.
- Alias authority: m54 owned "alias authority" and the browser-divergence acceptance; the owner explicitly **waived** PSL inclusion (see `w7/done/m54`). In scope here: residuals *beyond* the waived PSL gate — e.g. can a static-site host claim collide with or shadow another tenant's alias, and is the host→site resolution in the shared static-server authorization-safe across tenants.
- Excluded (m54-owned): the accepted browser divergence itself and the PSL decision — cite, don't re-litigate.
- Related open note `w9/014` codifies the manual prod fixes into gitops; if this audit finds a *security* (not drift) issue in those hand-applied resources, file it here and cross-reference `w9/014`.

## Steps

1. Read the publish/purge Job spec + the static-server origin client; confirm the publisher and reader secrets map to distinct, minimally-scoped S3 identities and buckets.
2. Trace the publish Job's image pull + object writes; probe for cross-tenant content pull/publish and for writes outside the dedicated bucket/prefix.
3. Review host→site resolution in the shared static-server for cross-tenant authorization safety and alias-shadowing beyond the waived PSL scope.
4. Record findings (severity + evidence) into the shared register draft for t006; cross-reference `w9/014` for any hand-applied-resource security finding.
