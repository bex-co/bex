# A Postgres point-in-time recovery window does not follow the workspace plan

Why: Render's plan table gives Hobby workspaces 3 days of point-in-time recovery and Pro and higher 7, while bex offers every paid database whatever its tenant ObjectStore keeps, so the window, the recovery picker and the storage bex pays for all differ from Render's.

- Found by w7/m429 t005, 2026-11-16, mapping every row of Render's plan tables to a bex disposition (`lego/backend/internal/pricing/plan_table_test.go` names this note for the row).
- Render (render.com/pricing comparison table, read 2026-11-16, captured in `docs/render-artifacts/pricing.md`): "Point-in-time recovery window: 3 days | 7 days | 7 days | 7 days" (Hobby, Pro, Scale, Enterprise).
- bex: tenant Postgres backups are kept by the barman ObjectStore's `retentionPolicy: 30d` (`deploy/gitops/charts/barman-cloud-objectstores/tenant-postgres.yaml:32`), the same on every plan. The recovery read reports CNPG's first recoverability point (`lego/backend/internal/postgres/recovery.go` `recoveryWindow`), so the offered window is up to 30 days on every workspace plan.
- To decide in the work: whether bex floors the window bex-api offers by the workspace plan (3 or 7 days, like the log and metrics read floors in `core/plan_gate.go`), shortens the stored retention, or both; and what an existing Hobby database's older recovery points do after the change.
- Done when: REST `GET /v1/postgres/{id}/recovery`, GraphQL, MCP and the dashboard's recovery picker offer 3 days on Hobby and 7 on Pro and higher, a recovery outside the window is refused, ADR009 and ADR018's Backups · PITR row state the rule, and the guard's row moves from `followUp` to `retention`.
