## Objective

Normalize the minimum Stripe invoice/payment/subscription events into the durable lifecycle state with signature verification, event-id deduplication, and reorder-safe transitions.

## Context

- Stripe can retry, delay, and reorder events.
- The webhook must acknowledge quickly; slow notification/enforcement work belongs after durable intake.

## Steps

1. Define the version-pinned event inventory for failure, success, action-required, and subscription status changes.
2. Store the event and normalized transition atomically before acknowledging.
3. Ignore stale transitions using authoritative Stripe timestamps/state rather than arrival order.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/billing/webhook.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/billing/lifecycle.go`
- `scripts/stripe-billing-setup.py`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Valid duplicate and out-of-order events converge without duplicate side effects.
- [ ] Invalid signatures and incompatible event versions fail closed.
- [ ] Accepted webhooks do not block on email or Kubernetes work.

## Out of scope

- Polling reconciliation and enforcement scheduling.
