## Objective

Automatically restore billing-owned restrictions after authoritative payment recovery without overriding independent user or operator intent.

## Context

- Recovery can arrive by webhook or polling.
- A resource manually suspended before or during grace must remain suspended after payment.

## Steps

1. Resolve successful payment/subscription state through the common transition path.
2. Reverse only mutations carrying the billing ownership marker.
3. Clear enforcement state transactionally after resource convergence and audit the result.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/billing/enforcement.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/billing/lifecycle.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/store/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Successful payment restores billing-owned changes exactly once.
- [ ] User/operator changes made independently are preserved.
- [ ] Partial recovery retries safely until converged.

## Out of scope

- Refunding or forgiving invoices automatically.
