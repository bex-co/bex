## Objective

Meter and bill every `muse` request exactly like the existing platform route, from Meta's Responses usage.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/sshgateway/modelproxy/modelproxy.go` enforces `PinnedModel` and reports usage only for platform exchanges; `usage.go` tee-parses Anthropic Messages usage and OpenRouter `usage.cost`.
- Responses streams end with `response.completed` carrying `usage {input_tokens, output_tokens, input_tokens_details.cached_tokens}`.
- Spend caps and the per-session ceiling refuse at the mint (`AGENT_MODEL_SPEND_CAP_REACHED`).

## Steps

1. Enforce the pinned model on Responses request bodies for `api.meta.ai`.
2. Parse Responses usage (streamed and non-streamed), including cached tokens.
3. Compute the customer cost from the t001 price row × markup and record it as `agent_model_cost_microusd` with source `computed`.
