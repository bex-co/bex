# Capture Render's answers where its PITR docs are silent: stalled backups, the refusal wording, a downgrade

Why: w7/m453 applied Render's documented recovery window, but chose three behaviors Render's page does not describe. A live capture would confirm them or turn up drift.

Found by w7/m453 t007 (2026-11-16). render.com/docs/postgresql-backups (read 2026-11-16) and the pinned OpenAPI (`retrieve-postgres-recovery-info`, `recover-postgres`) say nothing about these three. bex's choices are in ADR009 § Backups + point-in-time recovery (w7/m453 bullet):

1. **Stalled base backups.** Suppose the last base backup is older than the plan's floor (more than 3 days on Hobby). bex reports `recoveryStatus: BACKUP_NOT_READY` with no `startsAt`, and refuses recover with 400 until the next backup completes (`recovery.go` `flooredAt`). What does Render report?
2. **Refusal shape.** For a `restoreTime` before the window, bex answers 400 `RECOVERY_TARGET_BEFORE_WINDOW`, "targetTime is before the earliest restore point (<start>)". For one within ten minutes of now, it answers 400 `RECOVERY_TARGET_TOO_RECENT`, "targetTime is within ten minutes of now; choose a time no later than <t>". What status and `message` does Render's `POST /v1/postgres/{id}/recovery` return for each?
3. **Downgrade.** bex narrows the window to 3 days at once on Pro -> Hobby. Does Render?

Gate: a Render account with a paid Postgres on Hobby, plus one on Pro for item 3. Item 1 may not be reproducible on demand.

Done when: each answer is captured in `docs/render-artifacts/` (request, status, body, date), and any drift is fixed or recorded in ADR018 with its sign-off.

**Done in this run, read-only** (GETs on the reference account, no write). The capture is [docs/render-artifacts/postgres-recovery-window.md](../../../docs/render-artifacts/postgres-recovery-window.md).

- **Item 1 is answered, and it was drift.**
  - A paid database suspended on 2026-08-02 had taken no base backup for over 100 days. Render still answers `AVAILABLE`, with a `startsAt` from the window it had when it was suspended (2026-07-25T20:16:52Z). It does not answer `BACKUP_NOT_READY`.
  - Render's `startsAt` on the running paid database was 7 days 20 hours back (2026-11-09T16:27:44Z, read at 12:53:43Z). That is a base backup's time, the newest one at or before the 7-day floor, not now minus 7 days.
- **Fixed in bex** (`lego/backend/internal/postgres/recovery.go` `flooredAt`, tests in `pitr_window_test.go`):
  - Where the plan floor cuts a database's backups, the window starts at the newest completed base backup at or before the floor. This holds on REST, GraphQL, MCP, the dashboard picker and `recover`'s refusal.
  - A database whose backups stopped before the floor stays `AVAILABLE` from its last base backup. ADR009 and ADR018 say so.
- **Still open:**
  - Items 2 and 3.
  - A suspended database's window. Render keeps the 7 days before the suspension. bex counts back from now because it records no suspension time, so it starts at the last base backup.
  - Whether Render recovers a suspended database at all. Only a write can tell.
- **Effect on w7/m453 t006:** its step 1, "no earlier than now minus 3 days", now reads "no earlier than the newest base backup at or before now minus 3 days". A refused recovery to 4 days ago still holds with nightly backups.
