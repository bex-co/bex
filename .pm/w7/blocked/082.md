# Enterprise organizations are not billed per seat, and Billing-only members are not exempt, as on Render

Why: Render's Enterprise orgs with seat-based billing do not charge for a member who holds the Billing role in every org workspace they belong to; bex's organizations (w7/m464) have the two-Billing cap but no seat billing.

- Found by w7/m464 t014, 2026-11-17 (the milestone left it out of scope).
- Render (render.com/docs/organizations, read 2026-11-17): "For Enterprise orgs with seat-based billing: If an org member has the Billing role in every org-managed workspace they belong to, your org is not charged for their seat." The table: Billing + Billing → not charged; Billing + none → not charged; Billing + Developer → charged.
- bex: Enterprise is custom (no catalog rate, `pricing.yaml` has none, ADR030 §2); nothing counts seats. `store.OrganizationBillingHolders` already reads who holds Billing across an org.
- Shape: an inbox note until a user decision on Enterprise terms says seat billing is wanted; then a meter of billable seats per Enterprise org (members holding a non-Billing role in at least one org workspace), shown on the primary's Billing page.
- Done when: an Enterprise org's seat count excludes members whose every org role is Billing, on the API and the Billing page.

## Parked 2026-11-17 (`/loopx w7` triage)

- **The premise holds.** No org seat count leaves out Billing-only members. `members.Service.SeatUsage` (`lego/backend/internal/members/service.go`) counts one workspace's members and pending invites against its limit. `store.OrganizationBillingHolders` (`internal/store/organizations.go`) serves only the two-Billing cap.
- **Why this is not parity work that can ship now.** Render's exemption applies only to "Enterprise orgs with seat-based billing" (render.com/docs/organizations, re-read 2026-11-17). The page does not say which Enterprise orgs have it, so it is a contract term. Render's published plan table charges no plan per seat ("Team seats: 1 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited", Enterprise "Custom", `docs/render-artifacts/pricing.md`). bex's Enterprise plan has no catalog rate, and bex charges no licensed plan fee today (ADR030 §2). So every bex Enterprise org behaves like a Render Enterprise org without seat-based billing, and no member is charged for a seat. Showing a "billable seats" count to orgs that are not billed per seat would be a new divergence, so nothing is implementable before the decision.
- **Gate (the user, `/unblock w7`):** decide whether bex sells Enterprise with seat-based billing. If it does, set the seat rate and decide how a contract marks an org as seat-based. Then a worker builds the meter: billable seats are org members holding a non-Billing role in at least one org workspace, shown on the API and the primary's Billing page. If it does not, sign it off and this note is dropped (an ADR115 Consequences line in place of "a follow-up").
