# A Member's org Settings pending frame draws switches where the page shows status badges, and at phone width the 2FA row grows when it lands

Why: the root `AGENTS.md` skeleton rule asks the pending frame to match the ready page at the same width. For an org Member or Guest, the Security card's skeleton reserves a switch's box, but the page renders a wider "Not required" / "Required" badge. At 390 the badge wraps under "Require two-factor authentication", so everything below moves down when the page lands.

- **Found by:** w7/m464 t013, on production, 2026-11-17 (pin `14eaa71b98e5`). Fixture org `org-dbugoqde7qgs73b346ag` (since deleted), as its Member. Held navigation as in w4/706: `/members` → Settings, `**/graphql` held 4 s.
- **Observed:**
  - At 1440 the switch boxes and the badges sit on the label's line, so the geometry matches (`Security@431` both).
  - At 390 the ready 2FA row puts the badge on its own line under the label. Pending draws an inline switch box.
  - At 390 the frame was also role-less (no "Only Owners can rename" or "Only Owners can change these settings" note, `Security@419` pending against `@443` ready). That part is w4/706, fixed on main by `ef5ecb56f` and not yet live. The badge wrap remains after that fix.
- **Cause:** `dashboard/src/features/organizations/components/organization-route-skeletons.tsx:292-297` passes `control="pending"` (the switch's box) for every role. `organization-security-card.tsx:81` renders `control={canChange ? "switch" : "status"}`. `workspaces/components/login-requirement-row.tsx:47` is `flex-wrap`, so the wider badge wraps at 390.
- **Fix:** when the role is known and is not Owner, the skeleton reserves the status badge's box (`ReservedBadge`, already in that file) instead of the switch's. Owners and an unknown role keep the switch box.
- **Blast radius:** `LoginRequirementRow` also serves Workspace Settings' Two-factor and Google login sections (w7/m465, w7/m466). Their non-admin pending frame was not probed and may have the same mismatch.
- **Done when:** on production at 390, a Member's held `/members` → Settings frame places the Security rows at their ready y. At 1440 the frames still match. A skeleton test asserts the badge box for Member and Guest and the switch box for Owner. ~30m.
- **Related:** w7/m466 t006 owes the org Settings pending-vs-ready comparison. This note is that comparison's finding for the Member/Guest rows.

## Fixed in code, 2026-11-18 (parked for its production check)

- **Fix:** `LoginRequirementRow` has a fourth control, `pendingStatus`: the state badge's own box (`ReservedBadge`, now shared in `common/components/reserved-skeleton.tsx`), with the badge's variant and its "Required" or "Not required" text laid out invisibly. `OrganizationSettingsSkeleton` uses it when the cached org says the caller is a Member or Guest, with the state from that cached org. An Owner and an unknown role keep the switch's box.
- **Blast radius, fixed too:** Workspace Settings' 2FA and Google login skeletons had the same mismatch.
