# The change-plan dialog breaks "Scale" and "Enterprise" mid-word, squeezed by their "per organization" price

Why: at 1440 the dialog's plan cards read "Sca / le" and "Enter / prise", because the card header gives the price all the room once w7/m464 added "per organization" to it.

- **Found by:** w7/m464 t013, on production, 2026-11-17 (pin `14eaa71b98e5`): Workspace Settings → Change plan on a Scale primary, headless Chromium at 1440×900, en.
- **Cause:** `dashboard/src/features/workspaces/components/plan-picker.tsx:65-70`. The header row is `flex items-baseline justify-between gap-2`, and the name `<span>` has no `shrink-0`/`whitespace-nowrap`. In the dialog's two-column grid each card is about 220 px wide, so "$349.30/mo per organization" and "Custom terms per organization" (t011, `45147b896`) wrap to two lines and shrink the name until it breaks inside the word.
- **Blast radius:** `PlanPicker` has two callers, `change-plan-dialog.tsx:155` and `routes/new.workspace.tsx:245`. The `/new/workspace` page was not probed. Its cards are wider, but the same header could break at the `sm` two-column breakpoint.
- **Fix:** the plan name never breaks inside a word (`shrink-0 whitespace-nowrap` on the name, and the price wraps under it when they do not fit side by side). Check both callers at 1440, at 640 and at 390.
- **Done when:** on production the dialog and `/new/workspace` show "Scale" and "Enterprise" whole at 1440, 640 and 390; a component test pins the name's no-break class. ~20m.
- **Render:** n/a (layout).
