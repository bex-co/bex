# Loki's production volume is 20Gi while the manifest and its guard say 60Gi

Why: `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` passes on a size production does not have, so the 30-day log retention rests on a third of the disk the repository claims.

- Found by w7/m427, 2026-11-16, while reading the monitoring namespace's claims on production.
- `deploy/gitops/base/loki.yaml` sets `persistence.size: 60Gi`, and `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` fails a tree without it ("PVC must be sized for 21d + zot/static-server evidence (60Gi)", w2/m93). The live claim `monitoring/storage-loki-0` is 20Gi (`kubectl -n monitoring get pvc`), 128 days old.
- Likely cause: Loki is a StatefulSet, and a changed `volumeClaimTemplates` size does not resize a claim that already exists. `hcloud-volumes` allows expansion, so the claim itself can be grown.
- Not urgent: the volume was 10.5% full on 2026-11-16. Retention went from 21 to 30 days on 2026-11-14 (w9/m389), so usage will rise for a few weeks; `PersistentVolumeFillingUp` warns at 85%.
- Fix: grow the claim to the declared 60Gi (patch `spec.resources.requests.storage` on `storage-loki-0`, as the Key Value controller does for its StatefulSet claims), or lower the declared size and the guard to what the measured log volume needs. Either way the guard should stop asserting a size production does not have.
- Done when: the live claim and `loki.yaml` agree, and ADR010 § Durability & retention states the size and the measurement behind it.
