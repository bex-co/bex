## Objective

Ensure the alert's meaning survives how Alloy actually creates, resets and expires log-derived metrics.

## Context

- [Milestone scope, source evidence and definition of done](README.md).
- w7/m158 recorded 12 GC failures in 24 hours for an empty repository left across a Zot restart: removeTagsPerRetentionPolicy could not read repository metadata. The repository was repaired and later removed after successful GC. Prevention remains blocked on the upstream-versus-patched-build decision. Existing Zot alerts cover PVC capacity rather than this known failure signature; Zot platform logs already reach Loki.
- Adds detection only. m158 continues to own prevention, and its remaining DoD is not satisfied by this milestone. m26's Zot capacity alerts and the 047 build-cache trial stay separate.
- Prerequisites: `w7/m163/t002`.

## Steps

1. Handle first occurrence, restarts/config reloads and historical backfill without relying blindly on increase() of a series that starts at one.
2. Specify what resolves the alert and what evidence indicates that collection is missing rather than healthy.
3. Activate the configuration through the existing workflow and record loaded rules/targets plus bounded replay evidence; leave production registry contents untouched.
