## Objective

Fix the cause t001 identified, and close the class: no step between the Deployment apply and the Ingress apply may leave a service running-but-unroutable with no user-visible reason.

## Context

- The immediate fix depends on t001's finding. If it is the legacy per-App NetworkPolicy cleanup at line 1105, note what that step actually is: a **best-effort delete of an artifact from a superseded design** (`docs/ADR022`'s Option B, superseded by ADR043). Failing an entire deploy — and skipping routing — on a cleanup of something that may not exist is disproportionate to its purpose.
- The general shape to consider: steps whose failure should block the rollout versus steps whose failure should be recorded and stepped over. Today they are all `r.fail()` returns in one sequence.
- Whatever the fix, the failure must remain visible — silently swallowing the error trades one invisible failure for another. `w7/m79` owns the surfacing mechanism; this task must at minimum not make the signal worse.

## Steps

1. Fix the specific cause from t001.
2. Classify the steps between the Deployment apply and the Ingress apply as blocking versus best-effort, and apply that classification, justifying each in a comment.
3. Ensure a best-effort step's failure is still recorded (condition/event/log), never swallowed.
4. Verify a service whose reconcile hits the previously-fatal step now gets its Service and Ingress and a populated `serviceDetails.url`.
