## Objective

An Owner manages both requirements on the org Settings page. A workspace shows when its org sets them, and a blocked person is told which org requires what.

## Context

- The org Settings route is `dashboard/src/routes/org.$orgId.settings.tsx`, with its cards and skeletons in `dashboard/src/features/organizations/components/` (`organization-settings-frame.tsx`, `organization-route-skeletons.tsx`).
- The workspace switches are `dashboard/src/features/workspaces/components/workspace-login-requirement-card*`. The blocked pages are `workspace-two-factor-blocked-page.tsx` and `workspace-google-login-blocked-page.tsx`, each with a skeleton.
- Root `AGENTS.md`: a skeleton must match its ready state at desktop and narrow-mobile widths (`dashboard/AGENTS.md`).

## Steps

1. Add a **Security** card on the org Settings page with "Require two-factor authentication" and "Require Google login". Owners get working switches. Members and Guests see the state, read-only, with a line saying only Owners change it. Turning one on confirms first and shows the lock-out 409 in words. If t001 decided the confirmation lists who would be blocked, show that list.
2. In an enforcing org, the workspace's own switch reads on and locked, with "Required by <org name>" and, for an Owner, a link to the org Settings page.
3. The blocked pages name the org when the refusal's details carry `organizationId`. The org's own pages show the same blocked state to a blocked Owner.
4. Add English and every other shipped locale's strings. Extend the org Settings skeleton to the new card so pending and ready match.
