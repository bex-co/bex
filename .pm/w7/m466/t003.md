## Objective

Let an Org Owner turn each requirement on and off, under the workspace settings' rules, and record each change in the org log as Render's event.

## Context

- The workspace setters (`lego/backend/internal/workspaces/service.go:1215`) share `setLoginRequirement` with a `loginSetting` per requirement: the plan feature, the default-workspace refusal, the lock-out guard (`requireEnrolledActor`, `requireGoogleSignedInActor`), the write and the audit record.
- Org writes check `requireOwner` (`internal/workspaces/organizations.go:209`). `RenameOrganization` is the model for an Owner-only org mutation with an org audit row. Its verb `core.AuditVerbOrgRenamed` maps to `ChangeOrgNameEvent` (`internal/audit/service.go:219`).
- Org audit rows have the resource `org:<org-id>` (ADR115 D7). `RecordTwoFactorEnforcement` and `RecordGoogleLoginEnforcement` (`internal/core/twofactor.go`) carry `metadata.to`.
- `twofactor.Gate.Invalidate(workspaceID)` drops one replica's cached setting. bex-api wires it into the workspace setters (`internal/api/server.go:709`).
- `renderOrganizationEvents` (`internal/audit/render_inventory_test.go:158-159`) lists both events as `notYet`.

## Steps

1. Add `SetOrganizationRequireTwoFactor` and `SetOrganizationRequireGoogleLogin` to the org service. Each is Owner only (403 otherwise) and plan-gated on the primary's plan as t001 decided. Each refuses to turn a requirement on while the acting Owner does not meet it (t001's lock-out 409s, reusing the workspace guards) and where no identity lookup is wired. A no-op writes and records nothing.
2. Add verbs `organizations.SetRequireTwoFactor` → `ChangeOrg2FAEnforcementEvent` and `organizations.SetRequireGoogleLogin` → `ChangeOrgAllowedLoginMethodsEvent`. They are recorded on `org:<id>` with `metadata.to` and deferred until the write, as the workspace verbs are. Map both in `renderOrganizationEvents`.
3. After a change, invalidate the gate's cached setting for every workspace in the org on this replica. Other replicas expire within the 60 s TTL, as the workspace settings' do.
4. GraphQL: `Organization.requireTwoFactor` and `requireGoogleLogin`, readable by every org member and Guest. `setOrganizationRequireTwoFactor(organizationId, enabled)` and `setOrganizationRequireGoogleLogin(organizationId, enabled)`. `Workspace.loginRequiredByOrganization` (or the shape t001 picks), so the workspace card can show the lock. MCP `get_organization` carries the two fields, plus a mutation tool only if t001 chose one. Update the schema golden and the op-class scope matrix.
