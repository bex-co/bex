## Objective

Measure cache footprint for the largest actively rebuilt production workload shape so the trial is not admitted on tiny-fixture evidence alone.

## Context

- Drill residual: production-size cache growth/transfer unmeasured; tiny correctness fixtures are not a fleet forecast.
- Sep 5 measured ~173 MB unique dashboard cache content; native shapes can restore hundreds of MB with little unique add.

## Steps

1. Pick the largest actively rebuilt App (or an isolated fixture matching its build shape).
2. Capture cache manifest sizes and restore/save timings; observe allocated PVC growth across ≥2 GC intervals when practical.
3. Record whether the projected growth fits the 17.70 GiB-class reserve (recomputed in t002); if not, hold the trial with the shortfall named.

## Done 2026-09-15

No per-App cache switch exists and the largest actively rebuilt Apps build private sources, so the projection is analytic from the live Zot catalog (49 repos, 38.20 GiB dedupe-unique), not a fixture measurement — recorded as such. Six actively rebuilt shapes total 5.95 GiB of images (largest `block-eden-mono` 2.32 GiB native); one rolling `cache` tag per App ⇒ expected steady-state growth ≈ 6–9 GiB, all-3× worst case ≈ 17.9 GiB, plus a ≤2 h GC-lag transient per rebuilt App. **Fit:** expected fits the 20.03 GiB reserve; the worst case with a concurrent transient would cross 65% and is bounded by the trial's 70%-for-10-min stop rule. Verdict: go, with that caveat named in the drill.
