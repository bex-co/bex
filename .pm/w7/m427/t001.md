## Objective

Decide, from measured production numbers, how tenant-facing metrics are kept for 30 days, and write the decision and the per-plan windows into ADR010.

## Context

- `deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml`: `retention: 3d` (`:289`), an 8Gi volume, 512Mi memory limit; production raises memory to 1Gi/2Gi (`deploy/gitops/overlays/prod/kustomization.yaml`).
- w4/m144: production Prometheus was OOM-killed during WAL replay. Memory follows head series and the WAL, not retention; disk follows retention.
- Production access: root `AGENTS.md` § Local cluster workflow (`scripts/fetch-app-kubeconfig.sh`). Read-only queries are enough: `prometheus_tsdb_head_series`, series count by job, `prometheus_tsdb_storage_blocks_bytes`, `rate(prometheus_tsdb_head_samples_appended_total[1h])`, and the volume's used bytes.
- The series the API reads: cAdvisor container CPU and memory for tenant and datastore pods, Traefik service and router counters, kubelet volume stats, the CNPG and Valkey exporter series, kube-state-metrics limits, the egress and WebSocket meters (`lego/backend/internal/metrics/`).

## Steps

1. Read the numbers above on production and record them with the date.
2. Estimate 30 days of storage for (a) every series at today's interval and (b) only the tenant-facing series the API reads.
3. Choose one: raise retention and grow the volume, or keep the platform Prometheus short and keep only (b), at a coarser interval if needed, in a second store for 30 days. Say what the choice does to WAL-replay memory.
4. Write the measurement, the choice, the sized volume and the per-plan windows (7, 14, 30 days) into ADR010 § Durability & retention.
5. ADR018 § Metrics: add the retention claim to the Core metrics row, marked ◐ until t005.
