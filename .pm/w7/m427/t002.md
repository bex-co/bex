## Objective

Production keeps tenant-facing metrics for 30 days, as t001 decided, without filling its volume or failing WAL replay, and a guard keeps the retention from dropping unnoticed.

## Context

- `deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml` (`retention: 3d`, 8Gi) and the production overlay (`deploy/gitops/overlays/prod/kustomization.yaml`).
- `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` guards production manifests; alert rules have promtool tests.
- w4/m144's replay failure: restart behavior must be re-checked after the change.
- The local mock cluster stays small; its window may be shorter if ADR010 says so.

## Steps

1. Apply t001's choice under `deploy/gitops/`. The production overlay carries the production size.
2. Grow the volume first, then raise the retention, so the disk cannot fill between the two. State the order in the commit.
3. Add or extend a volume-fill alert for the metrics store, and a check in `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` that fails if production retention is below 30 days.
4. After the rollout, restart the store once in a quiet window and confirm it replays within its memory budget.
5. Record the rollout time in the milestone README: t005's date gate counts from it.

## Files

- `deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Production reports a 30-day retention for tenant-facing series on the sized volume.
- [ ] The fill alert passes its promtool test and is healthy on production; `bash scripts/gitops-validate.sh` passes and fails on a retention below 30 days.
- [ ] One restart after the change replays cleanly.
- [ ] The rollout time is recorded in the milestone README.
