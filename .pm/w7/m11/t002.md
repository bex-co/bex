## Objective

Implement t001's chosen admission mechanism: reject a pod whose image fails cosign signature verification.

## Context

- Tenant pods are created by the operator's reconciler (`lego/operator/internal/runtime/`) from `App` CRs — this check happens at the Kubernetes admission layer, not in the operator itself, so it catches every path to a running pod, not just the operator's.
- Reuse `w6/006`'s signing key Secret naming convention.

## Steps

1. Implement the chosen mechanism from t001 (policy manifest or webhook code).
2. Wire it to read the public key material and verify the pod's image signature against it.
3. Scope the check to tenant namespaces only (not platform/system pods).
