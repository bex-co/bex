## Objective

Render says members "can't change their Render account's associated email address" and "can't disconnect" Google while they belong to an enforcing workspace. bex must refuse both where they actually happen: in Kratos's settings flow, not only in the dashboard.

## Context

- The account settings page posts Kratos's own settings flow (`dashboard/src/features/auth/pages/settings-page/index.tsx`, Ory Elements). A client can call `/self-service/settings` directly, so hiding a control is not enough.
- Kratos is configured in `deploy/gitops/base/values/kratos.values.yaml`, with `flows.settings` at about line 189. The local overlay is `deploy/gitops/overlays/local/values/kratos.values.yaml`, and dev-env has `scripts/dev-env/values/kratos.values.yaml`. Production runs Kratos v26.2.0.
- Kratos's `web_hook` action can interrupt a flow when its response is parsed (`response.parse: true`). Probe which settings methods (`profile`, `oidc`) it runs before the change is saved.

## Steps

1. Probe on `dev-7` whether a parsed `web_hook` on `settings.after.profile` and `settings.after.oidc` can refuse a change with a message the UI shows. If it cannot, record the alternative t001 chose (for example, a `before` hook plus a re-check, or reverting through the admin API) and its limits.
2. Add an internal, authenticated bex-api endpoint the hook calls. It answers "refuse" when the identity belongs to a workspace that requires Google login and the change alters the email trait or removes the `google` OIDC credential. Its message names the workspaces to leave first.
3. Wire the hook in all three Kratos values files, with the shared secret out of git as the OIDC secrets are.
4. Make the dashboard show the refusal clearly in every locale.

- [ ] If bex-api is unreachable, the hook fails in the direction t001 recorded, and a test pins it.
