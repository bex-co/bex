## Objective

Write down what Render does and what bex will do, before any code. Every later task builds on the decisions recorded here.

## Context

- Render: [login-settings](https://render.com/docs/login-settings) (quoted in the milestone README), the pricing table row in `docs/render-artifacts/pricing.md`, and `ChangeTeamAllowedLoginMethodsEvent` in `lego/backend/internal/api/openapi/render-public-api-1.json`.
- The 2FA half's design: [ADR012 §9a](../../../docs/ADR012-auth.md#9a-workspace-enforced-two-factor-authentication-w22m402) and the ADR018 row "Workspace-enforced 2FA".
- Kratos sessions list `authentication_methods`, each with `method` (`password`, `oidc`, `totp`, …) and, for `oidc`, `provider`. Hydra's accept-login request takes `amr`.

## Steps

1. Save Render's page facts to `docs/render-artifacts/login-settings.md`, with the date read.
2. Add ADR012 §9b with these decisions, each marked as a Render fact or a bex choice:
   - **"Signed in with Google"** means the current Kratos session has an `oidc` authentication method with provider `google`. Decide whether a session that also used a second factor still counts (it should), and how a Hydra token inherits it.
   - **The invited-email rule.** Render requires the Google account that matches the invited email. Decide how bex checks it (the Google `email` claim equals the identity's email trait at sign-in).
   - **API keys.** A key counts when its creator was signed in with Google when the key was created. Decide bex's equivalent of Render's May 1, 2024 exemption: Render exempts keys older than the feature itself.
   - **SSH keys.** Render documents nothing for Google login. Apply the same rule as API keys, and record it as an inference.
   - **Error codes:** `WORKSPACE_GOOGLE_LOGIN_REQUIRED` (403), plus the admin lock-out 409 and the "unavailable" 409, mirroring the 2FA codes.
   - **Plan gate:** reuse `core.FeatureTwoFactorEnforcement` (Render's one row covers both), or add a sibling key. Record which, and why.
   - **Email change and unlink refusals:** where Kratos runs them (t006).
3. ADR018: give the setting its own row beside "Workspace-enforced 2FA", and remove "Sibling Render setting not built" from that row. ADR027: Google becomes a shipped provider. ADR030 §9: the row's Google half gets its disposition.
