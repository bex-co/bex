## Objective

Give the org a Members page (invite as Member or Owner, change the org role, remove, pending invites) and a Settings page. Settings has the org's name and Render's Audit Logs section: UTC start and end dates at most 90 days apart, and **Export as CSV** for Owners only.

## Context

- Render: org audit logs are exported from the org's Settings → Audit Logs, by Owners, 90 days per export (render.com/docs/audit-logs, read 2026-11-17).
- The workspace export to reuse: Workspace Settings → Audit Logs (w3/m305). It pages REST `GET /v1/owners/{ownerId}/audit-logs` itself, with columns timestamp, actor, event, status and metadata (ADR018 audit row, "Rows are REST's"). The org export pages t008's org route the same way.
- Team members UI to reuse: `dashboard/src/features/team/**`, `dashboard/src/features/invites/**`.

## Steps

1. Members page:
   - list Owners, Members and Guests, each Guest with the workspace they belong to;
   - invite, change role and remove, with the last-Owner refusal shown as the API words it;
   - pending invites with revoke;
   - a skeleton matching the page.
2. Settings page:
   - rename the org (if t001's ADR keeps an org rename; Render has an org name);
   - the Audit Logs section with the date pickers, the 90-day check and Export as CSV, hidden for non-Owners as the workspace card hides for non-admins;
   - a skeleton.
3. Add the strings to every locale.
