## Objective

Make plan changes follow Render's org lifecycle:

- An upgrade of a standalone workspace to Scale or Enterprise creates its org. The workspace becomes the primary, and the member who upgraded becomes the Owner.
- A secondary workspace's plan is its org's plan.
- A change on the primary between Scale and Enterprise moves every workspace in the org.
- A downgrade of the primary below Scale is refused until Render's preconditions hold. Once they do, the downgrade deletes the org and leaves the primary standalone.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/workspaces/service.go:1263` `ChangePlan`:
  - `requirePlanEntitlement`, then `RequirePlanBilling` for a paid upward move;
  - the member and invite capacity check, which already refuses roles the target plan cannot assign (`store.LimitsFor(...).AllowedRoles`);
  - `store.UpdateTenantPlan`.
- Render (render.com/docs/organizations, read 2026-11-17): "Downgrading from Scale deletes the org and its configuration". Before you downgrade, you must delete the secondary workspaces, reassign Billing, Viewer and Contributor members and pending invites to Developer or Admin, and delete the relevant inbound IP rules. The primary workspace continues as a standalone workspace.
- Inbound IP rules: `workspaces/ip_rules.go` (workspace), plus the service, static site and environment rules (`core.FeatureInboundIPRules`).
- GraphQL `changeWorkspacePlan` (`workspaces/graphql.go:312`) and the matching MCP tool, if one exists.

## Steps

1. Upgrade: in the same transaction as `UpdateTenantPlan`, create the org (t002) with the caller as Owner, and write its tuples (t003).
2. On a secondary workspace, refuse a plan change with a named code (for example `ORGANIZATION_PLAN_ON_PRIMARY`, with `params {organizationId, primaryWorkspaceId}`).
3. On the primary, a Scale↔Enterprise change writes every org workspace's `tenants.plan` in one transaction.
4. Downgrade below Scale: refuse with one named code that lists every unmet precondition. The list is Render's three, plus any precondition bex's existing plan-capacity check already reports. When all are met, delete the org, its members, its invites and its tuples, and leave the primary standalone with its own members unchanged.
5. Record the org's history start (`organizations.audit_visible_from`) at creation, for t008.
