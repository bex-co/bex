## Objective

Turn the one-time acceptance into a repeatable staffed-window test-mode procedure with clear pass/fail evidence and ownership.

## Context

- Evidence may record non-secret object ids and timestamps but never keys, webhook secrets, kubeconfigs, card data, or full environment dumps.
- The procedure must distinguish recurring smoke tests from destructive lifecycle/test-clock drills.

## Steps

1. Publish quick smoke, full lifecycle, reconciliation, cleanup, rollback, and escalation sections.
2. Record expected dashboards/alerts and exact evidence retention location.
3. Add cadence, owner, expiry/cleanup, and explicit live-mode prohibition.
