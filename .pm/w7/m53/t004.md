## Objective

Provide explicit inspect/acknowledge/repair workflows for permanent Stripe rejects and local-stamp ambiguity older than the provider deduplication window.

## Context

- Permanent non-429 4xx rows are consumed to avoid hot loops.
- A successful Stripe event with a failed local stamp cannot be safely replayed after the bounded identifier window without reconciliation.

## Steps

1. Persist enough non-secret dead-letter/ambiguity context for operator diagnosis.
2. Add dry-run-first commands to reconcile, mark repaired, or issue an explicit Stripe adjustment.
3. Audit operator decisions and prevent automatic old replay.

- `lego/backend/internal/billing/`
- `lego/backend/internal/store/`
- `scripts/stripe-billing-reconcile.sh`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Operators can identify the exact row/dimension/error without seeing secrets.
- [ ] Old ambiguity cannot re-enter automatic emission.
- [ ] Every repair/acknowledgement is dry-run capable and audited.

## Out of scope

- Silent deletion of billing evidence.
