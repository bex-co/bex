# Render's Scale and Enterprise plans cover an organization's unlimited workspaces with one fee; bex charges each workspace its own plan

Why: a customer with several Scale workspaces pays Render's $499 once but pays bex's $349.30 once per workspace, and bex has none of the organization features Render's Scale plan includes.

- Found by w7/070, 2026-11-17. That item settled what the pricing table's "Workspaces: 1 | 1 | Unlimited | Unlimited" row counts. The row counts the workspaces one plan's account holds, not the workspaces one user may create; the per-user rule is team-members' and bex already applies it.
- Render: "With a Render Scale or Enterprise plan, you can manage all of your team's users" in one organization, and "An org can contain unlimited workspaces at no additional cost". Downgrading an org deletes it and keeps only the primary workspace (render.com/docs/organizations, read 2026-11-17). Organizations also carry org-level roles (Owner, Member, Guest), org-level audit logs, and SSO and SCIM.
- bex: no organization scope. `store.LimitsFor` lets a user own unlimited paid workspaces, and each one pays its own plan fee (ADR030 §1). Production on 2026-11-17: 3 Scale and 1 Enterprise workspaces, and one admin held two or more of them.
- Board state: `plan_table_test.go` marks the Workspaces row `followUp` to this note. "Organization user roles" and "Org-level audit logs" are marked `nonGoal` there, citing ADR018's "bex has no organization scope", but ADR018 records no user sign-off for that. SSO/SAML · SCIM is a `DO_NOT_DO` non-goal.
- Decision needed from the user. Choose one:
  - build organizations (one plan fee across an org's workspaces, plus the org roles and audit logs);
  - bill Scale and Enterprise per organization without the org features;
  - record "no organizations" in ADR018 as a signed-off divergence.
- Done when: the decision is recorded in ADR018 and ADR030 §1/§9, and the three organization rows in `plan_table_test.go` carry the disposition it implies.
