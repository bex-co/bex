## Objective

Register the `muse` profile and every backend fact it needs, while keeping it out of every workspace's offer until m391 and m392 land.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/agentsession/agent-profiles.json` (profile manifest), `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/models.go` `agentProfiles()` (labels; tests require exact membership parity with the manifest).
- `RegisteredModelEndpoint` in `lego/backend/internal/agentsession/model.go`; egress host via `sessionegress.ModelEndpointHost`.
- Reserved secret names: `reservedSecretPrefixes` in `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/secrets.go` and `dashboard/src/features/agent-environment/lib/secrets.ts`.

## Steps

1. Add the `muse` profile: `executable` = the adapter bin, `npmPackage` `@bex-co/muse-code-acp@0.11.0`, `nativeBinary` from t002, `env` (`MUSE_CODE_EXECUTABLE`, `MUSE_CODE_ACP_ALLOW_YOLO=1`, per the ADR), `modelEndpoint` `https://api.meta.ai/v1`, `modelProxy` (`baseUrlEnv` `MUSE_CODE_ACP_GATEWAY_URL`, `credentialEnv` `MUSE_CODE_ACP_GATEWAY_KEY`, suffix `/v1`), `sessionState` per the ADR, `authentication` `environment`, `permissionPolicy` `sandbox-auto-approve`.
2. Label it "Muse Code" in `agentProfiles()`; reserve the `MUSE_` and `META_` prefixes in backend and dashboard.
3. Advertise `muse` in the capabilities projection only where a route serves it, and refuse `createAgentSession` with `agent: muse` elsewhere with a typed 400 (no route serves it until m391).

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/agentsession/agent-profiles.json`, `lego/backend/internal/agentsession/model.go`, `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/models.go`, `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/service.go`, `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/secrets.go`, `dashboard/src/features/agent-environment/lib/secrets.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Profile manifest tests pass with the new entry; a workspace secret named `MUSE_…` or `META_…` is refused.
- [ ] Capabilities omit `muse` and create refuses it with a typed error in a workspace no route serves.

## Out of scope

- The Meta platform route (m391) and admin enablement (m392).
