# Day-to-day operator RBAC — narrow jobs.create or bind serviceAccountName

**Type:** optional hardening / residual-risk record. **Not a milestone** (w7/m37 already scoped the credential; residual is accepted K8s RBAC semantics).

codex-security scan #6 (scanner medium → triaged low): `deploy/gitops/base/operator-daytoday-rbac.yaml:53-56` grants the `bex-operator` day-to-day ClusterRole `[create, delete]` on `batch/jobs` cluster-wide, with no `serviceAccountName` admission binding. w7/m37 intentionally kept one-shot Job create (for `kubectl create job --from=cronjob/...` maintenance) and dropped the heavier write verbs; but a Job creator can still name any namespace ServiceAccount without an impersonation check, yielding its mounted token.

**Largely mitigated already:** the credential is narrowed, short-lived-ish (1y), smoke-tested, and the privileged-SA surface in this platform is small.

**Trigger to promote:** if the ops role is ever broadened or a privileged platform SA appears in a tenant-reachable namespace. Fix shape: a ValidatingAdmissionPolicy binding `spec.serviceAccountName` to an operator-allowlist namespace set, or narrow `jobs.create` to a dedicated maintenance namespace via Role+RoleBinding instead of a ClusterRole. Until then: recorded, not built.

Source: codex-security scan `codex-security-bex-azfRGv`, finding #6.
