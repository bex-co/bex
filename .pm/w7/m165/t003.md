## Objective

Demonstrate what the monitoring detects and what happens to buffered or rejected lines without disrupting production ingestion.

## Context

- [Milestone scope, source evidence and definition of done](README.md).
- Loki's checked-in values disable bundled self-monitoring and Prometheus has no Loki/Alloy scrape at brainstorm/materialization. w7/m157 added API/operator retention. Existing periodic tenant-view/request-log probes verify some end-to-end results but do not directly identify rejected ingestion, exhausted delivery retries or absent collectors. This is a coverage gap, not evidence of a current ingestion outage.
- Complements m157 retention and m83 tenant synthetics. Reuses m163/t001's Alloy metrics scrape instead of creating a second one; this milestone covers transport/ingestion health while m163 covers registry GC semantics.
- Prerequisites: `w7/m165/t002`.

## Steps

1. Use isolated fixtures to interrupt delivery and provoke the selected rejection/final-loss paths, keeping test labels bounded and data non-sensitive.
2. Record alert activation/recovery, buffering/retry limits, and whether fixture lines are later recovered or deliberately lost.
3. Confirm production targets/rules/panels after rollout and record the deployed revision, leaving existing periodic tenant synthetics in place.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Failure and recovery evidence distinguishes delivered, buffered and unrecoverably dropped fixture lines.
- [ ] No production log stream is deliberately interrupted to obtain proof.
- [ ] Dated deployed-coverage evidence is separate from the isolated fault exercise and records practical limits.

## Out of scope

- External telemetry drains or a replacement/multi-component Loki architecture.
- Changing tenant log API shapes, retention policy or intentional CNPG chatter filtering.
- Paging on every intentionally dropped line or treating absence of tenant traffic as loss.
