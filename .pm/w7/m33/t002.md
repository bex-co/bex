## Objective

Rework the egress-deny policy so tenant application workloads retain the full node/metadata deny while platform-managed CNPG pods can reach only the Kubernetes API paths they require. Deny-overrides-allow means the fix must narrow the DENY's selector (or split it into two policies), not add an allow.

## Context

- Policy lives in `deploy/gitops` (the w7/m4 egress-hardening manifests; find via `grep -r "deny-tenant-node-and-metadata-egress" deploy/gitops`).
- CNPG pods are identifiable (e.g. `cnpg.io/cluster` label / operator-set labels) independently of the workspace label — use that to carve them out of the deny selector rather than dropping their workspace label (which same-workspace isolation needs).
- Do NOT reopen node-local (`host`) or cloud-metadata access for tenant app pods — that is w7/m4's whole point.

## Steps

1. Design the selector split (matchExpressions excluding CNPG-managed pods from the deny, plus a scoped policy giving CNPG pods kube-API egress only).
2. Apply to the gitops overlays; render both overlays.
3. Re-run the t001 probe: CNPG identity reaches the API service; a plain tenant app identity still cannot reach host/remote-node/metadata.

## Files

- `deploy/gitops/` (the tenant egress policy manifests)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] CNPG pods on tenant nodes reach the k8s API; tenant app pods provably still denied node/metadata egress (probe evidence for both).
