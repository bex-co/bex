# The social sign-in buttons sit above the email form; Render puts them below it, under "Or continue with"

Why: Render parity first. Render's login and sign-up pages lead with email and password and offer the providers after them, so bex's card reads in a different order from Render's.

- Found by w7/m468 t008 (2026-11-17), comparing dashboard.render.com/login and /register (headless, 1440 and 390) with bex's: Render shows Email, Password, "Forgot password?", "Sign in", then a divider "Or continue with", then GitHub · Google (· SSO on login) and, behind "More options", GitLab · Bitbucket. bex's Ory card draws the provider rows first, then a divider, then the email form (`dashboard/src/common/lib/ory/sso-buttons.tsx` `SsoButtonRows` as Ory Elements' `Form.SsoRoot`, which Ory Elements 1.2.1's login and registration cards render as `OryFormSsoForm` before `OryForm`).
- The skeletons draw the same order (`OryAuthCardSkeleton` in `dashboard/src/common/components/route-skeletons.tsx`), so both move together.
- End state: on login and sign-up the provider rows render after the submit button under an "Or continue with" divider in every locale, the skeletons match at 1440 and 390, and `sso-buttons.test.tsx` pins the order. The settings card's Connected accounts layout is unchanged.
