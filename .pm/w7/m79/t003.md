## Objective

Report the case where a service is meant to be publicly reachable but has no route — the `w7/m78` symptom — as its own explicit condition, so the state is legible even if a future bug reintroduces it.

## Context

- Production showed `serviceDetails.url: null` on a running service with no Ingress and no indication that a URL was ever expected. Nothing distinguished "this service has no public route by design" from "this service should have one and does not".
- `spec.expose` plus `EffectiveHosts` (`lego/types/v1alpha1/app_types.go:578`) already define whether a public host is expected; `lego/backend/internal/apps/domains.go` derives the reported URL. The expectation is computable — it just is not compared against reality.
- `store/reconciler.go:952` sets `Expose: true` for every projected App, so "expected a URL" is the normal case and its absence is genuinely anomalous.
- Deliberately-unrouted types (background workers, and services under `renderSubdomainPolicy: disabled` with no custom host, `w7/m31`) must **not** trip this.

## Steps

1. Compute the expectation (exposed, has effective hosts) and compare it against the observed route.
2. Report a specific condition when they disagree, distinct from the t001 dependency reason.
3. Exclude the by-design cases: workers, cron jobs, and the subdomain-policy-disabled path.
4. Confirm it clears once routing converges.

## Files

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/apps/domains.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An exposed service with no route reports a specific condition.
- [ ] Workers, cron jobs, and subdomain-policy-disabled services never trip it.
- [ ] The condition clears when routing converges.

## Out of scope
