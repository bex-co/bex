## Objective

A service's registry keeps its 5 most recent builds on a Hobby workspace, 15 on Pro and 30 on Scale and Enterprise, and every rollback surface offers exactly the builds that are kept.

## Context

- Render: "Render retains a fixed number of recent build artifacts for each service, based on your workspace plan" (render.com/docs/rollbacks); the pricing table gives 5, 15, 30 and 30 (read 2026-11-16).
- bex keeps each service's ten most recent releases plus its five most recently pushed tags on every plan (ADR060; w8/m156). The operator publishes what the registry holds in `App.status.storedImages` (`lego/operator/internal/controller/live_tag.go`), and bex-api refuses a rollback to a build that is gone and hides it from `deployActions` and `Deploy.buildAvailable` (`lego/backend/internal/deploys/actioncaps.go`).
- The operator has no database. A workspace setting reaches it as a label the projector writes on the workspace's Apps, as `app.bex.co/overlapping-deploy-policy` does (`lego/backend/internal/workspaces/deploy_policy.go`, `lego/backend/internal/store/reconciler.go`, `lego/operator/internal/controller/release_identity.go`).
- A Free web service rolls back only to its two most recent deploys (w8/262); that rule is separate and stays.
- The serving image and the image a release is rolling to are always protected, whatever the count.

## Steps

1. One table for the count by plan (5, 15, 30, 30), next to the other plan tables.
2. The projector writes the count for the workspace's plan onto each App; a plan change reaches the Apps within one projector pass.
3. The operator's retention uses that count in place of ten, defaults to today's behavior when the label is absent, and never removes the serving or the rolling image.
4. Re-estimate the registry volume for 30 kept builds on Scale and Enterprise workspaces (two Scale and one Enterprise on production at w8/m138's inventory) and record it in ADR060.
5. On a downgrade the extra builds age out by the normal retention pass. Say so in ADR060 and mark it as bex's choice (Render documents nothing).
6. Update ADR018's Rollback row (the "Difference, not signed off" sentence goes) and ADR060.
7. Tests: operator envtest for each count and for the protected images; bex-api tests that `buildAvailable` and the 409 follow `storedImages`; a projector test for the label and a plan change.
