# Cross-region backup replication

From the 2026-07-31 persistent-store backup audit: every backup copy — `etcd-snapshots/`, `openbao-snapshots/`, `bex-db/`, `postgres/`, and (once w7/m67+m68 land) `auth-dbs/` + `keyvalue/` — lives in **one** Wasabi region (`s3.eu-central-2`, bucket `bex-tfstate`). A regional object-storage loss or account lockout destroys all restore paths at once, invalidating every drilled runbook in ADR031.

Options to evaluate when picked up: (a) bucket-level replication to a second region/provider (Wasabi replication or a second bucket + `aws s3 sync` sweep CronJob), (b) dual-write from the backup jobs themselves (more moving parts per job), (c) a second Barman ObjectStore for the CNPG stores (doubles WAL traffic). (a) is the likely shape — one mechanism covers all prefixes, no per-job changes.

Needs a provider/cost decision, so it's a promotion candidate (multi-task: provisioning + credential custody + replication config + ADR031 amendment + a restore-from-secondary verification) — not committed yet; explicitly lower priority than m67/m68 per the audit conversation.
