## Objective

Close the documented orphan: cert-manager's ingress-shim GC's the Certificate with the Ingress, but the issued TLS **Secret** (private key + cert for the tenant's custom domains and app URL) stays in the namespace after App deletion. Delete it in the finalizer teardown.

## Context

- Documented at the `apps.Service.Delete` comment (`lego/backend/internal/apps/service.go` ~787–788) and ADR006; the fix belongs operator-side where the Ingress/TLS names are minted.
- `lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go` — Ingress construction (~825) sets the TLS `secretName` per host/app; the teardown must derive the same names (base-domain host + each `spec.hosts[]` entry, docs/ADR005-custom-domain.md).
- Registers as a t002 teardown func; IgnoreNotFound (cert may never have been issued, or TLS disabled — no `BEX_CLUSTER_ISSUER`).
- Private keys are tenant credentials — leaving them around is a w7 hygiene issue, not just clutter.

## Steps

1. Factor the TLS secretName derivation so Ingress build and teardown share it.
2. Teardown func: delete each derived TLS Secret in the App's namespace, IgnoreNotFound.
3. envtest: App with hosts + pre-created fake TLS Secrets → delete App → Secrets gone; App without TLS deletes cleanly.
4. Update the `service.go` orphan comment + ADR006 note to point at the fix.
