## Objective

Prove operators can stop Stripe traffic, preserve metering/evidence, rotate credentials, and resume the test sandbox without duplicate billing or production downtime.

## Context

- Removing the Stripe Secret should return estimate-only behavior while operational usage continues.
- Disabling emission does not cancel Stripe Subscriptions; the drill must state and verify that boundary.

## Steps

1. Disable Stripe config and verify estimate-only surfaces plus zero provider traffic.
2. Rotate the test RAK/webhook secret and restore the Secret through the installer.
3. Reconcile backlog before resuming and confirm no duplicate Customer/Subscription/event count.

## Files

- `scripts/stripe-billing-secret.sh`
- `scripts/stripe-billing-reconcile.sh`
- `docs/runbooks/stripe-billing-setup.md`
- `docs/drills/stripe-billing-test-mode.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Disable and restore complete without bex-api downtime beyond a normal rollout.
- [x] Metering/evidence remain intact and backlog resumes safely.
- [x] Old credentials are revoked only after the replacement passes verification.

## Out of scope

- Deleting Stripe catalog or invoice evidence during rollback.
