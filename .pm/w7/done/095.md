# The Team frame's own row lays out a placeholder email, so at phone width a caller whose email wraps sees Team grow when Workspace Settings lands

Why: the root `AGENTS.md` skeleton rule asks the pending frame to match the ready page at the same width. The frame has the caller's email in the session, but it lays out `member@example.com` instead, so a longer email wraps only on the ready page and every card below Team moves down.

- **Found by:** w7/094's production walk, 2026-11-19 (pin `dff52c596d13`). Fixture: a Developer of a Public Scale org secondary (since deleted). The Workspace Settings route chunk was held, and Settings was clicked in the sidebar from `/`.
- **Observed, 390:** the caller's own Team row is 55 px in the frame and 99 px on the ready page. `qa-20261119-w7094-member@example.com` (36 characters) wraps over two lines and the "You" badge drops to a third, where the placeholder fits on one line beside it. Team is `+293` pending against `+390` ready. 53 of those 97 px are the other member's row, the member-count bound; the other 44 are this, and Notifications and every card below land 44 px lower than that bound allows. At 1440 the row is 55 px in both frames.
- **Not role-dependent:** `TeamMembersSkeleton` draws the same placeholder for every caller, so an Admin with a long email gets the same jump.
- **Cause:** `dashboard/src/features/team/components/team-panel-skeleton.tsx:31-32` lays out `PLACEHOLDER_EMAIL` and `PLACEHOLDER_SUBJECT` in the caller's row. The session is in the root context: `useRootContext().session.identity.traits.email`, as `dashboard/src/common/components/user-nav.tsx` reads it.
- **Fix:** lay out the caller's own email and identity id from the session, transparent, in the frame's own row. Keep the placeholder when there is no session.
- **Done when:**
  - A skeleton test checks that the frame's own row prints the session's email and id in `MemberRow`'s classes, and the placeholder without a session.
  - On production at 390, a caller whose email wraps sees the frame's own row at its ready height, so Team differs only by the member-count bound.
  - ~30m.
