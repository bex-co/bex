### Source explanation and repair boundary

`resourceOwnedByBlueprint` and `resolveBlueprintResourcesFromIR` in `lego/backend/internal/apps/blueprint.go` prefer a nonempty durable owner, otherwise require the CR Blueprint label, after namespace/tenant/name resolution. This correctly refuses name-only attribution.

Labels were introduced in `1904585e2` before these August 21 CRs. Historical apply (`92a1f66dc`, Aug 25) created datastores before applying services and ran best-effort ownership stamping only after every apply succeeded. A later service/domain conflict skipped stamping; list/patch failures could also leave labels absent. This is a supported historical mechanism, not proof of the exact cause for either resource. `bb85560cb` introduced durable claims and stricter inventory filtering; migration `0112_blueprint_resource_claims.up.sql` creates the claim table without a legacy backfill. The documented PostgreSQL recreation provides an additional metadata-preservation uncertainty.

A follow-up repair must establish ownership from authoritative historical evidence or an explicit owner-reviewed reassignment decision, check competing claims and current labels again, preserve migrated datastore IDs/data, and revisit ADR018's Blueprint row.
