# A workspace cannot require its members to sign in with Google, as a Render workspace can

Why: Render's plan table and login-settings page offer "Enforce 2FA & Google login" together, and bex has only the 2FA half.

- Found by w7/070, 2026-11-17, while gating the 2FA half to Pro and higher.
- Render (render.com/docs/login-settings, read 2026-11-17):
  - "Google login enforcement" is set on the Workspace Settings page, by workspace admins only.
  - "Once it's on, team members can't change their Render account's associated email address."
  - "You can't disconnect your Google account if you belong to a workspace that enforces Google-account-based login."
  - The pinned OpenAPI's audit-log enum carries `ChangeTeamAllowedLoginMethodsEvent`. The pricing table puts it on Pro and higher.
- bex: Google sign-in exists through Kratos (ADR027). There is no workspace setting that requires it. ADR018's Workspace-enforced 2FA row lists it as "Sibling Render setting not built", and w9/m449 t001 notes that bex records no `ChangeTeamAllowedLoginMethodsEvent`.
- Shape: probably a milestone. It needs a setting beside `require_two_factor`, a gate on the same `core.Base` hook (`twofactor.Gate` asks whether the member's Kratos identity has a Google OIDC credential), the email-change and unlink refusals in Kratos settings, the audit event, and a dashboard switch. It takes the `core.FeatureTwoFactorEnforcement` plan gate or a sibling one.
- Done when: an admin on Pro or higher can require Google login; a member without a linked Google identity gets the same coded 403 treatment as the 2FA requirement on REST, GraphQL, MCP and the SSH gateway; and the audit row is Render's event.

Promoted to [m465](../m465/README.md) on 2026-11-17.

- **Why no user decision was needed:** under ADR008's "Render parity first", building the setting is the parity choice. Nothing in `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md` covers it (SAML SSO stays out). Production's Google OAuth client is the one external step; it gates only m465's production walk (t008).
- **Premises corrected:**
  - bex has no Google sign-in. `scripts/auth-secrets.sh` `oidc_fragment` configures only `id: github`, and ADR027 names Google as "the same shape", unbuilt. m465 t002 adds it.
  - Render checks how a member signed in ("if they log in using any method besides their Google account") and whether an API key was "created while signed in via Google account". It does not check whether a Google account is linked. m465 t003 carries the sign-in method through sessions, tokens and keys.
- **m465 covers:**
  - the design (ADR012 §9b);
  - Google sign-in;
  - the sign-in method on every principal;
  - the setting with its plan gate, lock-out guard and `ChangeTeamAllowedLoginMethodsEvent`;
  - the gate on REST, GraphQL, MCP and SSH;
  - the Kratos email-change and unlink refusals;
  - the dashboard and a production walk.

  That is 12 tasks with the closing ones.
