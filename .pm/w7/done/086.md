# Turning on a login requirement lists the members and keys it would block, as Render's SSO enforcement does

Why: an admin or Org Owner turning on 2FA or Google login today learns who loses access only after the fact; Render's org SSO enforcement shows "Each listed member will lose access" and "Each listed API key will be invalidated" before it commits.

- Render ([saml-sso](https://render.com/docs/saml-sso), read 2026-11-17): enforcing SSO lists the members and API keys that lose access. Render's pages do not say whether its 2FA or Google confirmation does the same (`docs/render-artifacts/login-settings.md` § Organizations, an inference recorded by w7/m466 t001).
- bex: the workspace card (`dashboard/src/features/workspaces/components/workspace-login-requirement-card.tsx`) and the org Security card (w7/m466 t005) confirm with a sentence and list nobody.
- Work: a read that names the members (and their API and SSH keys) who would not meet the requirement right now, through the gate's own checks (`internal/twofactor`, `Gate.Check` per member, the 60 s caches), on GraphQL for the workspace and the org; the confirmation dialogs show the list.
- Done when: turning on 2FA in a workspace or an org with one unenrolled member shows that member and their keys in the dialog before the change, and an all-enrolled workspace shows none; covered by backend and component tests.
