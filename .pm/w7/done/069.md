# The default number of concurrent sandboxes does not follow the workspace plan

Why: Render's plan table caps a Hobby workspace at 100 concurrent sandboxes and Pro and Scale at 1,000, while bex applies one platform-wide default to every workspace.

- Found by w7/m429 t005, 2026-11-16 (`lego/backend/internal/pricing/plan_table_test.go` names this note for the row).
- Render (render.com/pricing comparison table, read 2026-11-16, captured in `docs/render-artifacts/pricing.md`): "Default concurrent sandboxes: 100 | 1,000 | 1,000 | Custom".
- bex: `BEX_SANDBOX_MAX_ACTIVE_PER_WORKSPACE` (default `store.DefaultSandboxMaxActivePerWorkspace`, 100, documented as "a guess, not a Render number") and `BEX_SANDBOX_MAX_GROUP_CONCURRENCY` (default `sandbox.RenderMaxGroupConcurrency`, 1,000) apply to every workspace whatever its plan (`lego/backend/cmd/api/config.go:670-671`, lego/backend/AGENTS.md).
- To verify first: whether Render's "default concurrent sandboxes" is the workspace total or a sandbox group's default `concurrencyLimit` (ADR042 § Sandbox groups).
- Done when: the workspace cap (or the group default, whichever Render's row is) is 100 on Hobby and 1,000 on Pro and Scale on every surface that creates or resumes a sandbox, ADR104 or ADR042 records it, and the guard's row moves from `followUp` to `limit`.

**Done 2026-11-17 (w7/069).** Render's row is the workspace's total, not a group's default: "Render Pro defaults to 1,000 per workspace, with up to 5,000 on request" and "(Hobby: 100)" (render.com/articles/render-sandboxes-vs-vercel-sandboxes, 2026-11-06, read 2026-11-17), while render.com/docs/sandboxes-limits lists a group's own 1,000 separately. Every create and resume (REST, GraphQL, MCP, and the exec and connect-token resumes, all through `admit`) now caps a workspace's active sandboxes at its plan's number, `core.ConcurrentSandboxes`: 100 on Hobby, 1,000 on Pro, Scale and Enterprise, read from the stored plan before the admission lock. `BEX_SANDBOX_MAX_ACTIVE_PER_WORKSPACE` is only a ceiling now, unset by default. `TestWorkspaceCapFollowsThePlan` covers Hobby at 100, the same workspace on Pro at 1,000, the ceiling, and an unreadable plan; it fails with the old fixed 100. The guard's row is `limit` (`plan_table_test.go`). ADR042 § Sandbox groups, ADR104 D7 and D11 (the "same default on every plan" line amended), ADR018 (the divergence row is now a match) and ADR030 §9 record it. On Pro and higher the sandbox namespace quota binds first, at 116 `1c-2g` sandboxes: filed as [076](../076.md).
