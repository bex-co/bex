## Objective

Provision distinct least-privilege object-store identities for serving and publishing static content, with no authority over Terraform state, backups, or unrelated buckets.

## Context

- A dedicated bucket is not an access boundary if its credential can also read `bex-tfstate` or backup buckets.
- The static-server needs read/list access only; publish and purge Jobs need the minimum list/read/write/delete actions required by sync and cleanup.
- Credentials remain out of band and must never be committed or printed in evidence.

## Steps

1. Define provider-native policies for a read-only static-server identity and a separate publish/purge identity scoped to the static-content bucket.
2. Provision both identities out of band and record their non-secret identifiers and custody/rotation procedure.
3. Prove positive access to the required static bucket operations and negative access to tfstate, backups, and a control bucket.
4. Prepare rollback-safe Secret material for `bex-system` and `bex-build` without changing the live workload yet.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The static-server identity can list/get static content and cannot put/delete it.
- [x] The publish identity can perform only the static bucket operations required by publish and purge.
- [x] Both identities are denied from tfstate, etcd/OpenBao/CNPG backups, and unrelated buckets.
- [x] Rotation and rollback instructions identify Secret names and namespaces without containing secret values.

## Completion evidence

The exact provider policies live in `infra/wasabi/static-s3-read-policy.json` and `static-s3-publish-policy.json`; the production allow/deny matrix is recorded in `docs/drills/2026-07-29-static-site-trust-baseline.md`. `docs/runbooks/static-site-s3-rotation.md` documents namespace-local rotation without secret material.

## Out of scope

- Making the static-content bucket public or sharing credentials with Terraform state.
