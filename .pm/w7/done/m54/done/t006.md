## Objective

Close the tenant-facing review by comparing bex's platform-hostname browser behavior with Render and documenting the owner-accepted parent-cookie divergence.

## Context

- This milestone changes no REST, GraphQL, MCP, or dashboard schema; the relevant user-facing surface is browser isolation on the shared platform domain.
- Parent-domain cookies, host-only cookie guidance, Service Worker origin scope, redirects/rewrites, and custom domains are the observable contract.
- PSL membership was explicitly waived on 2026-07-30; equivalent parent-cookie isolation is not claimed.

## Steps

1. Capture current browser behavior for two controlled `onrender.com` sites where possible and the equivalent two `onbex.co` sites.
2. Compare parent-domain cookies, origin storage, Service Worker scope, redirects/rewrites, and custom-domain behavior.
3. Update the parity ledger and static-site ADR with evidence and explicit non-goals.
4. Record the parent-cookie difference as an explicit accepted divergence rather than silently presenting parity.
