# The auth card skeletons size Ory's subtitle for English, so in shorter languages the card moves up to a line when it lands at phone width

Why: the root rule says a skeleton matches its ready state at the same breakpoint, and in zh, ja and ko it still shifts below 640px.

- Found by w7/m468 t006 (2026-11-17) while matching the login, sign-up, forgot-password and verification skeletons pixel for pixel at 1440 and 390 (English).
- `OryCardSkeleton` and its callers in `dashboard/src/common/components/route-skeletons.tsx` reserve the lines Ory's English subtitle wraps to: two below `sm` on login and sign-up when a provider is offered ("Sign in with your email and password or a social provider"), three on forgot-password, two on verification. A shorter translation fits on fewer lines, so the card shifts up by a line (about 24px) when it lands. Drawing the subtitle from Ory's translated copy, as `ory-settings-copy` does for settings, would size it per locale.
- End state: at 390 every auth card skeleton's header matches its card in all eight locales, and a test pins the per-locale line count.

**Done 2026-11-18.** The premise held, and in both directions: at 390 on production Ory's subtitle took one line in ko login and zh and ko sign-up (the skeleton reserved two), three in de login and ru sign-up, two on forgot-password in zh and ko (three reserved) and three on verification in es, ru and de (two reserved). `OryCardSkeleton` now reserves Ory's own words instead of fixed heights: the title, the subtitle and, on login and sign-up, the footer link, each laid out invisibly in Ory's font and type scale under one bar (`ReservedText`), so it wraps to the card's lines at every width. The words come from Ory's catalog, mirrored per language in `dashboard/src/features/auth/ory-copy/` (renamed from `ory-settings-copy`, which already mirrored the settings cards; `ory-copy.test.ts` keeps every entry equal to `OryLocales`), and the subtitle is composed from its parts as Ory's `useCardHeaderText` does: login's "your email and password" (Ory passes the literal "email"), sign-up's "your <the forms' e-mail label>", plus "a social provider" when a provider is offered, joined with Ory's "or". `auth-card-skeleton-copy.test.tsx` renders Ory's real `<Login>`, `<Registration>`, `<Recovery>` and `<Verification>` on production's Kratos flows, with and without a provider, and asserts the skeleton's title, subtitle and footer equal the card's in all eight languages (it fails when a part's order or wording is wrong); with the same text in the same type the line count follows, which a jsdom test cannot measure, so the walk below measured it. Walk (Vite on this tree, the four flows served from production's own JSON with the request held, then released): header, card top and card height matched exactly for all four cards in all eight languages at 390, 768 and 1440, and 92 of 96 at 320; the four exceptions are login's e-mail form at 320 in es, ja, pt and ru, filed as 093. The footer was added after the 320 pass showed "Don't have an account? Sign up" wrapping to two lines.
