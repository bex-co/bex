## Objective

Give an org Render's membership:

- An Owner invites people to the org and changes their org role (Owner, Member, Guest).
- An Owner removes a person, which removes them from every org workspace, and the last Owner cannot be removed.
- Inviting a non-member to one org workspace makes them a Guest of that workspace only.
- An org holds at most two members with the Billing role across its workspaces.

## Context

- Render (render.com/docs/organizations, read 2026-11-17):
  - An Org Owner "invites or removes members (including other Owners)".
  - A Guest "can access only the single workspace they're invited to".
  - "Each org supports up to two members with the Billing role".
  - Standard members join on their first SSO login. bex has no SSO (`.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md`), so t001's ADR makes an org invite the way in.
- Workspace invites and roles today: `lego/backend/internal/members/` (`service.go`, `rest.go`, `graphql.go`, `mcp.go`), with REST at Render's `/v1/owners/{ownerId}/members` (`workspaces/rest.go:68-99`).
- Ids: invites use `id.Invite` (`inv-`).

## Steps

1. Org invites: `inviteToOrganization(orgId, email, role member|owner)`, accept, revoke and list, on GraphQL and MCP, gated by `can_manage_org`. Reuse the workspace invite's email and acceptance path where it fits.
2. `changeOrganizationRole` and `removeOrganizationMember`. Removal also removes the person's workspace memberships in the org. Refuse removing or demoting the last Owner.
3. A workspace invite in an org workspace to a person who is not an org member makes them a Guest bound to that workspace. A Guest invited to a second workspace is refused with a named code that tells the admin to make them a Member.
4. Billing cap: assigning the Billing role in any org workspace is refused once two distinct people hold it in the org (named code, `params {limit: 2}`), on REST `PATCH /v1/owners/{ownerId}/members/{userId}`, GraphQL and MCP.
5. Leave event hooks for t008: invite, accept, add, remove, role change.

## Note from t001 (2026-11-17)

Render without SSO makes a workspace invitee a standard Member, not a Guest ("If you haven't enabled SSO, workspace admins can invite any Render user to any org-managed workspace. These users automatically become standard members of the org", render.com/docs/organizations). Step 3 follows ADR115 D4 instead: a workspace invite makes a Member; an Owner makes someone a Guest by changing their org role while they belong to at most one org workspace; a Guest invited to a second workspace is refused with `ORGANIZATION_GUEST_SINGLE_WORKSPACE`.
