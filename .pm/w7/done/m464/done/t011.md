## Objective

Make every plan surface on the dashboard tell the org's story:

- A secondary workspace's Billing and plan card says its plan is the org's, managed on the primary, and offers no plan change.
- The primary's change-plan dialog says a downgrade below Scale deletes the org, and lists each unmet precondition the API returns.
- Creating a workspace inside an org shows "included in your organization's plan" instead of a price.
- Workspace Settings shows the privacy switch to admins of org workspaces.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/workspaces/components/plan-picker.tsx` (the per-workspace `$17.50/mo` / `$349.30/mo` labels, from `pricing.yaml` through locale lockstep), `change-plan-dialog.tsx`, `workspace-details-card.tsx`, `dashboard/src/routes/billing.tsx`.
- t005's codes and params, and t006's privacy verb.
- Render: "The org plan is managed from the primary workspace". To downgrade, go to the org's Workspaces page → the workspace labelled Primary → Workspace settings → Billing (render.com/docs/organizations).

## Steps

1. Plan picker and dialog:
   - on a secondary workspace, read-only, with a link to the primary;
   - on the primary, the downgrade warning and the precondition list taken from the error params;
   - on Scale/Enterprise pricing copy, "per organization".
2. Create-in-org flow: no price line, and the org's plan shown.
3. Privacy card in Workspace Settings for org workspaces (admins only), with a skeleton that matches it.
4. Add the strings to every locale.

## Outcome (2026-11-17)

A secondary workspace's plan is read-only on Workspace Settings and Billing ("Included in your organization's plan", with a link to the primary through `/w/$`), the primary's change-plan dialog warns that a downgrade below Scale deletes the org and lists each `ORGANIZATION_DOWNGRADE_BLOCKED` precondition translated, `ORGANIZATION_PLAN_ON_PRIMARY` has its own message, and Scale/Enterprise read "per organization" (`plan-billing-labels.test.ts` checks the labels against `pricing.yaml`'s fee and `per`). Workspace Settings has a Privacy card (Public/Invite-only) for admins of org workspaces, with a matching skeleton. The create-in-org flow with no price is the org Workspaces page's dialog (t009).
