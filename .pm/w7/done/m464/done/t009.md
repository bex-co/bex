## Objective

Show the org where Render puts it: in the workspace dropdown at the top of the left pane ("open the workspace dropdown at the top of the left pane, select your org", render.com/docs/audit-logs). Give the org a Workspaces page that:

- lists its workspaces, with the Primary label and each one's privacy;
- lets an Owner create one (Public or Invite-only, Invite-only selected);
- offers Join on Public workspaces the caller is not in;
- offers Owners "Join as admin" on every workspace.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/workspaces/components/workspace-switcher.tsx`; routes `dashboard/src/routes/workspace.settings.tsx` and `new.workspace.tsx`.
- `dashboard/AGENTS.md` (Apollo, shadcn, locales in every language, skeleton rule).
- The root `AGENTS.md` skeleton rule: a pending skeleton must match the ready page's geometry at desktop and narrow mobile.

## Steps

1. Switcher:
   - group workspaces under their org, with the org's name as a selectable entry;
   - list standalone workspaces as today;
   - show a Guest only their workspace.
2. Add the org route (for example `/org/$orgId/workspaces`) and its skeleton:
   - the workspace list with Primary and privacy;
   - create, using t006's verbs and the privacy choice;
   - Join and Join as admin.
3. Add the strings to every locale.

## Outcome (2026-11-17)

The switcher (`features/workspaces/components/workspace-switcher.tsx`) groups the caller's workspaces under each org, primary first, with the org's name as an entry that opens `/org/$orgId/workspaces`; standalone workspaces follow as before, and a Guest sees only their workspace. The org's Workspaces page (`routes/org.$orgId.workspaces.tsx`, `features/organizations/**`) lists each workspace with Primary and privacy, lets an Owner create one (Public or Invite-only, Invite-only selected, "Included in your organization's plan" and no price), offers Join on Public workspaces a Member is not in and Join as admin to Owners. Strings in all 8 languages; a route-shaped pending skeleton (`organization-route-skeletons.tsx`).
