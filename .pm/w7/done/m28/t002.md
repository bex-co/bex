## Objective

Extend the Alloy log-shipper pipeline to scrape build-namespace pods and ship their stdout into Loki stamped `type=build`, attributed to the owning App via the t001 labels.

## Context

- The w3/m5 pipeline ships app pod logs; w3/m8 added Traefik access lines as `type=request` with strict cardinality rules (`TestPathAndHostNeverBecomeLabels` is the precedent — no unbounded labels).
- Shipper config lives with the gitops manifests under `deploy/gitops/base/`.

## Steps

1. Add a discovery/relabel stage for pods carrying the t001 build labels (build namespace included).
2. Stamp `type=build` + App/tenant labels; carry the generation in the line or a bounded label.
3. Deploy to the mock cluster and confirm build lines land in Loki during a git-sourced deploy.

## Files

- `deploy/gitops/base/` (Alloy/log-shipper config)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A git-sourced deploy's BuildKit output appears in Loki labeled `type=build` and attributed to the owning App.
- [ ] No new unbounded-cardinality labels (per-build uniqueness stays in the line/Job name).

## Out of scope

- Backend query path (t003).
