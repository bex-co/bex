## Steps

1. On a Scale fixture org with two workspaces, the second with its own 2FA setting off, turn org 2FA on as an Owner who has a second factor.
2. As a fixture member with no second factor, confirm the 403 in both workspaces on REST, GraphQL and MCP, on the dashboard's blocked page and over SSH. Check the org pages as a blocked Owner too.
3. Read `ChangeOrg2FAEnforcementEvent` on `GET /v1/organizations/{orgId}/audit-logs` and on the org Settings page's CSV export.
4. Turn it off and confirm the member is back within the TTL and the second workspace's own setting is unchanged.
5. Repeat for Google login where production has Google sign-in, otherwise on dev-7.
6. Compare the org Settings page's pending and ready states at desktop and narrow-mobile widths. Delete the fixtures.
