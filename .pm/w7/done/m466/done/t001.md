## Objective

Write down what Render says about an org's login settings, which parts bex has to infer, and the rule bex ships, before any code.

## Context

- Render's sentences are quoted in the milestone README (`## Source + Goal linkage`). They come from audit-logs, saml-sso and login-settings, all read 2026-11-17.
- `docs/render-artifacts/login-settings.md` captures the workspace settings only. Its "What the page does not say" section is the shape to follow.
- ADR115 D7 lists the two events as follow-ups. ADR012 §9a and §9b are the workspace rules this extends.
- `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md` keeps SAML SSO and SCIM out.

## Steps

1. Re-read Render's audit-logs, saml-sso, login-settings and organizations pages. Add an **Organizations** section to `docs/render-artifacts/login-settings.md` with the quotes, the read date, and what the pages do not say.
2. Decide each open point. Pick the Render-consistent option and mark every inference:
   - **The settings.** "Require two-factor authentication", and "Require Login Method" offering Google, both in a Security section of the org Settings page. SAML SSO is absent by `DO_NOT_DO.md`.
   - **Who changes them.** Org Owners only. Members and Guests read them.
   - **Scope.** Every org workspace enforces the org's requirement in addition to its own: the effective requirement is the OR of the two. The org's own objects (`org:<id>`) enforce it too, with the workspace setting's exempt reads, so a blocked person still sees why and can leave.
   - **Who is held to it.** Guests are, in the one workspace they belong to. So are API and SSH keys, with the rules the workspace settings use.
   - **Refusals.** Reuse `WORKSPACE_2FA_REQUIRED` and `WORKSPACE_GOOGLE_LOGIN_REQUIRED`, and add `organizationId` to the details when the org's setting is the one that refused. Decide the lock-out 409 codes for an Owner who does not meet a requirement.
   - **The plan gate.** An org is always Scale or Enterprise, but the setters still go through `core.FeatureTwoFactorEnforcement` and `core.FeatureGoogleLoginEnforcement` on the primary's plan.
   - **Surfaces.** GraphQL mutations, and read fields on GraphQL `Organization` and MCP `get_organization`. Decide on an MCP mutation: follow the workspace settings, which have none (ADR018), unless t001 finds a reason they should differ.
   - **The audit rows.** Each change writes one row in the org log, with `metadata.to` as the workspace events carry it.
   - **The enable confirmation.** Does it list the members and keys who would be blocked, as Render's SSO enforcement does?
3. Record the rule in ADR012 (§9c), ADR115 (D7 and a new decision for org login settings), ADR018 (the Workspace-enforced 2FA row and its Google sibling: the org form) and ADR030 §9 if the plan rows change.
