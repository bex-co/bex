# The social sign-in buttons sit above the email form; Render puts them below it, under "Or continue with"

Why: Render parity first. Render's login and sign-up pages lead with email and password and offer the providers after them, so bex's card reads in a different order from Render's.

- Found by w7/m468 t008 (2026-11-17), comparing dashboard.render.com/login and /register (headless, 1440 and 390) with bex's: Render shows Email, Password, "Forgot password?", "Sign in", then a divider "Or continue with", then GitHub · Google (· SSO on login) and, behind "More options", GitLab · Bitbucket. bex's Ory card draws the provider rows first, then a divider, then the email form (`dashboard/src/common/lib/ory/sso-buttons.tsx` `SsoButtonRows` as Ory Elements' `Form.SsoRoot`, which Ory Elements 1.2.1's login and registration cards render as `OryFormSsoForm` before `OryForm`).
- The skeletons draw the same order (`OryAuthCardSkeleton` in `dashboard/src/common/components/route-skeletons.tsx`), so both move together.
- End state: on login and sign-up the provider rows render after the submit button under an "Or continue with" divider in every locale, the skeletons match at 1440 and 390, and `sso-buttons.test.tsx` pins the order. The settings card's Connected accounts layout is unchanged.
- **Done 2026-11-18:** Render re-read today (dashboard.render.com/login and /register at 1440): Email, Password, "Forgot password?", "Sign in" or "Create account", then "Or continue with" between two rules, then the providers and "More options". bex's login and sign-up cards now draw that order: `AuthCardContent` (Ory's `Card.Content`, `dashboard/src/common/lib/ory/sso-buttons.tsx`) moves Ory's provider form (`OryFormSsoForm`, its own `<form>`) after the e-mail form, so the tab order follows the page, and draws "Or continue with" (`auth.ssoContinueWith`, all eight languages) when the card has a form above the providers; `AuthCardDivider` drops Ory's rule on the identifier card, where it only separated the providers from the form. A method-choice card keeps Ory's rule above its methods and also puts the providers last. `OryAuthCardSkeleton` draws the fields, the divider, then the provider rows; pending and ready measured identical at 1440 and 390 on login and sign-up (card, form, divider and provider rows to the half pixel, dashboard dev server against a stand-in Kratos). `sso-buttons.test.tsx` renders Ory's real `<Login>` and `<Registration>` and pins the order, the single pair of rules, the divider's words in every language and the skeletons' order; with the overrides removed 12 of its tests fail. The settings card's Connected accounts are unchanged. ADR018's sign-in row, ADR012 §10 and `docs/render-artifacts/login-settings.md` record it.
