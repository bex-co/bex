## Objective

Decide what happens to a deleted Database's object-store backups (barman base backups + WAL under `BEX_DB_BACKUP_DESTINATION`) and implement it. Today they silently outlive the CNPG Cluster forever — which is both a tenant-data-retention hole and unbounded S3 growth — but backups are also the accidental-delete recovery path, so an instant purge forecloses "oops" recovery.

## Context

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/database_controller.go` (~277 `backupOwnerReference: self`, ~401 no-finalizer delete path); docs/ADR009-postgresql-management.md + docs/ADR031-platform-data-backup.md are where the decision gets recorded.
- Recommended shape (decide explicitly, don't drift into it): **grace-period retention** — on Database deletion record/tag the backup prefix as deleted, purge after N days (e.g. 7) rather than instantly; Render's own deletion is documented as irreversible, so instant purge is also defensible — pick one and write it down.
- Whatever the choice, the mechanism likely needs a Database finalizer (there is none today) or a platform sweep job with access to `BEX_DB_BACKUP_ENDPOINT` + `BEX_DB_BACKUP_S3_SECRET`.
- Backups may be disabled entirely (env unset) — teardown must no-op then.

## Steps

1. Write the decision (instant purge vs grace window, and who executes it) into ADR009 + ADR031 with rationale.
2. Implement: finalizer/sweep that deletes (now or after the window) the cluster's barman prefix; IgnoreNotFound; disabled-backups ⇒ no-op.
3. Test the prefix-scoping (never a sibling cluster's backups) with a fake S3.
