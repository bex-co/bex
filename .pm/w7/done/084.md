# Sign in with GitLab and Bitbucket, Render's other two social providers

Why: Render's sign-in page offers Google, GitHub, GitLab and Bitbucket; bex offers GitHub and, since w7/m465, Google, so a GitLab or Bitbucket user cannot sign in to bex the way they sign in to Render.

- Found by w7/m465 t009, 2026-11-17 ([ADR012 §10](../../../docs/ADR012-auth.md#10-social-login-sign-in-with-github-google-gitlab-and-bitbucket-kratos-oidc-w4003-w7m465-w7m468), [ADR018](../../../docs/ADR018-render-parity.md) row "Workspace-enforced Google login").
- bex today: `scripts/lib/kratos-oidc-fragment.sh` emits `github` and `google`, each on its own `BEX_*_OIDC_CLIENT_ID`/`_SECRET` pair; Kratos v26.2.0 ships `gitlab` and `bitbucket` providers of the same shape.
- Shape: about an hour. Add `kratos_oidc_gitlab_provider` and `kratos_oidc_bitbucket_provider` (scopes `read_user openid email` and `account email`), their pairs in `.env.example`, `deploy.yml` and the DRY_RUN line, cases in `scripts/kratos-oidc-fragment.test.sh`, the provider labels and icons on the dashboard's login, sign-up and account settings pages in every locale, and ADR012 §10. The production clients are the user's to create.
- Done when: with a pair set, the login flow offers the provider and a stand-in signs a person up and back in (`scripts/auth-oidc-e2e.sh`), and with neither set nothing changes.

Promoted to [m468](../blocked/m468/README.md) on 2026-11-17.

- **Why a milestone, not the hour above:** the note's "Kratos v26.2.0 ships `gitlab` and `bitbucket`" holds for GitLab only. Kratos v26.2.0's `supportedProviders` (`selfservice/strategy/oidc/provider_config.go:186-214`, and `master` too) has no `bitbucket`. Bitbucket Cloud is OAuth 2.0 only (`https://bitbucket.org/.well-known/openid-configuration` is 404), so Kratos's discovery-based `generic` provider cannot reach it either. Bitbucket needs an OIDC bridge (Dex's `bitbucketcloud` connector), and Ory Elements 1.2.1 has no Bitbucket logo.
- **Why no user decision was needed:** Render's [login-settings](https://render.com/docs/login-settings) lists Google, GitHub, GitLab and Bitbucket (fetched 2026-11-17), so offering them is the parity choice. The bridge is m468 t002's recommendation. Production's GitLab application and Bitbucket OAuth consumer gate only m468's production walk (t007).
- **m468 covers:** GitLab sign-in, the Bitbucket bridge decision, Dex in the `auth` namespace, Bitbucket sign-in, the e2e legs, the dashboard logos and skeletons, and a production walk. That is 11 tasks with the closing ones.
