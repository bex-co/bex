## Objective

Choose signals based on the actual node/image storage topology rather than guessing from PVC usage or container paths.

## Context

- [Milestone scope, source evidence and definition of done](../README.md).
- w7/m159 observed the September 28 tenant-node DiskPressure episode and shipped condition/recovery/missing-condition alerts. Its closeout explicitly records that no node filesystem or fill-rate series are scraped, so there is no advance headroom warning. prometheus-node-exporter remains disabled in prometheus.yaml at materialization.
- Extends m159 rather than reopening it. Existing PVC/Zot capacity alerts and per-pod ephemeral-storage limits do not measure node-local filesystem headroom. The historical no-node-exporter stance allowed adding it when a needed rule cannot be supported otherwise.

## Steps

1. Read the tenant-node kubelet/container runtime configuration and map nodefs/imagefs backing mounts with non-destructive inspection.
2. Record byte/inode eviction thresholds and distinguish node-wide pressure from a pod's own ephemeral-storage limit.
3. Check whether existing collection can supply these exact signals; if not, select a filesystem-only exporter and record why it is required.
