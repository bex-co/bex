# An org invite is redeemed only by signing in with the invited email; its link carries no token, unlike a workspace invite's

Why: a workspace invite's emailed link redeems for someone who signs up under another address (`/invite#invite=<token>`), and an org invite (w7/m464) cannot, so an invitee with a second email address cannot join the org from the email.

- Found by w7/m464 t014, 2026-11-17.
- bex today: `members.InviteToOrganization` mails a link to the dashboard root and the invite is redeemed at the invitee's next sign-in by verified email (`store.AcceptOrganizationInvitesForEmail`, `internal/api/tenancy.go`). The token is minted and only its sha256 stored (`organization_invites.token_hash`), but no route redeems it: the token-accept verb was dropped because nothing delivered the token.
- Render: org members join through SSO or a workspace admin's invite (render.com/docs/organizations, read 2026-11-17); how Render's own "invite to org" mail behaves is not documented.
- Shape: about an hour. Put the token in the mail's fragment (`/invite#org-invite=<token>`), teach the dashboard's invite page (`features/invites/invite-fallback-page.tsx`, `common/lib/invite-token.ts`) to review and redeem it, and add GraphQL `acceptOrganizationInvite(token)` back with its store method (accept, already-accepted and expired refusals, AcceptOrgInviteEvent).
- Done when: an org invite's link joins the org for a signed-in user whose email differs from the invited one, and a reused link is refused by name.
