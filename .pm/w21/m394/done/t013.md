## Objective

Make the 48-hour reclaim bound on unverified custom-domain claims hold across delete and re-add, and make every add path (explicit add, Blueprint, service create) honour it.

## Context

- Finding R25-10 (Low; control plane, outside the two planes but what the activator routes on). `lego/backend/internal/store/store.go:1443-1495` `AddDomainClaim` deletes a pending row older than 48 h (`:1457`) only on a conflicting add and compares `created_at`; `DeleteDomain` (`:1683`) and `RemoveDomain` (`:1745`) just delete the row, and a re-add gets a fresh `created_at`. Real Postgres 16: the victim is still blocked at 'first claim + 50 h'; a control row untouched 49 h is reclaimed. `ReplaceDomainClaims` (`:1610`, `ON CONFLICT ... WHERE domains.app_id = EXCLUDED.app_id`) has no expiry delete and returns `ErrConflict` (Blueprint update path, `blueprint_setting_events.go:87-89`); a create is refused earlier at plan time by `planNewApp` (`apps/service.go` ~2437-2448) via `Store.DomainHostsClaimed` (`store.go:2252`, `SELECT host FROM domains WHERE host = ANY($1)`, no state or age filter).
- Quotas cap a squatter at 100 domains per service and 500 per workspace (`cmd/api/config.go:675-676`). Production requires a card-bound account. The apex 'auto-verify' interaction is the Render-parity shared-edge proof and the no-TXT decision ADR005 accepts (`docs/ADR005-custom-domain.md:139-147`); do NOT add a TXT proof (ADR005 forbids it). Render's own squatting behaviour is the same, and whether it expires unverified claims is undocumented.
- Migrations are numbered up/down pairs under `lego/backend/internal/store/migrations/` (the latest at filing was 0256: take the next free number when you start); BEFORE DELETE triggers already exist (0084, 0114, 0175); `domains.app_id` is `ON DELETE CASCADE`, so a trigger also covers app deletion. The real-Postgres backend suite gates the push.

## Steps

1. Write the failing tests first in `lego/backend/internal/store/store_pg_test.go`: `TestAddDomainClaimDeleteReaddKeepsExpiry` (attacker adds, row aged 47 h, deleted and re-added, aged 3 h more, then the victim's `AddDomainClaim` must succeed; today `ErrConflict`); `TestReplaceDomainClaimsReclaimsExpiredPendingSquat` (another app's pending claim aged 49 h, then the victim's `ReplaceDomainClaims` must succeed; today `ErrConflict`); a create-path test that `DomainHostsClaimed` ignores a pending row older than 48 h.
2. Add a migration with `domain_claim_history(host primary key, since, recorded_at)` and a BEFORE DELETE trigger that records a pending row's first-claim time; in `AddDomainClaim` insert `created_at` from the history when the gap is under about 24 h and clear the history row on reclaim.
3. Add the expiry delete (`pending`, older than 48 h, `app_id <> $1`) at the top of the `ReplaceDomainClaims` transaction and make `DomainHostsClaimed` ignore pending rows older than 48 h.
4. Amend `docs/ADR005-custom-domain.md` § Accepted residuals: record that a squatted pending claim promotes when the owner's DNS later points at the shared edge, as accepted, and that the 48 h bound now holds across delete and re-add on every add path.
5. Run `cd lego/backend && go test ./...` against the real Postgres (as `scripts/ship-gates.sh` does) and `make lint`.

## Result (2026-11-14)

- **Migration `0260_domain_claim_history`** (up/down): table `domain_claim_history(host primary key, since, recorded_at)` and three triggers on `domains`, so every way a claim is added or removed honours the bound, including the `domains.app_id` cascade of a service delete.
  - `BEFORE DELETE`: a pending row deleted inside its 48 hours records `since = created_at` (a row already past 48 hours records nothing, so a reclaim leaves no history; rows recorded over 48 hours ago are swept in the same statement).
  - `BEFORE INSERT`: a new pending row takes `since` as its `created_at` when the record is under 24 hours old and `since` is still inside 48 hours; any longer gap starts afresh.
  - `AFTER INSERT`: the host's history row is spent. `AFTER INSERT` does not run for a row `ON CONFLICT` skipped, so a refused claim cannot consume it.
- **`store.go`:** `ReplaceDomainClaims` (Blueprint sync and service create) first deletes other apps' pending claims past 48 hours on the declared hosts; `DomainHostsClaimed` (the create plan) ignores such claims, so the plan agrees with what the create's claim will reclaim; the predicate is one constant, `expiredPendingClaim`, also used by `AddDomainClaim`. Verified rows never match it.
- **Tests** (`domain_claim_expiry_pg_test.go`, real Postgres): `TestAddDomainClaimDeleteReaddKeepsExpiry` (attacker adds, aged 47 h, removed through `DeleteDomain`, `RemoveDomain` and `DeleteApp`, re-added, 3 h more: the victim's `AddDomainClaim` reclaims; before: `ErrConflict` in all three), `TestOwnerCanDeleteAndReaddAClaimInsideTheWindow` (re-add succeeds, keeps the first `created_at`, still conflicts for another app), `TestClaimHistoryIsBounded` (inherits inside 24 h; a 25 h gap or a first claim over 48 h old starts afresh; the history row is consumed), `TestVerifiedClaimLeavesNoHistory`, `TestReplaceDomainClaimsReclaimsExpiredPendingSquat` (fresh squat conflicts, 49 h squat reclaimed; before: `ErrConflict`), `TestReplaceDomainClaimsKeepsAVerifiedClaim`, `TestDomainHostsClaimedIgnoresExpiredPendingClaims`. Run against the old code with only the history table present, the delete/re-add, replace and claimed-hosts tests fail on behavior and pass with the triggers and the store changes.
- **Suites.** `internal/store` (29 s) and `internal/apps` pass against real Postgres (`BEX_TEST_REQUIRE_DEPS=1`); `make lint` exit 0; the full backend suite runs in the ship gate.
- **ADR005 § Accepted residuals** now records the bound as counted from the first claim, the triggers, what remains (an expired claim its owner renews before anyone else tries is a fresh 48 hours; a claim made while a history row is live starts with the earlier time, so the next claimant has less than 48 hours to verify), and the squatted pending claim that later verifies through the shared-edge apex proof as accepted, with no TXT proof. `scripts/adr-registry-validate.sh` needs bash 4 and does not run on this Mac's bash 3.2; no ADR was added here (ADR109 is t005).
