## Objective

Record who spawned whom, with checks that prevent cycles and runaway trees.

## Context

- Spec: ADR102. `agent_sessions` gains nullable `parent_session_id`, `root_session_id` and `depth`; a session list filter by parent.
- Store file: `lego/backend/internal/store/agentsessions.go`; migrations in `internal/store/migrations/`.

## Steps

1. Write the next free migration pair and the store methods (create with parent, list children, count live children, subtree).
2. Enforce depth and per-parent fan-out limits in the transaction that inserts the child.
3. Real-Postgres tests, including two children inserted concurrently at the fan-out limit.
