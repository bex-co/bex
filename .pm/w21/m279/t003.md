## Objective

Make `Service.Create` accept a parent, apply ADR102's inheritance, roll child spend up to the parent, and cascade cancellation.

## Context

- Spec: ADR102. Authorization stays `can_create` plus, for a child, `can_operate` on the parent.
- Spend: the child's ceiling can never exceed the parent's remaining ceiling; child cost is added to the parent's total (`internal/agentsessions/modelcost.go`).
- Secrets (ADR098) and approval mode (ADR100) inherit and may be narrowed, never loosened.

## Steps

1. Add `ParentSessionID` to the create request and validation.
2. Apply spend, secrets and approval inheritance.
3. Cancel or terminate a parent: cancel its live descendants in order and record why.
4. Tests for each refusal and for the cascade against real Postgres.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A child cannot be created with a ceiling above the parent's remaining ceiling.
- [ ] Child spend appears in the parent's total.
- [ ] Cancelling a parent cancels every live child.
