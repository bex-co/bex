## Objective

Stop passing the registry password to `skopeo` as `--src-creds user:pass` on the command line.

## Context

- `cmd/registry-migrate/main.go` builds `skopeoCopyArgs` with `--src-creds` and `--dest-creds` (line 206) and `--src-tls-verify=false`; an argv is readable by every process in the container through `/proc/<pid>/cmdline` and lands in process listings and crash reports.

## Steps

1. Red-first test on `skopeoCopyArgs`: the password appears nowhere in the arguments.
2. Write a 0600 auth file in a private temp directory (`{"auths":{host:{"auth":base64(user:pass)}}}`), pass `--src-authfile` and `--dest-authfile`, and remove it when the copy ends.
3. Leave `--src-tls-verify=false` and record why (the source is the in-cluster Zot on a private address), or scope it to that host.
