# 068 — Admission-policy gaps the round-25 audit found: no policy binds the activator ServiceAccount, `bex-operator-workloads` admits a projected crown-secret volume, a `bex.co/platform` toleration and `hostPort`, and the drain-guard policy ignores pod-template fields

Why: each is a tenant-or-token-reachable path to more privilege than the design intends, found by the round-25 audit as hardening (needs a prior compromise of the component's own token), and none had a policy test.

- **Source:** w21/m394 round 25 (ADR110 § Residuals), `~/security-audit-skill/bex-w21/run-1/REPORT.md` § Hardening notes (Admission and RBAC), 2026-11-14.
- **Size:** about 2h across three policies; promote with `/pm promote w21/068` if the tests make it larger.

## What the audit found

- The activator ServiceAccount (`lego/operator/config/activator/role.yaml`: `apps`/`deployments` patch, cluster-wide) is bound by no ValidatingAdmissionPolicy. A policy limited to the two wake annotations and `spec.replicas` 0 to 1 would bound a compromised activator.
- `deploy/gitops/base/operator-workload-admission.yaml` (`bex-operator-workloads`) admits a `projected` volume that sources a crown Secret, a `bex.co/platform` toleration, and `hostPort`.
- `deploy/gitops/base/drain-guard-admission.yaml` allows the evacuation annotation on any Deployment and ignores pod-template `finalizers`, `ownerReferences`, `generateName`, `name` and `namespace`.

## Steps

1. For each policy, write the denial case first as a test against the rendered policy (the repo's `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` and any policy fixture tests show the pattern), then tighten the CEL expression.
2. Add the activator policy and bind it to its ServiceAccount.
3. Check that the operator's own workloads, the activator's wake, and a drain still pass.

## Files

- deploy/gitops/base/operator-workload-admission.yaml, drain-guard-admission.yaml, kustomization.yaml, and a new activator admission file
- lego/operator/config/activator/role.yaml, rolebinding.yaml (read)
- scripts/gitops-validate.sh

## Acceptance

- Each denial above is a test that fails before and passes after; the operator, activator wake and drain still work on a local cluster (`scripts/mock-cluster.sh`).
