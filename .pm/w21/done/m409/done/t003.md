## Objective

Add a ValidatingAdmissionPolicy and binding that confine the activator's writes, with the denial cases first in an envtest in the drain-guard pattern.

## Context

- The activator holds `get/list/watch/patch` on `apps.app.bex.co` and `get/patch` on `deployments`, cluster-wide (`lego/operator/config/activator/role.yaml`), and no policy binds it, so a compromised activator can rewrite any App spec or any Deployment.
- What it writes (`lego/operator/cmd/activator/main.go:340-366`): on an App, the annotations `app.bex.co/last-active` and, when the phase is Hibernated, `app.bex.co/wake-started` (RFC 3339 UTC values); on its Deployment, `spec.replicas` set to 1 when it is nil or below 1.
- Pattern: `deploy/gitops/base/drain-guard-admission.yaml` (a match condition on the ServiceAccount username, variables for what may change, one validation, a Deny+Audit binding with the sync waves) and its harness `lego/operator/internal/drainguard/admission_test.go`; the App CRD is in `lego/operator/config/crd/bases`.

## Steps

1. Write the failing cases first as an envtest next to the activator (`lego/operator/cmd/activator`): allowed, the two annotations on an App with RFC 3339 values, and `spec.replicas` 0 to 1 on a Deployment; denied, any other annotation, a label, `spec`, `finalizers` or `ownerReferences` on an App, a Deployment `replicas` above 1 or any other field, a create and a delete of either.
2. Write `deploy/gitops/base/activator-admission.yaml` (policy `bex-activator-writes` and its binding, same sync waves) and add it to the base `kustomization.yaml`.
3. Run the envtest: the policy denies exactly the denied cases, and does not touch other identities.

## Out of scope
