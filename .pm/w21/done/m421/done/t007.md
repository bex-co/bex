## Objective

After the operator's queue is short, a deploy can still sit `created` or `queued` for a moment with only `==> Deploy queued`. `waitLine` (`lego/backend/internal/logs/progress.go:177`) explains registry credentials and build holds only. When the wait is the platform not having picked the deploy up yet, say so in a tenant-neutral line, and show that a build hold already reads "Waiting for platform build capacity".

## Context

- Round 179 saw an image deploy narrate only `==> Deploy queued` for 4 minutes, with no operator status on the App.
- `waitLine` is an allow-list on purpose (m99 t002): it never echoes operator text.

## Steps

1. Re-measure the wait after t002 on five image creates; if every wait is under 15 s, add the neutral line only if a wait over 5 s can still occur, otherwise close the log bullet with the numbers.
2. Tests beside `progress_test.go`.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/logs/progress.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A wait over 5 s is narrated, or the measured waits are all under 5 s and the task records that.

## Out of scope

- The deploy status values.

## Result

No narration added, with the measurement that says none is due: image-backed deploys created one after another in `bex-canary` started 0.0 to 3.0 s after create (18 of 18 in three batches of six) after the operator fix and again after t002 (before: 3, 31, 1, 2, 2, 4 s and, in rounds 179 to 181, 1 to 4 minutes), and the operator queue's p99 wait is 0.68 s. A deploy that is held for the build ceiling or a datastore is already narrated as `==> Waiting for platform build capacity` (`logs/progress.go` `waitLine`). The remaining unexplained window is the operator's own restart, tens of seconds once per platform deploy.
