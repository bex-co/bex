## Objective

Production clusters admit the new list by reload, and the definition of done's items 1 to 3 and 5 hold.

## Context

- `extwlist.extensions` is `PGC_SUSET` (pgextwlist `pgextwlist.c:177-182`); CloudNativePG reloads a changed parameter that needs no restart. Read each cluster's postmaster start time before and after to prove no restart.
- w8/276's Acceptance list is the check list. QA workspace `bex-canary`; disposable databases are deleted at the end.

## Steps

1. Ship t002 and follow it to production; confirm postmaster start times unchanged.
2. On a new Free database per major, run every `CREATE EXTENSION` and the two uses; check `rolsuper`, the refused probe, and an export and its restore.
3. Record results in ADR092 and append them to w8/276 (archived as superseded by this milestone).

## Result (2026-11-24)

The full record is in ADR092 § D4 (Production acceptance of Render's untrusted contrib modules). It ran on pin `f46c44d9ddb1` in `bex-canary`, as the default user over the external host (TLS 1.3 with `sslmode=require`, `psql` 18.6), on one new Free database per major, one at a time: `qa-m585-pg13` (13.22), `-pg14` (14.25), `-pg15` (15.20), `-pg16` (16.16), `-pg17` (17.12) and `-pg18` (18.7), all (disposable).

- **Step 1 (no restart):** the first pin carrying `979d68fdc1` also brought m584's `b8c7ac47b2`, so the list arrived with m584's one restart. At 00:51Z and again at 01:30Z, all six operator-managed clusters (first-party workspaces, `qa-m586-existing-pg16` among them) had the same postmaster start time from that restart (00:04–00:18Z), 0 container restarts and `Cluster in healthy state`. Each `extwlist.extensions` lists the contrib names for its major.
- **Step 2:** on every major, every `CREATE EXTENSION` of definition-of-done item 1 succeeded: 22 of 22 on 13, 24 of 24 on 14, 15 and 16, and 22 of 22 on 17 and 18. `earth_distance(ll_to_earth(0,0), ll_to_earth(0,1))` is `111320.03184534168`. A `moddatetime` trigger set `modified` on the one updated row of 10. `rolsuper` is `false` and `is_superuser` `off` before and after, and `CREATE EXTENSION postgres_fdw` is refused before and after (`permission denied to create extension "postgres_fdw"`). The superuser-only functions stay refused: `get_raw_page`, `bt_index_check`, `heap_force_kill`, `pg_file_write`, `file_fdw`'s `CREATE SERVER`, a password-less `dblink_connect` and `SET pgaudit.log`. On each major, the owner was in `pg_monitor` within 7 s of the first connection.
- **Export and restore:** the owner's `pg_dump -Fc` over the external URL, restored by the owner into a new database it created, gave back every extension and the data using them on every major: the `earth` column, the trigger, the `bloom` index and the `xsyn_template` dictionary. A plain `pg_restore --no-owner --no-privileges --exit-on-error` stops on every major at `COMMENT ON EXTENSION pg_stat_statements` (`must be owner of extension pg_stat_statements`). That is w21/124's open fault, reproduced here by hand on production, so the restore left out that extension's two archive entries. Below 17, pg_restore 18.6's `SET transaction_timeout = 0` line was dropped, because no client of the database's own major could reach production from this machine (its Docker VM had no egress).
- **Step 3:** ADR092 § D4, ADR018's "Postgres extensions" rows, and w8/276's closing record.
- **Cleanup:** the six databases answer 404. The QA API key `qa-m585-t003` is revoked, and the session is logged out.
