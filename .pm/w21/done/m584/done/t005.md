## Objective

Production's managed Postgres clusters run the bex operand image.

## Context

- `bexOperandImages` (`lego/operator/internal/controller/database_operand.go:36`) takes precedence over `cnpgExportImages` per major; adopting a digest restarts every managed cluster of that major once, and the export Jobs follow the serving image.
- The single rolling restart is user-approved (w8/m92 README, 2026-10-29). ADR092 D4 asks for an off-peak window, health checks before and after, and a recorded rollback (empty the map entry).
- The adoption also changes `shared_buffers`' default for clusters that set none (`pgExtensionParameters`, `database_operand.go:155`).

## Steps

1. Count managed clusters per major and read each one's health before.
2. Commit the six digests, ship, and follow the platform pin to production.
3. Watch each cluster restart once and come back healthy; record counts, the window and any cluster that needed the rollback.

## Files

- lego/operator/internal/controller/database_operand.go
- docs/ADR092-postgres-extension-supply.md (D4 record)

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every managed cluster reports the bex image and one restart since adoption; none is unhealthy after.
- [x] The rollback is written down and was not needed, or its use is recorded.

## Out of scope

## Result (2026-11-24)

- **Window:** pin `f46c44d9ddb1` (`eb52a59622`, 23:57Z) carries `b8c7ac47b2`; the bex operator rolled out at 00:00:58Z and every managed `Cluster` changed within four seconds. The restarts ran 00:01–00:17Z (16:01 Pacific): the deploy's time, not a chosen off-peak hour.
- **Four rolled by themselves** within 3 minutes: `beancount-forum-db`, `tianpan-forum-db`, `blockeden-forum-db` (all `bex`) and `qa-m584-existing-pg18` (`bex-canary`, (disposable)).
- **Three stuck** on their upstream image: `twenty-postgres` (`bex`, PG16), `qa-20261117-m453-hobby` (`tian-personal`, PG18) and `qa-m586-existing-pg16` (`bex-canary`, (disposable)). CloudNativePG reloaded the new `local_preload_libraries = pgextwlist` into the instance still on the upstream image, and from 00:01:01Z every new connection failed with `FATAL: could not access file "$libdir/plugins/pgextwlist"` (58P01). The instance manager's status probe failed too, so CloudNativePG never rolled the pod (`Cannot extract Pod status`).
- **Remedy:** `kubectl delete pod <cluster>-1` recreated each pod on its PVC with the bex digest. The fixture was back at 00:13:34Z, `qa-20261117-m453-hobby` at 00:14:42Z and `twenty-postgres` at 00:17:19Z (after a 3-minute smart shutdown), so new connections were refused for about 12, 14 and 16 minutes. Open sessions kept working, and no app pod restarted. The operator fix is filed as w21/125.
- **Health after (00:20Z):** all seven `Cluster in healthy state`, one pod start since the pin and 0 container restarts, on the `16.16`/`18.7-postgres-6c11b6b0f678…` digests. `shared_preload_libraries` is `pg_stat_statements,timescaledb,pg_ivm,pg_duckdb,pgaudit` and `local_preload_libraries` is `pgextwlist`. Every app database keeps its extensions, and `vector` 0.8.6 answers a distance query on the 0.8.7 library. The fixture's `m584_before` row survived the restart. The four platform clusters stayed on `18.4-system-trixie`, healthy.
- **Rollback:** not needed. The record is in ADR092 § D4 (Rollout record).
