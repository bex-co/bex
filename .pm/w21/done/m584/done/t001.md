## Objective

ADR092 says an extension that fails a D3 property stays out of the allowlist; it no longer says the image waits for every extension.

## Context

- [ADR092](../../../../../docs/ADR092-postgres-extension-supply.md) D3: "If neither satisfies them on some major, the implementation stops and records it here rather than weakening a property", and the "Actual D3 result — 2026-10-31" paragraph that parked the whole image on TimescaleDB's ordinary-owner restore.
- The user's 2026-10-29 decision (w8/m92 README, `/unblock w8` topic 2) chose the image, the on-demand activation and one approved rolling restart. None of that changes.
- pgextwlist defines `extwlist.extensions` as `PGC_SUSET` (`dimitri/pgextwlist` `pgextwlist.c:177-182`), so a later allowlist change is a reload, not a restart. The preload libraries (`pgExtensionPreloadLibraries`, `database_operand.go:69`) stay projected for every extension in the image, allowlisted or not.

## Steps

1. Amend D3 item 5 and the "Actual D3 result" paragraph: each extension is gated on its own; a failure keeps that extension out of `extwlist.extensions` on that major and is recorded with its exact error; the image is adopted with the passed set.
2. Add to D4: a later admission is a reload of `extwlist.extensions`, with no restart, and the image keeps every preload from the start.
3. Record the amendment as the plan's recommendation under ADR008, not as a user decision, and point the ADR's status line at w21/m584.
