## Objective

Make a `type=build` or `type=predeploy` read of one deploy return only that deploy's lines: add `LogQuery.Deploy`, resolve the deploy's release generation, drop the other deploys' platform lines, and drop their build or pre-deploy Job pods from the Loki selector and the live pre-deploy read.

## Context

- Two deploys that overlap in time share every line in either window (`use-deploy-logs.ts` reads `createdAt..finishedAt` over the whole service); platform lines carry the deploy as `instance`, build pods only their pod name.
- A build Job is `bld-<name>-gen-<generation>` and a pre-deploy Job `predeploy-<name>-gen-<generation>` (`BuildRevision(generation)`); `store.Deploy.Generation` is the release generation.
- Fail open: only lines attributed to another deploy are dropped, so a kpack buildpack pod (named for its Image) stays visible, and an unknown deploy or a store error reads as the window alone.

## Steps

1. Add `PreDeployJobName` beside `BuildJobName` in `lego/types/v1alpha1/jobnames.go` (the operator's `predeploy.JobName` delegates).
2. `DeployProgress.Generation`, `LogQuery.Deploy`/`excludeJobs`, `scopeToDeploy`, `excludesPod` in `keepPod`, the platform-line filter, `excludeJobsMatcher` in the Loki selector, and the `validate()` rule (build or predeploy only).

## Files

- lego/types/v1alpha1/jobnames.go, lego/operator/internal/predeploy/predeploy.go
- lego/backend/internal/logs/{service,progress,loki}.go, lego/backend/internal/api/server.go

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `TestDeployScope*`, `TestExcludesPod*`, `TestDeployFilterApplies*` in `lego/backend/internal/logs/deploy_scope_test.go` pass and fail when the scoping is removed.

## Out of scope

- Attributing a kpack buildpack build's pod to a deploy (its pod is named for its Image, not the build Job; it stays visible on every deploy's page), and the live SSE tail, which is already per build pod.
