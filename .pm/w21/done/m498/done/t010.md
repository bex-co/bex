## Objective

Make quota and egress limits hold for what passes through bex-s3 — the gate must see completed writes, and reads must not burn the shared Wasabi allowance.

## Context

- ADR112 D8 (Gate, caps) and D3 (Concurrency, pacing and rates; Egress is the exposure here too). `quota.go` reserves nothing today; the gate must add a write-through counter of completed S3 puts, copies and multipart completions per workspace and region, the declared length of this replica's puts and parts in flight, and the `bytes` of unfinished multipart uploads (the hook t013 fills).
- Constants in `internal/objectstorage/limits.go` (bex's own, unverified, each a dated ADR018 entry): 16 streams per workspace on each replica within the replica-wide 64 (`MaxConcurrentStreamsPerReplica`), reads paced at 8 MiB/s per workspace per replica (a rate-limited writer, not D4's debt-and-429), uploads 16 MiB/s, 100 requests a second per workspace (burst 200) and 50 per key, 5 listings a second, 100 open multipart uploads per workspace, and a monthly S3 egress budget of the larger of the stored bytes and 5 GiB (403 `QuotaExceeded`).
- Refusals are 503 `SlowDown` (clients retry it) except the quota, which is 403 `QuotaExceeded` with a message naming the cap. Deletes and overwrites free quota only at the next observation (up to 72 h): documented, not fixed (D10).

## Steps

1. Add the counter (table or in-memory with a persisted floor) and fold it into the gate.
2. Implement the pacing writer, the request and listing limiters and the egress budget check against the existing egress meter.
3. Test the gate with concurrent puts on two simulated replicas and the pacing with a ten-range download of a large object.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Completed puts count against the cap before the next sweep; a 1 GiB download with ten parallel 8 MiB ranges succeeds under the pacing and does not hit a 429.
- [x] Each constant has a test of its refusal and the dated ADR018 entries are listed for m499.

## Out of scope

- Counting deletes incrementally (ADR112 D10).

## Result

Done 2026-11-19. Migration 0303 `object_storage_writes` is the write-through counter (bytes and objects of completed puts, copies and, in t013, multipart completions) that the gate adds to the last observation until the sweep clears it, so it holds on every replica; `quota_s3.go` adds the in-flight reservation, `pacing.go` paces reads at 8 MiB/s and uploads at 16 MiB/s per workspace on each replica with `golang.org/x/time/rate`, `limiter.go` caps requests (100/s per workspace burst 200, 50/s per key burst 100) and listings (5/s) with 503 SlowDown, never 429, and the monthly egress budget is max(stored bytes, 5 GiB). `limits_test.go` has one test per refusal, including ten parallel ranges under the pacing with no refusal; each was mutation-checked. **ADR018 entries for m499 t005, each dated 2026-11-19 and marked unverified (bex's own constants; changing one is a reviewed code change with a new dated entry):** 100 GB and 1,000,000 objects per workspace per region; 1,000 buckets per workspace; 5 GiB per object; 16 concurrent streams per workspace on each replica and the replica-wide 64; 5 listings a second per workspace per replica; reads paced at 8 MiB/s and uploads at 16 MiB/s per workspace per replica; 100 requests a second per workspace (burst 200) and 50 per key (burst 100); 100 open multipart uploads per workspace and the 7-day abort; a monthly S3 egress budget of max(stored bytes, 5 GiB); 100 persistent and 20 ephemeral live keys per workspace and creator; 20 failed authentications a minute per client address.
