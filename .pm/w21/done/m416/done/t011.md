## Objective

Record what customers use without charging for it, following the sandbox snapshot-storage precedent.

## Context

- `docs/ADR112-object-storage.md` D8: `object_storage_gb_seconds` from the sweep, `object_storage_ingress_bytes` and `object_storage_egress_bytes` (exact on Contract B and objectgw, an estimate on Contract A from the size a GET URL mint HEADs). Shadow only: no Stripe Price, shown at $0 like `sandbox_memory_gb_seconds`.
- Precedent: `lego/backend/internal/store/sandbox_storage.go`, `lego/backend/internal/pricing/pricing.go`, `docs/ADR023-usage-metering.md` § Sandbox snapshot storage.

## Result

Three usage kinds, `object_storage_gb_seconds`, `object_storage_ingress_bytes` and `object_storage_egress_bytes`, recorded at $0. Migration `0274_object_storage_meters` widens the kind CHECK on `usage_hourly` and `usage_monthly` and adds `object_storage_usage.gb_remainder`. GB-seconds accrue in the sweep, integrating the mean of two observations over the seconds between them, split at UTC hour boundaries, with the remainder carried (a first observation accrues nothing). `Service.RecordIngress` and `RecordEgress` sum in memory per workspace and hour and flush every minute and at shutdown (`SetUsageSink`, `RunMeter`; a failed flush keeps the sums); the Cloudflare routes record exact bytes (a completed put's length, the bytes a get actually streamed, counted once however many times the body is closed) and `MintGet` records the HEAD size as the Contract A estimate. Billing: `stripeMeterEvent` explicitly skips the three kinds (before this change its default branch would have sent each to Stripe under its own name), `rateFor` returns 0, `unitFor` names their units, and `TestBillableMeterNamesArePinned` asserts no `object_storage*` name is billable; `/v1/usage` lists them at $0. `scripts/stripe-billing-setup.py` is untouched: no Stripe item or dimension is added. Not done here: the dashboard's charges card has no label for the new kinds (it falls back to the raw kind name), and the sweep has no metrics or alert.
