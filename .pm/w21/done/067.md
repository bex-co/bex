# 067 — A cold static-site page load with more than 3 concurrent uncached assets from one visitor answers 503 on the extras, which Render's CDN never does

Why: Render serves a static site from its CDN with no documented per-visitor limit, while bex's static server answers `503 server busy` (Retry-After 1) when a visitor has more than 3 origin fetches in flight, and a deploy empties every replica's cache, so the first visitor after a publish can get a broken page.

- **Source:** w21/m394 t018 (Render parity check of t009), 2026-11-14. Not a regression of one commit: the per-site cap of 4 already shed the 5th concurrent cold fetch; t009's per-client cap of 3 (`lego/operator/internal/staticserver/clientkey.go`, `defaultMaxClientFetches`) moves the line one asset earlier for one visitor.
- **Evidence:** `fetchgate.go` and `clientkey.go` never wait: a request over a cap fails at once (`serveSiteBusy`, `staticserver.go:1071`), a browser does not retry a failed subresource, and a new revision is a new key prefix, so every object is cold on every replica after a deploy. No publish-time warm-up exists (`grep -i warm` finds only comments). Render's static-sites page (render.com/docs/static-sites, read 2026-11-14) says the site is served "over a global CDN" that caches "content on network edges around the world", and documents no per-visitor, per-IP or concurrent-request limit and no 503.
- **Size:** about 1h. If it grows, `/pm promote`.

## Steps

1. Measure it: a fake origin with 20 ms latency and one client fetching 12 distinct cold assets at once against the real handler; record how many answer 503 today.
2. Choose the smallest fix that keeps the R25-05 bound: let an over-cap request wait for a slot for up to about 1 s (a bounded number of waiters per replica, so goroutines stay bounded), then shed; or warm a revision's index page's linked assets at publish.
3. Keep the R25-05 regression tests (`client_gate_test.go`: one client cannot starve other tenants' sites) passing.

## Files

- lego/operator/internal/staticserver/fetchgate.go, clientkey.go, staticserver.go
- lego/operator/internal/staticserver/client_gate_test.go

## Acceptance

- A visitor's cold page of 12 assets loads without a 503 on a fresh replica, and one client still cannot hold other tenants' slots.

## Done (2026-11-14)

- **Measured first (step 1):** one client fetching 12 distinct cold assets at once against the real handler, origin latency 20 ms: with the wait off (`admission.wait = 0`, the old behavior) the extras were shed with 503; the regression test keeps that as its control and asserts all 12 answer 200 with the wait on.
- **Fix (step 2):** `lego/operator/internal/staticserver/admission.go`. A miss over its client cap (3), site cap (4) or the replica's 16 slots waits up to 1 s for a release and holds nothing while it waits, so R25-05 stands: one client still cannot take other tenants' slots and the origin never sees more than the caps. Waiters are bounded at 128 per replica and 48 per client (a page of about 50 cold assets); over either bound, or after the wait, the request is shed as before with 503 and `Retry-After: 1`. The choice was a wake-all on release, not per-key queues: measured at about 1.3 µs per waiter (roughly 170 µs for 128), which only costs anything while the replica is already saturated. Warming a revision's linked assets at publish was not needed.
- **Found in review:** a fetch the gate refused had taken its client's slot for a moment and gave it back without waking the waiters, so a waiter of the same client could sleep out its second although a slot was free; the refusal now notifies, with a test that fails without it.
- **Tests (step 3):** `admission_test.go` (cold page of 12, the wait is short and ends in the usual shed, waiter caps per client and per replica, a waiting client holds no slot for others, a cancelled request leaves at once, the refusal wake-up); each was shown to fail with its rule removed (waiters never woken, no per-client cap, no replica cap, context ignored, wait never ends). `client_gate_test.go`'s R25-05 tests pass unchanged, with `-race`.
- **Docs:** ADR110's per-client gate paragraph says what a miss over a cap now does.
- **Not done:** per-key FIFO queues (a woken waiter can lose a round to a newcomer, but only to time out after a full second of losing, where it used to fail at once).
