## Objective

Serve `wrangler r2 bucket create|list|info|delete` against the registry.

## Context

- `docs/ADR112-object-storage.md` D1, D3: `POST` creates a row (no Wasabi call), `GET` lists the workspace's buckets across regions, `DELETE` checks emptiness with a one-key `ListObjectsV2`; `PATCH` (storage class) and `storageClass: InfrequentAccess` answer `10042`.
- Location hints: `wnam` and the default give `oregon`; `weur` and the jurisdiction `eu` (header `cf-r2-jurisdiction`) give `frankfurt`; others 400. bex ignores the jurisdiction when looking a name up. `bucket info` reports the hint the bucket was created with.
- `bucket info` also POSTs a GraphQL query to `<base>/graphql`; unanswered, wrangler shows zeros (verified with wrangler 4.162.0). Answer `getR2StorageMetrics` from `object_storage_usage`.

## Steps

1. Add the four bucket handlers and the `PATCH` refusal; map name, cap, conflict and not-empty errors to `10005`, `10009`, `10073`, `10008`.
2. Add the GraphQL answer for `getR2StorageMetrics` under the Cloudflare prefix.
3. Test with the fake S3 and the store.

## Files

- [ ] `bucket info` shows the last sweep's bytes and objects.

## Out of scope

## Result

`cloudflare_r2_buckets.go`: `GET /buckets` (ordered by name, with Cloudflare's published query `name_contains`, `order=name`, `direction`, `start_after`, `per_page` 1 to 1000 and `cursor`, and `result_info`; `per_page` defaults to 1000 because wrangler needs every bucket in one call), create (the body is read leniently, since wrangler sends JSON as `text/plain`; a hint and `cf-r2-jurisdiction` become a region through `RegionFromHint`; `InfrequentAccess` is `10042`), info, delete (emptiness checked on Wasabi, `10008`; a listing reads the buckets without their CORS and lifecycle rules, `ListObjectBucketSummaries`, since a full workspace would otherwise load up to a gigabyte to print names), `PATCH` answering `10042`, and `POST /cloudflare/client/v4/graphql` answering `getR2StorageMetrics` from the sweep's `object_storage_usage` (a bucket the sweep has not seen answers an empty group list, which wrangler prints as zeros; a `eu_` or `fedramp_` prefix on the name is dropped; any other operation is 400). The location is reported in Cloudflare's published lower-case spelling, as the hint it was created with or the region's default (`wnam`, `weur`), and the jurisdiction always as `default`. Real wrangler 4.162.0 ran `bucket create` (plain, `--location weur`, `--jurisdiction eu`, `--storage-class Standard`, refused `enam` and `InfrequentAccess`, a duplicate), `list`, `info` and `delete`.
