- **Enable at 22:01:20:** `PUT …/domains/managed {"enabled":true}` answers `enabled: true`.
- **Reads over the next ~30 s alternated between `200` and `404` by replica.** The pre-enable miss is cached for `LookupTTL = 30 * time.Second`, misses included (`lego/backend/internal/objectgw/resolver.go:49-51`), so a replica that cached it answers 404 until it expires. This is by design.
- **From 22:02:06 every read held:**
  - Plain GET: `200`, ×8.
  - `If-None-Match` with the ETag, and `If-Modified-Since` in the future: `304`.
  - `Range: bytes=0-4`: `206` with `content-range: bytes 0-4/18`.
  - HEAD: `200`.
  - `PUT` and `DELETE`: `405`.
  - A missing key and `/`: `404` (no listing).
- **Unsatisfiable range:** `Range: bytes=100-` answered `200` with the whole object on a replica that had not cached it (Wasabi relays it so), and `416 content-range: bytes */18` once cached. Both are permitted by RFC 9110; noted, not filed.
- **CORS:**
  - After `cors set` with origin `https://example.com`, methods `GET` and `HEAD`, header `range`, expose `ETag` and `Content-Range`, `maxAge 600`:
    - A GET from that origin carries `access-control-allow-origin` and the expose headers on every replica at once.
    - A GET from another origin gets only `vary: Origin`.
  - Preflight:
    - The allowed method answers `204` with `allow-methods: GET, HEAD`, `allow-headers: range` and `max-age: 600`.
    - A preflight for `PUT` answers `403`.
- **Disable at 22:02:56:** the last `200` was at 22:03:08 and the first `404` at 22:03:11. All 41 probes from then to 22:04:53 answered `404`.
