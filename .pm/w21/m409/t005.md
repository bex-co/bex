## Objective

Apply the three changed policies to the local CAPD cluster and show the real flows are not refused.

## Context

- `scripts/mock-cluster.sh` brings up the kind/CAPD cluster and `scripts/mock-cluster.sh operator` builds and deploys the operator; local environments are pre-approved (root `AGENTS.md`), and the run reports what it rebuilt.
- A hibernated App woken through the activator exercises the activator policy; a node roll exercises drain-guard (its envtest already covers the shape); deploying `examples/whoami-app.yaml` exercises operator-workloads.

## Steps

1. Apply `activator-admission.yaml`, `drain-guard-admission.yaml` and `operator-workload-admission.yaml` to the cluster.
2. Deploy the sample App, let it hibernate (or suspend and resume it), send a request through the activator and watch it wake; run a drain or the drain-guard flow if the cluster supports a node roll.
3. Record the observed results (counts, statuses and the absence of `ValidatingAdmissionPolicy` denials in the operator and activator logs) in the milestone README.

## Files

- scripts/mock-cluster.sh (run, not edited)
- examples/whoami-app.yaml (run)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The sample App deploys and wakes with the policies applied and the logs show no policy denial; what could not be exercised locally is named.
