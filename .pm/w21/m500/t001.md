## Objective

Prove the S3 surface on production with the stock clients, with the QA user's workspaces as the tenants, and answer the probes ADR112 left open.

## Context

- ADR112 § Tasks (t109) and § Not verified. Production access: `bash scripts/fetch-app-kubeconfig.sh <path outside the repo>` (loads `.env` itself; SSH key `~/.ssh/bex`); never park on "no access" before trying it.
- Cases: 0 B, 1 B and a 20 MiB multipart round trip byte-identical with aws-cli, rclone and boto3; a 1 GiB multipart upload; `aws s3 sync` of a tree; `aws s3 mv` of a 20 MiB object; a cross-workspace key refused; a revoked key; a presigned URL's expiry; a creator who lost the role; `Content-Length` and trailer handling on production Wasabi; a rollout (deploy) during an upload.
- Probes: which addressing style and checksum framing each stock client sends by default; whether the trailer body reaches bex-s3 intact through Traefik and whether `100-continue` is refused before the first body byte; Wasabi's multipart limits, `UploadPartCopy`, conditional writes, `x-amz-meta-*` on a presigned PUT and `Range` past the end on an SDK get; 16 streams of 5 GiB against `bex-s3`'s memory and CPU (set the Deployment's limits from it); whether the CLI's device login can mint a key without a second login.
- Use only your own fixtures (throwaway buckets and keys), remove every fixture, port-forward, temp kubeconfig and session afterwards; never print a secret or a signed URL; report what could not be observed.

## Steps

1. Run each case and probe on production and record the result with evidence.
2. Size `bex-s3`'s resource limits from the measurement and file the change if they differ.
3. Write the results into ADR112 § Not verified with dates.
