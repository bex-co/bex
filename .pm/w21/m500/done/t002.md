## Objective

Remove the Cloudflare-shaped surface once nothing uses it.

## Context

- ADR112 D3 (Removal of Contract B). Gate: a count of requests to the `/cloudflare/client/v4` routes over the previous seven days from the router's metrics must be zero, and no non-system bucket exists outside the QA workspaces.
- Remove: `lego/backend/internal/api/cloudflare_r2*.go` and their tests, the wiring in `api/server.go`, `lego/backend/internal/api/testdata/wrangler-4.162.0`, the scope-matrix entries and overrides (`scope_matrix_ops.go`, `scope_matrix_overrides.go`) and the guard-test census entries (`alwayspublic_guard_test.go`, `scope_matrix_guard_test.go`, the audit-verb tests, `auth_apikey_surfaces_test.go`), the `/cloudflare/client/v4` IngressRoute and its `kustomization.yaml` and `deployment.yaml` references, and what only they used in `internal/objectstorage` (`RegionFromHint`, `DefaultLocationHint`, `MaxObjectBytesStreamed`, the `UpdateBucket` and `ListCustomDomains` audit verbs; `make lint`'s whole-program dead-code pass finds the rest). The `location_hint` column goes in a later migration numbered above `origin/main`'s highest.
- The new `objects.` route (m498/t007) already serves S3, so removing the old one leaves no gap.
