## Objective

Record which CA a host's certificate comes from and when it changes.

## Context

- `docs/ADR005-custom-domain.md` (Certificates); Render's tls page quoted in the milestone README.
- cert-manager has no issuer fallback of its own: the selection is the operator's.

## Steps

1. Write the rule: Let's Encrypt by default; Google Trust Services after a CAA refusal for Let's Encrypt, an ACME `rateLimited`, or N failed orders; a CAA lookup when the domain is added picks Google Trust Services at once when only `pki.goog` is allowed.
2. State what moves a host back, if anything, and that renewals stay on the CA that issued.
3. Add the ADR018 custom-domains row for the CA set, pointing at this milestone.
