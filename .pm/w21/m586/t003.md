## Objective

New databases are created without `vector`; existing ones keep it and are never reconciled into dropping it.

## Context

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/database_extensions.go:85` builds the `<db>-app` CloudNativePG Database child with `extensions: [{name: vector, ensure: present}]` and `databaseReclaimPolicy: retain`; `reasonInitializingExtensions` (`database_extensions.go:56`) and `database_controller.go:1819` keep a never-served database `creating` until it applies.
- A database on an upstream image (a major whose digest is not adopted) still needs the preinstall, because it has no allowlist.
- Verify in an envtest (or on a kind cluster with CloudNativePG 1.30.1) what deleting the child, or removing the extension entry, does to an installed extension: only an `ensure: absent` drops it.

## Steps

1. Decide by observed CloudNativePG behavior how an existing database stops being managed for `vector` without a drop (keep the child without the extension entry, or delete the child under `retain`), and implement it.
2. New databases on a bex operand image: no vector entry and no extension wait. Databases on an upstream image: unchanged.
3. Keep `ExtensionsReady` meaningful or remove it where nothing remains to apply; adjust the status mapping.
