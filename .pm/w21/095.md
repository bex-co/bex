# 095 — Wasabi may count the hourly sweep's listing bytes against the egress ratio

Why: a page of 1,000 keys is about 215 KB, so a workspace at the object cap lists 215 MB per region per pass, about 155 GB a month at one pass an hour, against about 67 GB of free egress on the whole shared Wasabi account, and I could not find whether Wasabi counts list responses as egress.

- **Source:** w21/088, 2026-11-15. One page of 1,000 keys measured from a pod in the production cluster to `bex-objects-oregon` with the platform credential: 215,226 bytes of XML, 1.26 to 1.28 s. The cap is `MaxWorkspaceObjects` = 1,000,000 per workspace per region ([ADR112 D8](../../docs/ADR112-object-storage.md)), and the free egress figure is D4's measurement of 2026-11-16.
- **Arithmetic:** 1,000,000 objects / 1,000 per page = 1,000 pages x 215 KB = 215 MB per region per pass; x 24 passes x 30 days = about 155 GB a month for one workspace at the cap in one region. Ten such workspaces would be ten times that. Small workspaces are not the problem: a workspace with under 1,000 objects lists in one 274-byte answer.
- **Unknown:** whether Wasabi's "egress" for the fair-use ratio includes the response bodies of LIST requests (ADR112 D4 reads the fair-use rule as monthly egress at or below active storage). Neither Wasabi's pricing FAQ nor its API pages that I read says.

## Steps

1. Find out: ask Wasabi support or find the policy text, or measure it. The measure is cheap: read the account's reported egress in the console or `GetUsage`, list a known large prefix N times, and read it again.
2. If list responses count, choose from: sweeping a big workspace less often than hourly (the pass already orders by oldest observation, so a per-workspace interval is a small change), lowering the object cap, or keeping the counts incrementally on the writes bex-api and objectgw see, so a listing is only a reconciliation. Pick the parity-sufficient one and file the variants.
3. Write the answer, its source and the date in ADR112 D4 and D8, and in the egress alert's notes if the sweep belongs in its meter.

## Progress, 2026-11-16 (w21/095 worker)

- **Step 1, the policy text: it counts.** Wasabi's documentation says a listing's metadata reads are downloads in its utilization and that its egress tool measures LIST (quotes, pages and dates in [ADR112 D8](../../docs/ADR112-object-storage.md)). The pricing FAQ is silent, which is why this note said unknown.
- **Step 1, the measurement: set up, reading due.** At 16:34 UTC three throwaway buckets in `us-west-1` (`bex-w21-095-probe-list`, `-small`, `-get`) took 400 full-page lists, 1,000 one-key lists and 6 GETs of an 8 MiB object; a fourth (`-logged`, with `-logs` as its log target) took a few lists to read Wasabi's per-request log, which records `BytesSent` `-` for a LIST. Wasabi's counters update once a day, so the account counter is readable from **2026-11-17 00:40 UTC**.
- **Step 2, shipped:** a region is listed again only after a gap of an hour per 100 objects (at most 72 hours), the sweep counts its estimated listing bytes, `ObjectStorageSweepListingHigh` and its panel watch them, and the ADR has the arithmetic.

### What is left: read the counter, then close

1. Read the per-bucket usage of 2026-11-16 UTC with the root credential in `.env` (`TF_STATE_ACCESS_KEY`, `TF_STATE_SECRET_KEY`; never print them): `GET https://stats.wasabisys.com/v1/standalone/utilizations/bucket?from=2026-11-16&to=2026-11-17&pageSize=100` with `Authorization: <access key>:<secret key>`. A record per bucket per day appears at about 00:06 to 00:35 UTC the next day; look at `Bucket`, `DownloadBytes` and `NumAPICalls` for the five probe buckets.
2. What the bytes mean. `-list`: 403 full pages were read, 100,470,318 bytes of response. `-small`: 1,000 one-key lists, 652,000 bytes. `-get`: 50,331,648 bytes. If `DownloadBytes` of `-list` is about 100 MB, a listing counts at its response size and the ADR's arithmetic is exact; if it is a small fraction, a listing counts for less than its size (write the ratio down, and `SweepKeyBytes` and the budget can be lowered); if it is about zero, Wasabi's utilization does not count a listing and the text above is the whole of it (state that in D8 and decide, with the numbers, whether to relax `SweepHourlyObjects`, since the scaling then costs freshness for nothing).
3. Write the reading, with its date, into the last line of D8's bullet ("Reading of the account counter"), in D8's "Measured" line (which says "Results: pending") and in the Wasabi item of Not verified.
4. Delete the five probe buckets: list each with the root credential, delete every object (a multi-object delete), then the bucket. Then move this note to `done/` and tick it in the workstream README.
