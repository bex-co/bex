# 095 — Wasabi may count the hourly sweep's listing bytes against the egress ratio

Why: a page of 1,000 keys is about 215 KB, so a workspace at the object cap lists 215 MB per region per pass, about 155 GB a month at one pass an hour, against about 67 GB of free egress on the whole shared Wasabi account, and I could not find whether Wasabi counts list responses as egress.

- **Source:** w21/088, 2026-11-15. One page of 1,000 keys measured from a pod in the production cluster to `bex-objects-oregon` with the platform credential: 215,226 bytes of XML, 1.26 to 1.28 s. The cap is `MaxWorkspaceObjects` = 1,000,000 per workspace per region ([ADR112 D8](../../docs/ADR112-object-storage.md)), and the free egress figure is D4's measurement of 2026-11-16.
- **Arithmetic:** 1,000,000 objects / 1,000 per page = 1,000 pages x 215 KB = 215 MB per region per pass; x 24 passes x 30 days = about 155 GB a month for one workspace at the cap in one region. Ten such workspaces would be ten times that. Small workspaces are not the problem: a workspace with under 1,000 objects lists in one 274-byte answer.
- **Unknown:** whether Wasabi's "egress" for the fair-use ratio includes the response bodies of LIST requests (ADR112 D4 reads the fair-use rule as monthly egress at or below active storage). Neither Wasabi's pricing FAQ nor its API pages that I read says.

## Steps

1. Find out: ask Wasabi support or find the policy text, or measure it. The measure is cheap: read the account's reported egress in the console or `GetUsage`, list a known large prefix N times, and read it again.
2. If list responses count, choose from: sweeping a big workspace less often than hourly (the pass already orders by oldest observation, so a per-workspace interval is a small change), lowering the object cap, or keeping the counts incrementally on the writes bex-api and objectgw see, so a listing is only a reconciliation. Pick the parity-sufficient one and file the variants.
3. Write the answer, its source and the date in ADR112 D4 and D8, and in the egress alert's notes if the sweep belongs in its meter.

## Done when

- [ ] ADR112 says whether Wasabi counts list responses as egress, with its source, and the sweep's listing volume is within the ratio's headroom or is reduced.
