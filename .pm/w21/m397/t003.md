## Objective

Change the `/pm` skill so that closing or moving an item rewrites the links that point at its old path, and so that the next free `mN` and `NNN` is computed from `origin/main` as well as the local tree.

## Context

- Today the `done` subcommand and the Closeout task move items to `done/` and `blocked/` without touching inbound links, which is how 12 of the 71 broken links arose (`.claude/skills/pm/SKILL.md`, `.pm/AGENTS.md`).
- Numbering today: "next free zero-padded 3-digit … Scan the tree first". Each worker has its own checkout, so two workers can take the same next number. Since m250, 16 of 143 milestone numbers are used by more than one workstream (m336 is a milestone in both w4 and w21; m348 in w4, w9 and w32).
- Skill layout: canonical `.claude/skills/pm/SKILL.md`, `.agents/skills/pm` is a symlink; validate with `bash scripts/skill-layout-validate.sh`.

## Steps

1. In the numbering rule, require `git fetch origin main` and take the highest number across the local tree and `git ls-tree -r --name-only origin/main -- .pm`.
2. In `done` step 3 and the Closeout wording, add: after a move, search `docs/`, every `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, and `.pm/*/README.md` for the old relative path and rewrite each link to the new one (report any that cannot be rewritten).
3. Mirror the wording in `.pm/AGENTS.md` where it restates the move; run `bash scripts/skill-layout-validate.sh`.
