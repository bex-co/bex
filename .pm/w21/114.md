# 114 — One S3 upload stream to `oregon` runs at about 1 MiB/s, so a single `PutObject` over about 1.7 GiB cannot finish inside the 30-minute stream deadline

Why: the production walk of w21/m500 t001 measured it, and ADR112 D3 promises streams "up to 5 GiB for up to 30 minutes" without saying that this holds only where the stream is fast; `aws s3 cp` (multipart above 8 MiB) is not affected, but an SDK `PutObject` of a large body is.

- **Source:** w21/m500 t001, 2026-11-19 (ADR112 § Not verified, "Streams").
- **Facts:** 16 concurrent 256 MiB `put-object` calls to `oregon` averaged 0.94 MiB/s each (271 s), from a laptop; the same single stream from a pod in the cluster was equally slow (the serving pod used 8 to 27 millicores, where the Frankfurt stream used 408). The same 5 GiB `PutObject` to `frankfurt` took 320 s (16.8 MiB/s, the pace of one workspace on one replica) and read back identical. So the slow hop is `bex-s3gw` to Wasabi in Oregon from the Hetzner nodes (a long-latency path per connection), not the client or the pacing. At 1 MiB/s the 30-minute deadline ends a stream at about 1.7 GiB.
- **Not yet known:** whether the cause is the TCP window of the Wasabi connection (socket buffers, the transport's settings in `internal/objectstorage` streaming), a per-connection limit at Wasabi, or the route; a single-stream `curl` PUT from a node to the Wasabi `us-west-1` endpoint, with and without larger socket buffers, tells which. Render's own object storage limits are unpublished, so no Render number applies.
- **Fix:** if buffers explain it, set them (and record the new rate); if not, document the limit where `bex s3` and the S3 section of `docs/bex-cli.md` say "5 GiB per object": a body over about 1 GiB to `oregon` needs multipart (which every aws-cli, rclone and boto3 transfer uses by default), and `bex s3`'s docs say so. Keep the 5 GiB cap either way.

## Done when

- [ ] The slow hop is identified with a measurement, and a single 1 GiB `PutObject` to `oregon` either runs at the pace of one workspace (16 MiB/s) or `docs/bex-cli.md` and ADR112 D3 state the length a single stream to `oregon` can complete.
