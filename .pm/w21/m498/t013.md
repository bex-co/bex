## Objective

Serve multipart upload and count its bytes, so `aws s3 cp` of a file over 8 MB, `mv` and bucket-to-bucket `sync` work, and unfinished uploads neither bypass the quota nor linger.

## Context

- ADR112 D3 (Multipart upload, CopyObject and UploadPartCopy) and D5. Rows `object_multipart_uploads(id, workspace_id, bucket_id, key, wasabi_upload_id, created_by, created_at, last_part_at)` and `object_multipart_parts(upload_id, part_number, size, etag)`; the upload id handed out is the row's typed id.
- `UploadPart` streams to a presigned `UploadPart` URL that signs `content-length` after the quota gate reserved its length; S3's limits (5 MiB to 5 GiB, last part smaller, 10,000 parts); a part that would take the upload past 5 GiB is `EntityTooLarge` when it starts. The parts' bytes count against the quota until complete or abort. `UploadPartCopy` copies a range of a same-workspace, same-region source. Complete passes the part list to Wasabi, rebuilt from `PartNumber` and `ETag` alone; abort, `ListParts` and `ListMultipartUploads` come from the rows and Wasabi.
- The sweep aborts an upload with no part for 7 days (`last_part_at`), runs the `AbortIncompleteMultipartUpload` rules, and deletes revoked and expired access keys with their OpenBao secrets after 7 days. A workspace or account delete aborts its uploads at Wasabi (ADR112 D9).
- Unverified (ADR112 § Not verified): Wasabi's part limits, `UploadPartCopy` support and `ListParts` pagination; probe and record.

## Steps

1. Write the migration, the handlers and the accounting hook the gate (t010) reads.
2. Write the sweep additions and the delete-time cleanup.
3. Test an interrupted upload, a part replaced by number, the 5 GiB refusal at the part, and `aws s3 mv` of a 20 MiB object against the fake S3.
