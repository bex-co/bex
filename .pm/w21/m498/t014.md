## Objective

Watch the S3 surface and be able to stop it, because it carries customer bytes through shared Wasabi egress.

## Context

- ADR112 D3 (What watches it; Egress is the exposure here too). Counters by operation and S3 error code, by authentication-failure reason, streams in flight against the cap, bytes in and out. Names must match bex-api's scrape keep-list (`deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml`, which dropped the first sweep metrics in w21/096).
- Alerts, each with a panel ([ADR088](../../../docs/ADR088-platform-observability-ui.md) § 6): a 5xx ratio, an authentication-failure rate, OpenBao errors on the key path, stream-slot saturation, egress-budget refusals and the S3 listing bytes; rules and tests under `deploy/gitops/base/rules/` as w21/096 did.
- The kill switch gains a second step: the `objects.` route pointed at a static 503 in GitOps; write it into `docs/runbooks/objectgw-kill-switch.md` (its anchor into D4 changed too) and its form into `scripts/gitops-validate.sh`.

## Steps
