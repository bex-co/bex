## Objective

Watch the S3 surface and be able to stop it, because it carries customer bytes through shared Wasabi egress.

## Context

- ADR112 D3 (What watches it; Egress is the exposure here too). Counters by operation and S3 error code, by authentication-failure reason, streams in flight against the cap, bytes in and out. Names must match bex-api's scrape keep-list (`deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml`, which dropped the first sweep metrics in w21/096).
- Alerts, each with a panel ([ADR088](../../../docs/ADR088-platform-observability-ui.md) § 6): a 5xx ratio, an authentication-failure rate, OpenBao errors on the key path, stream-slot saturation, egress-budget refusals and the S3 listing bytes; rules and tests under `deploy/gitops/base/rules/` as w21/096 did.
- The kill switch gains a second step: the `objects.` route pointed at a static 503 in GitOps; write it into `docs/runbooks/objectgw-kill-switch.md` (its anchor into D4 changed too) and its form into `scripts/gitops-validate.sh`.

## Steps

1. Add the metrics and the keep-list test that reads the live list and the registry.
2. Add the rules, rule tests and panels.
3. Update the runbook and the validation script.

## Result

Done 2026-11-19. `s3gw/metrics.go` and `cmd/s3gw`: bex_s3gw_requests_total{operation,code,status}, _auth_failures_total{reason} (with `secret_store` for OpenBao failing on the key path), _bytes_total{direction}, _streams_in_flight and _stream_limit, _egress_budget_refusals_total and _listing_response_bytes_total, on the probe port's /metrics, scraped per pod by a new `bex-s3gw` job in prometheus.yaml with the keep-list `bex_s3gw_.*`. `metrics_test.go` reads the live keep-list and the registry and counts requests, authentication failures by reason, bytes and refusals (each mutation-checked); `scripts/obs-scrape-keep-check.sh` and `obs-coverage-check.sh` pass with six new alerts. Alerts in `alerting_rules.yml` with a firing and a quiet test each in `rules/s3_gateway_alerts_test.yml` (promtool): S3GatewayServerErrors, S3GatewayAuthFailuresHigh, S3SecretStoreErrors (critical), S3StreamSlotsSaturated, S3EgressBudgetRefusals, S3ListingBytesHigh; six panels (ids 19 to 24) on the Billing and metering board. Kill switch: the second switch is the same mechanism as objectgw's, a reviewed `bex-s3gw` replica patch in the prod overlay (normally 2; Traefik answers 503 at 0), shown in the runbook's new 'The S3 API switch' section, whose ADR112 anchors were corrected (D4's heading had changed); `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` pins the patch's shape and that the runbook shows it (its block was run alone and fails when the patch is removed; the full script stops at an unrelated Helm chart download from this host). The task proposed a static-503 route; scaling to zero needs no new mechanism, and ADR112 D3 records why.
