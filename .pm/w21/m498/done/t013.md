## Objective

Serve multipart upload and count its bytes, so `aws s3 cp` of a file over 8 MB, `mv` and bucket-to-bucket `sync` work, and unfinished uploads neither bypass the quota nor linger.

## Context

- ADR112 D3 (Multipart upload, CopyObject and UploadPartCopy) and D5. Rows `object_multipart_uploads(id, workspace_id, bucket_id, key, wasabi_upload_id, created_by, created_at, last_part_at)` and `object_multipart_parts(upload_id, part_number, size, etag)`; the upload id handed out is the row's typed id.
- `UploadPart` streams to a presigned `UploadPart` URL that signs `content-length` after the quota gate reserved its length; S3's limits (5 MiB to 5 GiB, last part smaller, 10,000 parts); a part that would take the upload past 5 GiB is `EntityTooLarge` when it starts. The parts' bytes count against the quota until complete or abort. `UploadPartCopy` copies a range of a same-workspace, same-region source. Complete passes the part list to Wasabi, rebuilt from `PartNumber` and `ETag` alone; abort, `ListParts` and `ListMultipartUploads` come from the rows and Wasabi.
- The sweep aborts an upload with no part for 7 days (`last_part_at`), runs the `AbortIncompleteMultipartUpload` rules, and deletes revoked and expired access keys with their OpenBao secrets after 7 days. A workspace or account delete aborts its uploads at Wasabi (ADR112 D9).
- Unverified (ADR112 § Not verified): Wasabi's part limits, `UploadPartCopy` support and `ListParts` pagination; probe and record.

## Steps

1. Write the migration, the handlers and the accounting hook the gate (t010) reads.
2. Write the sweep additions and the delete-time cleanup.
3. Test an interrupted upload, a part replaced by number, the 5 GiB refusal at the part, and `aws s3 mv` of a 20 MiB object against the fake S3.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A 20 MiB upload, copy and `mv` round trip in parts; an unfinished upload's bytes count against the quota and disappear at abort or after 7 days.
- [x] A deleted workspace leaves no multipart upload at Wasabi.

## Out of scope

- Versioning and `UploadPartCopy` across regions or workspaces.

## Result

Done 2026-11-19. Migration 0304 (`object_multipart_uploads` with the bucket's region, `object_multipart_parts`), the `mpu` id kind, `store/objectmultipart.go`, `objectstorage/multipart.go` and `s3gw/handlers_multipart.go`: CreateMultipartUpload, UploadPart (streamed through a presigned UploadPart URL after the quota gate reserved its length), UploadPartCopy, CompleteMultipartUpload (part list rebuilt from number and ETag, checked against the rows), AbortMultipartUpload, ListParts and ListMultipartUploads. Unfinished parts count against the quota (`MultipartBytes`), a part that would take the upload past 5 GiB is EntityTooLarge when it starts, at most 100 uploads are open per workspace, an unfinished upload keeps its bucket from being deleted, and a part writes no allowed audit row. The hourly sweep aborts an upload with no part for 7 days, runs AbortIncompleteMultipartUpload rules (a lifecycle rule now does something), and deletes revoked and expired access keys with their OpenBao secrets after 7 days (`SweepEnded` existed and was never called); a workspace purge aborts its uploads at Wasabi first. **Found with stock aws-cli: `aws s3 cp` between buckets sends x-amz-copy-source-if-match on every UploadPartCopy, and bex answered 501 to every x-amz-copy-source-if-* header, so any copy of 8 MiB or more failed.** CopyObject and UploadPartCopy now evaluate the four conditions against the source they HEAD (412), and ADR112 D3 is amended. Verified with aws-cli 1.44 against the real handlers: a 20 MB cp up and down, cp between buckets, mv within and between buckets, bucket-to-bucket sync, an abandoned upload blocking rb until aborted, all byte-identical. Verified against production Wasabi through bex's own store code (throwaway prefix, removed): presigned UploadPart, the 5 MiB minimum (enforced), UploadPartCopy with ranges, NoSuchUpload, x-amz-meta-* on a presigned PUT, and ListObjectsV2 StartAfter with a prefix and delimiter; recorded in ADR112 § Not verified.
