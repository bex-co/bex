## Objective

Implement the pure schedule logic: validation and next-fire computation for hour, day, week, once and custom cron, with time zones.

## Context

- Spec: ADR101 D2. Presets compile to five-field cron; `once` uses `runAt`; `endsAt` stops firing.
- The cron dependency is `github.com/robfig/cron/v3` (already in `lego/backend/go.mod`, used by `internal/apps/service.go`).
- Minimum interval 5 minutes unless t001 changed it. Daylight saving: a skipped local time fires once at the next valid instant; a repeated local time fires once at the first occurrence.

## Steps

1. Create `lego/backend/internal/agentautomations/schedule.go` with `Validate`, `Next(after time.Time)` and the preset compilers; accept an injected clock.
2. Refuse an unknown zone, a bad cron, a past `runAt` and an interval under the minimum with named 400 codes (`AGENT_AUTOMATION_SCHEDULE_*`).
3. Table tests for every frequency, month ends, leap day, `endsAt` boundary, and both DST transitions in two zones.

## Files

- lego/backend/internal/agentautomations/schedule.go
- lego/backend/internal/agentautomations/schedule_test.go

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every frequency yields the expected next fire for a fixed clock.
- [ ] Spring-forward and fall-back each fire exactly once.
- [ ] A cron firing more often than the minimum is refused with `AGENT_AUTOMATION_SCHEDULE_TOO_FREQUENT`.

## Out of scope

- Persistence and the loop.
