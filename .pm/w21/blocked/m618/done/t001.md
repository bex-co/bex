## Objective

Record which CA a host's certificate comes from and when it changes.

## Context

- `docs/ADR005-custom-domain.md` (Certificates); Render's tls page quoted in the milestone README.
- cert-manager has no issuer fallback of its own: the selection is the operator's.

## Steps

1. Write the rule: Let's Encrypt by default; Google Trust Services after a CAA refusal for Let's Encrypt, an ACME `rateLimited`, or N failed orders; a CAA lookup when the domain is added picks Google Trust Services at once when only `pki.goog` is allowed.
2. State what moves a host back, if anything, and that renewals stay on the CA that issued.
3. Add the ADR018 custom-domains row for the CA set, pointing at this milestone.

- `docs/ADR005-custom-domain.md`

## Out of scope

- Choosing a third CA.

## Result

Done 2026-11-24. [ADR005 § Two certificate authorities](../../../../../docs/ADR005-custom-domain.md) records the rule, with Render's [tls](https://render.com/docs/tls) and [custom-domains](https://render.com/docs/custom-domains#caa-records) pages (read 2026-11-24: Render names Let's Encrypt and Google Trust Services and asks a domain with CAA records to allow `letsencrypt.org` and `pki.goog`, plus `issuewild` for a wildcard; it does not say how it chooses).

- Every host starts on `BEX_CLUSTER_ISSUER` (Let's Encrypt). With `BEX_SECOND_CLUSTER_ISSUER` set, a host's next attempt goes to the other CA after a CAA refusal or a rate limit (at once after the first failed attempt), or from the third failed attempt in a row (5 minutes before cert-manager's own retry, an hour doubling to 32). So a move never adds an attempt: a domain neither CA may issue for alternates on cert-manager's schedule.
- What moves a host back: nothing but the same rule. Renewals stay with the CA that issued, since a success clears the failure count and the Certificate keeps its issuer. A dropped TLS entry or a changed first host returns the Certificate to ingress-shim.
- **The CAA lookup at add time is not kept.** The operator does not resolve DNS (ADR005 § Projection and certificates), and one refused Let's Encrypt validation moves the host within its first failure, inside Let's Encrypt's 5 authorization failures per identifier per hour ([rate limits](https://letsencrypt.org/docs/rate-limits/)).
- Not covered, recorded in the ADR: an outage answered with retryable errors records no failed attempt, so nothing moves.

The [ADR018 custom-domains row](../../../../../docs/ADR018/custom-domains.md) "Certificates from Let's Encrypt and Google Trust Services" is ◐ with the difference until t002 and t005 land, and ADR005 § Render compatibility lists it as a named divergence until then.
