## Objective

Route tenant workload egress to public destinations through the fixed addresses, and keep the two things that already read that traffic correct.

## Context

- **ADR125 (t001):** production has no `ciliumegressgatewaypolicies.cilium.io` CRD until t002 enables the feature, so the policy cannot reach `deploy/gitops/` before then. Sources and exclusions are D5; the meter and SMTP checks are D10; the new-pod window to measure is D11.
- `deploy/gitops/base/tenant-node-egress.yaml` (`deny-free-plan-smtp-egress`, the `app.bex.co/free-plan=true` label).
- `lego/operator/cmd/egress-meter` and `scripts/verify-egress-meter.sh` (ADR030 §14 bills outbound bandwidth per service).
- Workloads: App serving pods, cron runs and pre-deploy Jobs in tenant namespaces; build Jobs keep their own path.

## Steps

1. Write a `CiliumEgressGatewayPolicy` selecting tenant workload pods, excluding cluster, node, private-network and platform destinations.
2. Confirm the SMTP deny still drops 25/465/587 from a free pod.
3. Run `scripts/verify-egress-meter.sh` (or its local equivalent) and confirm bytes are still attributed to the sending service.
4. Add a guard in `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` for the policy's selector and exclusions.

## Files

- `deploy/gitops/base/` (new policy manifest, `kustomization.yaml`)
- `scripts/gitops-validate.sh`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A tenant pod's request to a public endpoint leaves from a Floating IP (mock cluster or a dev environment).
- [ ] A free pod still cannot reach outbound 25/465/587.
- [ ] The egress meter's per-service bytes match before and after.

## Out of scope

- Build Job egress.
