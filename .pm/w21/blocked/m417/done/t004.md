## Objective

Bound what public reads can cost the shared Wasabi account, and warn before the kill switch is needed.

## Context

- `docs/ADR112-object-storage.md` D4: per-bucket and per-workspace rate and bandwidth caps, defaults set from measurement; an operator alert on month-to-date public egress, its threshold set from measurement. The account is shared with the platform's backups, so a Wasabi limit would reach restore paths.
- Wasabi's free egress is a fair-use ratio (monthly downloads at or below the active storage volume); how Wasabi measures it and whether it acts on an account or a bucket is unverified.
- The alert belongs where the platform's alerts live (`deploy/gitops`); `docs/ADR088-platform-observability-ui.md` §6 requires every alert to have a panel.

## Steps

1. Add token-bucket rate and bandwidth limits per bucket and per workspace; answer 429 with `Retry-After`; choose first values from a short measurement and record them as bex's own in ADR018 (constants, not environment variables).
2. Add the egress counter metric, the alert rule and its Grafana panel.
3. Test the limits.

## Files

- lego/backend/internal/objectgw/limits.go and tests
- deploy/gitops/ (alert rule and dashboard panel)
- docs/ADR018-render-parity.md

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A bucket over its bandwidth cap gets 429.
- [ ] The alert has a panel and fires in the rule's own test.

## Out of scope

- A CDN, signed public URLs.

## Result

Per-bucket and per-workspace caps in `internal/objectgw/limits.go`: requests (50 a second per bucket, 100 per workspace), bandwidth (10 and 20 MiB a second, bursts of 256 and 512 MiB; a response is charged as it starts, so the first reader is served whole and the next requests get `429` with `Retry-After` until the debt repays), open responses (32 per bucket, 64 per workspace, 256 per replica), and a write deadline that follows the reader (a minute ahead of its last byte, never past 30 minutes). All are per replica, bex's own and unverified, recorded in ADR018 and ADR112 D4 (constants, not variables). **Deviation from the task text:** the alert cannot read objectgw's counter over a month, because Prometheus keeps three days (`retention: 3d`). bex-api instead exports `bex_usage_object_storage_egress_month_bytes`, the month-to-date sum of the egress shadow meter over all workspaces and all three paths (a Postgres query over `usage_hourly` with a new partial index, migration 0275, tested), and `ObjectStoragePublicEgressHigh` (500 GB, warning) and `...Critical` (900 GB, critical) read it; promtool tests fire both and a mutation of the threshold fails them; the panel (month-to-date with both thresholds, objectgw bytes out by origin, requests by result) is on the Billing and metering dashboard, and `obs-coverage-check.sh` and `obs-scrape-keep-check.sh` pass. The gauge is an upper bound of Wasabi's egress (cache hits count too). **Not measured:** no production access here, so the caps and both thresholds are conservative first values below the 1 TB account minimum, not measurements; t008 must measure and reset them. Tests: a bucket over its cap gets 429 and recovers after the stated wait, refused requests never reach Wasabi, cache hits and streamed objects are charged, a charge is capped at the burst on each side, the workspace cap spans buckets and spares other workspaces, request rates, the open-response caps and that places never leak, and a zero cap panics at construction.
