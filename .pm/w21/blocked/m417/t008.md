## Objective

Prove public reads on production and close the unverified Traefik answer.

## Context

- Needs the credentials from w21/m415 t010 and the routing from t006.
- Evidence rule: conclusions only, never raw logs (`.pm/AGENTS.md`).
- If a step needs something missing, park the task in `blocked/` naming exactly that.

## Steps

1. With wrangler, enable `dev-url` on a test bucket holding a known object and read it from a browser origin with `Range` and CORS.
2. Disable `dev-url` and confirm 404 within the cache window.
3. Scale objectgw to zero in GitOps, record exactly what a visitor sees, then restore it.
4. Confirm the egress is counted and the alert's panel shows it.
5. Update ADR112's Not verified list.
