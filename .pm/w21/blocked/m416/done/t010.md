## Objective

Stop deleted workspaces' objects accruing Wasabi's 90-day minimum for nothing and stop any `pub-` host from answering.

## Context

- `docs/ADR112-object-storage.md` D9: purge `<tea-id>/` in both regions and drop the rows; an unavailable bucket is retried by the sweep, as ADR095 D7 does for attachments.
- `docs/ADR086-account-deletion.md` defines the durable offboarding flow; find where attachments are purged on delete and add the objects there.

## Steps

1. Add the purge step to the workspace and account deletion flow.
2. Record a pending purge when Wasabi is unavailable and retry it in the sweep until it succeeds.
3. Test deletion with the fake S3 down, then up.

## Files

- lego/backend/internal/objectstorage/purge.go and tests
- the account-deletion flow found above

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After deletion no object under `<tea-id>/` and no bucket row remains.
- [ ] A purge that failed once is retried and finishes.

## Out of scope

- Per-bucket deletion of non-empty buckets.

## Result

`DeleteTenant` retires the workspace's bucket rows in its transaction (`deleted_at`, no cascade, because `object_buckets.workspace_id` has no tenant foreign key), and `objectstorage.WorkspacePurger`, a post-cascade purger, then deletes `<tea-id>/` in both regions with `DeletePrefix`, drops the rows and their usage rows only when both regions are empty, and never fails the deletion: an unavailable Wasabi leaves the retired rows (`purge_attempted_at` stamped) for the hourly sweep, which retries until it succeeds. `TestTenantScopeCensus` now records `object_buckets` and `object_storage_usage` as real `purger:` dispositions. Tests: deletion with one region's fake S3 down, then up (retried and finished), no object under `<tea-id>/` and no row afterwards, another workspace untouched. Residual, recorded in `purge.go`: a presigned PUT minted just before the delete stays valid for 15 minutes, so an upload landing after the immediate purge leaves an object no row names; a follow-up could hold the retired rows for `PresignTTL`. A workspace with no bucket row at all whose immediate purge fails is not retried.
