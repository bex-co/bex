## Objective

Confirm that what a failed newest deploy leaves serving, and how a free service wakes over it, is consistent on every surface: REST `GET /v1/services/{id}` (phase, `suspended`), deploy rows, events, GraphQL `server`, MCP `get_service`, and the dashboard header. Record where bex diverges from Render.

## Context

- **Render's model.** A failed deploy leaves the previous deploy live, and a free web service spins down after 15 minutes idle and spins up on the next request.
- **Ledger.** The ADR018 deploy and free-instance rows, and ADR004 § Pre-deploy command.

## Steps

1. **Compare surfaces.** Through the parked, waking and serving states over a failed newest deploy, compare what each surface reports.
2. **Update the ledger.** Change ADR018 and ADR004 where the behavior or wording changed.
