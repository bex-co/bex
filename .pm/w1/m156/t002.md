## Objective

List every `Reconcile` path that returns before the Deployment/Service/Ingress writes, and every runtime transition that depends on those writes. Confirm t001 covers each pair, or record why a pair cannot occur.

## Context

- **Halts before the write.** `app_controller.go:537-594` (namespace, finalizer, protected secrets, registry credentials, canceled release, `resolveDeployImage`), the build admission and observation halts in `buildFromSource` (`:733-951`), and the pre-deploy gate (`:2051-2055`).
- **Transitions.**
  - wake;
  - auto-hibernate;
  - suspend and resume (`app_controller_test.go:477`);
  - manual scale;
  - autoscale (`applyAutoscaling`);
  - maintenance on and off (`maintenance_mode_test.go:171`);
  - custom domains and the IP allow-list (both ride the Ingress write);
  - the private-service routing hold (`private_service_routing_test.go:152`).
- **Settles after the write** (listed as controls): the failed rollout deadline (`settleFailedRollout`, `:2698`).

## Steps

1. **Build the table.** Cross each halt with each transition. For every cell, record covered by t001, cannot occur (and why), or needs work.
2. **Test what the table says is covered.** Add or extend a test for each covered cell that is not already pinned; suspend and resume over a failed pre-deploy is the most likely gap.
3. **Paste the table** into the m156 README.
