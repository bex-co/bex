# The deploys list and a service's most-recent-deploy bar count each deploy's instances, as Render's do

Why: Render shows the Scaling count of a multi-instance or autoscaled service in two more places than the deploy page, its deploys list and the bar above a service's pages; bex shows it only on the deploy page (w1/m661).

- **Found:** w1/m661 t006 (2026-11-26). Render's public dashboard bundle (entry `index-D5V4ZgbE.js`): the deploys list maps every row through the same count as the deploy header (`P$`) and shows it as an **Instances** column, "`<deployed>` / `<total>`" in monospace (a "—" for a row without one), when the service autoscales or runs more than one instance. The most-recent-deploy bar at the top of a service's pages (`CronJob` chunk's shared bar) shows "Instances: `<deployed>` / `<total>`" for its deploy. Recorded in [deploy-detail-page.md § Scaling](../../docs/render-artifacts/deploy-detail-page.md#scaling), "Elsewhere".

Neither the deploys list (`deploys-list-page.tsx`) nor the service header's latest-deploy line reads them.
