2. Open Rollback on the first deploy: its Confirm Rollback page (`…/deploys/<id>/rollback`) lists each row in Render's words; no env var value or secret file content appears on the page or in the `RollbackComparison` network response; Cancel.

4. At 1440 and 390 px, compare the comparison's loading frame with its ready frame, and the route's pending skeleton (`DeployRollbackSkeleton`, forced to stay pending) with the ready page.
5. Open Rollback on a deploy whose release record is reclaimed or older than records (an old deploy of a bex fixture service, if one is in the Deploys list): Render's "Unable to load configuration…" sentence.
6. Delete the service. Keep screenshots local (no evidence files in git).
