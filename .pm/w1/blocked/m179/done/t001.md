## Objective

Measure what a never-served failed release costs today and decide the parked end state from Render's behavior.

## Context

- w8/039 live (2026-10-03): the never-served first-release pod was killed by the startup probe every 15 minutes (07:04:53, 07:20:02, …) until the service was deleted (`.pm/w8/done/039.md:38`).
- Render health-checks doc (fetched 2026-10-17): "If this condition is not met within 15 minutes, Render cancels the deploy and continues routing traffic to your service's existing instances." It does not describe the first-deploy case; record what is known and apply the `.pm/w1/README.md` rule (if unsure, keep parity with Render).
- `bash scripts/dev-env.sh 1 up` (pre-approved).

## Steps

1. On dev-1 create a private service (not sleep-eligible) whose image listens on the wrong port, as in w8/039; also a crash-looping image.
2. After the deploy settles `update_failed`, record over 30 minutes: pod restarts, `GET /v1/services/{id}/instances`, phase, and whether `instance_seconds` accrues.
3. Record Render's contract and the chosen end state (Deployment at 0, phase Failed, deploy row keeps its diagnosis) in this task's outcome.
