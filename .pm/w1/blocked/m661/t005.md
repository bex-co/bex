## Production walk (2026-11-27, pin `6ba91ca5b72f`)

As the QA user in `bex-canary`, on three Starter web services from `traefik/whoami`: `qa-20261127-m661-two` (disposable, 2 instances), `qa-20261127-m661-one` (disposable, 1 instance) and `qa-20261127-m661-as` (disposable, 1 instance, then autoscaling on with minimum 2 and maximum 3). The workspace is on Scale, so autoscaling was allowed.

- **DoD 1:** a restart of `m661-two`, its deploy page opened once and never reloaded, read "0 of 2 instances" 2 s in, "1 of 2" at 10 s and "2 of 2" as it went live at 23 s, at 1440 px. A second restart at 390 px read the same: 0, 1 at 10 s, then 2 of 2 at 49 s, live half a second later, with nothing scrolling sideways. The first deploy, which starts both instances at once, went from "0 of 2" to "2 of 2". GraphQL `serviceInstances` named the restart's deploy on both instances.
- **DoD 2:** `m661-one`'s live deploy showed no Scaling fact, watched for 8 s at both widths. `m661-as` counted against its minimum: "1 of 2 instances" at both widths until its second instance ran, then "2 of 2" in all eight locales.
- **DoD 3:** `m661-two`'s first deploy, deactivated by the restarts, showed no Scaling fact at both widths.
- **DoD 5, frames:** each live deploy was opened from its Deploys list in all eight locales at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, sampled every animation frame from the click: hovered for 1 s first, and with every GraphQL batch carrying `Deploy` held for 3 s; for `m661-two` also with `DeployInstances` and `AutoscalingConfig` held for 3 s (112 runs).
  - At 1440 px every page mounted and never moved afterwards. With the read held, the frame gave way to the ready page with the log toolbar 27 px higher (360 → 333), the header reserve w1/m646 recorded.
  - At 390 px `m661-two` and `m661-one` never moved after mounting. With `DeployInstances` held, the Scaling fact kept its place until its count landed, and the grid did not change height.
  - **Failed:** at 390 px, `m661-as`'s page mounted without the Scaling fact and grew a row of facts when its autoscaling read landed, pushing the log toolbar down 48 px (341 → 389, 321 → 369 in Japanese), hovered or held, in all eight locales. Its instance count is 1, so only its autoscaling says it counts instances, and the page read that after mounting.
  - The window never scrolled while a frame was held (`window.scrollTo` left `scrollY` at 0 and the document was as tall as the viewport), and nothing scrolled sideways at either width.
  - Not this milestone's: with the read held at 390 px, `m661-two`'s restart deploy grew from the frame by 57 px, because its Image fact prints the digest-pinned reference on five lines (120 px) where the frame reserves one. Filed as [w1/179](../../179.md).
- **Fix (this tree):** the deploy routes' loader (`prefetchDeployPage`) reads the service's autoscaling, cache-first, whenever the header can carry the fact: a web service, private service or background worker whose deploy is live, queued or in progress. The page then mounts with the fact in place, its value a bar until the instances land. On dev-1, on `m661-as` (disposable, autoscaled 2 to 3 from an instance count of 1), the page mounted with the fact and never moved, hovered and with `Deploy` held, in all eight locales at 390 px and at 1440 px. With the fix switched off, the same page moved 48 px (341 → 389 in English, 321 → 369 in Russian). A regression test in `deploy-page-prefetch.test.tsx` fails without the read.
- The fixtures were deleted (`server` answers `NOT_FOUND`) and the QA session was logged out.
