## Objective

For each critical/high whose fix requires a major version: upgrade one dependency at a time, adapt call sites, and gate each on the affected feature's own tests (plus a targeted manual check where the feature has a live surface — e.g. an SSH-gateway dep gets `scripts/ssh-verify.sh`). If a major is genuinely too risky now, don't force it — hand it to t004 as a residual with the reason.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every critical/high upgraded locally; none requires a major or deferral.

the safe `x/crypto` 0.51.0→0.52.0 minor and `spdystream` 0.5.0→0.5.1 patch.

complete backend/operator suites passed.
