## Objective

GraphQL, MCP and Blueprint all default `sizeGB` to 10. REST requires it, because Render's `add-disk` body requires it. So the "default 10" contract holds on three of four surfaces — faithful to Render, inconsistent with bex.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/apps/disks.go:289-291`, `:494`, `:563`.
- Genuinely a judgment call: Render fidelity argues for keeping REST strict; internal consistency argues for defaulting everywhere. Either is defensible — being undecided is not.

## Steps

1. Pick one and apply it.
2. Record the choice in ADR082 D6 next to the other deliberate divergences.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/apps/disks.go`, `docs/ADR082-persistent-disks.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] the four surfaces agree, or the difference is written down as intentional with its reason

## Out of scope

- Changing the default value itself.
