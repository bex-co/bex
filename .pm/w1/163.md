# A sandbox's Cost in its details drawer, priced from its CPU and memory meters

Why: Render's sandbox drawer shows a sandbox's Cost beside its Duration; bex's (w1/m658) shows Duration only, because nothing prices one sandbox's run.

A sandbox created through the sandbox API is billed on `sandbox_cpu_seconds` and `sandbox_memory_gb_seconds` by running interval (`sandbox_running_intervals`, migration 0281; [ADR023 § Sandbox CPU billed on use](../../docs/ADR023-usage-metering.md#sandbox-cpu-billed-on-use-w22m314-adr104-d8)). Pricing one sandbox means summing its rows (hourly usage keyed by the sandbox) at the rates `pricing.yaml` bills, as an agent session's `outcome.cost` does for its sandbox (ADR094 D7).

Done when: GraphQL `Sandbox` answers the run's cost so far (what the bill charges for it, in the workspace's currency), the drawer's Details shows Cost beside Duration for a live and an ended sandbox, a test pins the sum against the billed rows, and `docs/render-artifacts/sandbox-groups.md` § What bex does no longer says Duration only.
