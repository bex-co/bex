# The deploy page's frame reserves a one-line title, so a wrapping commit title pushes the page down when it lands

Why: the deploy page and its Confirm Rollback page draw the deploy's title, the commit message's first line as on Render, over the full width. `DeployHeaderSkeleton` reserves one 28 px line for it. A long title wraps, so everything under the header moves down when the page replaces its frame, which the skeleton rule (`dashboard/AGENTS.md` § Navigation pending states) forbids.

- **Seen:** production pin `410850e80281`, the QA user in `bex-canary`, w1/m660 t004's walk (2026-11-26) on `qa-20261126-m660-st` (disposable). Its deploys build a 150-character commit title. Opening a deploy's Confirm Rollback page with the route held, the card sat 78 px lower at 390 px once the page landed: the title took four lines (112 px) over the frame's one. At 1440 px it took two lines, which the frame's fuller facts reserve happened to absorb (the card started 27 px lower in the frame).
- **Source:** `dashboard/src/features/deploys/components/deploy-page-skeletons.tsx`, `DeployHeaderSkeleton`'s title row (`flex h-7`, one bar). It is shared by `DeployDetailSkeleton` and `DeployRollbackSkeleton` (`dashboard/src/common/components/route-skeletons.tsx`).
- **Precedent:** the service header's frame lays out the cached name (`useCachedServiceName`, w3/m555). A deploy opened from its Deploys list or its own page is already in the Apollo cache, so the frame can lay out the cached title invisibly (`ReservedText`) and wrap where the ready title does, and fall back to one line on a cold cache.
- **Overlap:** w1/m659 (parked) re-checks the deploy page's frames at 1440 and 390 px. This note is the title line only.
- **Done when:** on a deploy whose commit title wraps at 390 px, opened from the Deploys list with the `Deploy` read held, the frame's title block is as tall as the ready title at 1440 and 390 px on the deploy page and the rollback page, and a test fails if the frame stops laying out the cached title.
