## Steps

1. Rerun the grep with a wider window (or an AST query). Record the full list and the count in this file.
2. Classify each site: **(a)** a mutation that can receive a user-fixable `bad request`/conflict/forbidden refusal → migrate; **(b)** a read, or a flow with no refusal the user could act on (billing portal redirect, logout, history load) → leave it and record the reason in an allowlist.
