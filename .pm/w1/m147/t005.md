## Objective

The quota refusal is identical on REST, GraphQL and MCP for services and env groups, and the difference from Render's limit is recorded.

## Steps

1. Drive the over-quota secret-file patch through REST (`secrets/rest.go:77`), GraphQL `patchServiceEnvironment`, and MCP (`secrets/mcp.go:113`). Record status and message for each, and do the same for the env-group trio as the control.
2. Record in `docs/ADR018-render-parity.md` (environment/secret files row) and ADR066's finding 6: bex caps secret files and env vars at **512 KiB / 500 per service or group**, where Render documents **1 MB combined** for secret files ("The combined size of all secret files uploaded to any given service or environment group cannot exceed 1 MB", `render.com/docs/configure-environment-variables`, 2026-09-14). The reason is the base64 projection into a 1 MiB Kubernetes Secret.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A six-row response table (3 surfaces × service/env group) is recorded in the m147 README.
- [x] ADR018 and ADR066 are updated. Any surface disagreement is filed as a `w1/NNN` note.
