## Objective

See DoD 1–2 on a real static site, on dev-1 and then on production once a pin carries the milestone.

## Steps

1. A disposable static site: deploy, then change an env var, a secret file and a linked group, deploy again, and open Rollback on the first deploy.
2. Check every change is listed by name in Render's wording, and that neither the page nor the GraphQL response carries a value.
3. Repeat on production as the QA user, delete the fixture, and record the results in the milestone README.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] DoD 1–2 seen on production; fixture deleted.
