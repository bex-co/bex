# Cluster Autoscaler scale-down still evicts single-instance services

Why: the m186 pre-drain hook covers CAPI deletions, but Cluster Autoscaler evicts a node's pods itself before it deletes the Machine. A consolidation therefore still costs a one-instance service its only pod for the replacement's start time (ADR004 § Rollout headroom).

- Path: `scale-down-utilization-threshold` / `scale-down-unneeded-time` 5m (`infra/clusterapi/autoscaler-values.yaml:56-57`). CA simulates the reschedule, evicts the pods, then scales the MachineDeployment down. drain-guard then finds nothing at risk and releases at once.
- Options:
  - Annotate the pods of single-instance Apps `cluster-autoscaler.kubernetes.io/safe-to-evict: "false"`. This blocks consolidation of every node they sit on, a cost trade-off.
  - Have drain-guard evacuate on CA's `ToBeDeletedByClusterAutoscaler` taint. The race is the same one ruled out for cordons, because CA evicts right after it taints.
  - Accept the gap and measure how often CA consolidates a node that holds a single-instance pod.
