## Objective

Add an admin-only read of the members who do not meet the workspace's enforced login requirement, and a mutation that sends the 2FA reminder to them. The mutation is limited to one batch per workspace per UTC day and leaves an audit entry. A new 2FA enforcement sends the reminders itself.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/workspaces/login_requirement_impact.go` (`LoginRequirementImpact`, w7/m471) already answers who a requirement would lock out; reuse it for the standing read.
- New operations need rows in `lego/backend/internal/api/scope_matrix_ops.go` and `key_permission_ops.go`; audit entries follow ADR018's audit-log row.

## Steps

1. Add the read for the enforced requirement, admin-only.
2. Add the mutation: it sends to the current non-compliant members, records the day per workspace in the control-plane DB, refuses a second batch with Render's limit error, and writes an audit event.
3. Make enabling 2FA enforcement send the batch, counting toward the day's limit.
4. Google login gets the list only, with no reminder, as on Render.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/workspaces/` (read, mutation, enforcement hook), a migration for the per-day record, scope and permission tables

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Real-DB tests cover a non-admin refused, the second same-day batch refused, a next-day batch allowed, and the enforce path sending once.
