## Objective

Stop the outbound-webhook failure-notice ("webhook is disabled") email from being re-sent on every restart or by a second replica, by moving the `emittedAt` suppression marker into the control-plane store.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/webhooks/worker.go:160` holds an in-memory `emittedAt` map: a restart re-emails, a second replica double-emails.
- The marker is one timestamp per endpoint; it belongs next to the endpoint's disabled/auto-disable state in the store.

## Steps

1. Add a `notified_at` column (or equivalent) on the webhook endpoint/disable state in the store.
2. Replace the in-memory map with a store read/conditional-write guarded by the timestamp.
3. Ensure the first-disabled email still sends once, and a re-disable after recovery re-sends once.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/webhooks/worker.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/store` (migration + read/write)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A restart does not re-send the failure notice for an already-notified disabled endpoint.
- [ ] Two replicas do not both send the notice for the same disable transition.
- [ ] Re-enabling then re-disabling sends exactly one new notice.

## Out of scope

- Delivery-claim dedup (t002) and the rate limiter (t004).
