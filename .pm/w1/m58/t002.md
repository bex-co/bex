## Objective

Move the outbound-webhook delivery worker from an in-process loop to a control-plane claim so that two bex-api replicas (or a restart) never deliver the same event twice.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/webhooks/worker.go:55` records that two replicas double-deliver every event today.
- The control-plane Postgres store (`lego/backend/internal/store`, opt-in via `BEX_CP_DB_URI`, on in prod) is the right shared claim surface; `SELECT … FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` is the standard multi-consumer claim.
- The delivery worker tails the deploys+audit_events composition through a durable watermark (w3/m11); the claim must compose with that, not replace it.

## Steps

1. Add a `delivery_attempts`/claim shape (or extend the existing outbox) so a row is atomically leased by exactly one consumer with a visibility timeout.
2. Convert the worker's poll loop to claim-then-deliver-then-ack; an unacked lease requeues after the timeout.
3. Keep retry/backoff/auto-disable semantics (w3/m11) intact on top of the claim.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/webhooks/worker.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/store` (migration + claim query)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Two concurrent workers claiming the same backlog each take disjoint rows (no double-delivery), proven by a test.
- [ ] An unacked lease becomes re-visible after its timeout.
- [ ] Existing retry/backoff/auto-disable behavior is unchanged.
