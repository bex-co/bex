## Objective

Enumerate every piece of correctness-relevant state bex-api holds in process memory, decide for each whether it must become shared/durable under two replicas or is acceptably per-replica, and write that contract down so t002–t004 implement against it.

3. Record the inventory as a short table and confirm t002–t004 scope against it.
