## Objective

In `streamAgentAttach`, tell a gone session from a working one whose sandbox is still starting; for the latter, keep the stream open (heartbeat comments) and splice the live driver stream once the sandbox address resolves, within a bound; past the bound, close without `[DONE]`.

## Context

- The session's state comes from the store; `[DONE]` stays for terminal sessions.
- Keep the D3 verbatim-forward guarantee and the transcript tee.

## Steps

1. Read the session state when the address does not resolve; for a working session, poll the address with backoff up to a bound (for example 5 minutes, above the sandbox pod-ready wait).
2. Send SSE comment heartbeats while waiting so proxies keep the connection.
3. Splice the live stream when it serves; close without `[DONE]` at the bound.
