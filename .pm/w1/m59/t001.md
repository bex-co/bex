## Objective

Change the write-back step's "production inputs advanced; refusing stale digest write-back" path from `exit 1` (run conclusion `failure`) to a neutral/skipped conclusion with a clear "superseded by newer run" message, so a correct refusal stops looking like a prod deploy failure.

## Context

- `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`, `build-and-deploy` job, step "pin image digests in GitOps (write-back)": the guard runs `git diff --quiet $GITHUB_SHA origin/main` over production-input paths and `exit 1`s when they advanced.
- A step that exits 0 but prints the supersession reason is the neutral form; `continue-on-error` or an explicit `::notice::`/conclusion override are alternatives to evaluate.

## Steps

1. Replace `exit 1` with a path that concludes the job neutrally while emitting a clear "superseded by newer run (…); this run's images are intentionally not pinned" message.
2. Ensure downstream steps (wait-for-operator, roll) are skipped, not failed.
3. Keep the guard's correctness (never pin stale images).

## Files

- `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A superseded run's write-back concludes neutral (not red) with a clear message.
- [ ] No stale image is ever pinned.
