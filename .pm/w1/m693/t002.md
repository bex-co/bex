## Objective

One dialog component that the Shell and Disk tabs both open for a Free service, as Render's single upgrade dialog serves both.

## Context

- Render's dialog is open from the moment the tab renders.
  - Its title is `Enable {feature}`. Its description is "Upgrade your instance and get more out of Render."; use bex's name.
  - Its body is the tab's sentence, then "Upgrading to the **Starter** compute plan also includes:" and t001's list, each line with a check icon.
  - Closing it or pressing Cancel navigates to the service's Events. **Review upgrade options** links to the service's Compute page (`/services/{id}/compute`).
- bex's dialogs use `dashboard/src/common/components/ui/dialog`.

## Steps

1. Build the dialog with props for the feature and the sentence.
2. Wire Cancel and close to Events, and the primary button to Compute.
