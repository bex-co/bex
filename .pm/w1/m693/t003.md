## Objective

A Free service's Shell tab looks as Render's does: a sample terminal behind the dialog.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/services/components/service-shell-page.tsx` picks between the Web Shell card and the unavailable card from `sshAddress` and `sshRemedy`.
- Render's order is: the shell permission, then Free, then running, else suspended. A suspended Free service therefore gets the Free dialog.

## Steps

1. For a Free web service, private service or background worker, render a static terminal-styled block with Render's welcome lines (using bex's name), ending at `{service id}:/#`, and open the dialog with feature "Shell Access" and the sentence "Shell is not supported for free compute plans.".
2. Keep the permission refusal first, as Render does.
3. Keep the static-site and cron-job explanation unchanged.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/features/services/components/service-shell-page.tsx`
- `dashboard/src/features/services/lib/ssh-remedy.ts`, if the Free reason is no longer used there
