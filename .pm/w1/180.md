# Protected environments are a Pro feature on Render: gate them below Pro, as its Permissions card does

Why: Render's environment settings page shows the Permissions card behind its plan gate below Pro ("Protected environments and team collaboration are available on the Pro plan."), while bex lets a Hobby workspace protect an environment from every surface.

- **Found:** w1/m683 t009 (Render parity), 2026-11-27.
- **Render:** the entry chunk of its public dashboard bundle (read 2026-11-26, find it by the string) draws the Permissions card disabled with that sentence and an Upgrade button when `updateEnvironmentProtected.permissionLevel` is `DENY_INSUFFICIENT_TIER`, the same gate it uses for cross-environment connections (Pro).
- **bex:**
  - `lego/backend/internal/core/plan_gate.go` has no plan feature for protection: `environments.Update` / `CreateWithACL` take `protectedStatus` on every plan (only the Admin rule, w9/106, applies).
  - The settings page's Permissions card (`dashboard/src/features/environments/components/environment-settings-cards.tsx`) has no plan gate, since the API has none.
- **Work:** confirm on render.com/pricing or a Hobby workspace that protection is Pro and higher; add a `PlanFeature` for it (`PLAN_FEATURE_REQUIRED` on REST, GraphQL and MCP, turning it off always allowed, as network isolation does), and show Render's paywall on the Permissions card below Pro. Decide with the user what happens to a Hobby workspace's environments that are already protected.
- **Done when:** a Hobby workspace cannot protect an environment on any surface, gets the coded refusal, and sees Render's sentence and Upgrade on the Permissions card; ADR030 and ADR018's Projects & environments row say so.
