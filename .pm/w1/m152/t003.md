## Objective

After a cancel, every surface agrees on what runs:
- the deploy list's **Live** row is the running release;
- a saved-but-undeployed change is labeled as such on the Environment page and service header;
- no pod rollout ever happens without a deploy row and events.

## Context

- The dashboard deploys table showed `Live · First Deploy` while the pod ran the canceled config (m152 README § Evidence).
- Cancel dialog copy: `dashboard/src/features/services/locales/en.ts:3852`.
- Events after the cancel: none, although a rollout happened at 15:28:51Z.

## Steps

1. **Invariant.** Add a check (operator or backend) that a Deployment template change maps to an open deploy row. Log and meter any violation.
2. **Pending indicator.** Surface "saved, not deployed" for env vars, secret files and linked group changes whose deploy was canceled, on REST/GraphQL/MCP (a field) and the dashboard (an indicator).
3. **Dialog copy.** Keep the Cancel dialog's copy true, or amend it to say the saved change stays saved and ships on the next deploy.
