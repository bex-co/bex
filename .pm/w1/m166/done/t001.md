## Result (2026-09-28, `/loopx w1`)

Done, measured not assumed. Two concurrent one-second clocks (external stdlib-`ssl` TLS
`AUTH`+`PING`, and the API status) across a maxmemory change, a persistence change and a
resume, on public fixture `red-dat25gq1pbgc73a24llg` (deleted afterwards).

The available-but-unreachable window is **24.6s / 26.3s / 26.5s** — larger than the
10–23s filed. The material finding is the **shape**: it is not one gap but a flap, with
the client alternating SERVING/DOWN three or four times after the API reports
`available`. A single pre-publish probe would have passed at +32.3s and Ready would have
published into a path that failed 1.3s later, so t002 cannot be "probe once".

Dominant failure is `tls:SSLEOFError`, not `conn:refused` or `-LOADING`, which points at
a routing/endpoint truth problem (a terminating endpoint still in the set) rather than
Valkey load time — and explains the coin-toss flap.

Controls already correct: suspended is DOWN throughout with no available claim; a new
store says `creating`; a previously-served store says `config_restart` while unavailable.
