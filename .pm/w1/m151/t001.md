## Objective

Before the operator hibernates a free web service, it asks whether the service received inbound requests since `last-active`. If it did, it moves `last-active` forward and requeues instead of sleeping. A service under steady traffic therefore never sleeps, and an idle one still sleeps 15 minutes (or `idleTTLSeconds`) after its last request.

## Context

- The decision is `shouldAutoHibernate` (`lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go:1942-1951`), called from `desiredReplicas` (`:2290-2311`). The requeue is `runningRequeue` → `idleRequeueAfter` (`:2405-2416`, `:1954-1964`).
- There are only two writers of `app.bex.co/last-active`: `runningRequeue` when absent, and the activator's `wakeApp` (`cmd/activator/main.go:169-196`).
- Prometheus is already available to the operator: `BEX_PROM_URL` (`config/manager/manager.yaml:215`), `promInstantQuery` (`internal/controller/autoscale.go:136`), and the reader-injection pattern `DatabaseReconciler.DiskUsageReader` (`cmd/manager/main.go:509-510`).
- The Traefik counter is `traefik_service_requests_total{service="<namespace>-<app>-<port>@kubernetes"}` (bex-api `traefikServiceLabel`, `lego/backend/internal/metrics/source.go:373-375`). The live label observed was `tea-d98210cbbpdc73dcrkvg-tea-d98210cbbpdc73dcrkvg-qa-20260914-stack-3000@kubernetes`.
- m151 README § Evidence 1–2 has the live timeline.

## Steps

1. **Add an injectable reader.** Add an `ActivityReader` on `AppReconciler` that returns whether the App's Traefik service counted any requests in `(lastActive, now]`, e.g. `increase(traefik_service_requests_total{service=…}[<since lastActive>]) > 0`, or the counter's last-change time. Wire it from `BEX_PROM_URL` beside the database reader. Duplicate or relocate the label helper; the operator must not import the backend.
2. **Consult it before hibernating.** When the stamp says the window elapsed but the reader reports activity, patch `last-active` to the latest activity time (or now), and requeue at `idleRequeueAfter`.
3. **Fail awake.** A reader error or nil reader means skip hibernation this pass, log it, and requeue. Record the decision in the annotation's doc comment and correct it: it is not updated "by the activator on each inbound request".
4. **Check series behavior across a hibernate.** Pods at 0 and a stale series must not read as activity after a real idle period, and counter resets must not either.
