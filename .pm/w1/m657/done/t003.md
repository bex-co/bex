## Objective

Sent as `Authorization: Bearer`, an org audit-log key reads exactly what render.com/docs/api-keys says it does, "audit logs for your organization and all of its workspaces", and nothing else.

## Context

- The bearer is resolved in `lego/backend/internal/api/auth_apikey.go`; a workspace key becomes an identity bound to its tenant. What a scoped key may call is `lego/backend/internal/api/key_permission_ops.go` and `scope_matrix_ops.go`, and the scope matrix tests walk every operation.
- The two readers: `audit.Service.ListOrganization` (`lego/backend/internal/audit/organizations.go`, which today checks `can_manage` on the org and then the caller's org role) and `audit.Service.List` (`audit/service.go`) behind `GET /v1/owners/{ownerId}/audit-logs`. GraphQL `organizationAuditLogs` and the workspace `auditLogs` field read the same.
- A workspace joins or leaves the org through create, delete and the support transfer (ADR115 D3); the key must follow the org's membership at request time, not a list taken at mint.
- The org's login requirements (ADR115 D10, `twofactor.Gate.CheckOrganization`) apply as t001 settled.

## Steps

1. Resolve an org key to a principal that carries its org and the audit-log-only capability, and record it as the audit caller the way other keys are.
2. Let that principal through `ListOrganization` for its own org and `List` for any workspace whose org is its org; refuse both otherwise with the shape an unauthorized key gets today.
3. Refuse it on every other REST route, GraphQL field and MCP tool, and on the SSH gateway and shell tickets. Prefer one deny-by-default check over a per-route list, so a new route cannot open to it by accident.
4. Write the bex addition to [ADR012](../../../../../docs/ADR012-auth.md) (the key kind and its one capability) and to [ADR115](../../../../../docs/ADR115-organizations.md) as D11.
5. `cd lego/backend && go test ./...`, and `make lint` from `lego/operator/`.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/api/auth_apikey.go`, `key_permission_ops.go` / `scope_matrix_ops.go` (or the check that replaces a list), `lego/backend/internal/audit/`
- `docs/ADR012-auth.md`, `docs/ADR115-organizations.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The key reads the org log and each org workspace's log on REST with Render's shapes, including a workspace created after the key, and stops reading a workspace that leaves the org.
- [ ] Every other operation in the scope matrix refuses it, a test fails if a new operation would let it through, and another org's log and a workspace outside the org refuse it.
- [ ] A revoked key is refused within ADR012 §8d's window.

## Out of scope

- The dashboard (t004).

## Result (2026-11-26)

`core.OrganizationKeyGrant` (the grant `store.BoundAPIKeyGrant` answers for an org key), the dispatch gate's `organizationKeyOps` (the two audit-log reads, deny by default), and `core.Base.AuthorizeOrganizationAuditLog` / `AuthorizeWorkspaceAuditLog`, which decide the key by its live row and the workspace's org at request time. ADR012 §8g and ADR115 D11.
