## Objective

Show a group's snapshots as Render's Snapshots page does, with a snapshot's details and delete.

## Context

- Render: README § What Render does (Snapshots page).
- bex: GraphQL `sandboxSnapshots(sandboxGroupId, …)`, `sandboxSnapshot`, `deleteSandboxSnapshot` ([ADR104 D4](../../../../docs/ADR104-sandbox-render-parity.md#d4--snapshots)); a snapshot's `error` carries bex's failure reason, which t001 maps onto Render's texts.

## Steps

1. The list: name, id, kind, status (Capturing, Available, Failed), source sandbox, size, captured and expires (Never for `ttlSeconds: 0`), with search, the time range, paging and live updates.
2. The drawer: Details and Sandboxes (started from it), the restore starter code, and Delete snapshot with Render's confirmation and error; a `creating` snapshot's delete shows bex's refusal.
3. Empty states ("Create your first snapshot") and errors; eight locales; a pending frame matching the ready state at 1440 and 390 px.
