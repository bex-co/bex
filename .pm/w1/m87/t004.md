## Objective

Prove the pipeline works against real infrastructure, not just the MinIO integration test w1/m85 already passes. The claim under test is ADR082 D5's: a disk is snapshotted daily, kept seven days, listable, and restorable.

## Context

- w1/m85 verified the byte pipeline (tar→gzip→age round trip, tar-escape refusal, wrong-key failure) and the store half against MinIO. This task verifies the **deployed** half.
- A restore is irreversible and scales the service down first — use a scratch disk on a scratch service, never a real one.

## Steps

1. Attach a small disk to a throwaway service; write a known file.
2. Trigger/await the CronJob; confirm exactly one object lands under the disk's prefix.
3. `GET …/snapshots` lists it with a 24h `snapshotKey`.
4. Mutate the file, restore, confirm the pre-snapshot content returns and the post-snapshot change is gone.
5. Delete the disk; confirm the purge Job removes its objects.

## Files

- verification only; fixes land in whichever file the failure points at

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] the literal sequence in Steps passes on real infrastructure
- [ ] purge-on-delete leaves no objects under that disk's prefix
- [ ] results recorded in the milestone README

## Out of scope

- Restoring any tenant's real disk.
