## Objective

Repeat a create that failed transiently, with backoff, within a total bound that stays well inside the dispatch budget; fail at once on anything else.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/service.go` dispatch: `sandbox.IsCapacityLimit` and `sandbox.IsStartFailure` already classify two non-transient failures.
- `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/client.go` already has a runtime-unavailable answer (`runtimeRetryAfterSeconds`).

## Steps

1. Define the transient class: connection refused/reset, dial timeout, `502`/`503`/`504`, runtime unavailable.
2. Retry through t002's safe path with backoff (for example 2 s, 5 s, 10 s), logging each attempt; keep the existing failure reasons and codes for the final failure.
3. Keep the provisioning metric honest: one observation per dispatch, with the attempts count.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tests: transient then success → running; non-transient → one attempt; transient every time → fails after the bound with today's reason.

## Out of scope

- Retrying a failure inside the sandbox after it started (driver errors).
