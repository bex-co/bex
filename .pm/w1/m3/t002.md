## Objective

Default kube-scheduler _spreads_ pods; bex wants it to _pack_ onto the fullest node so empty nodes free up for scale-down. Configure a `MostAllocated` scoring profile.

## Context

- `KubeSchedulerConfiguration` profile: plugin `NodeResourcesFit`, `scoringStrategy.type: MostAllocated`.
- Delivered via the KubeadmControlPlane scheduler `--config` in the CAPH overlay.
- No visible effect until multi-node (single node today).

## Files

- `infra/clusterapi/overlays/hetzner-caph/cluster.yaml` (KCP)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On a multi-node cluster, new pods land on the most-allocated node (verified in m3/t005).

## Out of scope

- Autoscaler (t003/t004); idle-evict is the other half of the allocator (m4).
