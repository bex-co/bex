# Sub-10GB disks cost bex more than they charge — Hetzner's volume minimum is 10 GB

Why: bex sells 1 GB and 5 GB disks for less than the Hetzner volume underneath them costs, so every one sold loses money, and ADR082 D8's ~71% margin table is wrong for the two smallest sizes it ships.

Found 2026-08-24 during w1/m87/t006, by reading the live disk's kubelet capacity rather than its spec.

**Evidence.** `beancount-ledger` has a disk provisioned at `sizeGB: 1`. Its actual volume reports:

```
kubelet_volume_stats_capacity_bytes{persistentvolumeclaim="disk-tea-da2isimlm39c739m4ofg-beancount-ledger"}
  = 10464022528     # ≈ 9.75 GiB — a 10 GiB volume after ext4 overhead, not a 1 GB one
kubelet_volume_stats_used_bytes = 32768
```

**The arithmetic**, using ADR082's own figures ([docs/ADR082-persistent-disks.md](../../docs/ADR082-persistent-disks.md) § Context and § D8):

| | 1 GB disk | 5 GB disk | 10 GB disk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| bex charges (provisioned × $0.175) | $0.175 | $0.875 | $1.75 |
| Hetzner bills (10 GB floor × €0.0440 ≈ $0.050) | ~$0.50 | ~$0.50 | ~$0.50 |
| margin | **−$0.33** | **+$0.38** | +$1.25 |

So 1 GB is sold below cost outright, 5 GB earns ~43% rather than the documented ~71%, and only at 10 GB and above does the D8 margin table hold. The `€0.0572` price-rise scenario D8 also models makes 1 GB worse still.

**Why it happened.** ADR082's own context section records the constraint — "1 GB increments, **min 10 GB**, max 10 TB" — but the implementation set `diskMinSizeGB = 1` (`lego/backend/internal/apps/disks.go:52`) and the dashboard ships Render's `SIZE_CHIPS = [1, 5, 10, 50, 100]` (`disk-section.tsx:65`). Render's chips were copied for parity without re-checking them against bex's own supplier floor. Render charges $0.25/GB and presumably absorbs or avoids the same floor; bex discounts 30% and cannot.

**Options, needs a decision:**

1. **Raise the floor to 10 GB** — change `diskMinSizeGB`, drop the 1 and 5 chips, default stays 10. Honest and simple; costs a little Render parity on the form. Note the CRD's grow-only CEL rule means existing sub-10GB disks cannot be shrunk into compliance, only grown, so this is safe for them.
2. **Keep the sizes, bill the floor** — meter `max(sizeGB, 10)` so the invoice matches the volume actually provisioned. Truthful to cost but surprising: a tenant who asks for 1 GB is billed for 10.
3. **Keep the sizes, absorb the loss** — defensible as a loss-leader on the smallest tier, but then D8's margin table must be corrected to say so rather than claiming ~71% across the board.

Whichever is chosen, ADR082 D8 needs updating — it currently states a margin that is not true of two of the five sizes the product offers.

**Also worth checking while here:** the Disk tab prints the tenant's *requested* size ("1 GB") while the usage chart's capacity axis shows the *real* volume ("10 GiB"). Those two numbers sitting on one page disagreeing is confusing regardless of which option above is taken.
