# Prod's kubelet-csr-approver races to deny every kubelet-serving CSR; CAPH's approval is what keeps serving certs working

Why: the approver that ADR072 finding 5 (closed by `w2/m81`) counts on approves nothing in production. It denies every kubelet-serving CSR, and nodes keep valid serving certificates only because cluster-api-provider-hetzner approves each CSR first. If the approver ever wins that race, the node's 6-hour serving cert is not renewed on time, and `kubectl logs`/`exec` and metrics-server's verified kubelet TLS fail for that node.

Evidence (prod read 2026-10-15, found while answering `w1/128`):

- `kubectl -n kube-system logs` across both approver pods, last 7 days: 385 lines `Denying kubelet-serving CSR. DNS checks failed. Reason:The SAN DNS Name could not be resolved`, and zero approvals or update errors. Node hostnames (`bex-tenant-0-bdn2q-5jjf9`) do not resolve in cluster DNS, and prod runs `bypassDnsResolution: false` (`deploy/gitops/base/kubelet-csr-approver.yaml`).
- Every live kubelet-serving CSR (`csr-f75jb`, `csr-h84tz`, `csr-ckm85`) is `Approved` with `reason: CSRValidationSucceed`, `message: Validation by cluster-api-provider-hetzner was successful`, and carries an issued certificate valid for about 6 hours (so each node rotates about 5 times a day, matching the deny count).
- The approver logs its deny before `UpdateApproval`; when CAPH has already approved, the update conflicts and the approver requeues silently, then ignores the approved CSR (`internal/controller/csr_controller.go` at v1.2.14). Whichever controller writes first wins; so far it is CAPH.
- Kubelet SANs carry both the node's InternalIP (`10.10.0.x`) and its public ExternalIP, so turning DNS resolution off and narrowing `providerIpPrefixes` cannot reproduce CAPH's check (`w1/128`).

Shape: stop the race in prod without weakening approval. CAPH validates each CSR against its HCloudMachine's addresses, which is the check the base comment wanted. Likely fix: keep the postfinance approver local-only (CAPD has no CAPH), by moving its Application out of `base` or excluding it in the prod overlay. Check that pruning the Application removes its Deployment and RBAC (the Application carries no `resources-finalizer`, so the prune may orphan them). Then extend the `w1/127` approver guard in `scripts/gitops-validate.sh`, correct the base comment, and add a dated correction to ADR072 finding 5 and ADR063:91 (they credit the approver with prod's approved serving certs). Done when prod runs no racing approver and new kubelet-serving CSRs are still `Approved` by CAPH and issued.

**Done 2026-10-15 (`/loopx w1`), shipped in `7f596cbb3`.** Base now sets `skipDenyStep: true`, which prod inherits; the CAPD mock keeps the deny step. `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` pins `[bypassDnsResolution, skipDenyStep]` to `[true,false]` locally and `[false,true]` in prod, checked on yq 4.44.6 and by mutating each overlay. ADR072 #5 and ADR063 #9 carry dated corrections.

I chose `skipDenyStep` over removing the Application from prod. The Application has no `resources-finalizer`, so pruning it would have orphaned a still-racing Deployment. CAPH v1.1.7 itself Denies a CSR that fails its address check (`CSRValidationFailed`), so prod still actively denies non-conforming CSRs.

Prod check after the rollout (Argo synced the Deployment to `SKIP_DENY_STEP=true` about 4.5 minutes after the push): the first new kubelet-serving CSR, `csr-vz9gg` for `bex-control-plane-hpjqx`, is `Approved` (`CSRValidationSucceed`, "Validation by cluster-api-provider-hetzner was successful") and issued. For the same CSR the approver logged "Skipping the 'deny' step, the CSR will be left untouched."

Left for later, not filed: the approver now does nothing in prod and could be removed there. That needs a two-step prune, first adding the finalizer and then deleting the Application.
