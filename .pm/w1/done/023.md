# Platform-pool baked-image roll is blocked on CNPG HA — single-instance DBs make the node un-drainable (w1/m36 Push 2b)

> **Renumbered from `022` on promotion 2026-07-15** — its original number collided with the already-done REST-error-body note (`done/022.md`), the concurrent-session numbering hazard.

`w1/m36` rolled the **tenant** pool onto the baked `bex-worker` snapshot (fast, github/pkgs-independent bring-up — done, live-verified 2026-07-15). The **platform** pool was left on `ubuntu-24.04` for a maintenance window. A read-only pre-flight then found the platform pool is **not drain-safe**, so Push 2b can't just be pushed:

- **All four CNPG Postgres clusters are single-instance and co-located on one node** (`bex-platform-b5tch-lv84c`): `hydra-db`, `kratos-db`, `openfga-db` (auth) + `bex-db` (control-plane). Each has a PDB `*-primary` with **minAvailable=1 / allowed-disruptions=0**. Rolling that node drains it → the DB evictions are **denied by the PDB** (roll stalls) or, if forced, take **all auth (Kratos/Hydra/OpenFGA) and the control-plane DB down** with no replica to fail over to.
- **OpenBao** is a healthy 3-member raft (quorum survives one member) but uses **Shamir/manual unseal** (no auto-unseal). Each member that reschedules onto a fresh node comes up **sealed** and needs a manual unseal to rejoin; its PDB `maxUnavailable=1` stalls the roll until then.

**This is a platform-resilience gap, not an m36 task.** m36's goal (autoscaler-hot-path node bring-up) is delivered by the tenant pool; the platform pool barely autoscales (min 2 / max 3). Do NOT roll it to chase cosmetic parity at the cost of an auth/control-plane outage.

**To do Push 2b safely (size + decide):**

1. **Make the four CNPG DBs HA** — `instances: 2`+ with pod anti-affinity so a replica lives on another platform node and CNPG can switch over during the drain. (This also fixes the standing single-copy risk the m19.1 review flagged for `bex-db`.) **or** accept a **planned auth + control-plane downtime window** and drain with the DB PDBs temporarily relaxed.
2. **Plan the OpenBao unseal** — 3 members, Shamir keys in `.env`/GH secrets; unseal each as it reschedules (quorum held by the other two), one node at a time.
3. Then flip the platform pool exactly like the tenant pool (the `STAGED ROLL` comments in `infra/clusterapi/overlays/hetzner-caph/cluster.yaml` mark every line: `bex-platform` → `bex-platform-baked`, `imageName: bex-worker`, trim the platform `preKubeadmCommands`) and roll one node at a time, watching CNPG switchover + OpenBao rejoin.

Evidence + timeline: `.pm/w1/m36/README.md` § "Platform-roll pre-flight". Prerequisite #1 (CNPG HA) is the real blocker and is worth its own milestone.

Source: `w1/m36` platform-roll pre-flight, 2026-07-15.
