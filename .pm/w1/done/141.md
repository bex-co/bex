# gitops (render) fails on transient downloads on the self-hosted runners

Why: two of the gitops (render) reds after m174 were network downloads failing, not code, and every such red teaches people to ignore the check again.

- **Evidence:**
  - `39406673216` (2026-10-20): the `cli-analytics` job's `docker pull` hit a `registry-1.docker.io` TLS handshake timeout, then `context deadline exceeded` after retries.
  - `39530428078` (2026-10-21): the `render` job's `azure/setup-helm` (`.github/workflows/gitops.yml:128`) could not download `https://get.helm.sh/helm-v3.19.0-linux-arm64.tar.gz`.
- **Directions:**
  - Serve the pinned helm binary from the runner's tool cache, or bake it into the runner image, so the job no longer downloads it.
  - Add a Docker Hub pull-through mirror on the runner host.
  - Or retry around these two fetches only.
- **Constraint:** stay on the self-hosted fleet (`DO_NOT_DO.md` `#CI-RUNNERS`, `#RUNNER-HOSTS`). Never move a job to GitHub-hosted runners.
- **Done when** neither download can turn a gitops run red on its own, shown by a fault-injection check or a 20-run green streak with no download failure.
- **Source:** `w1/m174` closeout, 2026-10-23.

## Done 2026-10-26 (`/loopx w1`)

Both fetches now retry, in `.github/workflows/gitops.yml`:

- **Docker Hub pull (`cli-analytics`).** Postgres runs as a step instead of a `services:` container, the same pattern `backend-test.yml` uses. The step pulls with 5 attempts and 20–80s backoff, and the tests `docker exec` into the named container.
- **helm (`render`).** `azure/setup-helm` is replaced by a `curl --retry 5 --retry-all-errors` download, verified against get.helm.sh's published sha256 for `helm-v3.19.0-linux-arm64.tar.gz` (`440cf7ad…`).
- **Same job, same class.** The yq and fga downloads got the same retry flags. fga now downloads to a file, because a retried transfer would re-send bytes into a `tar` pipe.

**Fault-injection check (local, the steps' own `run:` text extracted with yq):**

- The Postgres step with a `docker` shim whose pull fails N times: N=0, 2 and 4 all pass, with 1, 3 and 5 pulls. N=5 exits 1 without starting Postgres.
- The helm step in `kindest/node:v1.36.1` against a local server answering 503 to the first N requests: N=2 installed `v3.19.0+g3d8990f` after 3 requests. A persistent 503 failed after 6 attempts.
- With real Docker, the new Postgres step plus `test_cli_analytics.py` and `test_product_analytics.py`: 47 tests OK.
- `scripts/github-actions-validate.sh`: PASS.

**Still unretried (not seen failing):** `azure/setup-kubectl`, and the implicit Alloy/Loki pulls inside `test_log_shipper.py` / `test_log_delivery.py`. `helm-artifact.sh` already retries its chart pulls.
