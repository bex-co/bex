## Objective

Restart and specific-commit semantics are identical on REST, GraphQL, MCP and the dashboard, and match Render, or diverge on the record.

## Steps

1. Drive a restart on a pinned service through REST, GraphQL, MCP, the header and the row. Record each resulting deploy's commit. All five must equal the running commit.
2. Drive a specific-commit deploy through REST, GraphQL, MCP and the new dialog. Record the commit and `autoDeploy` for each.
3. Decide and record the adjacent rules: staged (`save_only`) env changes are not applied by a restart (Render); auto-deploy stays off after a pin until the user re-enables it (Render).
4. Update `docs/ADR018-render-parity.md` rows **Restart** (line 75) and **Trigger a deploy** (line 96) with the commit semantics, and `docs/ADR004-app-deployment.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The response tables are recorded in the m148 README.
- [ ] ADR018 and ADR004 are updated. Any divergence kept on purpose is written as a divergence with its reason.
