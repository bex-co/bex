## Objective

The environment allowlist layer applies to every member service, so its views name the members' Cloudflare-proxied custom domains while the layer restricts traffic.

## Steps

1. Add `EnvironmentView.IPAllowListProxiedDomains` and `proxiedDomainsByEnvironment`. It makes one tenant App list per call and matches members by `store.ManagedAppID`, falling back to the CR name. Deleting Apps are skipped.
2. Fill it in `view` (Get, SetACL, Update, membership verbs), `List` (the dashboard's read) and `ListWorkspace`.
3. Add it to REST `renderEnvironment` (omitempty), GraphQL `Environment.ipAllowListProxiedDomains`, and the MCP `get_environment`/`create_environment`/`update_environment` warning block.
