## Objective

Check that REST, GraphQL, MCP and the dashboard agree, and record the divergence from Render.

## Outcome (2026-10-02)

- **One field, one meaning, every surface.** The field is `ipAllowListProxiedDomains` on the service (REST nests it at `serviceDetails` beside `ipAllowList`) and on the environment. GraphQL has the same field on `Service` and `Environment`. MCP carries it in the structured JSON plus a plain-text warning block. The dashboard shows a notice on both allowlist editors.
- **Render.** Render has no such field. render.com documents neither the address its allowlist evaluates nor anything about upstream proxies (ADR018 row, fetched 2026-09-14). This is recorded on ADR018's inbound-IP-allowlist row as a **bex extension with no Render counterpart**. Omitting the field when empty keeps every other response byte-identical. The Render response schemas are open objects, so the conformance gate needs no new divergence entry; `TestRenderConformance` passes.
- **Known asymmetry.** Like `pushDeliveryMethod`, the field is not computed on service lists or on create responses. Create responses have no custom domains until a domain is added.
