## Objective

Confirm that each surface reports the same state while a failed or in-flight build sits over a serving release, through the parked, waking and serving states: REST `GET /v1/services/{id}` (phase, `suspended`), deploy rows, events, GraphQL, MCP `get_service` / `get_deploy`, and the dashboard header. Record where bex diverges from Render.

## Context

- **Render's model.** A failed deploy leaves the previous deploy live. A free web service spins down after 15 minutes idle and spins up on the next request. Suspend and resume act on the running service.
- **Ledger.** `docs/ADR018-render-parity.md` (deploy, free-instance and suspend rows) and `docs/ADR004-app-deployment.md`.
