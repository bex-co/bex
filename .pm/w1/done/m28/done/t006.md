## Objective

Prove the gate is real, not just present: deliberately break a test, confirm CI fails and the deploy job does not run, then confirm a passing commit deploys normally.

## Context

- Mirrors the live-verification bar every other milestone in this session holds itself to (`w1/m26/t007`, `w1/m27/t003`/`t005`/`t006`).
- Use a scratch branch or a throwaway commit that's reverted immediately after — don't leave a broken test on `main`.

## Steps

1. On a scratch branch, introduce a deliberate test failure (e.g. flip an assertion in one backend test).
2. Push and confirm the new CI workflow fails, and (if pushed to `main` directly, or simulated via `workflow_dispatch`) confirm `build-and-deploy` does not run.
3. Revert the deliberate failure, confirm CI passes and deploy proceeds normally.

## Files

- none (a scratch-branch experiment, reverted after)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A broken test demonstrably blocks the deploy job.
- [ ] The fix demonstrably unblocks it.

## Out of scope

- Testing every possible failure mode (lint failure, typecheck failure, etc.) — one representative test-failure drill is sufficient to prove the mechanism.
