# The Postgres Disk metric header crushes its title and shifts on load at phone width

Why: at 390px the datastore Metrics panel's Disk header squeezes "Disk" into one letter per line beside the autoscaling box, and its capacity label adds a row only once data arrives. The loading skeleton is 28px shorter than the ready section, against the dashboard's skeleton-parity rule.

**Evidence (production, 2026-10-17, found by the w1/m180 live check).**

- `dashboard/src/features/metrics/components/metric-section.tsx:79-80`: the title row is `flex items-center justify-between gap-2`, and the title `div` can shrink. `datastore-metrics-panel.tsx:169-180` passes a `headerExtra` holding the capacity label plus `diskHeaderExtra` (Postgres disk autoscaling, `databases.$databaseId.tsx`) in a `flex flex-wrap justify-end` box.
- Postgres `dpg-db9ipkm22tos73dl1sug` at 390: "Disk" renders as "D / is / k" when ready ("Di / sk" while pending). The section is 886px pending and 914px ready, Disk→CPU is 196 against 220, and every later chart keeps identical spacing. At 1440, and for Key Value at either width (no autoscaling box), pending and ready match exactly.
- The header predates w1/m180: `4cb488366` (disk autoscaling) and `7f591900a` (w1/m86 disk layout).

**Shape:** keep the section title from shrinking and let a wide header extra drop below it at narrow widths. Keep the capacity label's row stable between loading and ready, either always reserved while it loads or placed so it never adds a row. Add a test that pins the title's no-shrink and the reserved slot, then compare pending against ready on production at 1440 and 390 after deploy. About 45m.

**Fix (2026-10-17).** The root cause of the letter-per-line title is the body's `[overflow-wrap:anywhere]` (`shell-component.tsx:37`): it lets a flex item shrink below its longest word. `MetricSection`'s title now sets `[overflow-wrap:normal]`, so words stay whole and a two-word title still wraps between words. A blanket `shrink-0` was tried first and dropped: on a live service's Metrics tab at 360 it grew the "Total Requests" header from 48px to 128px by squeezing its extra. While `disk_capacity` loads, `DatastoreMetricsPanel` holds the capacity label's slot with an `h-4 w-24` skeleton (`aria-hidden`; MetricSection's own `role="status"` already announces the load). Simulating both changes on production in the DOM (free Postgres `dpg-db9jlfddkths73a1si80`) gave identical pending and ready sections at 390 (914px; Disk 130, CPU 330, Memory 477, Active Connections 623) and 360 (869px). 1440 and every service metrics header at 390/360 were unchanged. Tests: `metric-section.test.tsx` "keeps the title's words whole" and the panel's "holds the capacity label's slot" (loading → value → none); both fail without the fix. Dashboard typecheck, lint and 5,129 tests green. Waiting on the production check after deploy.
