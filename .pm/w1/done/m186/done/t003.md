## Objective

Limit what the drain-guard ServiceAccount can change to exactly its three writes, even if its RBAC grows. Document how to operate it.

## Context

- Admission-policy style: `deploy/gitops/base/operator-alias-admission.yaml`.
- Lock-down runbook: `docs/runbooks/operator-kubeconfig-isolation.md` § Verification.

## Steps

1. Add `deploy/gitops/base/drain-guard-admission.yaml`: a fail-closed ValidatingAdmissionPolicy and binding that match UPDATE on Machines, Nodes and Deployments by `system:serviceaccount:bex-system:bex-drain-guard`. It allows only:
   - the hook annotation on Machines;
   - `spec.unschedulable` → true on Nodes;
   - `drain-guard.bex.co/evacuated-from` on Deployment pod templates.
2. Runbook updates:
   - Extend the lock-down verification loop with the drain-guard identity, which must print `no` for every Secret.
   - Add a drain-guard runbook covering the hook, the bound, and manual release (`kubectl -n bex-capi annotate machine <m> pre-drain.delete.hook.machine.cluster.x-k8s.io/bex-drain-guard-`).
3. Add an ADR004 note: node rolls hand single-instance services over before the drain.

## Evidence (2026-10-25)

The policy was applied to `kind-bex-mgmt` (Kubernetes 1.36.1, CAPI v1.13.4), together with drain-guard's own ClusterRole and Role. The Role was pointed at the mock's CAPI namespace, `default`. Server-side dry runs were then sent as `system:serviceaccount:bex-system:bex-drain-guard` (`vap-probe.sh`, 32 probes). All 32 behaved as expected; the policy and the temporary RBAC were removed afterwards.

- **Allowed:**
  - adding its own hook (`bex-drain-guard`) to a Machine;
  - `kubectl cordon`;
  - `drain-guard.bex.co/evacuated-from` on a pod template.
- **Denied by the policy** (each one RBAC would have allowed):
  - Machines: the hook with another value, another owner's hook, any other annotation, a label, a spec field, a finalizer;
  - Nodes: uncordon, cordon plus a label, cordon plus a taint, an annotation;
  - Deployments: an evacuation value that is not a node name, another template annotation, replicas, image, template label, a Deployment annotation.
- **Denied by RBAC:**
  - Machine delete and Machines outside the CAPI namespace;
  - node status and node delete;
  - the Deployment scale subresource, create and delete;
  - `get` on `bex-kubeconfig`, `bex-ca`, `bex-etcd`, `bex-proxy` and `bex-sa`, and listing Secrets.
- The operator's `config/rbac/role.yaml` is unchanged (`git diff --stat` shows no change to it).
