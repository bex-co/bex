# Dashboard datastore twins: shared modules for databases/keyvalue

From the 2026-08-19 refactor review (§2.4). `features/databases/` and `features/keyvalue/` are one feature written twice — log viewers, rename hooks, and plan hooks are byte-identical (0 differing lines after noun normalization); status badges/name rows differ only in comments; `use-connection-info` differs by 1 real line. The pair already produced the two w1/m76 drift bugs (missing capability gate, missing eager poll) plus opposite `suspended` normalization conventions (`databases/lib/status.ts:36` raw string vs `keyvalue/lib/status.ts:37` boolean).

Land in the review's order (steps 1-2 near-zero risk): (1) promote the identical trio — `useRenameResource(doc, keyPrefix)`, `useUpdateResourcePlan`, `<DatastoreLogViewer kind>` — collapsing 6 files → 3; (2) one `deriveStatus` module in `common/lib/resource-status.ts` taking `{STATUS_MAP, labelPrefix}`, normalizing `suspended` to boolean in both mappers; (3) one `<ConnectionInfoPanel>` in common, parameterized `{fields, showPassword}`, capability gate applied once by construction; (4) one `<DatastoreDetailPage>` frame `{header, tabs, sections, skeleton}` — `service-detail-layout.tsx` already models the shape; also absorbs the routes' verbatim error/not-found trios and the 5 hand-rolled tab bars. ~600-800 lines; good test cover in both features' `__tests__` (700+ lines). Payoff compounds: the third datastore type becomes a config table instead of a copy-paste. Do after w1/m76 (its two fixes should land, then be preserved by construction here).

**Addendum (2026-08-19 simplify review):** the twins' shared eager-refetch pattern (`startPolling(3000)` before `refetch`, now via `eagerRefetch`/`CONVERGING_POLL_INTERVAL_MS` in `common/lib/polling.ts`) never restores the baseline cadence unless the resource actually passes through a converging status — a no-op verb or a fast transition can pin the detail page at 3s polling until unmount, and the eager poll bypasses `skipPollWhenHidden`. Behavior change, deliberately not made during the simplify pass; fix it once in the shared consolidation here (track an eager flag/timestamp the converging effect clears after one converging→settled cycle or a timeout).
