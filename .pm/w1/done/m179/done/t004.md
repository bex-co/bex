## Amendment (2026-10-17)

dev-1 shares the cluster's single (old) operator, so the check moves to production after deploy, on a **free** fixture so nothing is billed. As the QA user, create a free web image service whose image listens on the wrong port (for example `traefik/whoami`, which serves :80, with port 3000). After its deploy closes `update_failed` at the progress deadline:
- `GET /v1/services/{id}/instances` returns `[]` and stays `[]` for 10 minutes;
- the service reads Failed, not Hibernated;
- the deploy row keeps its diagnosis;
- a request to its URL gets a plain 503 and doesn't wake it.

Then deploy a corrected port and confirm it serves, and delete the fixture.
