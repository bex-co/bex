## Objective

On a TTY an omitted session opens a picker of the caller's 20 most recent sessions (`mine=true`; `--all` widens) with state, title, repo and age; the same picker serves automations. Without a TTY an omitted argument exits 2. Cobra `ValidArgsFunction` completes `ags-`, `aut-` and automation names with a 2 s budget and stays silent on any error.
