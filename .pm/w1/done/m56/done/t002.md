## Objective

Upgrade management templates and the application cluster from Kubernetes 1.31 to a currently supported release through supported one-minor transitions.

## Context

- infra/clusterapi/overlays/hetzner-caph/cluster.yaml pins v1.31.0 in control-plane and worker templates.
- Kubernetes 1.31 reached end of life on 2025-11-11. CNPG 1.30 supports Kubernetes 1.34 through 1.36 and only tests older 1.31.
- The exact target must also be supported by the installed CAPI, CAPH, Cilium, Traefik, cert-manager, kpack, and CNPG versions.

## Steps

1. Select a supported target at least 1.34 and document the complete component compatibility matrix.
2. Update templates and roll one minor at a time, verifying control plane, workers, CNI, storage, workloads, and CAPI health after each hop.
3. Update bootstrap and disaster-recovery instructions with the resulting version sequence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] No production or provisioning manifest pins the Kubernetes fleet to 1.31. The separate kpack v0.17 compatibility override is owned by t003.
- [x] Live nodes and control plane report v1.34.9 with all platform workloads Ready.
- [x] App create/deploy/suspend/resume/delete and managed Postgres smoke tests pass after the upgrade.

## Out of scope

- Skipping unsupported minor versions.
- Changing tenant product APIs.

- Fresh etcd and OpenBao recovery Jobs completed before the first hop and after every minor. Exact Jobs, object-store-safe evidence, manifests, worker snapshots, and workflow runs are recorded in `docs/drills/2026-07-27-platform-deprecation-preflight.md`.
