## Objective

Give Zot a retention/GC policy so a runaway rebuild loop (the incident: 44+ generations, ~9.7Gi, from a since-deleted app) can't silently fill the 10Gi volume again.

## Context

- Zot supports repo-level retention policies (tag/generation count caps, age-based pruning) and periodic GC.
- The incident was resolved manually: deleted all manifests for the offending repo, restarted Zot to trigger startup GC, dropped usage to 2.5Gi.
- Zot's chart/config lives wherever `deploy/gitops/` deploys the registry (`bex-registry` namespace).

## Steps

1. Decide the retention shape: cap generations kept per repo (e.g. last N pushes) and/or age-based pruning.
2. Configure Zot's retention policy accordingly (chart values or Zot config file, whichever the deployment uses).
3. Confirm GC actually reclaims space on a test repo with several generations pushed.

## Files

- `deploy/gitops/` (Zot chart values/config)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Retention policy is active and documented (what's kept, what's pruned, on what schedule).
- [ ] A repo pushed past the retention cap has its older generations pruned automatically.
