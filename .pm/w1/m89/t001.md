## Objective

27 dashboard files re-spell the same confirm-dialog JSX. Build the one primitive they should all use, shaped so the two existing specializations become thin wrappers rather than survivors.

## Context

- Count: `grep -rl AlertDialogCancel src/features/ src/common/ | wc -l` → 27.
- Two partial precedents to fold in, not duplicate: `common/components/revoke-icon-button.tsx` (icon-button flavor) and `common/components/protected-confirmation-dialog.tsx` (server-issued confirm-phrase flavor). The new component should be the plain flavor those two specialize.
- **Both trigger shapes are in live use** and must both be supported: an uncontrolled `AlertDialogTrigger` wrapping a button, and a controlled `open`/`onOpenChange` pair driven by a list selection (the disk tab's snapshot restore is the latter).
- The disk tab also needs the typed-phrase variant (`SudoConfirmDialog` in `disk-section.tsx`), which is a third shape worth absorbing.

## Steps

1. Design the API from the call sites that exist, not from the ones that would be tidy.
2. Support both trigger shapes and an optional typed-phrase gate.
3. Reimplement `RevokeIconButton` on top of it to prove the abstraction actually fits.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/common/components/confirm-dialog.tsx` (new), `revoke-icon-button.tsx`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] both trigger shapes and the phrase gate are expressible
- [ ] `RevokeIconButton` delegates rather than duplicating
- [ ] its existing tests pass unchanged
