## Objective

No surface that lists, counts, meters or connects to a service's instances includes the holder pod. It carries the App's `app.bex.co/app` label, like the pre-deploy and snapshot Job pods.

## Context

- `core.OwnAppPods` (`lego/backend/internal/core/base.go:2432`) already drops pods with a non-empty `app.bex.co/component` label, which covers `ListInstances` and SSH targets. Check the readers that select pods by `app.bex.co/app` without going through it:
  - `lego/backend/internal/metrics/source.go` (instance counts and per-instance series);
  - `lego/operator/internal/egressmeter/meter.go`;
  - the service events and logs readers;
  - the operator's `diskNode`, `relabelAppPods`, port detection and listener reads;
  - the disk snapshot Job's pod affinity (`disk_backup.go`), whose `beside` term matches the app label. Sitting beside a holder is the same node, so that one may stay.

## Steps

1. Grep every pod selection on `app.bex.co/app` / `PodLabelApp` / `labelApp` in `lego/backend` and `lego/operator`, and record each reader's verdict here.
2. Fix each reader that would count or show the holder, with a test that seeds a holder pod.

## Record (2026-11-21)

The holder pod carries `app.bex.co/app`, `app.bex.co/app-uid` and `app.bex.co/component=disk-holder`, and runs one container, `hold`. It carries no `bex.co/app-id` (the id label is `renderenv.LabelAppID`, not `app.bex.co/app-id`), no `app.bex.co/revision` and no `pod-template-hash`, and is controlled by a Job. Its pod name starts with `dskhld-`.

**Already excluded (verdict, evidence):**

- **Instances, SSH, crash loop.** `core.OwnAppPods` (`backend/internal/core/base.go`) drops component pods. That covers `ListInstances` (REST, GraphQL, MCP), `readySSHInstances` / `ResolveSSHSession` (the gateway also re-checks revision and the app container's image), `apps/crash_loop.go`, and the metrics instance filters, limits and counts.
- **Prometheus series and billing instance-seconds.** `metrics/source.go`, `datastore_activity.go` and `usage/service.go` match `pod=~PodNameRegex(<CR name>)`, which is anchored on the CR name. A `dskhld-` pod name never matches a store-made CR name (`tea-…-`).
- **Loki history and label values.** `lokiSelectorFor` plus `lokiTypeMatcher` (`logs/loki.go`) select `container="app"`, or `container=~"app|"` beside request streams. The holder's `hold` stream is shipped but never read, so the shipper needs no change.
- **Egress meter.** `egressmeter/meter.go` attributes by `bex.co/app-id` or `app.bex.co/egress-app-id`. The holder has neither, so it is skipped.
- **Services, `servingTargetPort`, build datastore reach.** These select or require `bex.co/app-id`.
- **Operator pod readers.** `diskNode`, `nudgeDiskPods`, `evacuatedNodeClear` and drain-guard require a ReplicaSet owner. `relabelAppPods` relabels only a Job pod with no component or with `predeploy`. Port detection, listener reads, private-port `readyPod`, `webImageCandidate`, `image_digest`, `templatePodsReady`, `deploymentPodsReady`, `serving_condition`, `instance_exits` and the rollout exit, pull and crash readers all require the revision label, the `app` container or `pod-template-hash`.
- **The Deployment controller's Recreate wait and its ReplicaSets.** They count only pods a ReplicaSet controls. The holder is controlled by a Job and has no `pod-template-hash`.
- **Snapshot writers.** `quiesceDiskSnapshotWriters` keys on `component=disk-backup`. The snapshot Job's `beside` affinity may land beside a holder, which is the volume's node, so it stays.
- **Unreachable for a disk App.** The autoscaler's metrics-server count (the CRD forbids a disk with autoscaling) and the cron Job lists (a cron job cannot have a disk).
- **Kubernetes Events.** No reader in either module serves Kubernetes Events to a customer, so a holder's Multi-Attach or scheduling event reaches none.
- **Quota.** The holder is a pod in `tenant-compute-quota` with zero requests. A workspace at its pod cap refuses it, and the rollout goes on without one (`fallback_refused`).

Seen on the way, not the holder's: the store-less log read fails the same way while a nightly snapshot pod (container `snapshot`) or a pre-deploy pod runs. That is pre-existing and only on installs without Loki, so it is left as it was.
