## Objective

No surface that lists, counts, meters or connects to a service's instances includes the holder pod. It carries the App's `app.bex.co/app` label, like the pre-deploy and snapshot Job pods.

## Context

- `core.OwnAppPods` (`lego/backend/internal/core/base.go:2432`) already drops pods with a non-empty `app.bex.co/component` label, which covers `ListInstances` and SSH targets. Check the readers that select pods by `app.bex.co/app` without going through it:
  - `lego/backend/internal/metrics/source.go` (instance counts and per-instance series);
  - `lego/operator/internal/egressmeter/meter.go`;
  - the service events and logs readers;
  - the operator's `diskNode`, `relabelAppPods`, port detection and listener reads;
  - the disk snapshot Job's pod affinity (`disk_backup.go`), whose `beside` term matches the app label. Sitting beside a holder is the same node, so that one may stay.

## Steps

1. Grep every pod selection on `app.bex.co/app` / `PodLabelApp` / `labelApp` in `lego/backend` and `lego/operator`, and record each reader's verdict here.
2. Fix each reader that would count or show the holder, with a test that seeds a holder pod.
