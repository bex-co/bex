## Record (2026-11-21)

Done. The `Server` query selects `disk { mountPath }` (`dashboard/src/features/services/api/server.graphql`; `definitions.ts` regenerated offline and only the `Server` hunk kept). `toServiceView` maps it to `ServiceView.hasDisk`, and Settings passes it to `MaxShutdownDelayRow`. With a disk the row reads 30 whatever the spec holds, is disabled with no pencil, and its hint is Render's sentence, "You can't change the shutdown delay for a service with a persistent disk.", followed by a **View disk** link to `/services/$serviceId/disk`. The reason replaces the 1–300 hint in the same place rather than adding a line beneath the row, so the General card keeps the shape its settings skeleton reserves. All eight locales carry `services.maxShutdownDelayDiskReason` and `services.maxShutdownDelayDiskLink`. A server `400` still toasts the server's sentence (`useFieldMutation` → `mutationErrorMessage`).

Tests: `max-shutdown-delay-row.test.tsx` (read-only at 30 with the reason and link; zh and ja), `status.test.ts` (`hasDisk` from a server node, undefined for a list node), and `services.$serviceId.settings.test.tsx` (the row is read-only with a disk and editable without one). Two Server-query mocks gained `disk: null`, without which Apollo reads their response as incomplete (`cron-late-read-rollback`, `env-groups-panel-link-order`). `yarn lint` and `yarn test` pass (817 files, 10013 tests).
