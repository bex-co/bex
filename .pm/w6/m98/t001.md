## Objective

Before touching any code, settle the actual Stripe mechanics: for an `Invoices.CreatePreview` call against a subscription with an active Billing Credit Grant and nonzero metered usage, which field (if any) reports the gross pre-credit charge, and which reports the amount after the grant is applied?

## Context

- Live-observed bug: `lego/backend/internal/billing/read.go:265` reads `inv.Total` for `currentCost.amountUsd`; on the QA workspace (`tea-d98210cbbpdc73dcrkvg`, credit grant "superadmin's privilege", $1000 balance) this returned `"0.00"` while the independently-computed `estimatedCost.totalUsd` was `"74.78"`.
- `lego/backend/internal/billing/read.go:44-46` already documents (for the separate `Credits` block) that "the invoice preview's total does not reliably reflect credit under flexible billing mode" — this spike is about finding what field *does* reliably reflect the gross charge, if any.
- Candidates to check on a real Stripe test-mode preview invoice with a credit grant applied: `Subtotal`, `SubtotalExcludingTax`, `TotalExcludingTax`, line-item `Amount`/`AmountExcludingTax` before credit application, `TotalPretaxCreditAmounts` (per Stripe SDK docs, lists pretax credit amounts applied — could be used to reconstruct gross = total + sum(pretax credit amounts)), and Stripe's Billing Credit Grants documentation on `docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/usage-based/billing-credits`.
- Go SDK version pinned in `lego/backend/go.mod` (stripe-go/v86 at time of this hunt) — read the actual struct field docs at that pinned version, not from memory.

## Steps

1. Stand up (or reuse) a Stripe test-mode customer + subscription with metered usage and an active Billing Credit Grant, mirroring the QA workspace's configuration (`docs/ADR071-tenant-billing-credits.md` describes how credits are issued).
2. Call `Invoices.CreatePreview` against it and dump the full raw JSON response (not just the Go struct) to see every field Stripe actually returns, especially `total_pretax_credit_amounts` and per-line-item `amount`.
3. Determine: does `Subtotal` already reflect the credit (i.e., is credit applied per-line-item before subtotal is computed), or is `Subtotal` genuinely gross and only `Total` nets out the credit?
4. If no single Stripe field is reliably gross, work out the reconstruction (e.g. `gross = Total + sum(TotalPretaxCreditAmounts[].Amount)`) and confirm it against the dumped JSON.
5. Write up the finding as a short comment/doc note (this becomes the basis for t002's implementation) — cite the exact field(s) and the JSON evidence.
