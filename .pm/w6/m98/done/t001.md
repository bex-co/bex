## Objective

## Findings (2026-08-26)

Settled from Stripe's own documentation plus the pinned SDK's field docs (`stripe-go/v86 v86.1.1`), **not** from a live test-mode run — no test-mode key was reachable in this session. `lego/backend/internal/billing/stripe_sandbox_test.go` (`BEX_TEST_STRIPE_KEY` + `BEX_TEST_DB_URI`) is the ready-made harness if a live confirmation is wanted; the shipped fix does not depend on it (see "Three candidate causes" below).

**Answer: `Subtotal` is the gross pre-credit charge, `Total` is already net of the credit, and the consumption itself is in `TotalPretaxCreditAmounts`. No `Total + Σ credits` reconstruction is needed.**

Evidence:

1. [Billing credits](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/usage-based/billing-credits): "Credits are applied to invoices **after discounts, but before taxes** and the `invoice_credit_balance`."
2. [Invoice object](https://docs.stripe.com/api/invoices/object): `subtotal` — "Total of all subscriptions, invoice items, and prorations on the invoice **before any invoice level discount** or exclusive tax is applied. Item discounts are already incorporated"; `total` — "Total **after discounts and taxes**." The ordering is therefore `subtotal` → invoice-level discounts → credit grants → tax → `total`: credit lands strictly *after* subtotal, so `Subtotal` is gross of it and `Total` net of it. The milestone's open question ("could credit apply per-line-item before subtotal?") is answered no — a line item carries `amount` and `pretax_credit_amounts` as separate fields (`invoicelineitem.go:537-565`).
3. `total_pretax_credit_amounts` — "Contains pretax credit amounts (ex: discount, credit grants, etc) that apply to this invoice", with `type` ∈ {`credit_balance_transaction`, `discount`} (`invoice.go:313-318, 3667-3676, 3889`). **Only `credit_balance_transaction` entries are grant consumption**; an ADR040 Mode B comp discount lands in the same list and must not be reported as credit.
4. Previews *do* show credit: same docs, § "Preview, draft, or finalized invoices" — credits are only *applied* at finalization, but the same credits appear on a preview/draft invoice and may change before then. A preview can therefore read `total: 0` while the grant's `available_balance` is still untouched — exactly the live reading (`remainingUsd: "1000.00"` beside a `$0.00` current cost).
5. `amount_due` is Stripe's own "final amount due at this time for this invoice" — the net figure to report, with no arithmetic of ours in between.

### Three candidate causes for the live `$0.00`, and why the fix is honest under all three

Offline evidence cannot separate them, and the milestone asserted only the first:

| # | cause | what Stripe returns | what the fixed page shows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a | credit grant absorbed the period (the milestone's hypothesis) | `subtotal` 7478, `total` 0, pretax credit 7478 | total **$74.78**, "Amount due after credits **$0.00**", credits applied **−$74.78** |
| b | ADR040 Mode B comp (100%-off coupon) | `subtotal` 7474, `total` 0, pretax **discount** 7474 | total **$74.74**, "Amount due after credits **$0.00**", credits applied **$0.00** (not misreported as credit) |
| c | Stripe has rated nothing yet (meter events not landed) | `subtotal` 0, `total` 0 | the rate-sheet estimate stays the total — never `$0.00` over a nonzero tree |
