## Objective

When the operator sees an App instance fail readiness or its rollout stall on its health check, read that pod's newest probe failure through t001's channel and record it on the App's status. Clear it as t001 decided.

## Context

- The backend turns the operator's conditions into `readiness_failed` observations (`lego/backend/internal/store/reconciler.go:2779, 2803`), and probe-stall deploy reasons come from the operator (`lego/types/v1alpha1/deploy_reasons.go`).
- RBAC: add `get`/`list` on `events` with a kubebuilder marker, then regenerate `config/rbac/role.yaml` (`make manifests`).
- Bound the read: only on a failing transition, one namespaced list filtered to the pod (`involvedObject.name`, reason `Unhealthy`), never a cluster-wide watch.

## Steps

1. Add the status field from t001 to the App type in `lego/types/v1alpha1`, then regenerate the CRDs.
2. On a readiness failure or a probe stall, list the pod's `Unhealthy` events, keep the newest message, trim it to t002's line, and write it.
3. Clear the field when t001 says.
4. envtest with a fake `Unhealthy` event: the field is written, bounded and cleared.

## Files

- lego/types/v1alpha1/app_types.go
- lego/operator/internal/controller/ (the readiness and probe-stall paths)
- lego/operator/config/rbac/role.yaml, lego/operator/config/crd/ (generated)

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] With an `Unhealthy` event carrying t002's line, the App status holds that line within one reconcile; without one, the field stays empty.
- [x] The operator never watches events; it lists them only on a failing transition.
- [x] `make test` and `make lint` pass.

## Out of scope

- The API surfaces (t004, t005).
