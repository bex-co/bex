# Verify a nightly disk snapshot and restore of a userns render-v1 service

Why: w6/m151 put render-v1 pods with disks in pod user namespaces. The snapshot/restore round trip was proven only on the local cluster, because production has no on-demand snapshot.

- **Steps:**
  1. Keep (or create) a disk-backed render-v1 service, such as unchanged ClickHouse, past one nightly `dskbak-` run.
  2. Confirm `GET /v1/disks/{id}/snapshots` lists the snapshot.
  3. `POST /v1/disks/{id}/snapshots/restore` into a fresh service and read a sentinel row back.
- **Expected:** passes. On-disk UIDs are unshifted under idmapped mounts, and the snapshot Job runs as host root with `DAC_OVERRIDE`/`CHOWN`/`FOWNER`.
- **If it fails:** remove `BEX_RENDER_V1_USER_NAMESPACES` from `lego/operator/config/prod/kustomization.yaml`. No data migration is needed.
