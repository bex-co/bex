## Objective

Stop callbacks started in an old session/workspace/access generation from publishing items, badges, bindings, or navigation in a new context.

## Context

- activeSessionRef guards only the login session; a workspace switch within one session can pass that guard.
- Store instances change per workspace, while provider items and asynchronous mark-read/receipt work can outlive the originating context.

## Steps

1. Capture the shared generation and exact identity/workspace for receipt, open, registration, and reconciliation work.
2. Check that boundary after every asynchronous step before publishing state, badge changes, bindings, or navigation.
3. Clear active items promptly during switch/logout/access reset; revalidate on foreground recovery and dispose obsolete listeners/controllers.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An A-workspace receipt/open/read completion delayed until after switching to B changes neither B's items/badge nor its navigation.
- [ ] A late registration callback cannot publish an obsolete notification binding.
- [ ] Logout clears protected notification state before best-effort remote cleanup, and re-entry does not restore revoked items.
- [ ] Recovery errors and waiting states have appropriate English/Chinese accessible copy.

## Out of scope

- New notification transports, release signing, or app-store machinery.
- Clearing unrelated accounts' data by an unscoped storage wipe.
