## Objective

No surface that lists, counts, meters, logs or connects to a service's instances shows the pre-pull pod, and the deploy's Ready condition and events never read its deliberate `StartError` as a failing instance.

## Context

- [w6/m559 t003](../done/m559/done/t003.md) did this for the holder and lists every reader with its verdict: `core.OwnAppPods`, the operator's `instancePods` (stall and node-loss messages), bex-api's store-less log reads, and the metrics-server fallback. Most drop any pod with an `app.bex.co/component` label. The logs reader drops `component=disk-holder` only.
- The pre-pull container ends in `StartError` on purpose, and the kubelet emits a `Failed` Warning event for it. Check what reads pod events for a service: deploy diagnostics, `stallDiagnosis`, and the service events feed.
