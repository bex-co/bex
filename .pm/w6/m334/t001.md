## Objective

bex-api publishes `bex_usage_oldest_unmetered_hour_age_seconds{source}`: across every open (or this month's closed) coverage stream, how old the oldest hour inside the catch-up window is that has no health row or an `unavailable` one.

## Context

- `store.UsageCatchupWindow` (48 h) and `usageSettledBefore` (49 h with the disk meter's extra hour): an hour older than that is skipped for good (w4/496)
- `usage_source_streams`, `usage_source_health`; the rollup loop in `usage.Service.RunWithIntervals`
- No existing alert watches the rollup's lag: the EgressMeter* alerts watch the meters, the Billing* alerts the export

## Steps

1. Add one fleet-wide store read (per source: the oldest missing or unavailable expected hour inside the window).
2. Publish it after each rollup.
3. Unit-test the read on a store with a hole.
