## Objective

`service_woken` should mean a service woke because something asked for it. Today four of them were emitted for wakes with no traffic.

## Context

- This is separable from t002 on purpose: even after the cycle is fixed, the event should be emitted from the wake's *cause*, not from an observed replica transition — otherwise the next un-park bug is silently mislabelled the same way.
- The event has three consumers, and the timeline is the least important: outbound **webhooks**, **push notifications** (the Notifications page exposes "Service recovered" among its event filters), and the Events tab. A false event reaches subscribers.
- `w6/m101`'s precedent applies to the webhook half — that milestone found the webhook feed joining a different source than the read surfaces and drifting from them.

## Steps

1. Find where `service_woken` is derived and confirm whether it keys on a request/activator signal or on a replica-count transition.
2. Make it key on the wake's cause.
3. Check all three consumers, not just the Events tab.
4. Confirm `service_hibernated`'s symmetry — an un-park that should not have happened must not produce a matching pair that looks legitimate.
