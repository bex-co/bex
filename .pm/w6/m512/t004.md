## Objective

Enabling internal authentication closes every connection the old, unauthenticated proxy pod relays within seconds, even though that pod now drains on `SIGTERM`. This keeps the w6/m463 guarantee and Render's "existing connections that use your unauthenticated URL will break" ([Key Value](https://render.com/docs/key-value)). Decide, with evidence, whether a credential rotation and making a store private must also cut at once, and give them the same treatment if so.

## Context

- The old pod's env and grace period are fixed when it is created, so it cannot learn the reason for its own `SIGTERM`.
- The operator has no pod `delete` RBAC, only `get`, `list`, `patch` and `watch`. It does dial each store's Valkey as `kvPlatformUser` (`+@all`), so `CLIENT LIST` and `CLIENT KILL ID` are available. The proxy's upstream connections arrive from its pod IP.
- Candidate mechanisms:
  - (a) While a pod of an older template revision still exists and the current template differs from it in a field that must cut access, the operator kills that pod's upstream clients in Valkey. Match on the pod IP in `CLIENT LIST`, and repeat until the pod is gone, because the pod accepts until it gets `SIGTERM`.
  - (b) A short-grace delete of the old pod. This needs new RBAC and a pod watch.
  - Prefer (a), unless the evidence says otherwise.
- The template already distinguishes the change: `BEX_KV_INTERNAL_AUTH`, `credentialRevisionAnnotation` and the TLS env. The current pod's ReplicaSet carries its template.
- Making a store private: check whether `kv-sni-proxy` already closes a removed route's streams. If it does, the internal proxy's TLS listener drains nothing reachable.

## Steps

1. Pick the mechanism and the list of changes that cut at once. Write both down in this task's progress section.
2. Implement them in the operator, reusing the existing platform-user dial.
3. Test them: an envtest or unit test with a fake Valkey, or the engine tests' real Valkey (`keyvalue_internal_auth_engine_test.go`).
