# A new SSH connection made in the seconds after a gateway release starts is closed before the SSH banner

Why: a release of `bex-ssh-gateway` should be invisible to someone who runs `ssh <srv>@ssh.bex.co` while it rolls. Instead, a connection that arrives just after the old pods' SIGTERM reaches a closed listener and fails with `kex_exchange_identification: Connection closed by remote host`.

- **Filed by** w6/147's production hold ([done/147.md](147.md), 2026-11-20, pin `4e37ec03f`). The probe opened a fresh plain `ssh` connection every 2 min for 4 h40m, and 140 of 141 answered `rc=0`. The one failure, `new=106` at 08:50:50Z, came during that pin's gateway roll:
  - 08:50:45.4Z: `bex-ssh-gateway-68877c47b6-qnw2n` logged `terminated: closing the listeners; open sessions keep running`.
  - 08:50:48.9Z: `-vkmhm` logged the same.
  - 08:50:50Z: the client got `rc=255`, `kex_exchange_identification: Connection closed by remote host` and `Connection closed by 49.12.20.236 port 22`.
  - The new pods `6777485846-6l8mv` and `-f6xbr` had been listening on `:2222` since 08:50:39Z and 08:50:46Z.
  - w6/m482's 169 fresh connections across two pins all answered, so this is a race inside a window of a few seconds, not a failure on every roll.
- **Mechanism.**
  - `drainOnSignal` (`lego/backend/cmd/ssh-gateway/main.go`, `ssh gateway: %v: closing the listeners`) closes the listeners as SIGTERM arrives.
  - The pod (`lego/operator/config/ssh/deployment.yaml`) has no `lifecycle.preStop`.
  - Traefik (`v3.7.17`, `IngressRouteTCP bex-system/bex-ssh-gateway`) keeps routing new TCP flows to the old pod's endpoint until it has watched the EndpointSlice change and reloaded its configuration, which takes a few seconds. A flow it opens to a closed `:2222` is refused, and Traefik closes the client side before the SSH banner.
  - The dashboard solved the same race on its own rolls with a `preStop` sleep (`dashboard/deploy/deployment.yaml`, "~3s of 502s on every roll"). w1's tenant-pod note cites Render's order: switch the traffic, wait, then send SIGTERM.
- **Fix direction.** Keep the listeners open for a short window after the pod starts terminating, and close them only after it. Either add `lifecycle.preStop.sleep` (Kubernetes v1.34 on production) of about 10 s, or wait in-process after SIGTERM while `/readyz` already answers 503, then close. Sessions keep running either way. The drain bound (`BEX_SSH_SHUTDOWN_TIMEOUT`, 1 h) plus the window must stay under `terminationGracePeriodSeconds: 3660`, and `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` checks that sum. Update ADR035 § Sessions across a gateway release.
- **Done when:** a test shows a new connection accepted during the window after SIGTERM and refused after it. On production, a client that opens a fresh `ssh <srv>@ssh.bex.co` every 2 s across a gateway release sees no failures.

## Shipped 2026-11-20 — the gateway keeps accepting for 10 s after SIGTERM; the production check waits for the pin

- **Triage: work.** The premise held. `drainOnSignal` closed every listener the moment SIGTERM arrived, the pod has no `preStop`, and Traefik v3.7.17's TCP provider (`pkg/provider/kubernetes/crd/kubernetes_tcp.go`) routes only to `ready` endpoints, so it kept the old pod's address until it had seen the EndpointSlice change and reloaded.
- **Shipped in `3285232a0`.**
  - The first SIGTERM or SIGINT turns `/readyz` 503 at once (a readiness lifetime inside the listeners'). Every listener (`:2222` and the auxiliary ones) keeps accepting for `BEX_SSH_ACCEPT_WINDOW` (default and production `10s`), then closes, and the drain runs as before, its 1 h bound counted from the close. A second signal inside the window closes the listeners and ends every session at once.
  - In-process rather than `lifecycle.preStop.sleep`, so any supervisor gets Render's order for a deploy (the traffic moves, then SIGTERM; [render.com/docs/deploys](https://render.com/docs/deploys)) and a Go test can exercise it.
  - `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` requires a positive window and `terminationGracePeriodSeconds` of at least window + bound + 30 s (10 + 3,600 + 30 ≤ 3,660, unchanged). ADR035 § Sessions across a gateway release and the backend env table say so.
- **Tests** (`lego/backend/cmd/ssh-gateway/drain_test.go`). `TestANewConnectionInTheWindowAfterSIGTERMIsServedAndRefusedAfterIt` opens a fresh SSH session and an auxiliary-listener request after SIGTERM, then sees both listeners refuse once the window has passed while that session keeps running. With the window removed it fails with `connect: connection reset by peer`, the production symptom. `TestDrainOnSignalClosesListenersAfterTheWindowAndSessionsOnTheSecond` and `TestASecondSignalInsideTheWindowEndsEverythingAtOnce` pin the signal order.
- **Next, once production's pin carries `3285232a0`.** The deploy that carries it rolls the gateway from pods without the window, so the check is the **next** gateway release after it (every production deploy runs `kubectl rollout restart deploy/bex-ssh-gateway`). Confirm both `bex-system/bex-ssh-gateway` pods run that pin and log `not ready; accepting new connections for 10s` when they terminate. In `bex-canary`, as the QA user, create a paid web service and an SSH key (w6/147's fixture shape, [done/147.md](147.md)), and open a fresh `ssh <srv>@ssh.bex.co true` every 2 s from before that release until both old pods have logged `closing the listeners; open sessions keep running`. Done when no attempt fails (`rc=255`, `kex_exchange_identification`). Then delete the fixture and key, and move this note to `done/`.
