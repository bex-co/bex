## Objective

On the local CAPD cluster, show these four things:

- An operator deploy that changes `/kv-internal-proxy` and the rendered template rolls no store's proxy.
- A client held through `<id>:6379` keeps its `CLIENT ID` across that deploy.
- The store's next customer change rolls the proxy once onto the new binary.
- An override bump rolls every proxy once.

## Context

- `bash scripts/mock-cluster.sh operator` builds `bex-operator:dev` from the tree, imports it and deploys it (pre-approved, root `AGENTS.md`).
- w6/m512 t005 and w6/m463 t003 ran the same kind of held-client checks. Reuse their client pods: a `redis-cli` loop printing `CLIENT ID` every few seconds over one connection, plus a `SUBSCRIBE` client.
- Build B is a local-only edit: a harmless change to `internal/kvinternalauth` (e.g. a log line) plus an added proxy env var. It never ships. Revert it after the proof.

## Steps

1. Deploy build A (this milestone's tree). Create two stores, `starter` and `free`, and confirm each proxy Deployment carries its inputs and revision records.
2. Start the held clients through `<id>:6379` on both stores.
3. Deploy build B. Read every proxy's ReplicaSets, pod names and ages, the `/kv-internal-proxy` hash in its image, and the clients' `CLIENT ID` lines across at least two manager passes.
4. Change the `free` store's plan to `starter`. The proxy rolls once onto B's image, with the added env and the w6/m512 drain. The held client reconnects once.
5. Enable internal authentication on the other store. A held unauthenticated client closes within seconds, and new ones read `-NOAUTH` (the w6/m463 t003 check).
6. Deploy build B with `kvInternalProxyRollRevision` bumped. Every proxy rolls once, and a further pass rolls nothing.
7. Restore the tree and redeploy build A. Delete the stores and client pods.
