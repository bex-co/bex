## Result — 2026-11-19 22:20Z (steps 2–3 done)

- **The precondition holds.** Every proxy Deployment on production carries both records: the three stores' since the shipping deploy's adoption at 21:15Z, and the probe's from its creation ([t005](t005.md) steps 3–4).
- **Deleted:**
  - w6/m469's image hold: `keyValueInternalProxyImage`, `carriesInternalProxyBinary`, `recordInternalProxyBinary`, `InternalProxyBinaryFromRegistry`, `kvInternalProxyBinaryAnnotation` and `kvInternalProxyStep`;
  - the reconciler's `InternalProxyBinary` and `InternalProxyBinaryOf` fields, and the manager's `fileSHA256` and `kvInternalProxyPath`;
  - the tests that only pinned them: `TestInternalProxyBinaryFromRegistryNeedsADigest`, `TestFileSHA256`, and three envtest specs on adopting through the image hold.
  - The three-way `kvProxyRoll` became one predicate, `keyValueInternalProxyHolds`, which also absorbs `keyValueInternalProxyRollRevision`.
- **What changes, only for a Deployment no current operator writes:**
  - One that records no inputs and runs another image now rolls once onto the manager's image. The envtest table "rolls a Deployment written before the hold once onto the manager's image and rendering, then holds it" covers it.
  - One that records inputs but no override revision now rolls, where it was taken to be at the current revision; only a hand edit makes one. The new spec "rolls a held proxy whose override record is missing or unreadable" covers it and fails under the old rule.
- **Kept, each live:**
  - `holdDatastorePriority` and `holdIsolationLabel` on the proxy Deployment still decide a rendering pass whose only difference is the priority class or the isolation labels: an unrecorded Deployment, an override bump, a suspension.
  - `registry.ImageFileSHA256` stays for `cmd/proxy-pin` (w6/m470).
  - The `app.bex.co/kv-internal-proxy-sha256` annotation stays on the Deployments that carry it. Nothing reads it, and removing it would write every proxy Deployment for no behavior. ADR021 § 10 says so.
- **Checks:** `make test` and `make lint` passed, including the whole-program dead-code analysis. The 12 hold envtest specs pass, and `TestKeyValueInternalProxyRenderingIsPinned` passes unchanged, so the rendering did not move. ADR021 § 10 now describes the image hold in the past tense.
