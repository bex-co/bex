# 139 — A Key Value backup pod becomes an endpoint of its store's backend Service

Why: while a paid store's nightly backup uploads, about half of the store's new connections are reset, and the proxy's readiness check can fail on the same coin flip.

- **The defect.** Backup and purge Job pods carry `app.bex.co/keyvalue: <id>` (`keyValueBackupLabels`, `lego/operator/internal/controller/keyvalue_backup.go`, and the purge Job's labels further down that file). That label is the whole selector of the `<id>-backend` Service (`reconcileKeyValueInternalProxy`, `keyvalue_internal_proxy.go`). It is also the selector of `<id>` itself for a store not yet behind its proxy (`keyValueIntentFor`'s `labels`). The snapshot runs as an init container, but the `upload` container has no readiness probe, so once it starts the pod is Ready and becomes an endpoint on 6379/6380, where nothing listens. `kv-internal-proxy` dials `<id>-backend` for every tenant connection (w8/204), and its `--check` readiness does too.
- **Evidence (w6/m463 t003, local CAPD cluster, 2026-11-17).** A Job running the backup CronJob's own snapshot container, with the backup labels, made the `<id>-backend` EndpointSlice list `red-m463probe-0 ready=true` and `m463-snapshot-… ready=true` while its main container ran. With a ready pod carrying those labels beside a second store, 20 of 40 new clients through `<id>:6379` were reset (`Connection reset by peer`) and 20 read Valkey's `-NOAUTH`.
- **Where it bites.** Every paid store on production runs a nightly backup (03:20–03:39 UTC), with or without internal authentication, and has done since w8/204. The proxy runs one replica, so three failed `--check` probes in a row take `<id>` off its only endpoint.
- **Fix direction.** It must restart nothing: the StatefulSet's pod labels cannot change without rolling the store. Either give the Job pods a label of their own instead of `app.bex.co/keyvalue` (and move `keyValueJobPeer` and the in-place relabelling to it), or select only the server's pods in the two Services (for example add `statefulset.kubernetes.io/pod-name`, which only StatefulSet pods carry).
- **Done when.** A ready backup or purge pod is never an endpoint of `<id>` or `<id>-backend` (a test pins both selectors against the Job pod labels), and a local check like t003's, with a backup pod Ready, resets none of 40 new clients.

## Outcome — done 2026-11-17

Triage: the premise held at `ef091b640`. `keyValueBackupLabels` stamped `app.bex.co/keyvalue` on the backup CronJob's Job and pod template and on the purge Job, `<id>-backend` and a not-yet-proxied `<id>` select exactly that label, and the `upload` container has no readiness probe.

- **Fix (the first direction).** The Jobs and their pods now carry `app.bex.co/keyvalue-job: <id>` (`labelKeyValueJob`) instead. The operator selects them by the parent-UID label `app.bex.co/keyvalue-uid`, which they have carried since August: `keyValueJobPeer` (the environment and internal-proxy ingress peers), the `<id>-backup-egress` pod selector, and the delete-time wait for backup Jobs. A backup running across the operator upgrade keeps its network path, and a pre-change Job still holds a deletion. `scripts/delete-audit.sh` counts leftovers by the new label. ADR021 § 5 records it.
- **Restarts nothing.** The StatefulSet template, the Services and the proxy Deployment are untouched. Only the CronJob template and the Job selector in up to three NetworkPolicies change.
- **Tests.** `TestKeyValueBackupPodsAreNeverAnEndpointOfTheStore` pins the selectors of `<id>` (pre-proxy) and `<id>-backend` against the rendered backup and purge pod labels, and `keyValueJobPeer` against the new and the pre-change Job pods. `TestKeyValueDeletionWaitsForBackupJobsAndPurge` now also waits for a pre-change Job. Reverting the label, the peer selector or the deletion selector fails them.
- **Local CAPD check** (operator built from this tree with `bash scripts/mock-cluster.sh operator`). A free probe store in dev-6's tenant namespace, behind its proxy. Clients were 40 sequential `valkey-cli PING` runs, each authenticating with the store password from its Secret, against `<id>:6379`.
  - With no Job pod: 40 of 40 `PONG`.
  - Control, a Ready Job pod with the pre-change labels: it was listed `ready=true` in `<id>-backend`'s EndpointSlice. 16 of 40 clients hit an I/O error (11 then failed with `Connection reset by peer`), and the proxy's `--check` failed twice.
  - The operator's own rendered backup Job (`keyValueBackupCronJobSpec` for that store; the upload swapped for a sleep, since the local cluster has no S3): its snapshot transferred the RDB over `<id>-backend`, so the new NetworkPolicy selectors admit it. While Ready it was in neither EndpointSlice, 40 of 40 clients read `PONG`, and the proxy logged no failed check. All probe objects were deleted.
- **Production** gets it with the next deploy carrying this commit. To confirm, `kubectl get cronjob kvbak-<id> -o jsonpath='{.spec.jobTemplate.spec.template.metadata.labels}'` shows `app.bex.co/keyvalue-job`, and a store's `03:2x` backup pod does not appear in `<id>-backend`'s EndpointSlice.
