## Objective

Check that the changed log-subscribe contract and the new empty-state semantics are consistent across every surface that exposes them, and that any render.com difference is recorded as a deliberate divergence.

## Context

- The logs surface has three adapters over one service: `lego/backend/internal/logs/rest.go`, `graphql.go`, `mcp.go` (`docs/ADR006-bex-api.md`). Only REST carries the SSE subscribe endpoint, but all three carry the windowed history read and the `BEX_MAX_QUERY_HOURS` bound.
- `docs/ADR010-observability.md` owns the live-tail/store split and documents which filters the tail can honour; a window is a new thing the tail now honours and the ADR should say so.

## Steps

1. Exercise the windowed history read on all three surfaces for the same service and window, and confirm identical rows, ordering and bound behaviour at the `BEX_MAX_QUERY_HOURS` edge.
2. Confirm the subscribe endpoint's `startTime` semantics are documented where a non-dashboard client (`curl -N`, the CLI) would look.
3. Compare against render.com's log viewer for the same two behaviours — does its live tail respect the range selector, and what does it say when a window is empty — and record any deliberate divergence in `docs/ADR018-render-parity.md`.
4. Update `docs/ADR010-observability.md` for the tail's new window parameter.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/logs/{rest,graphql,mcp}.go`
- `docs/ADR010-observability.md`, `docs/ADR018-render-parity.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The windowed read agrees across REST, GraphQL and MCP, including at the max-window boundary.
- [ ] The subscribe endpoint's `startTime` behaviour is documented for non-dashboard clients.
- [ ] Any render.com divergence is recorded with a reason.

## Out of scope

- New log filters or new surfaces.
