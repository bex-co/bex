1. **The drain in `Serve`.** t001's tests show it: a mid-command client still reads its reply after the drain begins, the old listener accepts no new connection, an idle client is closed at the deadline, and every budget slot is released.

3. **The local roll.** On the local cluster a binary-change roll answered a `BLPOP` 4.5 s after the old pod's `SIGTERM` and closed an idle client at +30.9 s (t005).
4. **Enabling internal authentication.** It closed a held unauthenticated client in under 2 s locally (t005). On production it closed one within a second of the old pod's `SIGTERM`, 3.4 s after the call, on a proxy draining for 30 s, and new clients read `-NOAUTH` (t007 step 2).
5. **The reserve.** `TestADrainingProxyLeavesTheReserveToThePlatform` keeps a store at its limit from taking the platform reserve during a roll (t003, t009).
