# 179 — A Stripe Customer created by Checkout or by usage does not take the workspace's billing email

Why: since w6/m627 a Hobby workspace's billing email is its owner's account email and cannot be changed anywhere, but a workspace with no Stripe Customer yet still gets one whose email is whatever its member types into Checkout, or none at all, so Stripe's receipts and invoices can go to an address bex shows nowhere.

- **Found by:** [w6/m627](../blocked/m627/README.md) t006 (Render parity), 2026-11-25.
- **Today:** `CreateCheckoutSession` (`lego/backend/internal/billing/sessions.go`) opens setup-mode Checkout with `customer_creation=always` and no `customer_email` when the workspace has no Customer, so Stripe asks for an email and the new Customer keeps it. `EnsureCustomer` (`lego/backend/internal/billing/stripe.go`), which the usage emitter and the admin path call, creates a Customer with no email. `SetCustomerEmail` only updates a Customer that already exists, and the Billing page's billing email (`workspaces.Service.BillingEmail`) then disagrees with the Customer's.
- **Do:** pass the workspace's billing email in effect (the stored address on Pro and higher, else the owner's account email, as `BillingEmail` reads it) as Checkout's `customer_email` when no Customer exists, and as the email of a Customer `EnsureCustomer` creates. Resolve it through one seam the composition root wires from `workspaces.Service`, as `CustomerEmail` is wired the other way, since `workspaces` imports `billing`.
- **Render:** Hobby's billing email is the account email and cannot be edited; Pro and higher set their own ([billing-email.md](../../../docs/render-artifacts/billing-email.md)).
- **Acceptance:**
  - [x] Stub-backed tests: a Checkout for a workspace with no Customer sends `customer_email` set to its billing email in effect (the owner's on Hobby, its own on Pro); a Customer `EnsureCustomer` creates carries the same.
  - [x] A workspace whose owner's email cannot be read still gets its Checkout, with Stripe asking for the email as today.
- **Estimate:** ~45m.
- **Done 2026-11-25:** `billing.StripeConfig.BillingEmail` is the seam, wired in `cmd/api` to `workspaces.BillingEmailsInEffect` (`BillingEmail`'s address read without an authorization check from the store and the identity provider alone, since the usage emitter starts before `NewServer` and has no caller; a function rather than a `Service` method, so it is no unauthenticated verb on the request-serving Service). Checkout for a workspace with no Customer sends it as `customer_email` (a failed or empty read opens Checkout without it); `EnsureCustomer` creates the Customer with it (a failed read creates nothing, so the emitter retries); and `fillCheckoutCustomer`, beside w6/m628's naming once the card binds, sets it on a Checkout-created Customer whose email differs, which covers an address typed while the owner's could not be read and a Pro address changed while Checkout was open. Tests: `TestCheckoutForAWorkspaceWithNoCustomerSendsItsBillingEmail`, `TestCheckoutForAnExistingCustomerSendsNoEmail`, `TestEnsureCustomerCreatesTheCustomerWithItsBillingEmail`, `TestCompletedCheckoutGivesItsCustomerTheBillingEmail` (stub-backed, `internal/billing`), `TestBillingEmailsInEffectAreTheAddressesBillingEmailReads` (`internal/workspaces`), and `TestNewCustomersTakeTheBillingEmailE2E` (real Postgres, composed as `cmd/api` composes it). No live Stripe write was needed or made.
