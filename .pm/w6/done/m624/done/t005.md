## Objective

Measure, as [w6/170](../../../done/170.md) did, whether a disk service that probes by exec is now down no longer than a named-port twin on the same node. Then record the result.

## Steps

1. As the QA user (`bash scripts/qa-login.sh`) in `bex-canary`, create a disk-bearing Starter image service (disposable) with no port and no `PORT`, as w6/170's fixture was.
2. Run three configurations of it on its node: A (no `PORT`), H (`PORT=80` and Health Check Path `/`) and T (`PORT=80` with no path, the twin). Give each a warm-up and at least three code deploys and two restarts.
3. Use the same method as w6/170: a fresh-TLS `GET /` every 250 ms, a stamped watch of the App's pods, and the node's syslog.
   - Read "node → running" from systemd creating the pod's cgroup to the app's `StartContainer` returning.
   - Read the window from the first failing probe to the first 200 after it.
4. Check that every A and H window is within 0.5 s of the twin's, and that the "exit → pull" phase is gone.
5. Record the table in this task. Then update ADR082 § D3 (the exec-probe exclusion), ADR004 and the ADR018 disk row.
6. Delete the fixture, revoke the run's key and log out.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The definition of done's window check holds, and the docs record it, or the fallback in the definition of done is recorded with its evidence.

**Against the definition of done.**

- **The installer and its relist are gone.** No A or H pod had an init container; each mounted the helper from the image volume. The "installer start", "installer run" and "exit → pull" phases no longer exist, and "node → running" took 1.88–2.01 s for A deploys and H runs, against 1.78–2.00 s for the twin. In w6/170 it was 1.67–2.64 s with the installer, bimodal with the relist, against 1.35–1.52 s.
- **The windows are within 0.5 s of the twin's, at the probe's 0.25 s resolution.** The twin was down 4.75–5.00 s (six of seven runs at 4.99–5.00 s). H runs took 5.25–5.26 s in six of seven, one 0.25 s probe step over, and H4 took 5.51 s. No-port code deploys took 5.50 s, two steps over. In probe counts that is 21–22 failed probes against the twin's 19–20, so no run was more than two steps (0.5 s) over; H4's extra 0.01 s is the jitter of the probes' start times. The spread across H runs fell from 1.25 s in w6/170 (4.25–5.50 s) to 0.26 s.
- **What the steps are.** The node phases match, so the difference comes after the app starts. The exec startup probe runs through the CRI and the helper's own check, where the twin dials a TCP port: one step. A no-port code deploy also waits for the operator to promote the port it found once the instance is Ready (`Detected HTTP on port 80; serving it`; 0.38–0.63 s in [w6/162](../../../done/162.md)): the second step. Neither is the relist this milestone set out to remove.
- **Docs.** ADR082 § D3 no longer excludes a service that probes by exec, and ADR004 (§ the helper from an image volume) and the ADR018 disk row record the windows.
