# Each plan's included outbound bandwidth is not applied, and a card-less workspace past it keeps running

Why: Render includes outbound bandwidth per workspace plan and spins a card-less workspace's services down once it is used up; bex prices every byte from the first and never stops, so a Hobby workspace pays for traffic Render includes (the same gap `w6/m308` closed for build minutes).

- **Render** ([outbound bandwidth](https://render.com/docs/outbound-bandwidth), read 2026-11-10): included per plan — Hobby 5 GB, Pro 25 GB, Scale 1 TB, Enterprise custom; overage $0.15/GB on the public internet ($0.03/GB private link). Past the allowance without a payment method, "Render spins down your workspace's services until the start of the next month."
- **bex today:** `egress_bytes` is metered and priced at $0.015/GiB from the first byte (ADR030 §2, 90% off); `docs/render-artifacts/pricing.md` notes free bandwidth per plan is not tracked; nothing stops a card-less workspace.
- **Shape (mirrors w6/m308):** a per-plan `bandwidth.includedGB` in `pricing.yaml` (Render's figures; Enterprise = Scale's until a contract says otherwise), `EstimateIncluding` covering `egress_bytes`, the Stripe emitter sending only the part above the allowance (the same frozen, row-ordered computation as `BuildSecondsBefore`), `includedBandwidth {used, included}` on the usage surfaces and the billing page card, and — past the allowance with no payment method — the services suspended until the month turns (the `freehours` suspension path, Render's `billing` suspender), with 80%/100% emails.
- **Open:** whether bex's 90%-off egress keeps the allowance at Render's GB figures in GiB, and whether private-link traffic exists in bex (it does not today). The parity-sufficient reading: the same GB numbers, applied to the existing public meter.
- **Source:** found while shipping `w6/m308` (2026-11-10).
