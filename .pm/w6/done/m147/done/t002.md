## Objective

A park must not silently swallow an unsettled rollout. Either defer auto-hibernate while the current generation's rollout is progressing/stalled (until it settles), or settle it at park time so `ConditionRollout` carries the stall diagnosis. Pick with Render evidence (does a free service spin down mid-deploy?) and record the decision.

## Decision (2026-10-03)

(a) defer the park. Implemented upstream as `rolloutAwaitingVerdict` (`app_controller.go`, bounded by the progress deadline plus `rolloutVerdictGrace`); it keeps a free service awake while the current release's template is rolling over a served release with no verdict. The wait is bounded by the 900 s progress deadline. Render evidence is indirect: a Render deploy passes its health check on a running instance before it goes live, and the 15-minute spin-down counts inbound traffic, so a deploy is never cut short by spin-down. (b) settle-at-park was rejected: at park time the pods are gone, so the diagnosis is whatever Ready last said, and the rollout would still restart on every wake.
