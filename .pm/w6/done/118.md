# ADR018 records that hosted bex requires a card for every workspace (ADR075 D7)

Why: AGENTS.md lets a Render divergence stand only when ADR018 records it with the user's sign-off; production runs `BEX_REQUIRE_PAYMENT_METHOD=all` by the user's decision (ADR075 D7, "hosted bex.co is a paid product"), but ADR018 records only the `1`-mode paid-intent gate, so the bigger divergence from Render's card-free Hobby is not on the ledger.

- **Render:** a Hobby workspace needs no payment method; past its included usage Render stops builds ("Build Minutes Exhausted"), spins down services past its bandwidth, and suspends Free web services past their hours ([build pipeline](https://render.com/docs/build-pipeline), [outbound bandwidth](https://render.com/docs/outbound-bandwidth), [free](https://render.com/docs/free)).
- **bex hosted:** every workspace binds a card at sign-up (ADR075 D7 and its 2026-08-29 revision, user decisions), so those card-less paths cannot occur on bex.co; they run for self-hosted installs with the gate in `1` mode, and are covered by unit and envtest tests (`buildminutes`, `bandwidth`, `freehours`, `build_minutes_exhausted_envtest_test.go`), not live checks.
