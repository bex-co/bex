## Findings + evidence (2026-08-21)

- **Three-surface 402 consistency:** architectural (one service verb → shared
  coded-error renderers); pinned by the payment_gate test family across
  apps/postgres/keyvalue/sandbox/agentsessions + billing_config. The official
  CLI receives the standard Render-shaped REST error with status 402.
- **Real drift found and FIXED here:** the dashboard's agent-session
  create/steer/resume mutations were NOT routed through the ADR046/w1/m62
  PAYMENT_REQUIRED interception dialog — under `all` mode a cardless dispatch
  would have surfaced as a raw error instead of inline Checkout, breaking the
  "interception, not a dead end" promise on the ADR048 centerpiece surface.
  `use-agent-session-mutations.ts` now wraps the three dispatch verbs in
  `paymentGate.run(...)` (the other verbs provision no compute and stay
  unwrapped), and the three call sites (new-session composer, steering
  composer, failure-callout retry) treat a closed payment dialog as a user
  choice, not an error. typecheck/lint/tests green.
- **No registration-session assumptions remain** in dashboard surfaces (swept
  during the D8 hybrid implementation; requireAuth handles the session-less
  edge with `next` preserved).
