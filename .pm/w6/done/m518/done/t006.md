## Objective

Ship t003 and t005, then show on production that across a deploy that moves an SNI pin, a held external client on each proxy keeps its connection past 30 s, and that the deploy's wait and Argo's health pass without waiting for the old pods.

## Context

- Production access: `bash scripts/fetch-app-kubeconfig.sh <path outside the repo>`. Probes go in `bex-canary`, through the edge LB: `<id>.kv.bex.co:6379` and `<id>.db.bex.co:5432`, with allowlists that admit the client. Streams close after 1 h idle and 24 h in total.
- w6/m470 t006's reconnecting probes (`conn=<n>` logs) are the pattern for a client that outlives node churn.
- `git log origin/main --grep "pin platform images"` lists the pins. Each write-back logs `kept` or `moved` per proxy, and the wait step's log is in the deploy run.

## Steps

1. Ship. On the shipping pin, record that each DaemonSet rolled once by surge, with the new bound and grace period.
2. Hold a Key Value client and a Postgres client that each send a command every few seconds, plus an idle client on each, across the next deploy that moves an SNI pin. Record when each closed against its old pod's `SIGTERM`, how long the deploy's wait step took against the new pods' Ready times, and the `bex-operator` Application's health throughout.
3. Delete the probes.
