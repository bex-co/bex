Reviewed; the diff reuses existing structure rather than adding parallel machinery. Two candidates considered and **dismissed with reason**:

- **Collapse `reconcileActivatorAlias` into `reconcileMaintenanceAlias`.** Rejected: they resolve different Service/namespace/port fields. `MaintenanceService` merely _defaults_ from `BEX_ACTIVATOR_SERVICE` in `cmd/manager`; the reconciler fields are independent (`maintenance_mode_test.go` already sets them to different values), so sharing one alias would silently route wake traffic to the maintenance responder whenever they diverge. The three thin wrappers over `reconcilePlatformAlias` are the established pattern and keep each admission tuple auditable on its own.
- **Fold `checkpointServiceSuspended` into `checkpointServicePhase`.** Rejected: returning a pair would touch every existing caller for one new use, and the current form states the coupling ("pin the reason to whatever phase was kept") more directly than a tuple would.

Applied: the new operator alias reuses `reconcilePlatformAlias` and `platformAliasName` verbatim rather than open-coding an ExternalName Service, so the length cap, labels, owner reference, and port defaulting stay in one place.
