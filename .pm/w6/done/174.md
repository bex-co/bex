# Native SSH sessions end at 4 h, though Render documents no maximum session length

Why: the ssh-gateway closes every native SSH session and Web Shell after `BEX_SSH_SESSION_TIMEOUT` (4 h), while [render.com/docs/ssh § Automatic session closure](https://render.com/docs/ssh) names only three reasons a session closes, and [ADR018](../../../docs/ADR018-render-parity.md) records no divergence.

- **Filed by** w6/164, 2026-11-23, from its "Seen in passing" line: "Check how Render behaves before choosing, and file that separately if it differs." Render's page lists only three reasons: the user ends the session, the service redeploys or restarts, or Render maintains its SSH infrastructure, with an hour's notice. No time limit is among them.
- **Where bex sets it.** `BEX_SSH_SESSION_TIMEOUT` defaults to `4h` (`lego/backend/cmd/ssh-gateway/main.go`, `lego/backend/AGENTS.md`), and production sets `4h` (`lego/operator/config/ssh/deployment.yaml`). [ADR035](../../../docs/ADR035-ssh.md) says "sessions [default] to four hours", and the Web Shell ends with the `timeout` reason. Every held probe of w6/147 and w6/153 ended at the cap with `END rc=255 after=14401s`.
- **What is not known.** Render's docs name no cap, but they do not say there is none. bex has no Render account to hold a session past 4 h. Before changing the cap, learn what Render does: hold a plain `ssh <srv>@ssh.<region>.render.com` session on a paid Render service for longer than 4 h, or get Render support's answer. Then record what you found here.
- **Keep the access bound.** A session without a cap is still checked by revalidation every tick: revocation, suspension and instance changes end it (ADR035 § A check that gets no answer). Removing the cap does not remove that bound.
- **Done when:** Render's behavior is recorded here. Then either bex matches it in native SSH and the Web Shell, with a test of the new bound and ADR035 amended, or ADR018 records the divergence with the user's sign-off.

## Outcome (2026-11-23, w6 loopx)

- **What Render does.** [render.com/docs/ssh § Automatic session closure](https://render.com/docs/ssh), read 2026-11-23, lists two cases in which Render closes a service's sessions itself: the service redeploys or restarts, or Render maintains the SSH infrastructure, with an hour's notice. Its advice for "long-running commands" is a one-off job, not a limit. No other Render page or community thread found names a session length. A session held past 4 h on Render was not measured, because bex still has no Render account (w10/002's gate). Under Render parity first, the documented list is the reference, as it is for the closure point of ADR035 § Sessions across a deploy.
- **bex now matches it.** A service shell, native SSH or the Web Shell, has no time limit. It ends with its client, its access (revalidation), its instance, or the gateway or edge drain hour.
  - `nativessh/server.go`: a service target's stream no longer runs under `SessionTimeout`. The timeout still bounds an agent-session connection (ADR054 D6, so the Completer's `SSHGraceTTL` holds) and a service connection that has not opened its shell yet.
  - `webshell/websocket.go`: the cap is gone. `timeout` is now only the drain bound's reason.
  - Agent attach and sandbox exec keep `BEX_SSH_SESSION_TIMEOUT` (4 h), and production still sets `4h`.
- **The new bound for a client that went away.** Native SSH sends `keepalive@openssh.com` every minute and closes a connection that has not answered for three minutes (sshd's `ClientAliveInterval 60` / `ClientAliveCountMax 3`). Without it, a vanished client would hold one of its owner's five session slots until the next gateway release. The Web Shell already had its 50 s ping and 60 s pong deadline.
- **Tests.**
  - `TestServiceShellOutlivesTheSessionTimeout`: a held `srv-` exec runs 10× past a 30 ms timeout while keepalives and revalidation tick, and ends when the client leaves.
  - `TestKeepaliveClosesAConnectionWhoseClientStoppedAnswering`: a client that never answers loses its shell, its audit row ends, and its single slot is reusable.
  - The agent-session cap cases of `TestGatewayCancelsExecOnTimeoutAndDisconnect` and `TestDrainingSessionStillEndsOnRevocationAndTheSessionCap`.
  - Reverting either fix fails its test. Removed: `TestWebSocketSessionTimeoutEndsTheShellNamed` and the opt-in live `TestGatewayRealKubernetesWebShellTimeout`, which tested the removed cap.
- **Docs.** ADR035 has a new § How long a session lasts, and its bounds, end-reason and drain lines are amended. `lego/backend/AGENTS.md`, `.env.example` and the gateway Deployment's comment say what the timeout still bounds. ADR018 needs no row, because bex no longer diverges.
