# Service pages judge permissions by the selected workspace, not the service's own

Why: a member who opens a service from another workspace (a direct link, search or a notification) sees its actions as denied even when they are allowed there, until they change the workspace switcher.

- **Evidence (dev-6, enforced OpenFGA, 2026-10-21):** member2 was a Contributor in `m146-shared2` while the browser's `bex.selectedWorkspaceId` pointed at another workspace.
  - On `/services/<id>` the deploy and restart items were `aria-disabled`.
  - Setting the cookie to the service's workspace and reloading enabled both.
  - Production on 2026-10-20 showed the same switcher mismatch: a bex-canary service page with "bex" selected.
- **Fix:** resolve the service's `ownerId` first, and request capabilities (`viewerCapabilities` and the resource-action projections) for that workspace, or switch the selection to it on entry. Add a test with a mismatched selection.
- **Safety:** this fails closed, so it is a correctness and UX bug, not a privilege leak.

**Done 2026-10-21.**
- **Query:** `server(id)` now selects `ownerId` (exposed as `useServer().ownerId`).
- **Hook:** `ServiceDetailLayout`, the shared chrome for `/services` and `/static`, calls the new `useAdoptResourceWorkspace`. It makes the owning workspace the selection if the caller is a member, at most once per resource, so a deliberate switcher change is never undone.
  - Capabilities, resource-action projections, breadcrumbs and the sidebar all follow that selection.
  - A non-member's selection never changes, so their page still fails closed.
- **Tests:** `use-adopt-resource-workspace.test.ts` (6 cases: mismatch, same, non-member, loading, no undo after a deliberate switch, next resource). Three full-shape `Server` fixtures gained `ownerId`.
- **Gates:** dashboard 5,493/5,493 tests and lint pass (one existing warning in `use-live-logs.ts`).
- **Not yet live-verified:** dev-6 is torn down; check on production after deploy by opening a bex-canary service with another workspace selected.
