## Result (2026-11-25)

Done. [render.com/docs/websocket](https://render.com/docs/websocket), read again 2026-11-25: "Render does not enforce a maximum duration for WebSocket connections". A connection closes when its instance is replaced, by a deploy or by platform maintenance, after a SIGTERM and a 30 s window (up to 300 s). Clients should reconnect with exponential backoff, and both sides should send keepalive pings. [ADR018's Services & lifecycle](../../../../../docs/ADR018/services-lifecycle.md) gains a WebSockets row (✅ on every column, scoring what every web service's host gets) beside Request duration, with t001's measurement. It notes that the load balancer's 1 h close of a flow that carries no data stays with w6/149, and that a pinged WebSocket is not affected. No drift found, so nothing filed.
