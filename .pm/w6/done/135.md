# 135 — Key Value stores with internal authentication stop at exactly their plan's connection limit

Why: since w6/m456 a store's tenant connections stop at Render's figure because `kv-internal-proxy` holds that budget. A store with `spec.internalAuth` (w5/m268) retires the proxy, so its tenants meet only the server's `--maxclients`: the plan's limit plus the platform reserve of 10, which they can fill and then lock the readiness probe, exporter and operator out.

- **Found by:** w6/m456 (2026-11-17), recorded in [ADR021 § 9](../../../docs/ADR021-keyvalue-management.md#9-connection-limits-w6m456-2026-11-17). No production store had `internalAuth` then (3 stores, all `starter`, all proxied).
- **Today:** `reconcileKeyValueInternalRoute` (`lego/operator/internal/controller/keyvalue_internal_proxy.go`) points the stable `<id>` Service straight at Valkey when `spec.internalAuth` is set and deletes the proxy. External TLS then also reaches Valkey directly. At its own limit, Valkey drops a TLS client before the handshake without an error (`acceptCommonHandler`; reproduced on the pinned Valkey 8 image).
- **Shape of a fix:** keep a counting front for these stores. Run the proxy's plain listener without AUTH injection (a passthrough budget) and route `<id>` through it. Its readiness check then has to work without the injected credential. Enabling internal auth must still drop the unauthenticated connections, as on Render ("Any existing connections that use your unauthenticated URL will break"), and the proxy rollout that switches modes does that.
- **Done when:** a free store with `internalAuth` accepts 50 authenticated connections (plain and TLS) and the 51st reads `ERR max number of clients reached`; the platform's clients still connect while a tenant is at the limit; enabling internal auth on a running store restarts no Valkey pod.
- Likely more than an hour across several tasks: promote it before working it.

Promoted to [m463](../blocked/m463/README.md) on 2026-11-17 by `/loopx w6`: the premise holds at `d0e19225e` (`reconcileKeyValueInternalRoute` still retires the proxy, `reconcileKeyValueService` selects the Valkey pods, and `keyValueBackupHost` dials `<id>` for an internal-auth store). The work is a passthrough mode in kv-internal-proxy, the operator keeping it in front of these stores, a real-cluster proof, the ADRs, the production check and the closing tasks (9 tasks).
