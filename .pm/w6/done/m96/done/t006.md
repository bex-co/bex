## Outcome (2026-08-26)

Review pass against the milestone's Definition of done, bullet by bullet. Two gaps found while reviewing and closed:

- **The 403-vs-404 pair really are two different scenarios.** They use different ids AND different caller-workspaces: another workspace's blueprint requested as your own workspace → 404 from the tenant-scoped store read; the same blueprint requested *as* a workspace you are not a member of → 403 from `Authorize`, before any store read. Not one fixture asserted twice under two names.
- **"Not 400 any more" is nowhere the assertion.** Every case asserts its exact expected status, and the success cases assert the response body or the store side effect too, so a 200 with the wrong body or a 204 that never reached the store fails. The garbage-id case additionally asserts the `invalid path parameter` **reason**, because those requests also carry `ownerId` — a bare 400 check there would have passed on the unrelated query rejection and quietly stopped testing the path pattern. That was a real weakness in the first draft, caught here.

DoD coverage: 200 with real body (`GET by id`); PATCH/DELETE/syncs clearing the validator (three subtests, two asserting the store side effect); nonexistent → 404; other-workspace → 404; non-member → 403; through the **actual composed server** (`newRenderRequestValidator` over the real `apps.Service` routes); the five-kind pattern table (`TestEveryConstrainedIDPathParameterAcceptsItsBexID`, completeness-checked both directions); GraphQL/MCP reconfirmed with `TestBlueprintByIDNotFoundIsIdenticalAcrossSurfaces` including a success control.

No padding added — every assertion here corresponds to a DoD bullet or to a way one of the three fixes could silently regress.
