# A datastore proxy roll that does change the proxy's code still closes every connection at once

Why: after w6/m469 a store's `kv-internal-proxy` rolls only when its binary, env or resources change, but each such roll still closes all of its clients the instant the old pod gets `SIGTERM`; draining would let short commands finish and idle pool connections close on the client's own schedule.

- **Filed by** w6/m469 t004 (2026-11-18), as w6/140's "drain on `SIGTERM`" direction.
- **Today.** `kvinternalauth.Serve` cancels every handler and closes every active connection as soon as its context is cancelled (`lego/operator/internal/kvinternalauth/proxy.go`), and `cmd/kv-internal-proxy/main.go` cancels it on `SIGTERM`. The proxy Deployment uses the default 30 s grace period. The SNI proxies (w6/141) close on `SIGTERM` the same way.
- **Idea:** on `SIGTERM`, stop accepting, keep relaying the open streams until each client closes or a grace period ends, and set `terminationGracePeriodSeconds` to match. The new pod is Ready first (`maxSurge: 1`, `maxUnavailable: 0`), and the Service sends new clients only to it.
- **Limits:**
  - A client that holds its connection (pub/sub, blocking commands, a pool) still meets the close at the end of any grace period. Draining shortens the outage for short-lived connections only.
  - Enabling internal authentication must keep closing the old pod's unauthenticated connections at once (w6/m463, ADR021 § 9). A drain must skip that roll, for example by the env change that causes it.
  - Changing the proxy's code rolls every store's proxy in one deploy, so a long grace period delays the whole operator rollout.
- **Render:** Render restarts a store for a customer change and does not document draining. Weigh this against [Key Value](https://render.com/docs/key-value) before building.
- **Done when:** on the local cluster, a proxy roll caused by a binary change lets a client mid-command get its reply, and an idle client is closed no earlier than the grace period. Enabling internal authentication still closes a held unauthenticated client within seconds (the w6/m463 t003 check).

Promoted to [m512](../m512/README.md) on 2026-11-19 by `/loopx w6`. The premise holds at `001351697`. `kvinternalauth.Serve` closes every stream when its context is cancelled, `cmd/kv-internal-proxy` cancels it on `SIGTERM`, and the proxy Deployment sets no grace period. Weighed against Render: Render breaks a store's connections for the customer's own changes, and for its own infrastructure only in announced, reschedulable maintenance at most about once every three months ([platform maintenance](https://render.com/docs/platform-maintenance), read 2026-11-19). bex rolls every store's proxy without notice whenever the proxy's code changes. A drain narrows that gap for short-lived traffic. The work is more than an hour across several tasks (10 in all):

- the drain in `Serve`, and on `SIGTERM`
- the Deployment's bound and a guard for Valkey's reserve
- an operator-side cut for the rolls that must close at once, because the old pod cannot learn why it was stopped
- a local proof, ADR021 with the SNI-proxy follow-up, and a production check
- the closing tasks

It ships only after w6/m469 t005's held-client deploy.
