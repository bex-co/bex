## Context

## Steps

1. After the deploy: create a `basic-1gb` probe database with pooling; from a probe service open 1,000 concurrent clients on the internal pooled URL; confirm `SHOW POOLS` holds at most 90 server connections and `SHOW CONFIG` reads Render's five settings.
2. ADR009 § Connection pooling records the settings; ADR018's pooler row and its "Datastore connection limits by plan" row record the evidence.
3. Delete the probes.

## Progress — 2026-11-17 (production, pin `e36296ae8`)

Probe `dpg-dbucp4g8vt4s73a1ougg` (`basic_1gb`, PostgreSQL 18, pooled, replica `r1`, public with this host allowlisted) in `bex-canary`, created as the QA user. The load ran from a pod in the workspace's namespace (`m458-pgbench`, the cluster's own `ghcr.io/cloudnative-pg/postgresql:18` image), which the namespace's `allow-same-namespace` policy treats as any service of the workspace.

- **`SHOW CONFIG`** on both PgBouncers (`-pooler` and `-pooler-ro`, PgBouncer 1.25.2): `max_client_conn` 30000, `default_pool_size` 90, `max_db_connections` 90, `client_idle_timeout` 86400, `server_login_retry` 2, `pool_mode` transaction. ✅
- **Replica through its pooled URL.** Through `<id>-pooler-ro:6432`: `inet_server_addr()` the replica Pod's address, `pg_is_in_recovery()` true, the 100,000 `pgbench_accounts` rows and a row written a moment earlier through `<id>-pooler:6432`; an `INSERT` read `cannot execute INSERT in a read-only transaction`. Externally, `-pool` and `-pool-ro` connected with `verify-full`. ✅
- **1,000 clients on the internal pooled URL.** ❌ `pgbench -c 1000 -j 10 -T 60` with a 0.2 s query: all 1,000 connected, but `SHOW POOLS` held 10 server connections with 990 waiting, 63 transactions per second, and PgBouncer logged `accept() failed: Too many open files` 11 times. PgBouncer ran at the container's open-file soft limit of 1,024. Fixed by [t010](t010.md); a production Pooler with the fix held 87 server connections for the same 1,000 clients.
- Probe, test Poolers and load pod deleted (see the README).

**Remaining.** After the deploy carrying t010's commit: a new `basic-1gb` pooled probe; its PgBouncer logs `kernel file descriptor limit: 524288`; 1,000 clients on the internal pooled URL with `SHOW POOLS` at most 90 server connections and none failing; delete the probe by ID and confirm `GET` answers 404. ADR009's production-check bullet and ADR018's row then drop "pending".

## Re-run — 2026-11-18 (production, pin `359aee37d`, which carries t010's `89d8ed7fd`)

Probe `dpg-dbuh1mle7qgs73b34710` (`basic_1gb`, PostgreSQL 18, pooled) in `bex-canary`, created as the QA user. The load ran from a pod in the workspace's namespace (`m458-pgbench-rerun`, the cluster's `ghcr.io/cloudnative-pg/postgresql:18` image, credentials from the database's Secret by reference, never printed).

- **The fixed command runs.** The operator's Pooler `<id>-pooler` and its Pod carry `pgbouncerRunCommand` as the `pgbouncer` container's command, and PgBouncer logged `kernel file descriptor limit: 524288 (hard: 524288); max_client_conn: 30000, max expected fd use: 30012`. ✅
- **`SHOW CONFIG`** (PgBouncer 1.25.2): `max_client_conn` 30000, `default_pool_size` 90, `max_db_connections` 90, `client_idle_timeout` 86400, `server_login_retry` 2, `pool_mode` transaction. ✅
- **1,000 clients on the internal pooled URL** (`<id>-pooler.<ns>.svc:6432`). ✅ `pgbench -n -c 1000 -j 10 -T 60` with `SELECT pg_sleep(0.2)`: all 1,000 connected in 5.1 s. Eight `SHOW POOLS` samples during the run read `sv_active` 90 with `cl_waiting` 907–910, plus 5 `cnpg_pooler_pgbouncer` lookup connections on `postgres`. 24,858 transactions, 434 per second (the pool's ceiling at 0.2 s is 450), 0 failed. PgBouncer logged no `Too many open files` and did not restart.
- **Cleanup.** The load pod was deleted (`kubectl get` `NotFound`), the probe deleted by ID (`DELETE` 204 at 02:49Z, `GET` 404), and its Database, Pooler, Pod and volume claim were gone by 02:52Z. Production runs no Pooler.
- ADR009's production-check bullet and ADR018's two rows no longer read "pending".
