# A workspace's third and fourth builds wait about two minutes for a build node

Why: Render starts the builds of a Blueprint's services together; on bex the first two take the warm build slots at once, and the next two wait for an elastic build node to scale from zero.

- **Measured (2026-11-11, production, `examples/build-probe`):** four seven-minute builds in one workspace. Builds 1 and 2 started at once; builds 3 and 4 started 1m41s and 2m00s later, while 1 and 2 still ran. Admission held nothing (`w6/m306`, closed on this record).
- **Why it waits:** ADR060 §D8. `bex-tenant-lg` (cx43, min 1 / max 1) is the only warm build node, with two 7 Gi build slots. `bex-tenant-burst` (cx33, min 0 / max 2) adds one slot per node, from zero, for builds 3 and 4.
- **Options:**
  - **A — burst min 1:** three warm slots; a third build starts at once, a fourth still waits. One cx33 always on.
  - **B — burst min 2 (recommended):** four warm slots, the same as `BEX_MAX_ACTIVE_BUILDS`, so a four-service Blueprint builds together as on Render. Two cx33 always on.
  - **C — leave it:** builds past the second wait about two minutes on an idle plane. No added cost.
- **Change for A or B:** the `bex-tenant-burst` MachineDeployment's autoscaler min-size annotation in `infra/clusterapi` (check the autoscaler's `--enforce-node-group-min-size` behavior, or set replicas with it), then re-run `examples/build-probe` and record the four `startedAt`.
- **Source:** `w6/m306` t006 production record, 2026-11-11.

## Gate released — 2026-11-11 (user chose B)

- **Gate (user, cost):** choose A, B or C. Each warm cx33 is a standing monthly cost (check Hetzner's current cx33 price). **Release condition:** a dated choice in this note.
