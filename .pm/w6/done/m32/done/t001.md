## Evidence (2026-07-15)

- Render's endpoint-specific [OpenAPI](https://api-docs.render.com/openapi/render-public-api-1.json) lists `name`, `createdBefore`, `createdAfter`, `updatedBefore`, `updatedAfter`, `ownerId`, `environmentId`, `cursor`, and `limit` (`1..100`, default `20`). Its 200 schema currently says bare `[]envGroupMeta`.
- That response schema is stale: Render's official [pagination contract](https://api-docs.render.com/reference/pagination) requires every list item to pair a sibling `cursor` with its resource, and a public generated-client integration documents that the live endpoint returns `[{"cursor":"...","envGroup":{...}}]` despite the code-generated `[]EnvGroupMeta` type.
- m32 follows the live wire behavior and the repository's established list convention: `{envGroup, cursor}` on REST; GraphQL/MCP retain their native result shapes and accept `cursor`/`limit`. The remaining documented filters are recorded as `w6/018` rather than silently accepted.
