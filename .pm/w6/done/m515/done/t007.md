## Result (2026-11-19, on the shipped diff, before the ship)

`/simplify` ran over the exact diff that shipped as `495d04b86`, before the commit. Doing it after the ship would have rolled both DaemonSets a second time for any code change. It found nothing to apply:

- **Reuse.** `sniproxy.Serve`'s drain repeats the shape of `kvinternalauth.Serve`'s (a drain goroutine, `AfterFunc` closing the tracked connections, a handler `WaitGroup`), and `sniproxy.DrainSeconds` repeats `cmd/kv-internal-proxy`'s `drainSeconds`. They stay apart on purpose. `/kv-internal-proxy` links only the standard library and `kvinternalauth` (`go list -deps`), and its bytes decide its image hold (ADR021 § 10). A shared package would put `sniproxy`'s Prometheus and logr imports, or a new package, into it. `kvinternalauth.Serve` also refuses over-budget clients with a reply, which the SNI loop does not.
- **Quality.** No redundant state or parameter sprawl: `Drain{Start, Timeout}` replaces the old "caller closes the listener" contract, and both mains lost their own listener-close goroutine branch. The two DaemonSets' comments are duplicated because they are two manifests.
- **Efficiency.** One mutex-guarded map entry per accepted connection, as in `kvinternalauth`, and no work on the relay path.
- `make test`, `make lint`, `go test -race` on the three packages and `bash scripts/gitops-validate.sh` passed on that tree (README § Unblock record).
