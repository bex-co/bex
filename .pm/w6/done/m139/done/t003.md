## Objective

Make the data boundary invalidate affected mounted UI, streams, requests, drafts, and history inside the same workspace.

## Context

- DataBoundary currently aborts leases and clears Apollo through registered handlers; a cache clear by itself does not prove component-local state or history was removed.
- Status/resource detail reads need confirmed can_view as well as the existing restricted-section gates.
- m140 will consume this generation/reset contract for its persistent inbox and asynchronous notification callbacks.

## Steps

1. Expose and propagate an access generation through the existing boundary and authenticated route tree.
2. Gate mounting/recovery of protected queries and streams, including ordinary supervision reads, and clear affected drafts and confirmations on boundary changes.
3. Reset inaccessible navigation entries on both platform navigators; ensure late responses cannot publish across a newer generation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A Contributor → Viewer transition while logs/session content is mounted stops associated requests/streams and clears affected UI on detection.
- [x] Back navigation cannot reopen restricted cached content, and late responses from an older generation do not render.
- [x] can_view checking/denied/unavailable does not mount protected supervision reads.
- [x] The reset contract is usable by m140 without duplicating identity/workspace tracking.

## Out of scope

- Notification persistence/reconciliation, implemented in m140.
- Changing server resource ownership or granting access from client state.
