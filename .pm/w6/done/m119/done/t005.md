## Objective

Confirm the unchanged field contract remains consistent across REST, GraphQL, MCP and the dashboard, and record where bex deliberately differs from Render.

## Context

- Render spins free **web** services down and wakes them on request. Render has no private-service auto-sleep, so bex's `private_service` sleeping is a bex extension — whatever `t001` decided is a bex product call, and ADR018 should say so rather than leave it unstated.
- Resume remains Render-shaped and unchanged: it is the idempotent inverse of explicit suspension, not a generic replica override.

## Steps

1. Confirm the idle-timeout field remains accepted/stored on all three API surfaces; operator enforcement, not adapter rejection, owns eligibility.
2. Record the private-service auto-sleep decision in `docs/ADR018-render-parity.md`.
3. Re-run this milestone's paired probe — free private service and free worker, both at `idleTTLSeconds: 60` — and paste both outcomes during deployed closeout.
