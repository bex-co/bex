## Objective

Once production's pin carries t001, repeat w6/157's measurement and, if every grow lands within 15 s, remove the growth gap from ADR018 and ADR082.

## Context

- w6/157's method: a Starter background worker in `bex-canary` (`docker.io/library/busybox:1.37`, `dockerCommand: sleep 86400`) with a 10 GB disk at `/data`; `df -k /data` in the pod every second through `kubectl exec`; the claim, PV, App conditions and pod UID polled alongside; time from sending `PATCH /v1/disks/{id}`.
- `bash scripts/fetch-app-kubeconfig.sh <path outside the repo>`, `bash scripts/qa-login.sh` (REST takes the session cookie).

## Steps

1. Check the pin carries t001's commit and the operator runs its digest.
2. Create the fixture, then grow 10→11, 11→12 and 12→13 GB, waiting for `DiskReady` between grows. Record each time, the pod UID and restarts, and the time from the claim's capacity to `DiskReady`.
3. Delete the fixture by id; check the service and disk answer 404, the PV is gone and the Hetzner volume answers 404.
4. If all three land within 15 s, rewrite ADR018's Persistent disks row and ADR082's resize row and § D4 to record online growth within seconds with `DiskResizePending` as the fallback. Otherwise record the times here and park the milestone with what is left.
