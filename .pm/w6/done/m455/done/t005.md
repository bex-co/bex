## Objective

After the deploy carrying t004, repeat w6/m333 t005's probe and read its hour.

## Context

- m333's probe: free image service `qa-m333-egress` in `bex-canary` served five 2 MB downloads (10,000,000 bytes) and was deleted; its hour billed 4,571,645.

## Steps

1. Wait for the production deploy carrying t004 (`f0fe4be41`): a successful `deploy.yml` run whose `deploy` job ran on a head that contains it (`git merge-base --is-ancestor f0fe4be41 <head>`).
2. Sign in as the QA user (`bash scripts/qa-login.sh <state>`, then `curl -b <jar>`). Create a free image service in `bex-canary` (`tea-daif693dqjvc73e7as3g`): `POST /v1/services` with `{"ownerId":…,"type":"web_service","name":"qa-<date>-m455-egress","image":{"ownerId":…,"imagePath":"traefik/whoami:v1.11.0"},"plan":"free","port":80}`. Wait for the deploy to be `live` and for `HEAD /` to answer 200, which carries no body. Then download `/data?size=2000000` five times and delete the service.
3. bex-api meters an hour on its hourly tick, counted from its start, so the row can appear up to an hour after the hour closes. Read the service's `egress_bytes` on `GET /v1/usage?ownerId=…`.
