## Objective

Establish, for the Traefik version production runs, what happens to `traefik_router_responses_bytes_total` when a router is removed and later recreated under the same name. The new-series rule is only safe if a series that disappears never comes back carrying its old cumulative value.

## Context

- Production runs `traefik:v3.7.17` (`deploy/gitops/base/values/traefik.values.yaml`), two replicas, scraped every 15 s as job `traefik` with labels `instance`, `job`, `pod`.
- Traefik's Prometheus registry (`pkg/metrics/prometheus.go`) records removed routers on a configuration update and deletes their series on a later collect.
- The /unblock record: if Traefik can bring a series back with its old value and that cannot be told apart, stop and do not switch billing.

## Steps

1. Read `pkg/metrics/prometheus.go` at `v3.7.17`: when a removed router's series is deleted, and whether a recreated one starts from zero.
2. Run `traefik:v3.7.17` locally with the file provider and Prometheus metrics. Serve traffic through a router, remove the router, scrape, re-add it, scrape again, and record each value.
3. On production Prometheus, read-only: find router series that disappeared and came back inside the retention, and compare the value they came back with to the value they left with.
4. Record the result in this task.

## Done 2026-11-17

- **Source.** In v3.7.17 the registry lives in `pkg/observability/metrics/prometheus.go`. `SetDynamicConfig` records each router and service missing from a new configuration. `Collect` writes every series it holds first, then deletes the series of each recorded router or service that is still missing. The metrics middleware (`pkg/middlewares/metrics/metrics.go`) looks up its series with `With(…)` on every response, so after a deletion the next response creates the series again at zero.
- **Local run of the production image** (`traefik:v3.7.17@sha256:30ff6249…`, file provider). Three 100 kB responses read 300,000. Once the router was removed, the next scrape still read 300,000 and the one after had no series; a request then got 404. Re-added, the series stayed absent until traffic and read 1,000 after one 1,000-byte response. Removed and re-added between two scrapes, it stayed one series (1,000, then 1,500).
- **Production, read-only, over retention (2026-11-13 06:00Z to 2026-11-17 08:00Z).** Router series appeared or reappeared 3,942 times. 1,678 of those were series seen before:
  - 1,526 came back right after Prometheus restarted (2026-11-16 12:57:11Z and 15:13:04Z), with no scrape of Traefik in between. 1,179 of them came back at or above the value they had left with: Traefik had held them all along. One router carried 11.7 GB.
  - 152 came back after a successful scrape that did not carry them. 99 came back lower. The other 53 had left at no more than 4,644 bytes.
- **Conclusion.** A series missing from a successful scrape restarts from zero. A series missing because nothing was scraped can carry its old value. `up` tells the two apart, so the decision record's stop condition does not apply. The rule counts a series from zero only after a successful scrape of its target that did not carry it (t002).
