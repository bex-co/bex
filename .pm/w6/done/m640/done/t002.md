## Objective

Have the operator put one label on every pod that must not receive private network traffic: the pods of every App for which `AppSpec.ReceivesPrivateTraffic()` is false (background workers, cron jobs, free web and private services), and every cron run, pre-deploy Job and one-off job pod whatever its App. Follow how `app.bex.co/free-plan` is synced, and keep that label for the SMTP deny.

## Context

- `ReceivesPrivateTraffic` (`lego/types/v1alpha1/app_types.go`) already encodes the rule (an addressable type on a paid tier), but only build reach (`build_datastore_reach.go`) and Blueprint `fromService` checks (`blueprint_ownership.go`) use it.
- The free-plan label is set in `lego/operator/internal/controller/container_policy.go`, synced on templates by `syncTemplateLabel` (`app_controller.go`) and put on pre-deploy pods in `lego/operator/internal/predeploy/predeploy.go`.
- A label change on a running template rolls its pods. Say in the commit whether existing workers roll at once or on their next deploy (a worker roll overlaps for 60 s since w6/178).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Tests: the pods of a worker, a cron run, a pre-deploy Job and a free web service carry the label; a paid web or private service's pods do not; a move from free to Starter removes it on the next rollout.

**Done 2026-11-25:** `app.bex.co/private-network: send-only` (`execution.LabelPrivateNetwork`), set by `networkPolicyLabels` beside the free-plan label in `appPolicyLabels` (Deployment templates and cron runs) and `syncPolicyLabels` (a held release's served template), and unconditionally on pre-deploy pods (`predeploy.Job`). Tests: `private_network_label_test.go`, and the free-plan envtest now asserts the label drops on Free → Starter and returns on the way back; removing the label fails all four unit tests. Existing workers and free services roll once when the new operator reconciles them; paid web and private services do not roll.
