## Objective

When a `check_suite` fails on a session's head, read the conclusion of the same checks (by name) on the PR base branch's head commit.

## Context

- CI-failure steers are built in `internal/agentsessions/pr_feedback.go` (~509, "CI failed on your head commit"). Check runs come from `github.Client.SuiteCheckRuns` (`internal/github/feedback.go` ~141) under a `checks: read` token, the one `checksPass` already mints.
- On 2026-11-13, four of five routine CI turns were steered by `scripts (test)` failing on a ShellCheck warning in `scripts/ci-schedule-drift.test.sh`, which already failed on main.

## Steps

1. Add a client call: `GET /repos/{owner}/{repo}/commits/{base-sha}/check-runs?check_name=…`, bounded to the failing names.
2. A failed read leaves the steer as it is today.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given a base commit whose same-named check failed, the call reports it; otherwise not.
