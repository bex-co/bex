## Objective

Extend the operator's NetworkPolicy generation so an Environment with `networkIsolationEnabled=true` gets a policy denying traffic between its member Apps and everything outside the environment.

## Context

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go:1343-1420` (`reconcileNetworkPolicy`) is a label-scoped generator, keyed only on `labelWorkspace` (`app.bex.co/workspace`) today — no project/environment selector exists.
- `docs/ADR022-tenant-isolation.md` documents the label-scoped NetworkPolicy mechanism this extends.
- Depends on `w6/m19/t003`'s `core.LabelEnvironment` label existing on member App CRs.

## Steps

1. Read the Environment's `networkIsolationEnabled` flag (via a lightweight lookup or by having bex-api set a mirror annotation/label on the CR, e.g. `app.bex.co/environment-isolated: "true"`, at assignment time — avoid the operator needing direct Postgres access).
2. In `reconcileNetworkPolicy`, when the flag is set, generate/attach a policy scoped to `core.LabelEnvironment=<id>` denying ingress from Pods outside that selector (within the same workspace namespace boundary).
3. Ensure the policy composes with, not replaces, the existing workspace-level policy.

## Files

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/environments/service.go` (to write the isolation flag as a label/annotation)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With isolation enabled, a Pod in the environment cannot reach a Pod outside it (same workspace, different environment) and vice versa.
- [ ] With isolation disabled (default), behavior is unchanged from today.
- [ ] envtest coverage for both states.

## Out of scope

- Cross-workspace isolation (already covered by the existing workspace-level NetworkPolicy).
