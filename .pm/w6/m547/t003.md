## Objective

Under `Recreate` the first passing probe ends the downtime, so a disk-bearing App's startup probe runs every 1 s with the same 15-minute budget, and readiness follows within a second.

## Context

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/deployment_projection.go:101-151`: `healthCheckPeriodSeconds = 10`, `healthCheckStartupFailureThreshold = rolloutBudgetSeconds / healthCheckPeriodSeconds`, readiness period 5 s (`healthCheckReadinessPeriodSeconds`), liveness derived from the same period.

## Steps

1. Give disk-bearing Apps a startup period of 1 s and a threshold of `rolloutBudgetSeconds / 1`; readiness period 1 s with a failure threshold that keeps today's 15 s window.
2. Keep liveness unchanged.
3. Tests: the derived values for a disk App and an unchanged projection for every other App.
