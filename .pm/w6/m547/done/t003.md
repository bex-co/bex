## Objective

Under `Recreate` the first passing probe ends the downtime, so a disk-bearing App's startup probe runs every 1 s with the same 15-minute budget, and readiness follows within a second.

## Context

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/deployment_projection.go:101-151`: `healthCheckPeriodSeconds = 10`, `healthCheckStartupFailureThreshold = rolloutBudgetSeconds / healthCheckPeriodSeconds`, readiness period 5 s (`healthCheckReadinessPeriodSeconds`), liveness derived from the same period.

## Steps

1. Give disk-bearing Apps a startup period of 1 s and a threshold of `rolloutBudgetSeconds / 1`; readiness period 1 s with a failure threshold that keeps today's 15 s window.
2. Keep liveness unchanged.
3. Tests: the derived values for a disk App and an unchanged projection for every other App.

## Files

- lego/operator/internal/controller/deployment_projection.go and tests

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A disk App's probes are 1 s with the same total budgets; other Apps' are unchanged
- [ ] `make test` and `make lint` pass

## Out of scope

- Changing rolling-update Apps' cadence

## Record (2026-11-21)

Done for the startup probe. A disk-bearing App's startup probe runs every 1 s with a threshold of `rolloutBudgetSeconds / 1` (900), so the 15-minute budget is unchanged (`diskStartupPeriodSeconds`, `startupPeriodFor` in `deployment_projection.go`). Every other App keeps 10 s × 90. Liveness is unchanged.

**Readiness is unchanged by decision (t001).** The kubelet runs the readiness probe as soon as startup passes, and Ready followed startup by 0.2–0.6 s in all seven runs. A 1 s readiness period would add probe load and would not shorten the window. It keeps 5 s × 3, Render's 15 s removal.

The period is adopted lazily (`storedStartupPeriod`): a stored 10 s template keeps 10 s until its template changes for another reason. So the operator upgrade does not Recreate every disk service at once. Tests are in `disk_rollout_test.go`.
