## Objective

After t003 is live, every `rt-*` automation names `agent-4c-8g`, and their daily start times move from hourly (00:00–07:00 America/Los_Angeles) to every three hours, so that at most two overlap.

## Steps

1. `bex agent automations export` the eight (the CLI built from `lego/cli`; the released v0.2.1 has no `agent` commands), set `template.agentConfig.template` and the `at` times, and `apply`.
2. `bex agent automations list` shows the new schedule.
