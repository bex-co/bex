## Objective

## Run 1 — 2026-11-13, cold caches (evicted)

- `rt-unify-duplicates` on demand, session `ags-dbr5rtnoj40s73fkt6u0`, on the cpx42 with `agent-4c-8g` (limits 4 CPU / 8Gi, requests 1 / 2Gi, both 300 s tolerations).
- The turn started 00:47:30 UTC. The sandbox held its 4 CPUs for most of the run with 0.8 to 1.6 GiB of memory, and reached 4.1 GiB at 01:36. The node never passed 66% CPU or 48% memory.
- At 01:37:04, after 49.5 minutes, the kubelet evicted the pod: "Pod ephemeral local storage usage exceeds the total limit of containers 10Gi." The 10Gi is the tenant LimitRange's default; nothing set a larger limit.
- The session's failure reason read "the sandbox's agent was killed by signal 9 (exit 137) (ContainerStatusUnknown)": the gateway preferred the container's synthetic termination over the Pod's `Evicted`. t009 fixes both.
- The three 2026-11-12 runs that ended "sandbox terminated before completion" fit the same cause; their events had expired, so that is not confirmed.
