## Objective

Ship t001–t004. Show on production that the shipping deploy rolls no store's proxy, that a held client survives a later deploy while its proxy runs a binary other than the manager's, and that a customer change then rolls the proxy once.

## Context

- **Ship gate:** pin must carry `d6d29f9a7` (w6/m512's ship commit), and w6/m512 t007 must have recorded every store's roll onto `BEX_KV_DRAIN_SECONDS=30` and the 35 s grace. Otherwise park with that gate: the hold must not keep a pre-m512 template. Check with `git log origin/main --grep "pin platform images"`, then `git merge-base --is-ancestor d6d29f9a7 <producer>`.
- Production access: `bash scripts/fetch-app-kubeconfig.sh <path outside the repo>`.
- Probe precedent: w6/m469 t005 and w6/m463 t005, free stores in `bex-canary` with a client pod.
- A proxy that runs a binary other than the manager's comes from one of two places:
  - the first later deploy that changes `/kv-internal-proxy`;
  - patching the probe's own proxy Deployment container image to an earlier production `bex-operator` digest whose `/kv-internal-proxy` differs. Under the hold the operator keeps it; check this by reading the template back after two passes.

  Never patch a customer's store.

## Steps

1. Before shipping, record every Key Value proxy Deployment's ReplicaSet and pod age.
2. Ship through `/ship`.
3. After the deploy carrying the commit: every proxy Deployment carries both records, and no ReplicaSet or pod changed.
4. Create a free probe store in `bex-canary`, start a client pod that prints `CLIENT ID` every 10 s over one connection through `<id>:6379`, and make its proxy run a binary other than the manager's (see Context).
5. Across at least one later production deploy, the probe's ReplicaSet and the client's `CLIENT ID` do not change.
6. Enable internal authentication on the probe. The proxy rolls once onto the manager's image, the held unauthenticated client closes within seconds, and new ones read `-NOAUTH`.
7. Delete the probe store and the client pod.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The shipping deploy rolled no store's proxy and recorded every one.
- [ ] The probe's held client kept its `CLIENT ID` across a later deploy while its proxy ran a binary other than the manager's.

## Out of scope
