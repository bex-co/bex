## Objective

Production's operator projects the image volume. It is turned on only once t003's gates are on every node that runs an App, and production's pin carries t001.

## Steps

1. Check t003's metrics again. Check that the newest `chore(deploy): pin platform images to <sha>` on `origin/main` carries t001's commit.
2. Turn the setting on in production's operator overlay and ship it. Park with ``pin must carry `<sha>` `` until production runs it.
3. As the QA user in `bex-canary`, deploy a disposable service with a Health Check Path. Check that its new pod has the image volume and no init container, and that it goes live.
