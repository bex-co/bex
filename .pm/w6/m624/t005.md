## Objective

Measure, as [w6/170](../done/170.md) did, whether a disk service that probes by exec is now down no longer than a named-port twin on the same node. Then record the result.

## Steps

1. As the QA user (`bash scripts/qa-login.sh`) in `bex-canary`, create a disk-bearing Starter image service (disposable) with no port and no `PORT`, as w6/170's fixture was.
2. Run three configurations of it on its node: A (no `PORT`), H (`PORT=80` and Health Check Path `/`) and T (`PORT=80` with no path, the twin). Give each a warm-up and at least three code deploys and two restarts.
3. Use the same method as w6/170: a fresh-TLS `GET /` every 250 ms, a stamped watch of the App's pods, and the node's syslog.
   - Read "node → running" from systemd creating the pod's cgroup to the app's `StartContainer` returning.
   - Read the window from the first failing probe to the first 200 after it.
4. Check that every A and H window is within 0.5 s of the twin's, and that the "exit → pull" phase is gone.
5. Record the table in this task. Then update ADR082 § D3 (the exec-probe exclusion), ADR004 and the ADR018 disk row.
6. Delete the fixture, revoke the run's key and log out.
