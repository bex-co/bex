# A control-plane surge Hetzner refuses still waits for someone to delete its Machine

Why: w6/098 closed on the reading that CAPH hands a server-less control-plane Machine to KCP for delete-and-recreate once the MHC marks it unhealthy. On 2026-11-26 that did not happen, and the roll sat on the refused surge until it was deleted by hand.

- **Evidence (2026-11-26, the w6/m624 control-plane roll, `30588c6353`):** the second surge, Machine `bex-control-plane-dzcs5`, was created at 14:04:15Z and refused by Hetzner (`ServerCreateFailedIrrecoverableError`, `resource_unavailable` for cx33 in fsn1). The CP MachineHealthCheck `bex-control-plane-unhealthy-5m` marked it unhealthy at 14:12:16Z (`HealthCheckSucceeded=False/NodeStartupTimeout`, 8m), and CAPH created HCloudRemediation `bex-control-plane-dzcs5`. Its status set only `RemediationSkipped=True` (`IrrecoverableServerCreateFailure`: "Delete the Machine to trigger a new creation attempt"). The Machine carried `ExternallyRemediated=False/WaitingForRemediation` and no `OwnerRemediated` condition, and KCP's `Remediating` read `NotRemediating` ("not to be remediated by KubeadmControlPlane"). Four minutes later nothing had changed. Deleted by hand at 14:16:57Z, its replacement `jmj64` got a cx33 at 14:17:41Z.
- **So w6/098's premise holds again:** with CAPH v1.1.7 and CAPI v1.13.2, a refused control-plane surge stays until a person deletes it. `scripts/wait-capi-rollout.sh` retries only MachineSet-owned Machines (`replace_failed_server_creates`) and only warns for a control-plane one ("goes through KubeadmControlPlane remediation (w6/098)"), so app-cluster's wait step would sit until its 3600 s timeout.
- **Look at:** let `replace_failed_server_creates` also delete a KCP-owned Machine with no `providerID` and a `ServerCreateFailedIrrecoverableError`, after the same `SERVER_CREATE_RETRY_SECONDS`. Such a Machine never joined, so it holds no etcd member and no node; KCP's `kcp-cleanup` pre-terminate hook has nothing to remove. Check that KCP then surges again, and that the self-test covers the KCP case. Correct the comment and w6/098's record.
- **Done when:** a control-plane Machine that Hetzner refused a server for is replaced without a person, either by the wait script during app-cluster or by an in-cluster mechanism, with a fixture test for the KCP-owned case.
