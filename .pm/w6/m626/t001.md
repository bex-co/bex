## Objective

In Stripe test mode, drive a workspace to `enforced` with a card that fails at charge time, then bind a working card once through the Billing page's Update payment method (a bex Checkout) and once through the Stripe portal. Record the invoice's `next_payment_attempt`, whether the portal change also moves the Subscription's `default_payment_method`, and how long each path takes to reach `healthy`.

## Context

- `bindDefaultPaymentMethod` (`lego/backend/internal/billing/sessions.go`) makes the Checkout's card the default on both the Customer and the Subscription.
- Stripe retries on the Subscription's default when it has one; after a hard decline, "retries only execute after detecting a new payment method", still on the schedule (Smart Retries default: up to 8 attempts in 2 weeks; docs.stripe.com/billing/revenue-recovery/smart-retries).
- Lifecycle: ADR040 §7 (`healthy → grace → enforcing → enforced → recovering → healthy`); `BEX_STRIPE_GRACE_PERIOD` (168h default) and `BEX_STRIPE_RECONCILE_INTERVAL` (5m) in `lego/backend/cmd/api/config.go`; shorten the grace for the test.
- A portal card change reaches bex only as `customer.updated`, which the live endpoint does not send yet ([w2/070](../../w2/blocked/070.md)); in test mode, check whether the test endpoint sends it.
