## Objective

When a payment method is bound while the workspace's lifecycle is `grace`, `enforcing` or `enforced`, attempt each open cycle invoice once, off-session, with an idempotency key per invoice and payment method. A success flows through the existing `invoice.paid` handling into `recovering`; a failure leaves the state as it is and records the reason. If t001 shows a portal change leaves the Subscription's default on the failed card, also move the Subscription's default when bex learns of the change.

## Context

- `Invoices.Pay` is called today only in deletion settlement (`lego/backend/internal/billing/settle.go`) and agent-model charges (`agent_model_charge.go`): reuse their off-session pattern.
- Bind paths: the Checkout completion (`bindDefaultPaymentMethod`, `sessions.go`) and, once the live endpoint sends `customer.updated`, `RecordPortalPaymentMethodChange` (`portal_audit.go`).
- Lifecycle state and reducer: `lego/backend/internal/billing/lifecycle.go`, `worker.go`, `reconciler.go`.

## Steps

1. Add the attempt after a successful bind, guarded by the lifecycle state.
2. Make it idempotent per invoice and payment method, and never charge more than the open invoices.
3. Handle the portal path behind the same function, so it works once `customer.updated` arrives.

- [ ] Store-backed tests: a bind in `enforced` pays the open invoice once and the lifecycle moves to `recovering`; a second bind of the same card does not retry; a bind in `healthy` pays nothing; a declined attempt leaves the state unchanged with its reason.
