# A declined agent-model threshold charge stays open while its usage is billed again by the cycle (or settle) invoice

Why: a paid threshold charge is netted out of the next cycle invoice by a negative pending item (ADR091 D10.2a), but a declined one is not: its usage is still exported to the meter, so the cycle invoice, or a deletion's settle invoice, bills it too. A customer who later pays the open threshold invoice through its hosted link has then paid the same usage twice (netted only on the following cycle, or never after deletion).

- Fix: when bex issues a cycle or settle invoice for a workspace (or when its next threshold charge is made), void that workspace's open, unpaid agent-model charge invoices whose usage that invoice now bills, and settle them in `billing_trust` (`SettleBillingTrustInvoice`, which also lifts the balance due the cycle invoice now carries).
- Files: `lego/backend/internal/billing/agent_model_charge.go` (the ledger's failed rows with an invoice), `settle.go` (before `ChargeBalance`), a hook on the cycle invoice (`invoice.created` / `invoice.finalized` for the subscription).
- From w6/m362 t015 (2026-11-13); recorded in ADR091 D10.2a "Open". ~1h.
