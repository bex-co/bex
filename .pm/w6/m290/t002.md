## Objective

Make `DeleteEnvironment` match t001: every contained datastore and env group goes through its own existing delete (so backups, billing stop, protected-environment checks and events behave exactly as a direct delete would), instead of being detached.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/environments/service.go:947-996` (current delete, detach at :950-952)
- Existing per-kind delete services for postgres, keyvalue and envgroups in `lego/backend/internal/`
- Protected environments: only Admins may delete resources (render.com/docs/projects; w3/m281 is adjacent)

## Steps

1. Read t001's verdict; skip with a recorded reason if Render does not delete them.
2. Collect contained datastores and env groups; delete each via its service (not raw SQL), failing the whole operation with a clear error if any delete is refused.
3. Keep ordering safe: services first (as today), then env groups, then datastores.
4. Make the operation idempotent on retry after a partial failure.
