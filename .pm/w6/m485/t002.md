## Objective

The Deployment brings a new pod up before an old one drains and lets the old one finish its bound before `SIGKILL`.

## Context

- `lego/operator/config/activator/deployment.yaml` (`replicas: 2`, TCP readiness on 8888, no grace set), `config/activator/pdb.yaml` (`minAvailable: 1`).

## Steps

1. Set `terminationGracePeriodSeconds` above t001's window plus bound (about 150 s) and a `RollingUpdate` with `maxUnavailable: 0`, `maxSurge: 1`.
2. Update any `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` assertion on these fields.
