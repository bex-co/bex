# ADR018 and ADR082 still call disk growth a restart-bound divergence, though production grew a LUKS disk online

Why: an unsigned "deliberate divergence" stays in the parity ledger although bex's own production record contradicts it; Render says growth "causes no downtime, and the added storage is available within a few seconds".

- **Evidence:** `docs/ADR018-render-parity.md` Persistent disks row lists "a grow may finish on the next restart (the hcloud CSI driver does not claim online expansion)" among its deliberate divergences, and `docs/ADR082-persistent-disks.md:87` and § D4 say the same, with no user sign-off; the same ADR018 row records w2/m86's production check of "online 10→11Gi growth through the encrypted mapping, data intact" (2026-09-02). [Render — Persistent Disks](https://render.com/docs/disks): "Increasing size causes no downtime, and the added storage is available within a few seconds."
- **Do (~45m):** on production, grow a disk on a paid fixture in `bex-canary` from 10 to 11 GB through `PATCH /v1/disks/{id}`, with no restart, and time from the request to `df` inside the instance showing the new size (poll every second through Shell or SSH); note whether `DiskResizePending` appeared. Delete the fixture by id.
- **Then:** if the size arrives within a few seconds, remove the divergence from ADR018 and ADR082 (keep `DiskResizePending` as the documented fallback); if it does not, record the measured time and file a fix as a milestone.
- **Out of scope:** disks below the provider floor (w8/221, w8/blocked/m144).
