## Objective

Add Billing info: company name, address and a tax ID typed as one of Stripe's tax ID types, read with `can_view_billing` and written with `can_manage_billing` on GraphQL, REST and MCP, stored on the Stripe Customer; and a Billing info button on the Billing page that opens a dialog where the tax ID field appears once a country is chosen, as Render's form does.

## Context

- Stripe validates a tax ID by type; surface its refusal as a field error.
- Showing a tax ID does not collect tax: Stripe Tax stays off per ADR040 §6.
