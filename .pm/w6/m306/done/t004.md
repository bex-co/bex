## Objective

When `BEX_MAX_ACTIVE_BUILDS` is reached, whichever held App's 30 s requeue lands first takes the freed slot. Admit instead the held release whose workspace has the fewest active builds; ties go to the one held longest (t001's timestamp), then by name.

## Context

- `app_controller.go:1111-1129` admission gate; `app_controller.go:1512` `buildCaps`; `app_controller.go:1529` `shedBuildOvershoot` and `build.TrimOvershoot` (re-count and delete-newest stay)
- docs/ADR060 §D6 "Fair order when the ceiling is reached"

## Steps

1. At the gate, compute free slots (limit − active). Rank the held releases cluster-wide from the cache by (active builds in their workspace ascending, held-since ascending, name). Dispatch only if this App ranks within the free slots; otherwise stay `BuildQueued` with the existing message.
2. Keep the post-create re-count and shed.
3. Unit-test the ranking; envtest two workspaces against a small ceiling.
