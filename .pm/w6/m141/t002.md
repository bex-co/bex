## Objective

Replace duplicated service/deploy/cron presentation eligibility with the server's per-resource decisions.

## Context

- ServiceActionsCard infers eligibility from service phase, suspension, and deploy statuses; cron controls have related local state predicates.
- Workspace permission alone does not establish protected-resource, billing, run, or rollback prerequisites.

## Steps

1. Map server decisions into service lifecycle, deploy trigger/cancel/rollback, and cron run/cancel action options.
2. Remove only predicates replaced by authoritative action projections, preserving status display and safety mechanics still independently needed.
3. Retain exact target selection and refresh decisions after successful/ambiguous actions or eligibility changes.

- `mobile/src/features/services/service-actions-card.tsx`

- `mobile/src/features/cron/cron-runs-card.tsx`
- `mobile/src/features/cron/cron-action-controller.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Permission-denied actions are absent; temporarily blocked permitted actions retain a visible reason.
- [ ] Trigger/cancel/rollback and cron targets follow server eligibility rather than parallel client status sets.
- [ ] A changed target or precondition cannot silently reuse an earlier confirmation.

## Out of scope

- Changing service/deploy/cron lifecycle semantics or schedules.
- Adding a permission mapping from role labels.
