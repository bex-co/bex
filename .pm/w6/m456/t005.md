## Objective

The proxy in front of every store stops capping connections at 1,024 and never refuses before the server does, so at the limit the client reads Valkey's error on both listeners.

## Context

- `kvinternalauth.Config.MaxConnections` defaults to 1,024 (`lego/operator/internal/kvinternalauth/proxy.go:50`), `lego/operator/cmd/kv-internal-proxy/main.go` sets it on neither listener, and `Serve` sheds the excess by closing the connection.
- The plain listener and the TLS passthrough listener reach the same server, so one budget covers both.
- The proxy Deployment is built in `keyvalue_internal_proxy.go`; changing its env rolls the proxy pods, not the Valkey pod.

## Steps

1. The operator passes the store's limit to the proxy in an env var (e.g. `BEX_KV_MAX_CONNECTIONS`); the proxy shares one budget across both listeners and sets it above the server's tenant share, so `valkey-server` refuses first.
2. The env change rolls each store's proxy once on upgrade, dropping its open internal connections once; note it for the t006 rollout.
3. Test with a fake upstream: past the limit the client receives the upstream's error bytes through the proxy, not a close; a `pro` store's proxy admits more than 1,024.
