## Objective

Know before t006 switches which live stores would start refusing connections, so the rollout reports them instead of surprising them.

## Context

- `redis_connected_clients` comes from each store's redis_exporter, scraped by the `valkey-instances` job (`deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml`); it includes the platform's own clients (t001's list).
- Production kubeconfig: `bash scripts/fetch-app-kubeconfig.sh <path outside the repo>`.

## Steps

1. Query Prometheus for each store's `max_over_time(redis_connected_clients[30d])` (or as far back as retention holds) beside its plan.
2. Subtract the platform clients and compare with t001's limit.
3. Record in the README: the query, its time, and the store ids over their new limit with plan and peak. No tenant names or data.
