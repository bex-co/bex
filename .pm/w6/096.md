# SSH gateway exec runs at ~43 KB/s and drops a quiet session after ~2 minutes

Why: piping a database into a service over `ssh srv-…@ssh.bex.co` (the Render-documented way to move data in) is unusable for anything over a few MB, and a long INSERT that prints nothing is cut mid-write.

- **Throughput:** 20 MB of random data through `ssh srv-dbg4lfdn83uc738uug70@ssh.bex.co wc -c` took 461 s (~43 KB/s) on 2026-10-27. A 13.7 MB ClickHouse Native stream took 384 s.
- **Idle cut:** an `INSERT … SELECT FROM url()` that printed nothing ended at ~128 s with exit 255. ClickHouse cancelled the query mid-insert and left a partial table. Detached (`setsid nohup`) inserts finished normally.
- **Look at:** channel window and packet sizes in `internal/sshgateway` (a tiny flow-control window would give exactly this bandwidth), the k8s exec stream path, and `BEX_SSH_SESSION_TIMEOUT` versus idle handling for exec sessions with an open stdin.
- **Expected:** throughput bound by the link, and no idle cut while a command runs.
