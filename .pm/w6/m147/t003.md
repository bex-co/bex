## Trace (2026-10-03, from code; live sampling in t004)

The dashboard is not the cause. `deriveStatus` maps the raw phase, and `useServer` keeps polling every 3 s while the phase reads Deploying, so a stale cache corrects itself. The CR phase itself was moving, for two reasons:

1. **A parking pass wrote Deploying.** `reconcileKubernetes` (`lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go`, the `setPhase(PhaseDeploying, …)` after `applyServingDeployment`) stamped Deploying whenever `rolloutPending`, which is true for any phase other than Running, including Hibernated. `parkKubernetes` then wrote Hibernated in the same pass. That Hibernated write goes through `updateStatusIfChanged`, which compares against the **cached** App. The cache has not seen the Deploying write yet and still reads Hibernated, so the write is skipped and the CR stays Deploying. The next pass writes Hibernated, and so on. Every reconcile of a sleeping service flapped its phase, and the result could stick until the next event. **Fixed:** the Deploying stamp is skipped on parking passes (`!plan.parked(app)`).
2. **Waking onto the unsettled broken template.** The activator scales to 1, the progress clock re-arms, and the service shows a real Deploying for up to 15 min before parking again. **Fixed** by t002 (no mid-rollout park) and w1/m172 (served template restored after a failed rollout).

Test: `TestParkingPassNeverWritesDeploying` (`wake_over_failed_rollout_test.go`, an interceptor over status writes). It fails with the gate removed.
