## Objective

A park must not silently swallow an unsettled rollout. Either defer auto-hibernate while the current generation's rollout is progressing/stalled (until it settles), or settle it at park time so `ConditionRollout` carries the stall diagnosis. Pick with Render evidence (does a free service spin down mid-deploy?) and record the decision.
