## Objective

A new-policy service gets the t003 capability set, and `hostUsers: false` where t003 adopted it, in its Deployment, CronJob, one-off cron run and pre-deploy Job. Its exec sessions inherit both. Every other workload keeps a byte-identical template.

## Context

- `appSecCtx()` (`lego/operator/internal/controller/container_policy.go:46`) is the single policy point. Callers:
  - `deployment_projection.go:264`;
  - `cronPodSpec` (`app_controller.go:4738`);
  - `reconcilePreDeploy` (`:5816`).
- `appPolicyLabels()` (`:29`) stamps `app.bex.co/container-policy`, `app.bex.co/app` and `app.bex.co/app-uid`, which admission keys on.
- Pre-deploy pods already get `hostUsers: false` from `execution.HardenPod()` (`predeploy.go:183`). Runtime and cron pod specs do not.
- Datastores, backups and platform containers use `tenantSecCtx()` directly and must not change.

## Steps

1. Extend `appSecCtx` with the new policy's capability set and the t003 `allowPrivilegeEscalation` value. Leave the `strict-v1` and `image-v1` branches unchanged.
2. Set the pod-level `HostUsers` for the new policy on the Deployment and CronJob templates, and the one-off run, per t003's go paths. Do not set the field at all for legacy policies; an explicit `true` would change the template hash and roll every service.
3. `appPolicyLabels` labels the new policy too.
4. Where a disk-backed Recreate rollout or a disk snapshot/restore Job interacts with the policy, follow t002's findings.
