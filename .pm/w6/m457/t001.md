## Objective

Write down which of the external proxies' caps change for parity and which stay as process protection, before any code moves.

## Context

- Caps (`lego/operator/internal/sniproxy/limiter.go`, defaults in `lego/operator/cmd/pg-sni-proxy/main.go:73-81` and `lego/operator/cmd/kv-sni-proxy/main.go:59-67`): global 1,024 per proxy process, 128 per source, 32 handshakes per network, 256 per workspace, 64 per resource. `deploy/gitops` overrides none of them.
- They came with `91a24194f` (2026-08-16, "remediate repository security review", findings 6 and 19 in the limiter's comments) and are recorded in no ADR.
- External endpoints already require the datastore's IP allowlist and its credentials (`sniproxy.AllowLayers`).
- ADR008 § Render parity first: amend an older hardening choice that conflicts with parity; never keep the divergence because it came first.

## Steps

1. Per resource: the route's own limit (Postgres: w6/134's `max_connections`; Key Value: w6/m456's limit; pooler routes: PgBouncer's `max_client_conn`, w6/m458) plus a small margin, so the server answers the refusal.
2. Per workspace: the sum of its public routes' limits, or off; pick one and give the reason.
3. Per source, handshake and global: keep each only as process protection that no tenant within its plan can reach; size the per-source cap to at least the largest route limit or record why not, and check the global cap against the production proxies' replica count.
4. Record the rule in ADR009 (external access) and ADR021, citing `91a24194f` and the caps it superseded.
