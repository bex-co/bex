# ChangePlan should account for pending invites (fail-early UX)

Follow-up from `w6/m13` (2026-07-13). The plan-limit **invariant** is now safe: `store.AcceptInvitesForEmail` enforces the workspace's current plan at accept time (`planAllowsJoin`), so a Hobby workspace can no longer end up over its member cap or holding a role its plan forbids — a violating invite is left pending and self-heals on upgrade.

What's still rough is the UX on the *downgrade* side:

- `workspaces.Service.ChangePlan`'s guards (`service.go`, member-count + role guards) count rows in `tenant_members` only. A Pro→Hobby downgrade with outstanding invites therefore **succeeds silently**, and those invites then sit un-redeemable (the invitee logs in and simply doesn't join) until someone upgrades again.
- Better: count pending `tenant_invites` in the member-count guard and check invited roles in the role guard, so the downgrade is refused with a reason naming the invites ("workspace has 1 member + 2 pending invites, exceeds hobby plan's limit of 1; revoke them first"), consistent with how the other guards read.
- Needs `ListInvites` on the `WorkspaceStore` interface (it exists on `PGStore` already) + the test fakes.

Deliberately NOT done in m13: `workspaces/service.go` was held uncommitted by a concurrent session at the time, and the invariant was already closed at the accept-time enforcement point. Sub-hour once that tree is clear.
