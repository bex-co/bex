# An hour in which an SNI proxy pod starts or exits bills no external Key Value or Postgres egress for any store

Why: the public datastore egress meters judge the whole `bex-kv-proxy` or `bex-pg-proxy` fleet at once, so every SNI roll bills zero external egress for every public store in at least one hour, though since w6/151 a clean roll loses no byte from the counters.

- **Filed by** w6/151 (2026-11-20). Read from production Prometheus for pin `4e37ec03f`'s roll, whose new pods started at 08:50:58Z (Key Value) and 08:51:00Z (Postgres):
  - Hour ending 09:00Z: health 0 for both. The new pods started inside it (process-start term), and they had 35–36 of the 240 expected `up` samples (sample term).
  - Hour ending 10:00Z: Key Value 0, Postgres 1. `bex-kv-sni-proxy-5wkfv` exited with the streams its 3,600 s bound closed, and Prometheus scraped the dead pod once more, `up` 0 at 09:51:11Z. Its old pods' 204–205 samples passed the 192 the sample term asks for, only because they exited at minute 51.
  - ADR023 § The WebSocket and datastore sources had already measured that only 68 of 104 hours passed the process-start term while the proxies rolled with every image.
- **Mechanism.**
  - `egressquery.Health` (`lego/backend/internal/egressquery/query.go`) for the `Postgres` and `KeyValue` specs multiplies `min(min_over_time(up{job=…}[3600s]))`, `min(count_over_time(up{job=…}[3600s])) >= bool 192` and `max(max_over_time(…_process_start_time_seconds[3600s])) < bool (time() - 3600)` over every pod of the job, whichever pods carry the store's series.
  - `queryEgressSourcesEvidence` (`lego/backend/internal/usage/service.go`) skips an unhealthy source: the hour records 0 bytes for it as `UsageSourceDegraded`, and the cursor moves on.
  - A surge roll starts a new pod per node (process-start term fails that hour), and an old pod that exits before minute 48 of a later hour fails the sample term there.
- **What changed with w6/151.** A draining pod counts each write as it lands and exits just after a scrape that read every byte it counted ([ADR021 § 10](../../docs/ADR021-keyvalue-management.md#10-a-platform-deploy-rolls-no-stores-proxy-w6m469-w6m470-2026-11-18)), so a clean exit no longer leaves an `up` 0 scrape or an unscraped tail. The process-start and sample terms still treat a clean roll like a crash: they guard against a process-local counter that lost bytes since its last scrape.
- **Directions to weigh.**
  - Judge the SNI sources per pod, as `directHealth` does for the node meter with its `holders`, so a pod that started cleanly (its series new from zero, `NewSeriesFromZero`) or exited after its final scrape does not fail the hour for the other pods.
  - Keep rejecting what can hide bytes: a container restart inside one pod (same `instance`, a counter reset), and a pod that vanished without a final scrape (for example, export when the drain began and require a successful scrape after the pod's last stream).
  - Keep "undercount, never invent" (ADR023 § Complete outbound-bandwidth contract).
- **Done when:**
  - In Prometheus's engine (`TestBilledOnPrometheus`'s harness), an hour that contains a clean SNI roll bills each public store's bytes on the old pod until its exit plus the new pod's from zero, and an hour with a crash-restart is still skipped.
  - On production, the hours of the next SNI roll record healthy Key Value and Postgres egress observations.
  - ADR023 states the rule.
