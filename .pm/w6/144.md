# An SNI-proxy roll closes every external Key Value and Postgres connection at once

Why: since w6/m512 a `kv-internal-proxy` roll lets open commands finish, but `bex-kv-sni-proxy` and `bex-pg-sni-proxy`, which carry every external datastore connection, still close all of theirs the instant a pod gets `SIGTERM`, on every deploy that changes their code.

- **Filed by** w6/m512 t006 (2026-11-19).
- **Today.** `cmd/kv-sni-proxy` and `cmd/pg-sni-proxy` close their listener and every relayed stream on `SIGTERM`. w6/m470 keeps their image while the binary is byte-identical, but they link the CRD scheme, `k8s.io` and controller-runtime, so any `lego/types/v1alpha1` edit rolls them: at most 22 of the 56 production pins from 2026-11-12 to 2026-11-18 (ADR021 § 10).
- **Why the internal proxy's drain does not carry over.** Both are `hostPort` DaemonSets (`lego/operator/config/kv-sni-proxy/daemonset.yaml`, `config/pg-sni-proxy/daemonset.yaml`) with the default rolling update: `maxUnavailable: 1`, no surge. The replacement pod cannot bind the node's port while the old one still holds it. A draining old pod therefore keeps the node's edge port from serving new connections for the whole drain. The edge load balancer's health check and `externalTrafficPolicy: Local` steer new clients to other nodes, but a single-node pool has none.
- **Directions to weigh:** `SO_REUSEPORT` handover (the old pod closes its listener but keeps its streams while the new pod binds); `maxSurge` with `maxUnavailable: 0` once the listener tolerates two pods; or a drain only while another node serves the edge. Postgres connections through `pg-sni-proxy` carry the same weight (ADR009).
- **Render:** Render breaks a datastore's connections for its own infrastructure only in announced, reschedulable maintenance, "usually … no more than once every three months" ([platform maintenance](https://render.com/docs/platform-maintenance), read 2026-11-19).
- **Done when:** on the local cluster, a roll of each SNI DaemonSet caused by a binary change lets an external TLS client mid-command (a Key Value `BLPOP`, a Postgres `pg_sleep`) get its reply, new external clients keep connecting throughout the roll, and the held client is closed no earlier than the chosen bound.
