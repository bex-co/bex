## Objective

The credit is recorded before the first crossing, so every surface agrees with the mint. That happens wherever bex already reads the balance: the Billing page's credits block (`billing.StripeClient.creditsFor`) and the invoice webhooks that move the charge window.

## Context

- Today only the charger records it (`AgentModelCharger.refreshWindow`), at a crossing (`store.RecordAgentModelCredits`, migration 0253).
- The Billing page's read and the charger's must use the same scope (metered grants, less grants expiring by the period end).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After a Billing page view, the agent environment reports the credit without a charge having happened.
