## Objective

Neither SNI DaemonSet asks for a `hostPort`. Both roll with `maxSurge: 1` and `maxUnavailable: 0`, set the drain bound, and give the pod a `terminationGracePeriodSeconds` that covers it. Production's edge is unchanged: the LB keeps reaching the proxies through the fixed `Local` NodePorts.

## Context

- `lego/operator/config/kv-sni-proxy/daemonset.yaml` (`hostPort: 6379`) and `config/pg-sni-proxy/daemonset.yaml` (`hostPort: 5432`).
- Production: `config/prod/kv-sni-proxy-lb.yaml` (31892) and `pg-sni-proxy-lb.yaml` (31056), `externalTrafficPolicy: Local`, and the Terraform LB services in `infra/terraform/main.tf`. `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` checks the fixed NodePorts.
- The ClusterIP Service's comment in `config/pg-sni-proxy/service.yaml` still names the `hostPort` dev path and a `deploy/gitops/overlays/prod/pg-sni-proxy-lb.yaml` that is now `config/prod/pg-sni-proxy-lb.yaml`.
- The bound: the milestone's Design § The bound. 30 s and a 35 s grace period unless this task finds a longer bound worth it.
- Guards that may pin today's shape: `scripts/gitops-validate.sh`, `scripts/test_platform_metrics.py`, `scripts/edge-proxyprotocol-validate.sh`, `scripts/operator-pins.sh` (`wait`) and its `.test.sh`.

## Steps

1. Drop both `hostPort`s, and add `updateStrategy.rollingUpdate` with `maxSurge: 1` and `maxUnavailable: 0`, the drain env and `terminationGracePeriodSeconds`.
2. Weigh the bound: a platform node drain waits for the grace period, and two pods of up to 512 Mi each share a node during it. Record the choice and why in this task.
3. Fix the stale Service comment. Run the guards above and update any that assert the old shape, without weakening what they protect.
4. On the local cluster, check that both DaemonSets now schedule (PodSecurity `baseline` refused the `hostPort`), and that a template change surges: two pods on the node, the new one Ready before the old one is told to stop.
