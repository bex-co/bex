## Objective

The SNI proxies' shared accept loop gets a drain that is separate from its hard stop. When the drain starts, the listener closes, so the pod accepts nothing new. Every open stream keeps relaying in both directions until its client or the backend closes it, or until the drain's deadline. At the deadline the remaining streams close. The caller learns when the last handler has returned, so the process exits as soon as it is idle. A hard stop still closes everything at once.

## Context

- `lego/operator/internal/sniproxy/limiter.go`: `Serve` takes the listener, the limiter, the meter and a `handle(net.Conn)` that owns the connection. It returns when the listener closes and does not track open handlers.
- `lego/operator/internal/sniproxy/meter.go`: `CopyBidirectional` relays until either side ends, the idle timeout or the max lifetime. Closing the client connection ends it.
- Today the process exit is what closes every stream: `main` returns right after `Serve`.
- The handlers also cover connections still in their handshake (PROXY header, `SSLRequest`, ClientHello). Those have their own deadlines of 2 s and the handshake timeout, so they finish or fail well inside any bound.
- Precedent: `kvinternalauth.Serve`'s `Config.Drain` and `DrainTimeout` (w6/m512, `lego/operator/internal/kvinternalauth/proxy.go`).

## Steps

1. Track every connection `Serve` hands to `handle`, and the handlers in flight.
2. Add the drain to the API. For example, a drain channel and a deadline beside `ctx`, or a server value with `Drain(deadline)`. Pick whichever keeps both mains simple.
3. On drain: close the listener, keep the handlers running, and wait for them. At the deadline, close every remaining client connection, which ends its relay. Return once every handler is done, at once if the last one ends early.
4. Cancelling without a drain behaves as today.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A client that sent a request before the drain reads the backend's reply when the backend writes it after the drain began.
- [ ] A dial after the drain began is not accepted by the old listener.
- [ ] An idle client stays open until the deadline and is closed then.
- [ ] Every global, source, handshake and route slot is released after the drain.
- [ ] `Serve` returns as soon as the last stream ends.
- [ ] A hard stop without a drain still closes everything at once.
- [ ] `go test -race ./internal/sniproxy/...` passes.
