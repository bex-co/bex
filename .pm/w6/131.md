# The composer warns a credited workspace bex cannot charge on month-to-date, while the mint holds its tier ceiling against the unbilled spend

Why: a T0 or locked workspace whose billing credits cover its spend is told "New model requests stop at the cap" at $8 of month-to-date, and keeps running past $10. The banner then reads "at 100% of this month's cap" while sessions start normally.

**Found:** w6/m400 t004, 2026-11-16, on production. bex-canary (`tea-daif693dqjvc73e7as3g`) is the case. Its GraphQL `agentEnvironment` reads:

- T0, `agentModelSpendCapTierLimited: true`, cap $10;
- `agentModelCreditUsd` 1000000, `agentModelUnbilledUsd` 0, month-to-date $0;
- `agentModelChargesAtThreshold: false`, because the workspace is `billing_excluded`.

**Cause.**

- The mint refuses on `RefusesAtCap` (`lego/backend/internal/agentsession/spendcap.go`, `ownCapReached() || tierCeilingReached() && !ChargeDue()`). `tierCeilingReached` compares `UnbilledMicroUSD()`, which is the window spend less credits and paid charges, with the tier's ceiling. This holds whether or not bex can charge the workspace.
- `dashboard/src/features/agent-environment/lib/spend.ts` `spendPosition` uses the unbilled spend for its 80% `nearing` only when `chargePoint` is true, that is, tier-limited **and** `agentModelChargesAtThreshold`. A tier-limited workspace bex cannot charge falls back to month-to-date ÷ cap. Such a workspace is locked, excluded, comped or has no Subscription.
- At $8 of month-to-date the composer shows `agentSessions.spendWarningBody`, and the Settings → Agents meter reads at warning level. Neither the cap nor a charge is near.

**Fix.**

- Wherever the tier's ceiling binds (`agentModelSpendCapTierLimited`), `nearing` compares the unbilled spend with the cap.
- The workspace's own cap still binds on month-to-date. Decide whether `nearing` should also take the larger of the two ratios against the own cap (`override ?? plan`, clamped to max), or whether bex-api should return the nearing ratio itself so the dashboard does not re-derive the mint.
- The warning copy and the meter `fraction` follow the same quantity, so the banner's percent matches what the mint holds.

**Done when:**

- `spend.test.ts` has a case for a tier-limited, non-chargeable workspace with credit ≥ month-to-date, which sees no warning, and one with unbilled ≥ 80% of the cap, which does.
- A locked workspace with credits behaves the same way.
- The T1 own-cap cases are unchanged.
