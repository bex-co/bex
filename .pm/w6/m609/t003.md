## Objective

Bill `instance_seconds` and count the Free instance hours by the plan the service's instances run, not the plan saved for the next deploy. Render: "Your service's compute plan does not change until the first successful deploy after you update it" ([render.com/docs/compute-plans](https://render.com/docs/compute-plans)).

## Context

- The hourly meter writes `instance_seconds` at `store.App.Tier` (`lego/backend/internal/usage/service.go`, `processAppMeterWindowResult`). `SetPlan` writes that column at the save (`Store.SetAppTier`).
- The meter already resolves the App CR for the window (`resolveAppCR`), so a running-plan field on the CR's status reaches it with no new read.
- The Free instance hours enforcer exempts a paid `a.Spec.Tier` (`lego/backend/internal/freehours/freehours.go`), and `requireFreeHoursAvailable` (`lego/backend/internal/apps/free_hours.go`) does the same for a resume.
- `AppStatus` has no field that names the running plan (`lego/types/v1alpha1/app_types.go`). The operator knows it: the pass's view (`pendingReleaseView`, `heldReleaseView`) or the saved spec when no save waits.

## Steps

1. Add a status field naming the plan the serving instances run (for example `status.servedTier`), written by the operator from the spec the pass renders the Deployment with. Regenerate the CRD (`make manifests` from `lego/operator/`).
2. The meter bills `instance_seconds` at that tier, falling back to `store.App.Tier` when the CR or the field is missing (an App reconciled before this change, a suspended one).
3. The Free instance hours enforcer and `requireFreeHoursAvailable` treat a service as Free while it runs Free, whatever plan is saved.
4. Check the other readers of the saved tier that stand for what the service runs (the Billing page's per-service rows, the plan shown beside instance metrics) and move any that bill or limit by it.

## Files

- `lego/types/v1alpha1/app_types.go` and the generated CRD
- `lego/operator/internal/controller/` (the status write)
- `lego/backend/internal/usage/service.go`, `lego/backend/internal/freehours/freehours.go`, `lego/backend/internal/apps/free_hours.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A usage test: a service saved from Starter to Standard and not deployed meters its hour at `starter`; once its status names `standard`, the next hour meters at `standard`.
- [ ] A Free instance hours test: a Free service with a saved paid plan is still counted and suspended when the hours run out.
- [ ] `make test`, `cd lego/backend && go test ./...` and `make lint` pass.

## Out of scope

- Prorating an hour split by a deploy: the meter already bills a whole hour at one tier, before and after this change.
