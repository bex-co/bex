## Objective

Cover the saved plan where it meets the work already shipped around save-only writes.

## Steps

1. A REST plan PATCH made within seconds of a dashboard Settings deploy waits for that deploy's adoption (`rollout.AwaitAdoptionBeforeHold`, w6/163), and the plan stays pending behind it.
2. After an `imageUrl` deploy, a saved plan and the deploy that applies it keep serving the deploy's image (`keepSelectedImage`, w6/159).
3. A saved plan on a suspended service stays saved through Resume, and a Restart or rollback keeps it pending.
4. A dry-run plan PATCH still previews the saved plan and writes nothing.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each test fails with its behavior reverted.
- [ ] `cd lego/backend && go test ./...` and `make test` pass.
