## Objective

The credit is recorded before the first crossing, so every surface agrees with the mint. That happens wherever bex already reads the balance: the Billing page's credits block (`billing.StripeClient.creditsFor`) and the invoice webhooks that move the charge window.

## Context

- Today only the charger records it (`AgentModelCharger.refreshWindow`), at a crossing (`store.RecordAgentModelCredits`, migration 0253).
- The Billing page's read and the charger's must use the same scope (metered grants, less grants expiring by the period end).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After a Billing page view, the agent environment reports the credit without a charge having happened.

## Done 2026-11-14

`StripeClient.BillingFor` records the agent-model credit after its credits read (`recordAgentModelCredit`, through the optional `AgentModelCreditRecorder` on the state store, which `AgentModelChargeStore` now embeds).

- It uses the charger's scope, the metered balance less what grants expiring by the Subscription's period end hold. That is computed from the grants `creditsFor` already read, so no extra Stripe calls are made.
- It is stamped with the time taken before the balance read.
- A read that stopped at `maxCreditGrants` records nothing.
- Test: `TestBillingReadRecordsTheAgentModelCredit`.
- **Not taken.** Recording on the invoice webhooks: moving the boundary clears the credit, and the next Billing read or crossing records it again.
