## Objective

Know the settings a bex pooler runs before changing them, and whether the deployed CNPG accepts each of Render's.

## Context

- `poolerSpec` and `poolerTemplate` in `lego/operator/internal/controller/database_controller.go`; pooler TLS in `database_pooler_tls.go`; pooled port `appv1alpha1.PoolerPort` 6432.
- CNPG's Pooler `spec.pgbouncer.parameters` accepts an allowlist of PgBouncer settings.
- PgBouncer's own defaults are `max_client_conn` 100 and `default_pool_size` 20.

## Steps

1. On dev-6 (`bash scripts/dev-env.sh 6 up`), create a paid database with pooling and read its PgBouncer settings (`SHOW CONFIG` on the admin console, or the generated `pgbouncer.ini` in the Pooler pod).
2. Record each of Render's five settings (`max_client_conn`, `default_pool_size`, `max_db_connections`, `client_idle_timeout`, `server_login_retry`) against the running value, and check the deployed CNPG version's allowlist accepts each.
3. Record both in the README.
