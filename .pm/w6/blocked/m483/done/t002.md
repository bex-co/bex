## Objective

A service has one Compute page with the compute plan (current plan and Edit), Manual Scaling and Autoscaling, in Render's order; the Plan and Scaling routes redirect to it.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/routes/services.$serviceId.plan.tsx` and `services.$serviceId.scaling.tsx`; test `routes/__tests__/services.$serviceId.scaling.test.tsx`.
- Nav: `features/services/components/service-nav.tsx`, locale keys `services.navPlan` and `services.navScaling` in `features/services/locales/*.ts` (8 locales).
- Plan gating for autoscaling (w7/m429) and the paid-only rules stay as they are.

## Steps

1. Create `routes/services.$serviceId.compute.tsx` composing the existing plan and scaling sections.
2. Make `/plan` redirect to `/compute` and `/scaling` to `/compute#manual-scaling` (or Render's anchor from t001), keeping search params.
3. Replace the two nav entries with one "Compute" entry in all 8 locales.
4. Move the route tests to the new route and add redirect tests.
