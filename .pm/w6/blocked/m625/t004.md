## Objective

With a Stripe test-mode key, finalize one paid and one open invoice for a test workspace, download the PDF from the Billing page, pay the open invoice through Pay Now with a working test card, and confirm the row reads paid after a refresh. Read the same invoice on REST, GraphQL and MCP.

## Context

- A card that fails at charge time, such as Stripe's `4000000000000341`, leaves an invoice open.

Needed from the user: a Stripe **test-mode** restricted key (`rk_test_…`) on the bex account, with write access to Customers, Invoices, Invoice items and Payment methods. Put it in `.env` as `BEX_TEST_STRIPE_KEY` for `stripe_sandbox_test.go`, and as `BEX_STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` for a dev-6 bex-api. Then:
