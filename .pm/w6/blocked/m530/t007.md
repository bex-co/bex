## Objective

Show that a day after the reader took over, no conntrack table the sweeps used to fill is filled any more: neither the operator's node's nor any tenant node's.

## Context

- Cilium's logs are not in Loki. Read each agent's own log: `kubectl -n kube-system logs <cilium pod> -c cilium-agent --since-time=2026-11-20T22:46:00Z | grep 'Conntrack garbage collector interval recalculated'`, ending at 2026-11-21T22:46Z (t006 step 5); each line carries `deleteRatio` (deleted entries over the 131,072-entry TCP table). An agent restarted inside the day has a shorter log: note it and read what there is.
- The baseline, 6 h to 18:29Z 2026-11-20: `bex-platform-k9wb4-dpjxh` (the operator's node) 408 passes, 105 at or above 0.5; `bex-tenant-0-wldb9-hbkdk` 212 and 49; `bex-tenant-0-wldb9-59ksr` 181 and 31; `bex-platform-k9wb4-jfc55` none.
- Pressure: production's Prometheus holds no Cilium series (the agents serve no Prometheus endpoint: `cilium-config` has no `prometheus-serve-addr`, and adding one restarts every agent), so read it in each agent, `kubectl -n kube-system exec <cilium pod> -c cilium-agent -- cilium-dbg metrics list -p bpf_map_pressure` (`ct4_global`), plus a spot `cilium-dbg bpf ct list global | wc -l` on the operator's node and two tenant nodes; the GC lines above are the day's continuous record. The operator may have moved nodes: read the node it ran on through the day (`kube_pod_info`).
- The listener-read counter is not in Prometheus either (the `bex-operator` keep-list in `deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml` drops `bex_port_listener_reads_total`) and resets when the operator restarts. Read the day's fallbacks from the operator's own log in Loki (`{namespace="bex-system", container="manager"} |= "no listener reader on the pod"`, which names the node, and `|= "reading listeners on node"` for a failed read), and the counter by a direct scrape of each operator pod (`kubectl -n bex-system port-forward pod/<pod> 18080:8080`, `/metrics`).
- Baseline after the switch, from t006: at 22:52Z `ct4_global` read 0.020 on `dpjxh` (0.758 at 22:28Z), 0.003 on `hbkdk`, 0.002 on `59ksr`; no GC pass from 22:45Z to 22:52Z deleted more than 0.037. The next pin (no earlier than about 04:08Z on 2026-11-21) rolls the operator and the reader DaemonSet inside the day.
- A reader roll (the next pin's) runs an old and a new reader pod on a node at once. `NodeListenerReader.PodListeners` (`internal/controller/listener_reads.go`) takes the first Running and Ready reader pod on the node and does not skip one that is terminating, so a read in that window can fail (`result="failed"`, the last record stands, no sweep). Count those failures around the roll. If any occurred, file a note to prefer a reader pod with no `deletionTimestamp` (found by t008's review).
- Parked on ``date gate: 2026-11-21T22:46Z``, 24 h after t006's first read through a reader (2026-11-20T22:46:27Z).

## Steps

1. For the operator's node(s) and every node egress-meter runs on, count the day's GC passes and those with `deleteRatio` at or above 0.5.
2. Read the day's maximum `ct4_global` pressure per node.
3. Read the operator's listener-read counter over the day: every sweep is a node without a reader, explained here.
4. If a pass at or above 0.5 remains, find what filled the table (`cilium-dbg bpf ct list global`, grouped by source and destination) and record it; a cause outside the sweeps gets its own inbox note.
