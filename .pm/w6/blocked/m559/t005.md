## Objective

On a production pin that carries t002 and t003, show the definition of done: ten or more consecutive same-node deploys and restarts with no detach, a holder through each, nothing left behind, and a cordoned run that still moves.

## Context

- The harness and method are [m547 t005](../m547/done/t005.md)'s:
  - a fresh-TLS `GET /` every 250 ms through the public edge;
  - stamped watches of the App's pods, the namespace's events and the PV's `VolumeAttachment`s;
  - the node's kubelet syslog and the leader `kube-controller-manager` log.
- Production access:
  - `bash scripts/fetch-app-kubeconfig.sh <path outside the repo>`;
  - `bash scripts/qa-login.sh` for the QA user;
  - fixtures go in `bex-canary` and are deleted by id afterwards, with their disk, PV and Hetzner volume.
- `bex-tenant-0` nodes sync every 2 s (`/configz`); `bex-tenant-burst` and `bex-tenant-lg` were not rolled and still read 10 s. Run on a `bex-tenant-0` node.
- Hetzner's shared-core limit is 80 and about 68 were in use on 2026-11-21, so avoid anything that surges nodes.
- What t001–t004 shipped (`disk_holder.go`, ADR082 § D3): the operator logs `holding the disk on its node through the rollout` when it creates `dskhld-<app>`, and `ended the disk hold` with its reason. The holder pod's `PodReadyToStartContainers` transition should come before the old pod's `SIGTERM`, and the holder's deletion after the new pod's `PodReadyToStartContainers`. If this measurement changes code, run t006 and t007 again over it.

## Steps

1. Confirm the newest `chore(deploy): pin platform images to <sha>` on `origin/main` carries the shipping commit. Park with ``pin must carry `<sha>` `` if it does not.
2. Create a Starter image service with a 1 GB disk and `PORT` named in `bex-canary`. Run at least ten consecutive same-node code deploys and restarts.
3. For each run, record:
   - no `DetachVolume` and no `Verified volume is safe to detach` for the PV;
   - the holder's start before the old pod's `SIGTERM` and its end after the new pod's `PodReadyToStartContainers`;
   - the window;
   - the publish phase. Did the LUKS open (about 2.2 s) disappear?
   - the sync gate. Did it fall to about 0?
4. Cordon the fixture's node for one deploy. There must be no holder, or one ended at once, and the move must take no longer than t005's 34.24 s plus the holder's deletion.
5. Afterwards: no `dskhld-` Job or pod is left. The instances list, the SSH target list, the metrics instance count and the events show no holder during a run. The `bex_disk_holds_total{result}` counts match the runs (`held` for each same-node run, `skipped_unschedulable` for the cordoned one), and `bex_disk_hold_ends_total{reason}` reads `mounted` for each same-node run (t002 named the metrics without the `bex_operator_` prefix no operator metric carries).
6. Add the measured bound to ADR082 next to t004's text. Delete the fixture by id.

## Record (2026-11-22): every holder timed out

**Gate.** The newest pin, `19fdb46f1758` (`5330be9f54`), carries `dd8dfaaf00` (`git merge-base --is-ancestor dd8dfaaf00 19fdb46f1758`), and `bex-controller-manager` and `bex-api` ran its digest, `sha256:7a7051cf219c`. All six `bex-tenant-0` kubelets read `nodeStatusUpdateFrequency: 2s`.

**Fixture.** `POST /v1/services` in `bex-canary` created the Starter image service `w6-m559-t005` (`srv-dc1mojl3vdpc73b0hvn0`, `docker.io/traefik/whoami:v1.10.1`, `PORT=80`) at 22:27:26Z, with the 1 GB disk `dsk-dc1mojl3vdpc73b0hvo0` at `/data` (PV `pvc-2d8c4910-c997-4e86-8c56-c4c5a5937a17`), on `bex-tenant-0-zdp6v-9xnq8`. The watches were m547 t005's, plus the namespace's Jobs, the operator's log and `GET /v1/services/{id}/instances` every second. `DELETE /v1/services/srv-dc1mojl3vdpc73b0hvn0` answered 204 at 22:36:38Z. The service and disk `GET`s answer 404, the PV is gone (detached 22:36:57Z), and no Job, pod, claim or `VolumeAttachment` is left. The run-minted API key was revoked.

**Both runs fell back.** W, a code deploy to `v1.10.2` at 22:28:27Z, and R0, a restart at 22:33:21Z. In each, the operator logged `holding the disk on its node through the rollout`, then 15 s later `the disk holder did not mount the volume in time; rolling without it` and `ended the disk hold` with reason `fallback`. The counters then read `bex_disk_holds_total{result="fallback_timeout"} 2` and `bex_disk_hold_ends_total{reason="fallback"} 2`, and nothing else. The holder's volume was not the delay: in W its `WaitForAttach` succeeded 40 ms after the pod was bound (22:28:28.58). containerd's `RunPodSandbox` then took 11.9 s (28.84–40.72) and `StartContainer` another 11.9 s (40.75–52.62), so the kubelet reported the pod started after 24.7 s. R0 took 11.1 s and 13.2 s, again 24.7 s in all. The new instance beside it started in 3.7 s.

**Cause: the holder's 10m CPU limit.** The kubelet puts a pod's summed CPU limits on the pod's cgroup, so runc's init, for the sandbox and then the container, ran at 1/100 of a CPU. Three pause pods of the holder's shape, with no volume, on the same node (in `bex-canary`, deleted at once) reproduced it. They started in 25.4 s at 10m (sandbox 13.9 s), 2.4 s at 100m (0.67 s) and 1.4 s at 500m (0.31 s), so a start costs about 0.25 s of CPU. The kubelet publishes `PodReadyToStartContainers` only after the container start, so the holder could never be seen mounted within `diskHoldMountBound` (15 s). The fix sets the limit to 500m, a Starter instance's CPU (`diskHolderResources`). `TestDiskHolderStartsWellWithinItsMountBound` fails at 10m. ADR082 § D3 records it.

**What the two runs still show.** Deleting the Job left its pod starting. The pod ended only about 10 s after the new instance had the volume (W: TearDown 22:28:53.80, new instance mounted 22:28:44.3). So both swaps ran with the volume in use on its node. That is close to the designed hold, but it is not the designed hold, and neither run counts toward the definition of done:

- the leader `kube-controller-manager` (`ff5b6`) logged no `DetachVolume` and no `Verified volume is safe to detach` for the PV until the fixture's deletion (22:36:48Z);
- the new instance did no `MountDevice`, so there was no LUKS open. It went from bound to `Pulling` in 0.7 s (W), against t005's publish of 2.8–3.4 s;
- the windows were 5.50 s for W (with a 2.16 s pull) and 4.00 s for R0, against m547 t005's 7.25–9.0 s and 5.74–6.25 s;
- each deploy waited the 15 s bound before the old instance stopped. The old instance served throughout, so the wait added no downtime;
- `GET /v1/services/{id}/instances` never listed more than one instance while a holder ran. The service's events named no holder. The holder's pod name cannot match the instance-count metric's `pod=~"<app>-…"` matcher. That metric read 2 in the minute of each swap, which is the old and the new instance inside its liveness window.
