## Objective

Compare a day of recorded agent CPU with the pod counter read straight from Prometheus, as w22/m314 did for `/v1/sandboxes`, and record the ratio.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] For at least five agent sandboxes, recorded CPU-seconds agree with the Prometheus counter within the tolerance w22/m314 used; the figures are in the milestone README.

## Result (2026-11-24)

All 37 agent sandboxes that ran in the first day agree with the pod counter within w22/m314's band; 32 to the hundredth of a CPU-second. Recorded 11,339.42 CPU-seconds against the counter's 11,341.69 (0.9998). The table and the method are in the [milestone README](../README.md#unblock-record-2026-11-24-loopx-w6-utc).
