## Objective

A held release re-checks admission only on its 30 s requeue, so a freed slot can sit idle for up to 30 s per waiter. When a reconcile observes a build reaching a terminal state (or its Job removed by a cancel), enqueue the held Apps at once; keep the 30 s requeue as the fallback.

## Context

- `app_controller.go:1126` (`RequeueAfter: 30 * time.Second`), `app_controller.go:1031` (`buildObserveRequeue` 5 s)
- `app_controller.go` `SetupWithManager` (~line 7380): no Job watch, Jobs live in `BEX_BUILD_NAMESPACE`

## Steps

1. Add a controller-runtime channel source (`source.Channel` with `GenericEvent`) to the App controller.
2. On a terminal build observation, send events for the held Apps (bounded by the ceiling's waiting set from t004).
3. Envtest the timing.

## Files

- lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Envtest: a held App dispatches within ~1 s of another App's build turning terminal, not 30 s
- [ ] No event storm: at most one event per held App per terminal observation
