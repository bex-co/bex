## Objective

Name every new Stripe Customer after the workspace's display name on all three paths, and update the name when the workspace is renamed unless a member has set a company name in Billing info (marked in the Customer's metadata).

## Context

- `renameWorkspace` lives in `lego/backend/internal/workspaces/`.
- A Stripe failure on rename must not fail the rename; retry it the way other best-effort Stripe writes are retried in `lego/backend/internal/billing/`.

## Files

- lego/backend/internal/billing/stripe.go
- lego/backend/internal/billing/workspace_creation.go
- lego/backend/internal/billing/sessions.go
- lego/backend/internal/workspaces/

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Tests: each path creates a Customer named after the workspace; a rename renames it; a rename after a company name is set leaves it.

## Done (2026-11-25)

- `EnsureCustomer` (`stripe.go`) and `workspaceCreationCustomer` (`workspace_creation.go`, which now takes the attempt's name) name the Customer after the workspace's label (`StripeConfig.WorkspaceLabel`, `store.Tenant.Label`); a workspace bex cannot find bills its id, and a name read that fails creates nothing.
- A Customer Checkout created is named after the workspace once its card binds (`nameCheckoutCustomer`, best-effort and logged), unless Checkout collected a business name with a tax ID, which stays, marked `bex_company_name`.
- `workspaces.Service.Rename` calls `FollowWorkspaceRename` with the old and new label. It renames the Customer only while its name is bex's own (empty, the `tea-` id or the old label, and not marked as a company name), so a company name and a name typed into the portal stay. A Stripe failure is logged and the rename stands; stripe-go's network retries are the retry, as for m627's email reset.
- Tests: `TestEnsureCustomerNamesTheCustomerAfterItsWorkspace`, `TestEnsureCustomerCreatesNothingWhenTheNameCannotBeRead`, `TestPrepareWorkspaceSetupOwnsCustomerAndUsesDynamicPaymentMethods`, `TestCompletedCheckoutNamesTheCustomerItCreated`, `TestFollowWorkspaceRenameRenamesOnlyANameThatIsStillBexs`, `TestRename_FollowsIntoTheStripeCustomer`, and the rename steps of `TestBillingInfoOnEverySurfaceE2E`.
