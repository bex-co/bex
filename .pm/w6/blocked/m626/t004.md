## Objective

In Stripe test mode, with a short grace, drive a workspace to `enforced`, bind a working card through the Billing page, and time the return to `healthy` and of each billing-suspended service to Running. Repeat with Pay now.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both paths reach `healthy` within one `BEX_STRIPE_RECONCILE_INTERVAL` of the payment, and the timings are in the milestone README.

## Gate (2026-11-25)

Not run: no Stripe test-mode key is reachable, see [t001](t001.md) (`.env` holds no Stripe secret key, production's `bex-system/bex-stripe` holds an `rk_live_` key, and the Stripe CLI on this host is not logged in). Needed from the user: a Stripe **test-mode** restricted key (`rk_test_…`) on the bex account, with write access to Customers, Invoices, Invoice items, Payment methods, Subscriptions and Checkout Sessions, in `.env` as `BEX_TEST_STRIPE_KEY` and, for a dev-6 bex-api, `BEX_STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` with a test webhook endpoint (its secret as `BEX_STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`), `BEX_STRIPE_DUNNING_ENABLED=1`, a short `BEX_STRIPE_GRACE_PERIOD` and `BEX_STRIPE_RECONCILE_INTERVAL=5m`.

Then, with the code of t002 and t003 deployed to that bex-api: the bind-time path should take seconds (the payment and the recovery are recorded in the webhook that binds the card), and Pay now should take until the `invoice.paid` webhook, both well inside one `BEX_STRIPE_RECONCILE_INTERVAL`. Time both to `healthy` and to every billing-suspended service Running, and a declined second card (`4000 0000 0000 0341` entered again) leaving the state as it was with `billing_overdue_payments.reason` set.
