## Objective

When a payment method is bound while the workspace's lifecycle is `grace`, `enforcing` or `enforced`, attempt each open cycle invoice once, off-session, with an idempotency key per invoice and payment method. A success flows through the existing `invoice.paid` handling into `recovering`; a failure leaves the state as it is and records the reason. If t001 shows a portal change leaves the Subscription's default on the failed card, also move the Subscription's default when bex learns of the change.

## Context

- `Invoices.Pay` is called today only in deletion settlement (`lego/backend/internal/billing/settle.go`) and agent-model charges (`agent_model_charge.go`): reuse their off-session pattern.
- Bind paths: the Checkout completion (`bindDefaultPaymentMethod`, `sessions.go`) and, once the live endpoint sends `customer.updated`, `RecordPortalPaymentMethodChange` (`portal_audit.go`).
- Lifecycle state and reducer: `lego/backend/internal/billing/lifecycle.go`, `worker.go`, `reconciler.go`.

## Steps

1. Add the attempt after a successful bind, guarded by the lifecycle state.
2. Make it idempotent per invoice and payment method, and never charge more than the open invoices.
3. Handle the portal path behind the same function, so it works once `customer.updated` arrives.

## Done (2026-11-25)

- `lego/backend/internal/billing/overdue.go`: after a Checkout bind (`CompleteCheckoutSession`, last, once the bind and its audit succeeded) and a portal card change (`RecordPortalPaymentMethodChange` → `followPortalPaymentMethod`), while the lifecycle is `grace`, `enforcing` or `enforced`, each unpaid cycle invoice of the live Subscription (open or uncollectible, as `unpaidContractInvoices` in `settle.go` reads them) is paid off-session with the new card, oldest first, stopping at the first decline. Idempotency key `bex-overdue-pay-<invoice>-<pm>-<attempt>`.
- Once per invoice and card: `billing_overdue_payments` (migration 0346, `store.ClaimOverdueInvoicePayment`), keyed by the card's fingerprint so the same card entered in a new Checkout is not charged again; a decline records the issuer's decline code. An attempt that failed for another reason, or was abandoned pending for 10 minutes, may be claimed again.
- When every invoice is paid the success is recorded at once through `RecordStripeBillingEvent` (`billing.invoice.paid_at_binding`), so enforced → recovering and grace → healthy without waiting for `invoice.paid` or the reconcile poll. Never more than the open invoices: only invoices that still owe are paid, each at most once.
- Portal path: without t001's answer, a Subscription default other than the portal's new card is always moved to it (Stripe's docs say the portal only may drop the override).
- Tests against Postgres: `TestBindInEnforcedPaysTheOpenInvoiceAndRecoversAgainstPostgres`, `TestADeclinedCardIsNotRetriedOnTheSameInvoiceAgainstPostgres`, `TestBindInHealthyPaysNothingAgainstPostgres`, `TestOverdueInvoicesArePaidOldestFirstUntilADeclineAgainstPostgres`, `TestOverduePaymentClaimsAgainstPostgres`, `TestPortalCardMovesTheSubscriptionDefaultAndPaysAgainstPostgres`; each was seen to fail with its guard removed.
