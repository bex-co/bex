## Objective

On production, start a QA session with a long turn, delete its sandbox Pod mid-turn, and read the transcript on REST and the session page.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The transcript holds the turn's parts up to about 30 s before the kill, and reads truncated with the reason.

## Run 2026-11-16 (production, QA user, workspace tian-personal; deploy `3c6654df6` and later)

- **The mechanism works.** Session `ags-dbtgogbu1inc739f8tug`: its turn-2 sandbox Pod was deleted at 14:03:44Z. Within 8 s the session read `failed` with `sandbox terminated before completion`. The turn kept the 12 items its running harvest stored at 14:03:08Z (steps 2–5), and reads `transcriptTruncated: true` with that reason on REST and GraphQL. The session page shows the prompt, "Some assistant output could not be preserved: sandbox terminated before completion", steps 2–5, then "Session failed".
- **The bound failed: it kept nothing after 14:01:45Z, almost 2 minutes before the kill.** The driver's turn log (`lego/agent-image/driver/src/turn-log.ts`) held parts in a 64 KiB buffer, written only when full or at close. In session `ags-dbtgkukc5bcc73fvh9e0`, parts 0–135 (13:51:59–13:55:11Z) reached `session.jsonl` only at 13:55:11Z, and parts 136–235 only at the turn's end. That run's harvests also showed no duplicates: 24 items while it ran, then 20 more at the finish, 44 in all, each part index once. The one-minute harvest lease (w4/677) adds up to a minute more.
- **Fix (shipped with this record).** The turn log writes each buffered part within 5 s (`flushDelayMs`; tests in `test/hotpath-baseline.test.ts` fail without it), and the running harvest runs every 30 s (`liveTranscriptInterval`). ADR051 records both.
- **Remaining.** After the next production deploy carries the fix (deploy.yml builds the agent image and bex-api), re-run this check. Accept when the last stored item ends within about 30–45 s of the kill.
- Cleanup: both QA sessions deleted (404), their sandboxes gone, both QA Kratos sessions logged out.
