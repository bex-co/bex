## Objective

ADR021 § 10's "Not covered" line becomes the rule. Record the drain, its bound and why, the reserve fix (t003), the rolls that still cut at once (t004), Render's basis, and the gap that remains. File the SNI-proxy drain as its own item.

## Context

- `docs/ADR021-keyvalue-management.md` § 10 ends with "**Not covered.** A deploy that does change a proxy's code still closes its connections at once".
- Render basis: [Key Value](https://render.com/docs/key-value) (a restart when the customer changes the store) and [platform maintenance](https://render.com/docs/platform-maintenance) (service-affecting maintenance "usually occur[s] no more than once every three months", with advance notice by email and a reschedulable window), both read 2026-11-19.
- The gap that remains: a held client (pub/sub, a pool, a blocking command longer than the bound) still meets the close at the end of the drain, without notice, on every deploy that changes the proxy code. The stricter variant is to defer a proxy-code roll to the store's next roll the customer causes, with a security override. Note that `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md` lists Render's maintenance runs as a non-goal.
- SNI proxies: `hostPort` DaemonSets with `maxUnavailable: 1` and no surge (w6/m469 § Findings), so a draining pod blocks its replacement on that node.

## Steps

1. Amend ADR021 § 10. If the gap that remains needs the user's sign-off as a divergence, say so and route it through ADR018 rather than assuming it.
2. Use `/pm add w6` to file the SNI-proxy drain as an inbox note, and the deferral variant as a second note, each with its evidence and a testable end state.
3. Run `npx prettier@3.4.2 --write` on the touched markdown.
