## Objective

`Serve` gets a drain that is separate from its hard stop. When the drain starts, the listener closes, so the old pod accepts nothing new. Every open stream keeps relaying in both directions until its client or the upstream closes it, or until the drain's deadline. At the deadline the remaining streams close as they do today. Cancelling the context still closes everything at once.

## Context

- `lego/operator/internal/kvinternalauth/proxy.go`: `Serve`, `proxy`, `refuse`, `Budget`.
- `Serve` is currently `Serve(ctx, listener, config)`. Its `context.AfterFunc` closes the listener and every connection in `active`.
- The drain covers both listeners and all three modes: `InjectAuth`, `PlainPassthrough` and `TLSPassthrough`. A refusal in flight (`refuse`) finishes within its own handshake deadline.
- The activator's drain (w6/m485, `lego/operator/cmd/activator`) and the ssh-gateway's (w6/m482) are recent precedents for shape and naming.

## Steps

1. Add the drain to `Serve`'s API. For example, a `Config.Drain <-chan struct{}` plus a deadline, or a `Drain(deadline)` on a server value. Pick whichever keeps both callers in `cmd/kv-internal-proxy` simple.
2. On drain: close the listener, keep `active`, and wait for the handlers. At the deadline, close every remaining connection. `Serve` returns once every handler is done.
3. When the last open stream finishes, `Serve` returns at once, without waiting for the deadline.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A client that sent a command before the drain reads the upstream's reply when it is written after the drain began.
- [x] A dial after the drain began is not accepted by the old listener.
- [x] An idle client stays open until the deadline and is closed then.
- [x] Every budget slot is released after the drain.
- [x] `Serve` returns as soon as the last stream ends.
- [x] Cancelling the context, without a drain, still closes everything at once.
- [x] `go test ./internal/kvinternalauth/...` passes with `-race`.

## Result — 2026-11-19

`Config` gained `Drain <-chan struct{}` and `DrainTimeout`. When `Drain` closes, `Serve` closes its listener and keeps every handler. A deadline of `DrainTimeout` then cancels the context, which closes what is left as before, and `Serve` returns as soon as the last handler ends. `Budget` now records the streams it admits, rather than counting slots, so a draining proxy can close some of them (t003). `lego/operator/internal/kvinternalauth/proxy_test.go`, all with `-race`:

- `TestDrainLetsACommandInFlightFinish`: a client's `BLPOP` is answered by the upstream after the drain began. A dial after the drain is refused, `Serve` returns at once when the client closes (deadline one minute), and the budget holds nothing.
- `TestDrainClosesAnIdleClientAtTheDeadline`: an idle client is still served after the drain began, is closed no earlier than the 400 ms deadline, and `Serve` then returns with the budget empty.
- `TestCancellationClosesAtOnceWhetherOrNotDraining`: cancelling the context closes a held client at once, before a drain and during one. The existing `TestCancellationClosesActiveAndAuthenticatingStreams` is unchanged.
