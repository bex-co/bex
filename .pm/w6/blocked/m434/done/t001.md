## Objective

In `bex-co/muse-code-acp`, add a client capability that a client negotiates in `initialize`. Under it, a prompt whose foreground turn has ended stays pending while Muse still has background work for the session. It resolves once that work, and the turn Muse starts to report it, have finished. Without the capability, 0.11.0's behavior is unchanged.

## Context

- `src/agent-capabilities.ts:116-130` shows how `muse/review`, `muse/approval` and `muse/goal` are negotiated. The client sends `clientCapabilities._meta["muse/<name>"] === 1`, and on the SDK backend only, initialize answers `_meta["muse/<name>"] = {version: 1}`. The new capability follows the same pattern.
- `src/muse-sdk-host.ts:486-531` `release(keepAlive)` decides what happens after a foreground turn.
  - It keeps the host only while `safeToReuse` holds: an in-progress `toolCall`, `workflow`, `subagent` or `userShell` item when `onTaskUpdate` is set, or an active host-owned turn under `onGoal`.
  - Even then, it keeps the host for at most `idleTimeoutMs ?? 60_000` or `maxTurns ?? 32`.
  - `close()` (`:533-560`) then reports `translator.lostWork()` as `failed` cards.
- `src/muse-sdk-host.ts:372` exposes `hasActiveTurn`. The fold exposes `activeTurnId`, `pendingApprovals()`, `pendingUserInputs()` and `items.list()`.
- `docs/async-tasks.md` says: "When a background task ends, Muse starts its own turn to report it." Muse 1.4.14's `workflow` launch result says the result "wakes you even after you end the turn".
- **The wake gap.** A workflow item reaches its terminal revision before Muse opens the turn that reports it. In between, there may be a moment when nothing is in progress.
  - Before choosing the settle condition, check what the real host (1.4.14-R7309.1) publishes in that window: an item flag, session state, or a pending notification.
  - If nothing public marks it, wait a short, documented settle window after the last background item finishes. Resolve the prompt if no reporting turn starts within it.
  - Do not depend on undocumented internals without feature detection (the adapter's `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md`).
- These count as background work:
  - an in-progress `workflow` or `subagent`;
  - a backgrounded `toolCall`, both from `task/background` and from Muse 1.4.14's `bash`, which backgrounds a command once it outlasts `yield_time_ms` (at most 300000);
  - an in-progress `userShell`;
  - an active host-owned turn;
  - a pending approval or user input.

## Steps

1. Negotiate the capability in `src/agent-capabilities.ts`, and record it on the session.
2. In the SDK prompt path (`src/sdk-prompt-turn.ts`), let the foreground turn settle as before. Then wait until the session is quiescent as defined above, and return the prompt's stop reason. Chained wakes count: a reporting turn may launch new work.
3. While a prompt is held, do not release the host: no idle timer, no `maxTurns` rotation, no `lostWork()` cards. Close it only on session close, dispose or cancel.
4. Add fixture tests using `src/tests/fixtures/fake-msp.cjs` with a new `FAKE_MSP_MODE`. With the capability, the prompt stays pending while a workflow item is in progress and resolves after the reporting turn ends. Without it, the prompt resolves as it does in 0.11.0.

## Done (2026-11-16)

Shipped in `bex-co/muse-code-acp` `0e38f27` (`muse/backgroundHold`, `src/background-hold.ts`, the hold in `src/muse-sdk.ts`), with `8ad6672` and `964c747` after it (see t002). Adapter CI is green on `0e38f27`.

- **The wake gap, as the real host behaves.** A probe of Muse 1.4.14-R7309.1 found nothing public between a background item's terminal `item/completed` and the reporting turn's `turn/started`. There were no `monitor/inbox*` frames and no item flag, and `session/statusChanged` stayed `idle` in between. The two came 32 to 43 ms apart (workflow and backgrounded `bash`). So the hold waits `MUSE_CODE_ACP_WAKE_SETTLE_MS` (default 3000) once the session is quiet after background work.
- **Finding the reporting turn.** New turns are found in the fold's turn list (`fold.turns()`), measured against the turns that predated the prompt. A short reporting turn can start and finish between two polls of `activeTurnId`.
- **Subagents.** A finished workflow leaves its children's `subagent` items `inProgress` at `controlStatus: "resultReady"`. Only working control states hold a prompt.
- **Fixture tests.** `src/tests/background-hold.test.ts` runs against new `FAKE_MSP_MODE=backgroundWake` scenarios: negotiation, the settle window, workflow, backgrounded bash, chained wakes, a late wake, no wake, and 0.11.0 behaviour without the capability. With the hold removed, 9 of its tests fail.
