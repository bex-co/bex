## Objective

Under t001's capability, the host-owned turn that reports finished work behaves as part of the held prompt:

- Its message, thought, tool and plan updates go out as `session/update`s for the same ACP session.
- Its approval requests go to ACP `session/request_permission`. The prompt is still open, so a foreground permission interaction exists.
- `session/cancel` during the hold does three things: it cancels the observed workflows (`workflow/cancel`), stops backgrounded tasks (`task/stop`) and interrupts the reporting turn. The prompt then resolves `cancelled`.

The "Muse is executing a host-owned turn" refusal no longer applies to the held prompt itself. A second ordinary prompt during the hold still fails as it does today.

## Context

- The refusal is at `src/sdk-prompt-turn.ts:251-255`. Other `hasActiveTurn` checks are in `src/acp-agent.ts` at `:252`, `:690`, `:797` and `:866`.
- `src/muse-sdk-events.ts` and `src/muse-view-events.ts` translate folded items into ACP updates.
- `src/muse-permissions.ts` maps MSP approvals to ACP. After the foreground turn, `release()` installs an approval handler that refuses every request (`src/muse-sdk-host.ts:489-494`). Under the hold, the live handler must stay in place instead.
- `docs/goal-extension.md` says `/goal set` already streams a host-started turn "like any turn (approvals included)". Reuse that path.
- `src/pending-watchdog.ts` (`MUSE_CODE_ACP_STALL_MS`) also applies to approval stalls during the hold.
- The adapter's `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md` rules out faked permissions: only real host requests, and only host-offered choices.

## Done (2026-11-16)

Shipped in `bex-co/muse-code-acp` `0e38f27`.

- Each adopted turn streams through the held prompt's translator, and the prompt answers every host request the session raises while it is held.
- Cancel during the hold calls `workflow/cancel` and `task/stop` where the host was verified to accept them (`MuseSdkHost.stopBackgroundWork`), and cancels the running reporting turn.
- Fixture tests cover four cases: an approval allowed, an approval denied or dismissed (never granted), cancel during the hold, and cancel during the reporting turn (`turn/cancel` for `wake-1`, no `approval/decide`).
- The capability is documented in the adapter's `docs/async-tasks.md#holding-the-prompt-for-background-work` and `CHANGELOG.md`.
- `8ad6672` fixes one ordering flaw. The finished work's card went out on the host's 100 ms observation tick, so a fast reporting turn could reach the client first. Retained cards are now flushed before each reporting turn is adopted. A fixture test reproduces it: 3 of 3 runs failed before the fix and 0 of 41 after.
- `964c747` stops adoption from dropping a decision in flight. A reporting turn's approval can be asked before the hold adopts the turn, and adopting it through `adoptTurn` started a new permission generation. The hold now only moves the current turn, and user inputs are tracked under their own turn. The fixture also completes the reporting message with the text it streamed, as Muse does.
