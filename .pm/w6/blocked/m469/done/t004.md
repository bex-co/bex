## Objective

ADR021 says when a store's proxy rolls and why an operator deploy usually does not roll it. The SNI-proxy half of w6/140 becomes its own inbox note with its evidence, as w6/140's done-when allows.

## Context

- ADR021 § 9 (`docs/ADR021-keyvalue-management.md`) describes `kv-internal-proxy` and its budget.
- README § Findings has the SNI evidence: 3 replicas each, `hostPort`, `maxUnavailable: 1`, the process exits on `SIGTERM`, 24 h and 1 h stream caps, binaries that change more often. The DaemonSets' image comes from the `controller=` pin, and `deploy.yml`'s "wait for PROXY-compatible datastore proxies" step expects the new operator digest on them.

## Steps

1. Add the rule to ADR021: the proxy rolls when its binary, env or resources change, never because the operator's image changed. Add how the hash is recorded and how a Deployment with no record is adopted. Run prettier on it.
2. File the SNI-proxy note with `/pm add w6` (next free number). Include the problem, the evidence, the two directions (hold the digest in `deploy.yml` when the binaries match, or a separate content-addressed image) and a testable done-when: an external TLS client survives a deploy that leaves the SNI binaries alone.
3. Add to the same note, or a second one, a draining shutdown for proxy rolls that do change the code. Note its limits: a client that holds its connection still meets the close at the end of any grace period, and enabling internal authentication must keep closing old connections at once.
