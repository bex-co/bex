## Objective

Neither SNI DaemonSet asks for a `hostPort`. Both roll with `maxSurge: 1` and `maxUnavailable: 0`, set the drain bound, and give the pod a `terminationGracePeriodSeconds` that covers it. Production's edge is unchanged: the LB keeps reaching the proxies through the fixed `Local` NodePorts.

## Context

- `lego/operator/config/kv-sni-proxy/daemonset.yaml` (`hostPort: 6379`) and `config/pg-sni-proxy/daemonset.yaml` (`hostPort: 5432`).
- Production: `config/prod/kv-sni-proxy-lb.yaml` (31892) and `pg-sni-proxy-lb.yaml` (31056), `externalTrafficPolicy: Local`, and the Terraform LB services in `infra/terraform/main.tf`. `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` checks the fixed NodePorts.
- The ClusterIP Service's comment in `config/pg-sni-proxy/service.yaml` still names the `hostPort` dev path and a `deploy/gitops/overlays/prod/pg-sni-proxy-lb.yaml` that is now `config/prod/pg-sni-proxy-lb.yaml`.
- The bound: the milestone's Design § The bound. 30 s and a 35 s grace period unless this task finds a longer bound worth it.
- Guards that may pin today's shape: `scripts/gitops-validate.sh`, `scripts/test_platform_metrics.py`, `scripts/edge-proxyprotocol-validate.sh`, `scripts/operator-pins.sh` (`wait`) and its `.test.sh`.

## Steps

1. Drop both `hostPort`s, and add `updateStrategy.rollingUpdate` with `maxSurge: 1` and `maxUnavailable: 0`, the drain env and `terminationGracePeriodSeconds`.
2. Weigh the bound: a platform node drain waits for the grace period, and two pods of up to 512 Mi each share a node during it. Record the choice and why in this task.
3. Fix the stale Service comment. Run the guards above and update any that assert the old shape, without weakening what they protect.
4. On the local cluster, check that both DaemonSets now schedule (PodSecurity `baseline` refused the `hostPort`), and that a template change surges: two pods on the node, the new one Ready before the old one is told to stop.

## Progress (2026-11-19, implemented and verified locally; in stash `loopx/w6/m515`, not on `main`)

- **Manifests.** Both DaemonSets now have no `hostPort`, `updateStrategy.rollingUpdate` `maxSurge: 1` / `maxUnavailable: 0`, the drain env set to `"30"`, a 5 s `preStop` `sleep`, and `terminationGracePeriodSeconds: 40`. `config/pg-sni-proxy/service.yaml`'s comment now names `config/prod/pg-sni-proxy-lb.yaml`, and the DaemonSet's two `hostPort` comments are gone. `kustomize build config/prod` renders all of it, and the fixed NodePorts 31056 and 31892 are unchanged.
- **The pause (found in t004's first run).** Without it, the old pod closed its listener at `SIGTERM` while kube-proxy still sent it a share of new connections, and one new Postgres client was refused at that second. The pause keeps the old pod accepting while its endpoint leaves every node's rules. A `preStop` `sleep` needs no shell in the distroless image, and it is GA on production's Kubernetes v1.34.9. The operator already uses it for tenant apps. With the pause outside the process, a dial after the drain began is still refused, as t001 requires.
- **The bound: 30 s, not longer.**
  - The milestone's finding that "a rollout completes when each node's new pod is available and does not wait for terminating pods" does not hold for `kubectl rollout status`. The DaemonSet controller's status reads each node's oldest pod.
  - A scratch `maxSurge: 1` DaemonSet in `dev-6` (old pod trapping `SIGTERM` for 90 s) took 91.5 s to report `successfully rolled out`. The controller itself moved on as soon as the new pod was available.
  - So `operator-pins.sh wait`'s 300 s `rollout status` covers 5 s + 30 s per node, but not an hour. A node drain does not wait for the pod, since Kubernetes evicts no DaemonSet pod. A second 512 Mi pod per proxy per node lasts at most 35 s.
  - The 1 h variant and the change it needs first are filed as [w6/146](../../done/146.md).
- **Guards.**
  - `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` gains "SNI proxy DaemonSets surge and drain within their grace period". It requires `RollingUpdate,1,0`, no `hostPort`, a positive pause and drain, and a grace period above their sum. Mutations caught: a grace of 35, a `hostPort`, a renamed drain variable, a zero pause.
  - `TestTheDaemonSetGivesTheDrainItsBound` (one per proxy) reads the base DaemonSet. It checks that the variable the binary reads is set to a positive bound, and that the grace period exceeds the pause plus that bound.
  - `test_platform_metrics.py`, `edge-proxyprotocol-validate.sh` and `operator-pins.sh` pin nothing this changes.
- **On the local cluster.** Both DaemonSets had shown desired 1, current 0 (PodSecurity refused the `hostPort`). On `bex-operator:m515-a` both ran, 1/1, on `bex-worker-0-cg5cm-8m4qs-6m2tf`. Every roll surged: the new pod became Ready, then the controller deleted the old one (t004: Ready 13:38:16Z, deleted 13:38:17Z).
  - By accident, a template naming an image without the proxy binary left both old pods serving while the new ones crash-looped. The old `maxUnavailable: 1` would have taken the node's proxy down.
