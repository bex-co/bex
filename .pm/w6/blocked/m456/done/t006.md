**Step 2, the three existing `starter` stores (t002's), found two defects in m456's rollout:**

- `red-dbpf5q5me2kc73f2l0og` kept its flagless template and adopted 260 by `CONFIG SET`, with no restart: the same `run_id` and an uptime since 2026-11-12. But the operator's record of it was refused on every pass, because it was a pod annotation, which the `bex-operator-pod-labels` admission policy lets the operator never change. The operator log read "Valkey client limit applied but not recorded … denied request: the operator may relabel a pod but never change its annotations" every ~30 s from 12:12Z, and it dialed the store again each time.
- `red-d9p49kdrtmes73c34ovg` and `red-dbnukk1ngmjs739tgulg` restarted once at 12:10Z. Their new StatefulSet revisions differ from the old ones only by `--maxclients 260` plus, respectively, `priorityClassName: bex-datastore` and the `app.bex.co/environment-id` template label. Both of those are deferred changes that an existing store adopts only when it rolls anyway (`holdDatastorePriority`, `holdIsolationLabel`). The limit's hold was applied after the write, so each hold saw the other's pending change as a reason to roll. Each new pod was Ready 58 s and 14 s after it was created (12:11:08Z and 12:10:30Z), and its store was unavailable from the old pod's stop until then.
- None of the three sees refusals: their peaks since the deploy are 17, 33 and 1 connections (Prometheus `max_over_time(redis_connected_clients[5h])`, which includes the exporter's own connection), against 250, and `rejected_connections` is 0.

**Fixed in the same commit** (`git log origin/main --grep 'w6/m456 t006'`):

- The held limit now goes through the template write itself (`keyValueIntent.heldMaxclients`), as the held maxmemory policy already did, so every change that rolls nothing holds together.
- The record moves to the StatefulSet's annotations, as the persistence handoff token's already is.
- Regression tests, each failing against the old code:
  - `TestKeyValueClientLimitHoldsBesideTheOtherDeferredChanges`;
  - the envtest "lets the Key Value client-limit pass record what it applied", run against the production policy;
  - `TestKeyValueMaxclientsReachesRunningServer`, which now also refuses a pod record.
- ADR021 § 9 records it. t011 checks the fixed rollout on production.
