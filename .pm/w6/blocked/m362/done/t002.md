## Objective

`pricing.yaml` and `lego/backend/internal/pricing/spendcaps.go` express D10.2a: T0's `capCeilingUsd` becomes null, and each tier with a charge threshold carries an `inFlightAllowanceUsd` (initial value $5 at T0, recommended): the spend allowed past the threshold while its charge settles.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/pricing/pricing.yaml` `agentModel.trustTiers` (T0: threshold $10, cap $10).
- `lego/backend/internal/agentsession/spendcap.go`: `SpendStatus.WithOverride`, `TierLimited`, `modelSpendNewWorkspaceReason`.

## Steps

1. Add `inFlightAllowanceUsd` to the tier schema with validation (non-negative; set wherever a threshold is set).
2. Set T0 `capCeilingUsd: null`; the plan cap and admin override stay the only cap (D5).
3. Retire `TierLimited` and the new-workspace refusal reason, or keep them only for a workspace held after a failed charge.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The sheet loads, and a T0 workspace's effective cap is its plan cap or override.

## Done 2026-11-13

`capCeilingUsd` is replaced by `inFlightAllowanceUsd` (`lego/backend/internal/pricing/spendcaps.go`, `pricing.yaml`): a tier's cap ceiling is its threshold plus the allowance, and only T0 has one ($10 + $5 = $15). T1 and up have none, so a workspace whose first charge is paid gets its plan cap. A payment-locked or disputed workspace, held at T0, also reads $15, and keeps its locked reason; an open failed invoice still stops it at once (`BILLING_BALANCE_DUE`).
