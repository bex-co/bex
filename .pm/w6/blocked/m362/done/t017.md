## Objective

A workspace that holds Stripe Billing Credits spends them on agent-model usage before its card is charged (user decision 2026-11-13: "应该先扣 credits 如果credits 不够了才扣钱").

## Found

- The threshold charge is a standalone invoice, and Stripe applies Billing Credit Grants only to a subscription's metered lines, never to a one-off invoice ([Stripe billing credits](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/usage-based/billing-credits)).
- Neither the mint's charge decision nor the charger read credits: `agentsession.SpendStatus.UnbilledMicroUSD` was the window's spend less its paid charges.
- So a workspace with credits was charged on its card at each threshold. The cycle invoice then still spent its credits on the metered usage, the negative net item took that invoice below zero, and the card's money waited as customer balance.
- The bex workspace's 08:39:52 `subscription_threshold` invoice (paid by credits) and the 09:50:32 $10.50 card charge show the two mechanisms side by side (t016).

## Steps

1. Read the credits the next subscription invoice applies:
   - the available balance of the customer's metered-scope grants (ADR071's scope; a draft invoice's credits are already out of it);
   - less what grants that expire by the Subscription's current period end hold.
   Read it in the same Stripe read as the charge window boundary, before every charge.
2. Record it (migration 0253). The spend read takes it off the window's unbilled spend while the read is inside the window. Moving the boundary clears it, since that invoice consumed credits.
3. If the charger reads a balance other than the one the request counted, it charges nothing and the mint re-reads. A failed credit read keeps the recorded balance and the charge goes ahead.
4. Open the charge window at the boundary even when that is in the previous month (never before the previous month's start), and at the Subscription's start before any invoice has billed the meter. One window then matches one invoice, so its credits are counted once.
5. Settings → Agents, the composer and the API field descriptions say that credits pay first.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A workspace whose credits cover its spend is neither charged nor stopped at T0's $10.
- [x] Past the credits, the card pays only what they leave, at the tier's threshold.
- [x] The workspace's own monthly cap still counts the month's whole spend.
- [x] The credit read leaves out grants that expire before the period ends, and voided ones.
- [x] A real-DB test: credits count only when read inside the window, moving the boundary clears them, and a boundary in the previous month still opens the window.

## Done 2026-11-13

- **Stripe.** `StripeClient.AgentModelMeter` replaces `AgentModelBilledUntil`. It returns the boundary (or the Subscription's `start_date` before any meter-billing invoice) and `agentModelCreditMicroUSD`: the metered balance less grants expiring by the items' `current_period_end`. `meteredCreditCents` and `grantCreditCents` are now shared with the Billing page's credits block (`read.go`). A failed credit read is logged as `credit_read` `error` and leaves `CreditsRead` false.
- **Charger.** `ChargeThreshold` takes a `store.AgentModelCrossing`, the type the mint's `agentsession.ThresholdCharge` aliases: the mint's read, including the credit it counted. `refreshWindow` records the boundary and the credits and answers `ChargeNotDue` when either moved. `subscriptionPeriod` now serves both this read and the settle contract's period start.
- **Store.** Migration 0253 adds `agent_model_credit_microusd` and `agent_model_credit_read_at`. `RecordAgentModelCredits`; `RecordAgentModelBilledUntil` clears both when it advances. `AgentSpendInputs.CreditMicroUSD`, a window that can start in the previous month, and paid charges summed across both months; `ChargeSeq` stays the month's.
- **Mint.** `SpendStatus.UnbilledMicroUSD` = window spend − credits − paid charges, floored at 0. The charge point and T0's ceiling use it; `ThresholdCharge.CreditMicroUSD` carries the read's credit.
- **Copy.** Settings → Agents (tier line and T0 note), the composer's charge-point warning, the GraphQL `nextChargeThresholdUsd` description and the MCP `get_agent_environment` description (golden regenerated), in English and Chinese.
- **Tests.** `TestCreditsPayBeforeTheCard`, `TestMintReadsTheCreditBeforeCharging`, `TestAgentModelChargeCreditsPayFirst`, `TestAgentModelMeterCredit`, and `TestAgentSpendInputsChargeWindow` (real DB, extended).
- **Docs.** ADR091 D10.2a § The charge window (amended) and § Billing credits pay first; the Stripe runbook's credit-read permission row.
- **Not verified on production.** Whether the live restricted key can read billing credits. Without that permission the charge goes ahead without counting credits. t010 checks it after the deploy.
- **/simplify, not taken.** On a credited workspace's first crossing in a window, if the credit does not cover it, the mint's re-read calls the charger again and the meter is read from Stripe a second time (about 1–2 s, once per window). Skipping that read would need cross-request state or another field on the crossing.
