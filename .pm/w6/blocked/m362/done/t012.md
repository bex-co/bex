## Objective

A threshold charge and the cycle invoice never bill the same usage, and the mid-cycle invoice changes neither the subscription's billing anchor nor its proration.

## Context

- The cost meter's hourly export (ADR091 D3; `lego/backend/internal/billing/emitter.go` ~121, `stripe.go` ~538).
- **Revised 2026-11-13:** not exporting charged usage to the meter cannot net it. When a charge lands, earlier hours of the month are already exported, and meter events cannot be taken back, so a month whose usage stops after the charge would bill those hours twice. The netting is instead a negative pending invoice item on the workspace's subscription, equal to the amount charged, which the next cycle invoice includes. It is not `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md`'s rejected ADR071 credit system: it issues no value and needs no approval, it nets a payment already collected.

## Steps

1. After a paid charge, add a negative pending invoice item for the charged amount on the workspace's subscription (idempotent), and record its id on the charge.
2. Create the threshold invoice as a standalone invoice on the customer, never on the subscription (no anchor reset, no proration, and hosting dunning never reads it as a contract invoice); assert the request parameters in a Stripe-stub test.
3. On production (t006), the next cycle invoice preview excludes the charged amount.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Over a cycle, threshold charges plus the cycle invoice's agent-model line equal the month's agent-model spend.

## Done 2026-11-13 (code; production check in t006)

A paid charge adds a negative pending invoice item of the charged amount on the workspace's subscription (idempotent key `…-net`, recorded as `net_item_id`; the sweep retries a missing one), so the next cycle invoice bills only usage not already charged. The charge invoice never names the subscription (asserted in the Stripe-stub test), so the anchor and proration are untouched. Open, recorded in ADR091: a declined charge is netted only once paid, and Stripe refuses negative items under automatic tax, which production does not enable (no tax code configured).
