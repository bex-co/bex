## Objective

Past a threshold, platform-model requests keep flowing while the charge is in flight, up to the threshold plus the tier's in-flight allowance; past that, the mint answers a retryable refusal until the charge settles. A successful charge records the payment at once, and the tier re-derives within the 20-second spend cache. A failed charge, or one that needs 3-D Secure (`authentication_required`), refuses with `BILLING_BALANCE_DUE` and a hosted payment link.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/agentsession/spendcap.go` (spend status, 20 s cache), the gateway model-proxy mint, ADR091 D10.3 (`BILLING_BALANCE_DUE`).
- A session's `failureReason` / `failureReasonCode`.

## Steps

1. Spend status gains the in-flight charge state and allowance.
2. Record a successful charge into `billing_trust` immediately (the webhook stays idempotent).
3. Map `authentication_required` to the balance-due path with the invoice's hosted URL, and email the workspace admins.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A session crossing $10 with a good card never fails.
- [ ] A declined card stops spend at once with `BILLING_BALANCE_DUE`.

## Done 2026-11-13 (reduced to the Stripe-threshold mechanism)

With the Stripe threshold path (t003 revised), there is no bex-side "charge in flight" state to wait on: T0's ceiling ($15) is what lets a workspace keep working while Stripe's threshold invoice is created, finalized at once and paid. Past it, the mint refuses with `AGENT_MODEL_SPEND_CAP_REACHED` and the new reason says the first charge is not paid yet and that new sessions continue once it is (`lego/backend/internal/agentsession/spendcap.go`). A failed or authentication-required charge already stops spend through D10.3's `BILLING_BALANCE_DUE`. Tests: `TestSpendCapEffectiveCap`, `TestPlatformRouteSpendCapRefusesAtTheLimit`.
