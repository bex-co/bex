## Objective

Send bex's billing notices (grace, enforced and recovered, and the Free instance hours notices) to the billing email as well when it is not already the address of a member who gets them.

## Context

- `notifyBilling` and `mailBillingOwners` in `lego/backend/internal/notifications/` mail `ListBillingOwnerSubjects` today.
- Deduplicate by address so nobody gets two copies.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Store-backed tests: a Pro workspace with its own billing email mails it once beside the members; a Hobby workspace's notices are unchanged.

## Outcome (2026-11-25)

Done. `notifications.billingRecipients` adds a Pro or higher workspace's own billing email to every billing lifecycle notice and to the worker notices built on `mailBillingOwners` (Free instance hours, build minutes, included bandwidth, Free database expiry), once, unless an admin who gets them has the same address (case-insensitive). Every address is still read in one batched lookup. A lifecycle notice records `billing-email` in `mailed_to`, so its retry does not mail it again; while an admin's address cannot be read, the billing email waits for the retry. Tests: `internal/notifications/billing_address_test.go`, including a Postgres-backed enforced notice delivered twice. The Stripe test-mode check of the definition of done is [t010](../t010.md).
