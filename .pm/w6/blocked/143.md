# A `basic-4gb` Postgres with HA and two read replicas cannot be placed on production

Why: Render sells `basic_4gb` with high availability and up to five read replicas, but production's general tenant pool cannot hold four 2-CPU instances, so such a database never reaches Ready.

- **Filed by** w6/m467 t006 (2026-11-18), on production at pin `1db506777e17`.
- **Seen.** A `basic_4gb` probe in `bex-canary` with HA, replicas `a` and `b`, 10 GB and pooling (`dpg-dbv2gbaoonls73bi9920`, created 22:35Z, deleted by ID at 22:43Z). The primary and replica `a` started. The HA standby (`-2`) and replica `b`'s instance (`-r-3e23e81600-1`) stayed Pending: `0/17 nodes are available: … 7 Insufficient cpu, 7 Insufficient memory`, and the autoscaler said `pod didn't trigger scale-up: 2 node(s) had untolerated taint(s), 4 max node group size reached` after `MachineDeployment/bex-capi/bex-tenant-0 6->8 (max: 8)`.
- **Why.** Each `basic-4gb` instance requests 2 CPU and 4 GiB (`lego/types/tiers/tiers.yaml`). `bex-tenant-0` is `cx33` (4 vCPU, about 3.8 allocatable) with max 8 (`infra/clusterapi/overlays/hetzner-caph/cluster.yaml`, user decision 2026-10-29, w2/m171), so a node holds one such instance. Five of the seven running nodes were at 99% of their CPU requests. The larger pools are tainted (`bex.co/build-only`, `bex.co/serving-pool=xl|xxl`) and datastores do not tolerate them.
- **Not this.** w6/m467's mechanism worked: the same probe on `1c-2g` (four 1-CPU instances) was Ready in about 2.5 minutes. w2/m250 and w6/m332 cover the large service tiers' pools, not Postgres.
- **Options (the cost is the user's call):** raise `bex-tenant-0`'s max; give datastores a pool of larger nodes they tolerate; or admit a Postgres create only when the cluster can place every instance, with a clear error instead of a database stuck in `creating`.
- **Done when:** on production, a `basic_4gb` database with HA and two read replicas reaches `available`, with all four instances Running, and is then deleted by ID.

## Triage 2026-11-19 — blocked on the user's capacity decision

- **The approved eighth node never existed.** Hetzner holds at most 10 servers in a spread placement group, and `tenant-0` (id 1766008) held exactly 10: seven `bex-tenant-0` nodes, the two `bex-tenant-burst` nodes and `bex-tenant-lg`. During the probe the autoscaler's eighth `bex-tenant-0` node (`-wldb9-jl4m7`) failed from 22:37Z with `placement_group_member_limit_reached` (CAPH log; `-pr5cd` again at 23:28Z). So `max node group size reached` meant seven nodes, not the user's eight.
- **Shipped with this park:** `bex-tenant-burst` moves to its own spread group `tenant-build` (template `bex-tenant-burst-k134-cx33-build`). The push's `app-cluster.yml` run rolls both burst nodes once; builds ride out the eviction. `scripts/clusterapi-validate.sh` now adds up each spread group's pools at their ceilings, plus one rollout surge, and fails above 10. It was red on the old overlay (`tenant-0` 11 + 1), and `clusterapi-validate.test.sh` proves four red cases. See ADR053 § Spread placement groups and ADR060.
- **Still not enough for "done when".** At 2026-11-19 12:15Z, two of the seven `bex-tenant-0` nodes had 2 CPU free: `-hbkdk` had 1217m of 4 CPU requested and `-khm29` 1167m. The other five had 2167m–3967m requested. With the eighth node that makes three places for four 2-CPU instances. Placing them takes more than eight serving nodes, which is above the user's cost bound (w2/m171, 2026-10-29). Render's maximum for one database, HA plus five replicas, is seven instances on distinct nodes.
- **Gate (the user, spend):** how many cx33 serving nodes beyond `bex-tenant-0`'s 8 the autoscaler may add. Each costs €8.99 a month gross and is billed only while a pod needs it.
  - `tenant-0` has no room left, so the extra nodes go in a pool with its own spread group (ADR053).
  - The worker recommends a second cx33 serving pool, min 0 and max 4. That places this probe at today's load.
  - The note's other options still stand: a datastore pool of larger nodes, or refusing the create. Refusing is a Render divergence that needs ADR018 sign-off.
