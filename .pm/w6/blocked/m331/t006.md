## Objective

The t001 script runs on every substrate. The results pick one VMM, and they replace ADR105's memory and density assumptions with measurements.

## Context

- Substrates:
  - today's `cx33` under gVisor (from t001);
  - the AX42-1 under runc, as the native floor;
  - the AX42-1 under runsc;
  - the AX42-1 under each Kata handler.
- Benchmark Pods are created by hand in a benchmark namespace. They tolerate the trial taint, use the agent image and the agent template's resources, and run with no sandbox regime label, so the sandbox admission policy does not apply to them.
- The gate is the replay at least twice as fast as the cloud baseline (ADR105 § Decision step 3, the user's to change).
- Memory per VM decides `default_memory`, RuntimeClass `overhead` and the slot count (ADR105 § Inputs).

## Steps

1. Run `scripts/sandbox-probe.sh` three times on each substrate. Record the median of each step.
2. Run 12 replays at once under the leading Kata handler. Record host CPU pressure and the spread between performance and efficiency cores.
3. Measure host-side memory per VM, idle and at the peak of the replay, including the VMM, virtiofsd and page cache.
4. Choose the VMM. Write the table, the chosen `default_memory`, the `overhead` and the slot count into ADR105.
