## Objective

Every control ADR042 verified on gVisor is verified again on a Kata sandbox on the AX42-1: the admission policy, the per-workspace egress deny and FQDN allowlist, the driver-ingress policy, and rootfs-commit pause and resume. The node's own exposure is verified as well.

## Context

- ADR042 D3 and its east-west section list the controls and how each was verified.
- `scripts/verify-sandbox-isolation-live.sh` and `scripts/verify-sandbox-pause-resume-live.sh` are the existing checks. Run them against a hand-made BatchSandbox that tolerates the trial taint, in a real `<ws>-sandbox` namespace.
- A microVM has its own kernel and network device. Cilium enforces at the host side of the Pod's veth, which must be confirmed, not assumed.
- The ssh-gateway is the only identity allowed to reach driver port 8787 (`lego/agent-image/README.md`).
- Overflow can resume a sandbox on the other runtime (ADR105 § Decision step 4).

## Steps

1. Re-run each ADR042 verification against a Kata sandbox on the AX42-1.
2. Confirm that a denied egress destination is denied, that an allowlisted registry is reachable, and that a ClusterIP Service and DNS resolve from inside the guest.
3. Pause and resume, both directions: Kata to Kata, Kata to gVisor, and gVisor to Kata. Check that a rootfs marker survives each time, and record whether `/tmp` survives under Kata.
4. Confirm that the sandbox's ephemeral storage is accounted, and that writing past it evicts that sandbox and not the node.
5. Re-run the t004 external port scan.
6. Record every result, pass or fail, in ADR105.
