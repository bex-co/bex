## Objective

The proxies know each route's limit from the resource itself, so the cap follows a plan change without a redeploy.

## Context

- Both proxies already watch the CRs: `dbRouter.set(*appv1alpha1.Database)` in `lego/operator/cmd/pg-sni-proxy/main.go` builds the primary, `-pool` and `-ro-<replica>` routes; `kvRouter.set(*appv1alpha1.KeyValue)` in `lego/operator/cmd/kv-sni-proxy/main.go` builds the store's route. Both import `lego/types`.
- `Limiter.AcquireRoute(workspace, resource)` applies one `maxResource` to every route.

## Steps

1. Resolve each route's limit from `Spec.Plan` through `lego/types/tiers` by t001's rule; the pooler route uses PgBouncer's `max_client_conn`.
2. Carry it on the resolved route and let `AcquireRoute` take a per-route cap; keep an env var only as the override t001 keeps.
3. Unit tests: a plan change on the CR changes the route's cap on the next event.
