## Objective

A database's external clients are admitted up to its limit plus the margin, so PostgreSQL itself refuses the next.

## Context

- PostgreSQL keeps `superuser_reserved_connections` (3) of `max_connections`, so the 98th client of a 100-connection database reads `FATAL: sorry, too many clients already`.
- Today the 65th external client is shed by the proxy with a closed connection, which `psql` reports as an SSL or EOF error.

## Steps

1. Apply t001's per-resource and per-workspace rule in pg-sni-proxy.
2. Test with a stub backend: the proxy admits the route's limit plus the margin and sheds only beyond it, still counting each shed in its rejection metric with the scope.
3. On dev-6 or the mock cluster, confirm the 98th client of a 100-connection database reads `too many clients already` through the proxy.
