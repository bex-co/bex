## Objective

Deleting a disk from a live service closes its `(service, disk_gb_seconds, disk)` coverage stream at the deletion hour + 1, so the month's coverage does not report `disk` degraded once the window settles (`w4/575`, absorbed here).

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/apps/disks.go` `DeleteDisk`
- `store.CloseUsageSourceStreams` closes by resource only; `ReconcileUsageSourceStreams` closes by resource, not by meter kind
- `usage.catchUpDisksThrough` writes no row for zero GB-seconds
- w4/496: a hole older than the catch-up window now reads as a degraded skip

## Steps

1. Add a per-kind stream close to the store (or close the disk stream from the disk meter when a service has no disk at the window).
2. Call it when a disk is deleted.
3. Test: a deleted disk's stream ends at the deletion hour + 1, and coverage 50 h later reads healthy.

## Already met 2026-11-12

`08175f644` (w4/575, 2026-11-11) shipped this before m333 started. Each window, the disk meter closes the open `disk_gb_seconds` streams of every service it did not meter (`store.CloseIdleUsageSourceStreams`). Each stream ends an hour after its last observed window, and a disk added back reopens its stream from its new first window. Tests: `TestDiskMeterClosesTheStreamOfADeletedDisk` (internal/usage) and the PG `TestCloseIdleUsageSourceStreamsKeepsCoverageHealthy`, which shows healthy coverage 72 h on and a control that reads `disk` degraded without the close.
