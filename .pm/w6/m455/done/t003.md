## Objective

Before billing switches, evaluate the old and new expressions on every hour production Prometheus still holds, and prove the new rule never counts more than was served.

## Context

- Production Prometheus keeps about four days (`30d or 30GiB`, size-bound).
- The comparison has to use the same expression builders the meter uses, not a copy.

## Steps

1. For every recorded whole hour, evaluate old and new per Traefik router series, read-only.
2. Show that they differ only on series the rule classifies as new.
3. For each new series, show it was not present earlier in retention with a larger value, so it did not come back carrying an old cumulative value. Check that its first value is in proportion to its own traffic.
4. Record the totals and the conclusion here (no raw dumps).

## Files

- A comparison runner beside the builders (an env-gated test or a script).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every recorded hour compared; differences only on new series; no new series carried an earlier value; the result is recorded.

## Out of scope

- —

## Done 2026-11-17

`TestBilledAgainstIncreaseOnRecordedHours` (`lego/backend/internal/egressquery/billed_live_test.go`) replays `Increase` and `Billed`, read-only, over every hour production Prometheus held. It reads the whole Traefik fleet as one source.

- **Hours.** 97 hours ending 2026-11-13T08:00Z through 2026-11-17T08:00Z.
- **Totals.** Old 91,165,861,593 bytes, new 91,195,474,271 (+29,612,677, +0.0325%).
- **Where they differ.** In every hour, new minus old is exactly the 2,168 new series' last values minus their own `increase()`. The test asserts it, and it passed.
- **Series seen before.** 60 of the new series had been present earlier in retention. The values of 35 show a restart from zero: fewer bytes, fewer requests, or more bytes for the same requests. The other 25 cannot tell: 19 single fixed-size responses of 0 to 21 bytes on both sides, and 6 retries of one size (14 → 42 bytes, 164 or 287 → 492 bytes). They total 803 bytes. t001 shows such a series restarted from zero.
- **What the guard kept out.** It kept 145 series on `increase()` in one hour, 2026-11-14 02:00Z, during Traefik's rollout. The health gate skips that hour under both rules.
- **Conclusion.** No hour counts more than its series served, so billing may switch (t004).
- **Reproduce.** Port-forward `svc/prometheus-server` in `monitoring`, then run `BEX_TEST_EGRESS_PROM_URL=http://127.0.0.1:19090 go test ./internal/egressquery -run OnRecordedHours -v -timeout 60m` from `lego/backend`.
