## Steps

Create a free image service in `bex-canary` (`tea-daif693dqjvc73e7as3g`): `POST /v1/services` with `{"ownerId":…,"type":"web_service","name":"qa-<date>-m455-egress","image":{"ownerId":…,"imagePath":"traefik/whoami:v1.11.0"},"plan":"free","port":80}`. Wait for the deploy to be `live` and for `HEAD /` to answer 200, which carries no body. Then download `/data?size=2000000` five times and delete the service.
3. bex-api meters an hour on its hourly tick, counted from its start, so the row can appear up to an hour after the hour closes. Read the service's `egress_bytes` on `GET /v1/usage?ownerId=…`.
