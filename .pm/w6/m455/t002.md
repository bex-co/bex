## Objective

Build the billing expression that counts a Traefik router series which first appears inside the hour from zero, and prove it with `promtool` in Prometheus's engine, the way `TestInstanceSecondsQueryOnPrometheus` and `TestExactDeltaOnPrometheus` do.

## Context

- Today the meter bills `sum(increase(counter{matchers}[3600s]))` (`egressquery.Increase`), gated by `egressquery.Health` (`resets() == 0`, scrape density, process start).
- `increase()` ignores what a series counted before its first sample in the range, and extrapolates only half a scrape interval toward the start.
- A series counts from zero only when its Traefik target was scraped successfully just before the hour and the series was not in that scrape: then it did not exist yet, and its whole value was counted inside the hour. Every other series keeps `increase()`.

## Steps

1. Add the expression to `lego/backend/internal/egressquery/query.go` for the HTTP spec, keeping `increase()` for every other series and every other source.
2. Write a `promtool` test with these cases: a series new in the hour counts from zero; a reset in the hour fails the gate; a series stale before the hour's end still counts; a series missing at the start because its target was not scraped keeps `increase()`; a series present at the start bills exactly what it did before.
3. Revert the new term and watch the new-series case fail.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/egressquery/query.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/egressquery/*_test.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The five cases pass in Prometheus 3.13.5's engine (`promtool`, CI's backend suite).
- [ ] Old series bill byte-for-byte what `Increase` billed.
