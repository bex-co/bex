## Objective

Five types the backend emits have no catalog entry, so today they are invisible and unfilterable, and after `t001` they would render only under the generic fallback label. Give each a proper group, label, icon and translations.

## Context

The five, with the audit verb that emits each (`eventTypes`, `lego/backend/internal/events/service.go:245`; `:655` assigns `ev.Type = eventTypes[r.Verb]`):

```
custom_domain_verified   service.go:281  "apps.VerifyDomain"
disk_attached            service.go:275  "apps.AddDisk"
disk_detached            service.go:277  "apps.DeleteDisk"
disk_updated             service.go:276  "apps.UpdateDisk"
disk_restored            service.go:278  "apps.RestoreDiskSnapshot"
```

- `custom_domain_verified` belongs beside `custom_domain_added` / `custom_domain_removed`, already in the `configuration` group (`service-event-catalog.ts:79-81`).
- The four `disk_*` types need a grouping decision — fold into `configuration`, or add a group. If a new group is added, the filter's group checkboxes and their i18n labels move with it.
- All five are missing from `LABEL_KEYS` (`service-event-catalog.ts:94+`) as well as from the groups.
- Only the `en` locale was grepped during the hunt; check `zh` too rather than assuming it mirrors `en`.

## Steps

1. Add the five to `SERVICE_EVENT_GROUPS` in their chosen groups.
2. Add `LABEL_KEYS` entries.
3. Add icons in `event-icon.tsx`.
4. Add `en` and `zh` strings in `dashboard/src/features/services/locales/`.
5. Follow the repo i18n rules: feature-prefixed keys, single-brace interpolation, `description` fields for translators.
