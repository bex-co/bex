## Objective

Emit the reassignment fact only after a project or environment membership write succeeds, with no duplicate for no-op or failed changes.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/projects/service.go` owns `SetServices` for projects.
- `lego/backend/internal/environments/service.go` owns `SetServices` for environments.
- The workspace audit event already proves the successful change but does not populate the service feed.

## Steps

1. Compute before/after placement at the two mutation funnels.
2. Persist the event after the authoritative write succeeds.
3. Deduplicate no-ops and keep partial failures event-free.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/projects/service.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/environments/service.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/store/event_facts.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Assign, move, and unassign each emit one accurate event per affected service.
- [ ] No-op and failed writes emit no reassignment event.
- [ ] Bulk membership replacement emits once per service whose placement actually changed.

## Out of scope

- Replacing the workspace audit log or changing project/environment membership semantics.
