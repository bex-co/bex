## Objective

Render spins down a card-less workspace's services once it has used its included bandwidth, until the next month. Suspend them the way the Free-instance-hours sweeper does (Render's `billing` suspender, refused manual resume with a named error), and resume them at the month turn or once a payment method is bound.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/freehours/freehours.go` and `apps/free_hours.go` (suspend/resume, annotation, refusal)
- `lego/backend/internal/buildminutes` (allowance + can-pay read)

## Steps

1. Store read: month-to-date egress per workspace with can-pay.
2. Sweeper: suspend every service of an exhausted card-less workspace (annotation with the month), resume when it can pay or the month turns; refuse a manual resume meanwhile.
3. Tests.

## Files

- lego/backend/internal/ (new sweeper or extend buildminutes)
- lego/backend/internal/apps/
- lego/backend/internal/store/usage.go

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A card-less Hobby workspace past 5 GB has its services suspended with the billing suspender
- [x] Binding a card or a new month resumes them

## Done

Done 2026-11-10: new `internal/bandwidth` sweeper (every 5 min, wired only with Stripe billing and the payment-method gate on). It reads `store.EgressBytesByWorkspace` (a shared `monthUsageByWorkspace` with build minutes, renamed `MonthUsage`), suspends every service of a card-less workspace past its allowance through `apps.BillingSuspensionWorker` (one worker for Free hours and bandwidth; `suspendForBilling`/`resumeFromBilling`), annotation `app.bex.co/bandwidth-suspended`, Render's `billing` suspender (`AppView.BillingSuspended`). It resumes at the month turn or once the workspace can pay; a manual resume that month is refused with `OUTBOUND_BANDWIDTH_EXHAUSTED` until a payment method is bound (a failed billing read fails closed). An owner's suspension is left alone; a workspace whose plan cannot be read keeps its state (the same guard now fixes `buildminutes`, which lifted the mark on a failed read). Datastores keep running (Render's sentence names services). Tests: `internal/bandwidth` (spin-down, resume on card or month, failed plan read), `apps/bandwidth_test.go`, PG `EgressBytesByWorkspace` and the three notice claims. The dev-6 live check was replaced by these tests: dev-N runs without Stripe, so the sweeper is not wired there, and the local cluster currently has no default StorageClass.
