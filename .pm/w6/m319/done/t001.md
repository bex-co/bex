## Objective

Add Render's included outbound bandwidth per plan (Hobby 5 GB, Pro 25 GB, Scale 1 TB; Enterprise = Scale's until a contract says otherwise) to `pricing.yaml`, and have the estimate charge only `egress_bytes` above it, generalizing w6/m308's included build minutes to a second meter.

## Steps

1. `bandwidth.includedGB` per plan; one allowance resolver per meter.
2. Generalize the estimate's allowance to a per-meter map (build_seconds, egress_bytes); covered lines report `included`.
3. Tests at, below and above the allowance.

## Done

Done 2026-11-10: `pricing.yaml` `bandwidth.includedGB` (Hobby 5, Pro 25, Scale 1,000, Enterprise 1,000); one per-meter allowance (`pricing.Included`, `Sheet.IncludedFor`, `IncludedForWorkspace`, `HasAllowance`) replaces the build-only resolver, and `EstimateIncluding` spends each meter's allowance on its rows with covered lines reporting `included`. The GB is 2^30 bytes (`pricing.BytesPerGiB`), the unit egress is billed and shown in: the parity-sufficient reading of Render's "GB", which includes slightly more, never less, and keeps the allowance, the charge line and the invoice in one unit (ADR030 §14). Tests: `TestIncludedBandwidthFollowsRendersPlans` (4 GB on Hobby $0; 7 GB charges 2 GB with 5 of 7 included); build minutes unchanged.
