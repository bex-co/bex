## Objective

Confirm the change is consistent across REST, GraphQL, MCP and the dashboard, and record where bex deliberately differs from Render.

## Context

- Render spins free **web** services down and wakes them on request. Render has no private-service auto-sleep, so bex's `private_service` sleeping is a bex extension — whatever `t001` decided is a bex product call, and ADR018 should say so rather than leave it unstated.
- Resume (`t003`) is Render-shaped: `resumeService` / `POST …/resume` must stay wire-compatible whatever its new behavior.

## Steps

1. Exercise Resume on an auto-hibernated service through REST, GraphQL and MCP; confirm identical status and message.
2. Confirm the idle-timeout field reads the same on all three surfaces for each service type.
3. Record the private-service auto-sleep decision in `docs/ADR018-render-parity.md`.
4. Re-run this milestone's paired probe — free private service and free worker, both at `idleTTLSeconds: 60` — and paste both outcomes.
