## Objective

A threshold charge and the cycle invoice never bill the same usage, and the mid-cycle invoice changes neither the subscription's billing anchor nor its proration.

## Context

- The cost meter's hourly export (ADR091 D3; `lego/backend/internal/billing/emitter.go` ~121, `stripe.go` ~538).
- `.pm/DO_NOT_DO.md` rejects the ADR071 credit system, so the netting is done by not reporting charged usage to the meter (ADR091 D10.2's fallback), not by a credit.

## Steps

1. Record each paid charge's micro-USD as an export offset; the meter export of later hours subtracts it until it is used up.
2. Create the threshold invoice without touching the subscription (no anchor reset, no proration), and assert the request parameters in a Stripe-stub test.
3. On production (t006), the next cycle invoice preview excludes the charged amount.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Over a cycle, threshold charges plus the cycle invoice's agent-model line equal the month's agent-model spend.
