## Objective

When a workspace's month-to-date agent-model spend reaches its tier's threshold, bex invoices the unbilled agent-model usage and pays it off-session at once. The meter export skips the invoiced amount, and no subscription keeps an agent-model item threshold.

## Context

- ADR091 D10.2a, and D10.2's former fallback (`InvoiceItems` + `Invoices.New` + pay, idempotency key `bex-threshold-{workspace}-{seq}`).
- `lego/backend/internal/billing/threshold.go` (`AgentModelThresholds`, `SyncAgentModelThreshold`, the sweep); `lego/backend/internal/billing/stripe.go` (subscription items; `AgentModelThreshold: thresholds.Want` in `cmd/api/main.go`).
- The meter export (ADR091 D3, near-real-time hourly rows).

## Steps

1. A charger that, at most once per (workspace, sequence), creates the invoice item for the unbilled agent-model amount and a `charge_automatically` invoice, then finalizes and pays it off-session with the default payment method, idempotently.
2. Record the invoiced micro-USD, so the meter export subtracts it and the cycle invoice bills only the rest.
3. Trigger it from the spend path (the mint's spend status crossing the threshold), and from a sweep that catches a crossing the mint missed.
4. Remove the item threshold sync, and clear `billing_thresholds` once on live subscriptions' agent-model items.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A crossing produces exactly one invoice per sequence, even with concurrent mints and replicas.
- [ ] The next cycle invoice preview excludes the charged amount.
