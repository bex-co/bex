## Objective

User decision 2026-11-13: when a workspace reaches the point where it would be stopped, bex charges the card there and then; a successful charge lets the request through without interruption, and only a failed charge interrupts. This replaces the Stripe item threshold (an hourly meter export plus 15 to 28 minutes) and T0's $5 in-flight allowance, which still stopped a workspace with a good card when Stripe was slow.

## Context

- The mint: `lego/backend/internal/agentsession/model.go` (`ModelMinter.Mint`, the `status.Reached()` check at ~378), `spendcap.go` (`ReadSpendStatus`, 20 s spend cache).
- ADR091 D10.2's former fallback: `InvoiceItems` + `Invoices.New` + pay, idempotency key `bex-threshold-{workspace}-{seq}`.
- Stripe client: `lego/backend/internal/billing/stripe.go`, `threshold.go`; trust: `billing/trust.go` (`RecordBillingTrustPayment`, idempotent per invoice id).

## Steps

1. A charge ledger (migration): `agent_model_threshold_charges (workspace_id, seq, invoice_id, amount_microusd, status, hosted_invoice_url, created_at, settled_at)`, unique on (workspace_id, seq), so one charge per crossing across replicas.
2. A charger: create a `charge_automatically` invoice on the workspace's customer and subscription for the unbilled agent-model amount (month-to-date minus this cycle's earlier charges), finalize it, and pay it off-session at once, idempotently.
3. In the mint, when unbilled spend reaches the tier's threshold: run (or join) the charge with a bounded wait (about 15 s). Paid → record the payment in `billing_trust` at once (the webhook stays idempotent), re-derive the tier, serve the request. Declined, `authentication_required`, or Stripe unreachable → refuse with `BILLING_BALANCE_DUE` and the hosted invoice link.
4. Retire T0's in-flight allowance (`pricing.yaml`) and the Stripe item `billing_thresholds` (the sweep brings every item to none), so only one mechanism charges.
5. Amend ADR091 D10.2a to this mechanism.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A session crossing the threshold with a good card is never refused; the crossing produces exactly one paid invoice, even with concurrent mints on both replicas.
- [ ] A declined card is refused at once with `BILLING_BALANCE_DUE` and a payment link.
