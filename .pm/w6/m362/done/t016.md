## Objective

A threshold charge never collects agent-model usage that a subscription invoice already billed. Today `unbilled` is the calendar month's spend minus the month's paid charges (`store.AgentSpendInputs`, `agentsession.SpendStatus.UnbilledMicroUSD`). A subscription invoice that bills the meter mid-month is not subtracted. That is a cycle invoice whenever the billing anchor is not the 1st, or a `subscription_threshold` invoice from the retired mechanism. So the next charge collects the same usage again. The negative net item then lands on a later invoice, and the money returns only as customer balance, not to the card.

## Found on production (t006, 2026-11-13)

The bex workspace's subscription is anchored on the 16th.

1. At 08:39:52, Stripe's `subscription_threshold` invoice `in_1UbG1R…` ($172.00, paid by credits) billed the meter to that point. The meter included about $10 of agent-model usage. That invoice came from t003's item threshold, which was still in place until the 09:46 deploy.
2. At 09:50:32 bex charged the card $10.50 for the month's "unbilled" spend, the same usage.
3. The cycle preview now reads −$10.29: the $0.21 metered since 08:39 minus the $10.50 net item. On 2026-11-16 that becomes customer balance.

## Steps

1. Record where the meter was last billed. Add a column, `billing_provider_mappings.agent_model_billed_until timestamptz` (it only moves forward, `GREATEST`). It is set to `period_end` of each bex subscription invoice (not a charge invoice) whose `billing_reason` bills the meter: `subscription_cycle`, `subscription_threshold`, `subscription_cancel`, or `subscription_update`.
   - Set it from `invoice.created` and `invoice.paid` (`billing.Trust.HandleStripeEvent`).
   - Also set it from the charger. Before a charge starts, the charger lists the Subscription's latest invoices, so a webhook that is late or not enabled cannot cause a double collection.
2. The charge window is `[max(month start, agent_model_billed_until), now)`.
   - `unbilled` is the agent-model spend in hourly rows that start inside the window, minus the paid charges created inside it.
   - Hours that started before the boundary count as billed. At worst a charge collects less, and the meter bills the rest; a charge never collects twice.
   - The workspace's monthly cap still compares the calendar month's spend.
3. T0's ceiling bounds the same window's unbilled spend, not the month's. A workspace whose cycle invoice billed part of the month is never stopped at $10 without first being charged (the user's rule, ADR091 D10.2a).
4. The charger re-reads the window after refreshing the boundary. If the crossing is no longer due, it returns a "not due" outcome and the mint serves the request.
5. Record the window in ADR091 D10.2a. Also record the bex workspace's one-off $10.50 overlap with the retired item threshold, which comes back as customer balance on its 2026-11-16 cycle invoice.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A real-DB test: after a cycle invoice whose `period_end` falls mid-month, `AgentSpendInputs` counts only the spend and paid charges from that boundary on. The month's spend is unchanged.
- [x] A test: a charge whose boundary refresh moves the window past the crossing makes no charge and serves the request.
- [x] A test: a T0 workspace with $6 billed by a cycle invoice and $4 since is not refused at $10 of month-to-date spend.
- [x] Trust records the boundary from `invoice.created` and `invoice.paid` for cycle and threshold invoices, never from a charge invoice.

## Done 2026-11-13

- **Boundary.** Migration 0249 adds `billing_provider_mappings.agent_model_billed_until`. `store.RecordAgentModelBilledUntil` moves it only forward. `billing.meterBilledUntil` reads it from a bex subscription invoice's `period_end` (cycle, threshold or update); a charge invoice never sets it.
- **Who sets it.** `Trust.recordMeterBilled` records it on `invoice.created` and `invoice.paid`. `AgentModelCharger.ChargeThreshold` first reads the Subscription's latest invoices (`StripeClient.AgentModelBilledUntil`). If that moves the boundary it answers `ChargeNotDue`, and the mint re-reads once and charges the narrower window if it is still due.
- **Spend inputs.** `store.AgentSpendInputs` returns `UnbilledFrom`, `WindowSpendMicroUSD` (the hourly half of the shared usage-sum builder, from the boundary) and the paid charges since the boundary.
- **Caps.** `SpendStatus.Reached` holds month-to-date against the workspace's own cap and T0's ceiling against unbilled spend.
- **Tests.** `TestAgentSpendInputsChargeWindow` (real DB), `TestAgentModelChargeNotDueWhenAnInvoiceBilledTheMeter`, `TestMeterBilledUntil`, `TestTrustRecordsWhereTheMeterWasBilled`, `TestSpendWindowExcludesWhatAnInvoiceBilled` and `TestMintRereadsWhenTheChargeIsNotDue`.
- **Docs.** ADR091 D10.2a § The charge window and the Stripe runbook.
- **Left to t007.** The dashboard composer still works out "reached" as month-to-date ≥ cap (`dashboard/src/features/agent-environment/lib/spend.ts`). A T0 workspace whose cycle invoice billed part of the month could be blocked there while the mint would serve it.
- **Efficiency follow-ups noted by /simplify, not taken.** A request joining an in-flight charge also pays the Stripe window read (rare: only within a charge's seconds). With a boundary set, the window sum scans a subset of the month's hours a second time (the mint's read is cached 20 s).
