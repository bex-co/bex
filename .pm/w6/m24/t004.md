## Objective

Linking/unlinking a group to a service must verify the group belongs to the service's workspace — else a caller injects another workspace's env group (and its secret values) into their own service, a write-side variant of the read leak.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/envgroups/service.go` (link/unlink verbs)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Linking a foreign-workspace group is refused; same-workspace link unchanged; test proves it.
