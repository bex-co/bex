## Objective

A small binary in the operator image that serves t001's answer for the pods of its own node, deployed as a DaemonSet on every node that runs tenant App pods, with `hostPID` as its only privilege and the operator as its only client.

## Context

- Entry points live in `lego/operator/cmd/` and ship in the one operator image (`lego/Dockerfile`). `cmd/egress-meter` is the nearest shape: a node agent with `/healthz` and `/readyz`.
- Placement: tenant App pods run on the untainted `tenant-0` pool, the serving pools (taint `bex.co/serving-pool`) and the sandbox pool (taint `bex.co/sandbox`). `config/egress-meter/daemonset.yaml` already tolerates exactly that set; copy it, and keep both in step. On production that is 8 nodes today (7 `tenant-0` and 1 sandbox). The platform, control-plane and build-only (`burst`, `lg`) nodes run no tenant App.
- `bex-system` enforces PodSecurity `privileged`, so the per-pod securityContext is the only control: `scripts/lib/platform-pod-hardening-guard.yq` (via `scripts/gitops-validate.sh`) requires pod `runAsNonRoot: true` and seccomp `RuntimeDefault`, and per container `allowPrivilegeEscalation: false`, `readOnlyRootFilesystem: true`, `drop: [ALL]`. The reader passes it with `hostPID` added.
- New components are registered in `scripts/image-pin-validate.sh` (the `controller:latest` allowlist row, like egress-meter's) and `config/default/kustomization.yaml`. A DaemonSet on the `controller` image rolls on every platform pin; while a node's reader rolls, t003 must not fall back to a sweep. Consider its own pin held while the binary is unchanged, as w6/m470 gave the SNI proxies (`deploy/gitops/base/bex.yaml`, `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` `operator_pins`), and write the choice here.
- Existing ingress rules in `bex-system` are NetworkPolicies, for example `allow-apiserver-to-operator-webhooks` (selects `control-plane: controller-manager`).

## Steps

1. `cmd/listener-reader` (name it as you see fit): `GET /v1/listeners?pod=<uid>&ip=<ip>` answers `{"ports":[…]}`; 404 when no process of that pod is on this node, 409 when its namespace does not hold that IP, 400 for a malformed UID or IP. Nothing else is served besides health. Bounded request time, `ReadHeaderTimeout`, no request body, a small concurrency cap.
2. `config/listener-reader/`: DaemonSet (`hostPID: true`, no `hostNetwork`, no hostPath, `automountServiceAccountToken: false`, uid 65532, `drop: [ALL]`, read-only root, seccomp `RuntimeDefault`, small requests and limits, `priorityClassName` as egress-meter, its tolerations), its ServiceAccount with no RBAC, and a NetworkPolicy that admits only the operator pod (`control-plane: controller-manager`) on its port. Add it to `config/default`.
3. `scripts/gitops-validate.sh`: pin that the reader has `hostPID` and no other host namespace, no capability, no hostPath, no token, and its NetworkPolicy. Register it in `scripts/image-pin-validate.sh`.
4. Unit-test the handler over a fake procfs (t001's fixtures): each status code, and that the answer carries ports only.
