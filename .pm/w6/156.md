# The operator's port sweeps fill the Cilium conntrack table on its node, so idle connections of every pod there are reset

Why: every pod on the operator's node shares that node's 131,072-entry TCP conntrack table, and the operator's port sweeps keep filling it. That includes bex-api, bex-db, Kratos, Hydra, OpenFGA, the SSH gateway and Argo CD. Once full, an idle pooled connection to a Service with more than one backend is reset at its next use, which is how the gateway's signed calls failed in w6/153.

- **Filed by** [w6/153](blocked/153.md) (2026-11-20), which made the gateway replay such calls on a new connection. The table filling up is the cause for every other client on the node.
- **Evidence (production, about 12:50Z, `cilium-dbg` in the Cilium agents):**
  - On `bex-platform-k9wb4-dpjxh`, the operator's node, `cilium_bpf_map_pressure{map_name="ct4_global"}` read 0.997. `cilium-dbg bpf ct list global` held 132,140 entries, 130,065 of them TCP connects from `bex-controller-manager` (`10.244.43.179`) to three tenant pods, one port each: 65,535, 60,598 and 3,932. On `jfc55`, which has no operator, it read 0.014.
  - From 01Z to 13Z the `dpjxh` agent logged 13 to 26 conntrack GC passes an hour that deleted at least half the table (`Conntrack garbage collector interval recalculated … deleteRatio=0.76…`). The `jfc55` agent logged none.
  - INGRESS `Policy denied` drops stood at 273,893 on `dpjxh` and 932 on `jfc55`.
  - The sampled sweep entries had a reset back and a 10 s close timeout. A closed tenant port that drops the SYN instead (w8/259) keeps its entry for the 60 s SYN timeout.
- **Mechanism.** Cilium's conntrack maps are LRU, so a full table evicts the entries of idle connections. That includes the `SVC` entry that pins a connection to one Service backend. The next segment picks a backend again and can reach a pod that resets it. A Service with one backend (the Postgres primary) picks the same pod again, so the reset hits Services with several backends.
- **Where.** `lego/operator/internal/portscan/portscan.go` connects to ports 1–65535 with 512 workers. It is called by:
  - `discoverPrivatePorts` (`internal/controller/private_ports.go`): every web and private service is rescanned every 2–16 min, two rescans at once, plus each new revision's first sweep;
  - port detection (`internal/controller/port_detect.go`): every 15 s per App with an unready pod.
- **Directions** (pick one; measure the table after):
  1. Read listeners without connecting, for example from the pod's `/proc/net/tcp{,6}` on its node. That keeps "binds the pod address, not loopback", which the connect scan exists to answer.
  2. Pace the sweep so its live entries stay under a budget, for example a tenth of the table. Check that against `portDetectTimeout` and how fast Render detects a port.
  3. As a stopgap only, raise Cilium's `bpf-ct-global-tcp-max`. That restarts the agents and recreates the maps.
- **Done when:** on the operator's node, no Cilium conntrack GC pass deletes half the table across a day, and `ct4_global` pressure stays below 0.5 when sampled. Private-port discovery and port detection must still find listeners as before: their tests, plus one live private service and one web service that binds another port.
