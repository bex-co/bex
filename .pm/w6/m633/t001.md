## Objective

Amend ADR004 with the rule every RollingUpdate App Deployment follows: `maxUnavailable: 0`, `maxSurge: 25%`. Record Render's sources, why `maxSurge` stays at 25%, and the two strategies that stay as they are.

## Context

- ADR004 reasons only about `replicas: 1` ("maxUnavailable: 25% → 0").
- render.com/docs/deploys: "your original instance continues to receive all incoming traffic while the new instance is spinning up"; "For services that are scaled to multiple instances, Render performs steps 2-5 for one instance at a time".
- render.com/docs/health-checks: "If all new instances pass at the same time, the deploy succeeds and traffic moves to them". The two pages disagree on the surge, and agree that old instances keep serving.
- `DeployGateTimeout` = `RolloutBudget` + 3 min, measured from the rollout phase's start (`lego/types/deploybudgets/deploybudgets.go`, `lego/backend/internal/store/reconciler.go` `deployTimedOut`).
- These stay: a disk-bearing service's `Recreate` (`disk.go` `applyDiskProjection`) and a held web-image release's `100%`/`0` (`heldReleaseStrategy`, w4/571).

## Steps

1. Read both Render pages again and quote them with the read date.
2. Write the amendment in ADR004's rollout section, beside the w4/571 and disk (`Recreate`) entries.
