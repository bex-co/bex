## Objective

`queryBuildSeconds` counts a Job only once it has a `completionTime`, which Kubernetes sets on success alone, so a failed build meters nothing. Render counts every pipeline task. Use the `Failed` condition's transition time as the end of a failed Job.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/usage/service.go:1576-1609` `queryBuildSeconds` (`ct == nil` → skipped)
- docs/ADR023 § `build_seconds` against Render's pipeline minutes

## Steps

1. For a Job without `completionTime`, take the `Failed` condition (status True) `lastTransitionTime` as its end; still skip running Jobs.
2. Keep the platform-rebuild exemption (w1/m184).
3. Unit-test succeeded, failed, running and exempt Jobs.
