## Objective

A pooled database runs one PgBouncer per replica, pointed at that replica's own reader, with Render's settings and a pool of `max_connections` − 10, in place of the shared `<id>-pooler-ro`.

## Context

- `reconcilePooler` (`database_controller.go:1416`) and `upsertPooler` (`:1460`) run the shared `ro` Pooler. `pgbouncerParameters` (`:738`) holds Render's five settings with `tiers.PostgresTier.PoolerPoolSize`. `poolerTemplate` and `pgbouncerRunCommand` (m458 t003, t010) set resources and the open-file limit.
- m458's evidence: a plan change rewrites the pool with `RELOAD` and rolls no Pod; at saturation, two poolers' `auth_query` sessions took tenant slots (m458 README, Production check).
- The mechanism comes from t001: a CNPG `Pooler` on a replica cluster, or a Deployment bex owns.

## Steps

1. One PgBouncer per replica, named as t001 decided, with `pgbouncerParameters`, the same template and command, `bex-datastore`, and the NetworkPolicy reach the primary's pooler has.
2. Each replica's `poolerHost` is its own pooler. Remove the shared `<id>-pooler-ro` when the per-replica poolers are ready.
3. A plan change rewrites every replica's pool without rolling a Pod. Turning pooling off or removing a replica removes its pooler and leaves the others' clients connected.
