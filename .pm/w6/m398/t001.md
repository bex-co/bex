## Objective

While a turn runs, the in-pod driver sends the transcript parts it has completed to bex-api about every 30 seconds, so a sandbox that dies mid-turn leaves everything up to its last flush.

## Context

- The driver writes each part to `/var/log/bex-agent/session.jsonl` (`lego/agent-image/driver/src/config.ts` ~310, `session.ts` ~158). The Completer harvests that file over pods/exec only when the turn ends or the sandbox is reaped (`internal/agentsessions/completion.go` `captureTranscript` ~825, ADR051).
- On production every transcript row of a turn carries one `created_at`, the harvest's. Session `ags-dbrhfr0k58is73auo6a0` (`rt-flatten-abstractions`, 2026-11-13) was OOM-killed after 1 h 25 min and 371 model requests, and kept 0 transcript rows and 0 tool outputs. In 30 days, 9 failed sessions with model spend kept no transcript at all.
- The harvest already merges idempotently by turn-local item index, with the same coalescer as the gateway's live tee (`agenttranscript`).

## Steps

1. Add a driver-to-gateway flush. Bound it (the cumulative quota, `transcriptQuota`), give it a fixed interval, and send only completed items since the last acknowledged index.
2. bex-api stores flushed items through the same merge the harvest uses, so a later harvest only appends what is missing.
3. A failed flush is retried on the next interval and never blocks the turn.

## Files

- `lego/agent-image/driver/src/` (session log writer, gateway client)
- `lego/backend/internal/sshgateway/` (the flush verb), `internal/agentsessions/`, `internal/store/agenttranscripts.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A running turn's transcript grows in `agent_session_transcripts` while it runs, at most about 30 s behind.
- [ ] A later harvest of the same turn neither duplicates nor reorders flushed items.

## Out of scope

- Streaming every token: completed parts only.
