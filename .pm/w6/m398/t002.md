## Objective

When a sandbox dies (OOM, eviction, node loss), the Completer keeps the flushed parts, marks the turn's transcript truncated with the failure reason, and its final harvest only appends what is missing when the pod can still be read.

## Context

- `markTurnTranscript` and the truncation reasons in `internal/agentsessions/completion.go` (~805-890). The OOM-killed turn today reads `transcript_truncated=true` with reason `the sandbox ran out of memory (exit 128)` and no rows.

## Steps

1. Keep the existing truncation reason, now on a transcript that holds the flushed parts.
2. Try one harvest before reclaiming a failed sandbox whose pod still exists. Store what it adds.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A turn whose sandbox is killed mid-turn keeps its flushed parts and reads truncated with the failure reason.
