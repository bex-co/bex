## Objective

An image-port private service under the **strict** policy picks a primary port its process can actually bind, publishes the right set of ports, and starts healthy. When nothing answers on the primary, the failed deploy names the ports bex expected, so the user isn't left with a generic probe line.

## Context

- `resolveImageNetwork` (`lego/operator/internal/controller/image_network.go:35`; primary choice `:72`) and `privateTCPPorts` (`lego/operator/internal/registry/image_config.go:178`).
- `privateServiceProjection` (`:101`) and `promoteImageNetwork` (`:123`): Services bind to the serving revision.
- The readiness TCP probe is on the primary (`deployment_projection.go:344`). `appEnv` injects `PORT` = primary (`app_controller.go:4890`). The probe-failure wording is `a TCP connect to port <n>` (`app_controller.go:5353`), carried into `stallReason`/`failureReason` by the w6/m147 path.
- Render wording to mirror in spirit: "Port scan timeout reached, no open ports detected. Bind your service to at least one port."

## Steps

1. Apply t001's primary rule, including the < 1024 handling, in `resolveImageNetwork`, and keep the digest binding unchanged.
2. Confirm the strict policy projects for an image-port app. The Deployment, pre-deploy and cron pod specs must add no capabilities when `containerPolicy` is strict, even when `portMode` is `image-v1` (`container_policy.go`).
3. When the primary's TCP readiness never passes, extend the diagnosis to name the expected primary and the declared set. For example: `nothing accepted a TCP connection on port 8123; the image declares 8123, 9000; bind your server to 0.0.0.0 on one of them or set the service port`. Keep exit-code diagnoses first, as today.
4. Record a metadata-read failure as an observable deploy failure, never as a guessed 3000 (ADR089 D3).
