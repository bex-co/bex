## Objective

Every pooler runs Render's settings, and its pool follows the database's plan.

## Context

- Render: `max_client_conn` 30000; `max_db_connections` / `default_pool_size` "`max_connections` minus 10"; `client_idle_timeout` 86400; `server_login_retry` 2 (render.com/docs/postgresql-connection-pooling).
- w6/134 puts each plan's `max_connections` in `lego/types/tiers`.

## Steps

1. Pass the plan to `poolerSpec` and set `pgbouncer.parameters`: `max_client_conn` "30000", `default_pool_size` and `max_db_connections` = the plan's `max_connections` − 10, `client_idle_timeout` "86400", `server_login_retry` "2".
2. A plan change rewrites the parameters; confirm on dev-6 that CNPG reloads PgBouncer without dropping connected clients.
3. Tests for each plan's parameters.
