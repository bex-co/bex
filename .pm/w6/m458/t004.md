## Objective

Turning on pooling pools a database's read replicas too, in the form Render exposes.

## Context

- Render: "Render automatically enables it for all of that database's read replicas", and "Each read replica has its own independent connection pool."
- bex replica endpoints: `<db>-ro-<replica>` resolves to the CNPG `-ro` Service (`dbRouter` in `lego/operator/cmd/pg-sni-proxy/main.go`); connection info builds per-replica strings in `lego/backend/internal/postgres/service.go`. bex runs one `type: rw` Pooler and none for replicas.

## Steps

1. Read Render's contract for a replica's pooled connection: the pinned OpenAPI (`lego/backend/internal/api/openapi/render-public-api-1.json`, the Postgres connection-info and read-replica schemas) and render.com/docs/postgresql-read-replicas. Record what Render returns in the README.
2. Implement bex's form of it: a CNPG Pooler of `type: ro`, or one per replica if Render addresses each replica's pool, sized per replica as t002 sizes the primary's, with pooled replica strings where Render has them, internal and external (the SNI proxy's `-pool` naming).
3. Tests, and a dev-6 check that a pooled replica connection reads replica data.
