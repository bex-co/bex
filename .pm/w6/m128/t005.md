## Objective

Pin the closed pair on the cancel path, and pin the neighbours that are correct today and that this change could break.

## Context

- The defect survived because no test cancelled a deploy mid-build and then asserted on the resulting fact set. That fixture shape is what the tests need: a deploy observed in `build_in_progress`, then closed by the **verb** rather than by the reconciler.
- The four neighbours worth covering are the ones the milestone's adjacent-classes list names: canceled-while-queued, canceled-after-build, canceled-by-supersede, and image-backed.
- Assert on the fact **set** for a deploy, not on a single row — the defect is a missing member, which a single-row assertion cannot see.

## Steps

1. Cancel while `build_in_progress` → assert exactly one `build_started` and one `build_ended` with a canceled outcome.
2. Cancel while queued → assert neither fact.
3. Cancel after the build finished → assert `build_ended` with a succeeded outcome.
4. Supersede-cancel → assert the existing reconciler path still emits its pair.
5. Successful and build-failed deploys → assert exactly one `build_ended` each, unchanged.
6. Image-backed deploy → assert neither fact.
7. Assert re-running the reconciler after a cancel does not double-insert.
