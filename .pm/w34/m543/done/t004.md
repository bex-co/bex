## Objective

Confirm, and fix where needed, that the operations which restart or recreate a database's instances keep its slots, publications and the role's attributes, and that the subscriber resumes from its position.

## Context

- Suspend is CNPG hibernation (the PVC stays); a plan change and a storage resize restart or roll the instances ([ADR009](../../../../docs/ADR009-postgresql-management.md)).
- While a publisher is suspended its subscriber's connection fails; the slot holds its position and the WAL bound (m541/t005) still applies once it resumes.

## Steps

1. On dev-N, with a subscriber streaming, run each operation and compare rows afterwards.
2. Fix any that loses the slot, the publication or the attribute; record the results in ADR009.

## Files

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/database_controller.go` (only if a fix is needed)
- `docs/ADR009-postgresql-management.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each operation keeps the slot and publication and the subscriber resumes with no lost or duplicated row.

## Out of scope

- Failover (t001) and upgrades (t003).

## Done (2026-11-21)

- **No code change needed.** A slot lives in `pg_replslot` on the volume and none of these operations replaces the primary's volume: hibernation keeps the PVC; a restart or a plan change recreates the primary's Pod on the same volume, with CNPG's default `primaryUpdateMethod: restart`, which bex keeps (so no switchover); a storage resize grows the PVC. CNPG's own slot synchronization (`synchronizeReplicas`) touches physical slots only (`slots/infrastructure/postgresmanager.go`). The local pg_upgrade drill also restarted a 17 publisher mid-stream twice, and its subscriber resumed with every row exactly once. Recorded in ADR009 § Lifecycle › Restarts, suspend, plan changes and storage resizes.
