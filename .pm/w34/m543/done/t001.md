## Objective

For an enabled HA database on PostgreSQL 17 or later, the operator turns on CNPG's logical decoding slot synchronization, so a slot created with `failover = true` exists on the standby and continues after promotion.

## Context

- CNPG v1.30.1 `docs/src/replication.md`: `replicationSlots.highAvailability.synchronizeLogicalDecoding: true`, plus `hot_standby_feedback: 'on'` and `sync_replication_slots: 'on'` under `postgresql.parameters`; CNPG manages `synchronized_standby_slots`.
- HA is `Database.spec.highAvailability` (`lego/types/v1alpha1/database_types.go:280`); the projection is in `lego/operator/internal/controller/database_controller.go`.
- `hot_standby` and `synchronous_standby_names` are already operator-managed (`lego/backend/internal/postgres/service.go:1654`); add the two new names.

## Steps

1. Set the CNPG flag and both parameters only while the database is enabled, HA and on 17+.
2. Add `hot_standby_feedback` and `sync_replication_slots` to `operatorManagedParameters`.
3. Say in ADR009 and the runbook that subscribers create their subscription (or slot) with `failover = true`.

## Files

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/database_controller.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/postgres/service.go`
- `docs/ADR009-postgresql-management.md`, `docs/runbooks/postgres-logical-replication.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On dev-N, `SELECT slot_name, synced FROM pg_replication_slots` on the standby lists the subscriber's slot as synced.
- [ ] A database that is not enabled, not HA or on 16 or earlier is unchanged.

## Out of scope

- PostgreSQL 13–16 (t002).

## Done (2026-11-21)

- **Projection:** an HA publisher whose cluster runs PostgreSQL 17 or later gets `replicationSlots.highAvailability.synchronizeLogicalDecoding: true` with `hot_standby_feedback` and `sync_replication_slots` on (`syncsLogicalSlots`, `logicalSlotSync` in `database_publication.go`). The running major counts first, as for `pg_create_subscription`, so a cluster upgrading from 16 waits until it runs 17; a legacy declared value of either setting gives way in any spelling; switching it off withdraws the stanza for CNPG to default. Both names are operator-managed in bex-api (`service.go`). Checked against CNPG v1.30.1's source: the webhook refuses the flag below 17 without `pg_failover_slots` and requires both settings, and `primary_conninfo` carries `dbname`, which the slot sync worker needs.
- **Docs:** ADR009 § Lifecycle › Failover and switchover and the runbook say subscribers create their subscription `WITH (failover = true)` (or the slot with `pg_create_logical_replication_slot(…, failover => true)`), and that such subscriptions wait while the standby is down (`synchronized_standby_slots`).
- **Left:** the standby-side check (`synced` in `pg_replication_slots`) needs a real HA cluster: t006, on production.
