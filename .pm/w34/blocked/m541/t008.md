## Objective

Walk definition-of-done items 1–5 on a dev stack before production: a real PostgreSQL 18 outside the cluster subscribes to an enabled bex database through its external URL and receives every change.

## Context

- `scripts/dev-env.sh <N> up` plus the operator from the tree (`bash scripts/mock-cluster.sh operator`).
- The workspace must be on Pro: `changeWorkspacePlan` on GraphQL, as the Terraform grading did ([terraform-compatibility-checklist.md](../../../../docs/terraform-compatibility-checklist.md)).
- The subscriber is a plain `postgres:18` container on the host; its tables must exist before the subscription (logical replication does not copy DDL).

## Steps

1. Create a 10 GB paid Postgres with public access and a dedicated credential; enable it through the verb with that credential, one schema and `allTables: true`.
2. On the subscriber, `CREATE SUBSCRIPTION` to the `FOR ALL TABLES` publication; insert, update and delete on the publisher; compare.
3. As the default user, `CREATE PUBLICATION p2 FOR TABLE t`; subscribe a second time; repeat the comparison.
4. Try the verb against a Hobby workspace and a 5 GB database; confirm the named refusals and that nothing was written.
5. Disable; confirm `rolreplication` is false, the all-tables publication is gone and the subscriber's reconnect is refused.
6. Record the conclusions in the milestone README; tear down.

## Status (2026-11-21): runs on production, with t012

No cluster can run this here: w34 has no `dev-N` (`scripts/dev-env.sh` takes N 1–10) and the shared mock cluster cannot start workers on this host (`fs.inotify.max_user_instances` 128, the w33/005 gate, which only the machine's owner can raise). What a cluster adds over the real-PostgreSQL tests is CloudNativePG applying the projection (`replication: true`, the `Publication`, `max_slot_wal_keep_size`) and `pg-sni-proxy` carrying a replication connection, and production is the first place both can be seen. So this walk runs on production in `bex-canary`, as the first half of t012, once the production pin carries this milestone's commits.

- The workspace goes to Pro for the walk and back to Hobby afterwards, under the user's standing permission for `bex-canary` (2026-10-31, recorded in [w4/done/295](../../../w4/done/295.md): switch it to a paid plan, operate there, clean up); the database and subscriber fixtures are deleted in the same run.
- Enable with the verb inside the production bex-api pod (`kubectl -n bex-system exec deploy/bex-api -- /api postgres-logical-replication …`, the runbook), using a dedicated credential from `POST /v1/postgres/{id}/users`, one schema and `--all-tables`.
- Also confirm on the instance: `SELECT rolreplication FROM pg_roles` for that credential (t003), `SELECT puballtables FROM pg_publication` (t004) and `SHOW max_slot_wal_keep_size` reading `2GB` (t005), each before and after the disable.
- Already shown off-cluster (2026-11-21): the SQL contract on PostgreSQL 17 and 18 with a real subscription (`logical_replication_pg_test.go`), and an external PostgreSQL 18 subscriber over TLS on a local pair (ADR009 § Logical replication, Verification).
