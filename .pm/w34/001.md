# 001 — Capture Render's answers where its logical-replication docs are silent, on a Pro workspace

Why: w34/m541–m543 have to choose behaviors render.com/docs/postgresql-logical-replication does not describe (the role's attributes, the retained-WAL bound, supported majors, failover, upgrades), and ADR018 may only keep a divergence the user signed off, so each choice needs a capture or a decision.

Render's page (read 2026-11-20) gives the prerequisites (a Pro workspace, at least 10 GB), the request inputs (database ids, the subscriber role, schemas, all tables or not) and the privilege split (`FOR TABLE` by the user, `FOR ALL TABLES` by support), and nothing else. In a Render workspace on Pro, with a 10 GB Postgres enabled through Render support as a publisher and a second as a subscriber, capture:

- `SELECT rolname, rolreplication FROM pg_roles` for the named role, and `pg_has_role(<role>, 'pg_create_subscription', 'member')` on the subscriber;
- `SHOW max_slot_wal_keep_size`, `max_replication_slots`, `max_wal_senders`, `wal_level`, before and after enablement;
- which PostgreSQL majors Render accepts for either side;
- on an HA instance: `SHOW sync_replication_slots`, and whether a slot appears on the standby;
- whether a major-version upgrade keeps a slot, and what Render does when one lags;
- whether the schemas named in the request were granted to the role (`has_schema_privilege`);
- anything the dashboard shows for an enabled database, and how long support took.

Write it to `docs/render-artifacts/postgres-logical-replication.md`. For each answer that differs from what m541–m543 shipped, file a fix or record the divergence in ADR018 with the user's sign-off; for each that matches, replace "bex's choice" in ADR009 § Logical replication with the capture's citation.

End state: the artifact exists and ADR009 § Logical replication calls no rule above an uncaptured choice. Needs a Render workspace on Pro (none is in `.env.example`; the same gate as [w10/blocked/002](../w10/blocked/002.md)), so it starts in `blocked/` if none exists. ~1h with the workspace, plus Render support's turnaround.
