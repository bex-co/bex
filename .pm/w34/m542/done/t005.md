## Objective

A subscription keeps working across the restarts bex causes (plan change, suspend and resume, storage resize), and disabling a subscriber that still owns subscriptions does not leave them orphaned.

## Context

- Suspend maps to CNPG hibernation, which keeps the PVC ([ADR009](../../../docs/ADR009-postgresql-management.md) § suspend / resume); a subscription's state lives in the database, so it should resume, but the slot on the publisher keeps WAL meanwhile (bounded by m541/t005 when the publisher is bex).
- Removing `pg_create_subscription` does not drop existing subscriptions, and their owner then cannot alter them.

## Steps

1. On dev-N, suspend and resume a subscriber mid-stream, then change its plan; confirm the apply worker resumes with no lost or duplicated rows.
2. Make the verb refuse disable while `pg_subscription` lists subscriptions owned by the role (`LOGICAL_REPLICATION_SUBSCRIPTIONS_EXIST`, naming them), and say in the runbook to `DROP SUBSCRIPTION` first.

## Done (2026-11-21)

- **Disable refusal:** `LOGICAL_REPLICATION_SUBSCRIPTIONS_EXIST` names each `<database>.<subscription>` the subscriber role owns (`pg_subscription`, readable by every role apart from its connection string), for a disable and for naming another subscriber role, before anything is written. The premise needed a correction, recorded in ADR009: on PostgreSQL 17 a subscription whose owner loses `pg_create_subscription` keeps applying, and the owner can still disable, alter and drop it, only not create another. The refusal stands because such a subscription would keep the database receiving changes bex reports as not enabled.
- **Restarts:** a local PostgreSQL 17 subscriber stopped while its PostgreSQL 18 publisher took 1,000 inserts came back with every row exactly once (counts and sums equal). CNPG's suspend and resume and a plan change are that restart on a real cluster; that replay moves to t006, on production, since no cluster runs here.
