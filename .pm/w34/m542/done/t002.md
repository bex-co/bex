## Objective

While a database is enabled as a subscriber, its named role is a member of `pg_create_subscription` (16+), so `CREATE SUBSCRIPTION` succeeds as that role; when the field is cleared the membership is removed.

## Context

- Role memberships are projected through CNPG managed roles' `inRoles` (`lego/operator/internal/controller/database_controller.go:430`–`:452`).
- For 13–15, implement t001's decision (an owned CNPG `Subscription` from stored inputs, with its connection password in a Secret the operator owns, and `subscriptions` added to the RBAC marker at `database_controller.go:876`; or a refusal in the verb).

## Steps

1. Add `SubscriberRole string` beside m541's fields; regenerate the CRD.
2. Project `inRoles: [pg_create_subscription]` for that role on 16+, only while set.
3. Implement the 13–15 path as decided.

## Done (2026-11-21)

- `spec.logicalReplication.subscriberRole` and `subscriptions[]` in `lego/types` (CRD regenerated; `publisherRole` is now optional, with CEL rules that something is named and that schemas and `allTables` need a publisher). `Publishes()` gates the publisher projection, so a subscriber alone gets no `REPLICATION` and no WAL bound.
- `markSubscriberRole` (`database_subscription.go`) adds `pg_create_subscription` to the role's `inRoles` only while named and only when the cluster runs 16 or later, the running major first (a cluster still upgrading from 15 lacks the role and CNPG would fail every managed role).
- The 13–15 path: each subscription is an `externalClusters` entry (password from its Secret, written by CNPG to a passfile) and an owned CloudNativePG `Subscription` `<id>-sub-<hash>`, reclaim policy `delete`; the `LogicalReplication` condition folds their verdicts with the publication's. RBAC (`subscriptions` in the marker, `role.yaml`, the GitOps read role and the operator's namespace admission policy) and the v1.30.1 Subscription CRD in envtest's testdata.
- `CREATE SUBSCRIPTION` succeeding while enabled and refused after disable is shown on real PostgreSQL with the operator played by a superuser loop (`logical_subscription_pg_test.go`); the check on a real CloudNativePG moves to t006, on production.
