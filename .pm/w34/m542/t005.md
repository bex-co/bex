## Objective

A subscription keeps working across the restarts bex causes (plan change, suspend and resume, storage resize), and disabling a subscriber that still owns subscriptions does not leave them orphaned.

## Context

- Suspend maps to CNPG hibernation, which keeps the PVC ([ADR009](../../../docs/ADR009-postgresql-management.md) § suspend / resume); a subscription's state lives in the database, so it should resume, but the slot on the publisher keeps WAL meanwhile (bounded by m541/t005 when the publisher is bex).
- Removing `pg_create_subscription` does not drop existing subscriptions, and their owner then cannot alter them.

## Steps

1. On dev-N, suspend and resume a subscriber mid-stream, then change its plan; confirm the apply worker resumes with no lost or duplicated rows.
2. Make the verb refuse disable while `pg_subscription` lists subscriptions owned by the role (`LOGICAL_REPLICATION_SUBSCRIPTIONS_EXIST`, naming them), and say in the runbook to `DROP SUBSCRIPTION` first.
