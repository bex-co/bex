## Objective

While `spec.logicalReplication` names a publisher role, the operator projects that role with CNPG's `replication: true`, so a subscriber connecting as it can start a walsender. When the field is removed, the attribute goes back off.

## Context

- Managed roles are projected onto CNPG `spec.managed.roles` in `lego/operator/internal/controller/database_controller.go` (`:358`, `:666`). The comment at `:384` notes CNPG undoes a manual `ALTER ROLE`, so the attribute must come from the projection.
- The default owner role is bootstrapped by CNPG `initdb`, not by `managed.roles`; decide in this task how it gets the attribute (a managed-role entry for the owner, or refuse the owner as publisher role and require a dedicated credential, which is what Render recommends). Record the choice in ADR009.

## Steps

1. Add `replication: true` to the named role's projection while enabled; never to any other role.
2. Handle the owner-as-publisher case as decided.
3. Remove the attribute when the field is cleared or names another role.
