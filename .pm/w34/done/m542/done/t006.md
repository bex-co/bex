## Objective

Walk definition-of-done items 1–4 on a dev stack: a bex subscriber follows a bex publisher in another workspace, and follows a PostgreSQL outside bex through the move-onto-bex path (initial copy, streaming, lag 0, cutover).

## Context

- Both workspaces on Pro (`changeWorkspacePlan`); the publisher enabled by m541's verb.
- The outside publisher is a `postgres:18` container on the host with `wal_level=logical` and a reachable address.
- The subscriber's tables must exist first; sequences are copied by hand at cutover.

## Steps

1. bex→bex: create the tables on the subscriber, `CREATE SUBSCRIPTION` to the publisher's external URL, write on the publisher, compare.
2. Outside→bex: same, then stop writes, wait for lag 0 (`pg_stat_subscription`), copy sequences, compare row counts.
3. A database not enabled as a subscriber: confirm the permission error and no new egress.
4. On a 15 database, confirm t001's behavior.
5. Record the conclusions in the milestone README; tear down.
