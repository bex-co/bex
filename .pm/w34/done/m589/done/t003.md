## Objective

Ensure MCP `get_metrics` and GraphQL `metrics` route datastore `cpu`/`memory`/`cpu-limit`/`memory-limit` queries with `instance` and `percentage` through the updated datastore core consistently with REST, and record the parity updates in ADR018 and ADR010.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/metrics/mcp.go` calls `s.datastoreAppMetric` for datastore `resourceId`s on `cpu_usage`/`memory_usage`/`cpu_limit`/`memory_limit`.
- `lego/backend/internal/metrics/graphql.go` (`metrics` resolver) handles `RESOURCE` filter arrays — verify how it dispatches datastore IDs vs service IDs.

## Steps

1. Verify `mcp.go` (`get_metrics`) passes `Instance`, `Percentage`, and `ReplicaAggregate` into `datastoreAppMetric` cleanly for `dpg-` and `red-` resources and returns Render's `MetricsResponse` shape.
2. Check `graphql.go` (`metrics` query) for datastore `RESOURCE` IDs with `INSTANCE` and `percentage` and align if needed so REST, GraphQL, and MCP do not disagree.
3. Update `docs/ADR018-render-parity.md` § Metrics and `docs/ADR010-observability.md` (explicitly noting that `percentage` is bex's cross-surface extension shared with services, while repeatable `resource` and datastore `instance` are Render OpenAPI parity).
