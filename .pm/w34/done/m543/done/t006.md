## Objective

Walk definition-of-done items 1, 3, 4 and 5 on a dev stack, with a counted write stream so lost or duplicated rows are visible.

## Context

- `scripts/dev-env.sh <N> up`, the operator from the tree, a Pro workspace, an HA PostgreSQL 18 database enabled through m541's verb, and an external `postgres:18` subscriber created with `failover = true`.
- A write stream with a monotonically increasing key makes gaps and duplicates countable.

## Steps

1. Start the stream; delete the primary's pod (forced failover); later run a switchover; compare keys on the subscriber.
2. Suspend and resume, change the plan, resize storage; compare again.
3. On a 17 database: request an upgrade while the subscriber is paused (refused), resume it until caught up, upgrade to 18, confirm the slot and streaming survive.
4. Recover a point in time from the enabled database; confirm the copy reads not enabled.
5. Record the conclusions in the milestone README; tear down.

## Status (2026-11-21): runs on production, with t010

No cluster can run this here: w34 has no `dev-N` and the shared mock cluster cannot start workers on this host (`fs.inotify.max_user_instances` 128; the local CAPD app cluster reads no available control plane), as for [w34/m541 t008](../../../done/m541/done/t008.md) and [w34/m542 t006](../../../done/m542/done/t006.md). What a cluster adds over the real-PostgreSQL tests and the local `pg_upgrade` drill is CloudNativePG applying the projection: the slot synchronization on an HA pair, its in-place upgrade, its hibernation and rolling restarts, and a recovery's worker guard. So this drill runs on production in `bex-canary`, on owned fixtures, as the first half of t010, once the production pin carries this milestone's commit and m541's and m542's.

- `bex-canary` goes to Pro for the walk and back to Hobby afterwards, under the user's standing permission (2026-10-31, [w4/done/295](../../../../w4/done/295.md)); every fixture is deleted in the same run (by-id `GET` 404).
- Fixtures: a 10 GB HA PostgreSQL 18 publisher enabled through m541's verb, an outside `postgres:18` subscriber created `WITH (failover = true)` and fed a write stream with a monotonically increasing key; a 10 GB PostgreSQL 17 publisher for the upgrade.
- On the HA pair: `SELECT slot_name, synced FROM pg_replication_slots` on the standby lists the subscriber's slot as synced (t001); delete the primary's Pod mid-stream, later `POST /v1/postgres/{id}/failover`, and compare keys (zero missing, zero duplicated).
- Suspend and resume, a plan change and a storage resize, comparing keys after each (t004).
- On the 17 publisher: pause the subscriber, `PATCH` `version: 18` (refused, naming the slot), resume it, upgrade, confirm the slot and the stream survive (t003).
- Recover a point in time from the enabled publisher: the copy reads not enabled through the verb, its `max_logical_replication_workers` is `0`, and the publisher role cannot start a walsender on it (t005).
- Already shown (2026-11-21): the `pg_upgrade` behavior on PostgreSQL 16, 17 and 18 binaries, and every rule at the SQL level on real PostgreSQL (`logical_slots_pg_test.go`).

## First pass (2026-11-21, production pin `7e481dece7ac`)

Step 3 could not run: the upgrade gate refused every paid fixture with `POSTGRES_UPGRADE_BACKUP_REQUIRED` before reading a slot, fixed by t011 (`29d664572`).

**Left (step 3, once the pin carries `29d664572`):** a 10 GB PostgreSQL 16 publisher with a subscriber: REST `PATCH` `version: 17` with `dryRun`, and MCP `update_postgres` with `dryRun`, answer the `upgradeNotice` naming the slot; GraphQL `updateDatabaseVersion` upgrades it and answers the notice, and the subscriber then reads `replication slot … does not exist`. On 17 (that database, or a 10 GB 17 publisher), pause the subscriber (`ALTER SUBSCRIPTION … DISABLE`) and write: REST, GraphQL and MCP refuse `version: 18` with 409 `POSTGRES_UPGRADE_LOGICAL_SLOTS_BEHIND` naming the slot, writing nothing. Enable it, wait for it to catch up, upgrade, and confirm the slot and the stream survive with no row missing or duplicated. Delete the fixtures (by-id `GET` 404).
