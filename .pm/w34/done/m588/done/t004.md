## Objective

Wire the extended `MetricsFilters` query through REST `GET /v1/metrics/filters/application`, REST `GET /v1/metrics/filters/http`, and GraphQL `metricsFilters`, and update the parity/observability documentation.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/metrics/rest.go:208-261` currently rejects >1 `resource`, ignores `startTime`/`endTime`, and 400s `?host=`/`?statusCode=` on `/filters/http`.
- `lego/backend/internal/metrics/graphql.go:679-689` (`metricsFiltersQueryFromArgs`) drops extra `RESOURCE` values, ignores `start`/`end`, and ignores `HOST`/`STATUS_CODE` filters.

## Steps

1. Update `filterValues`, `applicationFilters`, and `httpFilters` in `lego/backend/internal/metrics/rest.go` to parse repeatable `resource`/`service`, `startTime`/`endTime`, and (for `/filters/http`) repeatable `host` and `statusCode`, removing the `400` refusal in `httpFilters`.
2. Update `metricsFiltersQueryFromArgs` in `lego/backend/internal/metrics/graphql.go` to parse all `RESOURCE` values, `start`/`end` via `windowFromInput`, and `HOST`/`STATUS_CODE` entries from `filters`.
3. Update `docs/ADR018-render-parity.md` § Metrics, `docs/render-artifacts/metrics-page.md`, and `docs/ADR010-observability.md` (explicitly noting datastore IDs on `/filters/application` as an additive extension consistent with `/metrics/cpu` & `/metrics/memory`).
