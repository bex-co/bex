## Objective

Show the working set once.

## Context

- On `/agents` in `dev-1`, the sidebar rail renders `Agent sessions → Recent` (with its own "No sessions yet" empty state) while the page renders a second `Recent` heading with Recent/Archived/All tabs and its own list. Both are visible simultaneously.
- ADR047 D9a and `w5/m64` established the **one-rail** convention and moved the list into `DashboardSidebar`; the page-level list is what D9a did not remove.
- The page list is not pure duplication — it carries the archived/all filters, which the rail does not. Whatever is removed, those filters must stay reachable, and `/agents?archived=archived` must keep working (the sidebar's Archived entry targets it, and `validateSearch` still accepts the legacy `?archived=true`).

## Steps

1. Pick the canonical surface — the rail, per D9a — and record the decision in the milestone README.
2. Re-home the archived/all filters so they survive.
3. Remove the redundant list, keeping the composer as the page's primary content.
4. Verify the existing `?archived=` and `?phase=` URLs still resolve.
