# 081 — dev-N cannot provision a paid Postgres or Key Value: the operator's `hcloud-volumes` StorageClass does not exist locally

Why: every tenant datastore volume is projected onto production's `hcloud-volumes` class (`lego/operator/internal/controller/database_controller.go` `dbStorageClass`, `keyvalue_controller.go` `kvStorageClass`). The rebuilt local cluster has only `local-path`, so a paid datastore's PVC sat `Pending` for ever with "storageclass hcloud-volumes not found". No datastore walk could run on dev-N.

- Found by w2/m307's dev-2 walk, 2026-11-10: `dpg-dbph0qpjg4rb8daumoo0` (basic-256mb) stayed `creating` for 10 minutes, with its PVC Pending.
- Fix: `scripts/dev-env.sh` `ensure_tenant_storage_class`, called right after `ensure_cnpg` in `up`, applies `scripts/dev-env/hcloud-volumes-storageclass.yaml`, a shared alias on `rancher.io/local-path` (WaitForFirstConsumer, no expansion). A contract test ties its name to the operator's `dbStorageClass`/`kvStorageClass`. `status` reports it, and two `dev-env.test.sh` contract checks pin it.
- Limitation: local-path cannot expand a volume. Locally, a Postgres storage grow is recorded (spec and CNPG `storage.size`) but its PVC keeps its size, and a Key Value grow is refused (`StorageClassNotExpandable`). Production's class expands.

**DONE 2026-11-10:** after `dev-env.sh 2 up` the Pending PVC bound, and the database went `available` 465 s later.
