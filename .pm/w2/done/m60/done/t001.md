## Objective

Ensure a `Database` deletion timestamp or finalizer change always enqueues reconciliation and retries cleanup until completion.

## Context

- `database_controller.go` handles deletion in reconcile.
- Its primary `GenerationChangedPredicate` can filter deletion because deletion does not increment generation.
- The App controller already has a generation-or-deletion predicate pattern.

## Steps

1. Replace the Database primary predicate with metadata-aware generation/deletion/finalizer handling.
2. Preserve noise filtering for irrelevant updates.
3. Add an envtest that deletes a finalized Database and observes cleanup/retry behavior.

## Files

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/database_controller.go`
- `lego/operator/internal/controller/database_controller_test.go`
- `lego/operator/internal/controller/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Setting deletion timestamp enqueues reconcile without a spec-generation change.
- [x] A cleanup error keeps the finalizer and is retried.
- [x] Successful cleanup removes the finalizer and allows deletion.

## Out of scope

- Making backup-purge Jobs durable; m61 owns durable cleanup execution.
