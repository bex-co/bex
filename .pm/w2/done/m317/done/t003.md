## Objective

`apps/blueprint_plan.go`: a new database's explicit `diskSizeGB` must be 1 or a multiple of 5. A sync that raises `diskSizeGB` goes through `postgres.CheckStorageIncrease` and stamps `storage-increased-at` when applied. A refusal is a `BlueprintFieldConflictError` on `diskSizeGB` carrying the server's message.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Blueprint plan tests: off-step create, off-step increase and a too-soon increase are field conflicts; an unchanged odd size syncs cleanly.

## Result (2026-11-10)

`ApplyBlueprintDatabaseSpec` takes the sync's single `now`. An off-step or too-soon increase is a `diskSizeGB` field conflict, and an applied increase is stamped. Create sites run `CheckNewDatabase`.

Tests:
- `blueprint_postgres_storage_step_test.go`: a step applied and stamped; off-step refused with nothing written; too soon after either stamp refused, naming 12:01Z; 6 h later accepted; an unchanged legacy 7 GB size keeps syncing.
- Create: 7 refused; 15, 1 and the default accepted.
