## Objective

Close the one concrete identity-retention gap the widened census exposes: after an account is deleted, its subject and billing email survive in terminal workspace-creation attempt rows for up to 30 days, with no ADR086 disposition covering them.

## Context

- The table (migration 0103, one after ADR086's own 0102) holds `owner_subject`, `billing_email`, and Stripe correlations (`provider_customer_id`, `provider_setup_intent_id`, `provider_payment_method_id`, `provider_subscription_id`).
- Deletion **does** handle the live capability correctly: `BeginAccountDeletion` retires every non-terminal attempt in the tombstone transaction (`store/accounts.go:253-258`) — "a pending workspace-create handle is a financial capability bound to this subject". That part is right and should not change.
- What is missing is the terminal rows: `finalized`/`expired` attempts are only deleted by a bounded 30-day sweep (`store/workspace_creation.go:258-265`), and neither `CleanupAccountSubject` (`store/accounts.go:371`) nor `cleanupAccountEmail` (`:410`) touches this table — so `owner_subject` and `billing_email` sit there after the identity is gone.
- ADR086's marker convention is `deleted:<own-id>`; the email convention is `<marker>@invalid`. Reuse both rather than inventing a third.

## Steps

1. Add `workspace_creation_attempts` to `CleanupAccountSubject` (anonymize `owner_subject`) and to `cleanupAccountEmail` (anonymize `billing_email`), matching the existing marker conventions.
2. Decide and record whether terminal rows should instead be **deleted** for the departing subject — the idempotent-retry value of keeping them is gone once the account is.
3. Confirm the Stripe correlations may stay (ADR086 already decides financial records are retained) and say so in the disposition, rather than leaving it unstated.
4. Add the row to ADR086's table (t005 does the ADR edit; supply the wording here).

Five focused real-PostgreSQL tests passed in 0.949s. They cover all six attempt states, alternate or missing account emails, matching workspace billing emails, unchanged retention clocks/leases/provider correlations, retry after financial cleanup failure, remaining-owner controls, and deploy provenance. Terminal rows stay anonymized until the existing 30-day sweep; provider correlations remain for financial cleanup.
