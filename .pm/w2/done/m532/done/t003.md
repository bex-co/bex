## Objective

Store sandbox group credential secret values write-only in OpenBao under workspace- and group-scoped paths, and guarantee complete secret cleanup whenever a credential version is pruned, a credential is deleted, a sandbox group is deleted, or a workspace is torn down.

## Context

- Follow existing OpenBao secret storage patterns in `lego/backend/internal/secrets/` and `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/` (`agent-sessions/<ws>/secrets/...`, ADR013/ADR098) and S3 access-key custody (`w21/m498`).
- Sandbox group deletion runs through `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/groups.go` (`DeleteGroup` / `MarkSandboxGroupDeleting` + stale-delete sweeper); workspace teardown runs through `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/purger.go`.
- When OpenBao is unavailable or sealed, credential secret writes and broker secret lookups must fail closed with sanitized 503 errors (never falling back to Postgres plaintext).

## Steps

1. Implement a dedicated OpenBao store adapter for sandbox group credential secrets keyed by `(workspaceID, sandboxGroupID, credentialID, configFingerprintOrRef)` supporting `Put`, `Get` (internal broker-only use), `Delete`, and `DeleteGroupPrefix`/`DeleteWorkspacePrefix`.
2. Wire write-then-commit and rollback cleanup on failed DB commit (no orphaned OpenBao secrets if the Postgres transaction aborts) and post-commit purge of evicted version refs (when pruning past 3 distinct prior configs).
3. Wire secret purge into credential delete, sandbox-group deletion (`groups.go`), and workspace deletion (`purger.go`), ensuring retries remain idempotent when a secret is already absent.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Creating, updating, and restoring credentials write/read secrets in OpenBao only; failed DB transactions clean up newly written OpenBao entries.
- [x] Evicted prior configurations, deleted credentials, deleted sandbox groups, and deleted workspaces remove their OpenBao secrets cleanly and idempotently.
- [x] Sealed/unavailable OpenBao fails closed with a sanitized 503 and never leaks secret material in errors or logs.

## Out of scope
