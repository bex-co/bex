## Objective

Add `hcloud_load_balancer_service.postgres_pooled` (`infra/terraform/main.tf`): listen 6432 → destination 31056 (pg-sni-proxy's NodePort), with proxy protocol and the same TCP health check as `postgres`. pg-sni-proxy routes by SNI and is port-agnostic. Check that no firewall or NetworkPolicy keys on the client-facing port, and update the comment in `lego/operator/cmd/pg-sni-proxy/main.go` that says "all on port 5432". `infra.yml` plans and applies on push to main.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `terraform fmt`/`validate` (the uncredentialed CI job) passes.
- [ ] After apply, the LB lists 6432 (t009).

## Result (2026-11-10)

- **New listener:** `hcloud_load_balancer_service.postgres_pooled` listens on 6432 and forwards to NodePort 31056 (pg-sni-proxy), with proxy protocol and the same TCP health check.
- **Validators:** `scripts/edge-proxyprotocol-validate.sh` lists it (validator and self-test green), and `verify-substrate.sh` expects ports `22,80,443,5432,6379,6432`.
- **Comment:** pg-sni-proxy's header says the proxy routes by SNI, never by the port dialed.
- **Not checked locally:** `terraform fmt`/`validate` (no terraform binary here). The uncredentialed CI job runs them, and `infra.yml` applies on push to main.

## Blocked (2026-11-10)

- The listener shipped in `245a2eac9`, and `infra.yml`'s apply refused it: "a load balancer cannot have more than 5 listeners (resource_limit_exceeded)". The edge LB `bex-traefik` is `lb11`: 5 services, and it already serves 22, 80, 443, 5432 and 6379.
- Nothing was created. `3bc94d538` removed the listener (infra green again), kept the external pooled string on `:5432`, and recorded the divergence in ADR009/ADR018.
- The remaining work waits on the cost decision in the milestone README.

## Superseded (2026-11-10, user decision)

External pooled URLs stay on 5432 as a signed-off difference (ADR018). No 6432 edge listener and no load-balancer upgrade.
