# 053 — Tenant Postgres cannot initialize on the local cluster: no CNPG → API-server egress under Calico

Why: every local tenant database hangs in `Setting up primary` and its initdb Pod dies with `dial tcp 10.128.0.1:443: i/o timeout`. Production allows that path with the Cilium `allow-cnpg-apiserver-egress` cluster-wide policy, but `deploy/gitops/overlays/local/kustomization.yaml` deletes it, and the local cluster's Calico has no replacement. No workstream can test managed Postgres end to end on `dev-N` until this is fixed.

Found 2026-10-30 during w2/m171 t006, which worked around it with a namespaced NetworkPolicy in a throwaway tenant namespace (egress to the apiserver endpoint `172.22.0.4/32:6443` and service `10.128.0.1/32:443` for pods with label `cnpg.io/cluster`).

## Do

1. Add a local-only replacement in the local overlay: a Calico `GlobalNetworkPolicy` (`crd.projectcalico.org/v1`) that allows egress from `has(cnpg.io/cluster)` pods to the kube-apiserver service and endpoint. Resolve the endpoint from the cluster rather than hard-coding the CAPD control-plane IP, or document why a CIDR is safe. Alternatively, have `scripts/mock-cluster.sh` render it.
2. Make `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` check that the local overlay keeps an equivalent for every production policy it deletes, or at least this one.
3. Verify by creating a Postgres through `dev-2`'s bex-api in a fresh tenant namespace, with no manual policy: it should reach `Cluster in healthy state`.

## Acceptance

- [ ] A fresh local tenant Postgres becomes healthy with no hand-made policy.

## Done 2026-10-30

`scripts/mock-cluster.sh` now applies a Calico `GlobalNetworkPolicy` `allow-cnpg-apiserver-egress` (order 100, `selector: has(cnpg.io/cluster)`, egress TCP to the apiserver ClusterIP and its EndpointSlice addresses, on 443 and the endpoint port). The addresses are resolved at run time rather than hard-coded, and the script warns if they cannot be resolved. Applied to the running local cluster, it rendered `nets: [10.128.0.1/32, 172.22.0.4/32]`, `ports: [443, 6443]`. A fresh tenant Postgres created through `dev-2`'s bex-api (`dpg-dbi0mupjg4r95gm776eg`, `basic-256mb`) reached `Cluster in healthy state` within 2.5 minutes with **no** hand-made policy in its namespace. The fixture workspace and identity were deleted afterwards. Step 2 (a `gitops-validate.sh` parity guard for every deleted production policy) was not done. Only this one deleted policy breaks a product flow locally; the others are Cilium-only hardening.
