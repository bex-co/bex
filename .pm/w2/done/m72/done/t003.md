## Objective

Execute ADR060 D1's structural change: the App controller admits a build, creates/adopts the Job, and **returns**; it re-enters on watched Job condition transitions (`Owns(&batchv1.Job{})` with the build-namespace cache scope) and advances the t002 status machine. A bounded `RequeueAfter` covers the kpack path and clock-based deadlines. After this, `BEX_APP_RECONCILE_WORKERS` is a pure responsiveness knob and build parallelism is governed by admission + capacity only.

## Context

- docs/ADR060-build-worker-reliability-and-performance.md §D1 specifies the shape; the pre-deploy observer (lego/operator/internal/predeploy) is the in-repo precedent.
- Today the reconcile blocks up to 30 minutes inside lego/operator/internal/build; the App-deletion fast-path does an uncached read every poll — both go away (deletion is covered by the App watch itself).
- `BEX_BUILD_NAMESPACE` may differ from the App namespace — the Owns watch needs the cross-namespace cache scope.

## Steps

1. Add the Job ownership/watch wiring (owner refs or a mapping handler keyed off the deterministic `bld-<name>-gen-<generation>` name) scoped to the build namespace.
2. Split the build package's blocking wait into admit/dispatch (called from reconcile) + observe/advance (called on watch events), driven by the t002 status fields.
3. Keep kpack builds on a bounded `RequeueAfter` observer; keep the 30-minute budget enforced from status timestamps, not an in-process timer.
4. Verify adopt-on-restart: kill the manager mid-build in envtest, confirm the build completes and status advances.

## Files

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No reconcile invocation blocks on a running build; a build's completion triggers reconciliation via the Job watch (envtest asserts the advance happens without a poll sleep).
- [ ] Manager restart mid-build: adopted Job completes, budget honored from status timestamps (no fresh 30-minute clock).
- [ ] Existing cancel/timeout/UID-scoped-teardown tests pass; `make test` green.
