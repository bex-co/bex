## Canonical matching and producer inventory

`logs/service.go:normalized` drops exact empty terms, lowercases, sorts and compacts into a fresh slice. Whitespace is retained. No effective term means no text predicate; duplicates after lowercasing and input order cannot multiply rows. `keep` tests an OR of literal substring matches, then applies the other supported line constraints. Commas and all regex metacharacters remain literal server-side. `lokiQueryFor` escapes each term before constructing one OR predicate, and `lokiScansLines` keys the bounded broad-search path on the whole normalized set. No first-only helper remains in `internal/logs`.
