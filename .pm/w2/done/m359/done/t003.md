## Objective

`DELETE /v1/sandbox-groups/{id}` removes a non-default group and everything its sandboxes stored, as Render's CLI describes.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/service.go:1552` (`Terminate`), `purger.go` (image GC), `storage_cap.go` and `meter.go` (storage meters, w22/m313), `lego/backend/internal/store/sandbox_groups.go`.
- Imported images: w2/m358's `sandbox_builds` and the per-group registry repository. If m358 has not landed, delete rootfs images only and note it in this task.

## Steps

1. Refuse the workspace's default group with 409 `default_sandbox_group_not_deletable` and Render's sentence.
2. Mark the group deleting (it admits no new sandbox and leaves list reads), then terminate each of its sandboxes through the existing terminate path, delete their suspended rootfs images and the group's imported images, and stop each storage meter.
3. Delete the group row once nothing is left. A repeated DELETE of a deleting or deleted group answers as Render does (404 once gone).
4. Serve REST; add GraphQL `deleteSandboxGroup` and MCP `delete_sandbox_group`; remove the route from `NotOfferedRoutes`; delete `SANDBOX_GROUP_OPERATION_UNSUPPORTED`.
5. Workspace deletion deletes the workspace's imported images too (`snapshots/<ws>-sandbox/images/*`): today the `sandbox_builds` rows cascade away with the tenant and the registry repositories stay behind (found by w2/m358, ADR104 § Images "Not yet").

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/{groups.go,groups_surfaces.go,graphql.go,notoffered.go,purger.go,service.go}`, `lego/backend/internal/store/sandbox_groups.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tests: the default group is refused; a group with running and suspended sandboxes ends with none, no rootfs image, no imported image and no open storage meter; a create into a deleting group is refused.
- [ ] A bex-api restart mid-delete resumes and finishes it.

## Out of scope

- Undo or a grace period (Render: irreversible).

## Result (2026-11-12)

- Two phases. `MarkSandboxGroupDeleting` (migration `0243`: `deleting_at`, a CHECK that the default is never marked, a partial unique name index so the name is free at once) hides the group from every read, the cap and admissions. Then `finishGroupDelete` terminates the group's live sandboxes (`endTerminated`: slot, meter, record), fails its in-flight imports, deletes its images (`RegistryReaper.DeleteImportedImage`), and removes the rows. Each step runs even if an earlier one failed, and the row goes only when all succeeded.
- The request's own attempt is capped at 20 s (inside the 30 s write timeout). `RunGroupDeleteSweeper` claims stale deletes (`ClaimStaleSandboxGroupDeletes` restamps them, so one replica retries each, every 3 minutes).
- The default group is refused with `409 default_sandbox_group_not_deletable`. A repeated delete is `404`.
- Races closed (found by `/simplify`): admission's `Bind` locks the group `FOR SHARE` and refuses a group being deleted, so a create cannot slip in after the mark; a binding with a live lease keeps the group until its create settles. An import that finishes after its group was deleted deletes its own image (`SucceedSandboxBuild` reports the lost update).
- Step 5: workspace deletion deletes the workspace's imported images before its rows cascade (`WorkspacePurger.Images`).
- `SANDBOX_GROUP_OPERATION_UNSUPPORTED` and the two not-offered routes are gone.
