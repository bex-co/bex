## Context

- kubectl context: `hetzner-prod` (memory note: prod cluster access).
- Register lines to annotate: `docs/ADR072-security-review-round7.md` lines 24 + 47 (finding 5), `docs/ADR061-security-review-round8.md` lines 23 + 45 (#11), `docs/ADR063-security-review-round9.md` lines 20 + 89–91 (#9).
