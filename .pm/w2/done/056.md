# 056 — Hobby workspaces hit the PVC and Pod count caps long before the 25-database cap

Why: after w2/m247 t001 a Hobby workspace's datastores are bounded by their count caps (25 Postgres / 25 Key Value), as on Render. But `tenant-quota` still charges every datastore volume and Pod against Hobby's `persistentvolumeclaims: 8` and `pods: 50`. A `2c-4g` HA database uses 2 PVCs (plus WAL, if separate) and 2+ Pods, so a Hobby workspace can run only about 3–4 HA databases. Render has no such cap.

## Do

1. Count the PVCs and Pods per datastore shape (single, HA, readers, pooler, Key Value) from the operator.
2. Raise Hobby's `persistentvolumeclaims`, `pods` and `requests.storage` so that the datastore count caps bind first, without letting services bypass the compute quota. Or exclude datastore PVCs through a storage class or quota scope if Kubernetes allows it. Record the derivation in `quotaForPlan`'s comment.
3. Verify in a Hobby workspace with several HA databases.

## Acceptance

- [ ] A Hobby workspace's datastore count caps bind before its PVC, Pod or storage quotas.

## Done 2026-10-30 (`7a4df5737`)

- **Per-shape derivation** (operator code): a single Postgres has 1 Pod and 1 PVC; HA has 2/2; each reader adds 1/1 (max 5, so up to 7/7); a pooler adds 1 Pod (+1 during a rollout); there is no separate WAL volume. A Postgres keeps up to 8 Jobs (3 exports, 3 expiries, 1 purge, 1 CNPG). A Key Value has Valkey, the internal proxy and a backup Pod, 1 PVC, and keeps up to 7 Jobs. Floors are 10 GB (Postgres) and 5 GB (Key Value).
- **Proven on the local apiserver:** a PriorityClass scope accepts `pods` but refuses `persistentvolumeclaims`, `requests.storage` and ephemeral storage. A scoped `count/jobs.batch` never charges anything. So `pods` moved into the scoped `tenant-compute-quota`, which now counts services, jobs and builds only (Hobby 50 / paid 500). PVCs, storage, ephemeral storage and Jobs got "datastore room" = count caps (25 Postgres + 25 Key Value) × the largest shape, on top of the plan budget. Hobby: 208 PVCs, 1995Gi, 975Gi/6475Gi ephemeral, 400 Jobs. Paid: 400 PVCs, 6995Gi, 2923Gi/10371Gi, 625 Jobs. The derivation is in the `quotaForPlan` comment and ADR043's table. Existing namespaces converge on the same reconcile path as m247 t001.
- **Live proof (local, throwaway namespace):** the old Hobby shape refused the 5th HA Postgres (`persistentvolumeclaims=8`) and a service Pod (`limits.ephemeral-storage=200Gi`). The new shape admitted 10 HA Postgres shapes; the 51st service Pod was refused at `pods=50` and a 2000Gi service disk at `requests.storage`. A quota-replay test pins that 25 HA Postgres + 25 Key Value fit while service overflow is refused.
- **Trade-off:** Hobby service disks are now bounded by the shared 1995Gi ceiling and the 25-service cap rather than about 120Gi. Disks are billed per GB.
- **Follow-up:** datastore Job Pods (exports, Key Value backups, CNPG bootstrap) carry no datastore class and are still charged to the services' compute quota. Filed as `w2/057`.
