# Tenant restart loop: eden-cms-v2 liveness-killed 33×/16h while pegged at its CPU limit

From the 2026-08-20 `hetzner-prod` inspection. `tea-d98210cbbpdc73dcrkvg-eden-cms-v2` restarted 33× in 16h: the pod runs at 497m of its 500m CPU limit (free-tier Guaranteed 500m/512Mi), so the liveness `GET /` (timeout 5s, failureThreshold 6) times out under throttle → kubelet SIGTERM → clean exit 0 → loop. `beancount-cms-v2` and `block-eden-mono` on the same tenant pool show intermittent readiness/liveness timeouts too.

Mostly a tenant-sizing problem, but check the platform response: (1) did the ADR052 crash/restart-loop notification actually fire for this service? (2) consider whether liveness timing for small tiers should tolerate CPU-throttle (longer `timeoutSeconds`/`periodSeconds` derived from tier) — a CPU-starved-but-serving app is arguably better left unkilled; (3) the dashboard should make "you are pinned at your CPU limit" visible next to the restart count. Investigate/decide first — no mechanism change without evidence.
