## Objective

Make `KeyValue.spec.storage` growth patch the bound PVC safely and report resize progress instead of only changing an immutable StatefulSet claim template.

## Context

- KeyValue currently sets storage only in `volumeClaimTemplates` at StatefulSet creation.
- The operator lacks PVC patch RBAC and resize status handling.
- Expansion depends on the StorageClass allowing volume expansion.

## Steps

1. Discover the KeyValue PVC and current request deterministically.
2. Reject shrink and unsupported StorageClass expansion with named conditions/errors.
3. Patch only larger PVC requests and observe filesystem/controller resize states.
4. Add scoped PVC RBAC and unit/envtest coverage.

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/keyvalue_controller.go`
- `lego/operator/internal/controller/keyvalue_controller_test.go`
- `lego/operator/config/rbac/`
- `lego/types/v1alpha1/keyvalue_types.go`
- `docs/ADR021-keyvalue-management.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Increasing storage patches the real PVC and is idempotent across reconciles.
- [ ] Shrink and non-expandable StorageClass cases surface actionable status without hot-looping.
- [ ] Ready is not reported from a stale pre-resize revision.

## Out of scope

- Online storage shrink or automatic Valkey disk autoscaling.
