## Objective

Run a bex-api worker that mints each opted-in App's token (Render's `sub`, `aud` `sts.amazonaws.com`, 60-minute lifetime) and rewrites its Secret before expiry.

## Context

- Multi-replica safety precedent: w1/m58 (SKIP LOCKED / single claimant).
- Workspace, environment (or `default`) and service ids from the store or the App's labels.

## Steps

1. Implement the loop with one writer across replicas, bounded work per pass, and a metric for refresh failures and token age.
2. Stop minting when the App leaves the plan or drops the trigger; delete the Secret when the App is deleted.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/oidcissuer/` or a new `internal/managedoidc/`
- `lego/backend/cmd/api/main.go` (worker wiring)
- `lego/backend/AGENTS.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tests against real Postgres: two replicas never both write; a token is replaced before 30 minutes; a downgraded or trigger-less App stops getting tokens.
