## Objective

Confirm durable cleanup, KeyValue credential behavior, and timeout failures remain consistent across every tenant-facing bex surface and the official Render CLI.

## Context

- Delete acknowledgement and resulting absence are user-visible even when cleanup is operator-internal.
- KeyValue connection metadata and health surface through REST, GraphQL, MCP, and dashboard.

## Steps

1. Exercise service/static/Postgres/KeyValue delete through relevant surfaces and verify subsequent NotFound/absence semantics.
2. Verify KeyValue credential/health reads never expose a split version.
3. Compare upstream-timeout error/state behavior across adapters.
4. Update ADR018 and CLI evidence or file deliberate drift.

## Files

- `lego/backend/`
- `dashboard/`
- `docs/ADR018-render-parity.md`
- `docs/cli-compatibility-checklist.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Delete returns and subsequent reads behave consistently across exposed surfaces.
- [ ] Dashboard and API do not report successful deletion/Ready while required cleanup or credential rollout is incomplete.
- [ ] Official CLI deletion checks pass without a bex-specific workaround.

## Out of scope

- Exposing internal cleanup Job details as a new public API.
