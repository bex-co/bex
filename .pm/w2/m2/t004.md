## Objective

Prove the full deploy-from-chat loop end-to-end from an agent's perspective.

## Context

- Uses the `hello-go` sample (`examples/hello-go/`) as the build-from-git target.
- "no dashboard, no kubectl" is the bar — everything through the API/MCP surface.

## Steps

1. From an MCP client, call `deploy` with the `hello-go` repo + its `bex.yml`; poll to `status.url` live and `curl` it.
2. Push a commit; confirm the HMAC webhook fires and the App redeploys (revision bumps, URL serves the new build).
