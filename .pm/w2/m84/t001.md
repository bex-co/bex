## Objective

Turn “delete my account” into an explicit lifecycle contract before writing a destructive verb.

## Steps

1. Inventory every current Postgres row, OpenFGA tuple, Hydra client/grant, Kratos session/identity, SSH session/key, and external resource keyed by the human subject directly or indirectly.
2. Classify each as delete, revoke, cascade through workspace deletion, retain with opaque attribution, or block pending another user action.
3. Settle workspace rules: sole-user personal workspace, shared membership, last-admin membership, and a surviving workspace whose `owner_identity_id` names the deleting subject.
4. Define confirmation and recent-auth requirements, retry/error semantics, and which state is shown to the dashboard.
5. Record the contract in the relevant ADR/runbook before implementation.

## Files

- `docs/ADR012-auth.md`, `docs/ADR024-members.md`
- `lego/backend/internal/store/migrations/`
- `lego/backend/internal/{workspaces,members,apikeys,cliauth,sshkeys,notifications}/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The inventory names every subject-bearing table and every external credential/session system.
- [ ] Every row/system has one explicit lifecycle disposition.
- [ ] Shared, last-admin, and owner-anchored workspace cases have deterministic outcomes and actionable blockers.
- [ ] The contract forbids deleting Kratos first and defines retry behavior after partial failure.

## Out of scope

- Implementing the deletion verb.
