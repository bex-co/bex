## Objective

Add a clear, accessible self-service deletion flow to Account Settings.

## Steps

1. Add a visually separate Danger Zone card under Account, with localized consequences and a destructive dialog.
2. Require the exact t001 confirmation and present workspace blockers with direct next actions rather than a generic toast.
3. Disable duplicate submission, distinguish retryable failure from terminal success, and never optimistically claim deletion.
4. On success invalidate Apollo/router/session caches and route to the signed-out state using the logout-page precedent.
5. Update the route pending skeleton so its bounds match the added ready-state region at desktop and narrow-mobile.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Danger Zone is reachable and keyboard/screen-reader usable.
- [ ] Exact confirmation and server blockers are visible and actionable.
- [ ] Success leaves no cached authenticated UI and redirects signed out.
- [ ] Errors leave the user in a truthful retryable state.
- [ ] Pending and ready layouts match at desktop and narrow-mobile.

## Out of scope

- Admin-driven deletion of another user.
