## Objective

An App Deployment whose tier does not fit a `bex-tenant-0` node tolerates its pool's taint and requires (node affinity) that pool; every other tier stays as it is. The mapping comes from `tiers.yaml` sizes, not hard-coded names. Operator workload admission still refuses any other toleration, and the build-only taint stays build-only.

## Context

- App Deployment builder in `lego/operator/internal/controller`
- `deploy/gitops/base/operator-workload-admission.yaml`
- `lego/types/tiers/tiers.yaml`
