# 061 — Hobby workspaces cap services at 3 CPU; Render documents no such cap

Why: on Render a Hobby workspace can run any paid instance type, billed per instance; on bex a Hobby customer who picks Pro Plus (4 CPU) for one service, or several Standard services, has the deploy refused by the workspace's compute quota. That is a customer-visible divergence with no recorded sign-off.

- **Found:** w2/m247 t004 / w2/060, 2026-10-31. `tenant-compute-quota` in a Hobby namespace is `requests.cpu 3 / requests.memory 6Gi` (`lego/backend/internal/store/namespaces.go`, `quotaForPlan`); a `pro_plus` service in `tian-personal` was refused with `exceeded quota: tenant-compute-quota, requested: requests.cpu=4`.
- **Render (re-read 2026-10-31):** [features by plan](https://render.com/docs/platform-features-by-plan) lists Hobby's limits as one team member, 25 total services and two environments per project; it states no instance-type or total-compute restriction. Instances are billed per instance on every plan.
- **What the cap does for bex today:** it is the only bound on a Hobby workspace's total compute, so it limits runaway or abusive spend before the first invoice. Datastores are already outside it (w2/m247).
- **Decision (user):**
  - **A. Match Render (recommended by the parity rule):** raise the Hobby services compute quota to the paid ceiling (or remove the CPU/memory axes for Hobby), keeping the 25-service count cap and per-instance billing as the bounds, plus, if wanted, a spend-risk control that is not a plan-based cap (check what ADR030/ADR040 already require before a paid instance type). Customers see the same behavior as on Render.
  - **B. Keep the cap as a recorded divergence:** record it in ADR018 with the user's sign-off and the customer-facing wording (060 makes the refusal explicit). Hobby customers keep hitting a limit Render does not have.
- **Release condition:** the user picks A or B. A becomes a quota change in `quotaForPlan` (namespace reconcile converges existing namespaces) plus tests; B is an ADR018 entry.
