## Objective

Prove on a real cluster that HA on the new tier fails over and announces it, through the pinned Render CLI.

## Context

- `bash scripts/dev-env.sh 2 up` (pre-approved); `scripts/mock-cluster.sh scale N` adds workers if two eligible nodes are not available (pre-approved).
- Use newly owned fixtures; delete them and verify absence afterwards.

## Steps

1. Create a new-tier Postgres with HA via the pinned CLI; confirm two instances on distinct nodes and `highAvailabilityEnabled: true` on REST/GraphQL/MCP.
2. Subscribe a webhook to `postgres_cluster_leader_changed`.
3. Delete the primary pod; confirm promotion, a working internal URL, exactly one event, one webhook delivery.
4. Run `POST /v1/postgres/{id}/failover`; confirm the same.
5. Upgrade a `basic-1gb` database to the new tier and enable HA; then exercise the t001 downgrade rule.
6. Clean up; verify detail/list absence.
