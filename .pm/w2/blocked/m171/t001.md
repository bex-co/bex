## Objective

Decide, from Render's current primary sources, which Postgres tiers bex adds so HA becomes reachable, and at what price — and get the user's approval before any catalog change.

## Context

- ADR009 §5 (dated): Render's Pro-4gb ($55, 1 CPU) sells availability + PITR; Basic-4gb ($75, 2 CPU). `lego/backend/internal/pricing/pricing.yaml` already records newer Render prices for the small tiers (basic-256mb $7, basic-1gb $20), so ADR009's figures may be stale.
- Pricing rule: [ADR030](../../../../docs/ADR030-pricing.md) — Postgres per instance-month = Render × 0.70.
- The pinned Render CLI's own tests name the plans without HA (`free`, `0.1c-256mb`, `0.5c-1g`, `basic-256mb`, `basic-1gb`; `w8/done/m43/README.md:36`) — Render also has flexible `Nc-Mg` plan ids; record how bex maps them (`renderPlan` precedent in the compute family).
- Also record Render's rule for a plan change that would leave HA enabled on an ineligible plan (refuse vs auto-disable), and how HA is billed (per instance?).

## Steps

1. Fetch render.com/pricing, render.com/docs/postgresql-high-availability and the pinned OpenAPI plan enum; record names, CPU/RAM, storage, HA eligibility, HA billing and price, with the fetch date.
2. Recommend: the smallest HA-eligible Render tier plus one larger tier; compute bex prices at × 0.70.
3. Ask the user to approve the tier list and prices; record the decision in this README and in ADR009 §5.
