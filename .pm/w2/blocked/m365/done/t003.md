## Objective

The name answers the live successor; the predecessor stays reachable by its `srv-` id and cannot harm the successor.

## Done

- `core.preferLive` filters the by-display-name tier and both label tiers; `core.Base.liveSuccessor` swaps a deleting object-name candidate for its live sibling (same tenant + `LabelServiceName`), which matters when a display name differs from the name and when unbound.
- `apps.Service.Delete` returns at once for an App already being deleted: every earlier step already ran (the App delete is last), and repeating the OpenBao purge, keyed by the service name, would wipe the successor's env vars.
