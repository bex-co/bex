## Objective

Assert the behaviour and the failure modes this milestone shipped — particularly the ones that are security properties rather than features. Real assertions only; no tautologies, no snapshot-everything.

## Context

- The existing suites encode the **old** cardinality and must flip, not be deleted: `internal/github/m74_multi_connection_test.go`, `m75_claim_flow_test.go`, `m67_connect_binding_test.go`, `internal/store/git_connections_pg_test.go`.
- The fail-closed branches in `webhook.go` are the highest-value tests here: they are what [ADR057](../../../docs/ADR057-security-review-round6.md) round-6 #9 and codex #7 bought, and t003 restates rather than removes them.

## Steps

1. **Cardinality:** one installation binds to two workspaces given two independent proofs; same-pair re-bind is idempotent; a caller lacking `can_manage` is refused; a callback whose OAuth user does not administer the installation binds nothing.
2. **Fan-out:** one push ⇒ one deploy per bound workspace, independent replay claims, redelivery refused in both. Plus a test per empty-scope branch (unbound, no installation id, unwired resolver, lookup error) asserting **nothing** is matched or mutated in multitenant operation.
3. **Selection:** happy path; replay; foreign subject; expired TTL; `installationId` outside the stored set; `can_manage` revoked between callback and selection.
4. **Quotas:** both caps refuse with their codes, and the admission is transactional — two concurrent binds at limit−1 must not both land.
5. **Migration:** up is a no-op for existing data; down succeeds at one-workspace-per-installation and raises at two.
6. Dashboard: picker renders, choosing binds, and no string tells a user to install or uninstall an App.
