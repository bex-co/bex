# Design note: round-6 #4 admission grammar for operator-authored workloads

From `/pm-brainstorm for w2` 2026-08-18 (round 1, item 6). The remaining slice of ADR057 round-6 #4 (re-confirmed ADR061 #2/#6): a ValidatingAdmissionPolicy constraining what the **operator identity** may author cluster-wide — by namespace label, ServiceAccount, and no-foreign-Secret-mounts — so a compromised operator credential can't author arbitrary workloads outside the tenant envelope.

This is still design-shaped, not implementation-shaped: ADR063 #13 (workload-admission grammar: nodeName/procMount/wildcard-tolerations, `hostUsers=false` in execution namespaces, restricted container grammar elsewhere) and ADR064's operator delete/PVC admission containment already shipped adjacent pieces. The deliverable here is a **short design note** scoping what's genuinely left against those, with a concrete policy sketch and a go/no-go recommendation — a milestone proposal only if the residual is real and >~1h.
