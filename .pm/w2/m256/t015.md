## Objective

The production re-check of t014 (2026-11-08) found that `create` right after deleting a Blueprint and its service answers 500 but still revives the row, and that `plan` on that row answers 500 reproducibly. The README's t014 re-check section has the exact sequence. Find the root cause (reproduce on dev-2), make create atomic, and make planning answer a plan or a named refusal, never a 500.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The production sequence ends with `plan` returning `planned` (`create static-site`), or a named 409 while the old App is still terminating, and `approve` then recreates the service.
- [ ] A failed create leaves no revived row behind.
- [ ] A real-DB test reproduces the 500 and fails without the fix.

## Implementation (2026-11-08, `/loopx w2`): awaiting the production re-check

- **Reproduced on dev-2.** The log showed `bex-api: internal error: object is being deleted: apps.app.bex.co "tea-…-static-site" already exists`.
- **Cause of the 500:** the plan's dry-run create met the still-terminating App's name. AlreadyExists is neither a bad request nor a conflict, so it surfaced as a 500.
  - Now `previewNameHeld` plans the create when the holder is the customer's deleting service, and approve answers `409 BLUEPRINT_SERVICE_STILL_DELETING` until the teardown ends.
  - Any other holder of the name gets the named `errServiceNameInUse`.
- **Half-revived row:** `CreatePlannedBlueprint` committed the revive before planning, so a failed plan left the row behind. It now returns the prior row, and a failed plan runs `UndoPlannedBlueprintCreate`. A fresh row is deleted; a revived row gets back its name, path, status, created_at and superseded waiting plans.
- **Tests, each failing without the fix:**
  - `apiServerDryRuns` gives dry runs real API-server semantics; the fake client returned nil, which is why the t014 replay passed.
  - `TestRecreateWhosePlanFailsLeavesNoRevivedRow(PG)` and `TestCreateWhosePlanFailsLeavesNoRow(PG)`.
  - The real-DB suites and `make lint` pass.
- **dev-2 re-walk, old App still terminating:** re-create → `planned`; `POST …/syncs` → `planned`; approve → `409 BLUEPRINT_SERVICE_STILL_DELETING`. After the teardown: plan → approve → `success`, the service is recreated and the Blueprint is `in_sync`. There was no 500 anywhere.
