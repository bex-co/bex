## Objective

Validate and install an externally issued, publicly trusted wildcard certificate without adding a DNS-provider dependency.

## Steps

1. Verify the leaf certificate SAN covers `*.onbex.co`, the private key matches it, and at least 30 days of validity remain.
2. Install the full public chain and private key in `traefik/onbex-default-wildcard-tls` before relying on the TLSStore.
3. Keep the Secret out of Git and keep the production-only TLSStore out of base/local overlays.
4. Preserve existing HTTP-01 issuers and Certificates for per-App/custom-domain hosts.

## Files

- `scripts/onbex-default-tls-secret.sh`
- `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Installed certificate covers `*.onbex.co`, matches its key, and is browser-trusted live.
- [ ] Rotation uses the same idempotent installer and live expiry monitoring warns before the 30-day deploy gate.
- [ ] Existing HTTP-01 issuers/certificates are unchanged.
- [ ] Local/mock overlays do not require production certificate material.

## Out of scope

- A wildcard for arbitrary custom domains.
