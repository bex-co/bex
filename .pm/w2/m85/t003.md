## Objective

Replace Traefik's generated self-signed fallback without changing normal SNI route selection.

## Steps

1. Add the singleton `traefik.io/v1alpha1` `TLSStore/default` referencing the wildcard Secret.
2. Configure Traefik's Kubernetes-CRD provider with an explicit default TLS-resource namespace.
3. Keep standard Ingress TLS Secrets authoritative when a matching App/custom-domain route exists.
4. Confirm an unknown SNI receives the wildcard cert and then reaches Traefik's 404 handler, with no catch-all tenant route.

## Files

- `deploy/gitops/charts/onbex-default-tls/tlsstore.yaml`
- `deploy/gitops/base/values/traefik.values.yaml`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Unknown `*.onbex.co` SNI gets the wildcard certificate.
- [ ] Known hosts keep their specific certificate and route.
- [ ] Default-store lookup is constrained to the trusted platform namespace.
- [ ] No catch-all Ingress or tenant backend is introduced.

## Out of scope

- Replacing Traefik or cert-manager.
