## Objective

bex measures `timeoutSeconds` from creation, so a suspended sandbox is terminated at `createdAt + timeout` (24 h by default), and a resume does not extend it. Render restarts the runtime timeout on resume and keeps a suspended sandbox resumable for 30 days. Renew the OpenSandbox expiry on resume, make the inventory reconcile and the reaper read the last resume time, and keep a suspended sandbox for 30 days.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After a resume, the sandbox expires at resume time + `timeoutSeconds` (no-timeout sandboxes are unchanged).
- [ ] A sandbox suspended for longer than its timeout but less than 30 days can still be resumed.
- [ ] Tests for the reaper and inventory with resume times, plus a real-DB check where one applies.

**Done 2026-11-08:** the transitions are stamped on OpenSandbox metadata (`bex.co/suspended-at`, `bex.co/resumed-at`).

- **On suspend:** the OpenSandbox expiry moves to now + 30 days. The BatchSandbox controller deletes at `spec.expireTime` even while paused (verified in the controller source at `e95681e`).
- **On resume:** the expiry renews to now + timeout, using 0.2.2 `POST /renew-expiration`.
- **Reaper deadline:** suspended → suspendedAt + 30 d; otherwise max(createdAt, resumedAt) + timeout.
- **Tests:** `TestResumeRestartsRuntimeTimeoutAndSuspensionKeepsTheSandbox`, `TestSuspendedSandboxIsReapedAfterThirtyDays` and others; `-race`, real-DB and `make lint` pass. ADR018's row is ✅.
