## Objective

Confirm the m99 change is consistent across every surface it touches and compare it with what Render tells a user while a build is queued. Record drift instead of silently diverging.

## Steps

1. **Surface check.** The narration is produced once and read by REST `GET /v1/logs`, GraphQL `logs`, MCP log reads and the SSE tail (t003's test). Confirm no surface gained a field or changed an error shape; `failureReason` semantics are unchanged.
2. **Compare with Render.** Capture what Render shows on a deploy that is queued behind its per-plan build concurrency limit (dashboard deploy page and `render.com/docs` on build concurrency). Note the copy and the date checked. At filing time this was not captured.
3. **Record.** If Render narrates the reason, note the match in the m99 README. If Render shows nothing, record bex as ahead in `docs/ADR018-render-parity.md` (the "bex ahead of Render" section) with a one-line row. File any other drift as a `w2/NNN` note.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The four log surfaces are confirmed identical for the new line.
- [ ] Render's queued-build behavior is captured with its date in the m99 README, and ADR018 is updated if bex is ahead or diverges.
