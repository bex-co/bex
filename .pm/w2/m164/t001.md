## Objective

Make the identity/provenance census match by shape instead of by an exact name list, so a column cannot evade it by being called `owner_subject` instead of `subject`.

## Context

- Today's census: `lego/backend/internal/store/accounts_pg_test.go:280-330` queries `information_schema.columns` for `column_name = ANY(...)` over exactly seven names, and compares the result with a hardcoded `want` list.
- Proven evasion: `workspace_creation_attempts.owner_subject` and `.billing_email` (migration 0103) are invisible to it — and they are not in ADR086's disposition table either.
- Shapes worth matching: `subject`, `%_subject`, `owner_identity_id`, `%_identity_id`, `email`, `%_email`, `created_by`, `invited_by`, `caller`, `actor`, `actor_id`, `user_id`, `client_id`.
- False positives are expected and fine — the census's job is to force a declaration, not to guess intent. Every match gets an entry saying what happens to it.

## Steps

1. Replace the exact-name predicate with a shape predicate (LIKE/regex over `column_name`).
2. Turn the flat `want` list into an inventory that pairs each column with its declared disposition (delete / anonymize / cascade / retain-with-reason), so the test reads as the contract rather than as a list.
3. Record every currently-matching column, including the newly caught ones, with its real disposition — `owner_subject`/`billing_email` marked as a gap that t004 closes, not silently blessed.
4. Prove the guard: seed a test-only column of an evading shape and confirm the test fails.
