## Objective

Know every place that pins the `bex-db` CA (so the durable fix covers all of them) and exactly when CNPG rotates it.

## Context

- Known pinned copies: `monitoring/grafana-product-analytics` and `monitoring/grafana-cli-analytics` (`ca.crt` beside the reader `password`).
- In-namespace clients in `bex-system` read CNPG's own Secret and follow renewals automatically; cross-namespace copies do not.
- CNPG reports `status.certificates.expirations` on the `bex-db` Cluster; the operator-generated CA is short-lived (the 2026-10-08 → 2026-12-30 renewal implies ~90 days).

## Steps

1. Read-only: list every Secret/ConfigMap in every namespace whose certificate fingerprint matches the current or previous `bex-db` CA; grep `deploy/`, `lego/operator/config/` and `scripts/` for `bex-db-ca`, `sslrootcert`, `verify-full`.
2. Record CNPG's CA and server-cert validity and renewal lead time from the Cluster status.
3. For each consumer, note whether it follows renewals automatically or pins a copy, and its failure mode.
