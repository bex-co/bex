## Objective

Prove end to end that a CA change propagates to the analytics readers with no bootstrap, reseal or restart.

## Context

- The local overlay includes Grafana (`deploy/gitops/overlays/local/kustomization.yaml`); the local/dev clusters are pre-approved for this work (repo AGENTS.md).
- CNPG regenerates its operator-managed CA when the CA Secret is removed; alternatively swap the CA in a throwaway CNPG cluster.

## Steps

1. On a dev stack with the t003 mirror and t004 alerts deployed, confirm both readers are healthy.
2. Force a CA change; observe the mirror update, the readers reconnect, and health return to OK without intervention.
3. Break the mirror deliberately; confirm the t004 alert fires; restore.
4. Record timings and evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Health OK after a CA change with no human step; time to recovery recorded.
- [ ] The alert fires on a broken mirror on the dev stack.

## Out of scope

- Forcing a CA rotation on production.
