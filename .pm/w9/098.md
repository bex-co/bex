# 098 — Audit history starts at the upgrade to Pro, as Render's retention does

Why: Render says "Retention starts only when you upgrade to Pro or higher. Earlier data is unavailable.", but bex records audit events on Hobby and shows them after an upgrade, a divergence ADR018 records without the user's sign-off.

- **Source:** `/pm-brainstorm one aspect of render parity to w9` (2026-11-16), filed by `/pmx them all to w9`; [render.com/docs/audit-logs](https://render.com/docs/audit-logs) § Retention, read 2026-11-16.
- **Today:** `audit.Service.List` (`lego/backend/internal/audit/service.go`) refuses a Hobby admin with `RequirePlanFeature(core.FeatureAuditLogs)` and otherwise reads every retained row. [ADR018](../../docs/ADR018-render-parity.md)'s Audit logs row (w8/m138): "Events are still recorded on Hobby, so an upgrade shows the retained history."
- **Do:** reuse w9/m389's mechanism ([ADR010 § Durability & retention](../../docs/ADR010-observability.md)):
  1. When `store.UpdateTenantPlan` moves a workspace from a plan without `FeatureAuditLogs` to one with it, stamp `tenants.audit_visible_from = now()` (next free migration, row locked as for `logs_visible_from`). A downgrade followed by an upgrade moves it forward; `NULL` (workspaces already on Pro) means no floor.
  2. `List` floors `Since` at it, so REST, GraphQL and the CSV export agree.
  3. Keep recording on Hobby; only reads hide it, which is what a Render customer observes.
  4. Amend ADR018's Audit logs row and ADR006 § Audit log.
- **Accept:** a Hobby workspace with recorded events, upgraded to Pro, lists none from before the upgrade on REST, GraphQL or the CSV; events after the upgrade list; a workspace already on Pro is unchanged; a real-Postgres test covers the plan-change stamp.
- **Decision state:** the `/pm-brainstorm` recommendation under ADR008 § Render parity first, not a user decision; keeping the pre-upgrade history visible would need the user's sign-off recorded in ADR018.
- **Size:** about 1h, so an inbox note rather than a milestone.
