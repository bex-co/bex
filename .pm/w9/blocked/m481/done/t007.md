## Objective

Review the implemented change adversarially against I1–I5 before it ships to production, and record the verdict in the security-review lineage.

## Context

- The boundary is in the milestone README. The design and its threats are in ADR024 (t001).
- Lineage: `docs/AGENTS.md` lists the chain (ADR028 → … → ADR110, plus feature reviews such as ADR103).

## Steps

1. Try each escalation path:
   - a demoted creator's key;
   - a key used in another workspace of its creator;
   - a creatorless key;
   - a key re-roled through the member verbs;
   - a key minting a key or SSH key, or inviting a member;
   - a delegated OAuth token or non-key machine client on webhook create;
   - a creator lookup that cannot answer;
   - a key bound before this change that keeps `developer` while its creator is Admin.
2. Fix each confirmed finding with a regression test, or file it with an owner.
3. Record the review: as a new lineage ADR, or as a section of ADR024 if the lineage's convention allows one.
