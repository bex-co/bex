`xdgcache_test.go` covers the recipe on every runtime and a static site, the pod (only a native S3 build carries the archive, no credential in BuildKit, kubelet expansion leaves every command intact), each phase script's outcomes against the fake store (round trip, miss, unreachable store, failed download, over the bound, symlinked archive, empty and oversized caches, export that never finished, purge), the wrapper against a fake buildctl (solve order, the image solve's exact args and exit status, Render's restore line and size format, base lookup past resolver stages, failed seed and export), and the seed and save steps on GNU userland (round trip with links and dotfiles, empty, over the bound, corrupt gzip, CRC failure, `..` member, symlink write-through, decompression bound). The GNU steps skip on a BSD userland and run in CI and in `golang:1.27-bookworm`. Mutation spot-checks: fifteen guards each removed in turn, fourteen caught. The survivor (`tail` to `head` on the base lookup) is equivalent, because the resolver names every stage ahead of the final one.
