## Objective

Give the top-up rows a Stripe meter and price so they appear on the invoice as "Cron job minimum", attached to every existing and new workspace subscription.

## Context

- Contract item sync: `lego/backend/internal/billing/contracts.go` (`EnsureContract`, `resolvePriceIDs`, `syncContractItems`, `validatePriceCompliance`).
- Meter targets for settlement: `billing/settle.go` (`meterTargets`, `AwaitMeteredUsage`).
- Price IDs and env: `.env.example` and the env inventory in `lego/backend/AGENTS.md`; `.env.example` must mirror `.env` names (repo rule).

## Steps

1. Add the meter (event name, micro-USD value, sum aggregation) and a metered price at $0.000001 per unit, named "Cron job minimum", following the agent-model meter's provisioning path.
2. Wire its price id into `resolvePriceIDs` / `syncContractItems` so existing subscriptions gain the item on their next sync and new ones get it on create.
3. Make `meterTargets` / `AwaitMeteredUsage` wait for the new meter like the others, so settlement does not invoice before the top-up lands.
4. Document any new env var in `.env.example` and `lego/backend/AGENTS.md`.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/billing/contracts.go`, `settle.go`, `stripe.go`
- `.env.example`, `lego/backend/AGENTS.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] In the Stripe sandbox, a test workspace's subscription carries the "Cron job minimum" item after a contract sync.
- [x] A $1.00 top-up row exported to the sandbox appears on the upcoming invoice as "Cron job minimum $1.00".

## Out of scope

- Live production acceptance (the closeout replays the DoD).

**Done 2026-11-16, by a different design.** `pricing.Default.BillableMeterNames()` already has 20 dimensions, Stripe's limit of 20 items per Subscription (`TestCatalogFitsOneSubscription`). A new meter and Price would therefore break every contract.

- **What ships instead:** `billing/cron_minimum.go` sends each `cron_minimum_microusd` row as a pending invoice item on the workspace's live Subscription.
  - Description: "Cron job minimum: <retained name>, <Month YYYY>".
  - Amount in cents, rounded half up.
  - The row's deterministic identifier is the idempotency key.
  - Tax follows the Subscription's `automatic_tax`.
- The settle path skips it, because `stripeMeterEvent` returns skip and so no meter is awaited.
- There is no new Price, no env var and no live-Stripe step.
- Stub-backend tests cover the request shape and the outcomes: accepted, 4xx reject, 5xx retry, missing Subscription, under half a cent.
- The Stripe sandbox criterion was not run, because no `BEX_TEST_STRIPE_KEY` is available in this checkout. Production evidence is t010's check.
