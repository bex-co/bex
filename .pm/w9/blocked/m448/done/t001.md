## Objective

Pin down exactly what Render's "minimum monthly charge of $1 per cron job service" means before bex charges it, so t003 implements Render's rule and not a guess.

## Context

- [render.com/docs/cronjobs](https://render.com/docs/cronjobs): "Billing is prorated by the second, based on active running time during a given month." / "There is a minimum monthly charge of $1 per cron job service."
- render.com/pricing did not render rates to a fetcher in w4/546; try the pricing page, Render's billing/invoices docs (`render.com/docs/billing`), its community forum and changelog.
- bex bills per Stripe subscription period, which is anchored to the subscription start, not the calendar month (`lego/backend/internal/billing/settle.go`, `agent_model_charge.go` "current period ending at periodEnd").

## Steps

1. Find Render's wording on: (a) whether the $1 is prorated for a cron job created or deleted mid-month; (b) whether a suspended cron job still owes it; (c) how the invoice names the line; (d) whether it counts against any included usage.
2. Where Render says nothing, record the literal reading as the recommendation: every cron job service that exists at any point in a billing period owes max(0, $1 − its run charge for that period), not prorated, suspended included. Mark each such point **unverified**.
3. Write the findings into this task file under `## Findings`, with links and the date read.

## Findings

Read 2026-11-16.

- [render.com/docs/cronjobs](https://render.com/docs/cronjobs) says only: "Billing is prorated by the second, based on active running time during a given month." and "There is a minimum monthly charge of $1 per cron job service." Neither sentence covers suspended or deleted cron jobs.
- Render staff on the community forum ([does-deleting-a-service-automatically-stop-charging-me-for-it](https://community.render.com/t/does-deleting-a-service-automatically-stop-charging-me-for-it/234)): "deleting or suspending a service … automatically stops billing for that given resource."
- (a) Proration of the minimum for a cron job created or deleted mid-month: **unverified**. Literal reading: not prorated. A cron job active at any point in the month owes it once.
- (b) Suspended cron job: **unverified** for the minimum itself. Render's staff say suspension stops billing, so bex reads it this way: a cron job suspended the **whole** month owes nothing, and one active at any point owes the minimum.
- (c) Invoice line name: **unverified**. bex uses "Cron job minimum: <cron job>, <Month YYYY>".
- (d) Counts against included usage: **unverified**. No Render plan includes compute, so no.
- Billing period: Render says "during a given month", so bex uses the calendar month (UTC). That is also the month bex's included allowances use (ADR030 §13, §14). It is not the Stripe subscription period.
- **Amount: $0.70, not $1 (deviation from the milestone text).** ADR030 §2 prices every Render-derived amount at Render × 0.70, flat fees included: the disk at $0.175, snapshot storage at $0.056 and, from today (w7/m429), custom domains at $0.175. A $1 floor would be the one undiscounted Render price. $0.70 keeps every cron job's bex charge at exactly Render's charge × 0.70, since max($1, r) × 0.70 = max($0.70, 0.70r). It is a one-line `pricing.yaml` value (`cronJob.minimumUsdPerMonth`), and ADR030 §18 records charging $1 as a variant for the user.
