# A static site's Redirects/Rewrites and Headers editors stay enabled for a member who cannot save them

Why: w9/m619 disables every service setting with the role's reason for a member without `can_create`, but the static site's rule editors still let a Contributor or Viewer edit and then answer 403 on save.

- bex-api: `apps.Service.AddRoute`, `DeleteRoute`, `MoveRoute`, `SetRoutes`, `AddHeader`, `DeleteHeader` and `SetHeaders` gate on `can_create` (the role-ladder sweep, `lego/backend/internal/api/roleladder_test.go`).
- Dashboard: `dashboard/src/features/services/components/static-edge-rule-page.tsx` reads no capability (no `useCapabilities` / `useCapabilityGate`), so its add, edit, reorder, delete and save controls are enabled for every role.
- Fix: gate them with `useCapabilityGate("canCreate")` (`features/capabilities/hooks/use-capability-gate.ts`), as w9/m619 t005 gated the Settings page, with a Contributor and a Developer test.
- Done when: as a Contributor the Redirects/Rewrites and Headers pages hold every write control with "Your role can’t make this change. Ask a workspace admin.", and a Developer's are unchanged.
