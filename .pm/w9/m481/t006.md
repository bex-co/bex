## Objective

The API keys page tells the truth about what a key can do. Today it says "The key acts with full authority in this workspace", which was never true (a key was a Developer) and is not true now (it acts with your role).

## Steps

1. Reword the shown-once secret note and the panel description: the key acts with your current member role in this workspace and follows it if your role changes; treat it like a password.
2. Update every locale and the component tests that read the copy.
