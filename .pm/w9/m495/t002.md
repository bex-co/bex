## Objective

Update `lego/operator/internal/controller/build_datastore_reach.go` so per-build egress and ingress NetworkPolicies admit TCP traffic from the App's build pods to both datastores and eligible `web_service`/`private_service` pods within the App's canonical workspace namespace and isolation scope.

## Context

- `build_datastore_reach.go` currently converges `bex-build/bld-<app>-datastore-egress` and `<ws>/<app>-build-ingress` with `ports := buildReachPorts()` (`5432`, `6379`, `6380`).
- Web and private services may listen on `PORT` (`10000` default) or any discovered/configured TCP port (`serviceDetails.openPorts`, ADR089), so target pod selection must match eligible workspace service pods (not arbitrary non-tenant pods) and respect `buildReachTargets(app)` (`network-isolation`).
- Keep `build_datastore_reach.go` ownership checks (`checkOwnedArtifact`, `deleteOwnedObject`) and `buildReachEligible` canonical-namespace guard intact.

## Steps

1. Update the egress and ingress NetworkPolicy rules in `lego/operator/internal/controller/build_datastore_reach.go` so they cover both datastore pods (existing ports) and eligible `web_service`/`private_service` pods in `app.Namespace` matching `buildReachTargets(app)` (TCP only; preserve datastore rule untouched).
2. Ensure no port restriction breaks custom/discovered `PORT` values on `web_service`/`private_service` pods while still denying platform namespaces, other workspaces, isolated-environment crossings, and non-eligible pods.
3. Confirm `deploy/gitops/base/build-boundary.yaml` comments stay accurate.

## Files

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/build_datastore_reach.go`
- `deploy/gitops/base/build-boundary.yaml`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Generated egress and ingress NetworkPolicies allow TCP traffic from the App's build pods to eligible `web_service`/`private_service` pods in the same workspace and isolation scope.
- [ ] Cross-workspace, cross-isolated-environment, legacy shared-namespace, and platform-namespace traffic remain denied.

## Out of scope

- Revoking policies on build cancel (tracked separately as `w9/124`).
