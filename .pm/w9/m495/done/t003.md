## Result (2026-11-19)

`TestBuildServiceReachIsOwnWorkspaceAndScopeOnly` runs 22 probes through the NetworkPolicy evaluator. The allowed probes are a private service on 10000/3000/8080, a paid web service, an empty-type service, B→B, and isolated→own environment. The denied probes are a free web service, a worker, a cron pod, a pre-deploy pod, KV proxy/Postgres off their ports, A↔B cross-workspace, ordinary→isolated, isolated→ordinary, a static-site build, a platform pod wearing a service's labels, a forged UID, and a publish pod. Structural pins assert TCP only. `TestBuildServiceReachFollowsTheWorkspacesServices` covers the service half appearing, leaving with the last service, App ownership, and revoke removing all three. `TestBuildJobSearchesTheAppNamespace` covers the DNS search. The envtest `Build reach to the workspace's own services (w9/m495)` covers dispatch, the Job's DNS search, the sibling-selecting rule and ingress, and all three gone after completion. Mutation checks each failed the suite: dropping `app-id Exists`, dropping the isolation term, ignoring `ReceivesPrivateTraffic`, removing the static-site exclusion, no service grant, and no DNS config. `make test` and `make lint` pass.
