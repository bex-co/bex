## Objective

Complete a focused threat review and amend ADR022 before widening per-build NetworkPolicies from datastore ports (`5432`, `6379`, `6380`) to eligible `web_service`/`private_service` pods in the App's own canonical workspace namespace.

## Context

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/build_datastore_reach.go:132-139` currently restricts per-build egress/ingress policies to datastore ports only.
- `w8/done/m129/README.md:36` recorded the boundary: builds may reach only the owning workspace's own databases and services, never another workspace, another isolated environment, platform namespaces, or host/cloud metadata endpoints.
- Any protected operator Secret, control-plane service, or non-canonical shared namespace must remain strictly unreachable.

## Steps

1. Review the attack surface of allowing build pods in `bex-build` (selected by operator-set `app.bex.co/build`, `app-uid`, `component=build`, `workspace`) to reach TCP ports on eligible `web_service`/`private_service` pods in the App's canonical workspace namespace.
2. Confirm `buildReachTargets` (`network-isolation` environment selector) prevents an ordinary App's build from reaching an isolated environment's services and vice versa, and that `buildReachEligible` continues to refuse the legacy shared namespace.
3. Record the threat review and invariants as an addendum in `docs/ADR022-tenant-isolation.md`.
