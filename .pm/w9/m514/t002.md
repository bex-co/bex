## Objective

A Viewer, Contributor or Billing member mints a key in their workspace on REST, GraphQL and MCP; the key is seated at their role and acts with exactly it.

## Context

- `apikeys.Service.CreateAPIKey` and `PermissionCatalog` (`lego/backend/internal/apikeys/service.go`); `store.BindClientForCreator` (`internal/store/store.go`); `tenancy.BindKey` (`internal/tenancy/tenancy.go`).
- The FGA model (`deploy/gitops/authz/model.fga`) and its consumers (`core/base.go` relations, `core/scope.go`, `core/audit.go`, `core/keyrole.go`); migrations that enumerate relations (0089, 0293) if a new relation is added.
- I3: a key with no recorded creator keeps the Developer ceiling; this task must not change that.

## Steps

1. Gate `CreateAPIKey` and `PermissionCatalog` on t001's mint relation, keeping `AuthorizeMintClass` (I4) and the uncached re-check.
2. Let `BindClientForCreator` seat every person role, still refusing a non-member and an unsettled creator.
3. Mark catalog levels for Billing per t001.
4. Keep the active-key quota and the creation rate limit per workspace.
