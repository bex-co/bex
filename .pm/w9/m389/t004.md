## Objective

On an upgrade, logs that had aged out under the old plan stay hidden: Render says "Logs older than your current retention period are no longer available, even if you upgrade your plan".

## Context

- Workspace plan changes in `lego/backend/internal/workspaces/` (billing plan updates).
- The floor from t003.

## Steps

1. On upgrade, store a visible-from time of (upgrade time − old window); the floor is the later of that and (now − new window), so the restriction expires on its own.
2. A downgrade simply narrows the window.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/workspaces/`, `lego/backend/internal/logs/`
- `lego/backend/internal/store/migrations/<next>_workspace_logs_visible_from.{up,down}.sql`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tests: Hobby→Pro shows no line older than 7 days at the upgrade time; after 14 days the full Pro window applies; a downgrade narrows at once.

## Out of scope

- Billing changes.
