# Decision: cron Free-tier vs Render's Starter-min per-minute pricing model

Surfaced 2026-08-20 comparing bex vs render.com cron create forms. **A product decision, not implementation work — do not materialize a milestone until decided.**

Observed divergence:

- **Render** offers **no Free tier** for cron jobs. Instance types start at **Starter** and are priced **per run-minute** ($0.00016/min Starter … $0.00405/min Pro Plus), matching cron's ephemeral, per-invocation billing. The form shows the per-minute price on each instance-type card.
- **bex** defaults cron jobs to the **Free** tier and offers up to **Pro Ultra**, showing RAM·CPU only, **no price** on the cards.

Questions for the user:

1. Is Free-tier cron intended, or should cron follow Render and require a paid (Starter-min) instance? This intersects payment gating (`docs/ADR046-payment-onboarding-and-paid-gating.md`) and pricing (`docs/ADR030-pricing.md`) — cron is billed on per-run compute, so a "Free" cron that runs every minute has a real cost.
2. Should the instance-type cards surface estimated per-run / per-minute cost like Render does (ties to ADR030 `estimatedCost`)?

Minor related observation (record only, not a decision): Render exposes **Cron Job** as a top-level New-menu item; bex nests it under New → New Service. Discoverability nicety, low priority.

No DO_NOT_DO conflict, but pricing/tier changes are user-decision territory — hold for direction.
