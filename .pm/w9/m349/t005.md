## Objective

A turn that is still making progress is never killed by the wall-clock cap.

## Steps

1. In `lego/agent-image/driver/src/session.ts` (~220-245), reset an inactivity timer on every ACP update and model-proxy exchange. While a tool call runs, pause it and apply the tool call's own limit.
2. Add `BEX_AGENT_TURN_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS` (`config.ts`), injected by bex-api. Keep `BEX_AGENT_TURN_TIMEOUT_MS` as a hard ceiling, no longer than the 24 h sandbox lifetime.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Driver tests with fake timers: updates every 10 minutes survive past the idle bound's multiple; a silent agent fails at the idle bound; a long tool call is not cut early; the ceiling still fires.
