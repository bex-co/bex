## Objective

Stop failing and deleting sessions on short platform stalls.

## Steps

1. Make the retry window in `agentsessions/completion.go` (~126) configurable, defaulting to 10 minutes (an env var listed in `lego/backend/AGENTS.md` and `.env.example`).
2. Only transient errors count toward the window; a success resets it. NotFound still fails at once.
3. An unknown sandbox state counts as transient, not Errored (`sandbox/models.go:239`).
4. Give each status read in `finalize` a 30 s timeout, and give the exec HTTP client a timeout (`cmd/api/main.go:1354`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `completion_test.go`: nine minutes of transient errors leaves the session working; ten fails it; a success resets the count; NotFound fails at once.

## Done (2026-11-12)

`BEX_AGENT_STATUS_RETRY_WINDOW` (default 10m). Transient read failures count toward it, any success resets the count, and NotFound fails at once. Unknown or empty states are transient and name themselves when they outlast the window. Status reads are bounded at 10 s and transcript reads at 2 min; the exec client gets bounded dial and response-header timeouts. A session still unreadable after its turn's ceiling plus the window fails as `status_deadline`, so no error class holds a slot forever. The new `AgentSessionStatusReadsFailing` alert has promtool tests (`completion_test.go`, `deploy/gitops/base/rules/alerts_test.yml`).
