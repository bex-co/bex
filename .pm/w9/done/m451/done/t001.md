## Objective

Let the ssh-gateway put shell-session rows into the workspace audit log without widening what a stolen gateway credential can do beyond what this needs.

## Context

- The gateway connects as the scoped `bex_ssh_gateway` role with table-wide access only to `ssh_sessions` and `shell_ticket_nonces` (w7/m56, [ADR035](../../../../../docs/ADR035-ssh.md) "Database blast radius").
- Shell sessions are written to `ssh_sessions` (`lego/backend/internal/store/sshsessions.go`), which no tenant surface reads; the only reader is `AgentSessionSSHActivity`.
- Needs w9/m450's `source_ip`/`user_agent` columns and their REST rendering.

## Steps

1. Choose: (A) **recommended** grant the role `INSERT` only on `audit_events` (no SELECT, UPDATE or DELETE) in a migration and write rows through a store method; or (B) keep the grants and have `audit.Service.List` merge `ssh_sessions` into its pages (keeps the role narrow but breaks the single keyset cursor, the CSV path and one-table retention).
2. Record the choice and its residual in ADR035 (with A: a stolen gateway credential can insert forged audit rows but cannot read, alter or delete any; compare `.pm/FUTURE-MAYBE.md`'s ssh-gateway RLS entry).
3. Apply the grant in the migration and the local/production role setup.

- [x] The gateway role can insert an audit row and cannot select, update or delete one (real-DB test).
