## Done (2026-11-06)

Migration 0205 adds `agent_session_decisions` (`dcs-`). A paused turn reads as `needs_input` with `waitingFor` approval|user, and `outcome.pendingDecision` holds the oldest open decision (several may be open in parallel; the mirror carries a count). The answer route is REST `POST /v1/agent-sessions/{id}/decisions/{decisionId}`, GraphQL `answerAgentSessionDecision` and MCP `answer_agent_session_decision`. It needs `can_create`, is idempotent and is audited; refusals are 400 INVALID, 404 NOT_FOUND and 409 ANSWERED/EXPIRED. Expiry is 24h (approval → deny, question → continue). A trigger cancels open decisions when the turn ends. Each decision fires the `agent_session_needs_input` webhook and the `agent_needs_decision` push once, and wait settles on it. The gateway serves `/agent-decisions` with its own await limiter and quick-await budget.
