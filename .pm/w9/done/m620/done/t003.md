## Objective

The dashboard's billing and usage pages follow the new relation: a Developer sees invoices and costs read-only, a Contributor and a Viewer get the no-access state with its reason, and no other page leaks the figures.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/usage/` (`billing-summary-cards.tsx`, `billing-onboarding.tsx`, `billing-lifecycle-alert.tsx`, `agent-model-card.tsx`, their skeletons).
- The workspace navigation entry for Billing; any free-hours, pipeline-minutes or bandwidth figure shown outside the billing page.

## Steps

1. Gate the pages and the nav entry on `canViewBilling`; keep payment-method and spend-limit edits on `canManageBilling`.
2. Check pending and ready states at desktop and narrow-mobile widths.
3. Run `yarn lint` and `yarn test`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] As a Developer: invoices and costs visible, no edit controls. As a Contributor or Viewer: no billing figures anywhere, with the reason shown.
