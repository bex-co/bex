## Done (2026-11-14)

Production, QA user, Scale workspace `bex-canary`, deploy pin `c99e7815ebec` (includes `879c64ef8`). The checker was this machine, `162.224.81.143`. Environment and service rules were left at allow-all.

- 13:22 UTC: free Postgres `dpg-dbs60vfla3oc73cu7j30` created (public, allow-all). Baseline at 13:25:58: `psql` connected and `hello-go.onbex.co` answered 200.
- 13:26:45: `setWorkspaceIPAllowList` set the rules to `[192.0.2.0/24]`, which excludes the checker.
  - The Postgres refused at 13:26:47: `SSL error: unexpected eof` from the SNI proxy.
  - `hello-go` answered 403 from 13:28:28, about 1 min 43 s later (operator reconcile, then the Traefik middleware).
  - `GET /v1/owners/{id}` returned `[{cidrBlock: 192.0.2.0/24, …}]`.
- 13:28:28: reset to `0.0.0.0/0` + `::/0`. `psql` reconnected at 13:28:31 and `hello-go` answered 200 again at 13:30:35.
- Audit log (`GET /v1/owners/{id}/audit-logs`): `UpdateIPAllowListEvent` at 13:26:45 and 13:28:28, user actor, `can_manage`.
- REST owners, GraphQL `workspace.ipAllowList` and MCP `list_workspaces` all show the reset allow-all list. The dashboard shows the Inbound IP Rules card under Workspace Settings.
- Cleanup: rules are back to allow-all and the Postgres was deleted (404). Side effect: for about 3 to 4 minutes, other sessions on this machine could not reach `bex-canary`'s public web services, and for about 2 seconds its databases.
