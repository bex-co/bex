## Objective

Record workspace member management under Render's audit-log names: an invite is `InviteToTeamEvent`, an accepted invite `AcceptTeamInviteEvent`, a role change `ChangeTeamMemberRoleEvent`, and a removal or a member leaving `RemoveUserFromTeamEvent`.

## Context

- [render.com/docs/audit-logs](https://render.com/docs/audit-logs) (read 2026-11-16), workspace member management: `InviteToTeamEvent` (user invited), `RemoveUserFromTeamEvent` (user removed), `AcceptTeamInviteEvent` (invite accepted), `ChangeTeamMemberRoleEvent` (member's role changed). All four are in the pinned enum (`lego/backend/internal/api/openapi/render-public-api-1.json`, `components.schemas.auditLog.properties.event`).
- bex's verbs: `core.AuditVerbMemberInvited` (`members.Invite`), `AuditVerbMemberRoleChanged`, `AuditVerbMemberRemoved`, `AuditVerbInviteAccepted`, `AuditVerbMemberLeft` (`members.Leave`) in `lego/backend/internal/core/audit.go` (~396-408), recorded by `RecordMemberInvited` … `RecordMemberLeft` (~1021-1055) after the change succeeds; the verbs live in `lego/backend/internal/members/service.go`.
- `renderEvents` (`lego/backend/internal/audit/service.go`) maps none of them, so REST answers the bex names.
- Keep `members.Leave` stored distinctly from `members.Remove` (the events feed tells them apart, w5/m102); both render as `RemoveUserFromTeamEvent`, since Render documents no leave event, and the actor tells them apart.
- Render documents no event for resending or revoking an invite: those keep bex's names.

## Steps

1. Add the five mappings to `renderEvents`.
2. Confirm each verb still records once, after success, and a refused call records only its `error` row.
3. Keep the existing role from/to metadata.
4. Extend `internal/audit` tests to assert the REST `event` value for each verb, and that resend/revoke are unchanged.
