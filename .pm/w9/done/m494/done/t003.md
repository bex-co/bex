## Done — 2026-11-19

- Unit tests (`depscache_test.go`, `xdgcache_test.go`): every runtime's recipe (mount on the build `RUN`, seed first in the loader, save step right after, none without the S3 store), and the loader's seed on bash (missing, empty, brought by the source, a link, the mount emptied). The save step on sh (links kept as links, modes kept, a link directory skipped, stale mount content dropped, the bound). The pipeline across builds: build, save, export, seed solve, loader. Same key restores everything; another key drops `deps/` and keeps `xdg/`; an unkeyed archive or build keeps none; the shared bound; deps alone are archived. Corrupt, climbing, link-through and oversized archives empty both mounts. The wrapper's key: the hash of the part up to `COPY . .`, unchanged by later lines, changed by another image, empty when hashing fails. The log keeps the save step out, and a failure header with both mounts names the command. The GNU-userland tests ran in `golang:1.27-bookworm`.
- BuildKit `v0.30.0` drill of the generated `buildkit` container, recorded in [ADR060 D3's w9/m494 amendment](../../../../../docs/ADR060-build-worker-reliability-and-performance.md#d3-amendment-w9m494--native-dependency-directories-across-builds). Ruby: `bundle install` took 8.1 s cold and 0.3 s after a source-only commit. Same source stayed `CACHED`. Ruby 3.4 → 3.3 reinstalled every gem, and clear cache installed cold. Go: `go build` took 6.3 s cold and 0.5 s warm with no download line; random bytes as the archive built clean; clear cache downloaded again. Rust: 11 crates downloaded and 12 compiled cold; warm, `Compiling drill` alone in 0.32 s.
- Elixir is covered by the unit tests only (its recipe and directories); the drill ran Ruby, Go and Rust.
