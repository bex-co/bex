## Objective

While `maintenanceMode.enabled`, every host the service serves answers with the default maintenance page at the captured status code — app pods untouched (no scale change, no deploy). Pick the mechanism deliberately: Traefik errors/redirect middleware vs pointing the Ingress at a shared responder (the static-server or activator pattern — the activator already serves an interstitial for sleeping services; prefer reusing one responder over minting a third). Record the choice + why on this task.

## Context

- Defined interactions: maintenance owns public routing while enabled, but does not override replica policy. Auto-sleep and suspension may scale the workload to zero while the maintenance responder remains public; deploys proceed and the page persists until disabled.
