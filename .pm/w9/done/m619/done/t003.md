## Objective

A Contributor cannot add, verify or delete a custom domain or change a service's inbound IP rules (Render: ❌ "Modify service configuration", ❌ "Manage inbound IP rules").

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/apps/domains.go` `addOne`, `VerifyDomain`, `DeleteDomain` on `can_operate`.
- `IPAllowList` row in `servicePatchTable` and `SetIPAllowList`.
- Environment- and workspace-level IP rules already need an Admin (ADR032, w9/m388); leave them.

## Steps

1. Move the domain verbs and the service IP rules to `can_create`.
2. Update their tests on REST, GraphQL and MCP.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/apps/domains.go`, `lego/backend/internal/apps/service.go`, `lego/backend/internal/apps/settings.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Contributor's domain add, verify and delete and service IP-rule change answer 403 on every surface; a Developer's succeed.

## Out of scope

- Render's "varies by resource type" for a Developer's IP rules: no per-type rule is documented.
