## Objective

A Contributor cannot change a datastore's settings, plan or version; restart, suspend, resume and failover stay theirs.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/postgres/service.go` `updatePostgres`, `setPlan`, `SetVersion`; `lego/backend/internal/keyvalue/service.go` `updateKeyValue`, `setPlan`.
- Stays on `can_operate`: `postgres/lifecycle.go` `Restart`, `Failover`, `setSuspended`; `keyvalue/service.go` `setSuspended`.
- Dry runs (`dryRun`) take the same relation as the write.

## Steps

1. Move the setting verbs to `can_create`, dry runs included.
2. Update the REST, GraphQL and MCP tests.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/postgres/service.go`, `lego/backend/internal/keyvalue/service.go` and their tests

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Contributor's `PATCH /v1/postgres/{id}`, `PATCH /v1/key-value/{id}`, plan and version changes answer 403 and change nothing; a Developer's succeed.
- [ ] A Contributor still restarts, suspends, resumes and fails over a database.

## Out of scope

- Postgres credentials and connection details (already `can_view_sensitive`).
