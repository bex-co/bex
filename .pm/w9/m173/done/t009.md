## Objective

A hung step must not hold a shared `bex-ci` runner for GitHub's 6 h default. On 2026-11-06 the go-lint run 42181112873 hung in `actions/checkout` from 08:54 to 12:30 (cancelled), and deploy tests queued behind it.

## Done (2026-11-06)

- The 20 `bex-ci` jobs that had no job-level `timeout-minutes` now have one. Each budget is about 2.5× the maximum of the last 15 successful runs (measured per job), with a 15-minute floor. For example: backend 180, operator 100, go-lint 60, govulncheck 125, gitops render 125, secret-scan 90.
- `scripts/github-actions-validate.sh` check 10 fails the build if a `bex-ci` job lacks one. It is a stdlib-only text scan, since the runners have no YAML library. It was checked to fail on a removed timeout.
