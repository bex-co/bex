## Objective

Fix the two defects the production live check (t008) surfaced on `ags-dbs60nnla3oc73cu7j20`.

## Context

- The driver named every turn's setup tool calls `bex-setup-<n>` (`lego/agent-image/driver/src/repo-setup.ts`). Turn 2's failing `setup: broken` therefore reused turn 1's id `bex-setup-1`. In the dashboard it replaced turn 1's `setup: secret-probe` ("Ran 2 commands · 1 failed" under turn 1). The REST transcript kept both parts.
- Turn 2 read "transcript read failed / Conversation only partially loaded", yet bex-api logged `captured transcript … turn=2 … inserted=3` at 13:27:09.
  - Every bex-api replica runs a Completer, with no leader election. One replica failed the turn, captured its transcript and terminated the sandbox.
  - The other replica's reap of the still-listed session then read a sandbox that was gone (`ErrNotFound`, not logged), and `captureTranscript` overwrote the complete verdict.

## Steps

1. Number setup tool call ids by turn: `bex-setup-<turn>-<n>`.
2. A failed transcript read marks a turn only when no harvest has recorded a verdict on it (`store.MarkAgentSessionTurnUnread`); a successful harvest still always records its own.
3. Tests for both, including a mutation check of the Completer change.
