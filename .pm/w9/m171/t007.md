## Objective

`upload_agent_attachment` advertises 4 MiB decoded, but `/mcp` sits behind the global 2 MiB `BEX_MAX_BODY_BYTES`, so ~1.5 MiB+ uploads fail at the transport with 413. Make the advertised and enforced limits agree.

## Context

- Found by the 2026-11-04 live acceptance with the official MCP SDK (1.4 MiB ok, 1.6 MiB transport 413).

## Steps

1. Either lower maxMCPUploadBytes to fit base64 + JSON overhead under the /mcp body limit, or give /mcp a dedicated body limit sized for 4 MiB decoded; pick the smaller blast radius and record it in ADR095.
2. Tool description states the real limit; a just-over-limit call returns AGENT_ATTACHMENT_TOO_LARGE, not a transport 413.

## Files

- lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/limits.go, mcp.go, api/server.go (if body limit), tests

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An upload at the advertised maximum succeeds over the real /mcp transport.
