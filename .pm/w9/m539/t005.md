2. For an Admin, the panel shows a two-way switch ("Your keys" / "All members' keys"); it defaults to the caller's own. In the all-members view, every row names its creator. 3. A non-Admin sees no switch. Copy goes in all nine locales.
4. Update the panel's tests and add cases: a Developer sees no switch and only their keys; an Admin switches, sees another's key without a gear, and revokes it.
