## Done (2026-11-06)

Every dispatch snapshots the enabled skills plus the ones invoked this turn. They are capped at 1 MiB enabled and 128 KiB per expanded document, tarred, and copied by a platform ticket into `/var/lib/bex-agent/skills` (`/bex/skills`). User-only skills get `disable-model-invocation: true`. The driver links each into `~/.agents`, `~/.claude` and `~/.codex` `skills/`, skipping symlinked or file folders and never overwriting an entry. It adds an overlay index capped at 8 KiB (names and paths only for engines with native loaders) and prepends JSON-quoted invocation lines. Dispatch failure codes are SKILL_COPY_FAILED, SKILL_LIST_FAILED and SKILL_TOO_LARGE.
