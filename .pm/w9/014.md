# Codify static-site's manual prod fixes into infra/gitops

Filed during the w9/m44 closeout merge (2026-07-18), from `w9/done/012.md`'s residual: three of the five static-site chain fixes were **manual `kubectl` interventions on non-Argo prod resources** — working now, but they **drift on the next redeploy** until codified.

What was applied by hand on prod (see `w9/done/012.md` for the full chain):

1. **`bex-registry-pull` re-mint** (chain #3): prod's copy authenticated as the stale `bex-puller` (no Zot ACL grant under per-App mode); re-minted from the `bex-registry-push` `bex-builder` credential. **Codify:** re-run `scripts/registry-secrets.sh` against prod, or add `bex-registry-pull` to the out-of-band bootstrap so it can't drift.
2. **static-server ClusterRoleBinding** (chain #4's live half): `bex-static-server-apps-reader` covering both static-server SAs. The durable config fix shipped (`bb92f86d`, `config/staticserver` Role→ClusterRole) — verify the shipped manifests supersede the hand-applied binding, then drop it.
3. **static-server deployment drift** (chain #5): the Ingress-targeted `default/bex-static-server` ran a stale image and lacked `BEX_BASE_DOMAIN`; hand-updated to the working image + `BEX_BASE_DOMAIN=onbex.co`. There is also a second, unmanaged `bex-system/bex-static-server`. **Codify (the real fix):** make the static-server(s) **Argo-managed** from `config/staticserver` so they track the current image + config automatically; add `BEX_BASE_DOMAIN` to the `bex-static-config` ConfigMap (the design already `envFrom`s it — prod's ConfigMap just lacks the key); **consolidate to ONE static-server** placed per the Ingress topology (in the App namespace, since the operator points static-site Ingresses at a same-namespace Service).

**Evidence when done:** Argo shows the static-server app Synced/Healthy tracking the CI image; `kubectl` shows one static-server, config-complete, no hand-applied residue; `scripts/samples-lifecycle.sh static-site` stays GREEN through an operator/platform redeploy (the drift scenario that motivated this note).
