## Objective

ADR097 Blueprints §1 says names are unique in the workspace, but REST/GraphQL/MCP do not enforce it (`store/store.go:2118` skips carriers; `createBuildSourceCarrier` does not check), so a later Blueprint sync fails with "used more than once" (`blueprint_build_sources.go:211-213`). Refuse a duplicate name on create and rename with Render's error shape. Also: §2, a root/`ungrouped` declaration of a project-scoped source must be refused (the check runs only when `b.project != ""`, line 358); §8, export must write project-scoped sources under `projects[].buildSources` and keep `fromGroup` and other non-literal env forms (`blueprint_generate.go:491-520`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Duplicate names are refused on all three surfaces; a root declaration of a project-scoped source is refused; export of a project-scoped source with `fromGroup` re-plans as a no-op (tests).
