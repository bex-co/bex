## Objective

Give the top-up rows a Stripe meter and price so they appear on the invoice as "Cron job minimum", attached to every existing and new workspace subscription.

## Context

- Contract item sync: `lego/backend/internal/billing/contracts.go` (`EnsureContract`, `resolvePriceIDs`, `syncContractItems`, `validatePriceCompliance`).
- Meter targets for settlement: `billing/settle.go` (`meterTargets`, `AwaitMeteredUsage`).
- Price IDs and env: `.env.example` and the env inventory in `lego/backend/AGENTS.md`; `.env.example` must mirror `.env` names (repo rule).

## Steps

1. Add the meter (event name, micro-USD value, sum aggregation) and a metered price at $0.000001 per unit, named "Cron job minimum", following the agent-model meter's provisioning path.
2. Wire its price id into `resolvePriceIDs` / `syncContractItems` so existing subscriptions gain the item on their next sync and new ones get it on create.
3. Make `meterTargets` / `AwaitMeteredUsage` wait for the new meter like the others, so settlement does not invoice before the top-up lands.
4. Document any new env var in `.env.example` and `lego/backend/AGENTS.md`.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/billing/contracts.go`, `settle.go`, `stripe.go`
- `.env.example`, `lego/backend/AGENTS.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] In the Stripe sandbox, a test workspace's subscription carries the "Cron job minimum" item after a contract sync.
- [ ] A $1.00 top-up row exported to the sandbox appears on the upcoming invoice as "Cron job minimum $1.00".
