# 198 — `workspaces.Service.{SetPipelineSpendLimit,UpdateMember,RemoveMember,Delete}` and `authorizeWorkspaceCreation` omit `WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit`, recording `"status": "success"` audit rows on `400`/`404` refusals, double-auditing member mutations, and auditing read-only `ResumeWorkspaceCreation`

Why: `w31/121` tracked four `workspaces.Service` write verbs (`SetBillingEmail`, `SetOverlappingDeployPolicy`, `Rename`, `ChangePlan`) that omit `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)`, but four additional workspace write families in the same package still authorize without deferring allowed-write auditing: (1) `SetPipelineSpendLimit` (`workspaces/pipeline.go:86`, behind GraphQL `Mutation.setPipelineSpendLimit`) emits `"status": "success"` on `400 INVALID_SPEND_LIMIT` refusals and on unchanged no-op writes; (2) `memberSubject` (`workspaces/service.go:1118`, shared by `UpdateMember` and `RemoveMember` behind REST `PATCH`/`DELETE /v1/owners/{ownerId}/members/{userId}`) emits `"status": "success"` `workspaces.UpdateMember` / `workspaces.RemoveMember` rows on `404 member not found`, `400` bad role, and `409` refusals, and **double-audits** successful mutations alongside `s.Members.ChangeRole` / `s.Members.Remove`'s canonical `ChangeMemberRoleEvent` / `RemoveMemberEvent` rows; (3) `authorizeWorkspaceCreation` (`workspaces/service.go:508`, backing `PrepareWorkspaceCreation`, `ResumeWorkspaceCreation`, `FinalizeWorkspaceCreation`, `CancelWorkspaceCreation`) emits `"status": "success"` on validation/`404`/`402`/`409` refusals and even on read-only GraphQL `Query.workspaceCreationAttempt` (because `workspaces.ResumeWorkspaceCreation` is missing from `core/audit.go`'s `readVerbs`); and (4) `Delete` (`workspaces/service.go:1860`) emits `"status": "success"` `workspaces.Delete` on mistyped confirmation (`400`) and org-delete guard refusals.
