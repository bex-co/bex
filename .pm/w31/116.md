# 116 — `webhooks.Service.{Update,Delete,SetEnabled,Resend}` and `registrycreds.Service.{Create,Update,Delete}` omit `WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit`, recording `"status": "success"` audit-log rows on `400`/`404`/`409` refusals across MCP, REST, and GraphQL

Why: `webhooks.Service.Create` (`lego/backend/internal/webhooks/service.go:576,617`, fixed in `w4/m321`) wraps `ctx` in `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)` so refused webhook creates write zero audit rows, yet its four siblings in the same file — `SetEnabled` (`:788`), `Delete` (`:812`), `Resend` (`:900`), and `Update` (`:964`) — plus all three write verbs in `registrycreds.Service` (`Create` at `lego/backend/internal/registrycreds/service.go:231`, `Update` at `:312`, `Delete` at `:395`) authorize without deferring, so every refused call (`404 not found`, `400 WEBHOOK_URL_INVALID`, `409 WEBHOOK_ENDPOINT_DISABLED`, `400 bad request`) writes a false `"status": "success"` row (`DeleteWebhookEvent`, `UpdateWebhookEvent`, `webhooks.Resend`, `registrycreds.Create/Update/Delete`) into the workspace audit log across MCP, REST, and GraphQL.

- **Attribution:** server (`lego/backend/internal/webhooks`, `lego/backend/internal/registrycreds`)
- **Root cause:**
  - `lego/backend/internal/webhooks/service.go:788` (`SetEnabled`), `:812` (`Delete`), `:900` (`Resend`), and `:964` (`Update`) call `s.Authorize` / `s.AuthorizeTarget` without `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)`, unlike `Create` at `:576,617` (which calls `s.RecordAllowedWrite` only after `CreateWebhookEndpoint` succeeds).
  - `lego/backend/internal/registrycreds/service.go:231` (`Create`), `:312` (`Update`), and `:395` (`Delete`) call `s.Authorize(ctx, core.RelCanManage)` without `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)`, emitting `"success"` before input validation, quota checks, `GetRegistryCredential`, `REGISTRY_CREDENTIAL_IN_USE`, and OpenBao writes.
- **Fix:**
  - Wrap `ctx` with `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)` in `webhooks.Service.{SetEnabled,Delete,Resend,Update}` and `registrycreds.Service.{Create,Update,Delete}`, then call `s.RecordAllowedWrite(...)` on the success path right before returning (matching `webhooks.Service.Create` at `webhooks/service.go:576,617`, preserving each verb's exact audit verb name, relation, workspace ID, and target — e.g. `core.WebhookTarget(id)` / `core.WebhookAttemptTarget(endpointID, sourceAttemptID)`).
  - Surface impact: fixes MCP, REST, and GraphQL together because all three delegate to `webhooks.Service` and `registrycreds.Service`.
