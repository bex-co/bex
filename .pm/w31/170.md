# 170 — `core.QueryTimeWindow` and `envgroups.envGroupListFilter` never reject inverted time ranges (`After > Before`), silently returning `200 []` across 8 resource-list endpoints

Why: Complementing `w31/155` (`events`), `w31/159` (`agentsessions`, `webhooks`, `audit`, `deploys`, `jobs`), and `w31/167` (`apps.parseBuildSourceFilter`, `sandbox` group pages), the shared REST time-window parser `core.QueryTimeWindow` (`lego/backend/internal/core/pagination.go:255-265`) and `envgroups.envGroupListFilter` (`lego/backend/internal/envgroups/rest.go:27-47`) parse `createdBefore`/`createdAfter` and `updatedBefore`/`updatedAfter` without checking `after <= before`. Eight resource-list families (`GET /v1/services`, `GET /v1/postgres`, `GET /v1/key-value` & `/v1/redis`, `GET /v1/env-groups`, `GET /v1/projects`, `GET /v1/disks`, `GET /v1/registrycredentials`, and `GET /v1/services/{id}/runs` & `/v1/cron-jobs/{id}/runs`) silently accept impossible inverted windows (`createdAfter > createdBefore`) and answer `200 []` instead of `400 bad_request`, contradicting `logs` and `metrics` (which properly reject inverted `startTime > endTime` with `400`).

- **Root cause:**
  - `lego/backend/internal/core/pagination.go:255-265`: `QueryTimeWindow(q, beforeKey, afterKey)` parses `before` and `after` independently via `QueryTime` and returns `TimeWindow{Before: before, After: after}` without checking `!before.IsZero() && !after.IsZero() && after.After(before)`. Callers across 9 packages inherit the omission:
    - `lego/backend/internal/apps/rest.go:941,945,1403` (`GET /v1/services`, `GET /v1/{services,cron-jobs}/{id}/runs`)
    - `lego/backend/internal/apps/disks.go:463,467` (`GET /v1/disks`)
    - `lego/backend/internal/postgres/rest.go:127,131` (`GET /v1/postgres`)
    - `lego/backend/internal/keyvalue/rest.go:326,331` (`GET /v1/key-value`, `/v1/redis`)
    - `lego/backend/internal/projects/rest.go:135,139` (`GET /v1/projects`)
    - `lego/backend/internal/registrycreds/rest.go:140,144` (`GET /v1/registrycredentials`)
    - plus `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/rest.go:49` and `lego/backend/internal/webhooks/rest.go:235` (REST halves of `w31/159`).
  - `lego/backend/internal/envgroups/rest.go:27-47`: `envGroupListFilter` calls `core.QueryTime` directly for `createdBefore`/`createdAfter` and `updatedBefore`/`updatedAfter` instead of `core.QueryTimeWindow`, and `ListEnvGroupsFiltered` (`envgroups/service.go:294-328`) never checks `After > Before`.
  - `lego/backend/internal/environments/rest.go:36-43`: `filterEnvironmentList` (`GET /v1/environments`) likewise calls `core.QueryTime` directly for `createdBefore`/`createdAfter` instead of `core.QueryTimeWindow` and never checks `createdAfter > createdBefore` (verified live 2026-11-27 on deployed pin `207a050b22` / `7d1dce861173` against disposable project `prj-dc50ver7laqs73bafc40` + environment `evm-dc50ver7laqs73bafc4g` in `bex-canary` `tea-daif693dqjvc73e7as3g`, both deleted and confirmed `404`: control `GET /v1/environments?projectId=prj-dc50ver7laqs73bafc40`, `rndr-id: ca324b1e-26e9-b85a` → `200` with 1 environment; inverted `GET /v1/environments?projectId=prj-dc50ver7laqs73bafc40&createdAfter=2027-01-01T00:00:00Z&createdBefore=2020-01-01T00:00:00Z`, `rndr-id: fb8d2067-d151-882d` → `200 []`).
- **Fix:**
  - In `core.QueryTimeWindow` (`lego/backend/internal/core/pagination.go:255-265`), reject `!before.IsZero() && !after.IsZero() && after.After(before)` with `fmt.Errorf("%w: %s must not be after %s", ErrBadRequest, afterKey, beforeKey)` (and add a reusable `TimeWindow.Validate(afterKey, beforeKey string) error` helper so service-layer filters share the exact same check).
  - Switch `envgroups.envGroupListFilter` (`lego/backend/internal/envgroups/rest.go:34-45`) and `environments.filterEnvironmentList` (`lego/backend/internal/environments/rest.go:36-43`) to `core.QueryTimeWindow`, and call `TimeWindow.Validate` in `ListEnvGroupsFiltered` (`envgroups/service.go:294`) as well so direct callers cannot bypass it.
  - Note: coordinate with `w31/155`, `w31/159`, and `w31/167` so every time-range filter across MCP, REST, and GraphQL shares one consistent `After > Before` check and error wording.
