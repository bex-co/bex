# 073 — `mcputil.NumberOrString` overflows `float64` into `int64` without range checks, turning large numbers like `create_postgres.version: 1e30` into `"-9223372036854775808"`

Why: `mcputil.NumberOrString` (`lego/backend/internal/mcputil/mcputil.go:195-209`) validates only `x == math.Trunc(x)` before executing `strconv.FormatInt(int64(x), 10)` — so any out-of-range whole `float64` (e.g. `1e30`, `1e20`, `-1e30`) silently overflows `int64` to `-9223372036854775808` (on amd64) and leaks that garbage integer into downstream domain errors on MCP `create_postgres`.


    In Go, converting a `float64` outside `[math.MinInt64, math.MaxInt64]` to `int64` produces `-9223372036854775808` on x86-64 (implementation-defined, and `math.Trunc(1e30) == 1e30` passes the fractional check; note also `float64(math.MaxInt64)` rounds up to `2^63`, which itself overflows `int64`).
- **Fix:**
  - In `mcputil.NumberOrString` (`lego/backend/internal/mcputil/mcputil.go:199-203`), reject `math.IsNaN(x) || math.IsInf(x, 0) || x != math.Trunc(x)` as `must be a whole number`, and reject `x < -9007199254740991 || x > 9007199254740991` (JSON/IEEE-754 exact safe integer bounds, or `< math.MinInt64 || >= 9223372036854775808`) with `fmt.Errorf("%w: %s is out of range", core.ErrBadRequest, param)`.
