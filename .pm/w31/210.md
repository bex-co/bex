# 210 — `scope_matrix_overrides.go` omits `OpClassSensitive` on `clone_env_group` (MCP, REST, GraphQL) and omits `OpClassMint` on all 8 `AuthorizeMintClass` workspace-creation operations (leaving `Query.workspaceCreationPolicy` and `Query.workspaceCreationAttempt` at `OpClassRead`)

Why: `envgroups.Service.CloneEnvGroup` (`lego/backend/internal/envgroups/clone.go:36-48`) reads every secret env var and secret file value server-side under `core.RelCanViewSensitive` + `authorizeGroupSensitiveFresh` (and `REST POST /v1/env-groups/{id}/clone` reveals those copied values in the response via `committedRevealed`, `lego/backend/internal/envgroups/rest.go:160`), yet `lego/backend/internal/api/scope_matrix_overrides.go` omits all three clone operations (`scope_matrix_ops.go:42,491,1077`), leaving them at `core.OpClassWrite` instead of `core.OpClassSensitive` like `link_env_group` (`:103-105`); similarly, all 8 workspace-creation operations backed by `AuthorizeMintClass` (`workspaces/organizations.go:237`, `workspaces/service.go:465,505,1190`) are omitted from `scope_matrix_overrides.go`, leaving `GQL Query.workspaceCreationPolicy` and `GQL Query.workspaceCreationAttempt` (`scope_matrix_ops.go:464-465`) at `core.OpClassRead` (`bex.read`) despite requiring `AuthorizeMintClass` + `RelCanCreate` (`bex.write`) and leaving the 6 workspace-creation mutations/tools (`scope_matrix_ops.go:37,62,76,120,137,505`) at `core.OpClassWrite` instead of `core.OpClassMint`.
