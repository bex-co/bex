# 143 — `apikeys.Service` leaks raw internal Kratos identity UUIDs in `APIKey.createdBy` across MCP, REST, and GraphQL

Why: `apikeys.Service` embeds `*core.Base` (which already carries `UserIDs core.UserIDLookup`), yet `CreateAPIKey` (`lego/backend/internal/apikeys/service.go:360-425`), `ListAPIKeys` (`:554-600`), and `UpdateAPIKeyPermissions` (`:740-788`) return `APIKey.CreatedBy` verbatim from Hydra's `bex.co/created-by` metadata (`apiKeyFromHydra`, `:972-974`) without projecting human subjects through `core.PublicUserIDs`, leaking raw internal Kratos identity UUIDs across MCP `list_api_keys` / `create_api_key` / `update_api_key_permissions`, REST `/v1/api-keys*`, and GraphQL `apiKeys` / `createApiKey` / `updateApiKeyPermissions` in violation of `docs/ADR020-identifiers.md`.

- **Attribution:** server (`lego/backend/internal/apikeys`)
- **Root cause:**
  - `hydraAPIKeys` stores the creator's internal Kratos subject in Hydra metadata `bex.co/created-by` (`lego/backend/internal/apikeys/service.go:957,986-988`) and `apiKeyFromHydra` (`:972-974`) copies it into `APIKey.CreatedBy`.
  - Internal authorization and teardown paths (`mintedByCaller` `:704-706`, `RequireGrantableKeyGrant` `:386,780`, `AccountTeardown` `:190`, `KeyCreator` `:1178,1198`) legitimately require the internal Kratos subject.
  - However, the three client-facing verbs `CreateAPIKey` (`:360-426`), `ListAPIKeys` (`:554-600`), and `UpdateAPIKeyPermissions` (`:740-788`) return `APIKey` straight to MCP, REST, and GraphQL without projecting `APIKey.CreatedBy` through `core.PublicUserIDs(ctx, s.UserIDs, ...)` — even though `apikeys.Service` already embeds `*core.Base` (which carries `UserIDs core.UserIDLookup`, wired at `lego/backend/internal/api/server.go`).
  - Note: `objectStorageAdmin.keyWire` (`lego/backend/internal/api/object_storage_admin.go:69-83`, REST/GraphQL S3 access keys) has the same raw `CreatedBy` pass-through if fixed in the same sweep or tracked separately.
- **Fix:**

  - Keep internal Hydra metadata (`bex.co/created-by`), `APIKeyStore.Get`/`List`, `KeyCreator`, `KeyCreatorWithSignIn`, `mintedByCaller`, `RequireGrantableKeyGrant`, and `AccountTeardown` on the internal Kratos subject so authorization, permission checks, and account deletion remain exact.
