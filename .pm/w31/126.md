# 126 — `apikeys.Service.{CreateAPIKey,RevokeAPIKey,UpdateAPIKeyPermissions}` and `sshkeys.Service.{Create,Delete}` omit `WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit`, recording `"status": "success"` audit-log rows on `400`/`403`/`404`/`409` refusals across MCP, REST, and GraphQL

Why: Credential-management write verbs in `lego/backend/internal/apikeys/service.go` (`CreateAPIKey` at `:331`, `RevokeAPIKey` at `:642`, `UpdateAPIKeyPermissions` at `:752`) and `lego/backend/internal/sshkeys/service.go` (`Create` at `:101`, `Delete` at `:161`) call `s.Authorize` without `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)`, so every refused call (`404 NOT_FOUND` on nonexistent key IDs, `400` invalid label/public key/permissions, `403` creator/grantability check, `409` duplicate SSH key or key rate limit) writes a false `"status": "success"` row into the workspace audit log across MCP, REST, and GraphQL.

    - `CreateAPIKey` (`:331`) calls `s.Authorize(ctx, relOwnKeys)` without `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)` before `TrimmedLabel` (`:347`), `ValidateKeyGrant` (`:356`), `RequireGrantableKeyGrant` (`:386`), `CreationLimiter` (`:394`), and `createBoundAPIKey` (`:404`).
