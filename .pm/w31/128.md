# 128 — `postgres.Service.Recover`, `postgres.Service.DeletePostgres`, and `keyvalue.Service.DeleteKeyValue` omit `WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit`, recording `"status": "success"` audit-log rows on `400`/`403` refusals across MCP, REST, and GraphQL

Why: Unlike sibling write verbs in the same packages (`postgres.Service.CreatePostgres`, `Restart`, `CreateExport`, `createUser`, `DeleteUser`, `DeleteCredential`, `fetchToChange` and `keyvalue.Service.CreateKeyValue`, `fetchToChange`), `postgres.Service.Recover` (`lego/backend/internal/postgres/recovery.go:420-421`), `postgres.Service.DeletePostgres` (`lego/backend/internal/postgres/service.go:1087-1088`), and `keyvalue.Service.DeleteKeyValue` (`lego/backend/internal/keyvalue/service.go:803-804`) call `fetchLiveDatabase` / `fetchLiveKeyValue` without `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)` — so every call refused after authorization (`400` bad request, `403` protected-environment admin required, `403` billing gate, or fresh-authz failure) emits a false `"status": "success"` audit row across MCP, REST, and GraphQL.
