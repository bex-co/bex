# 160 — `members.Service.ChangeOrganizationRole` omits `WorkspaceRoles` (breaking NonNull GraphQL `[OrganizationMemberWorkspace!]!` and returning `null` on MCP), and `RevokeOrganizationInvite` omits `AuthorizeMintClass`, `AuthorizeFreshOn`, and `WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit`

Why: Two sibling gaps in `lego/backend/internal/members/organizations.go` break organization membership contracts across MCP and GraphQL: (1) `ChangeOrganizationRole` (`organizations.go:533-551`) never populates `OrganizationMemberView.WorkspaceRoles`, leaving it `nil` — which causes GraphQL `Mutation.changeOrganizationMemberRole` to fail NonNull serialization whenever a client selects `workspaceRoles` (`[OrganizationMemberWorkspace!]!`, `organizations_api.go:46-47`) and causes MCP `change_organization_member_role` to return `"workspaceRoles": null` even when `workspaces` is non-empty; and (2) `RevokeOrganizationInvite` (`organizations.go:469-481`) is the only organization-membership write in `organizations.go` that omits `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)`, `s.AuthorizeMintClass(ctx)`, and `s.AuthorizeFreshOn(ctx, core.RelCanManage, object)` (and `scope_matrix_ops.go:168,707` classifies `revokeOrganizationInvite`/`revoke_organization_invite`, plus `createOrganizationWorkspace`/`create_organization_workspace` at `:62,504`, as `OpClassWrite` instead of `OpClassMint`).

### `ChangeOrganizationRole` leaves NonNull `workspaceRoles` nil, and `RevokeOrganizationInvite` lacks mint-class, fresh-authz, and deferred-audit guards

- **Severity:** major
- **Versions:** deployed pin `a6f9307d74` (`486e3f4afbf6`) and `origin/main` `445c2fa740` (zero diff in `lego/backend/internal/members/organizations*.go` and `lego/backend/internal/api/scope_matrix_ops.go`); `serverInfo`: `bex 0.1.0`; negotiated MCP protocol: `2025-06-18`; Render MCP pin: `render-oss/render-mcp-server@a18ef3ee866716933710fa5054323fd5c1ed0eb1`

- **Expected / actual:**
  - **Expected:**
    1. `ChangeOrganizationRole` returns the same complete `OrganizationMemberView` shape as `ListOrganizationMembers` (`members/organizations.go:227-239`), including a non-nil `WorkspaceRoles` slice (`[]OrganizationMemberWorkspaceView{}` when empty, or the member's `{workspaceId, role}` entries matching `Workspaces`).
    2. `RevokeOrganizationInvite` matches its three siblings in the same file (`InviteToOrganization` at `:249-262`, `ChangeOrganizationRole` at `:486-497`, `RemoveFromOrganization` at `:573-585`): it defers the allowed-write audit (`core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)` + emit only after `DeleteOrganizationInvite` succeeds), enforces `s.AuthorizeMintClass(ctx)` (or matches `OpClassMint` in `scope_matrix_ops.go` along with `createOrganizationWorkspace`/`create_organization_workspace` which already calls `AuthorizeMintClass` at `workspaces/organizations.go:237`), and reasserts `s.AuthorizeFreshOn(ctx, core.RelCanManage, object)`.

    2. `RevokeOrganizationInvite` (`members/organizations.go:469-481`) calls only `s.AuthorizeOn(ctx, core.RelCanManage, core.OrganizationObject(orgID))`: cached positive authz decisions are not rechecked via `AuthorizeFreshOn`, non-mint `bex.write` OAuth tokens are not blocked by `AuthorizeMintClass` or `scope_matrix_ops.go:168,707`, and `403`/`404` refusals from `requireOrgOwner` or `DeleteOrganizationInvite` record `"status": "success"` audit rows.
