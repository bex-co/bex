# 099 — `validateDiskMountPath` and `DiskSpec` CRD CEL check literal paths without normalization, so `/etc//secrets`, `/etc/./secrets`, `/data/../etc/secrets`, and `/bex-health/..` bypass reserved-mount and root-directory guards across MCP, REST, and GraphQL

Why: `validateDiskMountPath` (`lego/backend/internal/apps/disks.go:411-426`) and `DiskSpec` CRD CEL (`lego/types/v1alpha1/app_types.go:1371-1373`) check only `strings.HasPrefix(path, "/")`, `!strings.HasSuffix(path, "/")`, `len <= 255`, and literal membership in `diskReservedMountPaths` (`['/etc','/etc/secrets','/home','/home/render','/opt','/opt/render','/opt/render/project','/opt/render/project/src','/bex-health']`) without normalizing or rejecting `..`, `.`, `//`, colons, or control characters — while the operator (`lego/operator/internal/controller/disk.go:136-140`) passes `app.Spec.Disk.MountPath` verbatim into `corev1.VolumeMount.MountPath`, allowing unnormalized paths that resolve to `/`, `/etc/secrets`, or `/bex-health` to pass validation, create billed disks, and then collide with platform mounts or fail Kubernetes Pod admission.

- **Root cause:**
  - `lego/backend/internal/apps/disks.go:411-426` (`validateDiskMountPath`) checks only `path == ""`, `!strings.HasPrefix(path, "/")`, `len(path) > 255`, `strings.HasSuffix(path, "/")`, and `slices.Contains(diskReservedMountPaths, path)`. It never runs `path.Clean`, never checks for `..` or `.` segments or `//`, and never rejects `:` (which Kubernetes `ValidateVolumeMounts` rejects) or control characters (`< 0x20`, `0x7f`).
  - `lego/types/v1alpha1/app_types.go:1371-1373` (`DiskSpec` CEL rules) applies the same literal-string rules without checking for `//`, `.` / `..` segments, or `:`.
  - `lego/operator/internal/controller/disk.go:136-140` (`applyDiskProjection` → `diskMountPaths`) mounts `app.Spec.Disk.MountPath` verbatim into `corev1.VolumeMount{Name: diskVolumeName, MountPath: mountPath}`. Kubernetes Pod validation checks `path.Clean(mountPath)` for duplicate mounts and rejects `:` — so a disk accepted at `/etc//secrets`, `/bex-health/.`, or `/bex-health/..` either fails Deployment reconciliation after the App CR and billed disk row are created or mounts a persistent volume over `/`, `/etc`, `/etc/secrets`, `/home`, or `/opt`.
- **Fix:**
  - In `validateDiskMountPath` (`lego/backend/internal/apps/disks.go:411-426`): reject control characters (`r < 0x20 || r == 0x7f`), `:`, duplicate slashes (`//`), and `.` or `..` segments (equivalently `path.Clean(p) != p`), and check `path.Clean(p)` against `"/"` and `diskReservedMountPaths`.
  - Mirror the canonical-path guard in `DiskSpec` CEL (`lego/types/v1alpha1/app_types.go:1371-1373`, e.g. `!self.mountPath.contains('//') && !self.mountPath.contains(':') && self.mountPath.split('/').all(s, s != '.' && s != '..')`) and regenerate CRD manifests (`cd lego/operator && make manifests`).
  - Also verify `validateDiskName` (`lego/backend/internal/apps/disks.go:404-409`) rejects control characters (`\r`, `\n`, `\x00`) if not already covered.
  - Surface impact: fixes `add_disk`, `update_disk`, `validate_bex_yml`, Blueprint plan/apply, REST `POST /v1/disks`, `PATCH /v1/disks/{diskId}`, `POST /v1/services`, GraphQL `addDisk`/`updateDisk`/`createService`, and CRD admission simultaneously.
