# 049 — MCP `update_blueprint` omits `strings.TrimSpace` on `name` in its no-op check, bypassing Render's no-op notice and writing a redundant update

Why: `apps.Service.updateBlueprintTool` (`lego/backend/internal/apps/blueprint_onboarding.go:1007-1014`) trims `*in.Branch` before comparing against `current.Branch` (`:1007`) and normalizes `*in.Path` before comparing against `current.Path` (`:986-991,1008`), but compares raw `*in.Name != current.Name` (`:1010`) without `strings.TrimSpace` — even though `s.UpdateBlueprint` (`lego/backend/internal/apps/blueprint.go:2088-2094`) immediately trims `*req.Name` before writing — so passing a whitespace-padded identical `name` skips Render's `blueprintUpdateNoOpNotice` and performs a redundant DB update and audit write.
