# 144 — `agentsessions` leaks raw internal Kratos identity UUIDs in `AgentAutomation.createdBy` and `AgentSession.createdBy` and ignores `usr-…` IDs in `list_agent_sessions` `createdBy` filters across `/mcp`, `/mcp/cloud-agents`, REST, and GraphQL
