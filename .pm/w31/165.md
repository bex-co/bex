# 165 — `api.objectStorageAdmin` leaks raw internal Kratos identity UUIDs in `ObjectAccessKey.createdBy` and records `"status": "success"` workspace audit rows on `400`/`404` `CreateAccessKey` and `RevokeAccessKey` refusals (REST & GraphQL)

Why: `objectStorageAdmin` (`lego/backend/internal/api/object_storage_admin.go:69-83,196-267`) has two shared-service defects affecting every REST and GraphQL object-storage access-key caller: (1) `keyWire` (`:80`) copies `k.CreatedBy` (the raw internal Kratos identity UUID stored at `:221`) verbatim into `"createdBy"` instead of projecting it through `core.PublicUserIDs(ctx, a.base.UserIDs, ...)` as required by `docs/ADR020-identifiers.md`, and (2) `CreateAccessKey` (`:203`) and `RevokeAccessKey` (`:262`) authorize without `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)` before validating inputs or mutating `a.keys`, so `400 ACCESS_KEY_INVALID` and `404 ACCESS_KEY_NOT_FOUND` refusals emit false `"status": "success"` rows into the workspace audit log.

- **Attribution:** server (`lego/backend/internal/api/object_storage_admin.go`)
- **Root cause:**
  - `lego/backend/internal/api/object_storage_admin.go:69-83` (`keyWire`) copies `k.CreatedBy` (the raw Kratos `caller.Subject` persisted by `CreateAccessKey` at `:221`, which internal teardown and per-user ephemeral-key quotas in `objectstorage.AccessKeys` rely on) verbatim into `accessKeyWire.CreatedBy` without resolving public IDs via `core.PublicUserIDs(ctx, a.base.UserIDs, ...)` — even though `a.base` already holds `UserIDs core.UserIDLookup`.
  - `lego/backend/internal/api/object_storage_admin.go:203` (`CreateAccessKey`) calls `a.base.AuthorizeWorkspaceAs(...)` without `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)` BEFORE `core.TrimmedLabel(req.Name)` (`:213`), `req.ExpiresInSeconds < 0` (`:217`), and `a.keys.Create` (`:220`).
  - `lego/backend/internal/api/object_storage_admin.go:262` (`RevokeAccessKey`) calls `a.base.AuthorizeWorkspaceTargetAs(...)` without `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)` BEFORE `a.keys.Revoke(ctx, workspaceID, id)` (`:266`), which returns `ErrAccessKeyNotFound` on missing or foreign keys.
- **Fix:**
  - Project `CreatedBy` through `core.PublicUserIDs(ctx, a.base.UserIDs, ...)` at the wire boundary of `CreateAccessKey` (`:227`) and `ListAccessKeys` (`:245-249`, batch-resolving all distinct non-empty `k.CreatedBy` values in one lookup), while keeping the stored `object_access_keys.created_by` column on the internal Kratos subject so account teardown and ephemeral-key limits remain exact.
  - Wrap `ctx = core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)` before `AuthorizeWorkspaceAs` in `CreateAccessKey` (`:203`) and before `AuthorizeWorkspaceTargetAs` in `RevokeAccessKey` (`:262`), and record the allowed write (`a.base.RecordAllowedWrite` / `RecordAllowedWriteTarget`, passing `core.AccessKeyTarget(created.Key.ID)` on create) only after `a.keys.Create` / `a.keys.Revoke` succeeds.
  - Surface impact: fixes REST (`GET`/`POST`/`DELETE /v1/object-storage/{ownerId}/access-keys*`) and GraphQL (`objectAccessKeys`, `createObjectAccessKey`, `revokeObjectAccessKey`) from one file.
- **Blast radius:** `lego/backend/internal/api/object_storage_admin.go` and its tests in `lego/backend/internal/api/` (e.g., `object_storage_admin_test.go`); keep `objectstorage.AccessKeys` storage and `fakekeys` on internal Kratos subjects.
