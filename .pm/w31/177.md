# 177 — `scope_matrix_overrides.go` omits `GQL Mutation.executeDatabaseQuery` and `REST POST /v1/build-sources/{buildSourceId}/env-groups/{envGroupId}`, misclassifying both as `OpClassWrite` instead of `OpClassSensitive`

Why: `lego/backend/internal/api/scope_matrix_overrides.go` classifies `REST POST /v1/postgres/{id}/query` (`:109`) and `MCP query_render_postgres` (`:133`) as `core.OpClassSensitive`, and classifies `REST POST /v1/env-groups/{id}/services/{serviceId}`, `GQL Mutation.linkEnvGroup`, and `MCP link_env_group` (`:103-105`) as `core.OpClassSensitive` ("Linking materializes every environment-group value in App workload code"), yet omits their direct siblings `GQL Mutation.executeDatabaseQuery` (`scope_matrix_ops.go:88`) and `REST POST /v1/build-sources/{buildSourceId}/env-groups/{envGroupId}` (`scope_matrix_ops.go:1069`), leaving both at default `core.OpClassWrite` — breaking `bex.sensitive`-only OAuth callers on read-only GraphQL `executeDatabaseQuery(allowWrites: false)` and under-classifying build-source env-group linking at the OAuth scope gate.
