# 115 — `deploys.Service.{Trigger,Rebuild,Cancel,Rollback,TriggerBuild}` omit `WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit`, recording `"status": "success"` audit-log rows on `400`/`404`/`409` refusals across MCP, REST, and GraphQL

Why: Unlike `deploys.Service.Restart` (`lego/backend/internal/deploys/service.go:750-758`, fixed in `w8/379`) and `deploys.Service.RegenerateDeployHook` (`lego/backend/internal/deploys/deployhook.go:166`), `deploys.Service.Trigger` (`:704`), `Rebuild` (`:724`), `Cancel` (`:1259`), `Rollback` (`:1438`), and `TriggerBuild` (`lego/backend/internal/deploys/build_sources.go:286`) call `s.AuthorizeApp` without `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)`, so every deploy trigger, cancel, or rollback refused after authorization (`400 bad request`, `404 deploy not found`, `409 conflict`) writes a false `"status": "success"` row (`deploys.Trigger`, `deploys.Cancel`, `deploys.Rollback`) into the workspace audit log across MCP, REST, and GraphQL.
