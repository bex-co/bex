# 223 — `members.Service.{JoinWorkspace,JoinWorkspaceAsAdmin}` omit `AuthorizeMintClass`, `OpClassMint`, and `AuthorizeFreshOn`, allowing delegated third-party OAuth clients (`bex.write`) to permanently join organization workspaces and self-elevate to Admin

Why: Every other durable membership-granting or role-elevation verb in `members/` and `workspaces/` enforces `AuthorizeMintClass(ctx)` + `OpClassMint` (`lego/backend/internal/api/scope_matrix_overrides.go:152-169`, "Membership issuance (an invite, its resend, a role change) is mint too: it outlives the caller's credential (round 24, H-1)") + uncached `AuthorizeFreshOn` (`members/service.go:686,791`; `members/organizations.go:253,488`; `workspaces/organizations.go:237`), yet `members.Service.JoinWorkspace` (`lego/backend/internal/members/organizations.go:650-659`, granting durable `developer` membership in a Public org workspace) and `members.Service.JoinWorkspaceAsAdmin` (`:664-673`, granting durable `admin` membership in any org workspace — including `invite_only` workspaces — and upgrading existing non-admin members to `admin`) omit `AuthorizeMintClass(ctx)`, omit `OpClassMint` in `scope_matrix_overrides.go` (leaving all 4 MCP/GraphQL operations at `core.OpClassWrite` in `scope_matrix_ops.go:125,126,613,614`), and omit uncached `AuthorizeFreshOn`.

- **Expected / actual:**
  - **Expected:** Matching `docs/ADR012-auth.md`, round 24 H-1, and sibling membership verbs (`members/service.go:686,791`, `members/organizations.go:253,488`, `workspaces/organizations.go:237`, `scope_matrix_overrides.go:152-169`):
    1. `members.Service.JoinWorkspace` and `members.Service.JoinWorkspaceAsAdmin` must call `s.AuthorizeMintClass(ctx)` before `s.AuthorizeWorkspace`, refusing delegated third-party OAuth clients and API keys from minting permanent workspace memberships or Admin role elevations that outlive their credential.
    2. All four operations (`GQL Mutation.joinWorkspace`, `GQL Mutation.joinWorkspaceAsAdmin`, `MCP join_workspace`, `MCP join_workspace_as_admin`) must be classified `core.OpClassMint` in `lego/backend/internal/api/scope_matrix_overrides.go` and `scope_matrix_ops.go`.
    3. Both verbs must pair `AuthorizeWorkspace` with uncached `s.AuthorizeFreshOn(ctx, rel, core.WorkspaceObject(workspaceID))` before mutating `tenant_members` and OpenFGA tuples, matching `InviteToOrganization`, `ChangeOrganizationRole`, and `RemoveFromOrganization`.
  - **Actual:**
    - `lego/backend/internal/members/organizations.go:650-673` calls only cached `s.AuthorizeWorkspace(ctx, core.RelCanJoin / core.RelCanJoinAsAdmin, workspaceID)` with zero `AuthorizeMintClass(ctx)` and zero `AuthorizeFreshOn` call.
    - `lego/backend/internal/api/scope_matrix_ops.go:125,126,613,614` leaves all four operations at `core.OpClassWrite` because `scope_matrix_overrides.go` omits them.
    - Consequently, any consented third-party OAuth client holding `bex.write` acting for an Org Owner can call `joinWorkspaceAsAdmin` on every `invite_only` workspace in the org, permanently elevating the user to workspace `admin` (and immediately gaining full `admin` access in those workspaces for the rest of the token's lifetime), and acting for an Org Member can permanently join every `public` workspace as `developer`.
