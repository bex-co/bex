# 114 — `postgres.Service.Failover`, `postgres.Service.setSuspended`, `keyvalue.Service.setSuspended`, and `keyvalue.Service.EnableInternalAuth` omit `WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit` (recording `"success"` audit rows on refused calls), and `EnableInternalAuth` omits `requireUnprotected`

Why: `postgres.Service.Failover` (`lego/backend/internal/postgres/lifecycle.go:104-123`), `postgres.Service.setSuspended` (`:50-73`), `keyvalue.Service.setSuspended` (`lego/backend/internal/keyvalue/service.go:864-886`), and `keyvalue.Service.EnableInternalAuth` (`:835-848`) never wrap `ctx` with `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)` (unlike `postgres.Service.Restart` at `lifecycle.go:78` and `apps.Service.setSuspended` at `apps/service.go:6414`), so calls refused after authorization — such as `failover_postgres` on a suspended database returning `409 Conflict` — emit false `"status": "success"` rows into the workspace audit log across MCP, REST, and GraphQL; additionally, `keyvalue.Service.EnableInternalAuth` omits `s.requireUnprotected` despite irreversibly enabling internal authentication and dropping existing unauthenticated connections.

- **Expected vs Actual:**

Additionally, `keyvalue.Service.EnableInternalAuth` — which is CEL-enforced enable-only (irreversible) and rolls the proxy to drop all open unauthenticated internal connections (`docs/ADR021-keyvalue-management.md:36`) — must enforce `s.requireUnprotected(ctx, kv, ...)` on protected-environment members just like `DeleteKeyValue`, `Suspend`, `Resume`, `UpdateKeyValue`, and `KeyValueConnectionInfo`.

- **Root cause (researched):**
  - `lego/backend/internal/postgres/lifecycle.go:109` (`Failover`): calls `s.fetchLiveDatabase(ctx, core.RelCanOperate, name)` without `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)`, so the audit row is recorded before `s.requireUnprotected` (`:113`), `d.Spec.Suspended` (`:116`), and `s.patchDatabaseObj` (`:119`).
  - `lego/backend/internal/postgres/lifecycle.go:51` (`setSuspended`, backing `Suspend` and `Resume`) and `lego/backend/internal/keyvalue/service.go:865` (`setSuspended`, backing `Suspend` and `Resume`): both call `fetchLiveDatabase` / `fetchLiveKeyValue` without `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)`, so calls refused by `requireUnprotected`, `RequireBillingMutation`, or `freeExpired` still record `"status": "success"` `SuspendPostgresEvent`/`ResumePostgresEvent`/`keyvalue.Suspend`/`keyvalue.Resume` rows.
  - `lego/backend/internal/keyvalue/service.go:835-848` (`EnableInternalAuth`): calls `s.fetchLiveKeyValue(ctx, core.RelCanCreate, name)` without `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)` and never calls `s.requireUnprotected(ctx, kv, ...)` (compare `DeleteKeyValue` at `:808`, `setSuspended` at `:870-874`, `updateKeyValue` at `:1375-1384`, and `KeyValueConnectionInfo` at `:1504`).
- **Fix direction:**
  - Wrap `ctx` with `core.WithDeferredAllowedWriteAudit(ctx)` in `postgres.Service.Failover` (`lifecycle.go:109`), `postgres.Service.setSuspended` (`lifecycle.go:51`), `keyvalue.Service.setSuspended` (`keyvalue/service.go:865`), and `keyvalue.Service.EnableInternalAuth` (`keyvalue/service.go:836`), and emit the corresponding allowed-write audit row only after every post-authz guard and CR write succeeds (matching `postgres.Service.Restart` at `lifecycle.go:78,95` and `apps.Service.setSuspended` at `apps/service.go:6414,6444`).
  - In `keyvalue.Service.EnableInternalAuth` (`keyvalue/service.go:835-848`), add `if err := s.requireUnprotected(ctx, kv, "enable internal authentication on"); err != nil { return KeyValueView{}, err }` (using `core.VerbChangeAccessControl` or a clear verb string matching `keyvalue/protection.go`) before mutating `kv.Spec.InternalAuth`, so a non-Admin Developer receives `403 PROTECTED_ENVIRONMENT_ADMIN_REQUIRED` on a protected-environment Key Value store.
  - Surface impact: fixes MCP (`failover_postgres`, `suspend_postgres`, `resume_postgres`, `suspend_keyvalue`, `resume_keyvalue`, `enable_keyvalue_internal_auth`), REST, and GraphQL uniformly.
