## Objective

When `pushDeliveryMethod` is `unavailable`, a create without `autoDeploy` stores it off (`autoDeploy: "no"`, `autoDeployTrigger: "off"`), and a create or update with `autoDeploy: "yes"` (REST `POST`/`PATCH /v1/services`, GraphQL `setAutoDeploy`, MCP `update_service`) is refused with 400 naming the reason and the way out, like the prebuilt-image refusal (w4/418). `unknown`, `manual_webhook` and `github_app` keep today's default; existing services keep their stored value.
