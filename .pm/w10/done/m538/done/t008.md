## Objective

Make sure the shipped behavior is covered by tests that assert real outcomes and failure modes, and prove the important ones are real with mutants.

## Steps

1. Cover with tests:
   - every refusal code;
   - the head pin, so a moved head is never merged;
   - the method choice, both named and defaulted;
   - another session's call is refused;
   - a closed or merged pull request;
   - the immediate `prState: merged`;
   - the token's permissions and its fallback;
   - the gateway admission;
   - the tools' listing and rendering;
   - the overlay's "only when asked" text.
2. Kill at least these mutants:
   - drop the `sha` pin;
   - accept a pull request number from the request;
   - skip the state record;
   - list the tools in a repo-less session.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Each mutant above fails a test. The mutants and the tests that kill them are recorded in the milestone README.
