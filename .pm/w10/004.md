Why: after m16 the replay of a long turn is still 41% of the old bytes, and all of that is inline tool output. Moving full outputs behind a download link would shrink replays further without losing information.

- Store tool outputs over ~8k chars as head and tail inline, with the full text in object storage. Add a "Download full output" link in the activity group.
- Pick the storage: the Hetzner/Wasabi agent bucket was retired with hibernation (w10/m15), so this needs a bucket decision or reuse of the backups store.
