## Objective

Workspace and account deletion first flushes and charges all unbilled usage, refusing deletion with `BILLING_BALANCE_DUE` and a payment link if the charge fails; binding a card whose Stripe fingerprint carries written-off agent-model debt is refused.

## Context

- ADR091 D10.3–D10.4; ADR086 account deletion (amendment 2026-10-30).
- Payment method binding: ADR075 D7 `PaymentGate`.

## Steps

1. Deletion path: flush unbilled usage for the workspace bypassing the seal horizon, invoice, charge; on failure return typed `BILLING_BALANCE_DUE` with a payment link and keep identity.
2. Persist written-off debt by card fingerprint; refuse binding a matching card with a typed error.
3. Dashboard: deletion dialog shows the balance-due state and pay link.
4. Update ADR086 body to match what shipped.
