## Objective

Replace the token quota with a per-workspace monthly USD spend cap checked at mint time, plan defaults Hobby $20 / Pro $200 / Scale $2,000, admin-adjustable up to 10×, with a $10 trust-ramp cap until the first successful agent-model charge.

## Context

- ADR091 D5, D10.1 trust tiers (amended 2026-11-02).
- Webhooks: `lego/backend/internal/billing/lifecycle.go:187` handles `invoice.paid` / `invoice.payment_failed`; `stripe_billing_events` stores no amounts and is purged after 90 days, so tiers need their own durable record.
- Shipped quota path: mint check + `ReportQuotaExceeded` in `lego/backend/internal/agentsession/modelusage.go`, code `AGENT_MODEL_QUOTA_EXCEEDED`, `failureReasonCode` on the session.

## Steps

1. `pricing.yaml`: `agentModel.monthlySpendCapUsd[]` per plan; the trust tier table (`agentModel.trustTiers[]`: reach conditions, charge threshold, cap ceiling, concurrent sessions); remove `monthlyTokenQuota[]`.
2. Migration: `billing_trust` (workspace, lifetime paid cents, paid cycle invoices, tier, lock-until).
3. Feed it from webhooks: `invoice.paid` with `amount_paid > 0` adds to the counters; `invoice.payment_failed` and `charge.dispute.created` (new handler) drop the workspace to T0 and lock it for 30 days after settlement. The tier is a pure function of counters + table.
4. Store a per-workspace cap override (nullable) with admin-only write on REST/GraphQL/MCP, bounded to 10× the plan default.
5. Effective cap = min(override or plan default, tier cap). Concurrent platform-model sessions are limited by the tier (T0: 2).
6. Mint: month-to-date `agent_model_cost_microusd` ≥ effective cap → 429 `AGENT_MODEL_SPEND_CAP_REACHED` (rename the shipped code).
7. Emit a tier-changed signal that t014 uses to update the Stripe item threshold.
8. Ops alert at 80% of the OpenRouter key credit limit (platform-wide spend); document the credit-limit console step for the user.
