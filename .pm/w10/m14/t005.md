## Objective

Replace the token quota with a per-workspace monthly USD spend cap checked at mint time, plan defaults Hobby $20 / Pro $200 / Scale $2,000, admin-adjustable up to 10×, with a $10 trust-ramp cap until the first successful agent-model charge.

## Context

- ADR091 D5, D10.2.
- Shipped quota path: mint check + `ReportQuotaExceeded` in `lego/backend/internal/agentsession/modelusage.go`, code `AGENT_MODEL_QUOTA_EXCEEDED`, `failureReasonCode` on the session.

## Steps

1. `pricing.yaml`: `agentModel.monthlySpendCapUsd[]` per plan; remove `monthlyTokenQuota[]`.
2. Store a per-workspace cap override (nullable) with admin-only write on REST/GraphQL/MCP, bounded to 10× the plan default.
3. Effective cap = min(override or plan default, $10 until first paid agent-model invoice).
4. Mint: month-to-date `agent_model_cost_microusd` ≥ effective cap → 429 `AGENT_MODEL_SPEND_CAP_REACHED` (rename the shipped code).
5. Ops alert at 80% of the OpenRouter key credit limit (platform-wide spend); document the credit-limit console step for the user.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/agentsession/modelusage.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/pricing/`, `pricing.yaml`
- workspace settings store + REST/GraphQL/MCP resolvers
- new migration

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Real-DB test: workspace at $19.99 month-to-date on Hobby mints; at $20.00 the mint returns 429 `AGENT_MODEL_SPEND_CAP_REACHED` and the session fails readably.
- [ ] Test: a new workspace with no paid agent-model invoice is capped at $10 even on Pro.
- [ ] Test: non-admin cap write is refused; admin write above 10× is refused with a typed error.
- [ ] The 80% alert fires in a unit test with a stubbed platform spend.

## Out of scope
