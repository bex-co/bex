## Objective

A caller who may view a session's sandbox gets a short-lived, single-use Web Shell ticket for it and the gateway's WebSocket URL. When the session's sandbox closed and the caller may start it, the mint starts it through `Service.Resume` as the caller and answers with the phase, so the browser knows the shell opens after the start.

## Context

- The service mint: `apps.Service.CreateShellSession` (`lego/backend/internal/apps/service.go:1931`), REST route `lego/backend/internal/apps/rest.go:1015`, GraphQL `createShellSession` (`lego/backend/internal/apps/graphql.go:1699`), audit `apps.CreateShellSession` → `StartShellEvent` (`lego/backend/internal/audit/service.go:323`).
- Agent-session surfaces: `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/rest.go`, `graphql.go`, `mcp.go`; `Service.Resume` (`editor.go:54`); `sshReachable` and `errNoSandboxToOpen` (`ssh.go`) already tell closed, starting, canceled and archived apart.
- Route tables every new operation must enter: `lego/backend/internal/api/scope_matrix_ops.go`, `scope_matrix_overrides.go`, `key_permission_ops.go`.
- Ticket package: `lego/backend/internal/shellticket/shellticket.go` (or the flavor t001 chose over `internal/hmacticket`).
