Every behavior in the definition of done has a test that fails when its code is reverted: the mint's authorization and wake, the gateway's held WebSocket and its endings, the dashboard's states. Tests assert real behavior and failure modes, never trivial or snapshot-everything checks.

## Acceptance criteria
