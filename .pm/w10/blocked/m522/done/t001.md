## Objective

Settle exactly which GitHub writes a session's `gh` may make on its repository, what each sends, what stays refused and why, and which token carries them, and record it in the ADRs before any code changes.

## Context

- The route and its policy: `lego/backend/internal/sshgateway/agentcred/githubapi.go` (`githubAPIRequestRefusal`, `graphQLRefusal`, `readOnlyRefusal`). The read design is ADR090 D4's amendment of w10/m513 and [ADR047 D2 § `gh` reads](../../../../../docs/ADR047-cloud-coding-agent-sessions.md#gh-reads-amendment-w10m513-2026-11-19).
- The milestone README's "Direction for t001" is the starting set. Devin's App permissions and `gh` token are cited there.
- Production's App `bex-co` and all of its installations grant `actions`, `issues`, `pull_requests` and `statuses` write (2026-11-19). The self-hosters' manifest in ADR026 § 2 does not grant `actions` or `issues` write.

## Steps

1. With `gh` 2.105.0 (the image's pin) pointed at a recording Unix socket via `http_unix_socket`, record the REST method and path or the GraphQL operation and its top-level mutation fields for each DoD 1 and DoD 3 command. The socket answers enough for each command to reach its write.
2. Decide the admitted REST patterns and GraphQL mutations, and the refusals with a reason for each: PR create/edit/ready/draft/merge/close, `APPROVE` (check whether an App approval counts toward required reviews), workflow dispatch, statuses and checks writes, code, refs and settings.
3. Decide the write token's permissions and how an installation missing one is served: intersect with `GET /app/installations/{id}` permissions, or retry on 422. Decide the internal verb's path, the write body limit, and whether writes need their own budget.
4. Write the refusal message so it says what `gh` can write here and names `open_or_update_draft_pr` and `reply_on_pull_request`.
5. Amend ADR090 D4 (the "Writes stay refused" bullet and the follow-ups line), ADR047 D2's `gh` amendment, and ADR026 § 2 (manifest permissions, existing-App steps, the token line in the trust boundary). Add a Design record section to the milestone README.

## Result

Done 2026-11-19: the pinned gh 2.105.0 was recorded through a socket that forwarded reads to GitHub and answered every write itself, for every DoD 1 and DoD 3 command and the writes beside them (README § Design record; the requests are the test fixture `lego/backend/internal/sshgateway/agentcred/testdata/gh-2.105.0-requests.json`). Decided: the admitted REST writes and GraphQL mutations, the refusals with their reasons (an App approval counts toward required reviews; REST `PATCH issues/{n}` reaches pull requests, while GitHub types GraphQL ids, checked live), the write token and its intersection fallback, the verb `/v1/agent-session-github-write-credentials`, a 1 MiB body limit and no separate write budget. ADR090 D2 and D4, ADR047 D2 and ADR026 § 2 amended.
