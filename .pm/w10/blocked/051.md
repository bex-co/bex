# Decide whether a workspace may let members turn `gh` off for their own agent sessions and automations

Why: w10/044 made every `gh` binding admin-only, as Devin gates every security-profile selection. Before it a member could turn `gh` off for their own session or automation, which only removed access, and w10/044 suggested keeping that as a workspace option.

- **Today (w10/044, 2026-11-23).** A member's `agentConfig.githubCli` of `true` or `false`, on a session create or an automation template, is refused `AGENT_SESSION_GITHUB_CLI_LOCKED` or `AGENT_AUTOMATION_GITHUB_CLI_LOCKED` with `reason: admin_required` (`refuseGitHubCLIBinding`, `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/githubcli.go`).
- **Devin** ([Security profiles](https://docs.devin.ai/product-guides/security-profiles), read 2026-11-23). It has no such option: "Members without the permission can't enumerate, select, or change profiles". It widens who may select by granting "Manage security profiles" to a custom role, and bex's roles are fixed (ADR024).
- **The variant.** An agent-environment field, off by default, that lets a member's `false` through again. It would be on REST, GraphQL, MCP, `bex agent env set` and Settings → Agents, and the composer and automation form would open Off to members while it is on. A member who wants no `gh` can already ask the agent not to use it.
- **Gate.** The user's decision. The option diverges from Devin, so it needs the user's sign-off recorded in ADR018 before it is built.
- **Done when.** The user declines and this note closes, or the option ships on every surface with ADR018's sign-off and tests that pin a member's off with the option on and off.
