## Objective

`formatDateTime`, `formatDateLong`, `formatInstantDetails` and `formatLogTimestamp` take the dashboard language: zh reads `2026年11月11日 下午11:06` (date-fns `zhCN` long date with a short time), date-only fields `2026年11月11日`, the tooltip `2026年11月11日 下午11:06:22 PST`; en is byte-for-byte unchanged (`November 11, 2026 at 11:06 PM`, `11:07:28 PM`).

## Steps

2. Update the comment that says en-US is deliberate: the invariant becomes one pattern per language.

## Result (2026-11-14)

`formatDateTime`, `formatDateLong` and `formatInstantDetails` take the i18n language (`common/lib/format/language.ts`: `isChinese`, `dateFnsLocale`, `intlLocale`): zh reads `2026年11月12日 上午7:06`, `2026年11月12日`, and the tooltip `2026年11月12日 UTC 上午7:06:22`; en and no language keep the exact old output (`November 12, 2026 at 7:06 AM`). The log line clock moved to `formatClockTime` (`common/lib/format/format-clock-time.ts`, cached `Intl.DateTimeFormat` per language): `07:06:22 AM` / `上午07:06:22`, never the browser's locale. `LogLine.time` (computed once at ingest, before the language was known) is gone; `LogRow` formats from `timestamp` with the list's language. Callers pass `i18n.language`: `LocalTime`, `useLocalDateTime`/`useLocalDate`, `InstantTooltip`, the deploy header. The other locale-less call sites t005's guard found now pass the language too: chart tooltips and event markers (`formatClockTime`), cron run times, skill version times, the Usage month labels (`periodLabel(period, language)`, and `periodMonthLabel` replacing a `split(" ")[0]` that only worked in English), and a disk snapshot's time (`LocalDateTime`). Tests: `format-language.test.ts` (en unchanged, zh, empty and bad input).
