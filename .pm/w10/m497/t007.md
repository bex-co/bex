## Objective

The operator knows when sessions wait for the sandbox pool, how many and for how long. Growing the pool, which is ADR105's decision, then rests on numbers.

## Context

- The agent-session metrics live in `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/` (`observeProvision`, `observeDispatchFailed`). The alert rules and the dashboards are under `deploy/gitops/` (ADR088 § 6 names the bex-api panels).
- The pool is a fixed `cpx42` (`infra/clusterapi/overlays/hetzner-caph/sandbox-pool.yaml`, ADR105 § Until the AX42-1 arrives).

## Steps

1. Add a gauge of sessions waiting for sandbox capacity and a histogram of the wait, labelled by cause, not by workspace.
2. Add an alert, `AgentSandboxPoolFull`, for sessions that keep waiting, using t001's threshold. Add a panel beside the existing agent-session panels.
3. Note in ADR105 § Until the AX42-1 arrives that this alert is the signal for the pool-size decision.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/` (metrics), `deploy/gitops/**` (alert rules, dashboard JSON), `docs/ADR105-agent-sandbox-hetzner-bare-metal.md`
