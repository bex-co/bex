## Objective

Write down, before code, when a session's checkout is saved and what each surface says, so t002–t007 build one design.

## Context

- ADR047 D12's last As built (w22/m413) keeps only a stopped turn's disk. Its "Not done" list names "No skip for a clean tree".
- The three ends that lose work today: a failed turn's settle (`teardown` skips the grace), a follow-up's teardown of the previous sandbox (`runSteerDispatch`), and the idle close (`teardown` → `terminate`). A lost sandbox (`failLostSandbox`) has nothing left to save.
- Devin keeps a session's machine across sleeps and follow-ups for 14 days ([release notes](https://docs.devin.ai/release-notes/2026), 2026-09-25, 2026-10-05, 2026-10-09).

## Steps

1. Amend ADR047 D12 with a new As built section: the retention triggers (stopped, failed, replaced by the next message, idle-closed), the clean-tree skip (decided from the turn's recorded Git facts, re-read in the sandbox when an editor connected since the turn ended), the cause recorded on the turn, the wait a follow-up pays while its predecessor's disk is saved, and what each surface shows.
2. Decide whether `BEX_AGENT_INTERRUPT_RETENTION` keeps its name (now covering every kept disk) or gains a general alias; record it, and sync `.env.example` and the operator/backend AGENTS.md inventories if a name changes.
3. Update ADR018's agent-session retention row: the divergence "only a stopped turn keeps its disk" closes; the remaining ones (processes are not kept, 24 h against Devin's 14 days) stay.
4. Record why a recovery-ref push is not taken (ADR090 custody).
