## Objective

A turn that fails while its checkout holds uncommitted changes or unpushed commits is settled as failed and its disk is saved as a retained image, which the next message boots from.

## Context

- `Completer.fail` → `finalizeFailure` → `teardown`, which skips the idle grace for a failed session (`completion.go:997`).
- `finalizeRetaining` / `FinalizeAgentSessionRetaining` settle as completed and insert the `saving` row in one transaction; `saveRetention` pauses, copies and ends the sandbox (`completion_retention.go`).
- `failMissingOutput` also goes through `fail`. `failLostSandbox` has no sandbox to save.

## Steps

1. Let the retaining finalize settle a failed turn (phase failed, its reason and failure code) with the `saving` row in the same write, when retention is on, the sandbox is there, no editor is open and the turn's facts say the checkout holds undelivered work.
2. Run `saveRetention` for it as for a stopped turn; a clean or unknown checkout keeps today's immediate teardown.
3. Record the retention cause (`failed`) and, for `failLostSandbox` with undelivered facts, the loss with a reason (`sandbox_lost`).

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/completion.go`, `completion_retention.go`, `lego/backend/internal/store/agent_retained_images.go`, `agentsessions.go`, tests

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A real-Postgres test: a failed turn with `dirty` keeps a retained image and the next steer claims it; a failed clean turn is torn down at once and keeps nothing.
- [ ] The session still reads `failed` with its reason and code, and a Cancel releases the image.
