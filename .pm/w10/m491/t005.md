## Objective

When the idle grace closes a completed turn's sandbox and the checkout holds undelivered work, its disk is saved as a retained image instead of being discarded.

## Context

- `reapIdleSandboxes` → `teardown` → `terminate` (`lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/completion.go:503`, `:1009`); the reaper already skips a session whose save is in progress.
- t004 adds the decision (recorded facts, re-read in the sandbox when needed) and the "begin retention on a settled turn" write.

## Steps

1. At the point `teardown` would call `terminate` for a completed, unarchived session, apply t004's decision; with undelivered work, begin retention (cause `idle_closed`) and run `saveRetention`, which ends the sandbox itself.
2. A clean checkout, an archived session or a failed decision read terminates as today.

- `lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/completion.go`, `completion_retention.go`, tests

- [ ] A real-Postgres test: past `IdleTTL`, a completed turn with an uncommitted change is saved and the next steer claims it; a clean one is terminated with nothing kept; an archived one is reclaimed at once as today.
