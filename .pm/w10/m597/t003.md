## Objective

On a login into a closed session the caller may start, the gateway calls t002's verb, admits the login, and holds its channels while the sandbox starts: it writes progress to each waiting channel's stderr, resolves the target again once the session settles, and opens the exec in the started sandbox. Every refusal keeps today's banner or bare rejection.

## Context

- `VerifiedPublicKeyCallback` (`lego/backend/internal/sshgateway/nativessh/server.go:300-352`): the resolver call, `toldRefusal` (`:899`) and `keyOwnerAction` (`:880`). `sessionRefusal` (`:212`) recognises `AGENT_SESSION_NOT_ATTACHABLE`.
- `serveConn` (`:466`) decodes the target the callback encoded and builds the sticky `connectionTarget`; `serveMultiChannel` (`:687`) opens each sandbox channel through `reauthorize` (`:819`) and `reassert` (`:839`); `revalidator` (`:945`) re-checks the open stream.
- The resolver (`lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/ssh.go:62`) must tell a wakeable closed session (`steerable` phase, not archived, no sandbox) or a `resuming` one apart from the other refusals, so the gateway knows when to call the verb and when to wait.
- Wiring: `lego/backend/cmd/ssh-gateway/main.go:200-205` (the other verbs' URL env vars default to `http://bex-api.bex-system.svc:8091<path>`). A new env var goes in `lego/backend/AGENTS.md`'s inventory, the gateway's manifests and `.env.example` if it is a secret (it should reuse `BEX_SANDBOX_EXEC_SECRET`).
- Metrics: `lego/backend/internal/sshgateway/metrics.go`, no identifying labels.
- Existing tests to extend: `nativessh/sessionrefusal_test.go` (runs the gateway over the real `agentsessions.SSHResolver`), `nativessh/multichannel_test.go`, `nativessh/drain_test.go`, `gatewaytest/`.

## Steps

1. Resolver: return a typed "wakeable" or "starting" answer for those sessions (keeping `errNoSandboxToOpen`'s wording for the banner).
2. Callback: on "wakeable", call the verb as the key owner after the login requirements; on its refusal, send the banner t001 chose and count it; on success or "starting", admit with a waiting target that names only the session.
3. Channels: a channel on a waiting target writes the progress lines to stderr, polls the session until it settles or the ceiling passes, re-resolves, and then runs as today. A start that ends without a sandbox prints why and exits with the status t001 chose. A drain or a closed connection ends the wait.
4. Metrics for the wake and its outcomes; `main.go` wiring and env inventory.
5. Gateway tests: the wake (a shell opens in the started sandbox), a second channel during the wait, a refusal for each reason with its banner, the start that ends without a sandbox, the ceiling, a drain during the wait, and the bex-api that answers 404 (old release) falling back to the banner.
