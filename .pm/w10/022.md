# 022 — A session waiting for a sandbox machine loses its wait when its bex-api replica restarts

Why: w10/m497 made a session whose sandbox the pool cannot place wait up to an hour for a machine, but the wait lives in the goroutine of the replica that accepted the turn, so a deploy's rollout during a burst still fails those sessions ("Sandbox provisioning was interrupted …"), which Devin never does.

## What holds today (w10/m497, ADR047 § w10/m497)

- `createWaitingForRoom` (`lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/sandbox_wait.go`) repeats the OpenSandbox create while the start fails with cause `pool_full`, and keeps the dispatch alive with `MarkAgentDispatchWaiting` (`lego/backend/internal/store/agentdispatch.go`), which moves `agent_session_dispatches.next_check_at` 15 minutes ahead before every create.
- So another replica's restart, and dispatch recovery on any replica, never end or double a wait.
- But when the waiting replica itself exits (every rollout drains every replica within minutes), its goroutine dies. The dispatch becomes due within 15 minutes of its last create, and recovery abandons it with `interruptedDispatchReason`. An automation run loses its slot the same way.

## What is missing

The dispatch's inputs (`dispatchSpec`: template, model endpoint, egress, driver env, delivery, turn, task, retained image claim) exist only in memory. Recovery cannot resume a wait it did not start.

## End state (testable)

- A real-Postgres test fails on today's code: a dispatch waiting for `pool_full` whose replica stops (its context ends without settling) is picked up by another replica's recovery tick. That replica creates the sandbox again, the session reaches running, and there is never more than one sandbox for the turn.
- A sketch: store the spec beside the dispatch while it waits. It holds no secret: secrets are resolved per dispatch, and the model key is the session placeholder. Recovery claims a due waiting dispatch with one CAS on `next_check_at`, so exactly one replica resumes it.
- The wait's bound still counts from `agent_sessions.sandbox_wait_since`. Cancel, Delete and Archive still end it at once.
- ADR047 § w10/m497's "One limit stands" paragraph is rewritten to say a wait survives a rollout.
