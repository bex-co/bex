# A pre-deploy step stopped at its 30-minute limit, or whose program cannot be launched, closes as Render's log does: `==> Pre-deploy timed out`, or `==> Exited with status 128`

Why: Render parity in the deploy log of a failed pre-deploy step. w32/m572 gave a step whose command exits Render's lines, but kept bex's `==> Pre-deploy failed: <reason>` for these two failures, because its definition of done allowed the timeout's lines only from jdegreef/open-word-bible#1023, which quotes none. Two other public logs show what Render writes.

- **Severity / estimate:** minor, ~1h: two cases in `rendersVerdict` and `preDeployLines` (`lego/backend/internal/logs/progress.go`), with their tests and the contract rows.
- **Seen:** w32/m572's public-log search, 2026-11-22.
- **Render reference (public issue logs):**
  - **The 30-minute limit.** MalaysiaKaki/astrail-webmcp#10 (2026-10-03, `runtime: docker`): the command's last output line at 08:07:52, then `==> Pre-deploy timed out` at 08:40:43, lowercase "timed out". The excerpt ends there, so whether `==> Exited with status N` or the troubleshooting line follows is not shown. jdegreef/open-word-bible#1023 (2026-11-17) only paraphrases its log as "Pre-deploy has failed / Timed out".
  - **A program that cannot be launched.** briankeane/alloy-client-portal#8 (2026-05-09, `runtime: image`, `preDeployCommand: release`): `==> Starting pre-deploy: release`, then `==> Pre-deploy has failed` and `==> Exited with status 128`, with no output. ernestkasajtp-glitch/albania-sky#8 (2026-10-31, `runtime: docker`) paraphrases the same: 31 s of no output, then `Exited with status 128`.
- **bex today (w32/m572):** the operator records no exit status for a step its Job's deadline stopped, or for a container the runtime could not launch (`StartError`, which the kubelet reports with exit code 128; `predeploy.ContainerRun`). Both close with `==> Pre-deploy failed: <reason>` after `==> Starting pre-deploy: <command>`.
- **Still not observed:** an out-of-memory kill of a pre-deploy step. bex keeps `==> Pre-deploy failed: <reason>` for it until a log shows Render's lines.
- **Done when:** a step stopped at its window closes with `==> Pre-deploy timed out` at the step's end (plus whatever a fuller Render log shows after it, or nothing), and a step whose program could not launch closes with `==> Pre-deploy has failed`, `==> Exited with status 128` and the troubleshooting line. Unit tests cover both, and `docs/render-artifacts/live-deploy-following.md` § Platform progress-line contract and ADR018 § Logs, Build logs cite the logs above.
