## Objective

A person whose personal workspace id this replica resolved recently is not refused because one cache-miss read failed. The fault t001 named is fixed at its cause, or absorbed by serving the recently known tenant.

## Context

- `EnsureTenant` caches the tenant for 30 s (`core.PositiveTTL`, `internal/core/http.go:595`) and returns an error on any cache-miss failure (`internal/api/tenancy.go:159-193`).
- The account-deletion tombstone must keep winning over any cache (ADR086). A failure to read it is a separate decision to record, not to silently pass.
- `auth.go`'s comment explains why onboarding fails closed: "a request that can't be tenanted must not be served un-tenanted". A recently resolved tenant is still tenanted.

## Steps

1. Fix the cause t001 found where it lives, for example pool sizing, a missing retry on a transient Postgres error, or OpenFGA load.
2. Keep the last resolved tenant per identity for a bounded window (minutes). On a transient cache-miss fault, serve it and log the fault, instead of answering 503.
3. Decide and record what a failed tombstone read answers. Keep it fail-closed unless ADR086 says otherwise, and log it either way.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] With a fake store or OpenFGA that fails one cache-miss call, a returning caller is served, and a first-ever caller still gets 503.
- [ ] A tombstoned account is refused even while a stale tenant is held.

## Out of scope

- Client-side retries in the CLI launcher. Upstream semantics stay as they are.
