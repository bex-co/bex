# A deploy with a pre-deploy command narrates the step as Render's log does: `==> Starting pre-deploy: <command>`, then `==> Pre-deploy complete!` or `==> Pre-deploy has failed` and the exit status

Why: Render parity in the deploy log, at the step that most often fails a paid deploy (a migration). Render's log names the command it starts, shows the output, and closes the step with its own verdict and the command's exit status. bex's log shows the output with no opening line, and closes a failed step with `==> Pre-deploy failed: <reason>`.

- **Severity / estimate:** minor, likely more than 1h: the narration needs the step's start, its command, its outcome and its exit status on the deploy row. Promote when picked up if it is.
- **Seen:** w32/085's search of public Render logs, 2026-11-22. bex writes no line when the step starts or passes. A failed step closes with `terminalLine`'s `==> Pre-deploy failed` plus `: <failureReason>`, and `rendersVerdict` (`lego/backend/internal/logs/progress.go`) keeps bex's line because "Render's lines for a pre-deploy failure … are not observed". They are observed now.
- **Render reference (public issue and PR logs):**
  - Unwrenchable/ATOMIC-FIZZ-CAPS-VAULT-77-WASTELAND-GPS#82 (2026-02-03, native Node, with timestamps): `==> Build successful 🎉` at 11:29:11.25, `==> Starting pre-deploy: Node.js` at 11:29:14.99, `==> Running 'Node.js'` at 11:29:27.17, bash's `command not found`, `==> Pre-deploy has failed` at 11:29:31.49, `==> Exited with status 127` at 11:29:32.47, and the troubleshooting line at 11:29:32.53. No `==> Deploying...` comes before the step.
  - dukens11-create/Flupflap#206 (2026-05-12): `==> Build successful 🎉`, `==> Starting pre-deploy: <command>`, `==> Running '<command>'`, Prisma's output, `==> Pre-deploy has failed`, `==> Exited with status 1`, the troubleshooting line.
  - stranske/learning-management-system#191 (2026-05-31), ganjasan/mapsurvey#72 (2026-08-15), healthkey-ai/promop#1636 (2026-09-29), briankeane/alloy-client-portal#8 (2026-05-09): the same failure shape, with exit statuses 127, 2, 127 and 128.
  - BerriAI/litellm#9549 (2025-03-26) and ganjasan/mapsurvey#73 (2026-08-15): a step that passes ends `==> Pre-deploy complete!`.
  - jdegreef/open-word-bible#1023 (2026-11-17): a step killed at Render's 30-minute limit reads "Pre-deploy has failed / Timed out".
- **Likely shape:** the deploy row already learns when the step starts and ends (`status.preDeploy.startedAt`/`finishedAt`, the `pre_deploy_started`/`pre_deploy_ended` facts) and its outcome (`pre_deploy_status`). Add the command the release ran it with (the release record's `preDeployCommand`) and its exit status (the terminated container's exit code, already in `status.preDeploy.message`). `progressLines` then writes `==> Starting pre-deploy: <command>` at the start, `==> Running '<command>'` on a native runtime as for the start command (w32/m560), `==> Pre-deploy complete!` at a passing end, and `==> Pre-deploy has failed`, `==> Exited with status N` and the troubleshooting line at a failing one. An out-of-memory kill, a step that never started and the 30-minute timeout keep their own wording (the last as `==> Pre-deploy has failed` and `==> Timed Out`, if the capture in w32/085 confirms the timeout's lines).
- **Done when:** on a paid native service whose pre-deploy command exits 1, `bex logs -r <srv> --type build` reads `==> Build successful 🎉`, `==> Starting pre-deploy: <command>`, `==> Running '<command>'`, the command's output, `==> Pre-deploy has failed`, `==> Exited with status 1` and the troubleshooting line, in that order, and a passing step's `==> Pre-deploy complete!` comes after its output, with the rollout's lines after it. REST, the subscribe stream, GraphQL, MCP and the dashboard's deploy page read the same lines. `docs/render-artifacts/live-deploy-following.md` § Platform progress-line contract and ADR018 § Logs, Build logs cite the logs above.
