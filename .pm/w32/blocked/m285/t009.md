## Objective

The t008 walk on production (2026-11-17) saw three filesystem captures of the same running sandbox end `failed` with `error: "the sandbox's filesystem could not be committed"`, which is the substrate's `Failed` state (`settleSnapshot`, `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/snapshots.go:791`). All three came between 06:55 and 07:00 UTC, after a production deploy rolled bex-api at 06:53: 3 of the 9 captures in that window failed. The 3 captures before it and the 18 after it (12 in the walk from 07:00:45, 6 in the SDK runs) all reached `available`. Render documents no such window: a capture of a running sandbox reaches `available`. Find the substrate's reason, and make a capture requested during or just after a platform deploy reach `available`.

## Context

- **Failed captures**, all of `sbx-dbtvi9teg69s73cfbvfg` in `bex-canary` (`tea-daif693dqjvc73e7as3g`), all deleted since:
  - `snp-dbtvkpjvsrns73ebrk00`, requested through MCP at 06:55:34 UTC;
  - `snp-dbtvli0mlqgs73cq4dl0`, requested through MCP at 06:57:11; it failed within 5 s;
  - `snp-dbtvmvrvsrns73ebrl10`, requested through REST at 07:00:16.
- **The deploy:** run 44031784852 (`21762a1df1fb`) wrote pin `2806a07b8` at 06:48:15, and its `roll bex onto the freshly-pushed image` step ran from 06:53:01 to 06:53:44. The pin also changed the opensandbox-controller digest (`6a1a8b05…` → `fd0307d5…`, `deploy/gitops/base/values/opensandbox-controller.values.yaml`). Argo then rolls the controller that runs the commit Jobs.
- **Not the surface:** MCP, REST and the CLI each also captured successfully in the same session. Plain back-to-back captures, a capture right after a delete, and a capture right after a TTL expiry all succeeded outside the window.
- **Where the reason is:** bex-api logs `sandbox snapshots: capture of <snp> failed: <reason>: <message>` for each failure (`snapshots.go:793`). The substrate's own record is gone: the sweep deletes a settled capture's `SandboxSnapshot` with its commit Jobs (`cleanCapture`). The logs need production cluster read (`scripts/fetch-app-kubeconfig.sh`, Loki), which this host does not have (§ Blocked — 2026-11-17).

## Steps

1. Read the three log lines and the controller's rollout and pod start times around 06:48–07:01 UTC. Decide between three causes: a controller roll, a bex-api roll, or something else.
2. Fix the cause where it lives, with a regression test on the bex-api side when the fix is there. Two possible fixes:
   - deploy.yml reuses the controller image when its inputs are unchanged, as it already does for the OpenSandbox server (`OpenSandbox server — reuse the image built from the same inputs`);
   - bex-api retries a capture whose substrate failure is transient, instead of failing the snapshot.
3. Check it on production: run captures back to back in `bex-canary` across a deploy's `roll bex onto the freshly-pushed image` step and the following controller sync, and confirm every one reaches `available`.
4. Change the `[~]` grades of the two checklist rows to `[x]`, and the 🚧 on ADR018's capture row.
