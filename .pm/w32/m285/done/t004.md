## Objective

Serve snapshot list/get/delete on REST, GraphQL and MCP with Render's shapes, refuse delete while `creating`, and purge expired snapshots (metadata and registry image) without affecting sandboxes restored from them.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/purger.go` (existing sandbox purger)
- CLI `bex sandboxes snapshots list|get|delete`

## Steps

1. Add the read and delete handlers with cursor pagination.
2. Add expiry purge to the purger, deleting the image too.
3. Make workspace deletion remove its snapshots.

## Files

- lego/backend/internal/sandbox/*.go

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `snapshots list` shows the snapshot with status and `expiresAt`
- [x] Delete while creating answers a named 409
- [x] An expired snapshot is gone from list and from the registry

## Out of scope

- Snapshot billing beyond what t001 decides

**Done 2026-11-16:** list (newest first, `name`, `status`, cursor; REST requires `ownerId`), get and delete on all three surfaces; `409 snapshot_creating`; a repeated or expired delete is 204; the sweep reaps deleted and expired images and closes the meter; group and workspace deletes take their snapshots. Covered by `TestSnapshotReadsAndDelete`, `TestSnapshotLifetimesAndExpiry` and `TestGroupAndWorkspaceDeletesTakeTheirSnapshots`.
