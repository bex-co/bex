## Objective

Serve snapshot list/get/delete on REST, GraphQL and MCP with Render's shapes, refuse delete while `creating`, and purge expired snapshots (metadata and registry image) without affecting sandboxes restored from them.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/purger.go` (existing sandbox purger)
- CLI `bex sandboxes snapshots list|get|delete`

## Steps

1. Add the read and delete handlers with cursor pagination.
2. Add expiry purge to the purger, deleting the image too.
3. Make workspace deletion remove its snapshots.

## Files

- lego/backend/internal/sandbox/*.go

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `snapshots list` shows the snapshot with status and `expiresAt`
- [ ] Delete while creating answers a named 409
- [ ] An expired snapshot is gone from list and from the registry
