# A first image deploy that fails after its 15-minute registry wait records `image_pull_failed` at the deploy's start, 15 minutes before the pull failed

Why: the Events timeline, the webhook and the Image Pull Failed email date the failure 15 minutes early, before the deploy had failed or even waited. A user reading the timeline sees the pull fail at once and the deploy end 15 minutes later, the opposite of what happened. Render dates the event when the pull fails.

- **Seen:** `/qa-find-bugs-cli` sweep 182, 2026-11-16, released `bex` v0.7.0 (Render CLI v2.36.0 pin), pin `284b83e1f540`, QA human device login, `bex-canary`:
  - `bex services create --name qa-20261116-ebe5-reg --type web_service --image qa-20261116-ebe5.invalid/qa/none:v1 --plan free --region frankfurt --confirm -o json` created `srv-dbtnklse0r6s73de9lh0` at 21:49:11Z (exit 0). The `.invalid` host never resolves, so the operator's registry read fails as a network error, the transient case w4/692 bounds. No request left the platform.
  - `bex deploys list srv-dbtnklse0r6s73de9lh0 -o json`, polled every minute: `dep-dbtnklse0r6s73de9lhg` read `update_in_progress` from 21:49:24 to 22:03:29, then `update_failed`, with `"startedAt": "2026-11-16T21:49:11.551144Z"` and `"finishedAt": "2026-11-16T22:04:16.91703Z"`. The w4/692 bound works live.
  - `GET /v1/services/srv-dbtnklse0r6s73de9lh0/events?limit=20` (diagnosis over REST with the same QA token; the CLI has no events command), newest first, trimmed:
    - `2026-11-16T22:04:16Z deploy_ended` with `"failureReason": "the registry qa-20261116-ebe5.invalid did not answer the read of the image qa-20261116-ebe5.invalid/qa/none:v1 for 15 minutes. Check that the registry is reachable, then deploy again"`, `"fullDeployStatus": "update_failed"`;
    - `2026-11-16T21:49:11Z deploy_started` with `"firstBuild": true`;
    - `2026-11-16T21:49:11Z image_pull_failed` with `{"deployId": "dep-dbtnklse0r6s73de9lhg", "imageURL": "qa-20261116-ebe5.invalid/qa/none:v1", "message": "The image could not be pulled"}`.
  - The service was deleted afterwards (`bex services delete … --confirm`, absent from `bex services`).
- **Cause:** `observedImagePullFailure` (`lego/backend/internal/store/reconciler.go:1570`, called at `:1038`) dates the fact with the Ready condition's `LastTransitionTime` (`:1579`). The operator sets Ready to False when the registry wait starts (`imageRegistryWait`, `lego/operator/internal/controller/image_network.go:655`, reason `ImageRegistryUnavailable`, through `setPhaseWith`, `app_controller.go:7067`). When the wait expires, `fail` writes Ready False with reason `ImagePullFailed` through `setNotReadyCondition` (`app_controller.go:7148`). `meta.SetStatusCondition` keeps `LastTransitionTime` when the status does not change (False to False), so the condition, and the fact, keep the wait's start.
- **Precedent:** `pipelineMinutesExhaustedFact` (`reconciler.go:2132`) hit the same thing with the Build condition and leaves `At` to the insert's now: "the operator keeps the Build condition False across failures, so its LastTransitionTime is the App's first build failure, not this refusal" (w9/m345 t002).
- **Fix:** date the deploy-bound `image_pull_failed` fact when the reconciler first sees the failure (leave `At` to the insert's now, as the precedent does). The fact is keyed by the deploy (`deploy:<id>:image_pull_failed`), so a later pass does not move it. Keep `servedReleasePullFailure` (`:1661`) as is unless the check below shows the same lag; its source key includes the transition time.
- **Blast radius to check:** any first deploy whose Ready was already False before the pull failed takes the earlier time: a registry refusal (404/401/403) after `RegistryCredsPending` or another pre-dispatch wait (seconds), and a kubelet `ImagePullBackOff` after the rollout's own Ready False (minutes). The 15-minute registry wait is the case that makes the gap large. The webhook (`webhooks/service.go:248` maps the fact) and the alert email (`notifications/alert_email_worker.go:247`) read the same fact; whether each prints its time is not checked.
- **Done when:** a reconciler test with an App whose Ready condition went False (reason `ImageRegistryUnavailable`) well before it turns `ImagePullFailed` records `image_pull_failed` at the observation time, not the old transition time, and fails with the old dating. Live: the CLI journey above (an image from a `.invalid` registry), after its 15-minute wait, shows `image_pull_failed` within seconds of `deploy_ended` on `GET /v1/services/<id>/events`, and the service is deleted afterwards.
- **Unverified:** the dashboard timeline's ordering (not opened; it reads the same events) and whether the Image Pull Failed email prints the time. A served release (`servedReleasePullFailure`, keyed by the Rollout condition's transition time) was not exercised.
- **Same sweep, no action:** the deploy closes `update_failed` at 15 minutes 5 seconds, and its `failureReason` names the registry and the image (w4/692 live, which had only an envtest DoD). `bex deploys list` on the deleted service prints `Error: unexpected response: 404 Not Found`; that wording comes from the pinned client (`pkg/tui/views/deployview.go:45`), the class w8/093 records upstream. sweep 181 (pin `284b83e1f540`) found no regression from the ssh-gateway transports refactor (`7bffab727`): `bex sandboxes exec` exit codes and split streams, `bex sandboxes cp` up and down (a 1 MiB file's sha256 and a symlink), and a 75-second exec across the one-minute revalidation tick all held with released v0.7.0.
- **Dedupe:** `git grep -n -i 'image_pull_failed' origin/main -- .pm` finds w2/072 (the event's introduction), w8/m156 t005 (`servedReleasePullFailure`), w7/m66 and w9/m345 t002 (the precedent above); none covers the dating. `git log -S observedImagePullFailure` shows no later fix.
- Minor, ~45m.
