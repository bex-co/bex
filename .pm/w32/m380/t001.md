## Objective

`bex sandboxes copy ./dir <sbx>:path` stores each symlink in the archive as a symlink with its target unchanged, as the pinned client's `writeTar` sends it and Render documents ("Symlinks are preserved, and sockets, FIFOs, and other special files are skipped"). Today one symlink fails the whole upload with `400 invalid or unsupported sandbox archive: links and special entries are unsupported`.

## Context

- `copyArchive` (`lego/backend/internal/sshgateway/sandboxfiles/sandboxfiles.go:668`) vets the upload before the sandbox's `tar -xf - -C "$stage/tree"` (`stageScript`, `:336-354`) extracts it. It refuses anything but regular files and directories (`:691-693`).
- Its other rules stay as they are:
  - names are clean, relative, unique, with no control characters, at most 4096 bytes and 64 levels;
  - a parent entry must precede its children and be a directory (`:707`);
  - 10,000 entries and 256 MiB;
  - modes keep permission bits only, and mtimes are kept (w32/018).
- The parent rule already stops a later entry from being written through a symlink entry: a symlink can never be a parent.

## Steps

1. Accept `tar.TypeSymlink` as a leaf. Keep `Linkname` byte for byte, whether relative, absolute or dangling: the tenant is root in its own sandbox and can `ln -s` anything through exec. Refuse an empty or over-long `Linkname`, or one with control characters.
2. Accept `tar.TypeLink` only when `Linkname`, cleaned like an entry name, names a regular-file entry earlier in the same archive. That keeps a hard link inside the uploaded tree.
3. Drop char/block devices, FIFOs and other special types from the stream instead of failing. The pinned client never sends them, but another tar producer might, and Render skips them.
4. Check that BusyBox `tar -x` in the base image restores both kinds in the stage, and that `publishScript` moves the tree with them intact.
