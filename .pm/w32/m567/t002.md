## Objective

`progressLines` follows a web service's `==> Your service is live 🎉` with Render's six lines, built from the row's recorded URLs. REST, the subscribe stream, GraphQL and MCP all read them.

## Context

- The adapter is in `lego/backend/internal/api/server.go:1019-1022`, where `store.Deploy` becomes `logs.DeployProgress`. `DeployProgress` is defined at `lego/backend/internal/logs/progress.go:55`.
- The live line is written in `progressLines` (`progress.go:432-440`) through `closingLines` and `terminalLine`, at `FinishedAt`.
- A line's id is its instance, timestamp and message (`logID`). Three `==> ` lines and two rules at one timestamp would collapse into one in the follower's dedupe and in clients. Each block line needs its own moment.
- The follower's `emitRow(…, holdClosing)` (`:1002`) holds back only the lines stamped exactly at `FinishedAt`. A block stamped after `FinishedAt` would print before the build's last line in `emitStarted`.
- `hookLines` (`:614`) starts the initial deploy hook at `FinishedAt + 1ms`. `hookEnd` (`:648`) bounds a row's story for `synthesizeProgress`'s window skip (`:820`).

## Steps

1. Add `LiveURLs []string` to `DeployProgress` and pass `r.LiveURLs` in the adapter.
2. For a `live` or `deactivated` row with recorded URLs, write the six lines after the live line, each 1 ms after the one before:
   - `==> ` (with the trailing space);
   - `==> ` and 59 `/`;
   - `==> `;
   - `==> Available at your primary URL <primary>`, plus ` + 1 more domain` or ` + N more domains` when the record has others;
   - `==> `;
   - the rule again.

   The row's own record decides whether the block appears, so check no service type here. Put the format strings beside `timedOutLine` with a comment citing the milestone README's logs.
3. `emitRow` with `holdClosing` holds every line stamped at or after `FinishedAt`, not only the ones stamped exactly then.
4. Start the hook's lines after the block's last line, and extend `hookEnd` (or the window skip) so that a window opening inside the block keeps its lines.
