## Objective

When the reconciler closes a web service's deploy `live`, it records the URLs the service answers at on the deploy row, primary first. The deploy log reads this record, never the App's current hosts, so the block in an old deploy's log does not change after a rename or a new domain.

## Context

- The only live close is `Reconciler.recordDeploy` (`lego/backend/internal/store/reconciler.go:1101-1112`). It fills `RanImage` (the image, digest, detected port and commit a live close records) and passes it to `TransitionDeploy` (`:1171`). `CloseDeploy` (`store.go`, ~`:3811`) closes only cancels.
- `RanImage` is at `store.go:3675`, and `TransitionDeploy`'s `UPDATE` at `:3696`. `deployColumns` and `scanDeploy` are at `:3573-3581`, and the fake store is in `fake_test.go`. w32/m560's migration `0327_deploy_rollout` shows the pattern. The next free number is `0328`; re-check origin/main first.
- bex's hosts:
  - `status.url` and `status.urls` are what the operator serves (`setStatusURLs`, `lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go:4006-4022`): wildcards dropped, in `EffectiveHosts` order, which is `spec.host`, then the platform host `<slug>.onbex.co`, then `spec.hosts`.
  - Verified custom domains are appended to `spec.hosts` in the order they were added (`apps/domains.go`).
  - A root domain's `www` counterpart, or the reverse, is in `spec.hostRedirects` (source → target).
- Render's primary URL is a custom domain when the service has one, and its line counts the other domains (milestone README § Render's block).

## Steps

1. Migration `0328_deploy_live_urls`: `ALTER TABLE deploys ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS live_urls text[]`, with a comment naming this milestone. NULL means no record. Add the down migration.
2. Add `URLs []string` to `RanImage` and `LiveURLs []string` to `Deploy` (`json:"-"`, since it is not a Render deploy field). Write the column on the transition to `live` only, and only when the row has none yet. Read it through `deployColumns` and `scanDeploy`, and mirror both in the fake store.
3. In `recordDeploy`'s live branch, fill `ran.URLs` for a `web_service` from `cur`, primary first. The primary is the first `spec.host` or `spec.hosts` entry that `status.urls` serves and that is not a `spec.hostRedirects` source; failing that, `status.url`. The rest of `status.urls` follows in its own order. A private service, a worker, a static site and a cron job record nothing.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A real-database test shows that a live close writes `live_urls`. A second close, or a later transition to `deactivated`, does not rewrite it.
- [ ] Unit tests pick the primary:
  - the platform URL when there is no custom domain;
  - the first verified custom domain over the platform URL;
  - the root over its `www` redirect, and `www` over the root when the root is the redirect;
  - no wildcard;
  - nothing when the platform subdomain is disabled and no custom domain exists.
- [ ] A static site, a private service, a background worker and a cron job record no URLs.
- [ ] `cd lego/backend && go test ./...` passes against the real dependencies (`scripts/backend-test-deps.sh`).

## Out of scope

- Backfilling rows that went live before the column existed. They keep no block.
