## Objective

`progressLines` writes `==> Starting pre-deploy: <command>` at the step's recorded start, and, on a native runtime, `==> Running '<command>'` at its container's recorded start. Both quote the command as configured, as Render's do (`Starting pre-deploy: Node.js`, `Running 'Node.js'`).

## Context

- `progressLines` (`lego/backend/internal/logs/progress.go:367`) and `rolloutLines` (`:485`): `rolloutLines` holds each line just after the one before it when its moment does not order it (`rolloutLineStep`). Use the same rule so `Running` never sorts before `Starting pre-deploy`.
- Render's log: `Starting pre-deploy` about 4 s after `Build successful`, and `Running` about 12 s after that (Unwrenchable/ATOMIC-FIZZ-CAPS-VAULT-77-WASTELAND-GPS#82).
- A record stamped after the row closed is not the row's, as in `rolloutLines`.
- Look for a public Render log of a Dockerfile or prebuilt image service's pre-deploy step. Record what you find in the milestone README. Without one, `Running` follows w32/m560's start-command rule: native runtimes only.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Unit tests: a native step gets both lines in that order, a Dockerfile or image step gets only `Starting pre-deploy`, a deploy with no step gets neither, and neither line sorts before the build's last line.
- [x] `cd lego/backend && go test ./internal/logs/...` passes.

## Done (2026-11-22)

`preDeployLines` (`lego/backend/internal/logs/progress.go`) writes `==> Starting pre-deploy: <command>` at the recorded launch and, for a native launch, `==> Running '<command>'` at the container's start, each held just after the line before it. Only a step whose launch was recorded is narrated. Public logs settle the launch rule: ClipFarmVB/ClipFarm#173 and ganjasan/mapsurvey#72 (Docker) and BerriAI/litellm#9549 and briankeane/alloy-client-portal#8 (prebuilt image) print the output straight after the start line, while every native log has `Running` (recorded in the README). Tests: `TestPreDeployLinesFollowRendersLogByOutcomeLaunchAndServiceType`, `TestPreDeployLinesCarryTheRecordedMoments`, `TestPreDeployLinesKeepTheirOrderInsideTheDeploy`, `TestPreDeployLinesReadAroundTheBuildAndTheStepsOutput` (the start after the build's last line in the same second).
