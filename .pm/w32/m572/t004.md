## Objective

A step that passes ends with `==> Pre-deploy complete!` (BerriAI/litellm#9549, ganjasan/mapsurvey#73) at its recorded end. The line sorts after the command's last output line and before the rollout's `==> Deploying...`.

## Context

- The rollout's lines come from `rolloutLines` (`lego/backend/internal/logs/progress.go:485`) at `status.releaseRollout`'s start, which the operator writes only after the step has passed.
- If t001 kept whole seconds, hold the line to the close of its second, as `hookMoments` (`progress.go:629`) does, and keep it before the rollout's start.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Unit tests: a passing step's line sits between the step's output and `==> Deploying...`. A step still running has no line, and a failed one has none.
- [ ] `cd lego/backend && go test ./internal/logs/...` passes.
