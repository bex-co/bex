## Objective

A `pre_deploy_failed` deploy whose command exited non-zero closes with Render's three lines: `==> Pre-deploy has failed`, `==> Exited with status N` and `==> Common ways to troubleshoot your deploy: https://render.com/docs/troubleshooting-deploys`. They sort after the command's last output line. The other failures keep their own wording.

## Context

- `rendersVerdict` (`lego/backend/internal/logs/progress.go:763`) and `closingLines` (`:745`). Today a `pre_deploy_failed` row falls through to `terminalLines`: `==> Pre-deploy failed: <failureReason>`.
- Render writes the three lines within a second of each other (Unwrenchable/ATOMIC-FIZZ-CAPS-VAULT-77-WASTELAND-GPS#82). w8/393's start-command close has the same pair after its own verdict.
- The failures with no Render log keep bex's line: an out-of-memory kill, an image pull failure, no build minutes left, a program the runtime could not launch, and a step that could not start.
- The 30-minute window: read jdegreef/open-word-bible#1023 for its exact lines ("Pre-deploy has failed / Timed out"). If it gives them, write them, with `timedOutLine` (`==> Timed Out`) if they match it. If it does not, keep bex's line and record the gap in the milestone README.
- A step canceled while it ran is a canceled deploy, whose own close is unchanged.
