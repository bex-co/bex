## Objective

Prove the change reaches only what it should, and decide and record what agent-session sandboxes do.

## Context

- `dialGateway` also serves agent-session sandboxes (`raw.Metadata[metadataAgentSession]`, the stronger `can_view_sensitive` gate). Their image is `BEX_AGENT_SESSION_IMAGE`, and their working directory may be `/workspace` (ADR047, ADR054), so a blanket `cd $HOME` could change what agents and their users see.
- A sandbox suspended before this change resumes from a committed Alpine rootfs (w2/m278, ADR042 D5). It has no bash.
- Other `/bin/sh` callers: `service.go:1206`, `:1251` (agent status and transcript reads), `sshgateway/nativessh/session.go:105,117,129`, `sshgateway/webshell/websocket.go:368`, `sshgateway/sandboxfiles/sandboxfiles.go:343`.

## Steps

1. Enumerate every `dialGateway` and `mintAndDial` caller and classify it as a Render sandbox, an agent session or internal.
2. Decide agent-session behavior: unchanged, or the same wrapper. Record the reason in ADR047 or ADR104, using Devin as the reference product for agent sessions.
3. Add a test for a resumed pre-change sandbox (no bash), plus one for each caller class.
