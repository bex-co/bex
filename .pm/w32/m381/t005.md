## Objective

Show on production that a disconnected command keeps its sandbox awake and completes, and that the history records it.

## Steps

1. After the deploy carrying t002–t004 (check with `git merge-base --is-ancestor`), in `bex-canary` with the released CLI:
   - `bex sandboxes create --wait --timeout 1800 --idle-timeout 300 --network-policy deny-all -o json`
   - `timeout 5 bex sandboxes exec <sbx> -- bash -c 'sleep 480; date -u +%T > ~/done'`, then poll only `bex sandboxes list --all -o json`. The sandbox reads `running` for the whole 480 s and `suspended` about 300–330 s after the command ends.
   - Resume with `bex sandboxes exec <sbx> -- cat ~/done`, which prints the command's end time.
   - Repeat the disconnect with `timeout -s INT 5` (Ctrl-C).
   - `GET /v1/sandboxes/<sbx>/execs` shows that execution with its real `stoppedAt` and `exitCode: 0`.
   - Control: a background process (`bash -c 'nohup sleep 900 >/dev/null 2>&1 &'`) does not stop the sandbox suspending about 300 s later.
   - `bex sandboxes stop <sbx> --confirm`
2. Record the results and timings in the milestone README.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every check in step 1 gives its target result on production.
