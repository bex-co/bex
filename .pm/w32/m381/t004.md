## Objective

Check every other way a command's stream ends before the command does, and record for each whether the new rule covers it.

## Context

- `sshgateway/sandboxsse/sandboxsse.go:126`: the exec session cap defaults to 4 h.
- A bex-api or ssh-gateway rollout cuts open streams (compare w4/m352 for service shells).
- `ExecBuffered` (`exec.go:448-465`, MCP `sandbox_exec`) stops reading at the 2 MiB output limit and closes the upstream stream.
- File transfers also hold activity (`file_connect.go:260`), but a transfer whose client leaves is aborted, so it needs no change. Confirm that.
- Agent-session sandboxes never idle-suspend. Sandboxes created before the change may lack t001's wrapper.

## Steps
