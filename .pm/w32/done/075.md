# A planned Blueprint sync that is not approved never expires on bex; Render deletes it after 24 hours

Why: Render parity is the top product rule. Since w32/073 a no-changes re-plan no longer supersedes a waiting plan, so on bex a plan nobody approves stays approvable forever, while Render deletes it a day after planning.

- **Severity / estimate:** minor, parity, ~1h.
- **Contract (pinned `render-oss/cli` `v1.1.3-0.20261106232710-4fd34231b637`, `pkg/client/client_gen.go:242`, `CreateBlueprintSync`):** "A planned Sync that is not approved within 24 hours will be automatically deleted." Render's public API docs do not describe planning or approve (read 2026-11-21).
- **bex today (main after w32/073, 2026-11-21):** nothing retires a `created` row in `blueprint_syncs`. `LatestBlueprintPlan` (`lego/backend/internal/store/blueprint_plans.go`) returns it as the newest plan however old it is, and `ApproveBlueprint` (`lego/backend/internal/apps/blueprint_onboarding.go`) applies it. Before w32/073, a no-changes re-plan superseded it, so an unwanted plan could be cleared by planning again. Now only approving it, or a newer plan, replaces it.
- **Fix direction:** a plan still `created` 24 hours after it was planned is deleted, or is no longer the plan approve and `PlannedSync` answer for. Then approve answers as it does for a Blueprint with no waiting plan (`BLUEPRINT_NOTHING_TO_APPROVE`, or w32/071's no-changes sentence). Decide between a sweep and a read-time cutoff by what the dashboard's Approve control and `GET …/syncs` must show. A `created` Blueprint whose first plan expires must still have a way forward: re-plan, as on Render.
- **Tests:** a plan 24 h old is not approved, and approve answers "nothing to approve". A plan 23 h old still is. Use the service clock on the fake store and real Postgres.

## Acceptance

- Approve, GraphQL `blueprintPlannedSync` and MCP `approve_blueprint` treat a plan older than 24 hours as gone.
- ADR049 § Blueprint onboarding states the expiry.

DONE 2026-11-21: a planned sync not approved within 24 hours is gone, as Render's is. Render's own wording is the pinned CLI's: `CreateBlueprintSync` "A planned Sync that is not approved within 24 hours will be automatically deleted" (`pkg/client/client_gen.go:242`) and `ea blueprints plan` "A planned sync that is not approved within 24 hours is deleted" (`cmd/blueprintplan.go:27`). render.com's public docs say nothing about it (searched 2026-11-21). Render's approve takes "the newest Sync in the `created` state" (`client_gen.go:206`), and its `GET …/syncs` never lists a `created` sync (`client_gen.go:233`). So once a plan is 24 hours old (`started_at`, `store.BlueprintPlanApprovalWindow`), `LatestBlueprintPlan` passes over it on the service clock. Approve, GraphQL `blueprintPlannedSync` and MCP `approve_blueprint` then answer as if the plan had never been made. The attempt before it decides: an applied sync gets `409 BLUEPRINT_NOTHING_TO_APPROVE` naming it, a failed plan `BLUEPRINT_SYNC_PLANNING_FAILED`, a no-changes plan w32/071's sentence. With no attempt before it, the answer is `409 BLUEPRINT_NOTHING_TO_APPROVE` "this Blueprint has no planned sync (a plan not approved within 24 hours is deleted); plan one first". Both are used: the read-time cutoff keeps approve exact at the boundary, and the Blueprint recovery sweep, which runs every minute, deletes the row oldest first (migration 0322, index `blueprint_syncs_waiting_plan_idx`) so `GET …/syncs` stops listing it. A `created` Blueprint whose first plan expired stays `created`, and a re-plan is approvable. Tests: `blueprint_plan_expiry_test.go` covers a plan approved at 23 h; a first plan, a re-plan and a plan behind a no-changes plan, each gone at 24 h on the service, GraphQL and MCP; the sweep on the fake store; and the same on real Postgres with the history read. `TestBlueprintPlanApprovalWindowPG` covers the store cutoff and the sweep: only `created` rows expire, never a run or a failed plan. With the SQL cutoff turned off, both PG tests fail. With the fake's mirror turned off, the four fake expiry tests fail. ADR049 § Blueprint onboarding states the expiry. Dashboard: the Approve panel reads `blueprintPlannedSync`, which answers null once the plan expires, so w32/076 needs no extra rule to hide an expired plan. Follow-up w32/077: bex lists a waiting plan in `GET …/syncs` until it expires, and Render's list never does.
