## Objective

Check that every surface that reads a deploy's log shows the same three lines:

- REST `GET /v1/logs?type=build` and `GET /v1/logs/subscribe`;
- the GraphQL logs query;
- MCP `list_logs`;
- the dashboard's deploy page log pane;
- `bex logs`.

They all read one synthesis in `logs.Service`. Compare each with Render's logs in the milestone README.
