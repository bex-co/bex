## Objective

The control plane copies t001's record onto the deploy row whose generation it names, and `logs.DeployProgress` carries it. The deploy log can then narrate a deploy long after the App's status has moved on to a newer release.

## Context

- Migration `lego/backend/internal/store/migrations/0327_deploy_rollout.{up,down}.sql`, the next after `0326_deploy_port_scan_found` (re-check `git ls-tree origin/main lego/backend/internal/store/migrations` after every rebase, since workers often pick the same number). It adds columns such as `rollout_started_at`, `app_started_at timestamptz[]`, `rollout_command` and `rollout_web_concurrency`.
- `store.Deploy`, `deployColumns` and the scan (`lego/backend/internal/store/store.go:440-500`, `:3337-3342`). The precedents are the port-scan columns (w8/385, `SetDeployStallReason`) and `DeployEnd` (`reconciler.go:1261`, w8/m146).
- The adapter: `lego/backend/internal/api/server.go:1019` builds `logs.DeployProgress`. Add fields such as `RolloutStartedAt`, `AppStartedAt []time.Time`, `StartCommand` and `WebConcurrencyDefault`.

## Steps

1. Write the migration, the `Deploy` fields and the scan.
2. In the reconciler, stamp the row from `status.releaseRollout` only when the record's generation is the row's. Stamp the start, command and default once, and let the app starts grow up to the bound.
3. Extend the adapter.

- [ ] A real-database store test (`bash scripts/backend-test-deps.sh up && eval "$(bash scripts/backend-test-deps.sh env)"`) shows that:
