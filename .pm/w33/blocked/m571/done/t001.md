## Objective

Each native build records how many seconds BuildKit spent writing the image archive, exporting the layer cache and archiving `$XDG_CACHE_HOME` plus the dependency directories. The numbers go somewhere the operator or `kubectl` can read after the build, such as an operator metric, the build pod's termination message or an annotation, never the tenant log.

## Context

- BuildKit's plain transcript has per-vertex `DONE <seconds>` for `exporting to oci image format`, `exporting cache` and the XDG export solve. `nativeLogDisplay` (`lego/operator/internal/build/build.go:202`) drops it, and `bex_run` keeps only a bounded tail (`classifyBufferBytes`, `:330`).
- The termination-message budget is shared across containers (`terminationMessageBytes`, `podTerminationMessageBytes`, `build.go:174-184`).
- Build metrics live in `lego/operator/internal/build/census.go` and `signals.go`.

## Steps

1. Capture the three durations from the transcript in the buildkit phase, without touching what the tenant sees.
2. Expose them per build, for example as a histogram `bex_build_export_seconds{part=…}`, and as a line the operator logs with the build's Job name.

## Files

- `lego/operator/internal/build/build.go`, `xdgcache.go`
- `lego/operator/internal/build/signals.go` or `census.go`
- tests beside them

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A test transcript with known `DONE` seconds yields the three numbers.
- [x] The tenant's build log is byte-identical to before.

## Outcome (2026-11-22)

- The buildkit phase's display filter (`nativeLogDisplay`, `build.go`) reads BuildKit's own seconds off the transcript it already filters: the `exporting to oci image format` step's `DONE` (`image`), its `exporting layers … done` status (`layers`, the compression within it) and the `exporting cache to client directory` step's `DONE` (`cache`). The `$XDG_CACHE_HOME` wrapper (`xdgBuildctl`, `xdgcache.go`) times its archive's solve and reports it on a `bex-export-xdg <seconds>` line that the filter keeps out of the log (`xdg`). A phase that succeeds leaves them, plus `total` (from `==> Uploading build...` to the phase's end), as `export <part> <seconds>` lines in its termination message (`nativeExportReport`). A failed phase keeps the transcript's tail there, as before.
- The operator reads them with the build's other signals (`exportSignals`, `signals.go`), observes `bex_build_export_seconds{part}` and logs `build export seconds` with the App and Job names (`meterBuildSignals`). ADR060 D5 lists the series.
- Tests: `exportseconds_test.go` runs the phase under busybox, mawk and gawk on a transcript with known seconds and checks the message, the log (unchanged) and what the operator reads back; `total` counts from the upload line; a failed or Dockerfile phase reports nothing; the parser keeps only the closed set. `TestXDGBuildctlReportsTheSecondsItsArchiveTook` and `TestRecordBuildSignalsMetersEachExportPart` cover the wrapper and the metric.
