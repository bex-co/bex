## Objective

No existing service runs its hook twice, loses it, or stays stuck because the hook changed role.

## Context

- A completed hook is `status.initialDeployHookCompleted`, or on older services the `bex.co/initial-deploy-hook-ran` annotation; the Blueprint apply reconciles both (`lego/backend/internal/apps/deploy.go:4409`).
- A service created before this milestone may be mid-flight: its first release still waiting on, or failed by, the hook running as its pre-deploy step.

## Steps

1. A service with the hook completed (either marker) never runs it again.
2. A service whose first release failed on the hook as a pre-deploy step: its next release runs `preDeployCommand`, goes live, then runs the hook.
3. A service whose hook Job is running as a pre-deploy step when the new operator starts: let that Job finish and count its success as the hook's completion; do not start a second one.
4. Cover each case with an envtest or a controller test.

## Files

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go`
- `lego/operator/internal/controller/predeploy_initial_hook_test.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/apps/deploy.go` (only if the apply's reconciliation changes)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The three cases above pass as tests.
- [ ] No stored App needs a manual edit.

## Out of scope

- Re-running hooks for services whose first deploy succeeded without their `preDeployCommand`.

## Outcome (2026-11-16)

- Completed by either marker: `TestInitialDeployHookCompletedByEitherMarkerNeverRuns`.
- First release failed on the hook as its step: the recorded verdict stands (`legacyHookStep`), so an operator upgrade does not roll a failed release, and the next release runs its own command, then the hook once it is live: `TestReleaseFailedOnLegacyHookStepStaysFailedAndTheNextRunsItsOwnCommand`.
- Hook Job running as the step when the new operator starts: the older operator stamped no `app.bex.co/predeploy-step` label, which is how its Job is recognized (`ranHookAsStep`). It is watched to its end, no second run starts, and its success sets `status.initialDeployHookCompleted`, with or without a `preDeployCommand`: `TestLegacyHookStepStillRunningCarriesOver`, `TestLegacyHookStepReapedUnreadLeavesTheHookOwed`, `TestCanceledLegacyHookStepStillCompletesTheHook`.
- `lego/backend/internal/apps/deploy.go` needed no change to the apply's reconciliation; the ran-once annotation's name moved to `lego/types` so the operator reads the same marker.
- No stored App needs an edit.
