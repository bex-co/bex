## Outcome (2026-11-16)

`/simplify` ran with its three reviews (reuse, quality, efficiency) over the diff. Applied, behavior-preserving unless noted:

- One builder: `predeploy.Job` builds both Jobs; the controller shares `stepJobOptions`, `runningStep`, `failedStep`, `outOfBuildMinutes` and `endedByPullFailure` between the step and the hook.
- Redundant state removed: the step label lives in `internal/predeploy` (only the operator reads it); whether a Job shares the disk is derived from its volumes, not a second option; `OwedInitialDeployHook` returns one string; `scalableRuntime` delegates to `App.RollsDeployment`; `diskRestoreOwed` is the one restore-pending predicate.
- Backend: one `progressEntry` for platform lines, one `store.PreDeployRowStatus` mapping, one `pointerLeaf`.
- Dashboard: `hookWindowEnd` reuses `stepLogEnd`.
- Two findings were defects, fixed with tests: a deploy replaced while its hook ran was never read again, so its log window polled forever (`useDeploy` now reads on until the hook ends); and every deploy-scoped log read named a hook Job per other deploy even for services with no hook (now only for a service that has one, so a hook-less service's selector is byte-identical to before).
- Not applied: hoisting the failure-sentence fragments into shared constants (the hook-wording test already fails on drift), and a cross-package "ran for this deploy" predicate.

The suites in the definition of done were re-run after the changes.
