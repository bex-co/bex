## Objective

Once t001 has reported a build succeeded, what `cache-save` does afterwards cannot change the build's verdict or its release. That covers a `cache-save` that is still running, exits non-zero, is OOM-killed, or leaves the Job `Failed`. The cache's own guarantee stays "the cache changes only speed" (`build.go:1342-1346`).

## Context

- `buildPodFailurePolicy` and the Job's retry budget (`build.go:91-123`, `:1409`) decide whether a failed container fails the Job. A Job that fails because of `cache-save` after the push succeeded would today read as `JobFailed` → `PhaseFailed`.
- The reconcile already stamps the build's success onto the App (`stampBuildRun`, `lego/operator/internal/controller/build_failure.go:131`, called at `app_controller.go:1458` and `:5056`) and dispatches the release.
- The build-slot census (`lego/operator/internal/build/census.go`) counts build pods. A pod still saving its cache is still on its node, and must still count until it exits.

## Steps

1. Find where a later `EnsureBuild` read could flip a recorded success, and make a success recorded for the release generation final. A `JobFailed` whose only failed container is `cache-save` reads as succeeded.
2. Check that the cache-save script exits 0 on a failed upload (it is best-effort), and record that in a test.
3. Confirm the census still counts the pod while `cache-save` runs, and the Job's TTL reaps it as before.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A build whose `push` succeeded and whose `cache-save` then failed (exit non-zero, and `OOMKilled`) stays succeeded, and its release proceeds (envtest). (`TestACacheSaveFailureAfterThePushLeavesTheBuildSucceeded`, `TestABuildRollsOutAtItsImagePushWhileItsCacheSaveRuns` and the envtest `A build's verdict is its image push (w33/m569)`)
- [x] A build whose `push` failed still ends `build_failed`, whatever `cache-save` does. (`TestAFailedPushFailsTheBuildWhateverCacheSaveDoes`, `TestAFailedPushStillFailsTheBuild` and the same envtest)
- [x] The census counts the pod until `cache-save` exits. (by construction: `CountBuilds` counts every Job not finished, and the Job finishes only once `cache-save` exits; the save scripts' exit 0 on a failed upload is pinned by `TestS3CacheScriptsNeverFailTheBuild` and the registry scripts' by `native_log_display_test.go`)
