# A build's `build_seconds` still runs to its Job's end, past the image push where the build now ends

Why: since w33/m569 every surface says a build ended at its image push, but bex-api still bills its pipeline minutes up to the moment `cache-save` exits, a minute or more later on a large cache.

- **What changed:** w33/m569 settles a build when its image's last phase exits 0. `build_ended`, `status.buildRun.finishedAt` and the deploy row's move to `update_in_progress` now follow the push. The build Job still runs until `cache-save` has stored the layer cache.
- **What did not:** `jobBilledSeconds` (`lego/backend/internal/usage/build_seconds.go`) bills from `app.bex.co/build-placed-at` to the Job's `completionTime` (or its `Failed` condition), so the `cache-save` tail is billed as build time. On `eden-dash-v3` (first-party `bex` workspace, 2026-11-22) that tail was 70 s.
- **Render:** its deploy log goes from `==> Build successful 🎉` to `==> Deploying...`. Whether Render counts a build-cache upload in its pipeline minutes was not checked; read its pipeline-minutes docs first.
- **End state:** pick whether a build's billed window ends at its push, citing what Render documents. If it does, the operator stamps the push's end on the Job beside `build-placed-at` when it meters the build, `jobBilledSeconds` bills to it, and a backend test shows a build whose Job completed 70 s after its push billed to the push. This is a price-affecting change: record the choice in ADR023 and ADR018.

**Done 2026-11-22.** Choice: a succeeded build's billed window ends at its image push. Render defines pipeline minutes as the time it spends running build commands and pre-deploy commands (its docs' glossary) and says builds consume them while they are running (render.com/docs/build-pipeline, read 2026-11-22); it documents no cache upload after `==> Build successful 🎉`. When it meters a succeeded build, the operator stamps the image phase's end on the Job as `app.bex.co/build-ended-at` (`build.StampMetering`), and `jobBilledSeconds` bills from `build-placed-at` to it. The stamp also keeps a build that succeeded at its push from turning into a platform failure (zero billed) when a disruption retry after the push spends the Job's backoff: that rare build now bills its successful build, the one case that bills more than before. The run is still metered in the hour its Job ended, so a stamp written seconds after the push cannot miss its hour. Guards: `TestBuildSecondsEndsAtTheImagePush` (a Job completed 70 s after its push bills 240 s, not 310 s; it fails on the old code), `TestStampMeteringRecordsPlacementEndAndFault`, and `TestABuildRollsOutAtItsImagePushWhileItsCacheSaveRuns` (the Job carries the push's end). Recorded in ADR023 § `build_seconds`, ADR018 (pipeline-minutes row), ADR030 §13 and ADR060's D3 amendment. Not yet seen on production: it needs a pin carrying the change and a native build whose `cache-save` outlasts its push, the same build w33/m569 t007 waits for.
