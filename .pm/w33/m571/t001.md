## Objective

Each native build records how many seconds BuildKit spent writing the image archive, exporting the layer cache and archiving `$XDG_CACHE_HOME` plus the dependency directories. The numbers go somewhere the operator or `kubectl` can read after the build, such as an operator metric, the build pod's termination message or an annotation, never the tenant log.

## Context

- BuildKit's plain transcript has per-vertex `DONE <seconds>` for `exporting to oci image format`, `exporting cache` and the XDG export solve. `nativeLogDisplay` (`lego/operator/internal/build/build.go:202`) drops it, and `bex_run` keeps only a bounded tail (`classifyBufferBytes`, `:330`).
- The termination-message budget is shared across containers (`terminationMessageBytes`, `podTerminationMessageBytes`, `build.go:174-184`).
- Build metrics live in `lego/operator/internal/build/census.go` and `signals.go`.

## Steps

1. Capture the three durations from the transcript in the buildkit phase, without touching what the tenant sees.
2. Expose them per build, for example as a histogram `bex_build_export_seconds{part=…}`, and as a line the operator logs with the build's Job name.

- `lego/operator/internal/build/build.go`, `xdgcache.go`
