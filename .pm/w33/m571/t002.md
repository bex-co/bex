## Objective

With t001 live, read the split on a large native Node build and choose the change that removes the most time without slowing pulls or losing cache hits.

## Context

- Candidates, to be chosen from the numbers rather than in advance:
  - (a) **zstd image layers.** The `oci` exporter's `compression=zstd` (plus `compression-level`), with `force-compression` where needed. Check that every consumer handles them: Zot, `skopeo copy` (the push), cosign (signing), containerd on the tenant nodes (the pull), and the admission webhook's signature check.
  - (b) **A cache that skips the layer a source change always invalidates.** `mode=max` exports the build command's layer, whose cache key includes `COPY . .`. The dependency directories and `$XDG_CACHE_HOME` already travel in the XDG archive (`depscache.go`, `xdgcache.go`).
  - (c) **Less work for the gzip archive**, such as BuildKit's parallel compression options.
- Measure on a disposable `bex-canary` Node fixture with a dependency tree of 1 GB or more (a copy of a public large Next.js app), or on the next organic build of `eden-dash-v3`. Do not trigger deploys of other first-party services to measure.

## Steps

1. Run at least two builds and record the split.
2. Prototype the leading candidate on the same fixture and record the split, the pull time and the next build's cache restore.
3. Write the choice and its numbers into ADR060 (§D3/§D4), marked as this milestone's recommendation and not a user decision.
