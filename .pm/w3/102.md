# A phone's project Application Logs view opened on Live tail stays at the newest line

Why: at 390 a project's Application Logs view on Live tail sometimes opens mid-history, about 400 to 1,000 lines above the newest one, with Return to Bottom and "100+ new logs" showing. The reader has scrolled nowhere, yet sees old lines while the tail holds the new ones. Render's Live tail opens at the newest line and follows it.

Found by w3/m537 t006's production walk (2026-11-21, pin `f797f525f5df`, QA user in `bex-canary`, a `busybox:1.36` web service logging a line a second for about 35 minutes, so Live tail's 4-hour window held about 1,700 lines):

- **Project Application Logs at 390** (`/project/<prj>/observability/logs/application?savedSearchId=<svs>&r=live`), read 7 s after load: 4 of 9 fresh loads were not at the newest line. Examples: scrollTop 7789 of 42797 (n 210–215 visible), 30162 of 44093, 30768 of 43838 and 18239 of 45884. Picking Live tail from Last hour or Last 4 hours did the same in 2 of 4 tries. One showed n 422 with nothing arriving for 5 s; the other sat at scrollTop 11332 of 17761, n 350–355, behind "100+ new logs". The rest sat at the end (scrollTop equal to its maximum).
- **Not seen elsewhere:** the same view at 1440 (0 of 5 loads and 0 of 3 picks; rows 24 px) and the service Logs tab at 390 (0 of 4 loads and 0 of 2 picks). At 390 the project rows, which carry the `[resource]` tag, measured 67–86 px against `ROW_ESTIMATE = 24`.
- **What the view reads:** Live tail subscribes from the window's start (`GET /v1/logs/subscribe?project=…&type=app&startTime=<now − 4h>`), so the whole window arrives as one SSE burst.

Likely cause (to confirm): `LogLineList` (`dashboard/src/features/logs/components/log-line-list.tsx`) recomputes its pin on every scroll (`recordPin`, `fromBottom(el) <= PIN_THRESHOLD`, 24 px). During the burst, `scrollToNewest` (`virtualizer.scrollToIndex(last, { align: "end" })`) lands on estimated heights. The rows then measure up to three times taller, and a scroll event in between reads the reader as away. Then `holdWhileAway` (`project-log-viewer.tsx`, `holdWhileAway={tailing}`) freezes the view. The opening replay predates w3/m537: Live on read its window the same way.

Done when a project's Application Logs and Request Logs views, the service Logs tab and a datastore Logs tab, opened on Live tail at 390 or with Live tail picked there, sit at the newest line after the opening replay, every time, over at least 10 tries on a window of more than 1,000 wrapped lines. A reader who scrolls away still gets held lines (w3/m528). A test fails if the pin is released by a growth the reader did not scroll.
