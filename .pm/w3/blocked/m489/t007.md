## Objective

On production, an edge-cached fixture keeps its cache hits across a Restart and a scale-out, shares hits across two instances, and empties on a successful deploy and a purge.

## Context

- Production pin containing this milestone's commits; QA user in `bex-canary`; production kubeconfig via `bash scripts/fetch-app-kubeconfig.sh <path outside the repo>`.

## Steps

1. Warm a cacheable path; Restart the service; the path still reads HIT.
2. Scale to two instances; a new key fills once (one upstream request in the app's logs) and then reads HIT from both.
3. Deploy successfully: the path reads MISS once, then HIT; purge: the same.
4. Write the record into the milestone README.
