## Objective

Design and record the per-service edge-cache tier that replaces the per-pod sidecar: topology, fail-open, purge rules, capacity, replica count, resource accounting, tenant NetworkPolicy and cost.

## Context

- Today: `docs/ADR004-app-deployment.md` § Edge caching: "The cache is per instance and in memory, bounded at 48 MiB" (6 MiB per object, `lego/operator/cmd/edge-cache/main.go` `maxBytes / 8`). A Restart (`spec.restartedAt`), a scale-out (cold instance), a scale-in, an OOM or eviction, a node drain and turning caching on (sidecar injection) each empty or cold-start the cache; N instances fill each key N times per TTL.
- Render: "Edge caching is powered by the same global CDN as Render static sites"; it purges "each time you successfully deploy", and "failed deploys do not trigger a purge" ([web-service-caching](https://render.com/docs/web-service-caching)). w6/m484 adopted "the cache empties only on a successful deploy or a manual purge" as the rule; a per-pod cache cannot meet it.
- Recommended shape (the brainstorm's recommendation under ADR008 Render parity first, not a user decision): a cache Deployment + Service per caching App in the tenant's own namespace (keeping ADR004's reason for per-pod: no cross-tenant cache), the Ingress pointing at it, forwarding to the app Service; a disk-backed `emptyDir` with a `sizeLimit`; fail-open to the app Service when the tier is unavailable.
- The purge signal from `w3/m486` (a purge generation read at run time) is reused.

## Steps

1. Write the ADR004 amendment: topology, Ingress switch rule (only when the tier is Ready, as the sidecar's "Service stays direct until every pod has the sidecar"), fail-open mechanism in Traefik, replicas (1 with fail-open, or 2 with key affinity), capacity and object cap, NetworkPolicy, who pays (caching is free on Render's paid services), and the migration from sidecars.
2. Record the per-service cost (pod resources × caching services on production).
3. Mark in ADR018's Edge caching row that convergence is scheduled here.

## Record (2026-11-19)

Done. ADR004 § Edge caching now records the per-service tier as option A under ADR008 Render parity first, with option B (keep the sidecar and sign off the divergence in ADR018) left as the user's call. It covers topology (a `bex-edge-cache-<app>` Deployment plus a headless Service in the App's own namespace, owned by the App), the upstream (port 10000 of the App's own Service, which follows the primary through a port change), the route switch (to the tier while one of its pods is Ready, back to the Service otherwise; caching off moves the route first, then removes the tier), replicas and fail-open (two replicas, `maxUnavailable: 0`; one cannot fail open, because Traefik v3.7 fences a terminating endpoint and answers 503 when only fenced endpoints are left, per `pkg/server/service/loadbalancer/wrr`), one fill per key (rendezvous hashing over the Ready peers), capacity (1 GiB per replica on a 1792 MiB `emptyDir`, 128 MiB per object), NetworkPolicy (none added: the namespace's own allows select the tier pods by their workspace and environment labels), who pays (bex; the tier pods carry no `app.bex.co/app`, so they appear in no bill or metric), image hold, what empties the cache, the migration from sidecars, and the tests. Cost: about 0.2 vCPU and 384 MiB requested per caching service; production had 0 of 27 Apps caching (2026-11-19, read-only through `scripts/fetch-app-kubeconfig.sh`). ADR018's Edge caching row replaces "divergence in mechanism, not behavior" with the tier and the mechanism differences that stay (per-service, not a global edge; bounded capacity). prettier and `bash scripts/docs-links-validate.sh` pass.
