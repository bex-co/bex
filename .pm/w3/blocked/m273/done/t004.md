## Objective

When a new revision's discovered ports differ, update the Service without dropping ports the running revision still serves, and settle on the new set once the rollout completes.

## Context

- Rollout logic in `lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go`

## Steps

1. Union old/new during rollout.
2. Converge after.

## Files

- lego/operator/internal/controller/

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] envtest: no port flaps to absent during a rollout that changes listeners

## Result (2026-11-05)

During a rollout the old revision's ports stay published; after completion the set converges; a timed-out scan can add but never remove. Envtest covers each step and a no-op rescan.
