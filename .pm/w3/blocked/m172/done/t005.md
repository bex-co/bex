## Objective

Make an abandoned workspace-create attempt delete its Stripe Customer even when `ProviderCustomerID` never reached the database.

## Context

- `PrepareWorkspaceSetup` (`lego/backend/internal/billing/workspace_creation.go:50`) creates the Customer, then the SetupIntent; `SetWorkspaceCreationSetup` (`lego/backend/internal/workspaces/service.go:416`) persists the ids afterwards. A failure in between leaves `ProviderCustomerID = ""`.
- `CleanupWorkspaceSetup` (`workspace_creation.go:232`) skips Customer deletion when the id is empty.
- These Customers carry `workspaceCreationAttemptMetadataKey = <attemptID>`.

## Steps

1. On cleanup with an empty id, search Customers by the attempt metadata and delete matches (same livemode, same workspace) — or persist the Customer id before creating the SetupIntent; pick whichever leaves no window.
