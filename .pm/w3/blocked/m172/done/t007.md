Confirm `workspaceBillingReadiness` (`customerReady`, `subscriptionReady`, `paymentMethodReady`) and the checkout-session endpoint behave identically on REST, GraphQL, and MCP after the lifecycle change, and the dashboard onboarding/payment surfaces render correctly before, during, and after Checkout and after a reclaim.

## Outcome (2026-10-02)

Done. REST/GraphQL/MCP readiness and checkout funnel through one `billing.Service` → provider call (existing `TestBillingStatusRESTGraphQLMCPParity`), so the lifecycle change cannot drift between surfaces. Semantic change, identical on all three: before a card is bound `customerReady`/`subscriptionReady` stay false (new `TestStripeReadinessForNewWorkspaceIsEmptyAndMintsNothing`). Dashboard needs no change — the Add-payment button never depended on `customerReady`; Portal correctly stays disabled until a Customer exists. Render publishes no billing/payment-method API, so this is a bex extension with nothing to diverge from.
