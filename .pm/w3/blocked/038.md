# Move the warning digest off email to a low-noise operator channel

Why: email has no ack, snooze, or dedupe. The 2026-09-11 tiering keeps warnings to one daily digest and critical to a real page, but the right home for tickets is a webhook receiver (ntfy / Telegram / Discord) with only `critical` staying on email — that needs a channel decision from the operator and one out-of-band Secret (same custody rule as `alertmanager-smtp`).

- Add a `webhook_configs` receiver for the `severity="warning"` route in `deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml`, URL from a mounted Secret (`extraSecretMounts`), never committed.
- Keep `scripts/alerts-verify.sh`'s receiver swap in step (it must list every receiver the route references).
- This is the operator's own on-call channel, not a tenant-facing Slack integration (`DO_NOT_DO.md` round 14 is untouched).
