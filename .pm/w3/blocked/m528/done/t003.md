## Objective

While Live is on and the reader is away from the newest line, arriving lines are held back instead of joining the list under the reader. Render's centred `BufferedLogsButton` counts them. Clicking it, or scrolling back to the newest line, releases them.

## Context

- Render (milestone README § Render's explorer): the button reads `New logs` below 10 held lines, otherwise `${Math.ceil(Math.min(n, 100) / 5) * 5}+ new logs`. It carries an arrow (`MoveDown` ascending, `MoveUp` descending) and is centred at the newest edge. It applies only in Live tail to runtime logs.
- bex today: `LogLineList` keeps an unpinned reader's row in place by key as lines arrive (`anchorRef`, the `useLayoutEffect` at about line 407). New rows join the list beyond the reader's view.
- Where the lines come from: each explorer merges history and `useLiveLogs` lines (`log-viewer.tsx` `mergeLogLines`, and the datastore and project viewers), then passes `lines` to `LogLineList`. The list does not know which lines came from the tail.
- The explorers with a live tail of runtime lines: `log-viewer.tsx`, `datastore-log-viewer.tsx` and `project-log-viewer.tsx`. A deploy's log (`deploys/components/deploy-log-panel.tsx`) follows a build and keeps adding lines.

## Steps

1. Decide where the hold lives. One option is a `holdWhileAway` prop on `LogLineList`: while unpinned, the list renders the lines it had when the reader left plus any older pages, and counts the newer ones. The other is a hook the three explorers share. Record the choice in a code comment. Older pages loaded at the far edge must still appear while lines are held.
2. Render the centred button at the newest edge with the count wording above, through pluralized keys (`dashboard/AGENTS.md` § pluralization). It sits beside t002's Return button without overlapping it at 390 px.
3. Clicking it releases the held lines and pins the reader to the newest line. Scrolling back to the newest edge releases them too. `Shift+L` (clear) drops the held lines along with the shown ones.
4. Turn it on in the three explorers. Leave a deploy's log as it is.
5. Write tests in `log-line-list-explorer.test.tsx` or a new file. Cover: lines held while away, both wordings (9 held, then 10, 23 and 140), release by click and by scroll, descending order, and a deploy's log not holding.
