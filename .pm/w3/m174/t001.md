## Objective

Give the alert pipeline a heartbeat and make its own failures alertable: an always-firing `Watchdog`, scrapes of Prometheus and Alertmanager themselves, and rules for failed notifications and failed rule evaluations.

## Context

- `deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml`: Alertmanager values at `:122-158` (SendGrid SMTP, out-of-band Secret `alertmanager-smtp`), scrape jobs at `:263-742` (no `prometheus`/`alertmanager` job), rule groups below.
- t002 consumes the `Watchdog` alert from outside the cluster; route it so it is never emailed (a dedicated null/heartbeat route), never silenced by the warning digest.

## Steps

1. Add scrape jobs for Prometheus and Alertmanager.
2. Add `Watchdog` (`vector(1)`, its own route), `AlertmanagerNotificationsFailing` (`increase(alertmanager_notifications_failed_total[15m]) > 0`), `PrometheusRuleEvaluationFailures` and `PrometheusNotificationsDropped`/queue-full, with severities and descriptions in the file's existing style.
3. Ensure the Watchdog route does not reach the email receiver.
4. Run `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` and render the chart values.

## Files

- `deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Rendered config contains both scrape jobs and the four rules; `gitops (render)` passes.
- [ ] `Watchdog` fires continuously and is excluded from email delivery.
