## Objective

Bring the operator's nightly tenant disk-snapshot CronJobs under the same staleness alert as every other backup, and record disks in ADR031's backup tables.

## Context

- `BackupCronJobStale` (`deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml:1687-1694`) matches `etcd-backup|openbao-backup|kvbak-.*`.
- The operator names disk-snapshot CronJobs `dskbak-…` (`lego/operator/internal/controller/disk_backup.go:48`); they are the only recovery point for tenant disks (ADR082 D5 — Hetzner has no volume snapshots).
- ADR031 store table `:9-16` and RPO / re-drill sections `:443-455` have no disk row.

## Steps

1. Add `dskbak-.*` to both regex arms of `BackupCronJobStale` (success-age and never-succeeded).
2. Add the disk row to ADR031's store and RPO tables (schedule, retention, restore path from ADR082).
3. Extend the rule test with a stale and a fresh `dskbak-*` series.
