## Objective

Find the step that holds a delivery open for up to an hour, and why the handler is not cancelled when GitHub disconnects.

## Steps

1. Add per-step timing logs (scope resolution, `repoCandidates` list, claim, `prepareCheckedPush`/`awaitChecks`, blueprint intents, each redeploy) with the delivery key.
2. Check whether the request context is cancelled when the client (GitHub, 10 s) or Traefik disconnects; a handler past `WriteTimeout` (30 s) keeps running.
3. Reproduce locally (dev-N, a repo with checks-gated apps) or read production logs after the instrumentation rolls out (read-only access per the w3 2026-10-29 D1 grant).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The blocking step named with evidence (log excerpt or reproduction).
