## Objective

Return `202` once the delivery is verified, claimed and durably recorded; perform candidate resolution and redeploys in a worker, reusing the blueprint auto-sync intent pattern.

## Steps

1. Persist a delivery intent (signed body digest, scope, event) in the same transaction as the replay claim.
2. A worker (bounded concurrency, per-step timeouts) processes intents: candidates, checks gating, blueprint intents, redeploys; failures retry with backoff and are visible in events.
3. The handler keeps its fast refusals (signature, shape, multitenant manual key) and returns `202 {accepted:true}`.
4. Keep replay protection: a redelivery of the same signed body is a no-op.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Handler returns within 10 s under a slow downstream (test with a blocking fake).
- [ ] Exactly-once redeploy per delivery across a bex-api restart mid-processing.
