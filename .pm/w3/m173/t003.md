## Objective

Make every job that reads a production-class secret run under the environment t002 assigned, so moving the secrets into environment scope (t005) cannot break a workflow and no job outside a production environment can read one.

## Context

- Secret-reading workflows with no `environment:`: `cnb-run-image.yml`, `deploy-canary.yml`, `isolation-matrix.yml`, `log-shipper-image.yml`, `ssh-edge-liveness.yml`; multi-job workflows (e.g. `deploy.yml`, 35 secret references, one environment job at :438) where other jobs read secrets too.
- `scripts/github-actions-validate.sh` already enforces self-hosted labels, the `bex-ci`/`bex-production` pool split and the SHA-pinned action inventory — keep it green.

## Steps

1. Add the t002 `environment:` to each production-secret job in `.github/workflows/` and `lego/operator/.github/workflows/`.
2. Where a job mixes non-production steps (lint, build, test) with a production secret, split it so the secret is confined to the environment job; pass outputs/artifacts between jobs instead.
3. Keep runner labels and the production pool routing unchanged.
4. Run `scripts/github-actions-validate.sh` and its self-test.

## Files

- `.github/workflows/*.yml`
- `lego/operator/.github/workflows/*.yml`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every job in the t001 table that reads a production-class secret has the t002 environment.
- [ ] No production-class secret is read by a job reachable from `pull_request`.
- [ ] `scripts/github-actions-validate.sh` and `scripts/github-actions-validate.test.sh` pass.

## Out of scope

- The fail-closed guard (t004) and the settings change (t005).
