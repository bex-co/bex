## Objective

Decide, per production environment, the deployment-branch policy, the reviewer requirement and the secrets it holds, and record the decision where the obligation lives, so the gates are both load-bearing and compatible with deploy-per-push and the scheduled probes.

## Context

- ADR083:101 obligation 5 says to keep all six environments configured with required reviewers; ADR080:58 left creating them as an operator action. `#RUNNER-HOSTS` waived obligations 1–2 only.
- `deploy.yml` runs on every push to `main` (several production deploys a day); required reviewers on `production-deploy` would make every push wait for a human.
- Scheduled jobs that read production credentials (t001) cannot wait for a reviewer.

## Steps

1. Recommended shape (confirm or amend with the user): a main-only custom deployment-branch policy on **every** `production-*` environment; required reviewers on the rarely-run, high-blast ones (`production-restore`, `production-infra`, `production-snapshot`, `production-cluster`, `production-release`); **no** reviewers on `production-deploy`; scheduled probes get their own main-only environment (e.g. `production-observe`) holding only the credentials they read.
2. Assign each production-class secret from t001 to the environment(s) whose jobs read it; secrets read by no workflow are listed for deletion.
3. Amend ADR083 obligation 5 and ADR019 §Decision 5 with the table (names only) and the reasoning (deploy-per-push, scheduled probes); add a dated note at ADR080:58 pointing at this milestone.
