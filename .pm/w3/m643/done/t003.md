## Objective

A service its owner suspended reads "Suspended by you" in a Hobby workspace and "Manually suspended" on every other plan, as Render's notice does.

## Context

- Render: `suspension-*.js` `g = tier => tier === TIER1 ? "Suspended by you" : "Manually suspended"`, called by `CronJob-*.js` `Fr` (the user suspension) and for the removed-card suspension, with the current workspace's tier. `TIER1` is Hobby (`billing-confirm-plan-utils-*.js`).
- bex: `suspendedNoticeTitleKey` and `suspendedNoticeTitleKeyFor` (`dashboard/src/features/services/lib/status.ts`), drawn by `ServiceSuspendedNotice` (`dashboard/src/features/services/components/service-suspended-notice.tsx`) and the header's loading frame. The workspace's plan id is `plan` on the workspace context (`dashboard/src/features/workspaces/types.ts`, `WORKSPACE_PLAN_IDS`).

## Steps

1. Pass the current workspace's plan to the title choice; a `user` suspender in a `hobby` workspace takes a new "Suspended by you" key in every locale.
2. Keep the loading frame's title the same as the ready one.
