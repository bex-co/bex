## Objective

Resume service (the notice's link, the Settings card, the row menu) opens the deploy the resume answers, and toasts what Render's does.

## Context

- Render (`CronJob-*.js` `Pr`): success toasts "**`<name>`** has been resumed." (auto-dismissed) and opens `t.build` for a cron job or `t.deploy` for a server when present; failure toasts "An error occurred while resuming **`<name>`**. Please try again." (auto-dismissed).
- bex: `useServiceLifecycle` (`dashboard/src/features/services/hooks/use-service-lifecycle.ts`) toasts "Resuming {name}…" or "Could not update {name}. Please try again." and polls; `ResumeServiceButton` (`dashboard/src/features/services/components/resume-service-button.tsx`).

## Steps

1. On a resume that answers `latestDeployId`, navigate to `/services/<id>/deploys/<deploy>`; with none, stay.
2. Use Render's two resume toasts in every locale; a protected environment's confirm still runs first.
