## Objective

A cron job's "Last successful run" reads as Render's: the date in the viewer's zone with the zone's abbreviation ("November 21, 2026 at 9:00 AM PST"), and, only when the viewer is not on UTC, a tooltip reading "UTC: Nov 21 05:00:00 PM".

## Context

- Render: `page-templates-*.js` `CronJobRunMetadata` with `AuthContext-*.js` `H` (`LL [at] LT z`), `G` (the local zone's `z`, compared to `UTC`) and `q` (`MMM D hh:mm:ss A`, in UTC). See the milestone's § What Render does.
- bex: `CronRunFacts` in `dashboard/src/features/services/components/service-detail-header.tsx` wraps `LocalDateTime` (`dashboard/src/common/components/local-time.tsx`) in `InstantTooltip` (`dashboard/src/common/components/instant-tooltip.tsx`, local, UTC and Unix).
- `LocalDateTime` renders a phrasing-content placeholder before hydration because the line is a `<p>` (w3/m555's React #418 fix, `a43d36790f`): keep that.

## Steps

1. Format the date with the zone's short name (`Intl.DateTimeFormat` `timeZoneName: "short"`) after the time, in English as Render does; other languages keep their own date words and add the zone.
2. Replace `InstantTooltip` on this line with Render's tooltip: "UTC: " and the instant as `MMM D hh:mm:ss A` in UTC inside a `<time dateTime>`, drawn only when the viewer's zone is not UTC; the date is the hover-underlined trigger.
3. Keep the header's pending frame equal to the ready line at 390 and 1440 (`data-skeleton-item="cron-last-run"`).
