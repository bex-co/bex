# A Postgres's and a Key Value's header carries Render's eyebrow, Service ID row and Upgrade your instance link, and no status badge

Why: after w3/m675 the datastore header has Render's plan badge, View docs and Connect, but it still lacks three of Render's elements and keeps one Render does not have.

Found by w3/m675 t006 (2026-11-26) against Render's bundle `index-D5V4ZgbE.js` and its `page-templates-TTlbtDSy.js` chunk (the shared resource header, found by `badge-link-upgrade-instance-path`). The record is in [postgres-dashboard.md § Header](../../docs/render-artifacts/postgres-dashboard.md#header-w3m675).

- **Eyebrow.** Render opens the header with the resource's icon and its type in small caps: `POSTGRESQL`, `KEY VALUE`. bex's service header has one (`SERVICE_TYPE_LABEL`), and the datastore headers in `dashboard/src/routes/databases.$databaseId.tsx` and `keyvalue.$keyValueId.tsx` do not.
- **Service ID row.** Under the name, Render shows "Service ID" with the `dpg-…` or `red-…` id and a copy button, as bex's service header does (`services.headerServiceId`).
- **Upgrade your instance.** On a Free plan, Render adds a highlight link with a forward arrow after the plan badge, "Upgrade your instance", going where the badge goes (`/plan` for Postgres, `/info#compute` for Key Value). Render tests Free by the plan's name (`freeTierPlans-PE7Ufc9i.js`).
- **Status badge.** Render's datastore header has none: the status is a field of Info's General card. bex shows `DatabaseStatusBadge` and `KeyValueStatusBadge` beside the name, and its Details card shows the status too. Remove it from the header, or record why bex keeps it in ADR018's header row ([managed-postgres.md](../../docs/ADR018/managed-postgres.md)).
- Render's Postgres Connect note links Info's `#networking`. bex's IP rules are on `#access-control`, and `/databases/<id>/info#networking` scrolls nowhere. Alias the hash as `KEY_VALUE_HASH_ALIASES` does for `#compute`.

Done when both headers show the eyebrow and the Service ID row on every page, a Free datastore shows "Upgrade your instance", the status badge is gone or its divergence is signed off, `#networking` lands on Access control, the pending frames reserve the new rows at 1440 and 390, and route tests cover each.
