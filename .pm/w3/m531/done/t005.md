## Objective

Appearance gains Render's **High Contrast Mode** switch (_"Increase the visibility of interactive elements."_). On, the dashboard uses a high-contrast twin of its light or dark theme, and the account stores the twin (`SYSTEM_HIGH_CONTRAST`, `LIGHT_HIGH_CONTRAST`, `DARK_HIGH_CONTRAST`).

## Context

- Tokens: `dashboard/src/style.css` (`:root`, `.dark`, `@custom-variant dark (&:is(.dark *))`).
- `dashboard/src/test/__tests__/theme-contrast.test.ts` computes the stylesheet's colour pairs against WCAG AA (4.5:1 text, 3:1 focus ring).
- Render: the twin is labelled like its base, the theme choice offers only twins while the mode is on, and `currentThemeSupportsHighContrast` gates the switch (README § Render's Appearance, t001's capture).
- Appearance card and skeleton: `src/features/auth/pages/settings-page/appearance-card.tsx`, `appearance-card-skeleton.tsx`.

## Steps

1. Add a high-contrast token set for light and for dark (a class on `<html>` next to `light`/`dark`), raising at least `muted-foreground`, `border`, `input` and `ring`, and applied by the provider and the pre-paint script.
2. Appearance: the switch row with Render's title and description, translated into all eight languages. Turning it on or off writes the current theme's twin or base through `updateUserThemeSetting`. Dashboard Theme keeps reading System, Light, Dark in either state, and picking one there or in the account menu keeps the switch's state.
3. The skeleton gains the switch row at the ready card's size.
4. `theme-contrast.test.ts`: the high-contrast sets pass 7:1 for text and 4.5:1 for borders and the focus ring, for every pair the normal sets check.
