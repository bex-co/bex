## Objective

The tier empties exactly when Render's CDN does: on a successful deploy and on a manual purge; never on a failed deploy, a Restart, a scale change or an instance crash.

## Context

- `w3/m486` t002/t003: the runtime purge generation.
- The operator knows a release went live when `status.activeRevision` advances; a failed rollout keeps the previous revision.

## Steps

1. Advance the generation when a new revision becomes active and when the App's purge stamp changes.
2. envtest: a successful deploy purges, a failed one does not, a Restart does not.
