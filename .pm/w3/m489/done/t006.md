## Objective

When the tier is unavailable, requests are served by the app directly (`DYNAMIC`/`MISS`), with no 5xx.

## Context

- The fail-open mechanism t001 chose; the local mock cluster (`bash scripts/mock-cluster.sh operator`) or `scripts/dev-env.sh 3 up`.

## Steps

1. On the local cluster, run a request loop against a caching service, delete the tier pod, and count non-2xx answers.
2. Record the result in the milestone README.

## Record (2026-11-19)

Done on the local CAPD cluster. The operator was built from this tree (`bash scripts/mock-cluster.sh operator`). A paid web service (`traefik/whoami`, two instances, `origin-controlled-all`) ran in `default`, and the loop ran in-cluster through Traefik over HTTPS, about 7.5 fresh connections a second across 20 keys. Its route moved to `bex-edge-cache-edge-drill:80` once the tier was Ready, and both tier pods found each other through the headless Service. The results are in the milestone README § Fail-open drill. In short, deleting a tier pod (`kubectl delete pod`) cost 0 of 450 answers, and a forced kill (`--grace-period=0 --force`) cost 2 of 451 requests, which got no answer and no 5xx. The drill App and client were removed afterwards. The drill script lives outside the repo (it creates the client, runs the loop, deletes one tier pod a third of the way in, and counts answers by status and `CF-Cache-Status`), and the method above reproduces it.
