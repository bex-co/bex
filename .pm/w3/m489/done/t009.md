## Record (2026-11-19)

Done. `/simplify` ran three lenses over the diff, and these fixes were applied.

- **Reuse:** `edgeCacheTierImage` uses `edgeCacheSidecar` and `templateEqualIgnoringIsolation`, and the test uses `labels.SelectorFromSet`.
- **Quality:**
  - The tier Deployment records the revision under `edgecache.RevisionAnnotation` instead of a second name.
  - The store limit is derived from its parts (1 GiB plus four 128 MiB fills plus 256 MiB of headroom, 1792 MiB; it had no slack before).
  - The tier has its own component constant, and the field is renamed `storedCache`.
  - The annotation map is built without add-then-delete.
  - A still-routed tier is handed `no-cache` when caching turns off.
  - Stale comments were fixed in `main.go`, `edgecache.go` and `server_defaults.go`.
- **Efficiency:**
  - The upstream proxy has its own transport with a 128-connection idle pool. `http.DefaultTransport` keeps two, which over the cluster network would open a connection per request past two at once.
  - Both proxies share a buffer pool.
  - `main` waits for `Shutdown`, so in-flight requests finish. Before this, the process exited as soon as shutdown began.

Skipped:

- exported env-var constants shared by the operator and the binary (a wider change);
- unexporting `HopHeader`/`SetPeers`;
- folding the fill's generation copies;
- opening files outside the lock on HEAD/304;
- an allocation-free owner hash.

All are behavior-neutral and optional. `make test`, `make lint` and `go test -race ./internal/edgecache/ ./cmd/edge-cache/` pass.
