## Objective

Every service caching today is served through the tier after the operator upgrade, with no fleet roll and no dropped request; its sidecar is removed when its template next changes for another reason.

## Context

- The sidecar path in `deployment_projection.go`; the stored-template rule from `w6/m484`.

## Steps

1. Route existing caching services to the tier once it is Ready, leaving their pods alone.
2. Drop the sidecar from new templates; keep it in stored ones until they change.
3. envtest for the upgrade path.

## Record (2026-11-19)

Done. The App's own Services target the app itself on every port path (`servingTargetPort`, `applyClusterIPService`), so an existing sidecar gets no request once the operator upgrades. The route moves to the tier once a tier pod is Ready. A template stored with the sidecar keeps it, with its control volume and any pre-w3/m486 purge stamp, exactly as stored, while nothing else about the template changes (`keptEdgeCache` in `applyDeploymentSpec`). The next change for another reason drops all three. New templates carry no sidecar, so turning caching on rolls nothing. `TestAnUpgradeKeepsAStoredEdgeCacheSidecarUntilTheTemplateChanges` covers both stored shapes. Through the upgrade, a new helper image, a purge, a switch, caching off, a scale-out and all of them at once, the template stays unchanged; a restart and a deploy roll onto the current shape. `TestTurningCachingOnRollsNoInstance` and `TestACachingServicesOwnServicesTargetTheApp` cover the rest.
