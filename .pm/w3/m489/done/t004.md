## Objective

The tier empties exactly when Render's CDN does: on a successful deploy and on a manual purge; never on a failed deploy, a Restart, a scale change or an instance crash.

## Context

- `w3/m486` t002/t003: the runtime purge generation.
- The operator knows a release went live when `status.activeRevision` advances; a failed rollout keeps the previous revision.

## Steps

1. Advance the generation when a new revision becomes active and when the App's purge stamp changes.
2. envtest: a successful deploy purges, a failed one does not, a Restart does not.

## Record (2026-11-19)

Done. The tier Deployment's metadata records `app.bex.co/cache-revision`, the release whose successful deploy last emptied the cache (`edgeCacheRevision`). It moves to `status.activeRevision` once three things hold: that revision is the App's current release, the release is no Restart (`spec.releaseConfig.preserveGroupValues`, bex-api's Restart), and the App's Deployment has no old non-terminating pod. `annotateEdgeCacheTierPods` hands it to each tier pod beside the purge stamp and profile, and the proxy's control file purges on a new value after its first read (`edgecache.RevisionAnnotation`). Envtest shows a deploy still rolling not purging, the served deploy purging, and a scale-out, a purge and a switch not moving it. `TestTheCacheEmptiesOnlyOnASuccessfulDeploy` adds a failed or pending deploy, a Restart, a Rollback, and a template still on the old revision. The `bex-operator-pod-labels` admission policy admits `app.bex.co/cache-revision` beside the two w3/m486 annotations. That policy had admitted neither of those, so w3/m486's patches would have been refused on production; it was fixed first, alone, in `6d428736b`.
