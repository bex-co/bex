# A datastore's Logs loading frame draws a stream status line under the log panel that the ready page draws only on Live tail

Why: the loading frame of a Postgres database's or a Key Value instance's Logs page ends 24 px lower than the page it turns into, so the last row of the frame vanishes when the lines land (the repo's skeleton rule).

- **Seen** by w3/117 on 2026-11-27 against `yarn local-bex` at 1440 and 390, holding the database read to keep the layout's frame up: the heading (y 72 / 64) and the toolbar (y 124 / 116, 36 / 180 px tall) match, and the log panel is 520 px in both states. Under it, `DatastoreLogsSkeleton`'s `LogPanelSkeleton` always draws the 16 px status bar after an 8 px gap (544 px against 520), where `DatastoreLogViewer` draws `StreamStatus` only when the range is Live tail (`datastore-log-viewer.tsx`, `live ? <StreamStatus …/> : null`), and the default range is Last hour.
- **Where:** `dashboard/src/common/components/route-skeletons.tsx` (`DatastoreLogsSkeleton`), `dashboard/src/features/logs/components/log-panel-skeleton.tsx`. The skeleton is shared by `databases.$databaseId.logs.tsx` and `keyvalue.$keyValueId.logs.tsx` and the two layouts' page frames. It renders outside a router in `datastore-log-viewer.test.tsx`, so reading the URL's `range` needs care.
- **Done when** the frame draws the status line only for `range=live`, and the Postgres and Key Value Logs pages' frames end where their ready pages do at 1440 and 390, on Last hour and on Live tail, with a test pinning both.
