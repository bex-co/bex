# Remove the sandbox sub-minute timeout floor after 2026-12-10

Why: since w3/m303 a sandbox timeout is at least 60 s, so OpenSandbox's 60 s floor (`osMinTimeout`, `openSandboxTimeout`) only serves sandboxes created before the change with a 1–59 s timeout, which are gone once their 30-day suspended retention ends.

- **When:** after 2026-12-10 (w3/m303 shipped 2026-11-10; a suspended sandbox is kept 30 days, `suspendedRetention`).
- **Do:** in `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/lifetime.go` drop `osMinTimeout` and the `max(timeout, osMinTimeout)` in `openSandboxTimeout`, and the sub-minute exact-deadline note in `lifetimeExpired`'s callers if it is the only reason; keep the tests that pin the 60 s minimum on create.
- **Check first:** no live sandbox carries `bex.co/timeout-seconds` below 60 (read-only inventory, or the Completer's lifetime logs).
- Source: w3/m303 `/simplify` review, 2026-11-10.
