## Objective

Alertmanager itself proves it is alive to something outside the cluster, every minute.

## Context

- Routing: `deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml:214-226` (`Watchdog` → `"null"`). Watchdog rule: `deploy/gitops/base/rules/alerting_rules.yml:1016`; rule test `rules/silent_failure_alerts_test.yml`.
- Secret mount pattern: `alertmanager-smtp` (`prometheus.yaml:163,192`); Alertmanager `webhook_configs` accepts `url_file`.
- The heartbeat script reads Watchdog as active (`scripts/alerting-heartbeat.sh`); keep that working.

## Steps

1. A receiver `watchdog-deadman` with `webhook_configs: [{url_file: /etc/alertmanager/secrets/watchdog-ping-url, send_resolved: false}]`.
2. Route `alertname="Watchdog"` to it with `group_wait: 0s`, `group_interval: 1m`, `repeat_interval: 1m`.
3. Mount a Secret `alertmanager-watchdog` (key `watchdog-ping-url`) beside `alertmanager-smtp`; document its out-of-band creation; the local overlay keeps Alertmanager disabled.
4. `amtool check-config` on the rendered config; `scripts/gitops-validate.sh`.
5. After deploy, confirm pings arrive every minute.
