# An old anchor to a lower card on a Postgres database's Info page drifts out of view, because the page scrolls to it before the cards above it finish mounting

Why: w3/m641 kept the old Overview's anchors working and scrolls to the card the hash names, but on production a `#danger-zone` link at a 390 phone ends with the card off-screen, and `#sql-console` arrives seconds late, so the link does not show what it names.

- **Seen** on production pin `cdaa0a248615` (2026-11-25, w3/m641 t006) as the QA user in `bex-canary`, on a Basic 256MB Postgres (disposable) with an IP allow list and one credential, in Chrome at 1440×900 and 390×900, sampling the card's top every 250 ms after a full load:
  - `/databases/<id>#danger-zone` at 390 sat at y 800 once the read landed, then at y 1867 (scrollTop 957 of 2024) for the rest of the 20 s.
  - `#sql-console` at 390 sat at y 1404 for the first seconds, then at y 337 at the page's end. At 1440 it went from y 793 to y 441.
  - `/databases/<id>/metrics#insights` stayed at y 516–762, not under the header.
  - `#metrics`, `#plan`, `#apps`, `#recovery` and `#access-control` landed under the header. Locally, against `local-bex`, the access control's reserve equalled its ready height, so the drift did not show.
- **Where:** `useScrollToHashedCard` in `dashboard/src/routes/databases.$databaseId.tsx` calls `scrollIntoView` once, when the database read lands (`scrolledFor`). Above the lower cards, `features/databases/pages/database-info-page.tsx` mounts Access control in a `DeferredMount minHeight={240}`, and its ready card is 592 px at 1440 with an allow list and a credential. The SQL console's slot mounts after it. Chrome's scroll anchoring keeps the content at the top of the viewport in place, not the hashed card, so growth above the card pushes it down. On Metrics the scroll runs while the charts and Insights are still loading, so the page is not yet tall enough to bring Insights up.
- **Done when** a full load of `/databases/<id>#sql-console`, `#danger-zone` and `#access-control`, and of `/databases/<id>/metrics#insights`, on a database with an allow list ends with the card at the top of the scroll area at 1440 and 390, or at the page's end when the page is shorter, and the card stays there. A scroll by the user before the page settles is never overridden, and a test pins the re-scroll after a card above the anchored one grows.
