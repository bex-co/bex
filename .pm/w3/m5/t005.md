## Objective

Prove the milestone's point on the mock cluster: generate distinctive log lines from a running app, kill/restart its pod, and show the pre-restart lines still come back — over REST (`GET /v1/logs`-shaped query) and MCP `list_logs` — plus a bounded time-range query that excludes lines outside the range.

## Steps

1. Deploy the whoami sample; curl it to produce marked lines; note the timestamp window.
2. `kubectl delete pod` → wait for the replacement → query both surfaces for the pre-restart window; assert the marked lines return.
3. Repeat with `BEX_LOKI_URL` unset to confirm the truthful degraded answer (live logs only).
