## Objective

No link that worked before the split, and no Render-shaped link, lands on the wrong page.

## Context

- `?tab=logs` and `?tab=metrics` (with their range and search keys) come from bookmarks and from `features/logs/lib/datastore-log-search.ts`'s `datastoreLogContextHref` Key Value branch (View in context, still `?tab=logs`).
- The Overview's anchors (`#metadata`, `#connection`, `#networking`, `#plan`, `#maxmemory-policy`, `#persistence-mode`, `#danger-zone`) all stay on Info, so they need only to scroll into view there; the Postgres layout's hash scroll (`useScrollToHashedCard` in `routes/databases.$databaseId.tsx`) is the precedent, and w3/120 records how it drifts on a page whose upper cards mount late.
- Render's sub-paths arrive as `/keyvalue/<id>/<rest>` through `common/lib/render-alias.ts`: `/plan` (Render's plan chooser), `/metrics/<period>`, and its Upgrade instance link `/info#compute`. bex's plan picker is the Info page's plan card, so `/plan` and `#compute` land there, as w3/m641 sent Postgres's `/upgrade` to Plan (`features/databases/lib/database-pages.ts`, `databaseSubpathLanding`).
- Other links in: `global-search-dialog.tsx`, `features/projects/components/resource-table.tsx`, `features/blueprints/lib/resource-href.ts`, `features/keyvalue/pages/new-key-value-page.tsx` (after create).

## Steps

1. The index route redirects `?tab=logs` to `/logs` and `?tab=metrics` to `/metrics`, dropping `tab` and keeping the other keys and the hash.
2. The splat lands `/plan` on Info at the plan card, `/metrics/<period>` on `/metrics`, and anything else on Info, query and hash kept.
3. `#compute` on Info scrolls to the plan card (an alias for `#plan`).
4. `datastoreLogContextHref` builds `/keyvalue/<id>/logs` for Key Value, and its comment says so; check every other link in still means Info.
