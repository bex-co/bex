## Objective

Split the single Key Value page into Render's pages, each at its own URL, with the cards t001's § Page map assigns to it.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/routes/keyvalue.$keyValueId.tsx` today owns the header, the `DetailTabNav`, the Overview's seven sections behind `KeyValueDetailNavigation` and the Logs and Metrics panels (`DatastoreLogViewer`, `KeyValueMetrics` over `DatastoreMetricsPanel`).
- The w3/m641 tree is the precedent: `databases.$databaseId.tsx` as the layout (header, workspace adoption, not-found redirect, loader error retry, the title loader), and `databases.$databaseId.{index,info,logs,metrics}.tsx` as pages, with `features/databases/pages/*` and `database-page-head.ts` naming each page's document title ("Logs ・ <name> ・ Database ・ bex Dashboard").
- The Logs range and the Metrics range share the w6/065 URL keys (`datastore-log-search.ts`, `metricsRangeSearch`); each page keeps its own keys, and leaving a page drops them, as the tabs do today.

## Steps

1. Turn `keyvalue.$keyValueId.tsx` into the layout: the header with the status badge, the workspace adoption, the not-found redirect, the load error and an `<Outlet />`. Drop the `DetailTabNav`.
2. Add `keyvalue.$keyValueId.index.tsx` and `.info.tsx` (Info: the cards in t001's order, no section rail), `.logs.tsx` (the log viewer with its URL-backed search) and `.metrics.tsx` (the metrics panel with its URL-backed range).
