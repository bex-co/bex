## Objective

Give static-site finalization a stated operational bound and ensure cleanup failure produces the tenant/operator outcome selected in t001 instead of an indefinitely terminating App.

## Context

- Normal App controls finalized in 1–15 seconds, while the observed static site remained for 2+ hours.
- The distinguishing step is static-site object-prefix cleanup before `app.bex.co/finalizer` removal.
- The design must preserve honest cleanup semantics: removing a finalizer must not silently orphan data unless that trade-off is explicit and observable.

## Steps

1. Trace the cleanup Job lifecycle, retry behavior, deadlines, and finalizer-removal conditions.
2. Add the timeout/retry/terminal mechanism selected in t001.
3. Project an actionable operator and tenant signal when cleanup exceeds the bound.
4. Keep successful cleanup idempotent and safe across reconciler restarts.

## Files

- `lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/apps/service.go`
- `docs/ADR029-static-sites.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Static-site finalization has a configured or documented finite bound enforced by code.
- [ ] Cleanup timeout/failure yields the t001 terminal outcome and an actionable operator signal.
- [ ] Successful cleanup remains idempotent and removes the finalizer.
- [ ] Failure tests do not rely on real-time multi-minute sleeps.

## Out of scope

- Generalizing this into a workflow engine for every Kubernetes finalizer.
