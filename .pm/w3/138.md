# A datastore's Connect panel keeps the 16 px gutter on a phone instead of touching the screen's left edge

Why: at 390 px the open Connect panel of a Postgres or a Key Value sits flush against the screen's left edge, where every dropdown menu in the dashboard stops 16 px short of it.

Found by the w3/m675 t005 production walk (2026-11-27, pin `486e3f4afbf6`, `bex-canary` with a Postgres and a Key Value created for it (disposable)): at 390×844 the panel's box is x 0, width 352. `DatastoreConnectMenu` in `dashboard/src/common/components/datastore-connect-menu.tsx` opens `PopoverContent` with `align="end"` and `w-[22rem] max-w-[calc(100vw-2rem)]`, and `ui/popover.tsx` passes no `collisionPadding`, so Radix shifts the end-aligned panel only as far as the viewport edge. `ui/dropdown-menu.tsx` defaults `collisionPadding = 16` for exactly this ("Keeps a menu inside the page's 16px gutter instead of flush against the screen's edge"), and `log-filter-bar.tsx` sets it on its popover by hand.

- Fix: give `ui/popover.tsx`'s `PopoverContent` the same `collisionPadding = 16` default, or set it on the Connect panel alone. Check the service header's Connect (`service-detail-header.tsx`, `w-80`) and the other `PopoverContent` users at 390 with it.
- Done when: at 390 and 1440 the open Connect panel of a Postgres, a Key Value and a service has at least 16 px between it and each side of the screen, and no other popover moves at 1440.
