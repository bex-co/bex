# A client navigation empties the tab's title for up to two thirds of a second before the next page's title lands

Why: every sidebar click, breadcrumb and button that moves between dashboard pages leaves `document.title` empty for 250–690 ms, so the browser tab reads the bare URL until the destination's title commits. The router's own pending title ("Loading ・ bex Dashboard", `RoutePending`) never shows in that window.

Filed by w3/m706 t007 on 2026-11-28, from the production re-check on pin `45b1e8ec4f09` as the QA user in `bex-canary` (disposable resources), headless Chromium at 1440 and 390, tracing `document.title` every animation frame. Every m706 state button (Return home, Go to Webhooks, Go to project) showed the gap, and so did two live-to-live client navigations, so m706 does not cause it:

- `/webhooks` → `/` at 1440: "Webhooks ・ bex Dashboard" until 685 ms, then `""` until 1146 ms, then "Overview ・ bex Dashboard".
- `/services/<live srv>/deploys` → `/webhooks` at 1440: the service's title until 609 ms, then `""` until 887 ms, then "Webhooks ・ bex Dashboard".
- The gap opens 240–720 ms after the navigation starts, well past `defaultPendingMs: 150` (`dashboard/src/router.tsx`). The fresh-load SSR title of each destination is right, so only client navigations are affected.

- **Where to look (unconfirmed):** `HeadContent` (`dashboard/src/common/root-route/shell-component.tsx`) may drop the outgoing match's `<title>` before the incoming one renders it. `DashboardDocumentTitle`'s unmount reconcile (`dashboard/src/common/lib/document-head/document-title.tsx`) resets only to "bex Dashboard", so it is not what writes the empty string. Trace which commit leaves `document.head` with no `<title>`, or with an empty one, before you pick the fix.
- **Done when:** a client navigation between two live pages at 1440 and 390 never reads `document.title === ""`. The tab keeps the outgoing title, or reads "Loading ・ bex Dashboard", until the destination's title commits. A test pins the router-level behavior.
