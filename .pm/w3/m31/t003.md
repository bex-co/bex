## Objective

Give the operator and bex-api service accounts the authority to manage workloads across every tenant namespace, replacing the single `bex-operator-apps` RoleBinding in `default`.

## Context

- Today: `deploy/gitops/base/` binds the operator SA in one namespace (`bex-operator-apps` RoleBinding).
- Per-namespace RoleBindings would balloon with workspace count; a cluster-scoped ClusterRole (+ binding) scoped to the tenant-resource kinds is the clean shape.

## Steps

1. Define a ClusterRole for the operator + bex-api over App/Database/KeyValue + pod/service/secret/etc. verbs, scoped to tenant namespaces by label.
2. Bind it; remove the single-namespace RoleBinding.

## Files

- `deploy/gitops/base/` (RBAC manifests), `lego/operator/config/rbac/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Operator and bex-api can reconcile tenant workloads in any `<ws>` namespace.
- [ ] No namespace-scoped per-workspace RoleBinding churn.

## Out of scope

- Per-tenant quota (t004), NetworkPolicy (t005).
