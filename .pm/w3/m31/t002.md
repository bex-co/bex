## Objective

Move the operator and bex-api off the single `BEX_APPS_NAMESPACE` watch so they reconcile App/Database/KeyValue into each workspace's `<ws>` namespace.

## Context

- Operator env: `lego/operator/config/manager/manager.yaml`; `BEX_APPS_NAMESPACE` (default `default`) scopes the Secret cache.
- bex-api: `BEX_API_NAMESPACE` / `BEX_CP_APPS_NAMESPACE` scope its watch.
- ADR043 D4: activator/operator/bex-api manage wake across all `<ws>` namespaces (cluster-scoped or per-namespace).

## Steps

1. Switch the operator's namespace-scoped watchers to cluster-wide (or enumerate tenant namespaces), keeping the cluster-wide watch for non-tenant kinds.
2. Reconcile each App/Database/KeyValue into its workspace's `<ws>` (read workspace→namespace from the CR / projector).
3. bex-api: same scope change for its tenant-resource reads.

## Files

- `lego/operator/cmd/manager/main.go`, `lego/operator/internal/controller/`
- `lego/backend/cmd/api/main.go`, `lego/backend/internal/api/server.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A new App lands in its workspace's `<ws>`, not `default`.
- [ ] Operator/bex-api watch all tenant namespaces; non-tenant kinds stay cluster-wide.

## Out of scope

- RBAC authority for the wider watch (t003), NetworkPolicy in `<ws>` (t005), migrating existing `default` workloads (t006).
