## Objective

Move the operator, bex-api, and the egress-meter off their single-tenant-namespace assumptions so App/Database/KeyValue reconcile into each workspace's `<ws>` namespace.

## Context

- The operator's CR watches are **already cluster-wide** — only the Secret informer is namespace-scoped to `BEX_APPS_NAMESPACE` (default `default`), see `lego/operator/internal/controller/secret_cache.go`. The operator-side work is multi-namespace Secret caching + placing reconciled workloads in `<ws>`, not "switching watchers".
- bex-api: `BEX_API_NAMESPACE` / `BEX_CP_APPS_NAMESPACE` scope its watch and the projector's target namespace.
- The egress-meter (`lego/operator/cmd/egress-meter/`) watches one App namespace via `BEX_APPS_NAMESPACE` — usage/billing metering must follow the tenants or egress goes unmetered.
- ADR043 D4: activator/operator/bex-api manage wake across all `<ws>` namespaces (cluster-scoped or per-namespace).

## Steps

1. Operator: extend the Secret cache from one namespace to the tenant namespaces (cluster-scoped with a label selector, or dynamic per-`<ws>` informers); CR watches stay cluster-wide as today.
2. Reconcile each App/Database/KeyValue into its workspace's `<ws>` (read workspace→namespace from the CR / projector).
3. bex-api: same scope change for its tenant-resource reads + the projector's target namespace.
4. egress-meter: watch Apps across tenant namespaces (label-selected), not one `BEX_APPS_NAMESPACE`.

## Files

- `lego/operator/cmd/manager/main.go`, `lego/operator/internal/controller/` (incl. `secret_cache.go`), `lego/operator/cmd/egress-meter/`
- `lego/backend/cmd/api/main.go`, `lego/backend/internal/api/server.go`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A new App lands in its workspace's `<ws>`, not `default`.
- [ ] Operator/bex-api/egress-meter cover all tenant namespaces; non-tenant kinds stay cluster-wide.
- [ ] (Secret-dependent paths — Database/KeyValue creds, clone secrets — fully verifiable only once t003 grants Secret access in `<ws>`; assert them again there.)

## Out of scope

- RBAC authority for the wider watch (t003), NetworkPolicy in `<ws>` (t005), migrating existing `default` workloads (t006).
