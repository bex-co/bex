## Objective

Set Loki's ingestion limits from t001's measurement instead of the defaults, and alert before a limit drops lines silently.

## Context

- `deploy/gitops/base/loki.yaml` `limits_config`; Alloy's dropped-entries counters; the platform alert rules from w3/m6.

## Steps

1. Set `ingestion_rate_mb`, `ingestion_burst_size_mb` and the per-stream limits with headroom over the measured peak plus the cap's worst case.
2. Add alerts for Loki 429s and for sustained `ratelimited` drops per service.
