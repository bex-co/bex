# Render's settings and resource anchors land on their sections in bex

Why: Render's dashboard builds links to sections of a page (`/w/<tea-id>/settings#team-members`, `/d/<dpg-id>#delete-database`), and bex either drops the anchor on the way or has no section under Render's name, so the link opens the top of the page.

Filed by w3/m704 t002 on 2026-11-27, from the URL inventory in [dashboard-routes.md § Render's URL inventory](../../docs/render-artifacts/dashboard-routes.md#renders-url-inventory). w3/m707 owns `#account-security`, `#free-usage` and `#unbilled-charges`; this note owns the rest.

- **Render:** the `urls-*.js` chunk builds `/w/${id}/settings` with `#build-pipeline`, `#team-members`, `#invite-team-members`, `#audit-logs`, `#deploy-policy`, `#security` and `#registry-credentials`; `/u/${id}/settings#build-pipeline`; `/d/${id}#delete-database`; and `/project/${id}/environment/${env}/settings#networking`.
- **bex:** `dashboard/src/routes/w.$.tsx` keeps the hash only for `/w/settings…`; for `/w/<tea-id>/settings…` it switches the workspace and calls `navigate({to: "/workspace/settings"})` with no hash, so every anchor is lost. Workspace settings' sections are `#team`, `#overlapping-deploy-policy`, `#two-factor` and `#google-login` (`features/workspaces/components/workspace-settings-navigation.tsx`), so `#team-members`, `#invite-team-members`, `#deploy-policy` and `#security` have no target. A Postgres's Info has no `delete-database` anchor, the environment settings page (w1/m683) none named `networking`, and account settings no build pipeline (bex's is a workspace setting, `/workspace/settings#build-pipeline`).
