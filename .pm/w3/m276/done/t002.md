## Objective

Once a scan has run, `openPorts` reports what listens, not what the image merely declares: a declared port with no listener drops from `openPorts` (it stays routable while declared), matching Render's "open ports".

## Context

- `lego/types/v1alpha1/image_network.go` `App.OpenPorts()`
- `status.runtimePorts` (w3/m273)

## Steps

1. Intersect declared ports with the last scan when one exists.
2. Keep the pre-scan behavior (declared ports) so a fresh service is not empty.

## Files

- lego/types/v1alpha1/image_network.go
- lego/types/v1alpha1/image_network_test.go

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A declared-but-idle port is absent from openPorts after a scan
- [ ] Before any scan, declared ports are listed as today

## Result (2026-11-05)

`App.OpenPorts` leaves out an image-declared port nothing listens on once a scan of that revision exists; the primary always stays; before any scan, declared ports as before; Services still route every declared port; a timed-out first scan drops nothing. Tests: `TestOpenPortsListOnlyScannedListeners`, `TestScanPrivatePorts` timed-out cases, envtest with a ClickHouse-style image.
