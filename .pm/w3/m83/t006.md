## Objective

Give the three bex-api subsystems that already export health series but have no rule a falsifiable alert plus a panel: outbound webhooks (all endpoints failing = our egress, not theirs), push delivery (queue non-empty but nothing succeeded), and agent-session provisioning (failure ratio or p95 breach).

## Context

- Series (verify exact names on `/metrics`): `bex_webhooks_delivery_attempts_total{result}` (`webhooks/metrics.go`), the push gauges under the `bex` namespace / `push` subsystem — `last_success` timestamp + `queue` depth (`notifications/push_metrics.go`), `bex_agent_session_provision_seconds` histogram `{outcome}` (`agentsessions/completion_metrics.go`, w5/m81 SLO baseline in ADR047).
- The m86 lesson (`2761b94b2`): Prometheus scrape **keep-lists** silently dropped alert-backing series — check `deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml`'s bex-api keep-list includes all three families before writing rules; a rule on a never-scraped series is a fourth death layer.
- Falsifiable green: guard ratios with a traffic floor (`sum(rate(...)) > 0`), no `vector(0)` coercions; staleness = `time() - bex_push_last_success_timestamp_seconds > 2h and bex_push_queue_depth > 0`.
- Panels: api-gateways / data-plane dashboards in `deploy/gitops/base/values/grafana.values.yaml`; `scripts/obs-coverage-check.sh` must stay green with the waiver list untouched; `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` + `helm template` render.

## Steps
