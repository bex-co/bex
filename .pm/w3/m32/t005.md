## Objective

Give each workspace's `<ws>-sandbox` namespace (created by m31/t001) its isolation boundary: Cilium egress-deny/metadata-deny mirroring `build-boundary.yaml`, Kata runtime, scheduling to the sandbox pool (ADR043 D2/D3; ADR042 D3).

## Context

- `deploy/gitops/base/build-boundary.yaml` is the existing untrusted-execution analog (PSS + `hostUsers=false` + default-deny + scoped egress + Cilium egressDeny).
- `<ws>-sandbox` namespaces come from m31/t001; OpenSandbox routes sandbox pods into them (t006).

## Steps

1. Apply the build-boundary-shaped isolation to each `<ws>-sandbox` namespace (default-deny ingress/egress + DNS + scoped external + Cilium egressDeny to host/metadata).
2. BatchSandbox template: `runtimeClassName: kata`, `nodeSelector: bex.co/pool: sandbox`, `hostUsers: false`.

## Files

- `deploy/gitops/base/` (per-namespace boundary templates), OpenSandbox batchsandbox template

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A sandbox pod in `<ws>-sandbox>` cannot reach the internet/metadata/other tenants/its own `<ws>` hosting by default.
- [ ] Sandbox pod runs under Kata on the sandbox pool.

## Out of scope

- The bex-api↔OpenSandbox auth link (t006), workspace namespace creation (m31/t001).
