# A service's or cron job's compute change first reviews Render's line items and total

Why: Render opens the same "Apply changes?" review for a service's and a cron job's compute change as for a datastore's. bex still asks "Change to {name}?" with the new plan's price alone, so a service that runs extra instances is shown a fraction of what the change costs.

- **Render** (bundle `index-D5V4ZgbE.js`, read 2026-11-26 for w3/m674, [postgres-dashboard.md § Plan review](../../docs/render-artifacts/postgres-dashboard.md#plan-review-w3m674)): a service's Compute section Save opens the review with the lead "Review your compute selection.", **Compute plan** (the plan's CPU and RAM, Edit "Edit compute plan") and **Additional instances** when the service runs more than one instance without autoscaling ("1 instance", "2 instances", priced at count × plan price), the Total (plan price × every instance) and **Update compute**. A cron job's review has the Compute plan line alone, priced per minute, and Update compute. Neither shows a warning.
- **bex**: `dashboard/src/features/services/components/instance-type-picker.tsx` confirms through `ConfirmDialog` (`services.planPickerConfirm*`), used by `compute-plan-section.tsx` and `cron-plan-page.tsx`. w3/m674's `ReviewChangesDialog` (`dashboard/src/common/components/review-changes-dialog.tsx`) and `useComputePlanFigures` draw Render's review.
- **Done when**: a service with extra instances changing plan opens the review with both lines and a Total equal to plan price × instances, an autoscaled or Free service shows the Compute plan line alone, a cron job's review prices per minute, Edit returns to the picker with the choice kept, a refused change shows its error in the review, the replaced `services.planPickerConfirm*` keys are gone from all eight locales, and component tests pin each case.
