## Objective

Give GraphQL what Render's page reads: `Database.lastUpgradeRun` with `acknowledged`, `runId`, `startedAt`, `state` and `toVersion`; a mutation that acknowledges a finished run; and the installed extensions with whether each blocks an upgrade.

## Context

- t001 chooses where the attempt is recorded. If it is the `Database` CR's status, the type change is in `lego/types/v1alpha1/database_types.go`, the operator writes it when it starts and ends an upgrade, and the backend reads it.
- `lego/backend/internal/postgres/graphql.go` builds `Database` and holds `updateDatabaseVersion`.
- Keep Render's names where bex has the same meaning (`lastUpgradeRun`, `acknowledgeDatabaseUpgrade`, `blocksUpgradeDatabase`).

## Steps

1. Record each attempt's start, target, state (running, succeeded, failed) and end, and keep the last one.
2. Add the read, the acknowledge mutation (members who may upgrade only) and the extension list.
3. Regenerate the dashboard's GraphQL types.

## Files

- `lego/types/v1alpha1/database_types.go` and the operator's database controller (if t001 puts the record there), `make` codegen in `lego/operator/`
- `lego/backend/internal/postgres/graphql.go`, `dashboard/src/features/databases/graphql/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tests cover a running, a succeeded and a failed attempt, acknowledging one, a member who may not acknowledge, and a blocking extension.
