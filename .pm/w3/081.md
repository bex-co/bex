# Log explorer: Render's row menu, the "New logs in Live Tail" toast and "Return to Bottom", and Options' Theme Settings link

Why: w3/m509 gave every bex log explorer Render's gutter, timestamp hover, Options, Maximize and shortcuts; these are the pieces of Render's explorer the capture shows that it left unbuilt.

Found by w3/m509's parity check (2026-11-19) against [project-observability.md](../../docs/render-artifacts/project-observability.md) § The view page and the [ADR018](../../docs/ADR018-render-parity.md) Logs row "Log explorer display and shortcuts":

- **Row menu (…).** Hovering a row on Render highlights it and shows a row menu; bex highlights the row only. The capture does not list the menu's items: read them in a Render workspace first (read-only), then add them to `LogRow` in `dashboard/src/features/logs/components/log-line-list.tsx`.
- **Live tail.** Render shows a "New logs in Live Tail" toast while the reader is scrolled away, and its button reads "Return to Bottom"; bex's reads "Jump to latest" (`logs.jumpToLatest`) and has no toast.
- **Theme Settings.** Render's Options menu ends with a link to Theme Settings; bex's (`LogOptionsMenu`, `dashboard/src/features/logs/components/log-explorer.tsx`) has none, and bex's theme lives in the account menu.

Done when each explorer's rows carry Render's row menu items, a scrolled-away live tail shows the toast and Render's button wording, and Options links to wherever bex keeps the theme, each with a test; or ADR018 records why one stays different.
