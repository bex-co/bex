## Objective

Split the stacked page into a layout route (header and outlet) and one child route per Render page, moving each card as t001 mapped it, unchanged.

## Context

- `routes/blueprints.$blueprintId.tsx` holds the page, its sync dialog, its confirmation dialogs and `PlannedSyncPanel`.
- The `databases.$databaseId*.tsx` layout and child routes (w3/m641) and `features/databases/lib/database-pages.ts` are the pattern, including the sub-path landing for an unknown sub-path.
- Render's `*` child sends any other sub-path to Resources.

## Steps

1. Make `blueprints.$blueprintId.tsx` the layout (loader, header, not-found and load-error handling, adopt-workspace guard) with an `<Outlet/>`.
2. Add `blueprints.$blueprintId.index.tsx` and `.resources.tsx` (Resources), `.syncs.tsx` (the sync history and, until w3/m678, `PlannedSyncPanel`) and `.settings.tsx` (the metadata card's fields, then the bex extras where t001 placed them).
3. Give each page its heading and a document title, following the convention w3/m641 used (the page named first), and send an unknown sub-path to Resources.
4. Regenerate `routeTree.gen.ts` and add each route to `common/lib/route-skeleton-manifest.ts`.
