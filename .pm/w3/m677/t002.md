## Objective

On every `/blueprints/<id>/…` page, replace the workspace sidebar with a Blueprint sidebar shaped like Render's, the way a database's and a Key Value instance's pages do.

## Context

- `common/components/dashboard-layout/dashboard-sidebar.tsx` picks a resource sidebar by route param; `database-sidebar.tsx`, w3/m649's `key-value-sidebar.tsx` and its shared resource-page nav helpers are the pattern.
- Render: three entries with no group label, Resources active on the bare URL too, Syncs active on a sync's page; no entry depends on a permission.
- The Blueprint read is `useBlueprint` (`features/blueprints/hooks/use-blueprint.ts`); read it with the page's own document and variables so Apollo serves the sidebar from cache.

## Steps

1. Add `features/blueprints/components/blueprint-nav.tsx` (groups, the active-entry rule including `/sync/<id>`, label keys, a typed page route) with labels in all eight locales.
2. Add `common/components/dashboard-layout/blueprint-sidebar.tsx` and pick it in `DashboardSidebar` on a `blueprintId` param; a Blueprint the caller cannot see gets no nav, and the entries draw while the read loads so nothing shifts.
3. Keep `/blueprint/new` and `/blueprints/new` on the workspace sidebar.

## Files
