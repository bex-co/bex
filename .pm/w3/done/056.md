# Scale and Enterprise workspaces are capped at 25 webhook endpoints; Render allows 100

Why: a paying Scale customer is refused at their 26th webhook with `WEBHOOK_ENDPOINT_LIMIT`, although Render and bex's own plan gate allow up to 100.

- `core.WebhookDestinationLimit` gives Hobby 0, Pro 1, and Scale and above 100 (`lego/backend/internal/core/plan_gate.go:118-128`; applied at `internal/webhooks/service.go:547-565`). Render: one destination on Pro, up to 100 on Scale and Enterprise (`docs/ADR030-pricing.md:131`).
- The store's abuse cap `MaxWebhookEndpointsPerWorkspace = 25` (`internal/store/webhooks.go:135`, enforced at `:194`) fires first, so the plan limit above 25 is unreachable.
- ADR018 row 300 still lists the fixed 25-endpoint cap as a divergence.

Fix: raise the store cap to 100, matching the top plan limit. It stays as the abuse cap, and its comment is updated. Add a test that a Scale workspace creates its 26th through 100th endpoint and is refused at the 101st, then update ADR018 row 300. About 45 minutes.

Done 2026-11-08 (loopx w3): `MaxWebhookEndpointsPerWorkspace` is 100, equal to the top plan's destination limit, so the plan gate is the refusal a customer meets. `TestWebhookCreateFollowsThePlan` now creates 100 on Scale and Enterprise and expects `WEBHOOK_PLAN_LIMIT` at the 101st; `TestEndpointCapAdmitsTheTopPlanLimit` keeps the cap from undercutting the plan; `TestPGWebhookEndpointCapAdmitsOneHundred` (real Postgres) commits the 100th and refuses the 101st with `ErrWebhookEndpointLimit`. ADR018 row 300 and `webhooks-api.md` no longer list the 25 cap as a divergence.
