## Objective

No link that worked before the split, and no Render-shaped link, lands on the wrong page.

## Context

- `?tab=logs` (with its range and search keys) is linked from `features/logs/lib/datastore-log-search.ts` (a project log view's View in context) and from bookmarks.
- Overview anchors: `#metadata`, `#connection`, `#sql-console`, `#high-availability`, `#metrics`, `#plan`, `#apps`, `#insights`, `#recovery`, `#access-control`, `#danger-zone`. `database-expiry.tsx` links `#plan`.
- Render's sub-pages: `/upgrade`, `/high-availability-upgrade`, `/version-upgrade` arrive as `/databases/<id>/<rest>` through `common/lib/render-alias.ts`.
- Other links in: `global-search-dialog.tsx`, `features/blueprints/lib/resource-href.ts`, `recovery-panel.tsx` (a restore's landing), `new-database-page.tsx` (after create).

## Steps

1. Redirect `?tab=logs` to `/logs`, keeping every other key.
2. On the bare URL, redirect a hash that names a card moved off Info to that card's page, keeping the hash (a hash only exists in the browser, so do it on the client, without a flash of Info).
3. Route Render's three sub-paths to the page holding that control (from t001's record).
4. Point each in-app link at the page it means, and the Key Value side of `datastore-log-search.ts` at its unchanged `?tab=logs`.
