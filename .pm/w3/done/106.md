# A service's Logs, Metrics and Shell pages and a deploy's page have no service header, as Render's do

Why: Render's resource page hides its header on Logs, Metrics and Shell and on a deploy's or a build's detail, which gives those tools the page's full height; bex draws the service header, with its actions and facts, above every tab, so a log explorer on a phone starts a third of the way down.

- **Render** (public dashboard bundle read 2026-11-22 without sign-in, w3/m555 t001): `CronJob-*.js` `_a` is true on a path containing `/logs` (not under `/builds/` or `/deploys/`), `/metrics` or `/shell`, and the page passes `hideResourceHeader: _a() || buildDetail || deployDetail`; `index-*.js` passes `hideResourceHeader` the same way for the Server, Postgres and Key Value pages. Not yet walked signed in.
- **bex:** `dashboard/src/features/services/components/service-detail-layout.tsx` renders `ServiceDetailHeader` (or its skeleton) above every tab of `/services/<id>` and `/static/<id>`.
- **Done when** a service's Logs, Metrics and Shell tabs and a deploy's detail show no service header, on every type, after a signed-in check on Render confirms the bundle; the sidebar and the breadcrumb still name the service, each changed page's pending frame matches its ready one at 1440 and 390, and ADR018 says so.
