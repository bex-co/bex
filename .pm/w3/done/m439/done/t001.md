## Objective

The read side knows what extended `bex` commands mean and who the user is (ADR113 D7 "What the read side learns", D9).

## Context

- `scripts/cli-analytics.sql` defines `cli_analytics.events`; new columns must be appended (`CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW` can only add columns at the end, as its comment explains). The reader `bex_cli_analytics` may read only this view.
- `product_analytics_audiences` (migration 0114) classifies workspaces `customer|internal|qa|canary`; `docs/runbooks/product-analytics.md` § Audience classification.
- `duration_mode` today matches `(^| )(logs|ssh|shell|psql)( |$)` as session/stream.
- `bex agent` exit codes: `lego/cli/internal/agent/agent.go:58-69` (2 usage, 3 failed turn, 4 canceled, 5 needs input, 6 no session, 7/8 nothing to stop, 124 timeout, 130 interrupted).

## Steps

1. Append `exit_code`; `cli_fault` (true for `discovery_error`, `validation_error`, `setup_error`, `execution_error`; false for `success`, `explicit_exit`; null otherwise); `command_family` (closed set: `hosting`, `data`, `agent`, `automation`, `coding-agent`, `desktop`, `sandbox`, `account`, `other`, derived from the command path after the binary name); `started_at`; `audience` (LEFT JOIN `product_analytics_audiences` on the server-attributed `workspace_id`, default `unclassified`).
2. Widen `duration_mode`'s session/stream match to `agent chat|run|wait|send|logs|handoff`. The column keeps its name and type.
3. Grants stay as they are: the view owner's rights cover the join, and the reader still cannot read `product_analytics_audiences` directly.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The SQL applies on top of the current view generation (`CREATE OR REPLACE` succeeds against a database holding the old view).
- [x] The reader can select the new columns and is still refused on `product_analytics_audiences` and `cli_telemetry_events`.

## Outcome (2026-11-16)

`scripts/cli-analytics.sql` appends `exit_code`, `cli_fault`, `command_family`, `started_at` and `audience` after `bex_version`, joins `product_analytics_audiences` on the trimmed workspace (`unclassified` by default) and widens `session / stream` to `agent chat|run|wait|send|logs|handoff`. Production's command paths (2026-11-16) settled the mapping details: aliases arrive as `postgres`/`keyvalues`, `pgcli` and `kv-cli` are `data`, the early-access `ea sandboxes`/`ea sandbox-groups` are `sandbox`, `ea blueprints` is `hosting`, and `desktop` is `edit` plus `agent open`. HEAD's view generation replaced by the new SQL on a disposable Postgres succeeded; the reader selects the new columns and is refused on `product_analytics_audiences` and `cli_telemetry_events`. The join makes the view non-updatable, so a reader `DELETE` is now refused before its permission check.
