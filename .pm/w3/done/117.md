# A Postgres database's Logs and Metrics pages have no resource header, as Render's do

Why: Render's Postgres layout passes `hideResourceHeader` on its Logs and Metrics pages, giving the log explorer and the charts the page's full height; bex draws the database header (name, status, actions) above every one of its pages since w3/m641.

- **Render** (public dashboard bundle `index-BPMVVQol.js`, sha256 `4eb3557f6970…`, read 2026-11-25 without sign-in): the Postgres layout (`I_e`) renders the shared resource shell with `hideResourceHeader: i`, true on the full-screen pages; its Logs page (`wve`) asks for the full-screen layout. Not walked signed in; w3/106 holds the same check for a service's pages.
- **bex:** `dashboard/src/routes/databases.$databaseId.tsx` draws the header above the `<Outlet/>` on every page.
- **Done when**, after a signed-in check on Render confirms the bundle (w3/106's), the Logs and Metrics pages show no database header while the sidebar and the breadcrumb still name the database, their pending frames match their ready pages at 1440 and 390, and `docs/render-artifacts/postgres-dashboard.md` § Still apart drops the line.
