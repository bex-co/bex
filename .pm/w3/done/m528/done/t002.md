## Objective

The button an explorer shows while the reader is away from the newest line becomes Render's `LogViewerReturnButton`. It is a small icon-only button at the panel's right edge, named "Return to Bottom" in ascending order and "Return to Top" in descending order.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/logs/components/log-line-list.tsx`: the `!pinned` button (about lines 677–701) is centred (`left-1/2 -translate-x-1/2`), shows an arrow and the text `t("logs.jumpToLatest")`, and sits at `bottom-3`, or `top-3` when descending.
- `dashboard/src/features/logs/locales/*.ts`: `logs.jumpToLatest` in eight languages. `docs/ADR010-observability.md:155` names "Jump to latest" in the w4/m228 reflow paragraph.
- Tests that find the button by name: `components/__tests__/log-line-list.test.tsx`, `log-line-list-explorer.test.tsx` and `log-line-list-display-reflow.test.tsx`.
- Render: `right-6`, `bottom-4` ascending; `aria-label` "Return to Bottom" / "Return to Top"; `MoveDown` / `MoveUp` icons.

## Steps

1. Render the button icon-only (`size="icon-sm"`) at the panel's right edge: the bottom when ascending, the top when descending. Its `aria-label` and `title` come from two new keys, `logs.returnToBottom` and `logs.returnToTop`. Keep the w4/m228 behaviour: clicking it still ends a reflow's settling.
2. Replace `logs.jumpToLatest` in all eight locales with the two keys, translated in each language's voice (`dashboard/AGENTS.md` § Internationalization).
3. Update ADR010:155's wording.
4. Update the tests to find the button by its new names. Add an assertion that a descending list names it "Return to Top".
