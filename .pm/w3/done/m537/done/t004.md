## Objective

Give a datastore Logs tab the same single control: Live tail in its range picker, no switch, and still a one-shot read when it opens.

## Context

- `DatastoreLogViewer` (`dashboard/src/features/logs/components/datastore-log-viewer.tsx`) keeps `live` in local state (off on open) and `pausedAt`, the moment Live was turned off, which its New logs in Live Tail watch starts from (w3/m528). It renders its own `LiveToggle` (`datastore-logs-live-toggle`) and the `logs.liveWindowClosed` note.
- Its range comes from the route (`routes/databases.$databaseId.tsx`, `routes/keyvalue.$keyValueId.tsx`) through `datastoreLogSearchProps`, or local state when it is not hosted; the default is `DEFAULT_DATASTORE_LOG_RANGE` (`features/logs/lib/datastore-log-range.ts`).
- `datastoreLogContextHref` builds the tab's View in context link.
- The pending state: `DatastoreLogsSkeleton` in `dashboard/src/common/components/route-skeletons.tsx`.

## Steps

1. Derive `live` from the range and drop the local switch state. Leaving Live tail for a preset sets the moment the paused watch starts from, as turning the switch off did.
2. Remove the tab's `LiveToggle` and pass the Live tail entry to its `RangeSelect`.
3. The tab still opens on `DEFAULT_DATASTORE_LOG_RANGE` with no tail, and a `range=live` link (t002's spelling) opens it tailing.
4. Make `DatastoreLogsSkeleton`'s toolbar match the ready toolbar at 1440 and 390.
5. Update `datastore-log-viewer.test.tsx` and `datastore-log-viewer-window.test.tsx` to drive the picker.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Postgres and a Key Value Logs tab open with no tail on their one-shot range; Live tail in the picker starts the tail; a preset stops it and New logs in Live Tail returns to Live tail.
- [ ] No `LiveToggle` in the tab or its skeleton, and the pending and ready toolbars measure the same at 1440 and 390.

## Out of scope

- Changing the datastore tab's default range.

**Outcome (2026-11-20):** the datastore tab derives `live` from its range (`range=live` tails, the one-shot Last hour opens), its `LiveToggle`, `pausedAt` and window-closed note are gone (leaving Live tail resamples the window, so the watch starts at that moment), and `DatastoreLogsSkeleton` holds no switch slot.
