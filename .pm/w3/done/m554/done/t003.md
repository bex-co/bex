## Objective

Make every Render-shaped and old bex URL for a cron job land where Render's does.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/services/lib/service-root-redirect.ts`: `primaryTab` sends a cron job to `/services/$serviceId/builds`; `redirectHistoryTabByType` sends a cron job's `deploys` to `builds` with its search, and another type's `builds`/`runs` to its primary tab.
- `dashboard/src/routes/services.$serviceId.builds.tsx`, `services.$serviceId.deploys.index.tsx`, `services.$serviceId.runs.tsx`, `services.$serviceId.compute.tsx`, `services.$serviceId.index.tsx`.
- `dashboard/src/routes/cron.$.tsx` and `dashboard/src/common/lib/render-alias.ts`: `/cron/<id>/<rest>` maps to `/services/<id>/<rest>` (w5/m39).
- bex's bare service URL redirects to the type's primary tab (w5/m57), where Render renders Runs at `/cron/<id>` itself. A redirect to `/runs` is bex's equivalent; the sidebar highlights Runs either way.

## Steps

1. Cron root → `/services/$serviceId/runs`.
2. A cron job's `/builds` → `/deploys`, keeping its search (the filters w4/284 put in the URL). A cron job's `/deploys` renders the deploy history (today's Builds view), titled Deploys.
3. A cron job's `/compute` → `/services/$serviceId/settings#compute`. Other types keep their Compute page.
4. Check that `/cron/<id>`, `/cron/<id>/builds` and `/cron/<id>/deploys/<dep>` land on the same pages through the alias.
5. Route tests for each redirect, including a non-cron `/runs`.
