## Objective

Make the focus ring the shared controls draw reach 3:1 against the surface behind it in both themes, by changing the strength where it is set once, so every page inherits it.

## Context

- `--ring` at full strength is 4.90:1 on `--background` and 5.19:1 on `--card` (light), 9.20:1 and 8.80:1 (dark). At the 50% the controls apply it is 2.06 / 2.10 and 2.93 / 2.96 (milestone README, "What is drawn today").
- `ring-ring/50`, 12 uses in 11 files: `dashboard/src/common/components/ui/` `button.tsx`, `input.tsx`, `textarea.tsx`, `auto-textarea.tsx`, `select.tsx`, `checkbox.tsx`, `tabs.tsx`, `badge.tsx`, `scroll-area.tsx`; `dashboard/src/common/components/section-navigation.tsx`; `dashboard/src/features/logs/components/log-line-list.tsx`.
- `dashboard/src/style.css`: `* { @apply border-border outline-ring/50; }` (base layer) and the Ory rule `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px color-mix(in oklab, var(--ring) 50%, transparent)`.
- `ring-ring/40`, 3 uses: `dashboard/src/features/agent-sessions/components/steering-composer.tsx` and `new-session-composer.tsx`.
- `button.tsx`'s destructive variant draws `focus-visible:ring-destructive/20` (light) and `/40` (dark).
- A 3 px ring at full strength is heavier than today's halo. The bar is the contrast, not the width: a thinner full-strength ring (2 px) passes too. Choose one treatment and use it on every shared control.

## Steps

1. Decide the treatment (width and strength) that passes on `--background`, `--card`, `--popover`, `--muted` and `--sidebar` in both themes, and write the choice and its ratios into the milestone README.
2. Apply it in the nine shared controls, `section-navigation.tsx`, `log-line-list.tsx`, the base-layer outline and the Ory rule.
3. Bring the two composers' `ring-ring/40` and the destructive button's ring to the same bar.
4. Remove a local `focus-visible:ring-ring` that only restored full strength over a shared control (start with `service-source-card.tsx`, `service-source-picker.tsx`, `git-credentials-menu.tsx`); leave one that belongs to a control with no shared ring.
5. From `dashboard/`: `yarn vitest related --run <files>`, `yarn eslint <files>`, `yarn typecheck`.
