Why: a click from the workspace's service list paints a frame whose header names the service and draws a cron job's Trigger Run slot, while the sidebar beside it reads `srv-…` over five items and the breadcrumb reads `srv-…`; the ready page then renames both and inserts Runs, Deploys and Shell, so the sidebar jumps where the header holds still.
