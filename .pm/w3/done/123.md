# On a touch screen at desktop width, a service's Settings cards still grow when the page loads

Why: the Settings loading frame reserves each card's ready height so the swap does not move the page (root `AGENTS.md`), but the frame's classes step by viewport width only, while the ready rows' buttons and selects take 44 px under `pointer-coarse:h-11` at every width.

- **Measured** on 2026-11-25 by w3/104, in Chromium with `hasTouch` (`(pointer: coarse)` matches) at 1440 against `local-bex`, the settings chunk held with Playwright `page.route` and then released (ready minus pending, px): an image cron job's General +8, Deploy +16, Registry credential +16, Notifications +6, Deploy Hook +16, Suspend +8, Danger Zone +8; a native web service's General +24, Runtime +12, Build +48, its Deploy card +38, Port, Health Checks and Maintenance +8, Edge Caching +12, Suspend and Danger Zone +8. The section rail already matches (both take 44 px rows).
- **Where:** the settings card classes in `dashboard/src/common/components/route-skeletons.tsx` (`SETTINGS_CARD`, `generalHeight`, `suspendHeight`, `deployCardHeight` and the `SettingsSectionSkeleton` cases) have no `pointer-coarse:` heights.
- **Done when** every Settings section's pending card equals its ready card with a coarse pointer at 1024 and 1440, for the shapes w3/104 measured, with the mouse heights at 390 and 1440 unchanged.

## Result (2026-11-27)

Done. With a coarse pointer at 1024, 1280 and 1440, and with a mouse at 390, 1024, 1280 and 1440, every Settings card's pending height now equals its ready card for the 22 shapes w3/104 measured: a web service (native, Dockerfile and image builds, Starter and Free plans, with and without a region, suspended), a private service and a background worker (repo and image, suspended, without a region), a cron job (image, native and Dockerfile repo, with a region, suspended) and a static site (running and suspended).

- **Measured** in Chromium (Playwright, `hasTouch` for the coarse pointer, `(pointer: coarse)` checked) against `local-bex` with each shape served under a real-length `srv-` id and a Render-length URL, the settings chunk held with `page.route` and then released, as w3/104 did. Before the fix a coarse pointer at 1440 grew General 8–24 px, the Build cards 46–56, their Deploy cards 38, an image service's Deploy card 32, Runtime and Edge Caching 12, the cron Deploy, Registry credential and Deploy Hook cards 16, Notifications 6, and Port, Health Checks, Maintenance, the static site card, Suspend and Danger Zone 8. Domains, Networking, Source, the cron Compute section and the rail already matched.
- **1024 and 1280 were off for a mouse too:** the content column is 720 px at 1024 and 696 at 1280 against 768 at 1440, so Region's hint (below 738 px), each Build card's Root Directory hint (below 750) and the static site's publish directory hint (below 722) wrap a line more, 20 px a card. Those cards now step up below that column width (`lg:@max-[…]:`, the column is a container), for either pointer.
- **Changed:** `SETTINGS_CARD`, `generalHeight`, `suspendHeight`, `deployCardHeight` and the cron Deploy, Runtime, Build, static Deploy, Edge Caching and Danger Zone classes in `dashboard/src/common/components/route-skeletons.tsx` gain an `lg:pointer-coarse:` height. Every coarse and column step is gated at lg, so a phone keeps its own height. Pinned in `route-skeletons.test.tsx`.
- **Not covered:** between 640 and 1023 px (either pointer), and at desktop widths whose column is wider than 768 px (1088–1199) or narrower than 682 px (1200–1272), the cards still move by 16–40 px at desktop widths and up to 188 px at 768. Fixed heights cannot follow hints that wrap at a different column width each, so that is filed as [w3/175](../175.md).
