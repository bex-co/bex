## Objective

Point the planned and approved sync links at the sync, as Render's API documents.

## Context

- `apps/blueprint_onboarding.go` builds both links (with `BlueprintDashboardRoute` after w3/m677 t005).
- Render: `SyncWithPlan.dashboardUrl` "Link to this Sync in the Render Dashboard", `ApprovedSync.dashboardUrl` "Link to the sync in the Render Dashboard".

## Steps

1. Emit `<dashboard>/blueprint/<exs-id>/sync/<sync-id>` for both, on REST, GraphQL and MCP.
2. Decide whether the sync-failure email links the failed sync; if Render's email does not, keep the Blueprint link.
