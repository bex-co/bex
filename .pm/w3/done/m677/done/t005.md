## Objective

Make every Render-shaped Blueprint URL, every bex link and every link bex-api emits open the page it means.

## Context

- `common/lib/render-alias.ts` (`redirectPreservingSuffix`) carries the query and hash; `routes/blueprint.new.tsx` owns `/blueprint/new`.
- Render's `/blueprint/generate` with nothing selected goes to `/` (`ExportBlueprint-*.js`); w3/m681 builds the page.
- bex-api: `resourcemeta.Config.DashboardURL("blueprints", …)` in `apps/blueprint_onboarding.go` (planned and approved syncs) and `notifications/alert_email_worker.go` (sync-failure email); `resourcemeta/metadata.go` names the datastore segments.

## Steps

1. Add a `/blueprint/$` splat route: `/blueprint/<id>[/<rest>]` → `/blueprints/<id>[/<rest>]` with the query and hash, `sync` and `sync/<id>` → `/syncs` until w3/m678, `generate` → `/` until w3/m681; `new` keeps its own route.
2. Check the list, the search palette and the post-create landing open Resources, and a `blp-` bookmark still lands.
3. Add `BlueprintDashboardRoute = "blueprint"` to `resourcemeta` and use it at the three call sites, with tests on the emitted URL.
