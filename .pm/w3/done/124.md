# A service's Metrics and Shell loading frames still differ from their ready pages below the toolbar

Why: with the service header gone from Metrics and Shell (w3/106), the tab's own frame is all a navigation shows, and its cards grow or shrink when the page lands.

- **Measured** 2026-11-25 for w3/106, in Chromium against `yarn local-bex` with the `Server` read held to keep the layout's frame up (a client navigation from `/`):
  - **Metrics, Application card:** the frame's card is 1002 px at 1440 and 866 px at 390; the ready card is 926 and 818 (`eden-cms-v2`). `MetricsCardSkeleton` (`dashboard/src/common/components/route-skeletons.tsx`) draws a title and a subtitle with three 36 px controls beside them, where `ApplicationMetricsCard` draws the title alone with its two segmented groups under it, and each section's title row is 28 px against the ready row's own height. The Network card differs the other way at 390 (866 against 900). The stub answers no month-to-date figure, so compare the Network card on production before changing its frame.
  - **Shell:** `ServiceShellSkeleton` previews the web terminal and SSH cards (930 px at 1440), and a service with no shell (the stub's, or a free service on production) lands on the 174 px "Shell access isn't available" card. The cached service's plan and type may be enough to pick the frame.
- Metrics' toolbar is equal since w3/106: 160, 160 and 38 px wide, and 44 px tall below lg, in both states. The page heading and the first region start at the same place in both states on every page w3/106 changed.
- **Done when** each Metrics card's frame and the Shell frame equal their ready heights at 1440 and 390 for a web service, a worker and a static site (and a free web service's Shell), measured on production or against `local-bex` with the difference the stub causes named, and `route-skeletons.test.tsx` pins the new shapes.
- Filed 2026-11-25 by w3/106.

## Result (2026-11-28)

Done. Each Metrics card's frame and the Shell frame now equal their ready heights at 1440 and 390, on a web service, a worker, a static site and a Free web service's Shell.

- **Metrics.** `MetricsCardSkeleton` is gone. `ApplicationMetricsCardSkeleton` draws the title alone on its row, then the instance filter (144 px wide, 36 px tall, 44 px under the touch rule) and the Raw/Min/Max/Avg and Percentage/Total controls with their labels laid out invisibly, so they wrap where the ready ones do (one row at 1440, three at 390). The filter is left out for a suspended or asleep service, as the ready card has none. `NetworkMetricsCardSkeleton` replaces both the old full card and `BandwidthCardSkeleton`. It draws the subtitle and the Status Code, Host and Path filters, then Total Requests (its count and Group by), Response Times (its Percentile) and Outbound Bandwidth, and then the month-to-date line. Each section is MetricSection's geometry: a title row that wraps with its controls, 8 px, the 24 px event lane, and the plot. The old 28 px rows and 8 px gaps around the lane made the card 16 px a section too tall.
- **Shell.** `shellAccess` (`dashboard/src/features/services/lib/ssh-remedy.ts`) works out what the Shell page will draw. It uses the service's `sshAddress` when its read is cached or the parent route loaded it. Otherwise it predicts the address from the services list with bex-api's `sshEligible` rule: a running, paid web, private or background service. The frame draws the Web Shell and SSH cards, or the one "Shell access isn't available" card with the ready card's copy and button, and the SSH card is `max-w-3xl`, as the ready one is. The description, card descriptions, instance label, fallback and lifecycle lines are the ready copy laid out invisibly. The instance select and the copy button are 44 px under the touch rule. The service layout's frame and the shell route's own pending component both use it, and Metrics' route-level frame reads the parent's service for the filter.
- **Measured** in Chromium against `local-bex`. The `Server` read is held to keep the layout's frame up, on a client navigation from `/`. The tab's chunk is held for the route-level frames under a loaded parent. Pending equals ready at 390, 640, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1440, with a mouse, and with a coarse pointer at 1440:
  - Metrics on a web service: 926/1012 at 1440 and 870/944 at 390.
  - Metrics on a worker: 926/404 at 1440 and 870/344 at 390.
  - Metrics on a static site: 1012 at 1440 and 944 at 390.
  - Shell on a web service or worker with an SSH address: 604 + 260 at 1440 and 656 + 344 at 390.
  - Shell on a Free web service: 130 at 1440 and 162 at 390.
  - Shell on a service whose read says no address: 130 and 182.
- **The difference the stub causes.** `local-bex` labels its metric series `instance` where bex-api says `INSTANCE`, so the stub's Application card has no instance filter. It also answers no month-to-date figure. Both were measured with bex-api's answers patched in. A stub service has no `sshAddress`, because it has no `srv-` id and no gateway. On a first visit, the Shell frame therefore predicts the terminal for its paid web service, where the stub's page says shell access isn't available. Once its read is cached, the frame follows the address.
- **Production** (`dashboard.bex.co`, QA user, read-only walk in the `bex` and `bex-canary` workspaces), measured on the ready pages:
  - A starter web service's Metrics cards are 926/1012 at 1440 and 870/944 at 390.
  - A private service's are 926/404 and 870/344.
  - Its Shell is 604 + 260 (768 px wide) at 1440 and 656 + 344 at 390.
  - A Free web service's Shell card is 130 at 1440 and 162 at 390.
  - These are the frames' heights above. No static site or worker was on hand, so the private service stands in for the worker's card, and the web service's Network card for the static site's.
- **Tests.** `route-skeletons.test.tsx` pins the Application card's header and sections, the filter left out without instances, the Network card's filters, sections and month-to-date line for both shapes, and the Shell frames: eligible, Free, no address, unknown, and from the parent's cached access. `ssh-remedy.test.ts` pins `shellAccess`, and `use-cached-service-type.test.tsx` pins the two new cache hooks. With the skeleton change reverted, the 9 frame tests fail.
- **Not covered:** a figure only the data decides. A service with no requests in the window has no request count, so at 390 its Total Requests row is 24 px shorter. Two or more instances add a legend under Memory and CPU. A suspended service with series still in the window grows the instance filter after it loads. None of w3/170, 171, 173 or 175 is touched.
