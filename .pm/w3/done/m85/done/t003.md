## Objective

Give bex-api the least authority the new read needs, in the per-tenant namespaces where tenant App certificates actually live.

## Context

- Under ADR043 an App's Ingress, TLS Secret, and therefore its cert-manager objects live in the per-tenant `<ws>` namespace. The role bound there is `bex-tenant-api` (`deploy/gitops/base/tenant-namespace-clusterroles.yaml:27`), the same role that already carries the read-only CNPG `backups`/`objectstores` grants from w6/m117 — that is the precedent to follow.
- The legacy default-namespace Role (`deploy/gitops/base/bex-api-apps-rbac.yaml`) covers storeless/hand-applied Apps and needs the same read.
- **Correction made during the work:** `lego/operator/config/api/rbac.yaml` is NOT a twin of that Role — it is a **ClusterRole**, deliberately holding only the cluster-wide `app.bex.co` verbs bex-api needs to locate resources across dynamic tenant namespaces. Adding cert-manager rules there would create exactly the cluster-wide grant this milestone forbids, so it is left untouched; both grants are namespace-scoped.
- bex-api must NOT gain a cluster-wide grant; ADR028's deliberate omission of cluster-wide Secret authority is the standing constraint and `scripts/gitops-validate.sh` guards this file family.

## Steps

1. Add read-only (`get`/`list`) rules for `certificates.cert-manager.io` and `challenges`/`orders` in `acme.cert-manager.io` to the `bex-tenant-api` ClusterRole, with a comment explaining which verb reads them and why it is read-only.
2. Mirror the same rules onto the legacy namespaced `bex-api-apps` Role.
3. Run `bash scripts/gitops-validate.sh` and keep it green.

## Files
