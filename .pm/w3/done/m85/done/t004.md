## Objective

Turn the bare clock icon on a pending custom-domain row into something a tenant can act on.

## Context

- `CertificateStatusBadge` (`dashboard/src/features/services/components/custom-domains-section.tsx:558`) renders only a `Clock` + "Pending" today.
- `CustomDomainView` (`dashboard/src/features/services/types.ts:352`) is the mapped shape; the GraphQL document is `dashboard/src/features/services/api/custom-domains.graphql` and the mapping happens in `hooks/use-custom-domains.ts`.
- The ADR005 fix-it text is the second half of the note: the tenant's record must be DNS-only (not proxied) and point at `<name>.onbex.co` via ALIAS/CNAME.
- Every user-visible string goes through `t()` with a `services.` prefix, and every key needs both `en.ts` and `zh.ts` entries (`locale-parity.test.ts` enforces it).

## Steps

1. Add `certificateReason` to the GraphQL fragment, the generated types (offline codegen per `dashboard/CLAUDE.md`), `CustomDomainView`, and the hook's mapping.
2. Render the reason beneath a pending row as muted helper text, escaped as plain text — never HTML.
3. Add the ADR005 fix-it hint alongside it, naming the DNS-only requirement and the `<name>.onbex.co` target.
4. Add locale keys to `en.ts` + `zh.ts` and cover the new states in `__tests__/custom-domains-section.test.tsx`: pending-with-reason, pending-without-reason (unchanged from today), and verified (no hint at all).
