# A service's Settings skeleton reserves stale card heights, most at 390

Why: the Settings page's pending frame is meant to hold each card's ready height so the swap does not move the page (root `AGENTS.md`), but most cards' reserved heights no longer match, so the page jumps by up to 22 px a card when Settings loads.

- **Measured** on dev-3 on 2026-11-22 by w3/m554 t005, an image-backed cron job, the settings chunk held with Playwright `page.route` and then released (pending against ready, px): at 1440 Notifications 186/208, Suspend 134/154, the section rail 304/340 (9 entries); at 390 Source 188/168, Deploy 368/384, Image registry credential 268/284, Notifications 246/268, Deploy Hook 262/278, Suspend 174/182, Danger Zone 154/162. General and Compute match: w3/m554 measured and set those.
- **Where:** `SETTINGS_CARD`, `suspendHeight`, `SettingsSourceCardSkeleton` and the `deploy` case of `SettingsSectionSkeleton`, plus `ResponsiveSectionNavigationSkeleton`'s row height, in `dashboard/src/common/components/route-skeletons.tsx`. The heights date from w5/m124's dev-5 measurement; the 16 px steps at 390 look like the cards' 44 px touch-size buttons (`max-lg:h-11`) arriving after it.
- **Done when** every Settings section's pending card equals its ready card at 390 and 1440 for a web service, a private service, a background worker, a cron job (image and repo) and a static site, measured in a real browser as above, the classes are updated, and the section rail's rows match.
- Filed 2026-11-22 by w3/m554 t005.

## Result (2026-11-25)

Done. Every Settings card's pending height now equals its ready card at 390 and 1440, and the rail's rows match, for 22 service shapes: a web service (native, Dockerfile and image builds, Starter and Free plans, with and without a region, suspended), a private service and a background worker (repo and image, suspended, without a region), a cron job (image, native and Dockerfile repo, with a region, suspended) and a static site (running and suspended).

- **Measured** in Chromium (Playwright with the settings route's chunk held by `page.route`, then released) against `local-bex` run with the ids changed to real-length `srv-` ids, since the General card's hint names the id and a short one wraps a line less. Each shape was served by overriding the Service read, with Render's default allow-all rule (`0.0.0.0/0 everywhere`, what bex-api returns for a service nobody restricted; local-bex's empty list draws the blocked state) in the stub's Hobby workspace, no custom domain and no stored registry credential. dev-3 was down (the cluster's API refused connections). The first pass found 14 cards and the rail off. The rail row is a link's 36 px, not a 32 px pill. 44 px touch buttons and longer hints had grown most cards at 390. Port, Health Checks, Maintenance, Notifications, Domains and the Registry credential card had grown at 1440 too, and the cron Build card had shrunk by 180–206 px since its source moved to the Source card. Some cards depend on the service: a free plan's Deploy card says Pre-Deploy needs a paid plan, and a running cron job's Suspend copy is a line longer at desktop than a worker's. After the fix every pair was equal.
- **Changed:** `SETTINGS_CARD`, `generalHeight`, `suspendHeight`, a new `deployCardHeight` (image, Dockerfile and native Deploy cards, by plan), the Source, cron Deploy, cron Build, Build, static Deploy, Runtime (and its sm/md/lg steps, re-measured at 640, 768, 1024 and 1280), Edge Caching and Danger Zone classes, and the rail row (`@4xl/page:h-9`), which also fixes the Key Value overview's rail (264/264 at 1440). Pinned in `route-skeletons.test.tsx`.
- **Not covered:** a coarse pointer at desktop widths (a tablet or touch laptop) still gives the ready cards 44 px controls the frame does not reserve, filed as [w3/123](../123.md). A Scale workspace's Networking card (the rule editor) and a workspace with stored registry credentials are not the shapes the frame reserves.
