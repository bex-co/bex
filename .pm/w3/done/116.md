High Availability (`pve`) on the Plan page has no replica list.
- **bex:** `dashboard/src/features/databases/components/ha-panel.tsx` draws the HA status, the switch, failover and the read replicas (add, remove) in one card, rendered by `dashboard/src/routes/databases.$databaseId.plan.tsx`.
