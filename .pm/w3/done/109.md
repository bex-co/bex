# A cron job's header and Manual Build: the smaller differences from Render that w3/m555 left

Why: w3/m555 matched Render's cron header and actions on what the walk saw; these differences from Render's bundle remain, each small, and none is signed off in ADR018.

- **The last run's date:** Render prints "Last successful run November 21, 2026 at 9:00 AM PST" (moment `LL [at] LT z`) with "UTC: …" in a tooltip when the viewer is not on UTC (`page-templates-*.js` `Fn`); bex prints the date without the zone, and its tooltip lists local, UTC and Unix time (`InstantTooltip`).
- **The schedule's words:** Render words every valid schedule with cronstrue ("at 09:00 AM UTC, Monday through Friday"); bex words the shapes `describeCronRun` names (`dashboard/src/features/services/lib/cron.ts`) and shows any other as its expression. cronstrue also words `*/40 * * * *` "every 40 minutes", which misstates it (it fires at :00 and :40); bex declines that on purpose, as `describeCron` does.
- **The notice's title:** Render says "Suspended by you" for one account tier (`suspension-*.js`, `TIER1`); bex always says "Manually suspended".
- **Resume service:** Render opens the new build when the resume answers one; bex stays on the page.
- **Manual Build's toasts:** Render shows none on success and keeps a failure until dismissed; bex's deploy trigger (`use-trigger-deploy.ts`) toasts "Deploy triggered" and its failure toast closes itself.
- **Done when** each item matches Render, or ADR018's Cron job row lists it as a divergence the user signed off, with tests for the ones that change.
