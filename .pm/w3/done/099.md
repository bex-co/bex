# A project's observability views size their controls for touch on a phone, as a service's pages do

Why: on a phone, a project's Application Logs view keeps the desktop's 32 px controls (Return to Bottom, Load older, the range picker, Edit view), where the same log panel in a service's Logs tab gives each a 44 px target.

Found by w3/m528 t009's production walk (2026-11-20, pin `820edf28d22d`, Chromium at 390 × 844, project `qa-20261120-m528b`). In `/project/{prj}/observability/logs/application`, these measured 32 px tall: Edit view (103 × 32), Duplicate (107 × 32), Delete view (32 × 32), the Log history range picker (175 × 32), Load older (96 × 32), Return to Bottom (32 × 32) and the New logs in Live Tail button (103 × 32). The Live switch stayed at its drawn 36 × 20 with no larger hit area. Only Maximize and Log options were 44 × 44. In the service Logs tab at the same width, Return to Bottom measured 44 × 44.

Cause: the touch rule in `dashboard/src/style.css` (`@media (width < 64rem), (pointer: coarse)`, the block on `[data-touch-region]`) applies only inside a `data-touch-region`. `service-detail-layout.tsx` sets one on the service pages' scroll container, and `log-filter-bar.tsx` sets one on its phone filter sheet. Nothing in the project layout (`routes/project.$projectId.tsx`) or `project-log-viewer.tsx` sets one, so a project's views keep desktop sizes.

Done when a project's Application Logs, Request Logs and Service Metrics views at 390 give every button, link-button and select a 44 px target and the Live switch a 44 × 44 hit area, as a service's pages do, and a test pins the region on the project layout.

**Done 2026-11-25.** The project layout (`routes/project.$projectId.tsx`) wraps every project page in a `data-touch-region` (`display: contents`), and the route's pending frame (`ProjectRouteSkeleton`) carries one too, so Overview and Settings take the 44 px targets as well as the three views. Measured in Chromium at 390 × 844 on the local stub (`yarn local-bex`, which now serves a project's three default views and its Service Metrics charts): Edit view 103 × 44, Duplicate 107 × 44, Delete view 44 × 44, Search logs 358 × 44, the Log history range 254 × 44, Maximize and Log options 44 × 44, New logs in Live Tail 175 × 44 and its Dismiss 44 × 44, Return to Bottom 44 × 44 after scrolling up; on Service Metrics the Time range 160 × 44, each legend toggle 44 tall, Group by 178 × 44 and Percentile 148 × 44; the view-not-found "Open Application Logs" link-button 175 × 44 (now a `Button asChild` link, so the region sizes it). A log line's row menu and inline instance filter stay `data-touch-exempt`, as in a service's Logs tab. The Live switch the note names no longer exists: w3/m537 made Live tail a range. The loading frames of the five project pages (Overview with its environment card, Settings, the log view, Service Metrics) reserve the grown boxes through `common/lib/touch-target.ts`; pending and ready held the same geometry at 390, at 1440, and at 1440 with a coarse pointer. Pinned by `routes/__tests__/project-touch-region.test.tsx` (the region on the layout, on its adopting skeleton and on the route's pending frame, and every control box of the four project skeletons at the touch size; it fails with the region or a touch class removed).
