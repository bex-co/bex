## Objective

The telemetry pipeline alerts when it fails, goes silent while the CLI is in use, or a server change starts breaking CLI commands (ADR113 D8).

## Context

- Rules: `deploy/gitops/base/rules/alerting_rules.yml` (group `bex`; `severity: warning` is the daily-digest tier, e.g. `AnalyticsDatasourceProbeStale` at ~:1185). Tests: `deploy/gitops/base/rules/*_alerts_test.yml`, run by `bash scripts/gitops-validate.sh` (`promtool test rules`).
- `scripts/obs-scrape-keep-check.sh` fails an alert whose `bex_*` series a scrape keep-list drops.
- Why it matters: a `503` makes every affected CLI back off for 30 minutes and a `404` for an hour (upstream `pkg/analytics/backoff.go`), and a 404 is counted under the `unmatched` route, not the telemetry route.

## Steps

1. `CLITelemetryIngestFailing`: 5xx share of `bex_api_http_requests_total{route="POST /v1/cli-telemetry-events"}` above 5 % for 15 minutes, with a minimum-rate guard.
2. `CLITelemetryIngestSilent`: CLI requests continue (`bex_api_cli_requests_total`, a minimum over 2 h) while `bex_api_cli_telemetry_events_total` stored none in 2 h.
3. `CLIExecutionErrorShareHigh`: the `execution_error` share of stored events over 30 minutes is above three times its 7-day share, with a minimum-volume guard.
4. Each is `severity: warning`, with a description that says how to debug and links `docs/runbooks/cli-analytics.md`. Add `deploy/gitops/base/rules/cli_telemetry_alerts_test.yml` with fire and no-fire cases for each.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `bash scripts/gitops-validate.sh` passes, including the new promtool tests and the keep-list guard.

## Result (2026-11-16)

In group `bex` of `deploy/gitops/base/rules/alerting_rules.yml`, all `severity: warning`, each description says how to debug and links `docs/runbooks/cli-analytics.md#collection-health`:

- `CLITelemetryIngestFailing`: the 5xx share over 10 minutes above 5%, held for 15 minutes, with at least two failed deliveries per window (the window is shorter than the wait, so one burst cannot fire).
- `CLITelemetryIngestSilent`: at least 30 CLI requests in 2 hours with some in each hour and in the last 30 minutes, and nothing stored in 2 hours (the per-hour guard came from the first production night, see t005). Not `increase()` alone: a series that appeared within the window above 0 also counts as a stored event (the born-at-1 case from w3/m437's finding). The ingest route's 202/429/500/503 series and both CLI counters are exported at 0 as well.
- `CLIExecutionErrorShareHigh`: the recorded 30-minute share (`bex:cli_telemetry_execution_error_share:ratio30m`) above three times the recorded 7-day share (`…:avg7d`, from `bex:cli_telemetry_events:rate5m`), with 10 events and 5 execution errors. On production's last week it would have warned twice (18 and 74 execution errors in half an hour; the second was one workspace's `bex logs` failing in a loop).

`cli_telemetry_alerts_test.yml` has 14 cases (fire and no-fire for each, the born-at-1 rollout, a burst that stops, requests that already stopped, each floor alone). Thirteen mutations of the expressions (each guard and floor removed, windows, thresholds, the baseline window) each fail a case. `obs-coverage-check.sh` and `obs-scrape-keep-check.sh` pass.
