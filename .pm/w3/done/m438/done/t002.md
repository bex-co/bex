## Objective

`bex_api_cli_telemetry_events_total` no longer takes a label value verbatim from the request body (ADR113 G7, D8).

## Context

- Defined at `lego/backend/internal/api/httpmetrics.go:188` with labels `command, completion_kind, cli_version, output_format`; fed by `clitelemetry.Service.Record` (`lego/backend/internal/clitelemetry/service.go`) through the `TelemetryMetrics` interface.
- `sanitizeVersion` (same file) already accepts only a release-tag shape for the bex release.
- Today no panel or rule reads this counter (check with `rg cli_telemetry_events_total deploy`).

## Steps

1. Labels become `completion_kind` (the eight upstream kinds, otherwise `other`), `output_format` (`interactive|json|yaml|text`, otherwise `unknown`), `cli_version` (release-tag shape, otherwise `unknown`) and `client` (`bex` when the release header is present, otherwise `render-cli`). Drop `command`; per-command questions stay in Postgres.
2. Update the interface, `Record` and their tests. Stored rows are unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A test posts 50 events with distinct garbage command, version, kind and format values; the counter holds no more series than the closed sets allow, and all 50 rows are stored.

## Result (2026-11-16)

Labels are `completion_kind` (eight upstream kinds or `other`), `output_format` (four or `unknown`) and `client`, clamped by `clitelemetry.MetricLabels`; stored rows are unchanged. **Deviation:** `cli_version` is dropped too, not clamped. Production showed counter series born at 1 per replica (one live series 23 minutes after a rollout), which `increase()` cannot see; a version label cannot be created in advance, while the closed 9 × 5 × 2 set can be, so every replica now exports all 90 series at 0. ADR113 D8 is amended with the evidence. `TestCLITelemetryCounterHoldsOnlyClosedSeries` posts 50 deliveries with distinct garbage command, version, kind and format through the real REST route into a real registry: 50 rows stored, exactly 90 series, every value in its closed set, 25 events counted per client under `other`/`unknown`. `TestMetricLabelsAreClosedSets` and `TestRecordStoresGarbageVerbatimButCountsItClamped` pin the clamping and that rows keep what was sent.
