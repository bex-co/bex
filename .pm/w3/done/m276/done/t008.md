## Objective

A declared image port nothing binds leaves `openPorts` on production, where the listener sweep rarely finishes. After a sweep runs out of time, the operator dials the release's declared ports it did not find open, with a found listener as a control, and drops the ones that do not accept.

## Context

- Live check 2026-11-10 (m273 t005, production pin `4630a48a765e`): three `traefik/whoami` services serving 8080 kept `openPorts: [80, 8080]` for 7+ minutes (3+ scan intervals); `:80` from a sibling failed to connect. `status.runtimePorts.ports` was `[80, 8080]` on each, while the python service with no declared ports read `[8080, 9090]`.
- Cause: `scanPrivatePorts` merged the declared ports into an unfinished first sweep ("unproven") and a rescan of the same revision only adds. Closed ports drop probes on production (`LikelyHTTPPorts`, w4/393), so the 65,535-port sweep does not fit `portDetectTimeout` (15 s) and the declared 80 was never dropped.

## Steps

1. `declaredPortsClosed`: one bounded `ScanPorts` pass (3 s) over the unproven declared ports plus a found listener as control; a verdict only when the control answers.
2. Apply it to the first unfinished sweep and to every unfinished rescan; undeclared listeners keep the add-only rule (the Services route them by the record).
3. ADR089 D3 phase 2 follow-up item 4; ADR018 open-ports row.
