## Objective

The gateway's agent-session listener: verify the m39 HMAC ticket, claim its DB nonce, open the in-sandbox driver's `GET /stream`, and reverse-proxy it to the client **byte-transparently** while teeing every part into the t001 store.

## Context

- Trust design unchanged (ADR035/ADR047 D3): the gateway is the sole ingress into `<ws>-sandbox`; bex-api never joins the stream path. Same shape as the sandbox-exec SSE proxy (`internal/sshgateway/sandbox_exec.go`) and web-shell ticket flow (nonce store `shell_ticket_nonces`).
- Verbatim-forward guarantee (ADR047 § D3, added 2026-08-02): no re-encoding/filtering/reordering/injection; preserve `x-vercel-ai-ui-message-stream: v1` end to end; ignore any cookies (ticket is the only credential); additions limited to auth/tee/replay.
- Ticket claims bind subject + session + exact sandbox pod + `<ws>-sandbox` namespace (`internal/agentsessionticket/ticket.go`); resolve the pod the same way the credential broker does.

## Steps

1. New listener/route in `internal/sshgateway` (env-gated like the others; disabled when the ticket secret is unset).
2. Ticket verify + single-use nonce claim; reject expired/reused/mismatched claims with distinct named errors.
3. Dial the driver's stream port on the claimed pod; stream SSE frames to the client unmodified; tee parts to the store (append batched; backpressure must never corrupt ordering).
4. Multi-client: N concurrent attached clients per session share the tee (fan-out), bounded by a session cap (web-shell caps precedent).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Streamed bytes to the client are identical to driver output (test asserts byte equality incl. the v1 header).
- [ ] Reused nonce, expired ticket, wrong-pod claims each fail closed with named errors; cookies on the request are demonstrably ignored.
- [ ] Parts land in the store in order while a client is attached; a second concurrent client receives the same live parts.
