# 066 — Route skeletons of loader-less routes never paint on a cold navigation: `pendingComponent` is split into the route's own lazy chunk

Why: the dashboard rule asks every route to preview its ready layout while it is pending ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), "Dashboard preloading skeletons must match their post-loading contents"). For a route with no loader, the only slow part of a navigation is its chunk, and the chunk carries the skeleton, so the user sees the generic spinner instead.

- **Evidence (2026-11-17, w3/m305 live check, pin `6984548a30f8`):** from `/` in `bex-canary`, a Playwright route held every new `*.js` request and the sidebar's Settings link was clicked. At 3.5 s the page showed `RoutePending` (the spinner) and no `[data-skeleton-region]`; the held files were `workspace.settings-*.js` and its imports. With the chunk already loaded, the navigation renders at once, so `WorkspaceSettingsPageSkeleton` paints only when something else holds the route. The probe that did show it gave the route a pending `loader` through `window.__TSR_ROUTER__`.
- **Cause:** `vite.config.ts` groups `["component", "pendingComponent"]` in `codeSplittingOptions.defaultBehavior`, so `pendingComponent` is a lazy component in the same chunk. While that chunk loads, the lazy pending component suspends too, and the outer boundary falls back to the router's `defaultPendingComponent`. `dashboard/AGENTS.md` § Navigation pending states requires the grouping for the detail routes that reuse `component` as `pendingComponent`.
- **Affected:** every route whose `pendingComponent` is a route-shaped skeleton from `common/components/route-skeletons.tsx` and which has no loader, among them `/workspace/settings` and `/settings`. Routes with a loader show their skeleton once the chunk is in and the loader is still running.
- **Direction:** keep the detail routes' grouping, and give the list, settings and create routes a per-route `codeSplitGroupings` that leaves `pendingComponent` in the route's reference file (check which chunk `route-skeletons.tsx` then lands in), or the equivalent. Check the bundle (`ANALYZE=1 yarn build`) and that the detail routes' `ReferenceError` guard still holds.
- **Done when:** with the route's chunk held, a client navigation to `/workspace/settings` and to `/settings` paints the route's own skeleton (its `data-route-skeleton` attribute) within `defaultPendingMs`, at 1440 and 390; detail routes keep their component-as-pending frame; `yarn lint` and `yarn test` pass.
