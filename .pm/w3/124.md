# A service's Metrics and Shell loading frames still differ from their ready pages below the toolbar

Why: with the service header gone from Metrics and Shell (w3/106), the tab's own frame is all a navigation shows, and its cards grow or shrink when the page lands.

- **Measured** 2026-11-25 for w3/106, in Chromium against `yarn local-bex` with the `Server` read held to keep the layout's frame up (a client navigation from `/`):
  - **Metrics, Application card:** the frame's card is 1002 px at 1440 and 866 px at 390; the ready card is 926 and 818 (`eden-cms-v2`). `MetricsCardSkeleton` (`dashboard/src/common/components/route-skeletons.tsx`) draws a title and a subtitle with three 36 px controls beside them, where `ApplicationMetricsCard` draws the title alone with its two segmented groups under it, and each section's title row is 28 px against the ready row's own height. The Network card differs the other way at 390 (866 against 900). The stub answers no month-to-date figure, so compare the Network card on production before changing its frame.
  - **Shell:** `ServiceShellSkeleton` previews the web terminal and SSH cards (930 px at 1440), and a service with no shell (the stub's, or a free service on production) lands on the 174 px "Shell access isn't available" card. The cached service's plan and type may be enough to pick the frame.
- Metrics' toolbar is equal since w3/106: 160, 160 and 38 px wide, and 44 px tall below lg, in both states. The page heading and the first region start at the same place in both states on every page w3/106 changed.
- **Done when** each Metrics card's frame and the Shell frame equal their ready heights at 1440 and 390 for a web service, a worker and a static site (and a free web service's Shell), measured on production or against `local-bex` with the difference the stub causes named, and `route-skeletons.test.tsx` pins the new shapes.
