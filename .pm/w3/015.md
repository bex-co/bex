# Transcript part timestamps so "Worked for Ns" survives replay

Found researching Devin (2026-08-05): it shows "Worked for 40s" durations even on a replayed session — per-step timing is persisted. Our m43 v1 transcript parts carry no timestamps, so m44's durations are client-derived from stream arrival and collapse to a bare "Worked"/"Thought" label on a one-frame replay (m44 t004's recorded decision).

Fix: emit a timestamp on each part from the agent-image driver (`lego/agent-image/driver/src/session.ts` — e.g. an `at` field on the logged/streamed part, or a `data-acp` timing part per step), tee it through the m43 transcript store verbatim (the store is already verbatim text — no schema change), and have the dashboard's duration hook (`lib/stream-duration.ts`) prefer part timestamps over arrival timing. Mind the D3 verbatim-forward guarantee: additive fields on driver-emitted parts are fine; the gateway must not rewrite anything.

Touches driver + dashboard (and possibly the AI SDK part shape) — promote to a milestone if the driver change ripples.
