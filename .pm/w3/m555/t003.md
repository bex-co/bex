## Objective

Make the click do what Render's bundle does, and leave one Trigger Run per page.

## Context

- Render's bundle fires `runCronJob` with no confirm and opens `logs?r=live`. The walk could not check it: the walked cron jobs do real work, and the others are suspended.
- bex: the Runs card's header button opens a confirm dialog (`services.cronTriggerConfirmTitle`, the preemption text added by w4/m114 t003) and stays on Runs.
- Recorded decision state: following the bundle is the proposal's recommendation under ADR008's parity-first rule, not a user decision. Keeping the confirm would be a divergence that needs the user's sign-off in ADR018. A live check needs a disposable Render cron job (at least $1 a month), which is the user's call.

## Steps

1. Fire on click; on success navigate to the cron job's Logs with Live tail selected (bex's spelling of Render's `r=live`, w3/m537); on a run that came back without one, open Events.
2. Remove the Runs card's header button and its confirm dialog and strings; w3/m556 puts Trigger Run in the Runs page's empty states.
3. Record in `cron-dashboard.md` that this behaviour is bundle-only and untested on Render.
