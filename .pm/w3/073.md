# 073 — Workspace Settings' cold-load skeleton cannot know the workspace's organization or the caller's other workspaces, so an org admin's page grows on load

Why: the dashboard rule asks a pending frame to preview the exact ready state ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), "Dashboard preloading skeletons must match their post-loading contents"). On a direct load of `/workspace/settings`, the inline skeleton is drawn before the workspace list arrives. For an admin of an organization workspace who has another workspace, the frame therefore lacks Workspace privacy, the second section, so everything below General moves down when the page is ready. It also lacks the Delete workspace card.

- **Evidence (2026-11-18, [w3/m305](done/m305/README.md) live check, pin `054f29128f64`, `bex-canary`: Scale, admin, an org workspace, three workspaces).** The probe was a direct load with `api.bex.co/graphql` held. Every region the inline frame draws was within 4 px of its ready section at 1440 and 390, but two were absent:
  - **Workspace privacy:** the region and its rail link were missing. The ready card is 278 px at 1440 and 330 px at 390, so every later section moves down 302 px and 354 px.
  - **Delete workspace card:** the danger zone went from 154 to 434 px at 1440 and from 194 to 534 px at 390.
  - The shell's own workspace switcher is a skeleton in the same frame. With the list known (a client navigation, the route held by a pending loader), the route skeleton draws both cards and matches the ready page exactly.
- **Cause:** `useWorkspaceSettingsSkeletonSections` (`dashboard/src/features/workspaces/hooks/use-workspace-settings-skeleton-sections.ts`) shows privacy only once `currentWorkspace.organization` is known. That was w7/m464's choice: most workspaces are standalone. It shows the Delete card only when `workspaces.length > 1`. On a cold load both facts are unknown and default to absent. Before GraphQL answers, the client has only the `bex.selectedWorkspaceId` cookie (`dashboard/src/features/workspaces/lib/selection.ts`).
- **Direction:** hand the cold frame what the previous visit knew. For example, store a shape hint for the selected workspace beside the selection cookie when the list loads (in an organization, admin, more than one workspace), and read it in the hook while the list is loading. Another option is to prefetch the workspace list during SSR. With no hint, keep today's standalone default.
- **Done when:** take an org-workspace admin with another workspace who has loaded the dashboard before. A direct load of `/workspace/settings` with GraphQL held draws the privacy region, its rail link and the Delete card, and every region is within 4 px of the ready page at 1440 and 390. A first-ever load keeps the standalone default. `yarn lint` and `yarn test` pass.
