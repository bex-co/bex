## Record (2026-11-18)

Done. Every definition-of-done behavior and the failure modes beside it have a test in `lego/operator/internal/edgecache/edgecache_conditional_test.go`. Each test was proven by mutating the line it guards and watching it fail:

- HIT 304: no validator matching, the 304 keeping representation headers, strong comparison, `If-Modified-Since` evaluated beside `If-None-Match`, the date comparison inverted.
- A non-2xx answered 304.
- The fill: validators ignored, the body not stored after a 304, the 304 fill reading an overflowing body to its end.
- `Age`: absent, or ignoring the upstream's.

The test origin also counts validators that reach it, so the fill is proven to send none. `TestA304CrossesARealServerAndKeepsTheConnection` drives the cache behind a real `http.Server` and checks the 304 that reaches the wire, with no body and no `Content-Length`, on a reused connection.
