## Objective

When a stored response is fresh and the request's validator matches it, the edge cache answers `304 Not Modified` with no body, instead of the full 200 it writes today.

## Context

- `lego/operator/internal/edgecache/edgecache.go`: `ServeHTTP` (~196-220) and `writeEntry` (~489-499), which always writes the stored status and body.
- RFC 9110 §13.1.2 (If-None-Match, weak comparison, `*`), §13.1.3 (If-Modified-Since, GET/HEAD only, ignored when If-None-Match is present), §13.2.2 (precedence), §15.4.5 (a 304 carries ETag, Cache-Control, Content-Location, Date, Expires and Vary that a 200 would). RFC 9111 §4.3.2: a cache handles a conditional request against a stored response.
- The response keeps `CF-Cache-Status: HIT` (Render's header, [web-service-caching](https://render.com/docs/web-service-caching)).

## Steps

1. Evaluate the conditional against the stored entry's `ETag` and `Last-Modified`.
2. On a match, write 304 with the RFC's headers and `CF-Cache-Status: HIT`, no body.
3. Unit tests: strong and weak ETags, `*`, a list, IMS older and newer, IMS ignored beside INM, HEAD.
