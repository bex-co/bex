- **Fix 2, "mechanism, not behavior".** The Edge caching row says "Divergence in mechanism, not behavior". Replace it with the behaviors that differ, each dated 2026-11-18 and marked unsigned:

  - 48 MiB per instance and 6 MiB per object;
  - turning caching on adds a sidecar and rolls once.
  - Name `w3/m486` (purge and profile switch without a roll) and `w3/m489` (per-service tier) as the scheduled convergence.
