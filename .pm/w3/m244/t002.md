## Objective

The `w3/044` probe covered HTTP/1.1 only (no cutoff ≤ 6 min). Browsers use HTTP/2.

## Steps

1. Probe `api.bex.co` over HTTP/2 (e.g. `h2` client or `curl --http2` with a held connection): idle 30 s – 15 min, then a POST; record GOAWAY frames and when they arrive.
2. Read the live Traefik entrypoint settings (`respondingTimeouts`, HTTP/2 settings) and the Hetzner LB service/listener settings (Terraform under `infra/`); note what closes an idle flow first and whether it closes cleanly (GOAWAY/FIN) or silently.
3. Probe during a Traefik pod restart on the mock cluster to see what an in-flight browser connection experiences (pre-approved environment).

### Step 2 — live edge settings (2026-10-30, read-only)

- Traefik `websecure`: `proxyProtocol.trustedIPs=10.10.0.7/32` (the Hetzner LB), `respondingTimeouts.readTimeout=6000s`; **no** `lifeCycle.requestAcceptGraceTimeout` and no `preStop`, `terminationGracePeriodSeconds: 60`, so a terminating Traefik pod stops accepting right away while the LB may still route new flows to its node for a health-check interval.
- Prod Service is NodePort + `externalTrafficPolicy: Local` (`overlays/prod/values/traefik.values.yaml`): LB targets come and go with node membership. **The `w4/done/433` sighting (~08:02Z 10-29) coincides with node-count flaps 13→12→13→12 at 07:43–08:05Z** (`kube_node_status_condition` Ready count); the `m159` sighting (06:46Z) does not coincide with any node, Traefik or bex-api change.
- Candidates for t003, in order: (a) Traefik graceful termination (`--entryPoints.websecure.transport.lifeCycle.requestAcceptGraceTimeout` ≈ LB health-check interval + a `preStop` sleep) so a draining edge keeps accepting until the LB has moved; (b) LB target churn on node join/leave under `Local` policy; (c) the ~400 s HTTP/2 GOAWAY race. t001's net-log is what decides between them.
