## Done (2026-10-30, loopx w3)

The cause is not edge-side (t001): Chrome's own 29 s QUIC idle close raced the dashboard's 30 s poll on the HTTP/3 leg to Cloudflare. Fixed in the client:

- `RESOURCE_POLL_INTERVAL_MS` 30 s → **25 s**; the other recurring 30 s requests (agent-session idle poll, metrics/datastore-metrics default cadence, the Router overview poll) now use it. An open page keeps its session alive, so polls and mutations ride a live connection.
- `QUIC_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS = 29_000` documents the reason, and `poll-cadence-invariant.test.ts` fails on any numeric literal within ±3 s of it in non-test source unless an allowlist entry gives a reason (a freshness bound, a local clock tick). Mutation-checked: setting the baseline back to 30 s fails both tests.
- Edge candidates from t002 (Traefik graceful termination, LB target churn) are not the cause of these sightings; left unchanged.
- Verification after deploy: re-run the t001 net-log capture for 2 h (expect 0 `ERR_CONNECTION_CLOSED` from idle-timeout races) — part of t008's closeout evidence along with three clean w4 QA runs.
