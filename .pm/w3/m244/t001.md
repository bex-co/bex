## Objective

Find what actually closes the connection the browser reports as `ERR_CONNECTION_CLOSED`.

## Steps

1. Run the dashboard in Chromium with `--log-net-log=<file> --net-log-capture-mode=Everything` (Playwright `launchOptions.args`), signed in as the QA user (`scripts/qa-login.sh`), polling a service page for ≥2h with periodic mutations on a disposable fixture.
2. For every failed request: extract the socket/session events (H2 `GOAWAY`, RST, `ERR_CONNECTION_CLOSED` origin, idle age of the reused session) and the timestamp; correlate with edge codes / bex-api + Traefik rollouts (Prometheus) the same way `w3/044` did.
3. Record excerpts (no cookies/tokens) in this file.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least one failure captured and explained, or ≥2h captured with none (documented).
