# Render's invoice, usage and upcoming-invoice pages have a bex counterpart

Why: Render's Billing page opens each invoice at `/i/<id>/view`, a month's usage at `/usage/<id>/view` and the upcoming invoice at `/pending-invoice/<month>/<year>/view`; bex has none of these pages, and no board item owns the URLs.

Filed by w3/m704 t002 on 2026-11-27, from the URL inventory in [dashboard-routes.md § Render's URL inventory](../../docs/render-artifacts/dashboard-routes.md#renders-url-inventory). Related: w6/m625 gives each invoice a PDF download and Pay Now on the Billing page.

- **Render:** the urls chunk builds `/i/${id}/view`, `/usage/${id}/view` and `/pending-invoice/${month}/${year}/view`; the router serves each with `/*` beneath it, and the Billing navigation selects on `/i/*`, `/usage/*` and `/pending-invoice/*`.
- **bex:** `/billing` lists invoices (`features/usage/components/usage-page.tsx`) and `/usage` shows usage; neither opens a page per invoice, per month or for the upcoming invoice, and the three URLs answer bex's not-found page.
- **Done when:** after reading what each Render page shows, the three URLs open bex's equivalent (a page of its own, or the Billing or Usage section for that invoice or month), dashboard tests pin each, and the inventory's rows read `matched`, or a recorded decision says why not.
