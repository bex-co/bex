# Between 640 and 1023 px, and at desktop widths around the rail's arrival, a service's Settings cards still move when the page loads

Why: the Settings loading frame reserves fixed card heights measured at 390, 1024, 1280 and 1440 (w3/104, w3/123), but each ready card's hints wrap at a column width of their own, and the viewport moves that column continuously, so at other widths the swap still moves the page (root `AGENTS.md`).

- **Measured** on 2026-11-27 by w3/123 in Chromium against `local-bex`, for the 22 shapes w3/104 measured, the settings chunk held with Playwright `page.route` and then released, pending against ready every 8 px from 1024 to 1460 and every 16 px from 640 to 1016, with and without a coarse pointer:
  - **640–1023**, every width and either pointer (the touch region's 44 px controls, a 464–720 px column, and the `sm:` heights measured at 1440): up to +188 px a card at 768, e.g. a native web service's Build card 680/840 (pending/ready), General 434/490, Health Checks 246/314, Domains 473/537; Edge Caching −52 at 928.
  - **1088–1199**, before the rail arrives (a 784–895 px column, wider than 1440's 768): General, Notifications, Port, Health Checks, Domains, Edge Caching, Maintenance and Suspend 16–20 px shorter than reserved.
  - **1200–1272**, once the rail arrives (a 616–688 px column): General, the Build cards, Domains, Edge Caching, the static site card, Suspend and Danger Zone 16–40 px taller than reserved.
  - Equal for every shape at 390, 1024–1080 and 1280–1456, with either pointer.
- **Where:** the fixed heights in `SETTINGS_CARD`, `generalHeight`, `suspendHeight`, `deployCardHeight` and the `SettingsSectionSkeleton` cases in `dashboard/src/common/components/route-skeletons.tsx`. Region's hint wraps below a 738 px column, a Build card's Root Directory hint below 750 and the publish directory hint below 722, with more steps below 682 and 640, so per-width classes would multiply. Drawing the cards structurally instead, each translated hint reserved with `ReservedText` as `ServiceNetworkingCardSkeleton` does (w4/m710), would follow every wrap, in other languages too.
- **Done when** every Settings section's pending card equals its ready card at every width from 640 to 1460 with either pointer, and at 390, for those shapes, measured in a real browser as above.
