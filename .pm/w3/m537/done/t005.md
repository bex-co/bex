## Objective

Give a project's log views the same single control, with the default t001 recorded from Render.

## Context

- `ProjectLogViewer` (`dashboard/src/features/logs/components/project-log-viewer.tsx`) keeps `live` in local state, on unless a View in context link opened it (`useState(!contextLine)`), and renders its own `LiveToggle` (`project-logs-live-toggle`).
- Its range comes from `project-view-page.tsx` and `project-log-view-page.tsx`: the URL's `r` (`projectLogRange`), else the saved search's `timeRange.relative` (`viewTimeRange`), else `DEFAULT_LOG_RANGE`.
- `projectLogContextHref` builds the view's View in context link (`r=custom`, `contextLine`).
- The routes: `routes/project.$projectId.observability.logs.application.tsx` and `.request.tsx`.
- The pending state: `ProjectLogViewPageSkeleton` in `dashboard/src/common/components/route-skeletons.tsx`. w3/099 (touch-sized controls on a project view at 390) is separate; do not fold it in.

## Steps

1. Derive `live` from the range and drop the local state; a View in context link still opens paused, on its custom range.
2. Remove the view's `LiveToggle` and pass the Live tail entry to its `RangeSelect`.
3. Apply t001's default: the saved search's time range when it names one, else Render's default for a view.
4. Make `ProjectLogViewPageSkeleton`'s toolbar match the ready toolbar at 1440 and 390.
5. Update `project-log-viewer.test.tsx` and `project-log-view-page.test.tsx` to drive the picker.
