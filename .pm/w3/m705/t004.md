## Objective

Serve Render's create chooser and its slug loader, and the legacy team settings scheme.

## Context

- Render: `/new` (chooser); `/new/:serviceTypeSlug` with loader `Ez`/`Tz` (README § What Render does); `/new/team`, `/new/workspace`; `/t/:id/settings`, `/t/:id/settings/*`.
- bex: `routes/new.{database,redis,project,workspace}.tsx`, `_serviceCreate.*.new.tsx`, `services.new.tsx`, `blueprint.new.tsx`, `new.env-group.tsx` (t001); `RENDER_CREATE_LANDINGS` in `common/lib/render-alias.ts`; `routes/w.$.tsx` `landingFor`.

## Steps

1. Find bex's counterpart of Render's `/new` chooser and add a `new` index route landing there.
2. Add `routes/new.$.tsx` applying Render's table case-insensitively: a service type to `/<type>/new` with the query kept, the aliases to their create pages, `team` to `/new/workspace`, anything else to `/new`. Literal routes (`database`, `redis`, `project`, `workspace`, `env-group`) keep precedence.
3. Add `routes/t.$.tsx` sending `/t/<id>/settings[/*]` through the same handling as `/w/<id>/settings[/*]`.
