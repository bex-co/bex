## Objective

## Context

- `dashboard/src/routes/__tests__/` and `common/lib/__tests__/render-alias.test.ts` are the precedent.

## Steps

1. One table-driven test over every alias row of `dashboard-routes.md`'s URL table: the landing path, query and hash kept, never `routes/$.tsx`.

4. Revert one behavior at a time and watch its test fail.
