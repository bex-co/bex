## Objective

Split the single database page into Render's pages, each at its own URL, with the cards t001's § Page map assigns to it.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/routes/databases.$databaseId.tsx` today owns the header, the `DetailTabNav`, the Overview's eleven sections (each a lazily loaded panel inside `DeferredMount`) and the Logs viewer.
- The service tree is the precedent for a layout route with child pages (`services.$serviceId.tsx` plus `services.$serviceId.*.tsx`, `service-detail-layout.tsx`).
- The Metrics range and the Logs range share the w6/065 URL keys (`datastore-log-search.ts`); each page keeps its own keys, and leaving a page drops them, as the tabs do today.

## Steps

1. Turn `databases.$databaseId.tsx` into a layout: header, workspace adoption (`useAdoptResourceWorkspace`), the load-error and not-found states, and an `Outlet`.
2. Add `databases.$databaseId.index.tsx` and `databases.$databaseId.info.tsx` (Info), and `.logs.tsx`, `.metrics.tsx`, `.plan.tsx`, `.apps.tsx`, `.recovery.tsx`. Each renders its heading and its cards from the map, and the document title names the page through `routeResourceTitle`, as the service layout's `head` does in `services.$serviceId.tsx` ("Recovery ・ <name> ・ PostgreSQL", or the shape t001 records).
3. Remove the `DetailTabNav` and `DatabaseDetailNavigation`. If Info still holds more than a few cards, decide from t001's record whether it keeps a section rail.
4. Keep `DeferredMount` only where a page still scrolls past a heavy card.
