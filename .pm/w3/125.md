# A crash-looped or billing-suspended service's notice carries Render's own message and actions

Why: since w3/107 a suspended service's notice has Render's title for who suspended it, but Render replaces the not-billed line and Resume with its own message and actions for a crash loop and a spent free allowance, and bex cannot yet tell its two `billing` causes apart.

- **Render** (public bundle re-read 2026-11-25, `CronJob-*.js` `Lr`, recorded in [cron-dashboard.md](../../docs/render-artifacts/cron-dashboard.md#the-header-and-its-actions-2026-11-22-w3m555)): `Stuck Crashlooping` is a critical banner, "This service has been suspended in response to repeated crashes" with "After your service reported multiple failures over a 24-hour period, we automatically suspended it to pause billing for its usage. Consult your service's logs …", a link to Clear build cache & deploy, and the actions View Service Logs and Resume `<type>`. `Free Tier Usage Exceeded` reads "Free usage limit reached" / "Your service is now suspended until the next billing period. To resume the service immediately, upgrade its compute plan." with Upgrade compute plan (`<service>/compute`). `Monthly Quota Exceeded` reads "Service suspended by Render" / "Service is suspended because you've used all of the bandwidth in the Hobby workspace".
- **bex:** `ServiceSuspendedNotice` (`dashboard/src/features/services/components/service-suspended-notice.tsx`) always shows the not-billed line and Resume service. bex-api reports one `billing` suspender for both spent Free instance hours (`core.AnnotFreeHoursSuspended`) and included bandwidth with no payment method (`core.AnnotBandwidthSuspended`), so the dashboard cannot pick between Render's two banners (`lego/backend/internal/core/base.go` `SuspendersFor`).
- **Done when** a crash-looped service's notice has Render's message, View Service Logs and Resume; a service suspended for spent Free hours has "Free usage limit reached" with Upgrade compute plan, and one suspended for bandwidth Render's bandwidth banner, with bex-api exposing which (GraphQL first, REST and MCP kept to Render's `suspenders` enum); the header's loading frame reserves each; tests cover each; ADR018's Suspend / Resume row says so.
