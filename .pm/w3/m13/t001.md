## Objective

## Context

- `deploy/gitops/base/log-shipper.yaml` runs Alloy as a DaemonSet.
- Standard pattern: `env: [{name: NODE_NAME, valueFrom: {fieldRef: {fieldPath: spec.nodeName}}}]`, exposed to the Alloy config via `controller.extraEnv` or equivalent.

## Steps

1. Add the `NODE_NAME` downward-API env var to the DaemonSet pod spec.
2. Confirm it's readable from within the Alloy config (e.g. via `env("NODE_NAME")` in River config, or however Alloy exposes env vars to its config language).
