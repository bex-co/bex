# Log explorer Display: Render's three-way choice (Expand everything, Expand errors, Collapse everything), kept across visits

Why: Render's explorer shows every line whole by default and lets a reader collapse the stream to one line each, or wrap only its errors; bex clamps every long message at 10 lines or 1,000 characters behind a per-row Show more, and its one checkbox forgets itself on the next visit.

Read from Render's dashboard bundle by w3/m528 (2026-11-20, `Logs-DOHYAXbe.js`; find the chunk by `Expand errors`): Options ▸ **Display** is a submenu (`Display: <mode>`, header `Display settings`) with `expand-all` **Expand everything** (the default), `expand-errors` **Expand errors** (lines at `error`, `critical`, `alert` or `emergency` wrap with `whitespace-pre-wrap break-words`, every other line collapses to one ellipsized line) and `collapse-all` **Collapse everything** (every line one line, `overflow-hidden text-ellipsis whitespace-nowrap`). The choice persists in `localStorage` (`Se.getItem(j)`, falling back to `expand-all`).

bex today: `LogOptionsMenu` (`dashboard/src/features/logs/components/log-explorer.tsx`) has one **Expand everything** checkbox; `useLogExplorer` (`hooks/use-log-explorer.ts`) keeps `expandAll` per visit; `LogLineList` clamps with `clampMessage` (`lib/log-rows.ts`, `CLAMP_LINES`, `CLAMP_CHARS`) unless it is on. The deploy page's own wrap toggle (`deploy-log-panel.tsx`) is a separate knob.

Done when Options ▸ Display offers Render's three modes with Expand everything the default and showing every line whole (no clamp), Expand errors wraps only the error levels, Collapse everything shows each line on one ellipsized line, the choice survives a reload in every explorer, the ADR018 Logs row "Log explorer display and shortcuts" says so, and tests pin each mode and the persistence.
