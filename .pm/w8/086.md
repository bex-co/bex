Why: Selecting a project with no environments labels the CLI table with that project while displaying unrelated workspace resources.

**Status:** todo. **Worker:** worker8. **Severity:** minor. **Attribution:** upstream Render CLI consumer of Bex's supported bare-project shape. **Estimate:** 55m. Prepare the correction upstream and adopt through the documented pin workflow; preserve bare projects and the unfiltered All Projects choice.
