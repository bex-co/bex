## Objective

Turn the proven datapath into a node-local exporter that attributes direct public bytes to immutable pod UID plus App resource identity and exposes monotonic low-cardinality Prometheus counters.

## Context

- App pods carry `app.bex.co/app` and `app.bex.co/workspace`; only App service pods participate in direct tenant egress accounting.
- A runtime cgroup/interface id can be reused after pod deletion and must never be the durable attribution key.
- Prometheus counter resets must be recoverable by the hourly collector rather than converted to negative or free usage.

## Steps

1. Watch node-local pod lifecycle and program attribution maps keyed by the selected runtime identity.
2. Export counters labeled by namespace, resource kind, App id, and source instance with bounded cardinality.
3. Expose readiness and explicit lost-event/map-pressure/reset health metrics.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Pod replacement cannot inherit another pod's bytes.
- [ ] Exported labels map exactly to one App without workspace ids in high-cardinality series.
- [ ] Loss, reset, and unsupported-kernel states are machine-visible.

## Out of scope

- DaemonSet privileges and hourly usage aggregation.
