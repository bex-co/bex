Meter managed Postgres storage usage separately from compute. Render's flexible
Postgres plans bill the selected instance type per second and provisioned
storage at a separate GB-month rate prorated by the second; w8/m5 intentionally
adds only `instance_seconds`. Design a `storage_gb_seconds` (or equivalent)
meter, pricing semantics, retention behavior, and REST/GraphQL/MCP/dashboard
presentation before claiming full datastore-billing parity. Source:
`docs/ADR018-render-parity.md` usage-metering comparison, re-verified 2026-07-13.
