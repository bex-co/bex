## Objective

Implement [ADR092](../../../docs/ADR092-postgres-extension-supply.md) D1–D3:

- one bex-built CNPG operand image per major (13–18), carrying PostGIS, pg_partman, pg_duckdb, pg_ivm, pg_similarity and TimescaleDB on exactly the majors where Render offers each;
- a closed allowlist (supautils or pgextwlist, chosen by verifying the ADR's five properties on every major) that lets the database owner `CREATE EXTENSION` exactly those;
- operator-projected `shared_preload_libraries` and `timescaledb.license = 'apache'`.

This is the shared foundation for m92 (PostGIS), m104, m109, m110, m111 and m112.

## Steps

1. Choose the allowlist mechanism against ADR092 D3 properties 1–5 on PG 13–18. Record the choice and its evidence in ADR092 D3. If neither candidate satisfies them, stop and park with the exact gap.
2. Add the image Dockerfile and a publish-never-pin workflow for both architectures. Pin the digests in `cnpgExportImages` with a reviewed commit, with the export Jobs on the same image.
3. Account for the added per-backend memory of the preload libraries in plan sizing.
4. Tests: per extension, the owner creates it; a non-listed non-trusted extension fails; the owner is not superuser; `pg_dump`/restore works; the per-extension guardrails hold.
5. Do not roll production yet. The rollout is the single combined window with w8/m65 (ADR092 D4): coordinate it there, off-peak, with per-cluster health checks before and after, and a recorded rollback.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] ADR092 D3 records the chosen mechanism and its verification.
- [ ] Images published for both architectures, and digests pinned in a reviewed commit.
- [ ] The test matrix above passes on a dev cluster for every major Render covers.
