## Objective

REST, GraphQL, MCP and the dashboard agree on the settled deploy, the undeployed-change notice and the IP rule's effect, and match Render's documented behavior.

## Steps

1. For a fixed static site with a mid-build save, compare:
   - the deploy row (`GET /v1/services/<id>/deploys`);
   - the service read and its events;
   - GraphQL `service { phase undeployedChanges }`;
   - MCP `list_deploys`;
   - the dashboard's deploy page and banner.
2. Record the Render basis in ADR029 (static sites) and in ADR018's inbound IP allowlist row:
   - "Changes will not be deployed automatically" (update-service);
   - IP rules "take effect within a few seconds", answered with 403 (inbound-ip-rules).
