## Objective

Define what `egress_bytes` must include and exclude, identify the independently attributable counter for each traffic class, and reject sources whose reset, loss, or destination semantics make them unsafe for quota enforcement.

## Context

- At the audit's start, the collector queried only `traefik_service_responses_bytes_total`, so it missed shared-backend attribution, WebSocket frames, service-initiated public traffic, and public managed-datastore TCP responses.
- Render defines outbound bandwidth as responses plus service-initiated traffic to destinations outside its private network.
- Production uses Cilium 1.19.5, while the local CAPD overlay uses Calico; the accounting contract cannot silently depend on a Cilium debug surface.

## Steps

1. Define traffic categories, exclusions, byte basis, attribution keys, and failure/reset behavior.
2. Audit Traefik, cAdvisor, Hubble, and Cilium policy statistics against that contract.
3. Record the accepted hybrid architecture and the bandwidth evidence-clock consequence.
