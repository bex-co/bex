## Objective

`dashboard/src/routes/__tests__/env-groups.test.tsx` failed the deploy gate in run `35692226114`: `renames and typed-confirms deletion using the immutable group id` took 10186 ms and `moves scope and clones through server-side management verbs` took 15522 ms, both over the 10 s `testTimeout`. Find what those tests wait on and remove the slowness or the race.

## Context

- Typical causes are `findBy*`/`waitFor` polling for a condition that arrives late or only through a real timer, per-keystroke `userEvent.type` into a long typed-confirm string, or a mocked request that resolves only after a debounce.
- Compare the time these two tests take locally with their CI time to see whether they are slow everywhere or only under load.

## Steps

1. Time each test locally, both alone and in the full suite.
2. Find the dominant wait and fix it at the source (for example fake timers for a debounce, or `paste`/`fill` instead of per-character typing where the behavior under test is not the typing).
3. Run the file 20 times in a loop, with CPU contention, and record the pass count.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/routes/__tests__/env-groups.test.tsx`
- The component or hooks under test, if the wait is real product latency.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Both tests finish well under the limit locally, and the 20-run contention loop passes every time.
- [x] The tests still check the same behavior: the rename, the typed-confirm delete by immutable id, and the move/clone verbs.

## Out of scope

- The worker pool (t001).

## Outcome (2026-09-25)

**Root cause: CPU starvation, not a race.** Locally the two tests take 514 ms and 300 ms. Under 2× CPU oversubscription (56 busy loops on 28 cores) they take 2.1–2.5 s and 1.3–1.6 s. The CI 10.2 s / 15.5 s figures are the same work on a starved host (see t001: 10 runner containers with 4-CPU quotas share one 15-CPU Docker VM). Per-step timing showed the single largest cost was per-keystroke `user.type` of 42 characters (126 ms + 182 ms of the rename test's ~750 ms), each keystroke re-rendering the whole detail page. The remaining steps are Radix menu/select clicks at 20–60 ms each.

**Fix:** the rename field and the typed-confirm phrase use `user.paste` (the behaviour under test is the value, not the typing). The typed-confirm test now also asserts the button stays disabled on a partial phrase (`sudo delete env group`) before the full phrase enables it, so the gate is checked more strictly than before. Rename test: 514 → 267 ms. The rest of the starvation is addressed by t001's pool change.
