## Objective

Ensure a slow, failed, or empty event-type query produces a clear loading/error/retry experience instead of an unexplained disabled form.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/webhooks/hooks/use-webhook-event-types.ts`
- `dashboard/src/features/webhooks/components/event-picker.tsx`
- The authenticated audit observed a visible loading interval; current hook drops query errors.

## Steps

1. Surface loading, error, retry, and empty-catalog states from the hook.
2. Keep form submission safely disabled with a visible reason during unavailable states.
3. Preserve search, group, tri-state, and future-inclusive All events semantics after retry.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/features/webhooks/hooks/use-webhook-event-types.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Network failure renders a retry action and no false “zero events” state.
- [ ] Successful retry restores selection behavior without stale values.
- [ ] Loading/empty/error explanations are accessible and translated.

## Out of scope

- Hardcoding the backend event vocabulary into the dashboard.
