## Objective

Replace `patchApp`'s `client.MergeFrom` (`deploys/service.go:238-242`) with `MergeFromWithOptimisticLock` plus re-fetch-and-retry, and compute the release generation from the CR actually patched, not a stale `previousGeneration+1`. Precedent: `deployhook.go:196,240`.

## Outcome (2026-09-27) — done

`patchApp` (a plain `MergeFrom`) is gone. `openRelease` re-reads the App under the lock and patches with `MergeFromWithOptimisticLock`, re-reading and re-applying on conflict (5 attempts, then `409` "retry the deploy"). The release generation is `previous+1` from that fresh read, and the row gets `patchedGeneration(previous, patched)`.
