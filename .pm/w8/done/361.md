# A static site's ETag strength runs the opposite way to Render's: bex sends weak tags on encoded bodies and strong ones on identity, while Render sends strong on br and weak on identity

Why: w8/359 and w8/360 measured Render static sites and found that each file's `ETag` is strong (`"…"`) on a Brotli body and weak (`W/"…"`) on identity, on every site probed. Bex does the reverse: weak on an encoded body, strong on identity (`docs/ADR029-static-sites.md:66`). ADR029:68 records the difference but leaves it as is, and ADR018 does not list it among the signed-off differences (ADR018:182 covers only gzip). Under the parity rule it needs either a fix or a recorded sign-off.

- Severity: minor. Revalidation still works within each encoding. A client or tool that compares tag strength sees bex differ, as does a cache that keys on it.
- Evidence: `.pm/w8/done/360.md:33` and `:41` (four Render sites, read-only, 2026-11-20); `.pm/w8/done/359.md` (17 read-only Render requests).
- Context: filed by the `/loopx w8` orchestrator from the w8/360 worker's report. Production pin `11b1e8f85f06`; 358, 359 and 360 are parked on the next pin.

## Fix

First confirm the direction on 2–3 public Render static sites, read-only, a handful of requests:

- `Accept-Encoding: br` versus none versus `gzip`;
- whether a `304` crosses encodings with each tag.

Then pick the parity-sufficient rule:

- **Mirror Render:** strong on br, weak on identity. Keep `If-None-Match` matching across encodings, and keep the 404.html miss from w8/360 consistent.
- **Or keep bex's rule:** record why in ADR018's signed-off list and raise it through `/unblock w8` for the user's sign-off.

The code is in `lego/operator/internal/staticserver/`. It changed in w8/358 (`a1f432136`), w8/359 (`820edf28d`) and w8/360 (`67a1aa91f`), so build on those.

## Acceptance

On a free `bex-canary` static site with an `index.html` over 1 KiB and a `404.html`, after the fix is live:

- `curl -sI -H 'Accept-Encoding: br' https://<slug>.onbex.co/` gives an `ETag` of the same strength Render gives;
- `curl -sI https://<slug>.onbex.co/` with no encoding also matches Render's strength;
- `If-None-Match` with either tag still gets `304` for `/`;
- a missing path still behaves as w8/360 left it.

Then delete the fixture by ID and confirm a 404.

## Dedupe

- `rg -i 'strong on br|weak on identity' .pm` matches only w8/360's out-of-scope line.
- ADR018:182 signs off gzip ETag strength, not this direction.

Estimate: ~45m.

## Outcome

- 2026-11-21 triage: the premise held at main `19b5e31df`. `encodeBody` weakened the tag on every encoded body, and identity kept it strong.
- Render measured on 2026-11-21, 00:00 to 00:10 UTC (25 read-only requests to `sveltekit-static`, `mentorship-guide` and `buzz-docs`):
  - The tag on `/` is strong with `br` and with `gzip, br`. Its weak `W/` form goes with no `Accept-Encoding` on all three sites, with `gzip` on two, and with `HEAD`.
  - `sveltekit-static`'s `favicon.png` sends one strong tag with `br` and with no header.
  - Every `304` carries the strong tag. That holds for no header, `br` and `gzip`, with either form in `If-None-Match`, on two sites.
  - `br` with `Range: bytes=0-9` gets a `206` of the br bytes, with or without `If-Range` and the strong tag.
- Verdict: mirror Render. It is the parity-sufficient rule, so no sign-off is needed.
- Fixed in `870ffe3f9`:
  - `encodeBody` weakens a compressible file's tag, generated or authored, on every coding but br. A file with one representation keeps its tag.
  - `fileResponseWriter`, which replaces `encodedResponseWriter`, gives every 304 the strong form.
  - `If-None-Match` still matches either form in any coding. `If-Match` and `If-Range` hold on br, and fail on a compressible file's identity body. So br bytes are never spliced onto identity bytes.
  - The `404.html` miss keeps `/404.html`'s tag in the same coding (w8/360). That is strong to a br client and weak to everyone else.
  - A header-less `GET` goes through the gzip-decoding hop. It now carries the same weak tag as `HEAD` and identity, as on Render.
  - Tests: `TestETagStrengthFollowsRender`, `TestRangeResumeFollowsTheTagsStrength`, plus tag-strength checks in the encoding, conditional and 404 tests. All of them fail on the old direction. Seven fail with only the 304 restore removed.
  - Checks: `make test` and `make lint` pass. ADR029 § ETag direction and the ADR018 static-site row record the rule.
- **Seen, not judged here:**
  - Render's edge ignores `If-Match` on a static `GET` (`sveltekit-static`). A `"stale"` tag got `200` with and without br, and so did either form of the matching tag on identity. bex answers a failed `If-Match` with `412` (w4/272).
  - Render (`sveltekit-static`) ignores `Range: bytes=0-9` on a compressible file's identity body, with or without `If-Range`, and sends a `200`. bex sends a `206` when the request has no `If-Range`. A PNG gets a `206` on both.
- Parked: production's pin `820edf28d22d` predates the fix. Once the pin carries `870ffe3f9`, sign in as the QA user and, in `bex-canary`, create a free static site. Its build should write an `index.html` over 1 KiB, a `404.html` and a PNG. Then check:
  - `curl -sI -H 'Accept-Encoding: br'` on `/` gets a strong `ETag`. `HEAD` with no header, `GET` with no header and `gzip` each get its `W/` form.
  - `If-None-Match` with either form, for `br`, `gzip` and no header, gets a `304` that carries the strong tag.
  - The PNG sends one strong tag for every client.
  - A miss with `gzip, br` sends `/404.html`'s strong br tag, and a miss with no header sends its weak tag. A matching `If-None-Match` still gets the 404 page.
  - Delete the fixture by ID, confirm a 404, and close.
