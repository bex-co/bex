# Planning a Blueprint answers `500 internal error` for about a minute after one of its services is deleted

Why: right after a customer deletes a Blueprint-managed service (the journey w2/m256 t014 just fixed), `bex ea blueprints plan` and `POST /v1/blueprints/{id}/syncs` fail with an opaque 500 for over a minute, although the service already reads 404. t014 gave the **apply** a coded `409 BLUEPRINT_SERVICE_STILL_DELETING` for this window. The **plan** path got none, so the customer sees "internal error" for a state bex itself documents.

**Severity:** major (a core Blueprint journey returns an opaque 500 that looks like an outage; it recovers by itself after about 65–70 s). **Attribution:** server (bex-api Blueprint plan). **Estimate:** ~45m. **Source:** `/qa-find-bugs-cli` w8 loop sweep `bf43`, 2026-11-08, on the newly deployed production pin `2d453686f` (contains t014 `49ffa1735` and w5/m286 `fc93536e1`).

## Analysis

- t014 (`49ffa1735`) drops Apps with a `DeletionTimestamp` from the workspace snapshot and records their names in `deletingServices`. Only the apply consults it: `refuseServicesStillDeleting` (`lego/backend/internal/apps/deploy.go:836-856`) returns `409 BLUEPRINT_SERVICE_STILL_DELETING` ("…cleanup is still finishing; retry in a minute…").
- During the teardown window something on the **plan** path fails unclassified and reaches `core.WriteErr` as a 500. The window (~65–70 s) matches the operator finalizer teardown. Not the cause: `previewBlueprintHosts` (`deploy.go:1723`) returns early for a service without custom hosts.
- **Cause unverified:** look up the 14 Rndr-Ids above in the production bex-api logs for the underlying error, for example a create-time name/CR collision read, a by-name `Get` that sees the Terminating App, or a store write during plan.

## Fix

Plan must succeed during the teardown window, as Render's does: "the next time you sync your Blueprint, Render detects that a defined resource is missing and recreates it". It should return `planned` / `create <service>`, optionally with a notice that the apply will be refused until the deletion finishes. Approve during the window keeps t014's coded 409. Whatever plan step fails must either skip deleting Apps (as the snapshot already does) or classify the collision. No plan path may answer an unclassified 500 for a known state.

## Acceptance

- Unit/real-DB: a snapshot with the declared service's App Terminating → plan returns `planned` with a `create` action, and approve returns `409 BLUEPRINT_SERVICE_STILL_DELETING`.
- Live: delete a Blueprint-managed free service, then plan every 5 s → no 500 at any point; `planned create` from the first response.
