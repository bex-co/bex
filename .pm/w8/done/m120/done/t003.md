## Objective

While a release is held through its rollout and the spec has moved on, project its Deployment and write its release record from its own adoption settings.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Envtest: with gen-1 held and gen-3 saved, the applied template and record for gen-1 carry gen-1's start command, env and health path. Gen-3 rolls out with its own settings.
- [x] m117's cancel envtests, the suspend envtest and the supersede envtest stay green.

## Outcome (2026-10-19)

`heldRelease` covers a `releaseInFlight` or `survivorInFlight` release whose fingerprint no longer matches the saved spec. For such a release, `selectedRuntimeApp` / `selectedRuntime` render it from its own record through `projectRuntimeApp`, as for a Rollback, so its start command, command, health path, replicas and env come from it. `recordReleasePodTemplate` keeps the adoption record's `savedReplicas` and `settingsFingerprint` (`writeRuntimeConfigRecordStamped`). A held release adopted before this shipped has no record and keeps projecting the saved spec.

The envtest *A release held through rollout keeps its own settings* fails on the old code: A rolled with `serve B`. It passes now. In it:

- A rolls with `serve A` and `K=a`, and its record names `serve A`.
- B then rolls with its own `serve B` and `K=b`, and A's record still names `serve A`.

Known gap: `preDeployCommand` is not a record field, so a held release's pre-deploy step still runs the saved command.
