## Objective

Refresh webhook activity automatically without duplicating attempts, losing loaded pages, or polling when the view is inactive.

## Context

- `dashboard/src/features/webhooks/hooks/use-webhook-deliveries.ts`
- Existing behavior is manual Refresh over network-only pages.

## Steps

1. Poll only the newest page at a bounded cadence while Activity is visible.
2. Merge immutable IDs into loaded keyset pages without reordering older data.
3. Pause on hidden/unmounted views and retain manual Refresh.

## Files

- `dashboard/src/features/webhooks/hooks/use-webhook-deliveries.ts`
- `dashboard/src/features/webhooks/components/webhook-deliveries-card.tsx`
- `dashboard/src/features/webhooks/hooks/__tests__/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A newly appended retry or Resend appears without manual refresh.
- [ ] Polling creates no duplicate rows and preserves filters/date bounds/load-more state.
- [ ] Hidden or unmounted views stop polling.

## Out of scope

- Realtime subscriptions or SSE.
