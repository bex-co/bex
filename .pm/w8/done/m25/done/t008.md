## Objective

Prevent manual replay from becoming an authorization, amplification, or retention bypass while keeping the action auditable.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/webhooks/service.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/store/webhooks.go`
- Existing workspace rate limits and endpoint deletion cascades apply.

## Steps

1. Bound concurrent/repeated resend requests and make duplicate submissions idempotent where possible.
2. Record actor, endpoint, source event, result, and metrics without payload secrets.
3. Prove deletion, retention, auto-disable, and disabled-endpoint rules over manual attempts.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/webhooks/`
- `lego/backend/internal/store/webhooks.go`
- `lego/backend/internal/audit/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A caller cannot resend a foreign, deleted, or disabled endpoint's event.
- [ ] Concurrent duplicate requests do not fan out unbounded sends.
- [ ] Audit and metrics distinguish automatic retry from manual Resend.
- [ ] Cleanup removes all attempts without orphaning notification state.

## Out of scope

- New global rate-limit infrastructure.
