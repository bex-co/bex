## Objective

A bex-api restart mid-stream skips the deferred `stopExecution`, and the record stays `running` forever (`exe-dbpcul1pjd2c73f0qrtg`). Close such records: a record whose stream is gone reads as stopped, with `stoppedAt` and no `exitCode`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A test opens a record, abandons its stream as a restart would, and lists it stopped.

## Done — 2026-11-10

Migration `0220_sandbox_execution_heartbeat` adds `sandbox_executions.seen_at`. `keepExecutionAlive` refreshes an open record at once and every 30 s while a stream, buffered exec or file transfer runs, and stops before the record's stop. Before a history list or get, `closeStaleExecutions` ends the sandbox's open records unrefreshed past `LongResumeWait` + 2 min, at their last heartbeat (or their start, if never seen) and with no exit code. That window outlasts a minted token's resume wait. Tests: `TestAnAbandonedExecutionRecordReadsStopped` (open inside the window, stopped at its last sighting after it; the heartbeat refreshes) and `TestSandboxExecutionHeartbeatPG` (real Postgres: live stays open, seen and never-seen stop at the right instant, a stopped record never moves).
