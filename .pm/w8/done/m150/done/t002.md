## Objective

`lego/backend/internal/github/client.go`: a GET that fails with a 5xx, a stream reset/EOF or a connection error is retried with a short jittered backoff, at most twice, and never past the caller's context deadline (w5/m286's request budget). 4xx answers, 429 and non-idempotent methods are not retried.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Tests: 502 then 200 succeeds; a reset then 200 succeeds; three 503s return the last error; a 404 and a POST make one request; an expiring context stops retrying.

## Done — 2026-11-10

`retryTransport` (`lego/backend/internal/github/retry.go`) is the GitHub client's transport. A GET or HEAD without a body that fails in transit or answers 500/502/503/504 is retried up to three attempts in all. The jittered backoff is 125–375 ms, then 250–750 ms, and it stops at the request's deadline (`http.Client.Timeout`, and the caller's budget). A 4xx (429 included) and writes are never retried. `TestRetryTransportRetriesOnlyTransientReads` covers 502→200, reset→200, three 503s (last returned), 404, 429, POST, and an expiring context.
