## Objective

In `lego/backend/internal/core/base.go` `AuthorizeApp` (`:1018`, name path `:1054-1080`) and `GetApp` (`:1505-1530`): when the argument is not a `srv-` id and no workspace was explicitly named, collect every accessible candidate. Exactly one resolves. More than one returns `409` naming the ids ("service name 'x' exists in several of your workspaces: srv-…, srv-…; use the id").

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Two same-named services in two member workspaces give 409 with both ids. One gives resolution as today.

## Outcome (2026-09-26) — done

`core.Base.refuseAmbiguousName` (`lego/backend/internal/core/base.go`) runs whenever `AuthorizeApp` or `GetApp` resolves an App **by name** (direct default-workspace candidate or the cross-workspace sweep) and the request named no workspace. It lists `LabelServiceName = name` and keeps the services in other workspaces the caller can view (`checkWorkspaceAccess(can_view)`: membership + OpenFGA). More than one candidate returns `409 SERVICE_NAME_AMBIGUOUS` "service name "web" exists in several of your workspaces (srv-a, srv-b); address it by id", with `serviceIds` in params. Services the caller can't view stay invisible, a membership/authz outage fails closed, and typed ids, named workspaces, identity-less callers (git webhook) and store-off mode are untouched. `canonicalAppTarget` now shares `appPublicID`.
