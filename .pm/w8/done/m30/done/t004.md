## Objective

Make `BackupCronJobStale` and `PlatformDatabaseBackupStale` (severity: critical) reach a channel the operator actually sees. Detection worked perfectly during the 2026-08-25 incident — the alerts fired from 2026-08-23T05:33Z — and nobody noticed for two days.

## Context

- Rules live in `deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml` (`bex` group). Audit where Alertmanager currently routes (`monitoring` namespace) — the incident evidence suggests critical alerts terminate in a dashboard nobody watches.
- ADR052 is the platform's notification design (deploy + audit → email / webhooks / push); reusing its email path for platform-critical alerts is the least new machinery. A simple Alertmanager email/webhook receiver is acceptable if ADR052 wiring is disproportionate.

## Steps

1. Inventory the current Alertmanager config and receivers; document the gap.
2. Wire critical-severity alerts to the chosen human-visible channel (out-of-band credentials per repo secret conventions — never in git).
3. Fire a test alert end to end and confirm receipt.

## Files

- `deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml` (+ Alertmanager config/chart under `deploy/gitops/`)

## Progress 2026-08-25

Root cause found and fixed in the working tree: routing itself worked all along — Alertmanager sent 512 emails with zero failures — but the receiver was the never-adjusted placeholder `platform-alerts@bex.co`, and **bex.co has no MX record**, so every notification (including two days of `BackupCronJobStale`) was accepted by SendGrid and delivered nowhere. `deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml` now targets the operator inbox. Remaining after `/ship` + Argo sync: the still-firing kvbak alerts re-notify on the 4h `repeat_interval` — receipt of that email in the operator inbox is the live proof (no synthetic alert needed while the real one fires).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A critical alert demonstrably reaches the operator (the live BackupCronJobStale re-notification after sync, or a synthetic one; receipt noted in the milestone record)
- [ ] Config contains no credential bytes in git
