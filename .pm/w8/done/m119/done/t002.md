## Objective

Implement t001's operator change so the release that rolls out under the run-to-completion pin is recorded in `status.activeRevision` (or the chosen served stamp) before the backend can see a newer release generation as current.

## Context

- `release_identity.go:370` `buildRunning`, `:400` `prepareAppReleaseDecision`.
- `app_controller.go:2506` (`applyServingDeployment`), `:2963` `reportKubernetesRunning`, `:3043` `markRunning`.
- `build_supersede_envtest_test.go:99` currently asserts immediate adoption of gen-3 after gen-1's build, without a served assertion for gen-1.

## Steps

1. Apply the chosen mechanism (pin through rollout, or stamp the pinned revision served once its Deployment completes).
2. Handle a pinned rollout that fails or exceeds its progress deadline: the pending generation must still proceed, and the failed row must read `update_failed`, not `canceled`.
3. Keep prebuilt-image and direct-static-publish Apps unpinned, as today.
4. Update the envtest to drive gen-1's Deployment to available and assert the served record before gen-3 counts as the release.

## Out of scope

## Outcome (2026-10-19)

`buildRunning` is now `releaseInFlight` (`release_identity.go`). It pins a pinnable source release while it is `Building`. It also pins while it is `Deploying` and has not served yet (`releaseGeneration > successfulReleaseGeneration`). It never pins once the release's own deploy is canceled.

A failed rollout settles `Failed`, or `Running` over the prior release, which releases the pin. Its durable `ConditionRollout` carries the verdict to the row (t003). Prebuilt-image, suspended and direct-static-publish Apps stay unpinned through `pinnableSourceRelease`.

Tests:

- `TestPendingReleaseWaitsUntilTheReleaseInFlightServes` covers nine states. Before the change, the two rolling-out cases failed.
- The supersede envtest now drives gen-1's Deployment ready. It asserts `rev-1` before gen-3 counts and `rev-1` while gen-3 builds. It fails on the old code ("a rollout in flight keeps the release pinned") and passes now, alongside m117's six envtests.
