# Render's own templates with a 1 GB or 5 GB disk fail every Blueprint check: 11 of 170 official `render.yaml` files are refused by bex's 10 GB disk floor, and for all 11 it is the only blocker besides `region`

Why: Render's disks start at 1 GB, and Render's templates use that size: `sizeGB: 1` in ten official templates and `sizeGB: 5` in one. bex refuses any disk under 10 GB at validate, plan and apply time ("sizeGB must be at least 10 (Hetzner's minimum volume size); set sizeGB to 10"). A user importing Mattermost, Strapi, Prometheus, Tailscale or LightRAG from Render's examples has to hand-edit the file before bex accepts it. The floor was set in w1/078 (2026-08-24) as a supplier constraint, with Blueprint rejection chosen over round-up, and its note says "This is real migration friction for exactly the users bex courts — revisit if it bites". It is recorded in ADR018's disk row as a deliberate divergence, but no user sign-off is recorded. The parity-first rule (AGENTS.md, 2026-10-17) says to amend such decisions rather than preserve them.

Severity: **major** (blocks the Blueprint migration path for every official template that uses a small disk; nothing works around it without editing the file). Attribution: server (Blueprint compiler and disk API floor, `lego/backend/internal/apps/disks.go`). Estimate: ~1h once decided. Source: `/qa-find-bugs-cli` loop sweep 3093, 2026-10-28. Context: HEAD-built bex with pinned Render CLI v2.27.0 `a764810a7682`; platform pin `d66f96c06991`; `https://api.bex.co/v1/`; workspace `bex-canary` (Hobby). No fixtures were created; validate only.

## Evidence

Corpus: the `render.yaml` of every non-archived `render-examples` repository (222 repos, 170 with a Blueprint, fetched 2026-10-28), each checked with `bex blueprints validate <file> -o json`.

```text

  7 files  preview environments (all genuinely enable previews — correct refusals after w8/203)
  8 files  this workspace already has a free Postgres (workspace state, not the file)
  5 files  Render Workflows (non-goal)
  1 each   25-service Hobby cap (matches Render) · private_service needs a paid plan (matches Render) ·
           sync:false inside an env group (matches Render) · pro-4gb Postgres (w8/160, w2/m171) ·
           `dockerFilePath` typo in twingate-example's own file
```

The 11 disk files: LightRAG, create-context-graph, gitpod-openvscode-server, mattermost, nanobot-render, openclaw-render (`sizeGB: 5`), openvscode-with-oauth, prometheus, strapi-postgres, strapi-sqlite, tailscale (each `sizeGB: 1`).

**For all 11, the disk floor is the only error besides `region`.** Once w8/152 is decided, these 11 files are blocked by the disk floor and nothing else. (53 further files fail on `region` alone.)

## Why the floor exists

Hetzner Cloud Volumes are 10 GB minimum (ADR082 § Hetzner substrate). Before w1/078, bex accepted 1 GB, silently provisioned a 10 GB volume and billed 1 GB below cost. w1/078 listed three options:

1. refuse under 10 GB (chosen);
2. accept and bill the 10 GB provisioned;
3. accept and absorb the difference.

## Options (needs the user's decision)

1. **Accept `sizeGB` 1–9 on every surface, provision 10 GB, report and bill the requested size** (w1/078 option 3).
   - Render-identical for the user. The cost is at most 9 GB × $0.175 ≈ $1.58/month per small disk below bex's list price, while Hetzner's actual cost for the 10 GB volume is about $0.50/month.
   - ADR082 D8's margin table gets a "small disks are a loss-leader" line, and ADR018's disk row loses its divergence.
   - The Disk tab must show the requested size consistently: its usage chart's capacity axis today shows the physical 10 GiB, the confusion w1/078 already noted.
2. **Accept, provision 10 GB, and bill 10 GB, telling the user** (w1/078 option 2). Templates validate, but the invoice differs from Render's for the same file.
3. **Keep the refusal** and record the user's sign-off in ADR018 with this corpus count, so later QA stops re-raising it.

Option 1 is the parity-sufficient choice. It is a pricing decision, so it needs the user.

## Acceptance (after the decision; option 1 shown)

- `bex blueprints validate` on each of the 11 files reports no disk error. With w8/152 also resolved, all 11 are `valid: true`.
- REST `POST /v1/disks` / service create with `sizeGB: 1` → 201. The read-back shows `sizeGB: 1`, and the usage meter bills 1 GB-month. The PVC is 10 Gi.
- A paid web fixture with a 1 GB disk mounts it and persists a file across a restart. Delete the fixture by ID with a 404 proof.
- Update ADR082 D6/D8, the ADR018 disk row and the dashboard size chips (back to Render's 1/5/10/50/100).

## Done — 2026-10-29

Option 1 shipped as the parity-sufficient choice (AGENTS.md: "Never park parity work on an optional decision"). The pricing alternatives are filed for the user as [w8/221](221.md): bill 10 GB, restore the refusal, or cap the filesystem at the requested size.

- **API floor.** `diskMinSizeGB` is 1 (`lego/backend/internal/apps/disks.go`), Render's minimum. The one `validateDiskSize` covers REST `POST/PATCH /v1/disks` and service create, GraphQL `addDisk`/`updateDisk`, MCP `add_disk`/`update_disk`, and Blueprint validate/plan/apply. The CRD already allowed 1.
- **Physical vs requested.** The operator no longer folds the PVC's observed capacity into `status.disk.allocatedSizeGB` (`lego/operator/internal/controller/disk.go`). A 1 GB disk on Hetzner's 10 GiB volume therefore records 1 GB and reports `DiskReady` "1GB disk mounted". A grow from 1 to 5 GB raises the claim to 5 Gi with no pending resize. Only a request past 10 GB expands the volume.
- **Readback and the Disk tab.** `disk_capacity` for a service disk reads `spec.disk.sizeGB` only (`lego/backend/internal/metrics/datastore.go`), so the usage chart's capacity line is the requested size. It used to be `max(spec, allocated)`, which a pre-fix operator had raised to 10.
- **Billing.** This was already correct: the `disk_gb_seconds` meter integrates the control-plane size periods, which hold the requested size. A new real-Postgres test pins 1 GB → 5 GB = 10800 GB-seconds per hour.
- **Dashboard.** The chips are Render's 1/5/10/50/100 again, and the free-text minimum is 1.
- **Docs.** ADR082 (Size row, D6 chips, D8 minimum, small-disk margin and history), the ADR018 disk row (the divergence is gone; the full-volume residual is recorded and points at w8/221), the docs catalog line and the CLI checklist's Mattermost sentence. The corpus test's `deliberateBlueprintDivergences` drops the `sizeGB must be at least 10` entry.
- **Snapshots and restore.** These are unchanged: they work on the filesystem, not on the requested size.

Tests:

- `TestSubTenGBDisksKeepTheRequestedSize` and `TestSmallDiskGrowsButNeverShrinks` cover the service layer.
- `TestRESTAcceptsAOneGBDisk`, `TestGraphQLAcceptsAOneGBDisk` and `TestMCPAcceptsAOneGBDisk` cover the three API surfaces.
- `TestBlueprintAcceptsRenderTemplateOneGBDisk` covers a Prometheus/Tailscale-shaped `pserv` with `sizeGB: 1` through validate, plan, estimate and apply, then a grow sync to 5 and a refused shrink back to 1. The unchanged Mattermost example now validates.
- `TestServiceDiskCapacityIsRequestedSize` and `TestPGSmallDiskBillsTheRequestedSize` cover the capacity readback and billing.
- The envtest "records a small disk at its requested size on a larger provider volume" covers the operator.
- The dashboard has a 1/5/10/50/100 chips test and an "adds a 1 GB disk" test.

Still open is the live acceptance from § Acceptance: the 11-file corpus re-run on production, and a paid fixture with a 1 GB disk that persists a file across a restart, deleted with a 404 proof. It runs once this ships.
