## Objective

`TestSwitchedServiceReadsAsAnImageCreatedOne` excludes `port` and `openPorts`, and its comment calls the switched service's 10000 a gap. Before the switched source is deployed, 10000 is the port the native release still serves. After the deploy, detection finds the image's port, and the read should then match an image-created service's. Make the test say that.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/apps/native_image_switch_test.go`: `m461Switch`, `m461ComparableRead`, and `TestSwitchedServiceReadsAsAnImageCreatedOne`.
- The read path: `App.ServingPort`, `OpenPorts` and `ResolvedServicePort` in `lego/types/v1alpha1/image_network.go`. The image-created service reads its network's ports.

## Steps

1. Before the deploy, assert that the switched read is `port: 10000` and `openPorts: [{10000, TCP}]`, and fix the comment's rationale.
2. Simulate the deploy on both services. The switched one gets `status.detectedPort: 80` and a serving release. The image-created one gets a serving network `[80]` with primary 80. Then diff the full reads with no port exclusion, on REST, and on GraphQL and MCP where those read the port.
3. Add a private-service variant: a switched private service with t001's detected 6379 against an image-created private service whose network is `[6379]`.
