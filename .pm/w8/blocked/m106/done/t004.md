## Outcome (2026-10-17)

Done. `/simplify` ran its reuse, quality and efficiency reviews. The efficiency review found that the first design, a curl-and-verify `RUN`, produced a new ~18 MB layer per app and cold build, and refetched whenever the base changed. It was replaced with the FFmpeg pattern:
- per-architecture `ADD --checksum` helper stages selected by `TARGETARCH`;
- the pinned busybox unpacks only the binary;
- a `COPY --link` to `/hugo`, byte-identical on every base.

Other fixes:
- `rewriteNativeBase` now requires its one default-image `FROM`.
- The tests reuse the `git`/`buildResolvedImage` helpers and a shared `sourceTree` walker, which the Jekyll fixture also uses.
- The freshness validator checks each kind's upstream host.
- A stale stage-number comment was corrected.

The quality review found no correctness issue in the final design.
