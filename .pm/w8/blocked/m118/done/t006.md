## Objective

A service with its default platform subdomain and a verified custom domain serves trusted HTTPS on both even when issuance for the platform hostname is pending or rate-limited. The ready custom certificate must load independently of an unrelated missing TLS Secret.

## Context

- [Live Cloudflare reproduction and cleanup](../evidence/cf-20261027/README.md), 2026-10-27 UTC. Custom HTTP-01 issuance succeeds, yet normal HTTPS fails with the platform wildcard certificate. Disabling only the owned fixture's platform subdomain makes trusted custom HTTPS and the redirect/403 controls pass.
- `lego/operator/internal/controller/app_controller.go` `applyAppIngress` / `appendIngressHostRoute` put the platform TLS Secret first in one shared Ingress.
- Deployed Traefik v3.7.5 [getCertificates](https://github.com/traefik/traefik/blob/v3.7.5/pkg/provider/kubernetes/ingress/kubernetes.go#L741) stops at the first missing Secret. The `onbex.co` Certificate is rate-limited; the later custom certificate is Ready.
- w2/done/m85 already supplies production's wildcard fallback. No new DNS credentials or product decision are needed; the user's existing Cloudflare CLI can provision isolated temporary QA hostnames.

## Steps

1. Make the platform wildcard fallback and ready per-host certificates independent of pending per-App certificate references. Choose the smallest mechanism that works with the pinned Traefik and preserves self-hosted clusters without a wildcard fallback. Do not rely on waiting out the issuance limit, TLS verification bypass, or disabling a customer's platform hostname.
2. Verify isolation in both directions: pending platform plus ready custom; ready platform plus pending custom; two custom names with one pending. Preserve stable certificate naming/history, domain removal/deletion, redirect middleware, activator/static routes and log/metric attribution.
3. Add a regression that exercises the pinned provider's certificate-loading behavior and fails on the captured mixed-host configuration, then rerun the standing checks.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] With the platform subdomain enabled and its per-App TLS Secret absent, a ready custom domain serves its own trusted certificate and HTTPS 200; platform HTTPS remains valid through the configured fallback.
- [x] A pending custom certificate does not stop other ready host certificates from loading; non-wildcard self-hosted clusters retain their issuance path.
- [x] Fresh HTTP-01 issuance, permanent path/query-preserving redirects, HTTPS bodies, inbound 403, certificate cleanup and attribution still work.
- [x] A maintained pinned-Traefik regression replays the original failing projection and passes with ready certificates first. The production Cloudflare replay remains in t005 after deployment.

## Out of scope

- Increasing provider issuance limits, changing workspace plans, or changing existing tenant domains.

## Implementation and verification — 2026-10-28

Implemented `prioritizeIngressTLS` for serving and host-redirect Ingresses: usable references precede absent/incomplete references; all references and their original Secret names remain. Reads use the existing uncached tenant client. The shared Kubernetes path caps multi-host rechecks at 30 seconds, including direct-published static sites and sleeping services. Routing rules and middleware chains are unchanged.

`make test`, `make lint` across all four Go modules plus deadcode, the focused lifecycle tests, and `bash scripts/verify-ingress-tls.sh` pass. The real Traefik test reproduces the pre-fix certificate mismatch and verifies trusted HTTPS plus unchanged redirects for platform-pending, custom-pending, and two-custom cases. [Verification evidence](../evidence/provider-20261028/README.md). Production rollout and owned Cloudflare replay are explicitly retained in t005; no deployed-fix claim is made here.
