## Objective

Persist the release's digest on the deploy row that went live with it, and on no other row.

## Context

- `lego/backend/internal/store/reconciler.go:892–903` backfills `ResolvedImage` only on genuine live convergence (`matchesObservedRelease` / `servesGeneration`). The digest follows the same gate.
- `store.Deploy` (`lego/backend/internal/store/store.go:285–330`) and its scan list (`:2330`).

## Steps

1. Add a nullable `image_sha` column with a migration, plus the `Deploy.ImageSHA` field and the scan/insert changes.
2. Set it beside `ResolvedImage` from the App status digest when it belongs to the row's generation.
3. Rollback rows: copy the target's digest only if the operator reports that same digest when the rollback goes live. Never copy it blindly at open time.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/store/store.go`, `reconciler.go`, migrations

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A live close stores the matching generation's digest. A failed, canceled or superseded close stores none.
- [ ] Two consecutive live deploys keep distinct per-row digests.

## Out of scope

- Backfilling historical rows.
