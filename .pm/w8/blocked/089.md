## Concrete correction and acceptance

Add a platform-owned Traefik `RedirectRegex` middleware in `auth`, attached to Hydra's public ingress through `traefik.ingress.kubernetes.io/router.middlewares: auth-cli-dashboard-redirect@kubernetescrd`. Match only the supported dashboard prefixes and preserve the complete remaining path/query:

```yaml
redirectRegex:
  regex: "^https://oauth[.]bex[.]co/(web|worker|pserv|static|cron|d|r)/(.+)$"
  replacement: "https://dashboard.bex.co/${1}/${2}"
  permanent: false
```

Deliver the Middleware through a small GitOps chart/Application (`deploy/gitops/charts/cli-dashboard-links/`, `deploy/gitops/base/cli-dashboard-links.yaml`, base `kustomization.yaml`), ordered after the Traefik CRD installation and before attaching the ingress annotation in `base/values/hydra.values.yaml`. Render the production and local overlays; local development should keep its existing port-forwarded device URLs. This repairs configurations already carrying the OAuth origin as well as future equivalent configurations. The existing issuer and device-verification routes continue through their current handlers.

[Official RedirectRegex documentation](https://doc.traefik.io/traefik/reference/routing-configuration/http/middlewares/redirectregex/) supports domain replacement while retaining captured URL suffixes. The repository's pinned Traefik v3.7.5 [redirect implementation](https://github.com/traefik/traefik/blob/v3.7.5/pkg/middlewares/redirect/redirect.go#L87) uses302 for a non-permanent GET redirect. The [29-case local Go matrix](../evidence/089/routing-matrix.json) confirms twelve actual client URLs, their query/fragment variants, and five unaffected neighboring routes against the proposed expression. [Probe source](../evidence/089/routing-probe.go.txt). This is a regex check; the Middleware and ingress attachment have not been deployed or exercised.
