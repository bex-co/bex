## Objective

Switch `lineCap.admit` (`lego/backend/internal/logs/linecap.go`) from float tokens advanced by elapsed seconds to t001's rule: whole tokens on a grid of `1/appLineRate` steps fixed to the Unix epoch, capped at `appLineBurst`. Then the tail, its warm-up (`capWarmUp`) and the store-less read (`capWarmUpLines`) make the same per-line decision as the patched shipper.

## Context

- `admit` today: `tokens = min(burst, tokens + elapsed*rate)` on `float64`, starting full (`linecap.go`). The warm-up from `a8ef77ce0` stays. With whole tokens it now also brings the bucket into step with the shipper's, not just to the same level.
- An unstamped line (the rest of a split line) is still counted at the last line's step.

## Steps

1. Add `lego/backend/internal/logs/testdata/linecap-shared.json`: timestamped lines (a flood in 20-line bursts every 0.1 s, a quiet spell, out-of-order stderr stamps, equal stamps) with the admitted set expected. t001's Alloy test reads the same file.
2. Rework `admit` and keep `capWarmUp` and `capWarmUpLines`.
3. Tests: the table; two buckets that start at different levels agree from the point a flood drains both; a quiet pod still passes its 6,000-line burst; `TestATailOpenedMidFloodShowsWhatHistoryKeeps` now compares line identity, not only the count.
