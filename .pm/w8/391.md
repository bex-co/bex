# A deploy that runs out its 15-minute budget with no open port closes its log with `==> Deploy failed: …`, where Render's closes with `==> Timed Out`

Why: w8/389 matched Render's port-scan cadence. The closing line is the last difference on that path, ADR018 records it as an open gap, and no user sign-off lets it stand.

- Severity: minor. Only the wording differs: the deploy fails at the same budget, and bex's line names the cause.
- Render, from deploy logs quoted in GitHub issues: vincentbellemare90-commits/Mon-bot#1 (2026-09-18) ends `==> Timed Out`, and wifihaven/wifihaven#1194 (2026-11) logs `Timed Out` at 00:47:46, about 15 minutes after the process started. rkendel1/freq#97 (2026-01-12) ends `==> Timed out: …` nine minutes after the timeout line. w6/m148 quotes an archived `Timed out: Port scan timeout reached, …`. First confirm, from a 2026 log, whether Render's line carries text after it and what follows.
- bex: `terminalLines` (`lego/backend/internal/logs/progress.go`) writes `==> Deploy failed: <failureReason>` for every `update_failed` deploy. For the budget failure the reason begins "the container is running but its startup health check never succeeded within the rollout window, so the deploy failed: …" (w8/259).
- Dedupe: w8/385 and w8/388 cover the scan lines, w8/389 the cadence and the timeout pair, w8/259 the failure reason's wording. None covers the closing line.

## Fix

For an `update_failed` deploy that ran out its rollout budget, write Render's closing line, and keep bex's failure reason on its own line if Render prints none. REST `/v1/logs`, the subscribe stream, GraphQL `logs`, MCP and the dashboard's deploy log panel share the synthesis. Leave the `deploy_ended` event's `failureReason` as it is.

## Acceptance

- A unit test: a deploy that failed at the budget closes with Render's line, and other failures keep `==> Deploy failed`.
- Live in `bex-canary`, on a free `busybox:1.36` web service with `dockerCommand` `sleep 3600` run to its 15-minute budget: `bex logs --type build` ends with Render's line. Delete the fixture by ID and confirm the 404.
