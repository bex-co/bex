## Objective

Preserve ownership through failure and disconnect, so that concurrent and partially failed Blueprint syncs preserve one authorized resource owner.

## Context

- Parent: [w8/m40](README.md), approved 2026-09-09 brainstorm proposal 2.
- m23 checks ownership before apply and stamps labels best-effort only after the entire stack succeeds. Partial apply can leave changed resources unclaimed; two different Blueprints can pass the check concurrently. m37 coordinates one Blueprint, not competing resource owners. This is corrective coverage for the shipped ownership guarantee.
- Read cascading workspace/component instructions before implementation. Use isolated dev-8 for live checks; see [workstream instructions](../README.md).

## Steps

1. Define safe retention/release for claims on partial apply, failed create, retry, and disconnect.
2. Keep explicit takeover conditional on the owner the caller confirmed; preserve resources on disconnect.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Partial application retains truthful ownership of completed writes.
- [ ] Retries are safe and stale cleanup cannot clear a successor claim.
- [ ] A takeover confirmation cannot authorize transfer from a different intervening owner.

## Out of scope

No automatic takeover, sync-delete, new resource families, or ownership redesign for ordinary non-Blueprint operations beyond the enforcement needed for this guarantee.
