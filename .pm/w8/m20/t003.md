## Objective

Grouping creates/updates emit audit events (actor, workspace, blueprint id, grouping names) like every other mutating verb, and the per-grouping OpenFGA ACL write becomes conditional — only written when the grouping is actually new/changed, not unconditionally re-written on every sync.

## Context

- Round-7 triage: "grouping writes bypass the audit events" and "an unconditional per-grouping ACL write".
- Audit emission pattern: existing `audit_events` writes in the blueprint verbs (`docs/ADR006-bex-api.md` § Audit log); retention already handled by `BEX_AUDIT_RETENTION_DAYS`.

## Steps

1. Emit audit events from the grouping apply path (post-commit, consistent with other verbs).
2. Diff-detect groupings so unchanged ones skip the ACL write.
3. Tests: sync with N new groupings ⇒ N audit events; re-sync unchanged ⇒ zero new ACL writes and zero spurious audit events.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/apps/blueprint.go`
- audit emission helpers in `lego/backend/internal/apps/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Grouping mutations visible in the audit log with actor + target.
- [ ] Idempotent re-sync produces no ACL churn.

## Out of scope

- Audit backfill for historical groupings.
