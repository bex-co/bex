## Objective

Check that every surface that restarts or rolls back a service gets the fixed outcome. No field changes, so this is a walk of the entry points.

## Steps

1. Confirm REST `POST /v1/services/{id}/restart` and `/rollback`, GraphQL `restartServer` and rollback, MCP's restart and rollback tools, the CLI's `bex restart`, and the dashboard's Restart and Rollback buttons all reach `deploys.Service.Restart` or `Rollback`. A surface that does not gets the same selection.
2. Compare with Render's documented Restart ("the exact same Git commit and configuration as the running instance") and rollback (`render.com/docs/rollbacks`). File any drift you find as its own item rather than fixing it here.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each surface is listed in the README's results with its entry point, and no surface pulls a selected release with the saved binding.
