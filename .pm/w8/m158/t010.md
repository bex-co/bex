## Objective

Every exit-caused `server_failed` is closed by exactly one `server_available` once the service is Ready again, dated at that Ready transition. This holds whether or not bex-api's sampler ever confirmed an outage, so the Events tab, webhooks and alert emails show the failure and the recovery as a pair, the way a sampled outage already does.

## Context

- **Found:** QA loop round 186 replaying t009's acceptance criteria on pin `a5be11e4e517`, which carries t009's `3dd698375`, 2026-11-12 10:13–10:28 UTC. See the README's round 186 probe.
- **Fixture:** Free web service `qa-20261112-r186-crash` (`srv-dbqp1mi8vjhs73do98a0`), the round 176 `node -e` server on `node:22-alpine` with Health Check Path `/health`. It was deleted afterwards (DELETE 204, then GET 404).
- **Probe:** `curl /exit3` three times, each on a fresh instance after `POST …/restart`.

  | Run | Exit | Back at 200 | `server_failed` | `server_available` |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 1 | 10:13:41 | 10:13:52 | 10:13:42 `{"instanceID":"…-77ddff87dd-fk2tm","reason":{"nonZeroExit":3}}` | **none**, still none at 10:15:45 |
  | 2 | 10:17:14 | 10:17:35 | 10:17:14 `{"instanceID":"…-84c9f9dc94-628wx","reason":{"nonZeroExit":3}}` | **none** (read at 10:18:48) |
  | 3 | 10:20:44 | 10:21:05 | 10:20:44 `{"instanceID":"…-69799766d-5c5m9","reason":{"nonZeroExit":3}}` | **none** (read at 10:27:57) |

  - t009's duplicate is gone: each run has exactly one `server_failed`.
  - On the Events tab each run is an "Instance failed … exited with status 3" with no "Service recovered" after it.
- **Controls that pair:**
  - an OOM kill at 10:22:22, down until about 10:23:17, recorded `server_failed` `oomKilled` (`memoryLimit: 512Mi`) at 10:22:40 and `server_available` at 10:23:29;
  - `/sick` 75 s after that recovery recorded `server_failed` `unhealthy` at 10:24:53 and `server_available` at 10:27:35.
  - Both outages lasted long enough for the sampler to confirm them.
- **Mechanism (stated in the code):** t009's comment in `lego/backend/internal/store/event_facts.go:628-640` says "a crash that restarted before the sampler confirmed an outage leaves no server_available".
  - `server_available` is an edge of the sampled availability checkpoint (`observedStateFacts`, `heldAvailability`).
  - An exit fact (`instanceExitFacts`, `insertInstanceExitFactSQL`) is inserted beside the checkpoint and never moves it.
  - A crash shorter than the sampler's two-pass confirmation therefore leaves the checkpoint `healthy`, and no recovery edge ever comes.
  - Before t009 such a crash recorded a second, sampled `server_failed` that brought its own `server_available` (round 176, run 1: 07:34:03).
  - The target in t009's acceptance criteria, "exactly one `server_failed` … and one `server_available`", is therefore half met.

## Steps

1. When an exit-caused `server_failed` is recorded and the checkpoint did not see the outage, record the outage on the checkpoint too: availability `unhealthy` as of the exit's `at`. The next healthy observation then emits the one `server_available`, dated at the Ready transition after the exit.
   - Mind the case where the healthy observation was already recorded before the exit fact arrived. The exit is then read late, so emit the `server_available` at that Ready time directly, or on the next pass.
2. Keep t009's guarantees:
   - no second `server_failed`;
   - a separate outage after a recovered crash records its own pair;
   - a crash loop records one pair, not one per restart (`afterUnready`).
3. **Tests** in the store's `TestPGAnInstanceExitIsTheOutagesOnlyServerFailed`:
   - a crash whose outage was never sampled → one `server_failed` and one `server_available` after it;
   - an exit read after the healthy pass → the same;
   - the existing subtests stay green.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On production, with the fixture above, `/exit3` on three fresh instances gives each exactly one `server_failed` (`nonZeroExit: 3`) and one `server_available` within a minute of the service answering 200 again.
- [ ] The OOM and `/sick` controls above still give one pair each.
