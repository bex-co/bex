# A static site's text bodies under 1 KiB, and the bare 404, go out uncompressed to a `br` client, where Render Brotli-encodes them whatever their size

Why: Render's static sites answer a client that accepts `br` with `content-encoding: br` and `vary: Accept-Encoding` even on a 14-byte body, but bex serves identity with no `Vary` below 1 KiB, so a site moved from Render changes its encoding headers on its small files.

- Severity: minor. Every client still gets correct bytes. What differs is `Content-Encoding`, `Content-Length` and `Vary` on small text files (`robots.txt`, `version.json`, small manifests) and on the bare 404.
- Source: split out of w8/358 (cli-qa run `r17-0f97`) by its `/loopx w8` worker on 2026-11-20, which fixed the `Cache-Control` default and left this difference out because it is a separate mechanism.

## Repro

```text
# Render, 2026-11-20, Accept-Encoding: gzip, br
sveltekit-static.onrender.com /robots.txt          200  62 B   content-encoding: br, vary: Accept-Encoding
sveltekit-static.onrender.com /_app/version.json   200  27 B   content-encoding: br (29 B on the wire), vary: Accept-Encoding
sveltekit-static.onrender.com /qa-nope-x           404  14 B   bare "Not Found", content-encoding: br, vary: Accept-Encoding
```

The br body of `version.json` is larger than the identity body, so Render applies neither a size floor nor a savings test. bex, from the code at `81a9c93c8`: identity with no `Vary` for any object under 1 KiB, and identity for the bare 404.

## Cause

- `minBrotliBytes = 1024` (`lego/operator/internal/staticserver/encoding.go:46`) gates `encodingCandidate` (`:93`), which also decides `Vary`.
- `encodeBrotli` and `encodeGzip` (`:242`, `:258`) refuse an encoding that does not save 1/16 of the size, so a 14-byte body stays identity even without the floor.
- `serveBareNotFound` (`staticserver.go`) answers through `http.Error`, which never reaches the encoder.

## Fix sketch

For a client that accepts `br`, Brotli-encode every compressible-type body, the bare 404 included, with no size floor or savings test. Keep the 8 MiB ceiling, the encode slots and the variant cache. Probe gzip-only clients first: on 2026-11-20 Render gzipped `/robots.txt` but served `/_app/version.json` and two `404.html` pages identity, so its gzip rule is not settled. Update ADR029 and the encoding tests.

## Acceptance

- On a free static site, a `br` client gets `content-encoding: br` and `vary: Accept-Encoding` on a 62-byte `robots.txt`, a 27-byte JSON file and a bare 404.
- An identity client gets the identity bytes, with `vary: Accept-Encoding`.
- The ETag rules of w4/341 still hold: weak on the br variant, strong on identity, and a 304 across both.
