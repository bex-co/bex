# A web service with no reachable port prints nothing in its deploy log for the whole 15-minute port scan, where Render's log repeats `==> No open ports detected, continuing to scan...`

Why: cli-qa routine run 30 (sweep r29, 2026-11-21 ~21:20–21:40 UTC, production pin `7e481dece7ac`, bex v0.9.0) created free `busybox:1.36` web services that listen only on `127.0.0.1:$PORT`, or on nothing at all. The deploy log printed `==> Deploy queued` and `==> Deploying image …`, then nothing for 10 minutes (fixture `srv-dc10n0e0ri4c73c4017g`, still `update_in_progress`). bex's diagnosis of the stall appears only in the API's `stallReason`, not in the log a user reads with `bex logs --type build`.

- Severity: minor. The deploy still fails at the 15-minute budget with Render's port-scan-timeout reason (w8/259). But for those 15 minutes the log gives no sign of what is wrong, and Render's log does.
- Attribution: server, `lego/backend/internal/logs/progress.go`. `progressLines` writes lines only for the queued, started and finished states.
- Dedupe: w8/177 (done) fixed port detection, and w8/259 (done) fixed the final failure wording. Both quote Render's in-progress line only from community threads (w8/259 line 66). w8/384 is the health-check advice on the same no-listener deploy. No item covers the in-progress narration.

## Render

- Render's in-progress line, `==> No open ports detected, continuing to scan...`, is quoted in w8/177 from community threads. community.render.com now redirects to render.discourse.group, which did not resolve during the run, so the wording was not re-checked.
- First step: confirm the exact line and how often it repeats, from a reachable source: Render docs, an archived community thread, or a public build log in an issue or blog post. Cite it here.

## Fix

While a deploy has a no-open-port stall, the deploy's log stream carries a periodic `==>` scan line with Render's confirmed wording, then the final timeout line. Reuse the stall state the operator already reports (`stallReason`, the `DeployReasonHTTPPortNoneYet` family in `lego/types/v1alpha1/deploy_reasons.go`). Match Render's cadence, and never write more than one line a minute (no log floods). Check REST `/v1/logs`, the subscribe stream, GraphQL `logs` and the dashboard's deploy log panel.

- A unit test: a deploy stalled with no open port yields the scan line at the chosen cadence, and stops when the port appears or the deploy ends.
- Live in `bex-canary`, on a free `busybox:1.36` web service listening only on `127.0.0.1`: within about 2 minutes, `bex logs --type build` for that deploy shows Render's scan line. Delete the fixture by ID and confirm the 404.
