# 044 — Log lines are ordered by their timestamp *string*, so a whole-second or trailing-zero stamp sorts out of place

Why: every log read sorts and groups entries by `LogEntry.Timestamp` as an RFC3339Nano string. RFC3339Nano trims trailing zeros, so `…:28Z` sorts after `…:28.5Z`, and `…:28.92Z` (0.920 s) sorts after `…:28.922Z`. Lines then come back out of order. `capToLimit` and `pageCursors` take the page's oldest/newest from the wrong line, so a page can skip or repeat lines.

Severity: minor (ordering and paging correctness on millisecond- or second-precision sources). Attribution: server (`lego/backend/internal/logs`, `internal/datastorelogs`).

## Evidence (2026-10-03, found while fixing w8/043)

`TestLokiEndIsInclusiveAndPagesStayGapFree` (`internal/logs/loki_inclusive_end_test.go`) with a fixture starting at a whole second (`2026-10-03T11:34:28Z`, stamps 7 ms apart) returned the right 75 lines for `--end <t75>`, but the "last" line was `2026-10-03T11:34:28Z`, the oldest one: `"28Z" > "28.518000962Z"` as strings because `'Z' (0x5A) > '.' (0x2E)`. The test now starts at `.100000001` to stay on w8/043's subject.

## Mechanism

String comparisons of `Timestamp`: `logs/loki.go:206` (`sortedEntries`), `loki.go:581`, `render.go:168`, `service.go:419,426` (`capToLimit` boundary group by string equality, which is fine), `service.go:587,1437,1466,1562`, `progress.go:404`, `datastorelogs/logs.go:127`. Entries are formatted with `time.RFC3339Nano` (`loki.go:575` and the pod path).

## Fix

- Order by parsed time: one `logTimestampLess(a, b string) bool` (parse RFC3339Nano, falling back to string order for an unparseable stamp), used at every sort site in both packages. Keep the wire format unchanged.
- Test: whole-second and trailing-zero stamps (`…28Z`, `…28.92Z`, `…28.922Z`) sort chronologically, and the w8/043 paging test passes with a whole-second fixture.

Estimate: ~30m.
