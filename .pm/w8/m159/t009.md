## Objective

A `type=build` live tail (SSE, NDJSON or WebSocket) delivers the deploy's platform narration as the deploy row reaches each step, so its lines arrive in the order the window's history lists them:

- `==> Building from <repo>@<sha>` before `==> Running build command …`, never after `==> Build successful` or after the command's error line;
- the closing verdict line (`==> Build failed: …`, or the success line the history read ends with) before the stream ends, instead of the stream ending first.

## Context

- **Found:** QA loop round 195, 2026-11-12 12:58–13:03 UTC, pin `9a5b95e96210` (which carries t008's `d22cac15a`). The fixture was the Free Node web service `qa-20261112-r195-node` (`srv-dbqrdlp4nu2c739q1u50`, `render-examples/express-hello-world`), deleted afterwards (DELETE 204, then GET 404).
- **Probe:** `POST /v1/services/{id}/deploys`, then at once `GET /v1/logs/subscribe?ownerId=…&resource=<srv>&type=build` with `Accept: text/event-stream`, read until the stream ended. Arrival times are UTC.

  - t008's fix holds: the build's own output reaches the tail live.
- **Mechanism:**
  - **The line exists only once the row says so.** `progressLines` stamps `==> Building from` at the deploy row's `StartedAt` (`lego/backend/internal/logs/progress.go:291-293`). That is written when the reconciler observes the build's dispatch, after the build pod has already started printing (here 21 s and 6 s after `Using Node 24`).
  - **The narration is read only twice.** `followBuild` (`lego/backend/internal/logs/service.go:1891-2004`) calls the narration follower (`prog.emitReached`) once before it waits for the pod (`:1892`) and once after every container stream has ended (`:2001`). It never calls it while it streams the pod's containers.
    - A narration line that becomes due while the pod streams is therefore emitted only at the end, out of order and with a backdated timestamp.
    - A closing line due after the pod ends (the row closes after the pod: 16 s here) is not emitted at all. The doc comment calls this case "rides the history read's post-deploy grace poll" (`:1880-1883`), which a tail never does.

## Steps

1. While the pod streams, poll the narration follower on the existing pod-watch tick (`BuildPodWaitInterval`) and emit what became due, so `Building from` arrives within one tick of the row's `StartedAt`.
2. After the pod's streams end, keep the tail open until the deploy row is terminal (bounded, say by the post-deploy grace the history read uses), then emit its closing line and end.
3. Keep t002's rule: a tail replays no earlier deploy. Keep t008's: ended phases are read in pod order.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On production, the two probes above give a live tail whose lines arrive in history's order.
  - `Building from` comes before `Running build command`.
  - The failing build's tail ends with `==> Build failed: …` after the `error: build command … exited with code 3` line.
- [ ] The CLI form (`bex logs --tail --type build`, WebSocket) shows the same order.
