## Objective

REST `serviceDetails.ipAllowList`, GraphQL `ipAllowList` and `ipAllowListEntries`, MCP's service view and the Blueprint export report the three states. The export writes `ipAllowList: []` for a blocked service and leaves the field out for an open one, so re-applying an export keeps access as it is. The Cloudflare-proxied warning treats a blocked service as restricting.
