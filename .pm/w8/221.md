# Small disks (1–9 GB) now ship at Render's sizes and are billed at the requested size on a 10 GB Hetzner volume; keep that, bill 10 GB, or bring back the refusal?

**Status:** todo — 保持 requested-size 计费，实施原选项 4 的真实容量上限并验证 LUKS / 在线扩容
Why: w8/215 shipped the parity-sufficient choice (accept `sizeGB` 1–9 everywhere, provision Hetzner's 10 GB minimum volume, report and bill the requested size) without a pricing decision from the user. Small disks are now a loss-leader, and a stricter or looser variant needs the user's call.

Severity: **minor** (a pricing and margin decision; no user is blocked today). Attribution: pricing policy, `lego/backend/internal/apps/disks.go` (`diskMinSizeGB`), ADR082 § D8, ADR018 disk row. Estimate: ~30m for options 2 or 3 once decided; option 4 is ~2h plus a design check. Source: w8/215 implementation, 2026-10-29.

## What shipped (w8/215)

- REST, GraphQL, MCP, the dashboard's 1/5/10/50/100 GB chips and Blueprint validate/plan/apply accept `sizeGB` from 1. The 11 `render-examples` templates that declare `sizeGB: 1` or `5` no longer hit a disk error.
- The PVC requests the size asked for, and the hcloud CSI driver backs it with a 10 GB volume. The disk row, `spec.disk.sizeGB`, every read-back, the Disk tab's capacity line and the `disk_gb_seconds` meter all use the requested size. A grow inside the volume (1 → 5 GB) needs no expansion and bills 5 GB from that moment.
- Margin at the measured $0.0767/GB-month (≈ $0.77 per 10 GB volume): a 1 GB disk earns $0.175 (≈ −$0.59/month), a 5 GB disk $0.875 (≈ +$0.11/month). Only from about 9 GB is the margin near the sheet's ~56%.
- Residual: the filesystem is the whole 10 GB volume, so a 1 GB disk does not report "disk full" at 1 GB as Render's does (ADR018 disk row). The usage chart can then show used bytes above the capacity line.

## Options (needs the user's decision)

1. **Keep what shipped.** Render-identical sizes and invoice; small disks stay a loss-leader. Record the sign-off and the full-volume residual in ADR018.
2. **Bill 10 GB for any disk under 10 GB** (w1/078 option 2). Templates still validate, but the invoice differs from Render's for the same file. Tell the user at create time (API field, dashboard copy, Blueprint estimate line).
3. **Restore the refusal under 10 GB** (w1/078's original choice). The 11 templates fail validation again until hand-edited. Record the user's sign-off in ADR018 with the corpus count (11 of 170).
4. **Keep option 1 and cap the filesystem at the requested size** (for example a size-limited filesystem or a project quota inside the 10 GB volume), so a 1 GB disk fills at 1 GB as on Render. This closes the residual; it needs a design check against LUKS and the online-grow path.
