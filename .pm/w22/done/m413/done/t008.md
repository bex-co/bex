## Objective

Prove on a real stack that Stop then a new message continues from the interrupted turn's disk.

## Evidence summary 2026-11-17 (/loopx w22)

Both outcomes were observed on real stacks; the full record is in the milestone README under "Live check 2026-11-17".

- **Step 1, local (`dev-10`, `agent-stub`, real sandboxes):** a turn wrote `/workspace/m413-kept.txt` and made a local commit, and was stopped while still running. The turn read `interrupted: true` 2 s after the Stop, `retention: saving`, then `kept` with `retainedUntil` 24 h ahead, 38 s after the Stop (a 1.01 GB image). The follow-up message ran in a new sandbox whose image was `…/images/ari-…@sha256:…`; its shell printed the file's content, the commit, and the file as untracked. Turn 1 then read `continued` and the registry held no tag of the image.
- **Step 2, local, `BEX_AGENT_INTERRUPT_RETENTION=90s`:** `kept` until 90 s after the save, then `lost` with `retentionLostReason: expired` within 4 s of that time, and the image was deleted. The follow-up message started in a fresh sandbox: `cat m413-kept.txt` answered "No such file or directory".
- **Step 1, production:** isolation-matrix run 44042279676 (2026-11-17, agent leg with the retention probe, a disposable workspace, a placeholder key, no model request): the three retention lines passed, the registry check after Cancel included.
