## Objective

Fire an alert when one workspace's sandboxes send far more to the public internet than its plan makes plausible.

## Context

- ADR104 D11 requires "egress metering on `allow-all` sandboxes (the egress-meter exists), and an alert on a workspace whose sandboxes egress far above its plan". Today no sandbox egress is metered: the operator's egress meter (`lego/operator/internal/egressmeter/meter.go`, an eBPF netfilter counter keyed by Pod IP) lists pods by the label `app.bex.co/app` and attributes bytes by `bex.co/app-id` (`reconcilePods`, `desiredPodResources`), and a sandbox pod in `<ws>-sandbox` carries neither.
- Measured on production 2026-11-14/15 (sandbox-measure runs 43688904321 and 43689373598): a sandbox pod's network counters exist on the node (kubelet stats summary `pods[].network`; raw cAdvisor on the pause container with `container=""`), and Prometheus carries none of them because its `kubernetes-cadvisor` job drops `container=""` series. So the cAdvisor route is closed without a scrape change; the eBPF meter is the existing, workspace-attributed source.
- Alert rules live in `deploy/gitops/base/rules/alerting_rules.yml` with promtool tests in `*_test.yml` beside them (`scripts/gitops-validate.sh` globs them; promtool 3.13.5; `scripts/obs-coverage-check.sh` requires a panel per alert, ADR088 § 6). Look at the existing App egress alerts there for the pattern, thresholds and runbook style, and at m401's rules for a recent example with a drill.
- The Free (Hobby) plan gets the same `allow-all` default as paid ones (ADR104 D11), so the control is the alert, not a different default.

## Steps

1. Choose the signal and the threshold from the existing egress alerts and the plans' compute weights (ADR104 D8, `pricing.yaml`): a per-workspace rate of `increase(bex_app_direct_egress_bytes_total{app_id="sandboxes"}[1h])` over a stated number of GiB, with `for:` long enough to ride out a legitimate large download. Write the choice and its reason in the rule's annotation and in ADR104 D11.
2. Failing promtool tests first: the rule stays silent below the threshold and fires above it for one workspace while another stays quiet; silent when the meter is unhealthy (do not alert on a meter that reports nothing).
3. Add the rule with a runbook annotation; run promtool and `bash scripts/gitops-validate.sh`'s promtool and coverage steps (the whole script may fail on unrelated steps here: run the promtool and `scripts/obs-coverage-check.sh` steps directly).

## Outcome (2026-11-15)

- **Done.** `SandboxWorkspaceEgressHigh` (warning, `for: 15m`) in `deploy/gitops/base/rules/alerting_rules.yml`: more than 50 GiB sent to the public internet by one workspace's sandboxes in a rolling hour, counting a node only if its meter was healthy for the last 5 minutes and dropping a series whose counter reset in the hour; `EgressMeterTargetMissing` now expects a meter on sandbox-pool nodes. Eight promtool cases in the new `sandbox_egress_alerts_test.yml` (silent below, fires for one workspace while another stays quiet, summed across nodes, `for` honoured, silent when the meter is unhealthy or reports nothing, an unhealthy meter on another node does not silence a healthy one, silent after a counter reset, App egress ignored). The threshold's reasoning (the plans' monthly outbound allowances against an hour) is in the rule, its annotation and ADR104 D11.
- **Rollout note:** ship the rule and the operator image together; if Argo applies the rule first, `EgressMeterTargetMissing` can fire for a few minutes while the meter rolls onto the sandbox nodes.
