# Meter sandbox egress, then alert on a workspace whose allow-all sandboxes egress far above its plan

Why: ADR104 D11 requires an egress alert because `allow-all` is the default network policy and free workspaces can run sandboxes, but the operator's egress meter cannot see sandbox pods, so there is nothing to alert on; found by w22/m311 t006 on 2026-11-10.

- **Finding.** `lego/operator/internal/egressmeter/meter.go` (`reconcilePods`, `desiredPodResources`) lists pods by the label `app.bex.co/app` and attributes bytes by `bex.co/app-id`. A sandbox pod in `<ws>-sandbox` carries neither. The cAdvisor scrape also drops series with `container=""` (`deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml`, job `kubernetes-cadvisor`), where pod network counters probably live. Read from configs, not from a live scrape: verify first.
- **Work.** (1) Verify on a live sandbox pod whether any network byte counter exists for it. (2) Extend the meter's pod selector (an operator change) or use another source so a sandbox's egress is attributed to its workspace. (3) Add the alert in `deploy/gitops/base/rules/alerting_rules.yml` with a promtool test and the Grafana panel ADR088 § 6 requires. (4) Decide with the user whether sandbox egress is also a billed meter: Render's pages checked on 2026-11-10 give no sandbox egress price, so do not invent one.
- **Sizing.** Likely more than an hour across the operator, rules and dashboards: promote it with `/pm promote w22/002` when its turn comes.
