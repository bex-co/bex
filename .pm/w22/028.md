# A sandbox exec stream through api.bex.co is reset 15–46 s into a silent command: the usage leg cannot finish a 60 s burst

Why: since the evening of 2026-11-23 the `usage` leg of `sandbox-measure.yml` fails on every run before its first metering check, because the exec stream carrying a 60 s busy loop is reset mid-command; if customers' streams are cut the same way, `render sandboxes exec` of any command that prints nothing for 15 s or more fails.

Found by the w22/m314 worker, 2026-11-23, re-running the leg after the production pin carried `ceb23ce81` (w22/024).

- **Symptom.** Three runs in a row: [45275218820](https://github.com/bex-co/bex/actions/runs/45275218820) (22:00Z), [45276212231](https://github.com/bex-co/bex/actions/runs/45276212231) (22:05Z) and [45277163478](https://github.com/bex-co/bex/actions/runs/45277163478) (22:11Z). Each failed with `curl: (56) Recv failure: Connection reset by peer`, then `FAIL: busy burst N did not complete`, about 46 s, 16 s and 15.5 s after the stream reached Traefik. The first run's burst 1 held its full 60 s. The same leg passed at 09:29Z the same day ([45147416800](https://github.com/bex-co/bex/actions/runs/45147416800), five 60 s silent streams in a row).
- **Where the cut is.** Traefik's access log has each stream (`POST /v1/sandboxes/{id}/runs/{exe}/stream`) as `200` with 66 bytes written, ending 1.8 s, 3 s and 6.5 s _after_ the client's reset. So the connection broke between the client and Traefik, on the path through Cloudflare (`api.bex.co` resolves to Cloudflare), the `bex-traefik` load balancer and the NodePort (`externalTrafficPolicy: Local`). bex-api did not close it: no bex-api, ssh-gateway or Traefik pod restarted, and bex-api logged no panic or stream error. In the second run the stream reached Traefik about 13 s after the client sent it.
- **Not an idle timeout.** HTTP/1.1 keep-alive connections held idle for 90 s from a workstation survived both through Cloudflare and straight at the load balancer, and a second request on each was answered. What is cut is a request whose response is in flight and silent.
- **What changed between the passing and the failing runs.** bex-api rolled at about 17:40Z to pin `9fdf7e0a1171` (no exec-path change in `1a5a8e872398..9fdf7e0a1171`). Traefik rolled at about 20:05Z with `bca73171bb` (w6/164): its arguments differ only by `--entryPoints.ssh.transport.lifeCycle.graceTimeOut=3600s`, and the pods moved from required anti-affinity to a topology spread with a 3660 s grace. The gVisor pin moved with `33d658b497`. Cloudflare's settings were not read. The resets fall near multiples of the load balancer's 15 s health-check interval (15.5, 16.2, 46 s), which is suggestive only.
- **Not yet known.** Whether a customer's stream from outside GitHub-hosted runners is cut too (the leg's client is a runner); which hop sends the reset.
- **Done when:** the hop that resets the stream is named with evidence, and the fix is in place or the cause is shown to be outside bex with what a person must change; a silent 60 s exec stream through `api.bex.co` completes from a runner and from a workstation; `gh workflow run sandbox-measure.yml -f leg=usage -f usage_idle_seconds=600` prints PASS again.
- **Not in scope:** the meter itself. w22/m314 closed on the 09:29Z PASS, and the CPU series still exists for the pods of the three failed runs (read on production's Prometheus after the scrape change `6d5b219bd3`).
