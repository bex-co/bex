# Render's sandbox concurrency limit: per group or per workspace

Why: bex counts a workspace's concurrent sandboxes against its plan's number across all of its groups (100 on Hobby, 1,000 on Pro and Scale, w7/069), but Render's limits page gives Hobby's 100 per sandbox group; if Render counts per group, a Hobby workspace with two groups gets a 429 at 101 on bex where Render would let it run 200.

From w22/018 (2026-11-24): its worker read render.com/docs/sandboxes-limits on 2026-11-24 and found Hobby's 100-concurrent-sandbox limit stated per group, while [ADR018's row "Concurrent sandboxes per workspace"](../../docs/ADR018/render-operation-omissions.md) and [ADR042 § Sandbox groups](../../docs/ADR042-sandbox-cluster-substrate.md#sandbox-groups-w22m311) apply it per workspace, citing render.com/pricing (read 2026-11-16) and Render's "Render Pro defaults to 1,000 per workspace" article (2026-11-06). Not yet checked against what Render's API does.
