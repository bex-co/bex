# Agent-session sandboxes: decide whether their retained image is billed storage

Why: w22/m313 meters a suspended sandbox's retained image as `sandbox_snapshot_gb_seconds` for `/v1/sandboxes` sandboxes only, and leaves agent-session sandboxes out; found by w22/m313 t003 on 2026-11-10.

- **Finding.** `storageRef` in `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/meter.go` returns "" for a sandbox that carries `bex.co/agent-session`, and `syncSandboxStorage` in `lego/backend/internal/store/sandbox_meter.go` opens a storage object only for a non-empty ref. The reason is [ADR047](../../docs/ADR047-cloud-coding-agent-sessions.md) D6: a session's cost is its compute, priced into `outcome.cost`, and an agent-session sandbox is torn down by the agent-sessions feature, not idle-suspended (`idleTracked`).
- **Open question.** If a session sandbox is ever suspended (`agent_suspend_test.go` exercises one), its retained image is registry storage bex pays for and no meter charges. Check how long such an image lives in production: a query over the sandboxes with `agent-session` set and phase `Paused` on a live cluster, plus the age of their `snapshots/<ws>-sandbox/` repositories in Zot.
- **Decision for the user.** Bill it (the same meter, `kind = 'rootfs'`, ref set), absorb it, or tear such sandboxes down instead of suspending them. Devin is the reference product for agent sessions (ADR047); check how it prices a session's idle state before choosing, and until then keep absorbing it, bounded by Zot retention and the per-workspace storage cap.
- **Sizing.** Under an hour if the answer is "absorb" (record it in ADR047 D6) and about two hours to bill.
