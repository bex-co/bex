## Objective

Bound what the wider sandbox contract allows: a per-workspace concurrency cap across groups, and an alert on a workspace whose `allow-all` sandboxes egress far above what its plan implies.

## Context

- ADR104 D11: parity widens what a sandbox can do (`allow-all` by default, 30-day retention, Hobby plan); the control is the meter and the cap, not a different default.
- An egress meter exists (operator `egress-meter`; see `lego/operator/AGENTS.md`); whether it covers `<ws>-sandbox` pods is unverified.
- Alert pattern: `deploy/gitops/base/rules/alerting_rules.yml` with a promtool test beside it, and ADR088 § 6's every-alert-gets-a-panel rule.

## Steps

1. Check whether the egress meter sees sandbox pod egress; if not, record the gap and meter it through the lightest existing path.
2. Add the workspace-level cap (configurable) and its 429.
3. Add the alert, its promtool test, and the Grafana panel ADR088 § 6 requires.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/service.go`
- `deploy/gitops/base/rules/alerting_rules.yml` and a `*_test.yml`
- `deploy/gitops/base/values/grafana.values.yaml`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The cap answers 429 across groups in one workspace
- [ ] The promtool test fires the alert on a synthetic egress series and stays quiet below the threshold
