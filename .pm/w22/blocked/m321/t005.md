## Objective

Verify on production that the meter sizes a suspended sandbox's image and that the reaper deletes a terminated sandbox's tags.

## Context

- Together with w22/m313 t006, which compares the metered bytes with the registry and needs the Stripe Price. Follow the production-check pattern of w32/m297 t004.
- The reaper deletes only `snap-gen<N>` tags and is the first writer to the registry that bex-api has ever had.

## Steps

1. Suspend a sandbox; confirm `bex_sandbox_storage_objects{state="unsized_open"}` returns to 0 and its object has bytes within tolerance of the Zot manifest.
2. Terminate it; confirm `reap_pending` clears, its tags are gone from Zot, and a repository the controller did not write to is untouched.
3. Record the evidence summary in this task (no raw dumps).
