## Objective

Measure whether cAdvisor's CPU series for a gVisor sandbox pod is accurate enough to bill on, and record one of three verdicts: pass, escalate to the user, or fail.

## Context

- The `kubernetes-cadvisor` Prometheus job lists every node (no filter), so the tainted sandbox pool is scraped; confirm the series exist with `pod` and `container` labels for sandbox pods (`deploy/gitops/base/prometheus.yaml`, job `kubernetes-cadvisor`).
- gVisor's sentry spends host CPU emulating syscalls, so a syscall-heavy workload may read higher than it would under runc; Render's Firecracker has no such cost (ADR104 D8).
- Policy from ADR104 D8: bill the measured cgroup CPU with no subtraction. Starting thresholds: pass within ±5% of one CPU-minute; escalate to the user if a syscall-heavy load reads more than 2× its runc figure or an idle baseline exceeds 2% of one core. This task fixes the final numbers before running and records why.

5. Record the verdict in ADR104 D8: pass → continue; escalate → park this milestone with the exact question for the user; fail → t004 takes the fallback branch.

## Thresholds, recorded 2026-11-10 before any measurement

These are fixed now so the result cannot move them. They are ADR104 D8's starting numbers, kept as they are.

- **Accuracy (pass):** `stress-ng --cpu 1 --timeout 60s` in a gVisor sandbox must read **60 CPU-seconds ±5%** (57 to 63) from `sum(increase(container_cpu_usage_seconds_total{namespace="<ws>-sandbox",pod="<id>-0",container!=""}[…]))` over exactly the 60 s window, after the 15 s scrape cadence is allowed for by measuring over 5 minutes of repeated runs and dividing.
- **Idle baseline (escalate if above):** an idle running sandbox must read at most **2% of one core over 10 minutes**, 12 CPU-seconds.
- **Syscall-heavy load (escalate if above):** the same syscall-heavy workload (`stress-ng --sockfd`/`--getrandom` for 60 s, fixed before the run) in a plain runc pod on the same node type is the reference; a gVisor reading above **2x** the runc one escalates.
- **Verdict rule:** all three hold → **pass**; accuracy within ±5% but either escalation threshold exceeded → **escalate** (park with the question: bill the measured figure anyway, subtract an overhead factor, or keep the allocated charge); accuracy outside ±5% → **fail** (t004 takes the fallback branch). Exactly one verdict is recorded.
- **Why these:** ADR104 D8 chose "bill the measured cgroup CPU, no subtraction" because it is the figure a customer can reproduce; 2x and 2% are the points at which the bill is visibly worse than Render's for the same work, whose Firecracker VMs have no sentry overhead. They are starting values from the ADR, not measured ones.

## Facts established for t002 without a cluster, 2026-11-10

- The OpenSandbox list response bex-api reads (`osSandbox` in `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/client.go`) carries no pod name.
- The pinned controller names a non-pooled sandbox's pod `<batchsandbox name>-<index>` (`batchsandbox_controller.go:579` at `e95681e7`), and the BatchSandbox name is the substrate sandbox id, which the meter already holds as `StorageRef`. So the pod to read is `<id>-0` in `<ws>-sandbox`. Whether a resume recreates the pod under the same name is not shown by the source and must be read on a live sandbox before t002 relies on it.
- The `kubernetes-cadvisor` job drops series with `container=""`, so a query must filter `container!=""` and sum per series (`sum(increase(...))`), not `increase(sum(...))`, so a recreated pod's new series is counted from zero.
