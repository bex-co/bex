## Objective

Make a Stop turn settle in a few seconds instead of up to the Completer's 15 s tick, independent of the retention work.

## Context

- `Completer.Run` ticks every 15 s, hard-coded (`lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/completion.go` ~396-399; `lego/backend/internal/api/server.go` ~1221-1227 does not set `Interval`). The driver writes `interrupted` within about 3 s plus 15 s for git facts (`driver/src/session.ts` 63-67; the sentinel is polled every 500 ms, `driver/src/interrupt.ts`), and `interrupt_requested_at` is stamped before the sentinel is written (w22/m408).
- The status read is a gateway exec with a 10 s timeout (`completion.go` ~177, 554); reading it for every running session every 2 s would be too much, so read only sessions whose current turn has `interrupt_requested_at`.

## Steps

1. Failing test first (the Completer test harness in `completion_interrupt_test.go`): a session whose turn has a recorded stop request is read again within the short tick, one without it is not.
2. Add the short tick (2 s, a named constant) for sessions with a recorded stop request, bounded by the driver's own settle time; keep the 15 s tick for the rest.
3. Make `Service.Interrupt` tell the Completer in the same process (a shared `StopSignal`), which reads that session on the short tick. Decision while building: the request does not wait for the settle (it would hold an HTTP request open for seconds); every surface answers as before and shows the settled turn within a few seconds on its next read.
4. Update ADR047 D12 'As built' and the CLI notes that say Stop takes up to 15 s.

## Files

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A Stop on a running turn is settled within about 5 s in the real-Postgres test
- [ ] Sessions without a stop request are read no more often than before

## Out of scope

- Changing ADR090: the driver still never commits or pushes, and bex-api pushes nothing on a Stop.

## Outcome (2026-11-15)

- **Done.** `StopSignal` (`lego/backend/internal/agentsessions/stop_signal.go`) carries "a person asked to stop this session" from `Service.Interrupt` to the Completer, which finalizes that session on a 2 s tick for up to 2 minutes (`stopTick`, `stopWatchWindow`; `Completer.Stops`, `StopInterval`, `reconcileStopped`), on the same goroutine as the 15 s tick so a session is never finalized twice at once; wired in `internal/api/server.go`. Without a signal, or on another replica, the 15 s tick is unchanged.
- **Tests:** a settled turn on the short tick with the ordinary tick an hour away (fails without the short-tick case), a session nobody stopped is not read, the watch window forgets stale requests and a nil signal does nothing, and `Service.Interrupt` signals the Completer (real Postgres). ADR047 D12 and ADR018 say Stop settles in a few seconds.
