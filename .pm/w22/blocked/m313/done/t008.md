## Result — 2026-11-10

The usage surfaces agree on the new kinds (adapter test). The explicit suspend exists only on REST (`POST /v1/sandboxes/{id}/pause`; Render has no suspend verb and GraphQL and MCP never had one), and it answers 429 `SANDBOX_STORAGE_LIMIT` there (`TestPauseRouteAnswers429AtTheStorageCap`); the idle sweep refuses the same way and quietly. **Drift, all recorded:** (1) the storage cap itself, a divergence in ADR018 awaiting sign-off; (2) agent-session sandboxes are not metered for storage, w22/005; (3) the Zot read and delete users and bex-api's credential do not exist, w22/006; (4) Render documents no per-workspace storage cap, no price for sandbox egress and no Hobby rule, so those stay as ADR104 D8 and D11 say. Render's sandbox docs were not re-read in this task; the rates come from the 2026-11-06 article.
