## Objective

Bound the registry growth the sandbox path causes: a per-workspace cap on total sandbox storage, deletion of images when a sandbox terminates or reaches 30 days, and a gauge and alert for registry growth.

## Context

- ADR104 D11: the 30-day retention of suspended sandboxes and snapshots widens what storage a free workspace can consume; the control is the meter and the cap.
- `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/purger.go` stops a deleted workspace's sandboxes; registry GC already has alert tests (`deploy/gitops/base/rules/registry_gc_alerts_test.yml`).
- The per-group snapshot-count cap belongs to w32/m285.

## Steps

1. Find what already deletes a suspended sandbox's image on terminate and at 30 days (w2/m278); add what is missing.
2. Add the configurable per-workspace storage cap and the error returned when a suspend or commit would exceed it.
3. Add a registry-growth gauge in `sandbox/metrics.go`, an alert with a promtool test, and the Grafana panel ADR088 § 6 requires.

## Files

- `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/service.go`, `metrics.go`
- `deploy/gitops/base/rules/` (alert + test)
- `deploy/gitops/base/values/grafana.values.yaml`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Terminating a sandbox ends its storage accrual and removes its image
- [ ] A workspace at its cap cannot commit another image and gets a named error
- [ ] The alert test fires on a synthetic growth series

## Out of scope

- Snapshot-count cap (w32/m285)

## Result — 2026-11-10

**What already deleted a suspended sandbox's image:** nothing. Zot's retention keeps a repository's five newest tags forever (`mostRecentlyPushedCount: 5` in `lego/operator/internal/registry/zotconfig.go`), so a terminated sandbox's images stay. Added: the per-workspace cap (`BEX_SANDBOX_MAX_STORAGE_GB_PER_WORKSPACE`, default 500 GB, a bex figure recorded in ADR018 as a divergence awaiting sign-off) with 429 `SANDBOX_STORAGE_LIMIT` on a suspend, explicit or idle; `RegistryReaper`, which deletes a gone sandbox's `snap-gen<N>` tags **by tag** (checked on Zot v2.1.22: a digest delete took a non-controller tag with it); `bex_sandbox_storage_objects{state}`, `bex_sandbox_storage_bytes` and two failure counters; four alerts with promtool tests (all pass; the growth test fails when the rule is wrong) and two Grafana panels (`scripts/obs-coverage-check.sh` passes). **Not done here:** the Zot users with read and delete rights do not exist and bex-api has no registry credential; filed as w22/006. Without them the reaper is off and sizing is off, so no storage is billed and `SandboxStorageReapBacklog` says images are waiting.
