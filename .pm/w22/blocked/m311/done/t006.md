## Objective

Bound what the wider sandbox contract allows: a per-workspace concurrency cap across groups. **Scope change, 2026-11-10 (basis: evidence):** the alert on a workspace whose `allow-all` sandboxes egress far above its plan was dropped from this task because the operator's egress meter cannot see sandbox pods (it lists pods by label `app.bex.co/app` and attributes bytes by `bex.co/app-id`; `lego/operator/internal/egressmeter/meter.go`, `reconcilePods` and `desiredPodResources`; a `<ws>-sandbox` pod carries neither). An alert on that would read a series Prometheus never holds. Making the meter see sandbox pods is operator work, filed as `w22/002`, where D11's egress clause stays open.

## Context

- ADR104 D11: parity widens what a sandbox can do (`allow-all` by default, 30-day retention, Hobby plan); the control is the meter and the cap, not a different default.
- An egress meter exists (operator `egress-meter`; see `lego/operator/AGENTS.md`); whether it covers `<ws>-sandbox` pods is unverified.
- Alert pattern: `deploy/gitops/base/rules/alerting_rules.yml` with a promtool test beside it, and ADR088 § 6's every-alert-gets-a-panel rule.

## Steps

1. Check whether the egress meter sees sandbox pod egress; if not, record the gap and meter it through the lightest existing path.
2. Add the workspace-level cap (configurable) and its 429.
3. Add the alert, its promtool test, and the Grafana panel ADR088 § 6 requires.
