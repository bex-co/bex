# The dashboard's Overview lists ungrouped sandbox groups, and bulk Move can take a selection out of its environment, as Render's dashboard does

Why: Render's dashboard lists a sandbox group that is in no environment on its home page, and its bulk Move can ungroup a selection. bex's dashboard does neither, so a group out of every environment shows nowhere but the Sandbox Groups list, and leaving an environment takes a trip through Manage resources.

Found by w22/m603 t005, 2026-11-23, reading Render's dashboard bundle (entry `assets/index-9Nb9X1Dz.js`, no sign-in):

- **Ungrouped groups on Render's home page.** The home page's ungrouped table adds the workspace's groups that are in no environment (`O.push(...e.filter(e=>!e.environment))`, in `ServicesForUserList-*.js`). They are selectable there and move into an environment with the same bulk Move.
- **bex's Overview today.** The ungrouped list (`dashboard/src/routes/index.tsx` and `features/projects/hooks/use-grouped-resources.ts`) lists services, databases and key value stores only. Adding groups there changes the page's empty and getting-started decision and the shape its skeleton remembers, so w22/m603 left it out.
- **Ungrouping on Render.** On a `/project/…` page, the Move dropdown also offers the workspace's name, which takes the selection out of its environment (`selectUngrouped`, success text "… to be ungrouped.").
- **bex's bulk Move today.** `features/projects/components/bulk-resource-toolbar.tsx` offers environments only, for every kind, which predates w22/m603. A sandbox group, like any other member, leaves its environment by being unchecked in Manage resources.

Done when:
- the Overview's ungrouped list shows a workspace's sandbox groups that are in no environment, as rows that move with bulk Move, and its skeleton still matches its ready state at desktop and narrow-mobile widths;
- a project environment's bulk Move offers Render's workspace item, which takes the selected resources of every kind out of the environment, sandbox groups included (`setSandboxGroupEnvironment(id, null)`);
- both have component tests.
