# Enforce the 40 GB writable filesystem on sandbox pods, after checking kubelet's accounting under gVisor

Why: ADR104 D7 lists a 40 GB writable filesystem per plan as Render's figure; w22/001 (2026-11-10) confirmed nothing enforces it.

- **Finding.** There is no `ephemeral-storage` limit in `deploy/opensandbox/batchsandbox-template.yaml`, `deploy/opensandbox/gvisor-runtimeclass.yaml`, the create request (`osResourceLimits` in `lego/backend/internal/sandbox/client.go` has only `cpu` and `memory`) or the sandbox package. A sandbox can fill the node's disk. The tenant ResourceQuotas in `deploy/gitops/base/tenant-quotas.yaml` bound aggregate ephemeral storage for tenant namespaces only.
- **Why not just add it.** The kubelet enforces `ephemeral-storage` by eviction, from its own accounting of the container's writable layer. Under gVisor (`runsc`, root overlay) that accounting may be absent or wrong, so a limit could be inert or could evict a healthy sandbox. Add it only after reading a live sandbox pod's `ephemeral-storage` usage while it writes a known amount.
- **Work.** (1) On a live gVisor sandbox node, write 5 GB inside a sandbox and read what the kubelet reports (`kubectl get --raw /api/v1/nodes/<node>/proxy/stats/summary`). (2) If it tracks, add `ephemeral-storage: 40Gi` to the sandbox's limits (through OpenSandbox's `resourceLimits` or the pod template) and test that a write past it is refused or evicted as designed. (3) If it does not, record the cap as unenforceable on this substrate and ask the user whether a size-limited `emptyDir` or a project quota is acceptable. (4) Update ADR104 D7 and the ADR018 plan row.
- **Sizing.** About two hours plus a live node: a milestone if (3).
