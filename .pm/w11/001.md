# HELD: native-only launch extras after ADR048's evidence triggers

Do not schedule Live Activities, home-screen widgets, app-store release machinery, or critical-alert/DND override until all ADR048 D5 triggers are measured: agent sessions are live and notification engagement is proven; users demonstrate demand for Live Activities/widgets; and paid-plan volume justifies review/signing overhead. When they fire, promote this note into a fresh milestone covering ActivityKit/widgets, universal links, store signing/review, accessibility, privacy disclosures, and secure push credentials. The imported Expo shell does not by itself satisfy these triggers.
