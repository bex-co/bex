## Objective

Make the custom drawer robust on real phones and assistive technology, including interaction ownership with the app's existing horizontal charts/pagers.

## Context

- The Beancount reference limits open gestures to a 32-point left edge, requires horizontal dominance, and consults `hasActiveHorizontalSwipeOwnerTouch()` so charts/pagers retain their gestures.
- The app already uses Safe Area, rotation-aware `useWindowDimensions`, reduced-motion expectations from m2, and Android/iOS bundles.
- A custom non-Modal reveal drawer must explicitly hide inactive accessibility descendants, announce the open menu, move/restore focus logically, and avoid an invisible backdrop consuming touches after interrupted close.

## Steps

1. Apply safe-area insets, bounded responsive width, narrow-height scrolling, theme tokens, rotation recomputation, and reduced-motion animation behavior.
2. Preserve edge-only open and anywhere-left close gestures while respecting `horizontal-swipe-owner`, vertical scrolling, tab presses, and nested interactive content.
3. Add menu/open-button roles and labels, selected workspace state, logical focus/announcement on open, content accessibility hiding while open, and trigger focus restoration on close.
4. Verify Android back, backdrop, interrupted open/close, rapid repeated actions, foreground/background, light/dark, font scaling, and screen rotation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Drawer gestures do not steal chart/pager/tab/vertical-scroll interactions and remain usable by button alone.
- [ ] Safe areas, rotation, narrow heights, large text, reduced motion, light/dark themes, and interrupted animations render without clipped or untouchable controls.
- [ ] Screen readers encounter one named menu surface while open, hear the selected workspace, cannot traverse obscured content, and return logically after close.
- [ ] Android back closes the drawer before navigating away; no hidden catcher remains after closure.

## Out of scope

- Exact pixel-for-pixel Beancount animation cloning, analytics events, or adding a new gesture/navigation framework.
