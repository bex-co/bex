## Interim simplify pass (2026-08-16)

The required three-way `/simplify` review covered reuse, code quality, and
efficiency across the complete m7 diff. The behavior-preserving findings that
fit this task were applied:

- Mobile decision rendering now derives its decision shape from the generated
  GraphQL result rather than maintaining a casted manual copy; chat and recovery
  status props use the existing typed unions.
- The native streaming list auto-scrolls only while the reader remains pinned
  near the end, so live content no longer repeatedly pulls a reader back after
  they scroll up.

  SQL lower bound to the in-memory cursor after the recovery pass instead of
  rereading the entire 6/24-hour window every two seconds. Boundary keys still
  re-read the cursor timestamp, and tests prove a later row at that exact
  timestamp is not lost.
