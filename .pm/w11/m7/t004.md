## Objective

Let the user answer an explicit ACP-backed decision or send a follow-up turn once, then observe the same session/branch continue across app lifecycle changes.

## Context

- Submission acknowledgment comes from the durable API; stream rendering is not proof the turn committed.

## Steps

1. Build composer/typed-decision UI, idempotency, pending reconciliation, retry/cancel rules, system-browser URL elicitation, and AppState/NetInfo recovery that aborts on background and resumes with a new read ticket when active and online.

## Files

- `mobile/src/features/agent-sessions/{conversation,steering}/**`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Background/kill/reopen during submit neither loses nor duplicates the accepted turn.
- [ ] The app does not keep a stream or ticket alive in background; 401/403/409 responses are surfaced and not blindly retried.
- [ ] Permission UI offers only server-declared one-shot choices and never persistent/bypass approval.

## Out of scope
