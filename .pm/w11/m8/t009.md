## Objective

Open a dedicated OS-verified HTTPS invite link in the signed native app with dashboard fallback and complete the existing explicit bearer-token acceptance flow safely across OAuth.

## Context

- Direct `acceptWorkspaceInvite` tokens are intentionally single-use bearer capabilities independent of the caller's email; login-time email redemption remains verified-email gated.
- Custom schemes are development-only. Production needs exact iOS Universal Link and Android App Link associations for `/invite`.

## Steps

1. Add the dedicated dashboard fallback route and email link without changing invite authorization semantics.
2. Add exact iOS/Android associations plus SecureStore bootstrap, OAuth round-trip, explicit redemption, terminal clearing, retry retention, and workspace refresh.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `https://dashboard.bex.co/invite?invite=<token>` opens the correctly signed app on physical iOS and Android and falls back to dashboard sign-up when absent.
- [ ] Mobile validates and device-only SecureStore-stashes the token, removes it from the visible route, requires explicit acceptance, refreshes workspaces, clears success/terminal failures, and retains only retryable transport failures.
- [ ] Invalid, expired, reused, and seat/plan-refused tokens fail safely; direct redemption remains email-agnostic and login-time email redemption remains verified-email gated.

## Out of scope

- Member administration, billing controls, custom-scheme production links, or changing invite-token authorization semantics.
