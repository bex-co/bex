## Objective

Prove end-to-end push plus OS-verified invite handoff on signed real iOS and Android devices.

## Context

- Use release-equivalent signed builds, disposable devices/subscriptions/events/invites, and capture redacted evidence.
- This is the shared native signing/device gate for `w11/m5` and `w11/m8`; simulator routing and Expo exports do not satisfy it.

## Steps

1. Add/run a repeatable verification harness from event commit through tap-to-authorized target and cleanup.
2. Configure the production Apple Team ID and Android distribution fingerprints, verify hosted AASA/Asset Links, then exercise installed/absent-app invite links and negative paths.

## Files

- `scripts/mobile-push-verify.*`
- `mobile/e2e/**`
- `docs/runbooks/**`
- `dashboard/public/.well-known/**`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Exactly-one-visible delivery, deferral, notification deep link, badge, and cleanup are evidenced on both platforms.
- [ ] `https://dashboard.bex.co/invite?invite=<token>` opens the correctly signed app on both physical platforms, falls back to dashboard when absent, and wrong-path/malformed/reused links fail safely.
- [ ] Hosted association identities match the installed release signatures and only claim exact `/invite`.

## Out of scope

- Simulator-only proof or debug signing identities.
